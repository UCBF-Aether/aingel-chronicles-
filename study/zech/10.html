Study Notes — Zechariah

Chapter 10

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Ask ye of the Lord rain in the time of the latter rain, so the Lord shall make bright clouds, and give them showers of rain, to every one grass in the field, because rain must be asked of the Lord in its season, and he who makes the bright clouds gives showers to every man's field. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: For the idols have spoken vanity, and the diviners have seen a lie, and have told false dreams, they comfort in vain, therefore they went their way as a flock, they were troubled, because they had no shepherd, because idols speak vanity and diviners lie, comforting in vain, so the people wandered like a shepherdless flock. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Mine anger was kindled against the shepherds, and I punished the goats, for the Lord of hosts has visited his flock the house of Judah, and has made them as his goodly horse in the battle, because anger burns against the false shepherds and the goats that led astray, while Judah, visited by God, is made like a goodly war horse. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Out of him came forth the corner, out of him the nail, out of him the battle bow, out of him every oppressor together, because from Judah comes the corner stone, the nail that holds, the battle bow, and every ruler, all the furniture of a governed people. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: And they shall be as mighty men, which tread down their enemies in the mire of the streets in the battle, and they shall fight, because the Lord is with them, and the riders on horses shall be confounded, because Judah's men will tread enemies like street mire, fighting because the Lord is with them, until horsemen are confounded. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: And I will strengthen the house of Judah, and I will save the house of Joseph, and I will bring them again to place them, for I have mercy upon them, and they shall be as though I had not cast them off, for I am the Lord their God, and will hear them, because both houses are strengthened and saved, brought back and placed as if never cast off, since mercy and hearing belong to their God. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: And they of Ephraim shall be like a mighty man, and their heart shall rejoice as through wine, yea, their children shall see it, and be glad, their heart shall rejoice in the Lord, because Ephraim will rejoice like a mighty man cheered with wine, and their children will see it and be glad in the Lord. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: I will hiss for them, and gather them, for I have redeemed them, and they shall increase as they have increased, because God hisses, as a shepherd whistles, and gathers those he has redeemed, and they increase as they once increased. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: And I will sow them among the people, and they shall remember me in far countries, and they shall live with their children, and turn again, because even the sowing among the peoples becomes a sowing for harvest, since in far countries they remember God and live and return. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: I will bring them again also out of the land of Egypt, and gather them out of Assyria, and I will bring them into the land of Gilead and Lebanon, and place shall not be found for them, because the return from Egypt and Assyria ends in Gilead and Lebanon, where no place is found for them, so great is the multitude. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: And he shall pass through the sea with affliction, and shall smite the waves in the sea, and all the deeps of the river shall dry up, and the pride of Assyria shall be brought down, and the sceptre of Egypt shall depart away, because the passage through the sea repeats the exodus, smiting waves and drying deeps, while Assyria's pride falls and Egypt's sceptre departs. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: And I will strengthen them in the Lord, and they shall walk up and down in his name, says the Lord, because the end of the chapter is strength in the Lord, and a walk up and down in his name, which is the life of the returned people. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
