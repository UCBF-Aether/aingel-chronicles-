Study Notes — Zechariah

Chapter 8

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Again the word of the Lord of hosts came to me, saying, because after the rebuke about fasting, a new word of promise follows, still from the Lord of hosts. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord of hosts, I was jealous for Zion with great jealousy, and I was jealous for her with great fury, because the jealousy of chapter one returns, now with fury against Zion's enemies, as the ground of the promises that follow. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord, I am returned unto Zion, and will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem, and Jerusalem shall be called a city of truth, and the mountain of the Lord of hosts the holy mountain, because return means dwelling in the midst, and the city takes new names, city of truth and holy mountain. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord of hosts, there shall yet old men and old women dwell in the streets of Jerusalem, and every man with his staff in his hand for very age, because the sign of restored peace is age itself, old men and women sitting in the streets with staffs, living to very age. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: And the streets of the city shall be full of boys and girls playing in the streets thereof, because the other sign of peace is childhood at play in the streets, where war once emptied them. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord of hosts, if it be marvellous in the eyes of the remnant of this people in these days, should it also be marvellous in mine eyes, says the Lord of hosts, because what seems marvellous to the remnant is not marvellous to God, whose eyes measure differently. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord of hosts, behold, I will save my people from the east country, and from the west country, because salvation gathers from east and west alike, from every quarter of the scattering. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: And I will bring them, and they shall dwell in the midst of Jerusalem, and they shall be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness, because the gathering ends in dwelling, and the covenant is renewed in truth and righteousness. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord of hosts, let your hands be strong, ye that hear in these days these words by the mouth of the prophets, which were in the day that the foundation of the house of the Lord of hosts was laid, that the temple might be built, because the hearers of the prophets, in the very days of the foundation, are told to let their hands be strong for the building. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: For before these days there was no hire for man, nor any hire for beast, neither was there any peace to him that went out or came in because of the affliction, for I set all men every one against his neighbour, because before the rebuilding, labour went unhired and travel unsafe, since God had set every man against his neighbour. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: But now I will not be unto the residue of this people as in the former days, says the Lord of hosts, because the former treatment is expressly reversed for the remnant, and now is set against before. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: For the seed shall be prosperous, the vine shall give her fruit, and the ground shall give her increase, and the heavens shall give their dew, and I will cause the remnant of this people to possess all these things, because prosperity is spelled out in seed, vine, ground and dew, all given to the remnant to possess. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: And it shall come to pass, that as ye were a curse among the heathen, O house of Judah, and house of Israel, so will I save you, and ye shall be a blessing, fear not, but let your hands be strong, because the people who were a curse among the nations will become a blessing, and the command is repeated, fear not, let your hands be strong. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: For thus says the Lord of hosts, as I thought to punish you, when your fathers provoked me to wrath, says the Lord of hosts, and I repented not, because as surely as punishment was purposed and not repented of, so the following promise of good is equally sure. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

15 COMMENTARY: So again have I thought in these days to do well unto Jerusalem and to the house of Judah, fear ye not, because the same resolve that once purposed punishment now purposes good toward Jerusalem and Judah, and the command is fear not. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

16 COMMENTARY: These are the things that ye shall do, speak ye every man the truth to his neighbour, execute the judgment of truth and peace in your gates, because the promised good requires present duties, truthful speech and judgment of truth and peace in the gates. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

17 COMMENTARY: And let none of you imagine evil in your hearts against his neighbour, and love no false oath, for all these are things that I hate, says the Lord, because imagined evil and loved false oaths are named among the things God hates, not merely the acts that follow them. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

18 COMMENTARY: And the word of the Lord of hosts came unto me, saying, because the fast question of chapter seven is now answered directly, with a word from the Lord of hosts. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

19 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord of hosts, the fast of the fourth month, and the fast of the fifth, and the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth, shall be to the house of Judah joy and gladness, and cheerful feasts, therefore love the truth and peace, because the four fasts of captivity will become joy, gladness and cheerful feasts, and the condition is love of truth and peace. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

20 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord of hosts, it shall yet come to pass, that there shall come people, and the inhabitants of many cities, because the promise widens again, with people from many cities yet to come. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

21 COMMENTARY: And the inhabitants of one city shall go to another, saying, let us go speedily to pray before the Lord, and to seek the Lord of hosts, I will go also, because city will stir city, each urging the other to go speedily to pray and seek the Lord, and each answering, I will go also. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

22 COMMENTARY: Yea, many people and strong nations shall come to seek the Lord of hosts in Jerusalem, and to pray before the Lord, because the pilgrimage grows to many peoples and strong nations, all seeking the Lord in Jerusalem. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

23 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord of hosts, in those days it shall come to pass, that ten men shall take hold out of all languages of the nations, even shall take hold of the skirt of him that is a Jew, saying, we will go with you, for we have heard that God is with you, because ten men of every language will seize a Jew's skirt, asking to go with him, since they have heard that God is with the Jews. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
