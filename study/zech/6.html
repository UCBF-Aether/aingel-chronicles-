Study Notes — Zechariah

Chapter 6

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: And I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came four chariots out from between two mountains, and the mountains were mountains of brass, because the eighth vision shows four chariots issuing between brass mountains, the fixed decrees of God, from which his judgments ride out. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: In the first chariot were red horses, and in the second chariot black horses, because the chariots are distinguished by colour, red for war and black for famine and mourning, as the judgments differ. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: And in the third chariot white horses, and in the fourth chariot grisled and bay horses, because white signifies victory and peace, while the grisled and bay, mottled and strong, signify mingled judgments. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Then I answered and said unto the angel that talked with me, what are these, my lord, because again the prophet asks, and the pattern of question and answer carries the interpretation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: And the angel answered and said unto me, these are the four spirits of the heavens, which go forth from standing before the Lord of all the earth, because the chariots are the four winds, God's ministers, going forth from his presence to execute his will over the earth. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: The black horses which are therein go forth into the north country, and the white go forth after them, and the grisled go forth toward the south country, because the judgments are assigned their quarters, black and white to the north, toward Babylon, and grisled to the south, toward Egypt. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: And the bay went forth, and sought to go that they might walk to and fro through the earth, and he said, get you hence, walk to and fro through the earth, so they walked to and fro through the earth, because the strong bay horses are sent to patrol the whole earth, not one quarter only. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Then cried he upon me, and spoke unto me, saying, behold, these that go toward the north country have quieted my spirit in the north country, because the judgments on Babylon have quieted God's spirit, satisfying the anger that Babylon provoked. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying, because the visions give way to a word of action, a symbolic crowning to follow the chariots. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: Take of them of the captivity, even of Heldai, of Tobijah, and of Jedaiah, which are come from Babylon, and come thou the same day, and go into the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah, because the prophet is to take silver and gold from the newly arrived exiles and go the same day to Josiah's house, where the crowning will take place. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: Then take silver and gold, and make crowns, and set them upon the head of Joshua the son of Josedech, the high priest, because crowns of silver and gold are set on the high priest's head, joining priestly and kingly honour in one person, as a sign of the Branch. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: And speak unto him, saying, thus speaks the Lord of hosts, saying, behold the man whose name is The BRANCH, and he shall grow up out of his place, and he shall build the temple of the Lord, because the crowning points beyond Joshua to the Branch, who grows up from his place and builds the true temple. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: Even he shall build the temple of the Lord, and he shall bear the glory, and shall sit and rule upon his throne, and he shall be a priest upon his throne, and the counsel of peace shall be between them both, because the Branch both builds and bears glory, sitting as king and priest on one throne, with a counsel of peace between the two offices. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: And the crowns shall be to Helem, and to Tobijah, and to Jedaiah, and to Hen the son of Zephaniah, for a memorial in the temple of the Lord, because the crowns are laid up in the temple as a memorial to the donors, a witness that the Branch was expected and honoured. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

15 COMMENTARY: And they that are far off shall come and build in the temple of the Lord, and ye shall know that the Lord of hosts has sent me unto you, and this shall come to pass, if ye will diligently obey the voice of the Lord your God, because those far off will come to build, and the whole promise stands upon diligent obedience to God's voice. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
