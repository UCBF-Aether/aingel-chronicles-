Study Notes — Zechariah

Chapter 3

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: And he shewed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the Lord, and Satan standing at his right hand to resist him, because the fourth vision puts the high priest on trial, with the accuser at his right hand, the place of the accuser in court. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: And the Lord said unto Satan, the Lord rebuke thee, O Satan, even the Lord that has chosen Jerusalem rebuke thee, is not this a brand plucked out of the fire, because the rebuke rests on election, and Joshua is a brand plucked from the burning, saved from the fire of captivity. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Now Joshua was clothed with filthy garments, and stood before the angel, because the filthy garments are the iniquity of the priesthood and the people, standing visibly upon the high priest who represents them. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: And he answered and spoke unto those that stood before him, saying, take away the filthy garments from him, and unto him he said, behold, I have caused thine iniquity to pass from thee, and I will clothe thee with change of raiment, because the removal of the garments is the removal of iniquity itself, and the change of raiment is the gift of a new standing. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: And I said, let them set a fair mitre upon his head, so they set a fair mitre upon his head, and clothed him with garments, and the angel of the Lord stood by, because the prophet's plea adds the mitre, the crown of the priesthood, and the angel stands by to see the reinvestiture completed. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: And the angel of the Lord protested unto Joshua, saying, because the angel now solemnly charges Joshua, turning the vision of grace into a charge for the future. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord of hosts, if thou wilt walk in my ways, and if thou wilt keep my charge, then thou shalt also judge my house, and shalt also keep my courts, and I will give thee places to walk among these that stand by, because obedience restores the priest to judging the house and keeping the courts, with a place among the angels who stand by. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Hear now, O Joshua the high priest, thou, and thy fellows that sit before thee, for they are men wondered at, for, behold, I will bring forth my servant the BRANCH, because Joshua and his fellows are men of sign, wondered at as portents, since they point to the Branch, the servant who is to come. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: For behold the stone that I have laid before Joshua, upon one stone shall be seven eyes, behold, I will engrave the graving thereof, says the Lord of hosts, and I will remove the iniquity of that land in one day, because the stone with seven eyes is the foundation stone of the temple, engraved by God himself, and iniquity is removed in one day, which Henry reads as the one day of atonement. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: In that day, says the Lord of hosts, shall ye call every man his neighbour under the vine and under the fig tree, because the day of the Branch brings neighbourly peace, each man calling his neighbour under his own vine and fig tree. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
