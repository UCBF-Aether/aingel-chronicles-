Study Notes — Zechariah

Chapter 4

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: And the angel that talked with me came again, and waked me, as a man that is wakened out of his sleep, because the fifth vision begins with a waking, as if the prophet had slept between visions and must be roused to see. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: And said unto me, what seest thou, and I said, I have looked, and behold a candlestick all of gold, with a bowl upon the top of it, and his seven lamps thereon, and seven pipes to the seven lamps, which are upon the top thereof, because the golden candlestick, fed from a bowl by seven pipes, pictures the church supplied with oil without human tending. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: And two olive trees by it, one upon the right side of the bowl, and the other upon the left side thereof, because the two olive trees stand beside the bowl, feeding it continually, as living sources of the oil. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: So I answered and spoke to the angel that talked with me, saying, what are these, my lord, because the prophet asks the meaning of the whole apparatus, and the question opens the interpretation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Then the angel that talked with me answered and said unto me, knowest thou not what these be, and I said, no, my lord, because the prophet confesses ignorance, and the angel's question humbles him before the answer. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Then he answered and spoke unto me, saying, this is the word of the Lord unto Zerubbabel, saying, not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, says the Lord of hosts, because the vision's word to Zerubbabel is that the temple will be finished not by might or power, but by God's Spirit. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Who art thou, O great mountain, before Zerubbabel thou shalt become a plain, and he shall bring forth the headstone thereof with shoutings, crying, grace, grace unto it, because every mountain of opposition becomes a plain before Zerubbabel, and the headstone is brought forth with shouts of grace. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Moreover the word of the Lord came unto me, saying, because a second word follows, confirming the first with a personal pledge about the builder. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: The hands of Zerubbabel have laid the foundation of this house, his hands shall also finish it, and thou shalt know that the Lord of hosts has sent me unto you, because the same hands that laid the foundation will finish the house, and the finishing will prove the sending. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: For who has despised the day of small things, for they shall rejoice, and shall see the plummet in the hand of Zerubbabel with those seven, they are the eyes of the Lord, which run to and fro through the whole earth, because the day of small things is not to be despised, and the seven eyes are the Lord's watchful providence running through the whole earth. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: Then answered I, and said unto him, what are these two olive trees upon the right side of the candlestick and upon the left side thereof, because the prophet returns to the olive trees, asking their meaning apart from the candlestick. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: And I answered again, and said unto him, what be these two olive branches which through the two golden pipes empty the golden oil out of themselves, because the question narrows to the branches and pipes, asking how the golden oil reaches the lamps. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: And he answered me and said, knowest thou not what these be, and I said, no, my lord, because again the prophet confesses he does not know, and the repeated question marks the mystery of the symbol. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: Then said he, these are the two anointed ones, that stand by the Lord of the whole earth, because the two olive trees are the two anointed ones, Zerubbabel the governor and Joshua the priest, standing by the Lord of the whole earth, supplying the church through their offices. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
