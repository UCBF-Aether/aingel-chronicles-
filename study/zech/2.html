Study Notes — Zechariah

Chapter 2

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: I lifted up mine eyes again, and looked, and behold a man with a measuring line in his hand, because the third vision opens with a surveyor, measuring Jerusalem for rebuilding, as a city to be restored by line. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Then said I, whither goest thou, and he said unto me, to measure Jerusalem, to see what is the breadth thereof, and what is the length thereof, because the measuring is of the city itself, breadth and length both to be known, as a city planned anew. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: And, behold, the angel that talked with me went forth, and another angel went out to meet him, because the interpreting angel goes out, and a second angel meets him, carrying the message that will correct the measuring. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: And said unto him, run, speak to this young man, saying, Jerusalem shall be inhabited as towns without walls for the multitude of men and cattle therein, because the city will be too full for walls, inhabited like open towns, since its multitude cannot be walled in. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: For I, says the Lord, will be unto her a wall of fire round about, and will be the glory in the midst of her, because God himself replaces the wall, a wall of fire without, and glory within, which is better than any wall of stone. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Ho, ho, come forth, and flee from the land of the north, says the Lord, for I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the heaven, says the Lord, because the exiles still in Babylon are summoned to flee, since they were scattered like the four winds and must now gather. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Deliver thyself, O Zion, that dwellest with the daughter of Babylon, because Zion, still dwelling with Babylon's daughter, is told to deliver herself by departure, leaving the place of captivity. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: For thus says the Lord of hosts, after the glory has he sent me unto the nations which spoiled you, for he that touches you touches the apple of his eye, because the nations that spoiled Israel touched the apple of God's eye, and after the glory, God sends against them. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: For, behold, I will shake mine hand upon them, and they shall be a spoil to their servants, and ye shall know that the Lord of hosts has sent me, because the shaking of God's hand reverses the spoil, making the spoilers a spoil to their own servants, which proves the sending. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: Sing and rejoice, O daughter of Zion, for, lo, I come, and I will dwell in the midst of thee, says the Lord, because Zion is told to sing, since the coming is a dwelling in the midst, not a visit from afar. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: And many nations shall be joined to the Lord in that day, and shall be my people, and I will dwell in the midst of thee, and thou shalt know that the Lord of hosts has sent me unto thee, because the dwelling widens, and many nations are joined to the Lord and become his people. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: And the Lord shall inherit Judah his portion in the holy land, and shall choose Jerusalem again, because Judah is God's own portion in the holy land, and Jerusalem is chosen again, as at the first. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: Be silent, O all flesh, before the Lord, for he is raised up out of his holy habitation, because all flesh must fall silent, since the Lord has risen from his holy dwelling to act, and silence is the only fitting answer. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
