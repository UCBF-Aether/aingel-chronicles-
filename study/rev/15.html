Study Notes — Revelation

Chapter 15

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: John saw another sign in heaven, great and marvellous: seven angels having the seven last plagues, for in them is filled up the wrath of God. Henry notes the work was to finish the destruction of antichrist, since Babylon's sins were filled up and she should now find the full measure of God's vindictive wrath. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: John saw as it were a sea of glass mingled with fire, and those who had gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark, and over the number of his name, standing on the sea of glass, having the harps of God. Henry notes the sea may figure this brittle world, the gospel covenant like the temple's brazen sea, or the Red Sea congealed while Israel passed, with fire mingled as God's wrath against Pharaoh. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: They sing the song of Moses the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying, "Great and marvellous are thy works, Lord God Almighty; just and true are thy ways, thou King of saints." Henry notes they extol the greatness of God's works and the justice and truth of his ways, both in delivering his people and destroying their enemies, rejoicing in hope of what was not yet accomplished. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: They ask, "Who shall not fear thee, O Lord, and glorify thy name? for thou only art holy: for all nations shall come and worship before thee; for thy judgments are made manifest." Henry notes they call upon all nations to render God the fear, glory, and worship due to such a discovery of his truth and justice. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: After that John looked, and the temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven was opened. Henry notes the allusion to the holiest of all, where the mercy-seat covered the ark of the testimony: God was fulfilling the prophecies and promises of his word, answering his people's prayers, and avenging the quarrel of his Son. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: The seven angels came out of the temple, having the seven plagues, clothed in pure and white linen, and having their breasts girded with golden girdles. Henry notes this was the high priests' habit when enquiring of God: the angels act under divine appointment, preparing a sacrifice to the Lord, ministers of divine justice doing everything in a pure and holy manner. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: One of the four beasts gave unto the seven angels seven golden vials full of the wrath of God, who lives for ever and ever. Henry notes the vials were received from a minister of the true church, in answer to the prayers of ministers and people, to avenge their cause, and that God's wrath was divided into seven parts to fall successively on the antichristian party. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: The temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God and from his power, and no man was able to enter into the temple till the seven plagues of the seven angels were fulfilled. Henry notes antichrist's interests were so interwoven with civil interests that his destruction would shock the whole world, interrupting sabbaths and assemblies for a time, until the work done, the churches would have rest and the temple be opened. — Matthew Henry
