Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 43

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Judge me, O God, and plead my cause against an ungodly nation; O deliver me from the deceitful and unjust man: the companion to Psalm 42 continues the plea — judgment, advocacy against an ungodly nation, and deliverance from one deceitful, unjust man. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: For you are the God of my strength; why do you cast me off? Why go I mourning because of the enemy's oppression? The God of his strength is asked why he casts off, and why mourning under oppression continues. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: O send out your light and your truth; let them lead me; let them bring me to your holy hill, and to your tabernacles: light and truth are prayed for as guides, to lead him home to God's hill and dwelling. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Then will I go to the altar of God, to God my exceeding joy; yea, upon the harp will I praise you, O God my God: the journey ends at the altar, where God himself is exceeding joy, praised on the harp. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Why are you cast down, O my soul, and why disquieted within me? Hope in God, for I shall yet praise him, who is the health of my countenance, and my God: the refrain closes the pair of psalms as it closed Psalm 42 — hope, future praise, and God as health of countenance. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
