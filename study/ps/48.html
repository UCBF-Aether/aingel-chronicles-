Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 48

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, in the mountain of his holiness: greatness, great praise, and the city and mountain of holiness belong together. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth, is Mount Zion, on the sides of the north, the city of the great King: Zion's beauty and situation make it the joy of the whole earth, as the great King's city. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: God is known in her palaces for a refuge: palaces are the place where God is known — not for splendour alone, but as refuge. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: For, lo, the kings were assembled, they passed by together: assembled kings passing together picture a combined assault on Zion. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: They saw it, and so they marvelled; they were troubled, and hasted away: sight produced marvel, trouble, and hasty flight — Zion's defence was seen, not fought. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Fear took hold upon them there, and pain, as of a woman in travail: fear and travail-pain seized the attackers in the very place of their assault. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: You break the ships of Tarshish with an east wind: as an east wind shatters great merchant ships, so God shatters great kings' power. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: As we have heard, so have we seen in the city of the Lord of hosts, in the city of our God; God will establish it for ever: heard history becomes seen fact in God's city, and establishment for ever is its future. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: We have thought of your lovingkindness, O God, in the midst of your temple: in the temple's midst, lovingkindness is meditated — worship remembers mercy. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: According to your name, O God, so is your praise to the ends of the earth; your right hand is full of righteousness: praise as wide as God's name, to earth's ends, and a right hand full of righteousness. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: Let Mount Zion rejoice, let the daughters of Judah be glad, because of your judgments: Zion's rejoicing and Judah's daughters' gladness answer God's judgments. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: Walk about Zion, and go round about her; tell the towers thereof: a walk round Zion, counting towers, is invited as an act of faith — inspect the defences. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: Mark well her bulwarks, consider her palaces, that you may tell it to the generation following: bulwarks marked and palaces considered become testimony to the next generation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: For this God is our God for ever and ever; he will be our guide even unto death: the God of Zion is our God for ever, and guide even unto death — the psalm ends in lifelong and death-long guidance. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
