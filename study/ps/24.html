Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 24

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The earth is the Lord's, and its fullness, the world and its dwellers: ownership is universal because God made all and fills all. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: For he founded it upon the seas and established it upon the floods: the world stands firm on waters that would swallow it, by God's establishing power. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Who shall ascend into the hill of the Lord, or stand in his holy place? The question of access follows ownership: the earth's Lord must be approached on his terms. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: He who has clean hands and a pure heart, who has not lifted his soul to vanity nor sworn deceitfully: outward deeds and inward purity, with truth in oaths and freedom from idols, mark the one who may ascend. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: He shall receive blessing from the Lord, and righteousness from the God of his salvation: blessing and the righteousness that fits him for God's presence are both God's gifts. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: This is the generation of those who seek him, who seek your face, O Jacob: true seekers, not mere descendants, are Jacob's true generation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Lift up your heads, O gates, and be lifted up, you everlasting doors, and the King of glory shall come in: the gates of Zion — and, in the ascension reading of Henry and JFB, the gates of heaven — are bidden to open wide for the returning King. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Who is this King of glory? The Lord strong and mighty, the Lord mighty in battle: the answer names the warrior King, fresh from victory. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: Lift up your heads, O gates, even lift them up, you everlasting doors, and the King of glory shall come in: the summons is repeated because the entry deserves a double welcome. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: Who is this King of glory? The Lord of hosts, he is the King of glory: the final answer names him commander of heaven's armies, and the psalm closes in acclamation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
