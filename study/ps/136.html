Psalm 136

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry calls thanks again and again — sacrifice of praise continually, fruit of lips giving thanks to his name (Heb 13:15), never so earnestly called to pray and repent as give thanks, will of God abound most in pleasant exercises, work of heaven; give thanks to Lord Jehovah Israel's God, not only because he does good but because he is good, streams traced to fountain, not only merciful to us but mercy endures for ever drawn out to those after us, not only mercy handed out here on earth but that shall endure for ever in glories joys of heaven; singular composition latter half each verse same repeated yet no vain repetition, burdens keepings add beauty moving affecting, weightiest matter worthy repeated, twenty-six repetitions intimate mercies repeated drawn continuando beginning to end progress advance in infinitum, every particular favour notice mercy enduring still same now as been and for ever same always, everlasting continuance God's honour glory and saints' comfort glory, hearts full greatly affected most frequent mention instead of cloying raise more, subject of praise to all eternity, owned in Solomon's time (2 Chr 5:13 house filled cloud) and Jehoshaphat's (2 Chr 20:21-22 victory). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry thanks God of gods — God whom angels adore, from whom magistrates derive power, by whom all pretended deities are and shall be conquered; adorations must eye God's excellency transcendent, power dominion incontestably uncontrollably supreme; not Pharisee thanksgivings terminating own praise, directing all to God's glory; mercy disposition to save whom sin rendered miserable vile, provision redemption sinners by Jesus Christ, counsels from everlasting effects endure for ever to interested, Lord equally ready show mercy all seek, source of all hope comfort. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry thanks Lord of lords — Sovereign of all sovereigns, stay supporter of all supports; supreme dominion over every lordship, same enduring mercy as fountain for thanks. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry alone does great wonders: contrivance wonderful design laid infinite wisdom, performance wonderful execution infinite power; alone does marvellous things, none besides can do such, does them without assistance advice of any other. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry made heavens by wisdom, stretched them out: therein see wisdom power, taste mercy in benign influences; as long as heavens endure mercy of God endures in them. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry stretched earth above waters: raised earth out of waters caused dry land appear fit habitation for man, therein mercy to man still endures, earth given children of men and all its products. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry made great lights: settled correspondence between heaven and earth notwithstanding distance, sun moon stars placed firmament shed light influences upon earth; called great lights because appear so to us, astronomers tell moon less than many stars but nearer earth seems much greater. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

8 COMMENTARY: Henry sun rules by day: lights said to rule not only govern seasons year but useful world, benefactors best rulers (Luke 22:25); empire divided one rules day other night, yet all subject God's direction disposal; rulers Gentiles idolized are world's servants God's subjects. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

9 COMMENTARY: Henry moon and stars rule by night: night empire of moon and stars likewise subject to God, serving earth by light and seasons, not deities but servants under his direction. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

10 COMMENTARY: Henry smote Egypt in firstborn: great things God did for Israel first forming people setting kingdom among them, instances power and particular kindness; brought out Egypt mercy endured long, redemption by Christ typified endures for ever eternal redemption; of all plagues none mentioned but death first-born because conquering plague, by that God who in all plagues distinguished Israelites from Egyptians brought them at last from among them not by wile but strong hand arm stretched reach far do great things; miracles of mercy proved Moses commission give law, laid Israel lasting obligations obey law (Exod 20:2). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

11 COMMENTARY: Henry brought Israel out from among Egyptians: same conquering deliverance as v10 — not by wile but by distinction and strong hand, bringing a formed people out from among oppressors into covenant. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

12 COMMENTARY: Henry strong hand stretched arm: arm reaching far doing great things, power over Egypt and over hearts, miracles of mercy proving commission and laying obligation, strong hand not human contrivance. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

13 COMMENTARY: Henry divided Red Sea into parts: forced way through sea obstructing first setting out, power control common course nature divided sea two parts opened path, made Israel pass between parts now entering covenant (Jer 34:18); not only divided but gave people courage go through when divided — power over hearts as former over waters. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

14 COMMENTARY: Henry made Israel pass through midst: courage to walk between divided waters, covenant entry, path opened and people emboldened to take it. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

15 COMMENTARY: Henry overthrew Pharaoh and host in Red Sea: miracle justice as well as mercy — same sea lane to Israelites grave to pursuers, there shook off Pharaoh and his host. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

16 COMMENTARY: Henry led people through wilderness: conducted vast howling wilderness, led and fed them, camp victualled fortified constant series miracles forty years, though loitered wandered not lost; mercy and constancy more observable because often provoked him wilderness grieved desert. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

17 COMMENTARY: Henry slew great kings: destroyed kings before them make room, not deposed banished but smote slew, appeared wrath against them but mercy never-failing to Israel; magnified they were great kings yet God subdued as easily as least weakest meanest children of men, wicked kings grandeur lustre would not secure from justice. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

18 COMMENTARY: Henry slew famous kings: famous as well as great — more great famous more God's mercy to Israel appeared giving such kings for them, fame no shield against justice, mercy magnified by stature of fallen. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

19 COMMENTARY: Henry Sihon king of Amorites: particularly mentioned because first two conquered other side Jordan, detail of favours not gross view, each instance observe own mercy endureth for ever. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

20 COMMENTARY: Henry Og king of Bashan: second of first two conquered beyond Jordan, paired with Sihon as opening conquests making room for Israel. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

21 COMMENTARY: Henry gave their land heritage: he whose earth fulness world dwellers took land one people gave another as pleased him, iniquity Amorites now full therefore taken; Israel his servant though provoking wilderness yet intended some service out of them, to them pertained service of God; as said Egyptians Let my people go so Canaanites Let my people in that they may serve me. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

22 COMMENTARY: Henry heritage unto Israel his servant: land given not to strangers but servant people, service purpose, mercy endureth for ever because figure of heavenly Canaan, mercy of Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

23 COMMENTARY: Henry remembered us in low estate: many redemptions Jewish church out of oppressors, years servitude estate very low God remembered raised saviours judges and David at length gave rest all enemies; especially great redemption universal church types — remembered children of men low estate lost estate, sent Son redeem from sin death hell all spiritual enemies, sent redeem us not angels sinned. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

24 COMMENTARY: Henry redeemed us from enemies: church's redemption completed — saviours judges David rest from enemies as types, Christ's redemption from sin death hell spiritual enemies as substance, mercy enduring in deliverance itself. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

25 COMMENTARY: Henry gives food to all flesh: instance mercy of providence wherever given life gives food agreeable sufficient, good housekeeper provides so large family. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

26 COMMENTARY: Henry thanks God of heaven: denotes glorious God, glory of mercy taken notice in praises, riches of glory displayed vessels of mercy (Rom 9:23); denotes great benefactor every good perfect gift from above Father of lights God of heaven, trace every stream fountain; this and that particular mercy may endure but while, mercy in God endures for ever inexhaustible fountain. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
