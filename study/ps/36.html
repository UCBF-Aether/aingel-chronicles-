Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 36

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The transgression of the wicked says within my heart that there is no fear of God before his eyes: the wicked man's sin itself testifies — his life declares his godlessness. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: He flatters himself in his own eyes, until his iniquity is found hateful: self-flattery blinds him until his sin, long hidden, is exposed as hateful. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: The words of his mouth are iniquity and deceit; he has left off being wise and doing good: speech betrays him, and he has ceased both wisdom and goodness. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: He devises mischief on his bed, sets himself in a way not good, and abhors not evil: night plotting, a chosen bad way, and no hatred of evil complete the portrait. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Your mercy, Lord, is in the heavens, and your faithfulness reaches to the clouds: against man's small wickedness stands a mercy as high as heaven. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Your righteousness is like the great mountains, your judgments a great deep; Lord, you preserve man and beast: righteousness is mountain-firm, judgments ocean-deep, and preservation extends even to beasts. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: How excellent is your lovingkindness, O God! Therefore the children of men trust under the shadow of your wings: excellent lovingkindness is itself the reason men shelter under God's wings. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: They shall be abundantly satisfied with the fatness of your house, and drink of the river of your pleasures: God's house provides a feast and a river — abundance, not scarcity. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: For with you is the fountain of life; in your light shall we see light: life's fountain is in God, and only in his light is any other light truly seen. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: O continue your lovingkindness to those who know you, and your righteousness to the upright in heart: continuance is prayed for — the known God and the upright heart are the recipients. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: Let not the foot of pride come against me, nor the hand of the wicked remove me: pride's foot and the wicked's hand are the two threats to his standing. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: There the workers of iniquity are fallen; cast down, they shall not rise: the fall is already seen as accomplished — cast down beyond rising. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
