Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 47

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: O clap your hands, all you people; shout to God with the voice of triumph: clapping and shouting with triumph are the people's part in the King's victory. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: For the Lord Most High is terrible; he is a great King over all the earth: terribleness and universal kingship are the reasons for the triumph. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: He shall subdue the people under us, and the nations under our feet: subjugation of peoples and nations under Israel's feet is God's work for his people. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: He shall choose our inheritance for us, the excellency of Jacob whom he loved: the inheritance is chosen by God, and its excellency is Jacob, whom God loved. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet: the ascension — God going up amid shouts and trumpets — is celebrated, and Christians read here Christ's ascension. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Sing praises to God, sing praises; sing praises to our King, sing praises: the command is repeated four times, because praise for such a King cannot be said once. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: For God is the King of all the earth; sing praises with understanding: universal kingship calls for praise with understanding, not mere noise. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: God reigns over the heathen; God sits upon the throne of his holiness: reign over the heathen from a holy throne — power and holiness enthroned together. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: The princes of the people are gathered together, even the people of the God of Abraham; for the shields of the earth belong to God; he is greatly exalted: princes and peoples gather to Abraham's God, for earth's shields are his, and he is greatly exalted. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
