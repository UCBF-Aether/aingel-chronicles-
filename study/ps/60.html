Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 60

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: O God, you have cast us off, you have scattered us, you have been displeased; O turn yourself to us again: casting off, scattering, and displeasure are confessed as God's acts, and turning again is the prayer. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: You have made the earth to tremble, you have broken it; heal the breaches thereof, for it shakes: a trembling, broken, shaking earth needs healing of its breaches. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: You have shown your people hard things; you have made us to drink the wine of astonishment: hard things shown, wine of astonishment drunk — defeat has stunned the people. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: You have given a banner to those who fear you, that it may be displayed because of the truth: a banner given to the fearing, displayed because of truth — a rallying sign amid defeat. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: That your beloved may be delivered; save with your right hand, and hear me: the banner's purpose is the beloved's deliverance, by God's right hand and hearing. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: God has spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice, I will divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth: God's holy speech grounds rejoicing, and dividing Shechem and meting Succoth picture the land apportioned by God. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Gilead is mine, and Manasseh is mine; Ephraim also is the strength of my head; Judah is my lawgiver: Gilead, Manasseh, Ephraim as head's strength, Judah as lawgiver — the tribes are God's, each in its office. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Moab is my washpot; over Edom will I cast out my shoe; Philistia, triumph because of me: Moab as washpot, Edom under the cast shoe, Philistia bidden triumph because of God — neighbouring nations are God's servants and subjects. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: Who will bring me into the strong city? Who will lead me into Edom? The strong city and Edom are the objectives — who can lead there but God? — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: Will not you, O God, who had cast us off? And you, O God, who did not go out with our armies? The God who cast off and did not go out with the armies is asked, in the very terms of the complaint, to go out now. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: Give us help from trouble, for vain is the help of man: help from trouble is asked of God, for man's help is vain. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: Through God we shall do valiantly, for he it is that shall tread down our enemies: through God, valiant deeds, and God treading down enemies — the psalm ends in valour credited to God. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
