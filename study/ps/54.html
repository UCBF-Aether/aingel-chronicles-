Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 54

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Save me, O God, by your name, and judge me by your strength: salvation by God's name and judgment by his strength are the two pleas — the title recalls the Ziphites betraying David to Saul. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Hear my prayer, O God; give ear to the words of my mouth: hearing and giving ear are asked for the prayer's very words. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: For strangers are risen up against me, and oppressors seek after my soul; they have not set God before them: strangers and oppressors, who have not set God before them, are the enemies — godlessness explains their hostility. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Behold, God is my helper; the Lord is with those who uphold my soul: against risen strangers stands God as helper, with those who uphold his soul. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: He shall reward evil to my enemies; cut them off in your truth: evil rewarded to enemies and cutting off in God's truth — truth keeps the promise of justice. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: I will freely sacrifice to you; I will praise your name, O Lord, for it is good: free sacrifice and praise of God's good name answer deliverance before it is fully seen. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: For he has delivered me out of all trouble, and my eye has seen his desire upon my enemies: deliverance out of all trouble, and the eye seeing God's desire on enemies, close the psalm in accomplished fact. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
