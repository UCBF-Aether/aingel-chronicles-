Psalm 134

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry closes fifteen songs of degrees fitly: design stir up ministers go on with work in night when solemnities of day over; dialogue reading — first two verses priests Levites sat up all night keep watch of house called spend guard not idle talk but acts devotion, last verse those called pray for exhorter high priest or captain of guard, or mutually exhort and pray one another; two-fold blessing — our blessing God speaking well of him; call to Levites servants by office appointed minister holy things, attended sanctuary kept charge (Num 3:6-10), some by night stood guard holy things not profaned rich things not plundered, ark in curtains more need guards, attended fire altar lamps candlestick not go out, probably devout pious Israelites sat up with them, one departed not temple night or day (Luke 2:37); called bless Lord thus keep awake keeping employed, redeem time holy exercises, how spend time better than praising God, excellent husbandry fill vacancies pious meditations ejaculations, modest reasonable converse God when nothing else do; stood in house remember where, holiness holy work became house; bless in concert or each himself; call to us Christians made priests to God Levites (Isa 66:21), servants place name in house sanctuary stand before minister, even by night under eye access him, therefore bless again bless, think speak glory goodness. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry lifts hands: let them lift up hands in doing it token lifting hearts, lift hands in holiness (Hammond) or sanctification as fit when lift in sanctuary, remember appointed wash before minister taught lift holy hands prayer praise; ushered Behold note commanding attention — look about Sirs when in God's presence conduct accordingly; lift hands prayer praise vows, do work diligence cheerfulness elevation of mind. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry returns blessing: God's blessing us doing well for us taught desire; whether watchmen blessing captain, Levites high priest, whoever chief singular Lord bless thee, or one upon many Lord bless thee each particular thee and thee you blessing God Lord bless you — not material; learn need desire no more make happy than blessed of Lord, whom he blesses blessed indeed; blessings out of Zion spiritual blessings covenant communion with God best blessings most earnest for; great encouragement come God for blessing he made heaven earth therefore all blessings both at disposal upper nether springs; beg blessings not only selves but others, not only Lord bless me but Lord bless thee testifying fulness divine blessings enough for others as us and good-will, pray for those exhort us, though less blessed of greater (Heb 7:7) yet greater must be prayed for by less. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
