Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 63

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: O God, you are my God; early will I seek you; my soul thirsts for you, my flesh longs for you in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is: early seeking, thirsting soul, longing flesh, dry land without water — the title sets this in the wilderness of Judah, and thirst is the psalm's image. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: To see your power and your glory, so as I have seen you in the sanctuary: seeing power and glory as once seen in the sanctuary — wilderness worship longs for sanctuary sight. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Because your lovingkindness is better than life, my lips shall praise you: lovingkindness better than life itself is the ground of lips' praise. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Thus will I bless you while I live; I will lift up my hands in your name: blessing while living, hands lifted in God's name — praise is bodily and lifelong. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: My soul shall be satisfied as with marrow and fatness, and my mouth shall praise you with joyful lips: marrow-and-fatness satisfaction, joyful lips' praise — spiritual feasting satisfies as rich food. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: When I remember you upon my bed, and meditate on you in the night watches: bed remembrance and night-watch meditation — praise continues through the night. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Because you have been my help, therefore in the shadow of your wings will I rejoice: past help grounds present rejoicing under wings' shadow. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: My soul follows hard after you; your right hand upholds me: hard following after God, upheld by his right hand — pursuit and upholding meet. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: But those who seek my soul, to destroy it, shall go into the lower parts of the earth: soul-seekers to destroy shall go down to earth's lower parts. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: They shall fall by the sword; they shall be a portion for foxes: falling by the sword, portion for foxes — the destroyers become scavengers' prey. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: But the king shall rejoice in God; every one who swears by him shall glory, but the mouth of those who speak lies shall be stopped: the king's rejoicing in God, swearers' glory, liars' stopped mouths — truth's triumph closes the wilderness psalm. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
