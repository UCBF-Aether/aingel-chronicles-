Psalm 146

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry praise Lord soul: rest psalms follow begin end Hallelujah puts much praise little compass praise name Jah contraction Jehovah; excellent praise engages himself praise vv1-2, engages others trust necessary acceptable way praising; David supposed penned himself prince mighty prince thought exempted service praising enough see priests people do needed not own person, Michal thought disparagement dance ark far mind himself first foremost work, dignity far excusing rather obliged lead far lessening really magnified, stirred himself make business Praise Lord soul resolved abide — praise heart sing praises mouth, eye Lord infinitely blessed glorious himself my God covenant, praise most pleasant eye him ours interest relation. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry while live praise sing God while being: constantly while live every day life life end, nay while any being when no being earth hope being heaven better being doing better; great end being great employment delight while any being, thee time powers spent. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry put not trust princes son man no help: thought himself great blessing country adored usage heathen deified heroes come trust shadow make stay strong-hold — No Put not trust princes not me not any, repose confidence raise expectations; not too sure sincerity some thought knew reign knowing dissemble, not too sure constancy fidelity possible change minds break words; suppose wise good David yet not too sure ability continuance sons Adam weak mortal; Son of man help salvation not fail trust, other sons men like man sprung honour did not abide; cannot sure ability — power kings straitened cramped weakened not capacity do expected, David owned (2 Sam 3:39) I am this day weak though anointed king, son man often no help no salvation at loss wits end man astonished mighty man cannot save (Jer 14:9). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry breath goeth returneth earth day thoughts perish: cannot sure continuance — suppose power help while lives suddenly taken off expect most, breath goes forth every moment comes back intimation shortly go good all then returns earth; earth his original man taken must return sentence (Gen 3:19), his if worldly choice portion affections set, go own place, or rather property though large possessions grave all remain; earth God given children men great striving mark authority call lands own names, after while no part own but dead body bed theirs while earth remains; returns earth very day thoughts perish projects designs kindness vanish gone cannot step further purposes cut off buried (Job 17:11), then expectations? Princes mortal other men cannot assurance help Potentate immortality, Cease man breath nostrils will not long. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry happy God Jacob help hope Lord God: cautioned not trust princes miserably disappointed encourages confidence God happily secured — Happy God Jacob help interest attributes promises engaged hope Lord God; character upheld — take God serve worship accordingly, hope live dependence good thoughts encourage when supports fail; believer God Jacob church general expect relief public distresses his God particular depend personal wants straits; hope providence good things life now grace Christ life come; Dr Hammond latter part visible remarkable aspect eternal Son incarnation, rabbi v10 days Messiah, comparing vv7-8 characters Christ Messiah (Matt 11:5-6) blind receive sight lame walk closing Blessed whosoever not offended supposed refer v5 Happy hopes Lord God not offended. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry made heaven earth sea therein keepeth truth ever: Maker world all power command powers creatures derived depend — made heaven earth sea all in them arm not shortened cannot save, applicable Christ by whom God made world without whom not anything made, great support faith Redeemer same Creator good-will perfect knowledge case power help; God inviolable fidelity venture word keepeth truth ever no word fall ground true beginning therefore true end, Lord Jesus Amen faithful witness beginning author principle creation God (Rev 3:14), keeping truth ever committed him promises yea amen. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry executeth judgment oppressed giveth food hungry looseth prisoners: patron injured innocency pleads cause oppressed executes judgment, providence redress suffer wrong clearing integrity, judgment great day, Messiah rescue children men Satan great oppressor judgment committed executing persecutors (Jude 15); bountiful benefactor necessitous gives food hungry ordinary cravings nature extraordinary ravens fed Elijah Christ fed thousands miraculously intended one meal two family, encourages hope nourisher souls bread life; author liberty bound looseth prisoners brought Israel bondage Egypt Babylon, miracles dumb speak deaf hear Ephphatha Be opened cleansing lepers discharging confinements raising dead graves included loosing prisoners, encouragement hope spiritual liberty proclaim (Isa 61:1-2). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

8 COMMENTARY: Henry openeth eyes blind raiseth bowed down loveth righteous: sight long deprived — Lord open eyes blind often given afflicted see comfort before not aware witness (Gen 21:19) prophet servant (2 Kings 6:17), special reference Christ since world began not heard man opened eyes born blind till Christ (John 9:32) encouraged hope spiritual illumination; sets straight crooked makes easy pained ready sink raises bowed comforting supporting burdens due time removing burdens, literally Christ poor woman straight bowed together no wise lift (Luke 13:12) still grace rest weary heavily laden raising comforts humbled cast down convictions; constant kindness good people loveth righteous more confidence depend power sure good-will, Lord Jesus showed love righteous fulfilling all righteousness. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

9 COMMENTARY: Henry preserveth strangers relieveth fatherless widow way wicked turneth upside down: tender concern special need care — Jehovah repeated five times five lines intimate almighty power Jehovah engaged exerted relief oppressed glory God succour misery ride heavens name Jah (Ps 68:4); strangers exposed commonly destitute friends Lord preserves not run down ruined, many poor stranger benefit divine protection kept alive; widows fatherless lost head family took care affairs often hands make prey not justice nay injustice Lord relieveth raiseth friends (Exod 22:22-23), Lord Jesus help helpless receive Gentiles strangers kingdom poor sinners fatherless find mercy (Hos 14:3); destruction opposers kingdom oppress faithful — way wicked turns upside down hope not afraid fury oppressor ready destroy, glory Messiah subvert counsels hell earth militate church having him need not fear anything done against. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

10 COMMENTARY: Henry Lord reign ever God Zion generations praise Lord: kingdom continue revolutions time utmost ages eternity encourage trust times Lord reign ever spite malignity powers darkness even thy God Zion generations, Christ King holy hill Zion kingdom continue endless glory, cannot destroyed invader not left successor succeeding monarch monarchy stand ever, unspeakable comfort Lord reigns Zion God Zion king Messiah head all things church while world stands. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
