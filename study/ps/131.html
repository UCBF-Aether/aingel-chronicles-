Psalm 131

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry professes humility against Saul's courtiers calumny that David ambitiously sought kingdom under pretence of divine appointment — appeals to God: aimed at nothing high nor great, content to spend all days in sheep-folds if God so ordered, brother's pride charge (1 Sam 17:28) groundless unjust; heart not haughty — humble saints cannot think so well of themselves as others think, not in love with own shadow nor magnifying attainments, love of God subdues inordinate self-love; eyes not lofty — neither envy above nor disdain below, proud heart commonly proud look (Prov 6:17), humble publican will not lift eyes; employed not in things above station — studies too high amused not with nice speculation doubtful disputation nor wise above written, God's word meditation, to know God and duty learning sufficiently high, affairs too great followed ewes never set up politician nor soldier, brethren to wars he stayed home keeping sheep; wisdom and praise keep within sphere, not intrude unseen nor meddle not belonging, princes scholars not matters too great too high, low station ordinary capacities not pretend out of reach not cut out for, affecting undue honours falls under due shame. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry reconciled to every condition God placed him: behaved and quieted as child weaned of mother — not proudly aiming kingdom, since God appointed not insolent nor restless to get crown before time; humble as weanling, manageable governable, far from aiming high, entirely at God's disposal as child at mother's or nurse's, far from taking state though anointed king or valuing prospect of advancement, as child in arms, Saviour taught humility by comparison (Matt 18:3) must become little children; indifferent to wealth honour as child to breast thoroughly weaned — levelled quieted (Hammond); hearts naturally desirous of worldly as babe of breast, relish cry fond play cannot live without, but grace sanctified weans, providence puts wormwood on breast helps wean; child cross fretful in weaning thinks undone lost breast, in day or two forgotten fret over accommodates new feeding cares no longer milk can bear strong meat; gracious soul quiets under loss of loved and disappointment in hoped, easy whatever happens, lives comfortably upon God and covenant-grace when creatures prove dry breasts; condition not to mind bring mind to condition, easy to selves and all about, souls as weaned child. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry recommends confidence from own experience: let Israel hope in Lord henceforth and for ever — though David himself wait patiently quietly for crown designed, Israel his darling ready to attempt something in favour before time, therefore endeavours quiet them too, bids hope in Lord they should see happy change of face of affairs in due time; good to hope and quietly wait for salvation of Lord. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
