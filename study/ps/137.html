Psalm 137

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry posts captives by rivers of Babylon, strange land great way from country, prisoners of war; Babylon house of bondage as Egypt in beginning; conquerors quartered by rivers design employ there keep work in galleys, or chose most melancholy place suitable sorrowful spirits; if build houses (Jer 29:5) not cities places of concourse but rivers places of solitude mingle tears with streams; some river Chebar (Ezek 1:3), others river Ulai (Dan 8:2); sat down indulge grief poring miseries, Jeremiah taught under yoke sit alone keep silence put mouths dust (Lam 3:28-29), sat as expected stay content since will of God must be so; thoughts of Zion drew deliberate tears not sudden passion — sat down and wept tears with consideration, wept remembered Zion holy hill temple built; affection God's house swallowed concern own houses; remembered Zion former glory satisfaction courts (Lam 1:7), Jerusalem misery remembered pleasant things days old (Ps 42:4), remembered present desolations favoured dust good sign time for God favour not far off (Ps 102:13-14); daughter Zion covered cloud dwelling daughter Babylon, people God tears but sowing tears; penned captives Babylon insulted proud oppressors, probably latter end captivity now destruction Babylon hastening discharge, mournful lamentation Septuagint Jeremiah author. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry hangs harps upon willows: laid by instruments music — harps own diversion entertainment laid aside judgment ought not use now God called weeping mourning (Isa 22:12), spirits sad no hearts use, brought harps designing alleviate grief proved so great would not admit experiment, music makes some melancholy vinegar upon nitre sings songs heavy heart; harps God's worship Levites' harps not thrown away hoping yet again occasion use, laid aside no present use, God cut other work turning feasting mourning songs lamentations (Amos 8:10), everything beautiful season; did not hide bushes hollows rocks but hung up view sight affect deplorable change; yet perhaps faulty — praising God never out of season, will in everything give thanks (Isa 24:15-16). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry endures insult: carried captive own land then wasted in captivity took little had, not enough complete woes insulted — required mirth and song; very barbarous inhuman even enemy misery pitied not trampled, base sordid spirit upbraid distress former joys present griefs challenge merry out of tune, adding affliction afflicted; very profane impious no songs serve but songs of Zion with which God honoured, demand reflected God himself as Belshazzar drank wine temple-bowls, enemies mocked sabbaths (Lam 1:7). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry refuses to humour scoffers: laid by harps would not resume no not ingratiate with those at mercy lay, would not answer fools according folly, profane scoffers not humoured nor pearls cast swine, David prudently kept silence even from good wicked before him knew ridicule jest (Ps 39:1-2); reason mild pious — How sing Lord's song strange land? Not say how sing so much sorrow — if all might force oblige masters song — but It is Lord's song sacred peculiar temple-service dare not sing land stranger among idolaters; must not serve common mirth much less profane mirth with appropriated God, sometimes honoured religious silence as well religious speaking. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry vows right hand forget: constant affection Jerusalem city solemnities even Babylon, enemies banter talking doting, love not abated may be jeered for never jeered out of; heads full — always minds remembered not forget long absent, many never seen nor knew but report scripture yet graven palms hands even ruins continually before evidence faith promise restoration due time, daily prayers windows towards Jerusalem how forget; solemn imprecation mischief selves if let fall — for ever disabled sing play harp if so far forget religion country as use songs harps pleasing Babylon's sons praising Babylon's gods; let right hand forget art hand expert musician never can unless withered. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry cleaves tongue to roof: hearts full preferred above chief joy therefore remembered could not forget; what we love love think of, rejoice God for his sake make Jerusalem joy prefer before head of joy dearest world, godly prefer public good before private satisfaction gratification whatsoever; tongue cleave roof mouth if not good word say for Jerusalem wherever am; dare not sing Zion songs among Babylonians yet cannot forget, soon as restraint taken off sing readily as ever notwithstanding long disuse. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry remembers Edom: pious Jews afflicted thoughts ruins Jerusalem please prospect ruin impenitent implacable enemies not spirit revenge but holy zeal glory God honour kingdom; Edomites certainly reckoned all accessaries destruction aiding abetting helped forward affliction (Zech 1:15) triumphed, said day Jerusalem day judgment Rase it rase it foundations down with it leave not stone upon another; made Chaldean army more furious already enraged needed no spur, put shame Israel looked people worthy cut off next neighbours ill-will; fruit old enmity Esau Jacob birthright blessing branch ancient enmity seed woman seed serpent; Lord remember appeal justice, far be avenge ourselves if ever power but leave him Vengeance is mine; glad at calamities especially Jerusalem shall not go unpunished, confederate persecutors stir up set on pleased shall certainly called account another day God remember against them. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

8 COMMENTARY: Henry addresses daughter of Babylon proud secure: know well scriptures truth art to be destroyed, Hammond reads who art destroyer, destroyers shall be destroyed (Rev 13:10), perhaps reference man of sin head New-Testament Babylon son of perdition (2 Thess 2:3); destruction foreseen sure spoken just destruction — paid own coin served as thou served us barbarously used destroyers as we by thee (Rev 18:6), let not expect mercy who when had power did not show mercy; happy shall reward thee as thou served us, instruments fulfilling God's counsels. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

9 COMMENTARY: Henry dashes little ones: utter destruction — very little ones when taken storm all put sword dashed pieces enraged merciless conqueror, none escape if little ones perish, seed another generation cut off ruin not only total as Jerusalem's but final, sunk like millstone sea never rise; destruction reflects honour instruments — happy those do it fulfilling God's counsels, therefore calls Cyrus servant shepherd anointed (Isa 44:28; 45:1), soldiers sanctified ones (Isa 13:3), making way enlargement God's Israel happy serviceable; fall New-Testament Babylon triumph saints (Rev 19:1). — Matthew Henry (Complete)
