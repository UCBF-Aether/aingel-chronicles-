Psalm 144

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry blesses Lord strength teaches hands war fingers fight: four preceding penned before accession persecuted Saul, this afterwards still trouble no condition exempt, neighbouring nations molesting especially Philistines (2 Sam 5:17); acknowledges triumph thankfulness goodness advancing government vv1-4, prays help enemies vv5-8,11, rejoices assurance victory vv9-10, prays prosperity kingdom hopes vv12-15; prayer further mercy fitly begun thanksgiving former mercy waiting bless stir bless him; gives glory two things — what he was: Blessed Lord my rock my goodness fortress, covenant engaged depend, saints faith made theirs found not only answer but outdo expectations, matter trust made easy matter triumph made glad gloried; multiplies words satisfaction interest — strength stay power work warfare rock build shelter, weak strong Lord power might; made soldier — bred shepherd not designed parents self anything more, hands crook fingers harp God taught hands war fingers fight designed Israel champion, what God calls either finds makes fit, men war give glory military skill same teaches meanest husbandman art greatest general, pity fingers taught fight fight against him kingdom, special reason acknowledge qualified services never thought. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry goodness fortress high tower deliverer shield subdues people: goodness not only good me chief good favour felicity author goodness in me every good perfect gift; fortress high tower safe prince castle strong-hold, formerly sheltered strong-holds En-gedi natural fastnesses lately master strong-hold Zion fortified art dwelt fort (2 Sam 5:7,9) depends not these, Lord fortress high tower, divine attributes promises fortifications far exceeding nature art; deliverer emphatically deliverer to me not only interest but always nigh makes deliverances real benefit; shield guard malignant darts enemies not only fortress home shield abroad field battle, wherever believer carries protection, Fear not Abram I am thy shield; made sovereign — taught wield sceptre sword rule fight harder nobler art, subdueth people under me, providence acknowledged making people subject prince preserving order benefit societies, special hand inclining Israel subject David pursuant promise, typical great act grace bringing souls subjection Lord Jesus willing day power. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry asks Lord what is man takest knowledge makest account: admires condescension man himself — poor little thing takest knowledge makest account falls cognizance care tender regard mean worthless race had me; disgraces human nature reason admire honours God put mankind general saints especially some particular David Messiah applied (Heb 2:6) highly exalted humbled found fashion man authority execute judgment Son of man; question Ps 8:4 illustrated wonder dignity crowned glory honour (Ps 8:5), here illustrated meanness mortality notwithstanding dignity. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry man like vanity days shadow passeth: frail weak helpless compassed infirmities continuance short uncertain like vanity itself, nay vanity best estate; days little substance thoughts cares immortal soul employed poor dying body, shadow dark flitting transitory finishing sun when sets resolving all shadow, shadow passeth away no loss; David puts himself number mean despicable. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry bow heavens come down touch mountains smoke: begs strengthen success enemies invaded — not specify fear Scatter destroy, God knew meant named not; prays appear extraordinary conviction preferred dunghill-deities God Israel — Bow heavens make evident thine Lord them (Isa 66:1), providence threaten enemies look black clouds thick hang low big storm, fight fight against visibly thou for us, Touch mountains strong stately enemies smoke, show ministry angels as Mount Sinai. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry cast forth lightning scatter shoot arrows destroy: appear against enemies fight heaven lightnings arrows fiery darts hardest steel no armour proof penetrating force lightning, himself shoot arrows sure never misses mark hits aims. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry send hand above rid deliver great waters strange children: appear for him begs destruction order deliverance repose people — Send hand power above way look help, rid deliver great waters ready overflow; God's time help people sinking all other helps fail; strange children Philistines aliens bad neighbours heathens bound strange not leagues therefore carry strangely, advantages blessed arms still vexatious treacherous no confidence — mouth speaketh vanity, hand upon offer help no trusting right hand falsehood; against such cannot defend selves depend God truth justice hates falsehood defend. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

8 COMMENTARY: Henry mouth vanity right hand falsehood: same strange children as v7 — mouth vanity, right hand falsehood, treacherous no word no hand trusted, God of truth and justice defence. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

9 COMMENTARY: Henry sings new song psaltery ten strings: praises experiences goodness encouragements expect further mercy — midst complaints power treachery enemies holy exultation sing new song praise new mercies compassions new every morning, fresh favours fresh returns thanks, praise mercies hope promise as received providence (2 Chr 20:20-21); join music songs praise express excite holy joy, psaltery ten strings best manner thinking all little set forth praises. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

10 COMMENTARY: Henry giveth salvation kings delivereth David hurtful sword: new song — He giveth salvation kings, great kings cannot save themselves without him, life-guards armies means safety devised after all God gives salvation secures means could do without (Ps 33:16), kings protectors people God protector, service owe power gives salvations; good kings ministers good subjects protected saved engaged give salvation kings subjects rule him, witness great things David servant many time delivered hurtful sword Saul malice zeal service country often exposed; refer Christ Son David new song New-Testament song, God delivered hurtful sword upheld servant brought conqueror powers darkness (Isa 42:1; 49:8), gave salvation not himself only us raising horn salvation. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

11 COMMENTARY: Henry rid deliver strange children mouth vanity right hand falsehood: repeats prayer plea vv7-8 — persecutors still same character false perfidious certainly over-reach honest man too hard, therefore Lord deliver strange sort people. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

12 COMMENTARY: Henry sons plants grown youth daughters corner stones polished palace: public prosperity hopeful progeny — sons daughters all respects could wish, not only own family subjects seed next generation, comfort happiness parents see children promising likely well; sons plants grown youth olive-plants (Ps 128) planting Lord (Isa 61:3) plants not weeds thorns growing great not withered blasted healthful constitution quick capacity towardly disposition especially pious inclination likely bring fruit God day, youth growing time increasing everything good growing wiser better strong spirit; daughters corner-stones corner-pillars polished similitude palace temple, daughters families united connected mutual strength parts building corner-stones, graceful beautiful body mind polished nice curious structure, well-established stayed wisdom discretion corner-stones fastened building, faith united Christ chief corner-stone adorned graces Spirit polishing naturally rough become women professing godliness, purified consecrated God living temples think happy in them. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

13 COMMENTARY: Henry garners full manner store sheep thousands ten thousands oxen strong labour: great plenty — numerous families increase care perhaps more comfort not sufficient maintenance therefore prays growing estate growing family; store-houses well-replenished fruits products earth garners full good householder brings things new old (best new that state best kept that state), all manner stores selves friends living plentifully not luxuriously abuse plenty but cheerfully usefully, abundance thankful God generous friends charitable poor otherwise profit garners full (James 5:3); flocks greatly increase sheep bring forth thousands ten thousands folds, wealth country flocks (Prov 27:26) ours too wool staple, increase cattle blessing God acknowledged; beasts service fit oxen strong labour plough fat fleshy good working case, none made idle pray bodily health not easy pleasures but strong labour work place day else worse beasts when strong for labour. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

14 COMMENTARY: Henry no breaking in going out complaining streets: uninterrupted peace — no war breaking invaders going out deserters, let not enemies break in not occasion march out, war abundance mischiefs offensive defensive; no oppression faction no complaining streets people no cause complain government one another nor peevish complain without cause, desirable dwell quiet habitations. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

15 COMMENTARY: Henry happy people such case yea happy people God Lord: reflection prosperity desired — Happy people such case seldom never long, yea happy people whose God Lord; relation people God theirs either fountain blessings flow — happy Israelites faithfully adhere Lord God expect such case, national piety commonly national prosperity nations national capacity rewards punishments only life; or abundantly preferable enjoyments — began say most Happy people such case blessed prosper world, immediately corrects Yea rather happy people whose God Lord favour love grace tenour covenant though not abundance world's goods; all much more cannot make happy unless Lord God, if he be want loss reverse cannot make miserable. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
