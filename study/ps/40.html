Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 40

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: I waited patiently for the Lord, and he inclined to me and heard my cry: patient waiting met inclining and hearing — God's ear bends to the waiter. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: He brought me up out of a horrible pit, out of miry clay, and set my feet on a rock, and established my goings: pit and clay give way to rock and established steps — deliverance is firm footing. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: He has put a new song in my mouth, even praise to our God; many shall see it, and fear, and trust in the Lord: a new song is a witness — seen, feared, and trusted by many. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Blessed is the man who makes the Lord his trust, and respects not the proud, nor those who turn aside to lies: blessedness is trust that turns from the proud and from liars. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Many, Lord my God, are your wonderful works and your thoughts toward us; they cannot be reckoned up in order to you; if I would declare and speak of them, they are more than can be numbered: works and thoughts alike are innumerable, beyond orderly reckoning. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Sacrifice and offering you did not desire; my ears you have opened; burnt offering and sin offering you have not required: opened ears — obedient hearing — replace sacrifice as what God desires, as Hebrews expounds of Christ. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Then said I, Lo, I come; in the volume of the book it is written of me: the coming One, written in the book's volume, speaks — David's words, Christ's voice. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: I delight to do your will, O my God; yea, your law is within my heart: delight in God's will and law within the heart are the marks of the coming One. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: I have preached righteousness in the great congregation; lo, I have not refrained my lips, O Lord, you know: righteousness preached openly, lips unrefrained, in the great congregation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: I have not hidden your righteousness within my heart; I have declared your faithfulness and salvation; I have not concealed your lovingkindness and truth from the great congregation: nothing is hidden or concealed — faithfulness, salvation, lovingkindness, and truth are all declared. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: Withhold not your tender mercies from me, O Lord; let your lovingkindness and truth continually preserve me: the mercies preached to others are now prayed for himself, with preservation as their work. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: For innumerable evils have compassed me about; my iniquities have taken hold on me, so I cannot look up; they are more than the hairs of my head; therefore my heart fails me: evils and iniquities, innumerable as hairs, compass and seize him till he cannot look up. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: Be pleased, O Lord, to deliver me; O Lord, make haste to help me: pleasure in delivering and haste in helping are asked together. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: Let them be ashamed and confounded together who seek after my soul to destroy it; let them be driven backward and put to shame who wish me evil: joint shame and backward driving answer the soul-seekers. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

15 COMMENTARY: Let them be desolate for a reward of their shame who say to me, Aha, aha: desolation as the reward of shame answers the mocking 'Aha'. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

16 COMMENTARY: Let all who seek you rejoice and be glad in you; let such as love your salvation say continually, The Lord be magnified: seekers' joy and lovers' continual magnification answer the mockers. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

17 COMMENTARY: But I am poor and needy, yet the Lord thinks upon me; you are my help and deliverer; make no tarrying, O my God: poverty and need are met by God's thinking on him, and help and deliverance are asked without tarrying. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
