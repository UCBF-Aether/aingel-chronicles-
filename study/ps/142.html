Psalm 142

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry cries voice supplication: prayer substance offered when forced Saul shelter cave afterwards penned form; complaint God vv1-2 subtlety strength malice enemies vv3,6 coldness indifference friends v4, comfort God knew case v3 refuge v5, expectation hear deliver vv6-7, expectation righteous join praises v7; troubled mind body estate singing some measure David spirit warrant complaints fetch comforts; whether Adullam or Engedi not material plain distress, disgrace great soldier courtier shifts safety terror hotly pursued every moment expectation death yet presence mind pray, wherever still religion about him, prayers tears weapons, durst not stretch hands prince lifted God; no cave deep dark but out send prayers souls prayer God; Maschil instruction lessons learnt cave knees desired teach others; complained God danger over not ashamed own fright application God, no men first rank diminution disparagement affliction cry God cry children parents frightens; poured complaint free full copious particular, heart full grievances could hold made easy pouring out Lord great fervency cried voice voice mind some think hidden cave durst not audible lest betray, mental prayer vocal God hears groanings cannot dare uttered (Rom 8:26). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry pours complaint shows trouble: two things laid open — distress exhibited remonstrance memorial case showed trouble circumstances, did not prescribe God nor show as if God did not know without showing but confidence desired communion willing refer entirely unbosomed humbly laid matter then cheerfully left; apt show trouble too much selves aggravating poring no service whereas showing God cast care him careth ease selves; nor allow complaint selves others cannot due decency sincerity devotion make God stand before him; desire — complaint made supplication not claiming relief debt humbly begging favour, complainants must be suppliants God will be sought unto. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry spirit overwhelmed thou knewest path snare privily: complained way walked suspecting no danger privily laid snare entrap, Saul gave Michal daughter purpose snare (1 Sam 18:21), complains everything design against him; gone out way met snares thanked self, met way duty humble boldness tell God; comforted midst complaints — spirit overwhelmed ready sink burden grief fear quite at loss ready despair then thou knewest path pleasure think thou knewest, knewest sincerity right path walked not such persecutors represent, knewest condition particulars spirit overwhelmed could not distinctly show comforted thou knewest (Job 23:10), knewest protect preserve secure (Ps 31:7; Deut 2:7). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry looks right hand no man know refuge failed none cared soul: disowned deserted friends — favour court seemed great interest, made out-law dangerous harbour witness Ahimelech fate then no man know everybody shy; looked right hand advocate (Ps 109:31) friend speak good word, since Jonathan appearing like cost life nobody willing venture defence innocency all ready say knew nothing matter; looked round any open doors refuge failed, none old friends night lodging direct place secresy safety; many good men deceived swallow-friends gone winter; David life exceedingly precious yet unjustly proscribed no man cared nor move hand protection; type Christ sufferings forsaken all men own disciples trod wine-press alone none help uphold (Isa 63:5). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry cries Thou art refuge portion land living: lovers friends stood aloof vain call — cried thee O Lord knowest carest when none else wilt not fail forsake when men do, God constant love; said God cave Thou art refuge portion land living depend be so refuge save miserable portion make happy; cave poor refuge Lord name strong tower run into, refuge alone think safe; crown hopes poor portion never think well provided till Lord portion inheritance cup; sincerity take Lord God find all-sufficient refuge portion, no evil hurt no good wanting, humbly claim interest Lord refuge portion everything else refuge lies portion no value, so land living while live being whether world or better, enough God answer necessities present time, world dangers wants what danger fear God refuge what wants portion, heaven alone deserves land living believers both refuge portion. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry attends cry brought very low deliver persecutors stronger: satisfaction addressed God — Lord give gracious ear cry affliction supplication brought very low if help not quite sunk; deliver persecutors either tie hands turn hearts break power blast projects restrain rescue, stronger than I honour take part weakest; deliver or ruined not yet match. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry bring soul prison praise name righteous compass dealt bountifully: bring soul prison not only safe cave but all perplexities, spiritually souls good men straitened doubts fears cramped fettered weakness faith prevalency corruption duty interest apply God beg set liberty enlarge hearts run way commandments; much expected deliverance redound glory God — own thanksgivings present complaints turned, bring soul prison not enjoy self friends live ease nor secure country but praise thy name, eye prayers deliverance trouble occasion praise God live praise, greatest comfort temporal mercies furnish matter opportunity excellent duty praise; thanksgivings many behalf (2 Cor 1:11) — enlarged righteous encompass about cause make thee crown praise Chaldee, flock congratulate deliverance hear experiences receive Maschil instructions, encompass join thanksgivings because dealt bountifully; mercies others matter praises God, praises others behalf desired rejoiced. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
