Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 53

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The fool has said in his heart, There is no God; corrupt are they, and have done abominable iniquity; none does good: Psalm 14 is repeated here with small changes, for the lesson of universal corruption bears repeating. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: God looked down from heaven on the children of men, to see if any understood, that did seek God: the divine search from heaven is repeated — understanding and seeking are the marks sought. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Every one is gone back; they are altogether become filthy; none does good, no, not one: going back and altogether filthy — the verdict is total, without exception. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Have workers of iniquity no knowledge, who eat up my people as bread, and have not called upon God? Eating God's people as bread and not calling on God — oppression and prayerlessness together prove ignorance. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: There they were in great fear, where no fear was, for God has scattered the bones of him who encamps against you; you have put them to shame, because God has despised them: fear where no fear was seizes persecutors, for God scatters the besieger's bones and despises him. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Oh that the salvation of Israel were come out of Zion! When God brings back his people's captivity, Jacob shall rejoice and Israel be glad: the repeated longing closes the repeated psalm — salvation from Zion, captivity reversed, Jacob rejoicing. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
