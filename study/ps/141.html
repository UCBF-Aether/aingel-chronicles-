Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 141

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry cries make haste: David distress pursued most likely Saul violent man — is any distressed let him pray, David did and had comfort; prays favourable acceptance vv1-2, powerful assistance vv3-4, others instrumental good soul as hoped be to souls others vv5-6, he and friends last extremity God appear relief rescue vv7-10; mercy grace necessary as him therefore humbly earnest singing; those know value God's gracious presence more fervent prayers. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry sets prayer as incense hands evening sacrifice: presented through sacrifice intercession Saviour acceptable God as daily sacrifices burnings incense old; prayer spiritual sacrifice offering soul best affections. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry sets watch mouth keeps door lips: good men know evil tongue sins, enemies provoking danger speaking unadvisedly; live evil world evil hearts need pray neither drawn nor driven do anything sinful. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry inclines not heart evil thing practise wicked works eat dainties: mercy accept well grace keep ill two things taught pray; sinners pretend find dainties sin, consider how soon sin turn bitterness dread dainties pray God take out sight grace turn hearts against, good men pray against sweets sin. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry welcomes righteous smiting as kindness excellent oil not break head: desires told faults — enemies reproached false could not but complain, yet desired friends reprove really amiss particularly anything gave least colour reproaches; righteous God reading welcome rebukes providence tokens love means grace pray instruments trouble; commonly reproofs righteous men best becomes righteous reprove unrighteousness others from them best taken, if reproof just though reprover not so make good use learn obedience; taught receive reproofs — desire reproved whatever amiss done amiss, Lord put heart righteous smite reprove, if own heart not smite as ought let friend, never fall dreadful judgment let alone sin; account friendship not only bear patiently take kindness, reproofs instruction way life (Prov 6:23) means good repentance sins prevent relapses, though cut order cure much more desirable kisses enemy (Prov 27:6) song fools (Eccl 7:5), David blessed God Abigail seasonable admonition (1 Sam 25:32); reckon helped healed excellent oil wound mollify close, shall not break head as some reckon could as well bear heads broken told faults, David not mind sin broken head broken bones (Ps 51:8), reproof excellent oil cure bruises sin given, shall not break head if help break heart; requite kindness at least prayers calamities show take kindly; Hammond another reading reproach bruise righteous rebuke poisonous oil shall not break head destroy mischief intended, yet prayer in mischiefs God preserve prayer not vain. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry hopes judges overthrown stony places hear sweet words: persecutors some time bear told faults as willing told his — judges Saul officers judged condemned David themselves sole judges overthrown stony places rocks wilderness then hear words sweet; refers relentings Saul breast tears Is this thy voice my son David (1 Sam 24:16; 26:21); generally judges great may come overthrown, greatest figure not always level smooth ways; slighted word God before relish glad affliction opens ear instruction, world bitter word sweet; oppressed innocency cannot gain hearing pomp pleasure, when overthrown themselves more compassionate thoughts afflicted. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry bones scattered grave's mouth: complains extremity friends reduced — bones scattered grave's mouth out thrown up so long dead, or into ready thrown near pit, little regarded chips hewers wood thrown neglected heaps; as one cuts cleaves earth ploughman tears earth pieces plough-share (Ps 129:3); can dry bones live? — Matthew Henry (Complete)

8 COMMENTARY: Henry eyes unto thee leave not soul destitute: casts upon God depends deliverance — eyes unto thee case ever so deplorable thou canst redress grievances, expect relief bad things trust; those eye towards God hopes him; comfort — leave not soul desolate destitute still see where help is. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

9 COMMENTARY: Henry keeps from snares laid gins workers iniquity: prevent designs enemies — keep taken snare laid discover evade, gin placed ever subtlety God can will secure people taken. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

10 COMMENTARY: Henry wicked fall own nets escape altogether: justice turn designs enemies themselves mercy deliver ruined — let wicked fall own net intentionally procured me meritoriously prepared themselves, no law more just architects destruction perish own contrivances, bound over God's justice held cords own iniquity; at same time obtain discharge, entangling ensnaring wicked sometimes prove escape enlargement righteous. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
