Psalm 139

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry meditates omniscience — Jewish doctors call most excellent of David's psalms, pious devout meditation doctrine God's omniscience hearts fixed filled singing; asserted laid down vv1-6, confirmed two arguments everywhere present therefore knows all vv7-12, made us therefore knows us vv13-16, inferences pleasing admiration vv17-18, holy dread detestation sin sinners vv19-22, holy satisfaction integrity appeal vv23-24; truth God knows hearts all children men mixed faith seriously considered applied great influence holiness comfort; doctrine addressed to God acknowledging giving glory — truths look fully well prayed over as preached over much better disputed over, speak God to himself concerned utmost sincerity reverence impressions deeper; applied himself not Thou hast known all but Thou hast known me most concerned believe most profitable consider, know for good when know for selves (Job 5:27), acknowledge Lord all souls thine add My soul thine, thou hatest all sin hatest my sin, good to all good Israel good to me; searched known thoroughly as diligently exactly searched, David king hearts kings unsearchable subjects (Prov 25:3) not so Sovereign. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry knows downsitting uprising thought afar: motions — down-sitting rest up-rising work, temper mind compose sit down stir up rise, soul reposes stay support aims reaches felicity end, know when come home walk before house when go abroad errands; imaginations — nothing close quick thought always unknown others often unobserved ourselves yet understandest thought afar off, thoughts ever foreign distant understand chain connexion many slip notice myself cannot, or understand afar even before think them long after thought forgotten, or from afar height heaven seest depths heart (Ps 33:14). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry compasses path lying down acquainted ways: designs undertakings — compassest every particular path, siftest winnowest path thoroughly distinguish good evil as sifting separate corn chaff, actions ventilated judgment God (Ps 17:3), notice every step right step by-step, acquainted intimately knows rule walk by end walk towards company walk with; retirements — lying down withdrawn company reflecting passed day composing rest knowest heart thought go to bed. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry knows every word: not word tongue not vain nor good but knowest altogether, knowest meant from what thought came with what design uttered; not word tongue's end ready spoken yet checked kept in but knowest; when not word tongue O Lord thou knowest all, thoughts are words to God. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry besets behind before lays hand: knowest every part — beset behind before go which way under eye cannot escape, laid hand cannot run away; wherever under eye hand God, perhaps allusion physician laying hand patient feel pulse temper, God knows as know not only see but feel hands upon, all saints in his hand. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry wonders knowledge too wonderful high cannot attain: such knowledge me as have not myself nor can have, cannot take notice all own thoughts nor make judgment myself as thou makest; knowledge cannot comprehend much less describe, sure knowest all things but how cannot tell, cannot by searching find how God searches finds us nor know how known. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry asks whither flee from Spirit presence: great use know certainty instructed not only believe but tell why believe give reason hope; sure God perfectly knows ways because always under eye — omnipresent must needs omniscient, infinite immensity ubiquity presence, heaven earth whole creation Creator fills both (Jer 23:24) not only knows governs but fills, every part creation under intuition influence, acknowledges application sees himself open; no flight remove presence — whither go from Spirit spiritual presence thyself Spirit, folly think because cannot see him he cannot see us; not desired go away desired nothing more near him, only puts case suppose foolish think getting out sight shake off awe, revolting obedience disowning dependence shifting self, alas whither? Heathen Seneca whithersoever turnest see God meeting thee. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

8 COMMENTARY: Henry ascends heaven makes bed hell: remote places meeting God — heaven vast large innumerable company yet no escaping eye corner crowd, inhabitants that world necessary dependence open strict scrutiny as this; hell Sheol depth earth centre dig deep hide mistaken God knows path vulture eye never saw earth all surface, or state dead removed sight living yet not sight living God eye cannot hide grave, or place damned make bed hell uncomfortable no rest day night yet thousands make bed for ever flames, behold thou there power justice, God's wrath fire burn everlastingly (Rev 14:10). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

9 COMMENTARY: Henry takes wings morning dwells uttermost sea: remotest corners — wings morning rays morning-light wings sun (Mal 4:2) nothing swift flee uttermost parts sea earth (Job 38:12-13), most distant obscure islands ultima Thule Terra incognita find thee there, hand lead as far go right hand hold can go no further cannot out of reach; God soon arrested Jonah fled Tarshish presence Lord. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

10 COMMENTARY: Henry hand leads right hand holds: same remotest flight as v9 — hand leads wherever go, right hand holds cannot go further, presence as guide and restraint not merely witness. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

11 COMMENTARY: Henry darkness shall cover: no veil hide eye not thickest darkness — if say Yet darkness cover when nothing else, deceived curtains evening stand no more stead than wings morning, even night light about me; that favours escape pursued criminal retreat beaten army do no kindness fleeing; when God divided light darkness reservation prerogative to himself darkness light both alike. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

12 COMMENTARY: Henry darkness light alike: darkness darkeneth not from thee, no darkness shadow death where workers iniquity hide themselves; no hypocritical mask disguise specious save person action appearing true light God; secret haunts sin as open before God as most open barefaced villanies. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

13 COMMENTARY: Henry possessed reins covered womb: work of hands — framed engine knows motions, made us therefore knows, saw forming can hidden now formed? Possessed reins Master most secret thoughts intentions innermost recesses soul not only knowest governest as possess, possession rightful coveredst mother womb madest (Job 10:11) madest secret; soul concealed all about, who knows things man save spirit man (1 Cor 2:11), hidden man heart, God himself covered us therefore can when pleases discover, hid from world did not intend hide himself. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

14 COMMENTARY: Henry praises fearfully wonderfully made: glory given God entirely — he made not we ourselves, praise author being parents only instruments; done divine inspection substance hid womb nay yet in fieri forming unshapen embryo not hidden eyes did see substance, divine operation eye saw hand wrought work, divine model book all members written eternal wisdom plan almighty power noble structure; great marvel miracle but ordinary course nature fearfully wonderfully made astonished admirable contrivance living temples composition every part harmony together; great mystery soul knows right well marvellous but how describe know not, made secret curiously wrought womb lowest parts earth privately far out sight; great mercy members continuance fashioned according written book wise counsel when yet none, or none left out, if member wanting book wanting bodies through goodness all limbs sense want might burdens selves, reason praise creation conclude saw substance unfashioned sees now fashioned. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

15 COMMENTARY: Henry made secret curiously wrought lowest earth: same formation mystery as v14 — secret private out of sight, curiously wrought as lowest parts earth, soul knows marvellous yet cannot describe, divine inspection and operation in hidden forming. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

16 COMMENTARY: Henry eyes saw substance book members days fashioned: eyes did see substance yet unformed, book all members written eternal wisdom plan, days fashioned continuance when yet none — or none left out — mercy all members present, God who saw unfashioned sees fashioned. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

17 COMMENTARY: Henry precious thoughts: acknowledges wonder thankfulness care taken all days — God who knew thought of him thoughts love good not evil (Jer 29:11), omniscience justly watched do hurt employed for us watched do good (Jer 31:28); counsels welfare precious admiration deep themselves cannot fathomed comprehended, providence vast reach dispensations brought about good beyond contrivance foresight, dear reverence yet pleasure thankfulness, thoughts concerning God delightful above any other. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

18 COMMENTARY: Henry counts thoughts more than sand wakes still with thee: numerous admiration — cannot conceive how many kind counsels concerning us how many good turns variety mercies received, count heads much more particulars more number sand yet every one great considerable (Ps 40:5), cannot conceive multitude compassions all new every morning; constant all times — when awake every morning still with thee under eye care safe easy protection, continual devout sense eye God — when awake with thee thoughts, help keep fear Lord all day if awake first thoughts him set before us. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

19 COMMENTARY: Henry slays wicked: concludes doctrine ruin certainly end sinners — God knows all wickedness wicked therefore reckon, Surely thou wilt slay wicked O God all wickedness open before thee however artfully disguised coloured hide eye world, however suffer prosper while surely slay at last. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

20 COMMENTARY: Henry speaks against thee wickedly enemies take name vain: reason punish daringly affront set defiance — speak against thee wickedly set mouth heavens (Ps 73:9) called account hard speeches spoken against him (Jude 15), enemies declare enmity taking name vain as show contempt man make by-word name never mention but jest banter, profane sacred forms swearing praying impertinent irreverent manner take name vain thereby enemies; some description hypocrites — speak thee mischief talk God pretending piety ill design cloak maliciousness, enemies pretend friendship take name vain swear falsely. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

21 COMMENTARY: Henry hates haters grieved risers: use prospect ruin wicked — defies Depart bloody men shall not debauch will not admit friendship fellowship cannot destroy under God's protection force depart; detests — Lord knowest heart witness do not I hate those hate thee for that reason because hate thee? Hate because love thee hate affronts indignities blessed name, grieved rise against thee grieved rebellion foresee ruin certainly end; sin hated sinners lamented all fear God. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

22 COMMENTARY: Henry perfect hatred enemies: hate work them turn aside (Ps 101:3) sincere perfect hatred, count enemies God enemies me will not intimacy (Ps 69:8). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

23 COMMENTARY: Henry search know heart try thoughts: appeals sincerity — desires as far wrong God discover him; upright take comfort omniscience witness uprightness humble confidence beg search try discover selves (good man desires know worst himself) discover others; means honestly could wish window breast any man look heart — Lord hope not wicked way but see if any wicked way remaining corrupt inclination, let me see root out do not allow. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

24 COMMENTARY: Henry leads everlasting way: desires as far right forwarded — he knows heart knows how do effectually Lead me way everlasting; way godliness everlasting way everlastingly true good pleasing God profitable us end everlasting life, way antiquity good old way; saints desire kept led way not miss turn out tire in it. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
