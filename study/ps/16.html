Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 16

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Preserve me, O God, for in you I put my trust: the prayer is short because trust itself is the argument. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: David says to the Lord, You are my Lord; my goodness extends not to you: God gains nothing from our good deeds, which is why verse 3 turns to the saints. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: His delight is in the saints on earth, the excellent ones: goodness given to God is shown in love for his people. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Sorrows shall be multiplied for those who hasten after another god; their drink offerings of blood he will not offer, nor take their names on his lips: idolatry brings multiplied grief, and the godly refuse even its rituals and names. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: The Lord is the portion of his inheritance and of his cup, and maintains his lot: God himself, not land or wealth, is David's share. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: The lines fall to him in pleasant places, and he has a goodly heritage: the lot God measures out is good, even when it is not the world's lot. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: He blesses the Lord who gives him counsel, and his reins instruct him in the night: God's guidance continues in quiet, sleepless hours. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: He sets the Lord always before him, and because God is at his right hand he shall not be moved: constant awareness of God's presence is the secret of steadfastness. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: Therefore his heart is glad, his glory rejoices, and his flesh rests in hope: joy reaches body as well as soul, because hope is sure. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: God will not leave his soul in hell, nor suffer his Holy One to see corruption: Peter and Paul apply this to Christ's resurrection, of which David's deliverance was a shadow. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: God will show him the path of life; in his presence is fullness of joy, and at his right hand pleasures for evermore: the end of trust is not mere survival but endless joy in God's presence. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
