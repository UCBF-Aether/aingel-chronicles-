Psalm 140

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry prays deliverance from evil man violent man: character enemies dangerous wicked, ring-leader evil man man of violences probably Saul, Chaldee names Doeg Ahithophel distance time, violent evil; David type Christ suffered before reigned humbled before exalted, many loved valued sought honour many hated envied sought mischief; penned persecuted Saul, one prayer cave probably rest same time; complains malice prays preserve vv1-5, encourages God vv6-7, prays prophesies destruction persecutors vv8-11, assures afflicted troubles end well vv12-13 comfort singing; more malice enemies more earnest prayer protection, believers security holy serenity safe whom God preserves, if he for us who against us; keep from swallowed — not prevail take life reputation interest comfort prevent coming throne, keep from doing as they do or would have do or promise selves shall do. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry imagines mischiefs heart continually war: subtle crafty mischief — imagined laid scheme art cunning, purposed plotted overthrow goings good man draw sin trouble ruin blasting reputation crushing interest taking life; confederate gathered together war — agree nothing else agree persecute good man, Herod Pilate unite resemble Satan not divided himself devils agreeing Beelzebub; spiteful see v3, proud see v5. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry sharpens tongues serpent adders poison lips: spiteful full malice Satan himself — sharpened tongues serpent infuses venom tongue, malignity all say think nothing under lips but adders poison; calumnies counsels aimed destroy David secretly as stung serpent snake grass, endeavoured infuse malice others make seven times children hell; malignant tongue makes like old serpent, poison lips certain sign poison heart. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry preserves from hands wicked violent overthrow goings: same prayer as v1 — deliver preserve keep, violent men evil men, plotted overthrow goings draw sin trouble ruin reputation interest life, hunters hidden snare spread net set gins designs undiscovered likely take effect fall hands ere aware; great persecutors great politicians formidable, Lord preserves simple without arts. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry proud hid snare cords net gins way: proud conceited confident success resemble Satan reigning ruining sin pride; pride persecutors present terror yet encouragement persecuted, more haughty faster ripening ruin, pride goes before destruction; hunters snare net gins as v4 — hidden that undiscovered take effect. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry claims Thou art my God hear supplications: triumphs God thereby persecutors — enemies sharpened tongues did he sharpen his? No adders poison under lips grace poured into lips witness said unto Lord, to him looked directed danger malice, well have God go to; comforted interest God — said Thou art my God if my God shield mighty protector, troublous dangerous times good claim relation faith keep hold; access God — not only covenant but communion leave speak expect answer peace humble confidence Hear voice supplications O Lord. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry covers head day battle: assurance help happiness — O God Lord Jehovah Adonai, Jehovah self-existent self-sufficient infinitely perfect, Adonai stay support ruler governor therefore strength salvation strong Saviour nay salvation itself from whom in whom salvation, not only strong Saviour very strength salvation stress hope laid all in all make happy preserve happiness; experience formerly care — covered head day battle, pleaded Saul service country many time jeoparded life high places field, pleads God wonderfully protected provided better helmet securing head than Goliath's, kept day battle Philistines suffer not fall treacherous intrigues false-hearted Israelites; God able preserve secret fraud as open force, experience power care dangers one kind encourage trust depend dangers another, nothing shorten Lord's right hand. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

8 COMMENTARY: Henry grants not desires wicked further not device lest exalt: believing foresight shame confusion persecutors — disappointment prayed lusts not gratified lust ambition envy revenge, Grant not desires wicked frustrate let not see ruin interest earnestly wish, hear voice supplications; projects not take effect blasted — further not wicked device let not Providence favour designs cross them, suffer not proceed chain wheels stop career pursuits; pray enemies God's people not succeed enterprises, David prayer Ahithophel turn counsels foolishness; plea lest exalt value success evidence God favoured, proud prosper made prouder impudent God insolent people therefore Lord do not prosper them. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

9 COMMENTARY: Henry mischief lips cover heads compassers: destruction prayed or prophecy original bears, prayer spirit prophecy all one; foretels ruin own enemies — mischief own lips cover heads evil wished come themselves curses blown back faces designs laid turn own ruin (Ps 7:15-16); makers mischief slandering tale-bearing misrepresenting neighbours spreading ill-natured characters stories dread consequence think sad condition when mischief accessory made return themselves. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

10 COMMENTARY: Henry burning coals fall cast fire deep pits rise not: judgments God fall burning coals allusion destruction Sodom, deluge waters above beneath met drowning world windows heaven opened fountains great deep broken, complete ruin enemies Christ kingdom not only burning coals cast above (Job 20:23; 27:22) but themselves cast fire beneath heaven hell wrath God Judge rage Satan tormentor concur miserable; fire not furnace perhaps escape but deep pit cannot rise, Tophet deep large (Isa 30:33). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

11 COMMENTARY: Henry evil speaker not established earth evil hunt violent overthrow: all others like them — evil speakers shaken never established earth, got fraud falsehood calumny unjust accusation will not prosper last, wealth vanity diminished, let not Doeg think reign long doom theirs (Ps 2:5), lying tongue moment lip truth established for ever; evil doers destroyed — evil hunt violent man blood-hound hunts murderer discover, lion hunts prey tear pieces, mischievous brought light brought ruin destruction appointed run down overthrow, evil pursues sinners. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

12 COMMENTARY: Henry knows Lord maintain cause afflicted judgment poor: foresight deliverance comfort persecuted — God do justice delivering wronged commit themselves, know Lord maintain just injured cause afflicted people not suffer might always prevail right though right poor little pretend right to; God patron oppressed innocence much more persecuted piety, those know him cannot but know this. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

13 COMMENTARY: Henry righteous give thanks name upright dwell presence: they do him justice ascribing glory deliverance — surely righteous make conscience rendering God due as men theirs shall give thanks name when find cause pleaded jealousy prosecuted effect; closing upright dwell presence denotes favour — admit dwell presence grace here glory hereafter safety happiness, and duty — attend as servants keep presence masters both honour receive commands; true thanksgiving even thanksliving, use all deliverance serve God more closely cheerfully. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
