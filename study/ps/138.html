Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 138

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry praises with whole heart, before gods sing praise: sincerity zeal — with heart whole heart within and all within, uprightness intention fervency affection inward impressions agreeing outward expressions; freedom boldness before princes judges great men either other nations visited or own nation attended, even in presence, not only heart pious ejaculations any company but sing praise if occasion; praising God work greatest men need not ashamed, work angels work heaven; before angels some understand religious assemblies special presence angels (1 Cor 11:10); looks back thankfulness experiences goodness (vv1-3), forward comfort others praise like him and God go on do good (vv6-8). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry worships towards holy temple: way God appointed — priests alone went into temple, people nearest but worship towards it, might do at distance; Christ our temple towards him look eye faith Mediator between us and God in all praises; heaven God's holy temple thitherward lift eyes all addresses, Our Father in heaven. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry praises lovingkindness and truth: fountain comforts — goodness promise, mercy hidden thee mercy revealed thee, God gracious himself engaged so all trust him; magnified word promise truth above all name — made known many ways creation providence most clearly word, judgments mouth magnified even above hand, greater things done by them, wonders grace exceed wonders nature, discovered revelation much greater discovered reason; in what done for David faithfulness work appeared illustriously redounded glory more than any attribute; some interpreters Christ essential Word gospel magnified above discoveries fathers, magnified law made honourable magnifies gospel much more. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry cries and is answered: streams from fountain tasted Lord gracious — affliction remembers thankfulness sweet communion then had with God, cried prayed earnestly God answered gave understand prayer accepted should have gracious return due time; intercourse God saints carried promises prayers; sweet communications — strengthenedst me strength soul, answer prayer God gives more than good words (Ps 20:6); speedy answer day when cried — trade heaven prayer grow rich quick returns, while yet speaking God hears (Isa 65:24); spiritual answer strength soul real valuable answer prayer faith day affliction, if give strength bear burdens resist temptations do duties afflicted state, strengthen keep hold himself faith maintain peace minds wait patience issue, own answered bound thankful. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry hears kings sing: influence praising hoped upon others — David king hoped kings wrought upon experiences example embrace religion, kings religious kingdoms every way better; reference kings neighbours David Hiram others — shall all praise thee, visited David after death sought Solomon (all kings earth expressly 2 Chr 9:23) readily joined worship God Israel; look further calling Gentiles discipling nations gospel Christ all kings fall down (Ps 72:11); foretold kings earth hear words God — all came near David hear from him (Ps 119:46), latter days preachers gospel sent all world; then praise God as all reason hear word receive light love (Acts 13:48); sing in ways Lord — ways providence grace towards them, rejoice God give glory however pleased deal ways duty obedience; walk ways Lord reason sing ways go on cheerfulness ways pleasantness becomes pleasant in them, if so great glory Lord, honour God kings walk ways all walk sing proclaim good Master work own wages. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry respects lowly, knows proud afar: favour humble people — though Lord high neither needs creatures nor benefited, yet respect lowly smiles well pleased, overlooks heaven earth cast gracious look (Isa 57:15; 66:1), sooner later put honour upon them, while knows proud afar off knows but disowns rejects how proudly pretend favour; Hammond sum gospel kings hear welcome — penitent sinners accepted God impenitent cast out, witness Pharisee publican (Luke 18:9-14). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry walks midst trouble revived: care afflicted oppressed — David great good expects walk midst trouble encourages hope God comfort — spirit ready sink fail thou shalt revive make easy cheerful troubles, divine consolations enough revive even walk midst troubles ready die away fear; protect plead cause — stretch forth hand though not against enemies destroy yet against wrath enemies restrain set bounds; due time deliverance — right hand shall save, one hand stretch against enemies another save own people, Christ right hand Lord save all serve him. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

8 COMMENTARY: Henry perfects that concerning me: assurance whatever good work begun in and for people he will perform — most needful, knows best what so, careful cumbered many things not concern us but knows things really consequence (Matt 6:32) orders best; most concerned about — duty to God happiness in God former faithfully done latter effectually secured, if hearts most upon solicitous good work begun he begun will perfect confident (Phil 1:6); ground confidence mercy Lord endures for ever — made much matter praise (Ps 13:6) therefore more assurance matter hope, give God glory mercy take comfort, hopes persevere founded not own strength will fail but mercy God will not fail, well pleaded Lord mercy endures for ever let me for ever monument; use confidence not supersede but quicken prayer turns expectation petition — Forsake not do not let go work own hands, Lord I am work own hands soul so do not forsake, concerns so do not lay by care; whatever good in us work God's hands works will and do, fail if forsake, glory Jehovah perfecting God concerned progress end may faith pray Lord do not forsake, whom loves loves end, as for God work perfect. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
