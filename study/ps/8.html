Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 8

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The psalm opens in adoration: the Lord's name is excellent in all the earth, and his glory is set above the heavens. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Out of babes and sucklings God ordains strength, stilling enemy and avenger: weak praise confounds proud opposition, as Christ himself applied this verse. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Considering the heavens, the work of God's fingers, moon and stars ordained by him, David is struck by the order and vastness of creation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: What is man, that God is mindful of him, and the son of man, that he visits him? Human smallness makes divine attention wonderful. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Man is made a little lower than the angels and crowned with glory and honour: dignity is God's gift, not man's achievement. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Dominion over the works of God's hands is given to man, with all things under his feet: creation is entrusted, not abandoned, to human rule. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Sheep and oxen and beasts of the field are named as the subjects of that dominion, the familiar creatures of daily life. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Fowl of the air, fish of the sea, and all that pass through the paths of the seas complete the picture: no realm of creation lies outside man's stewardship, and the psalm's fullest sense, as Hebrews shows, is in Christ, the true Son of man. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: The psalm closes as it opened, with the excellent name of the Lord in all the earth: wonder at creation returns to worship of the Creator. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
