Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 56

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Be merciful to me, O God, for man would swallow me up; he fighting daily oppresses me: mercy is pleaded against daily, swallowing oppression — the title sets this in Gath, among the Philistines. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: My enemies would daily swallow me up, for they are many who fight against me, O most High: daily swallowing by many fighters, addressed to the Most High above them all. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: What time I am afraid, I will trust in you: fear's time is trust's time — the verse names the exact moment fear is to turn to trust. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: In God I will praise his word; in God I have put my trust; I will not fear what flesh can do to me: praised word, placed trust, and fearlessness before flesh — the threefold answer to verse 1. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Every day they wrest my words; all their thoughts are against me for evil: daily wresting of words, with all thoughts against him for evil — speech itself is twisted into a weapon. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: They gather themselves together, they hide themselves, they mark my steps, when they wait for my soul: gathering, hiding, marking steps, waiting for his soul — the conspiracy is patient and secret. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Shall they escape by iniquity? In your anger cast down the people, O God: escape by iniquity is questioned, and casting down in anger is prayed — iniquity is no escape route. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: You tell my wanderings; put my tears into your bottle; are they not in your book? Wanderings numbered, tears bottled, all written in God's book — no suffering of his is unrecorded. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: When I cry to you, then shall my enemies turn back; this I know, for God is for me: crying turns enemies back, and the knowledge rests on one fact — God is for me. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: In God will I praise his word; in the Lord will I praise his word: the praise of God's word is repeated, now in both divine names, God and Lord. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: In God I have put my trust; I will not be afraid what man can do to me: trust repeated, fearlessness repeated — the refrain steadies the psalm. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: Your vows are upon me, O God; I will render praises to you: vows upon him bind him to render praises — deliverance will be paid in praise. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: For you have delivered my soul from death; will you not deliver my feet from falling, that I may walk before God in the light of the living? Soul delivered from death argues feet delivered from falling, that he may walk before God in the light of the living. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
