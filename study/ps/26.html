Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 26

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Judge me, O Lord, for I have walked in my integrity, and trusted in the Lord, therefore I shall not slide: he asks for judgment on his walk, with trust as its ground and steadfastness as its fruit. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Examine me, Lord, and prove me; try my reins and my heart: the inward parts — affections and thoughts — are offered for God's searching. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: For your lovingkindness is before my eyes, and I have walked in your truth: God's mercy kept in view produces a walk in truth. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: I have not sat with vain persons, nor will I go in with dissemblers: company is a moral choice, and he refuses both the empty and the hypocritical. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: I have hated the congregation of evildoers, and will not sit with the wicked: hatred of the assembly, not of persons as such, keeps him from their seat. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: I will wash my hands in innocency, and so compass your altar, O Lord: cleansed hands are the preparation for approaching God's altar in worship. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: That I may publish with the voice of thanksgiving and tell of all your wondrous works: worship at the altar issues in public thanksgiving and testimony. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Lord, I have loved the habitation of your house, and the place where your honour dwells: love for God's house is love for the place of his manifested glory. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: Gather not my soul with sinners, nor my life with bloody men: he prays not to share the sinners' end, as he has not shared their ways. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: In whose hands is mischief, and their right hand full of bribes: the sinners are described by bribery and scheming hands — corruption that perverts justice. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: But as for me, I will walk in my integrity; redeem me and be merciful to me: his resolve is joined to a plea for redemption and mercy, not to self-righteousness. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: My foot stands in an even place; in the congregations I will bless the Lord: firm footing now becomes public blessing — the even place leads to the congregation's praise. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
