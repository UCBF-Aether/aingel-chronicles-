Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 145

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry David psalm praise particular pleasure sung often companion: five foregoing all prayers, this and five follow all praises, though only this entitled David psalm no reason think not all his as foregoing prayers; after five prayer follow six praise, much prayer not want matter praise sped prayer abound praise, thanksgivings mercy received exceed supplications pursuit; last begging promised praise (144:9) performs; book concludes praise all praise conclusion whole matter centre, people end life abound praise nearer heaven accustom work heaven; alphabetical as Ps 25 34 easily committed memory kept mind, Jewish writers extol star first magnitude bright constellation extravagant saying sing constantly three times day certainly happy world come; entitling David psalm praise intimate not only penman took particular pleasure sung often companion wherever went; engages himself others praise vv1-2,4-7,10-12, fastens proper matter greatness v3 goodness vv8-9 proofs administration kingdom v13 providence vv14-16 grace vv17-20 concludes resolution continue v21 hearts filled fixed singing. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry every day bless name for ever ever: excites engages heart enlarged, does will do more satisfaction, duty delight; work — extol bless name speak well made known express high thoughts raise like others, speak honourably graciously interpreted accepted extolling; repetition fervency affection fixedness purpose frequency performances; speak honour v5 declare greatness v6, glory not only solemn devotions common conversation, heart full God abundance mouth speak reverence praise all occasions, subject discourse noble copious pleasant useful unexceptionable glory God; resolution persevere — constant every day bless, praising daily work no day ever busy sorrowful without praising, reckon most needful daily employments most delightful daily comforts, God every day blessing doing well reason every day blessing speaking well; continue for ever ever — resolve continue end life throughout ever world, psalms penned made use praising church end time (2 Chr 29:30), hoped praising all eternity other world, make praise constant work earth everlasting bliss heaven. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry great Lord greatly praised greatness unsearchable: declare Great Lord presence infinite power irresistible brightness insupportable majesty awful dominion boundless sovereignty incontestable no dispute great greatly praised all within utmost power circumstances solemnity imaginable; greatness cannot comprehended unsearchable who conceive express great, so much more praised, cannot searching find bottom sit brink adore depth (Rom 11:33). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry one generation praise works another declare mighty acts: others forward — concur now join declare greatness men shall speak v6 abundantly utter v7 pour out gracious fluency better curious oratory, David zeal provoke many has done; keep up gone uninterrupted succession going off tell rising up seen days heard fathers fully particularly declare mighty acts (Ps 78:3), rising follow example going off death worshippers no diminution worship new generation rise room carry good work more less end time left world no succession generations. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry speak glorious honour majesty wondrous works: majesty glorious upper world above heavens set glory declaring greatness must speak glorious honour majesty splendour glory majesty brightly shines upper world dazzle eyes angels cover faces unable bear lustre; works wondrous lower world preservation maintenance government creatures proclaim Creator great, declare greatness observe unquestionable proofs declare mighty acts v4 speak wondrous works, various instruments all events God supreme director performs all things. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry men speak might terrible acts declare greatness: others concur declare greatness men speak, abundantly utter pour gracious fluency; power seen operations providence mighty acts cannot paralleled strength creature, justice terrible acts awful saints dreadful sinners, take occasions speak observing finger God hand arm all marvel; speak glory kingdom talk power extent efficacy can do anything does everything pleases, proof make known mighty acts sons men invited yield willing subjects protection mighty potentate. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry abundantly utter memory great goodness sing righteousness: goodness glory (Ex 33:19) glories (Ex 34:6-7) give glory — abundantly utter memory great goodness, treasures never exhausted never lessened ever rich mercy ever was, memorable goodness always lay before mind preserve memorials worthy everlasting remembrance, remembrance retain utter abundantly full desire others acquainted affected; whenever utter great goodness not forget sing righteousness, gracious rewarding serve faithfully righteous punishing rebel, impartial inflexible justice surely God inexhaustible goodness sing both together (Rom 11:22). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

8 COMMENTARY: Henry gracious full compassion slow anger great mercy: fountain goodness nature — gracious serve full compassion need slow anger offended great mercy seek sue, ready give ready forgive more ready ask repent. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

9 COMMENTARY: Henry good all tender mercies over works: streams goodness dispensations providence — good does good good all creatures highest angel meanest worm all but devils damned sinners shut out goodness; tender mercies over works — all works creatures receive fruits merciful care bounty extended all hates nothing made; works mercy out-shine other works declare more, nothing glory God ever illustrious vessels mercy ordained glory, divine goodness everlasting hallelujahs saints sung. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

10 COMMENTARY: Henry works praise saints bless: tribute praise expected — works praise minister matter praise capacity refuse honour get honour upon; saints bless not only peculiar blessings other creatures not but praise actively other works only objectively, collect rent tribute praise inferior creatures pay treasury above; works praise builder beautiful building painter well-drawn picture, saints bless children prudent tender parents rise call blessed; saints workmanship grace first-fruits creatures most reason bless. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

11 COMMENTARY: Henry speak glory kingdom talk power: kingdom thing often thought spoken, magnified greatness goodness general here application kingdom; greatness kingdom — kings kingdoms earth under control, pomp look within veil see believing speak glory kingdom glorious majesty, prepared throne heavens high lifted surrounded innumerable angels, courts Solomon Ahasuerus magnificent compared glorious majesty glow-worms sun, consideration strike awe approaches God; power speak glory talk power extent efficacy can anything does everything pleases proof make known mighty acts invited yield willing subjects protection mighty potentate. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

12 COMMENTARY: Henry make known sons men glorious majesty kingdom mighty acts: pomp power perpetuity — make known mighty acts sons men invited yield willing subjects, glorious majesty kingdom; perpetuity thrones earthly totter flowers crowns wither monarchies end, Lord kingdom everlasting govern world end time Mediator entrusted administration deliver up God Father all all eternity, dominion endures throughout generations himself eternal counsels unchangeable uniform Satan kingdom opposition conquered chain. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

13 COMMENTARY: Henry kingdom everlasting dominion throughout generations: perpetuity — thrones totter flowers wither monarchies end, kingdom everlasting govern end time Mediator deliver Father all all eternity, dominion endures generations eternal counsels unchangeable uniform Satan conquered chain. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

14 COMMENTARY: Henry upholdeth fall raiseth bowed down: goodness kingdom supports sinking honour help weak — upholds all fall though fall not utterly cast down; children men brought low sickness distresses ready drop grave Providence wonderfully upholds raises Return (Ps 110:3), if all died once seemed dying world very thin; children God ready fall sin despair experienced goodness preventing falls recovering speedily graces comforts though fell not utterly cast down (Ps 37:24); bowed oppression affliction raised God raised; heavy-laden burden sin come Christ faith ease raise. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

15 COMMENTARY: Henry eyes all wait givest meat due season: goodness creatures general provides food all flesh everlasting mercy (Ps 136:25), creatures live God being first supports being depend continuance; eye expectation attends — eyes all wait, inferior creatures not knowledge God capable yet said wait seek food instinct God nature put (sow not reap Matt 6:26) take God nature provided time way appointed content; hand bounty stretched — givest meat due season proper time need none creatures ordinarily perish want food not winter, openest hand freely liberally satisfiest desire every living thing except unreasonable children men satisfied nothing complaining crying Give give. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

16 COMMENTARY: Henry openest hand satisfiest desire every living thing: hand bounty stretched — openest hand freely liberally satisfiest desire every living thing except unreasonable children men satisfied nothing complaining crying Give give; satisfies desire every living, much more fulfil desire fear him feeds birds not starve babes. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

17 COMMENTARY: Henry righteous ways holy works: children men particular governs reasonable creatures — does none wrong righteous all ways unrighteous none holy acts like himself perfect rectitude works, acts government just injurious none administering justice all, ways Lord equal ours unequal, giving laws deciding controversies recompensing services punishing offences incontestably just bound own. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

18 COMMENTARY: Henry nigh all call truth: ready hear answer prayers grace kingdom subjects not only liberty petitioning encouragement petition; grant rich God nigh all call always within call prayers always within reach help, neighbour near better brother afar (Prov 27:10) much more God near, not only nigh satisfaction heard fulfil desires have ask find seek, said satisfies desire every living much more fulfil desire fear feeds birds not starve babes, hear call save hearing purpose heard David saved horn unicorn (Ps 22:21); proviso reasonable — fear worship serve holy awe otherwise expect accept, call truth desires truth inward part faithful sincere professions dependence devotedness, devotions inward impressions answerable outward expressions else not performed truth. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

19 COMMENTARY: Henry fulfil desire fear hear cry save: hear save purpose — fulfil desire fear, hear cry save, feeds birds not starve babes, proviso fear holy awe call truth faithful sincere inward answerable outward else not truth. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

20 COMMENTARY: Henry preserveth love wicked destroy: special protection confidence complacency — preserves love lie exposed world preserving integrity effectually secure no real evil befal; destroyed thank themselves wicked fitted destruction, magnifies goodness protection righteous eyes see reward wicked (Ps 91:8), God preserve people destroying wicked do mischief. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

21 COMMENTARY: Henry mouth speak praise Lord all flesh bless holy name ever ever: concludes resolution glory himself — said can praising still more said not only begin thanksgivings purpose v1 conclude here presently occasion begin again, end mercy beginning another end thanksgiving, while breath draw mouth still speak praises; call others — all flesh mankind bless holy name ever ever, some mankind blessing ever pity all so engaged. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
