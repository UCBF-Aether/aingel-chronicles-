Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 15

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Lord, who shall abide in your tabernacle, who shall dwell in your holy hill? The question is who may live in God's presence, as a guest in his house. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: He who walks uprightly, works righteousness, and speaks truth in his heart: character, conduct, and inward sincerity are joined, not separated. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: He does not backbite with his tongue, do evil to his neighbour, or take up a reproach against his neighbour: the tongue is the first test of a fit guest. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: In his eyes a vile person is contemned, but he honours those who fear the Lord; he swears to his own hurt and changes not: he judges by godliness, not by profit, and keeps his oath even at cost. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: He does not put out money to usury nor take reward against the innocent; he who does these things shall never be moved: justice in money matters and steadfastness complete the portrait, and such a man stands firm. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
