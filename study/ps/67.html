Psalm 67

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: God be merciful unto us, and bless us, and cause his face to shine upon us: mercy, blessing, and a shining face are prayed for — the Aaronic blessing's shape, applied to the people. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: That your way may be known upon earth, your saving health among all nations: the purpose of blessing is mission — God's way known on earth, his saving health among all nations. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Let the people praise you, O God; let all the people praise you: the peoples' praise, all the peoples' praise, is prayed for twice in this psalm. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: O let the nations be glad and sing for joy, for you shall judge the people righteously, and govern the nations upon earth: nations glad and singing, because God's judgment is righteous and his government is over the nations on earth. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Let the people praise you, O God; let all the people praise you: the refrain returns — praise, all peoples, again. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Then shall the earth yield her increase, and God, even our own God, shall bless us: earth's increase follows the peoples' praise, and God, even our own God, blesses — harvest and blessing are joined. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: God shall bless us, and all the ends of the earth shall fear him: blessing on us, fear at earth's ends — the psalm ends with blessing spreading to reverence worldwide. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
