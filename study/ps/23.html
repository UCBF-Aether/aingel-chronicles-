Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 23

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want: one relation — shepherd and sheep — contains the whole provision of the psalm. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: He makes me lie down in green pastures and leads me beside still waters: rest and refreshment are given, not merely promised, and the waters are still because the Shepherd quiets them. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: He restores my soul and leads me in paths of righteousness for his name's sake: restoration after wandering and guidance in right ways both serve God's reputation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for you are with me; your rod and staff comfort me: presence, not the absence of danger, removes fear, and the shepherd's rod and staff both defend and guide. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies, anoint my head with oil, and my cup runs over: honour and abundance are given in full view of enemies, not after they are gone. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever: goodness and mercy pursue the sheep as enemies once did, and the journey ends in God's house for ever. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
