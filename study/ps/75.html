Psalm 75

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Unto you, O God, do we give thanks, unto you do we give thanks, for that your name is near, your wondrous works declare: thanks twice given, name near, wondrous works declaring — God's nearness is proved by his works. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: When I shall receive the congregation I will judge uprightly: God speaks — when he receives the congregation, he will judge uprightly; judgment waits for God's appointed time. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: The earth and all its inhabitants are dissolved; I bear up the pillars of it: earth dissolved, inhabitants dissolved, yet God bears up its pillars — the Judge upholds the world he judges. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: I said unto the fools, Deal not foolishly, and to the wicked, Lift not up the horn: fools bidden not deal foolishly, wicked bidden not lift the horn — the horn is power proudly raised. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Lift not up your horn on high; speak not with a stiff neck: horn not lifted on high, no stiff-necked speech — pride's posture and pride's speech are both forbidden. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: For promotion comes neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south: promotion from neither east, west, nor south — the compass is searched and promotion is found in none of its quarters. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: But God is the judge; he puts down one, and sets up another: God the judge puts down and sets up — promotion's source is judgment, not geography. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: For in the hand of the Lord there is a cup, and the wine is red; it is full of mixture; and he pours out of the same; but the dregs thereof, all the wicked of the earth shall wring them out, and drink them: the Lord's hand holds a cup, red wine full of mixture, poured out — and the dregs, wrung out and drunk by all the earth's wicked, are judgment's bitter end. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: But I will declare for ever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob: declaring for ever, singing praises to Jacob's God — the psalmist's answer to God's judgment is endless declaration. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: All the horns of the wicked also will I cut off, but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted: wicked horns cut off, righteous horns exalted — God's judgment, promised in verse 7, is completed in the cutting and exalting. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
