Study Notes — Psalms

Psalm 12

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Help, Lord, for the godly cease and the faithful fail among men: David laments a time when true religion seems almost extinct. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Neighbours speak vanity, with flattering lips and a double heart: smooth speech hides divided, deceitful intentions. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall cut off flattering lips and the tongue that speaks proud things: God will silence speech used as a weapon of pride. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: They boast that with their tongue they will prevail, their lips are their own, and no one is lord over them: the claim to absolute freedom of speech is rebellion against God's lordship. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: For the oppression of the poor and the sighing of the needy, now God will arise and set the needy in safety from him that puffs at him: the cry of the oppressed moves God to action. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: The words of the Lord are pure, like silver tried in a furnace of earth, purified seven times: unlike flattering human words, God's words are wholly without dross. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: God shall keep his people and preserve them from this generation for ever: his pure word is matched by his preserving care. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: The wicked walk on every side when the vilest men are exalted: when base men rise to honour, evil grows bold everywhere. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
