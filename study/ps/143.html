Psalm 143

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Henry begs heard: prayer David full complaints distress danger probably Saul persecuted, prayed very much very often not same over again new thoughts; complains troubles oppression enemies v3 weakness spirit ready sink likely course support vv4-5, prays earnestly v6 hear vv1-7 not deal sins v2 not hide face v7 manifest favour v8 guide direct duty vv8,10 quicken v11 deliver troubles vv9,11 reckon persecutors v12; petitions spiritual blessings all need mercy grace; humbly begs heard not questioned earnestly desired care, directed prayer looked up see sped (Hab 2:1); suppliant God begs requests granted Hear prayer give ear supplications, appellant persecutors begs case brought hearing God judgment faithfulness righteousness Judge right wrong, or Answer petitions faithfulness promises made wilt just to; no righteousness own plead therefore plead God's righteousness word promise freely given caused hope. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

2 COMMENTARY: Henry begs not strict justice: explains plea Deliver me thy righteousness — mean righteous promises gospel not righteous threatenings law, if answered righteousness broken covenant innocency quite undone; petition Enter not judgment servant do not deal strict justice deserve; prayer own servants bound obey accountable solicitous favour approve, acknowledge many instances offended come short duty might justly enquire proceed law judgment certainly against, nothing move arrest mitigation execution taken awarded ruined for ever; encourage hope mercy forgiveness earnest benefit; Enter not judgment servant thou already entered judgment Son laid iniquity us all, servant enters judgment himself if judge ourselves shall not be judged; plea In thy sight no man living justified terms, no man plead innocency righteousness own either not sinned not deserve die sins nor satisfaction own offer, if God contend not able answer one thousand (Job 9:3; 15:20); David before prays removal trouble prays pardon sin depends mere mercy. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

3 COMMENTARY: Henry enemy persecuted soul smitten ground darkness long dead: prevalency enemies — Saul great enemy persecuted soul sought life restless malice carried so far already smitten down ground, though not yet under ground struck ground next door, forced dwell darkness not only dark caves dark thoughts apprehensions clouds melancholy helpless hopeless long dead; Lord let find mercy thee find no mercy men, they condemn Lord do not condemn, object compassion fit appeared for enemy object displeasure fit appeared against. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

4 COMMENTARY: Henry spirit overwhelmed heart desolate: bemoans oppression mind outward troubles — spirit overpowered overwhelmed almost plunged despair, without fightings within fears fears greater tyrants oppressors Saul himself not easily out-run; sometimes lot best men spirits time almost overwhelmed hearts desolate doubtless infirmity; David great saint great soldier yet sometimes ready faint day adversity, Howl fir-trees cedars shaken. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

5 COMMENTARY: Henry remembers days old meditates works muses hands: applies proper means relief troubled spirit — no force muster oppression enemy, if keep possession nothing else do what can keep possession soul preserve inward peace; looks back remembers days old God's former appearances afflicted people him particular, often relief straits think wonders fathers told (Ps 77:5,11); looks round notices works God visible creation providential government world meditate all works, many see do not see footsteps wisdom power goodness do not receive benefit might because do not meditate, do not dwell copious curious subject soon quit scarcely touched; muse discourse operation hands how great how good, more consider power God less fear face force man (Isa 51:12-13). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

6 COMMENTARY: Henry stretches hands soul thirsteth thirsty land: looks up earnest desires God favour — stretch hands begging alms big expectation receive something great standing ready lay hold bid welcome; soul thirsteth after thee to thee entire intent, as thirsty land parched excessive heat gapes rain so need crave support refreshment divine consolations afflictions nothing else relieve; best course spirits overwhelmed, justly sink load will not take ready way ease. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

7 COMMENTARY: Henry hear speedily spirit faileth hide not face lest pit: tells stretched hands begins earnest haste — Hear speedily defer no longer spirit faileth just ready faint reach cordial quickly quickly gone, not haste unbelief vehement desire holy love Make haste God help; manifestations favour prefers before any good (Ps 4:6); dreads frowns hide not face angry turn displeased, let not left apprehensions anger doubt favour, favour let not hidden, truth grace cannot but desire evidence; pleads wretchedness case God withdrew — lie wrath then like go down pit grave dead weak pale ghastly frowns worse death, or hell bottomless pit, even grace delivered going down pit sometimes terrors Almighty array look like going pit, disconsolate saints cried wrath God as damned sinners (Job 6:4; Ps 88:6). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

8 COMMENTARY: Henry cause hear lovingkindness morning trust cause know way walk lift soul: entreats favour — cannot think God kindness kind things say good comfortable words, present hurry affairs tumult spirits drowned pleasing whispers begs Lord not only speak kindly cause hear hear joy gladness (Ps 51:8); God speaks word providence both desire endeavour hear lovingkindness (Ps 107:43) set always before, cause hear morning every morning waking thoughts lovingkindness sweet relish abide spirits day; plea in thee trust only look not comfort other, goodness commonly wrought those trust (Ps 31:8) faith draw out; enlightened knowledge will first work Spirit order other works God deals men reasonable; cause know way walk — sometimes much care walk right doubt dark right way come boldly throne grace beg word Spirit providence show way prevent missing, good man asks not pleasant walking but right way should walk, pleads lift soul moulded fashioned will, importunately impartially desire duty taught. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

9 COMMENTARY: Henry deliver enemies flee hide: appearance providence rest troubles — Deliver enemies may not have will against, flee thee hide trust defend trouble therefore rescue; preservations pledges salvation shall find God hiding-place faith make such. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

10 COMMENTARY: Henry teach do will God Spirit good lead land uprightness: teach do will not only show what will teach how do turn hand dexterously duty, desire endeavour faithful servants know do will stand complete; pleads Thou art my God therefore oracle advised ruler will desire do, sincerity take God God depend teach do will master servant; lead land uprightness communion saints pleasant land upright settled course holy living lead heaven land uprightness holiness perfection holy holy still; desire led kept safe heaven not only blessedness but uprightness perfection grace; cannot find way bring land unless God show nor go way unless take hand lead as lead weak lame timorous dim-sighted, necessary grace not only put good way keep carry on; plea Spirit good able make good willing help at loss, Lord God Spirit guide character privilege led by Spirit. — Matthew Henry (Complete)

11 COMMENTARY: Henry quicken name sake righteousness bring soul trouble: enlivened do will — Quicken devotions lively quicken duty quicken in it for name sake, best saints often dull dead slow therefore pray quicken; explains for righteousness sake bring soul trouble promise sake mercy sake some righteousness kindness goodness, not only deliver outward trouble but trouble soul threatens overwhelm spirit, whatever trouble Lord let not heart troubled (John 14:1). — Matthew Henry (Complete)

12 COMMENTARY: Henry mercy cut off enemies destroy afflict soul servant: reckon instruments trouble — Of mercy cut off enemies no longer fear, destroy all whoever numerous powerful afflict soul create vexation, for I am thy servant resolved continue such therefore expect owned protected service; prayer prophecy utter destruction impenitent enemies Jesus Christ kingdom will not have reign grieve Spirit afflict soul afflicting people afflictions he afflicted. — Matthew Henry (Complete)
