Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 38

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD comes against Gog, in the latter days, when enemies joining together shall still not go unpunished. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Set thy face against Gog, land of Magog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal, and prophesy against him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: God is against Gog, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: God will turn him back, put hooks in his jaws, and bring him forth with all his army, horses, horsemen, armour, bucklers, shields, and swords. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Persia, Ethiopia, and Libya with them, all with shield and helmet. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Gomer and all his bands, Togarmah of the north quarters and all his bands, and many people with him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: Be thou prepared, and prepare for thyself, thou and all thy assembled company, and be thou a guard unto them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: After many days thou shalt be visited: in latter years thou shalt come into the land brought back from the sword, gathered from many peoples, against Israel's mountains long waste, now brought forth from nations, dwelling safely all. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Thou shalt ascend like a storm, like a cloud covering the land, thou, bands, and many people. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: At the same time things shall come into thy mind, and thou shalt think an evil thought. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: Thou shalt say, I will go up to unwalled villages, to them at rest dwelling safely, without walls, bars, or gates. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: To take spoil and prey, turning thy hand upon desolate places now inhabited, and upon people gathered from nations, gotten cattle and goods, dwelling in the land's midst. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: Sheba, Dedan, and Tarshish merchants with young lions shall say, Art thou come to take spoil, to carry silver, gold, cattle, and goods, a great spoil? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Therefore prophesy to Gog: in that day when God's people dwell safely, shalt thou not know it, finding endeavours to destroy them made in vain? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: Thou shalt come from the north parts, thou and many peoples, all riding horses, a great company and mighty army. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Thou shalt come against God's people as a cloud covering the land, in latter days, and God will bring thee against his land, that heathen may know him when sanctified in Gog before their eyes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: Art thou he of whom God spoke in old time by Israel's prophets, prophesying many years that he would bring thee against them? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: At the same time Gog comes against Israel, God's fury shall come up in his face. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: In jealousy and wrath's fire God has spoken: surely in that day a great shaking in Israel's land. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: Fishes, fowls, beasts, creeping things, and all men shall shake at God's presence, mountains thrown down, steep places falling, every wall falling to ground. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: God will call for a sword against him throughout all mountains, every man's sword against his brother. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: God will plead against him with pestilence and blood, raining overflowing rain, great hailstones, fire, and brimstone on him, bands, and many peoples. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: Thus God will magnify and sanctify himself, known in many nations' eyes, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
