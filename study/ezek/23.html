Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 23

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD comes again, with a history of God's people's apostasy and its aggravation. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: There were two women, daughters of one mother, Samaria and Jerusalem in one parable. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: They committed whoredoms in Egypt in their youth, idolatry learned early and never left. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Their names were Aholah the elder and Aholibah her sister, Samaria and Jerusalem, both God's, bearing sons and daughters. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Aholah played the harlot when she was God's, doting on her lovers, the Assyrians her neighbours. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Clothed with blue, captains and rulers, desirable young horsemen, Assyria's splendour drew her. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: She committed whoredoms with Assyria's chosen men, and with all their idols defiled herself. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: Neither left she her whoredoms brought from Egypt, her youth's idolatry poured upon her still. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Wherefore God delivered her into her lovers' hand, the Assyrians upon whom she doted. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: They discovered her nakedness, took sons and daughters, slew her with the sword, and she became famous among women for executed judgment. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: When Aholibah saw this, she was more corrupt in inordinate love and whoredoms than her sister, warned yet worse. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: She doted upon the Assyrians, captains and rulers gorgeously clothed, desirable horsemen, as Samaria had. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: Then God saw she was defiled, that both sisters took one way. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: She increased her whoredoms when she saw Chaldean images portrayed on the wall in vermilion. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: Girded with girdles, exceeding in dyed attire, all princes to look to, after Babylonian manner, they dazzled her eyes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: As soon as she saw them she doted upon them and sent messengers into Chaldea. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: The Babylonians came, defiled her with whoredom, she was polluted with them, and her mind was alienated from them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: She discovered her whoredoms and nakedness, and then God's mind was alienated from her, as from her sister. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: Yet she multiplied whoredoms, calling to remembrance her youth's harlotry in Egypt. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: She doted upon Egypt's paramours, returning to her earliest idolatry with aggravated appetite. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: Thus she called to remembrance the lewdness of her youth, bruised by Egyptians for the paps of her youth. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: Therefore, O Aholibah, God will raise up her lovers, from whom her mind is alienated, against her on every side. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: Babylonians, Chaldeans, Pekod, Shoa, Koa, and Assyrians, desirable captains, rulers, lords, and horsemen, shall come. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: They shall come with chariots, wagons, wheels, and an assembly, buckler, shield, and helmet round about, and God will set judgment before them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: God will set jealousy against her, and they shall deal furiously, taking nose and ears, slaying the remnant, taking sons and daughters, devouring residue by fire. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: They shall strip her of clothes and take away fair jewels. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: Thus God will make her lewdness cease, and her Egyptian whoredom, so she shall not lift eyes to them nor remember Egypt any more. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: God will deliver her into the hand of those she hates, from whom her mind is alienated. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

29 COMMENTARY: They shall deal hatefully, take away all her labour, leave her naked and bare, and discover the nakedness of her whoredoms. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

30 COMMENTARY: God will do these things because she went whoring after the heathen and is polluted with their idols. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

31 COMMENTARY: She walked in her sister's way, therefore God will give her sister's cup into her hand. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

32 COMMENTARY: She shall drink her sister's cup deep and large, laughed to scorn and derided, for it contains much. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

33 COMMENTARY: She shall be filled with drunkenness and sorrow, the cup of astonishment and desolation, Samaria's cup. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

34 COMMENTARY: She shall drink it and suck it out, break its sherds, and pluck off her own breasts, for God has spoken it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

35 COMMENTARY: Because she forgot God and cast him behind her back, she must bear her lewdness and whoredoms. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

36 COMMENTARY: The LORD said, Wilt thou judge Aholah and Aholibah? Yea, declare their abominations. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

37 COMMENTARY: They committed adultery, blood is in their hands, with idols they committed adultery, and caused sons born to God to pass through fire to devour them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

38 COMMENTARY: Moreover they defiled God's sanctuary in the same day and profaned his sabbaths. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

39 COMMENTARY: Having slain children to idols, they came the same day into God's sanctuary to profane it, in the midst of his house. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

40 COMMENTARY: Furthermore they sent for men from far, for whom she washed, painted eyes, and decked herself with ornaments. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

41 COMMENTARY: She sat on a stately bed with a prepared table, setting God's incense and oil before her guests. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

42 COMMENTARY: A multitude at ease was with her, and Sabeans from the wilderness put bracelets and beautiful crowns on their hands and heads. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

43 COMMENTARY: Then God said to her old in adulteries, Will they now commit whoredoms with her, and she with them? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

44 COMMENTARY: Yet they went in unto her as unto a harlot, so went they in unto Aholah and Aholibah, the lewd women. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

45 COMMENTARY: Righteous men shall judge them as adulteresses and women shedding blood, for they are adulteresses with blood in their hands. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

46 COMMENTARY: God will bring up a company upon them and give them to be removed and spoiled. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

47 COMMENTARY: The company shall stone them, dispatch them with swords, slay sons and daughters, and burn houses with fire. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

48 COMMENTARY: Thus God will cause lewdness to cease out of the land, that all women may be taught not to do after their lewdness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

49 COMMENTARY: They shall recompense lewdness upon them, and they shall bear the sins of their idols, and know he is the Lord GOD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
