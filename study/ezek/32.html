Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 32

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: In the twelfth year, twelfth month, first day, the word of the LORD came, a lamentation for Pharaoh. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Take up a lamentation for Pharaoh: like a young lion of nations and a whale in seas, he came forth with rivers, troubling and fouling waters, a beast of prey. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: God will spread his net over Pharaoh with many peoples, and they shall bring him up in the net. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Left upon land, cast on the open field, fowls remaining upon him, beasts of earth filled with him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: His flesh laid on mountains, valleys filled with his height. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: His blood watering the land wherein he swam, even to mountains, rivers full of him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: When God puts him out, heaven covered, stars dark, sun clouded, moon giving no light. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: All bright lights of heaven dark over him, darkness set upon his land. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: God will vex many peoples' hearts, bringing his destruction among nations into unknown countries. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Many peoples amazed, kings horribly afraid when God brandishes his sword before them, trembling every moment for life in his fall day. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: The sword of Babylon's king shall come upon him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: By mighty swords' terrible nations, his multitude shall fall, Egypt's pomp spoiled, multitude destroyed. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: All Egypt's beasts beside great waters destroyed, neither man's foot nor beasts' hoofs troubling them any more. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Then God will make waters deep and rivers run like oil, universal sadness and heaviness upon the nation. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: When Egypt is made desolate, destitute of its fulness, and all dwellers smitten, then they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: This is the lamentation wherewith they shall lament her, nations' daughters lamenting Egypt and all her multitude. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: In the twelfth year, fifteenth day of the month, the word of the LORD came again, to wail for Egypt's multitude. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: Wail for Egypt's multitude and cast them down, with famous nations' daughters, to earth's nether parts, with pit-goers. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: Whom dost thou pass in beauty? Go down, and be laid with the uncircumcised. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: They shall fall among sword-slain, delivered to the sword, drawn with all multitudes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: The strong among the mighty shall speak from hell with his helpers: gone down, lying uncircumcised, slain by sword. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: Asshur is there with all her company, graves about him, all slain, fallen by sword. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: Graves set in the pit's sides, company round her grave, all slain, fallen by sword, who caused terror in the land of the living. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: Elam is there with all her multitude round her grave, all slain by sword, gone down uncircumcised, bearing shame with pit-goers. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: A bed set amid the slain with all her multitude, graves round about, uncircumcised, sword-slain, terror in life turned to shame in death. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: Meshech and Tubal with all her multitude, graves round about, uncircumcised, sword-slain, though they caused terror in life. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: They shall not lie with fallen mighty uncircumcised gone down with weapons, swords under heads, iniquities upon bones, though terror of the mighty in life. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: Yea, thou shalt be broken among the uncircumcised and lie with sword-slain. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

29 COMMENTARY: Edom, her kings and princes, laid with sword-slain despite might, lying with uncircumcised and pit-goers. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

30 COMMENTARY: Princes of the north and all Zidonians gone down with slain, ashamed of might despite terror, lying uncircumcised with sword-slain, bearing shame. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

31 COMMENTARY: Pharaoh shall see them and be comforted over all his multitude, Pharaoh and all his army sword-slain, a poor comfort, only in fancy. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

32 COMMENTARY: For God caused his terror in the land of the living, and he shall lie among uncircumcised sword-slain, even Pharaoh and all his multitude. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
