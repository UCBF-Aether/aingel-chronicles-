Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 11

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Lifted to the east gate, Ezekiel sees twenty-five men, among them Jaazaniah son of Azur and Pelatiah son of Benaiah, princes of the people. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: These are the men who devise mischief and give wicked counsel in the city. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Saying it is not near, let us build houses, and the city is the caldron and we the flesh, they flatter themselves they are as safe as flesh in a boiling pot. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Therefore prophesy against them, prophesy, son of man, for sinners flattering themselves to ruin must be told they shall have no peace. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: The Spirit fell on him and said, Thus have ye said, O house of Israel, for God knows every thought that comes into their minds. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: They have multiplied their slain in the city and filled its streets with the slain. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: The slain they laid in the city are the flesh and the city the caldron, but God will bring the living rulers out of its midst. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: They feared the sword, and God will bring a sword upon them, for judgment pushed to a distance still arrives. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Brought out and delivered into strangers' hands, judgments will be executed among them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: They shall fall by the sword and be judged in the border of Israel, and know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: The city shall not be their caldron, nor they the flesh in it, for judgment comes at the border, not behind brass walls. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: They shall know he is the LORD, because they walked not in his statutes but after the manners of the heathen round about. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: As he prophesied, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died, a singled-out sinner made a warning to others, and Ezekiel pleads for the remnant. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD came again, turning from Jerusalem's rulers to the insulted captives in Babylon. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: Jerusalem's inhabitants told the exiled brethren, Get you far from the LORD, for the land is given to us in possession. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Though cast far off and scattered, God will be to them as a little sanctuary in the countries where they come. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: God will gather them from the peoples and assemble them from the countries, and give them the land of Israel. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: Coming thither, they shall take away all detestable things and abominations from the land. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: God will give them one heart and a new spirit, taking the stony heart out of their flesh and giving a heart of flesh, tender and fit to receive impressions. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: That they may walk in his statutes and keep his ordinances, they shall be his people and he their God, practice agreeing with principle. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: But those whose hearts walk after detestable things shall have their way recompensed upon their own heads. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: The cherubims lifted their wings, wheels beside them, and the glory of the God of Israel over them above. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: The glory of the LORD went up from the midst of the city and stood on the mountain east of the city, God's presence departing from temple and city. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: The Spirit brought Ezekiel in vision into Chaldea to the captives, and the vision went up from him, as from the Mount of Olives Christ would ascend. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: He spoke to the captives all the things the LORD had shown him, delivering the whole vision faithfully. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
