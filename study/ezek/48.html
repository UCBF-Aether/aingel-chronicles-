Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 48

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Now these are the tribes' names, beginning north: from Hethlon's way to Hamath, Hazarenan, Damascus' north border to Hamath's coast, east and west sides, a portion for Dan. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: By Dan's border, east to west, a portion for Asher. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: By Asher's border, east to west, a portion for Naphtali. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: By Naphtali's border, east to west, a portion for Manasseh. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: By Manasseh's border, east to west, a portion for Ephraim. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: By Ephraim's border, east to west, a portion for Reuben. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: By Reuben's border, east to west, a portion for Judah. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: By Judah's border, east to west, the offering, twenty-five thousand reeds broad, length as other parts east to west, sanctuary in its midst. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: The LORD's oblation, twenty-five thousand long, ten thousand broad. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: For the priests this holy oblation: north twenty-five thousand long, west ten thousand broad, east ten thousand broad, south twenty-five thousand long, the LORD's sanctuary in its midst. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: For sanctified Zadok's sons, who kept God's charge and went not astray when Israel went astray, as Levites did. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: This offered land oblation shall be most holy to them, by the Levites' border. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: Over against the priests' border, Levites shall have twenty-five thousand long, ten thousand broad, all length twenty-five thousand, breadth ten thousand. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: They shall not sell, exchange, or alienate the land's firstfruits, for it is holy to the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: The five thousand left in breadth over against twenty-five thousand shall be a profane place for the city, dwelling and suburbs, city in its midst. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Its measures: north, south, east, and west sides each four thousand and five hundred. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: The city's suburbs: north, south, east, and west each two hundred and fifty. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: Residue in length over against the holy oblation, ten thousand eastward and ten thousand westward, its increase for food to city servants. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: City servants shall serve it out of all Israel's tribes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: All the oblation twenty-five thousand by twenty-five thousand, holy oblation foursquare, with the city's possession. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: Residue for the prince, each side of holy oblation and city possession, over against twenty-five thousand east and west, holy oblation, sanctuary in its midst. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: Moreover from Levites' and city's possession, in the midst of the prince's, between Judah's and Benjamin's borders, shall be for the prince. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: As for the rest, east to west, Benjamin shall have a portion. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: By Benjamin's border, east to west, Simeon a portion. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: By Simeon's border, east to west, Issachar a portion. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: By Issachar's border, east to west, Zebulun a portion. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: By Zebulun's border, east to west, Gad a portion. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: By Gad's border, south side southward, from Tamar to Kadesh's strife waters, and to the river toward the great sea. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

29 COMMENTARY: This is the land divided by lot to Israel's tribes for inheritance, and these are their portions. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

30 COMMENTARY: These are the city's goings out on the north side, four thousand and five hundred measures. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

31 COMMENTARY: The city's gates after Israel's tribes: three northward, Reuben, Judah, and Levi, free access from every side into Christ's church. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

32 COMMENTARY: East side four thousand and five hundred, three gates, Joseph, Benjamin, and Dan. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

33 COMMENTARY: South side four thousand and five hundred measures, three gates, Simeon, Issachar, and Zebulun. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

34 COMMENTARY: West side four thousand and five hundred, three gates, Gad, Asher, and Naphtali. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

35 COMMENTARY: Round about eighteen thousand measures, and the city's name from that day shall be, The LORD is there, for the Lord is in his church, nigh to all who call upon him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
