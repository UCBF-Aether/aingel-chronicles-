Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 17

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD comes, to put forth a riddle and parable for Israel. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Son of man, put forth a riddle and speak a parable to the house of Israel, for mighty conquerors are aptly likened to birds of prey. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: A great eagle, great-winged, longwinged, full of feathers of divers colours, came to Lebanon and took the highest branch of the cedar. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: He cropped the top of its young twigs and carried it to a land of traffick, a city of merchants, Babylon. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: He took also seed of the land and planted it in a fruitful field by great waters, as a willow tree, a vassal king in place. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: It grew into a spreading vine of low stature, branches toward the eagle and roots under him, a kingdom kept base by covenant. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: Another great eagle came, and the vine bent roots and shot branches toward him, seeking Egypt's watering, changing one carnal confidence for another. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: Planted in good soil by great waters to bear fruit as a goodly vine, it had every advantage of place. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Shall it prosper? Shall not the first eagle pull up its roots and cut off its fruit, that it wither in all spring leaves, without great power? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Being planted, shall it prosper? Utterly withered when the east wind touches it, in the furrows where it grew. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: Moreover the word of the LORD came, now to explain the parable. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Say to the rebellious house, Know ye not what these things mean? The king of Babylon came to Jerusalem, took king and princes, and led them to Babylon. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: He took of the king's seed, made a covenant and took an oath of him, and took the mighty of the land as pledges. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: That the kingdom might be base, not lifting itself up, but standing by keeping his covenant. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: But Zedekiah rebelled, sending ambassadors into Egypt for horses and much people, and shall he prosper, escape, break covenant, and be delivered? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: As God lives, in the place where the king dwells who made him king, whose oath he despised and covenant he broke, even in Babylon he shall die. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: Neither shall Pharaoh with mighty army and great company make for him in war, by mounts and forts, to cut off many persons. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: Seeing he despised the oath by breaking the covenant, though he had given his hand, he shall not escape. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: As God lives, his own despised oath and broken covenant God will recompense upon his own head, for truth is a debt owing to all men. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: God will spread his net, take him in a snare, bring him to Babylon, and plead with him there for his trespass against God. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: All his fugitives and bands shall fall by the sword, and the rest scattered toward all winds, and they shall know the LORD has spoken. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: God himself will take a tender twig from the highest branch of the high cedar and plant it upon a high and eminent mountain. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: In the mountain of the height of Israel it shall bring forth boughs and fruit as a goodly cedar, and all fowl shall dwell in its shadow, the Messiah's kingdom. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: All trees shall know the LORD has brought down the high tree, exalted the low, dried the green, and made the dry flourish, for he has spoken and done it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
