Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 47

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Brought again to the house door, behold waters issuing from under its threshold eastward, from the right side at the altar's south side, signifying Christ's gospel flowing from Jerusalem. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Brought out by the north gate way, led about without to the east-looking utter gate, behold waters running out on the right side. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: The man with the line went eastward, measured a thousand cubits, and brought him through waters to the ankles, plain things first understood. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Again a thousand, waters to the knees, again a thousand, waters to the loins, deeper search required as grace progresses. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Afterward a thousand, a river he could not pass, waters to swim in, a river unpassable, depths to adore, not penetrate. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Son of man, hast thou seen this? Then brought back to the river's brink. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: Returning, behold at the river bank very many trees on each side. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: These waters issue toward the east country, go down into the desert and sea, and the sea's waters shall be healed. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Every living moving thing whithersoever the rivers come shall live, a very great multitude of fish, healed, living wherever the river comes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Fishers shall stand from Engedi to Eneglaim, spreading nets, fish according to kinds, as the great sea's, exceeding many. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: But miry places and marshes shall not be healed, given to salt. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: By the river bank on each side shall grow all trees for meat, leaf unfading, fruit unconsumed, new fruit monthly, waters from the sanctuary, fruit for meat and leaf for medicine. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: This shall be the border whereby ye inherit the land by twelve tribes, Joseph having two portions. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Ye shall inherit it, one as well as another, as God lifted his hand to give it to fathers, falling to you for inheritance. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: The north border: from the great sea, Hethlon's way, as men go to Zedad. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim between Damascus and Hamath borders, Hazarhatticon by Hauran's coast. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: The border from the sea shall be Hazarenan, Damascus' border, north northward, and Hamath's border, this the north side. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: The east side measured from Hauran, Damascus, Gilead, and Israel by Jordan, from border to east sea, this the east side. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: The south side southward, from Tamar to Kadesh's strife waters, the river to the great sea, this the south side. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: The west side the great sea from the border till over against Hamath, this the west side. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: So shall ye divide this land according to Israel's tribes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: Divide it by lot for inheritance to you and sojourning strangers begetting children among you, as born in the country among Israel's children, inheriting with you among tribes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: In whatever tribe the stranger sojourns, there give him his inheritance, for all united to Christ share Israel's privileges. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
