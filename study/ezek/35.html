Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 35

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Moreover the word of the LORD comes against Mount Seir, Edom, whose constant hatred to God and his people can only expect everlasting desolation. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Set thy face against Mount Seir and prophesy against it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: God is against Mount Seir, will stretch out his hand and make it most desolate. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: He will lay its cities waste, make it desolate, and it shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Because it had perpetual hatred and shed Israel's blood by the sword in their calamity time, when their iniquity had an end. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: As God lives, he will prepare Seir unto blood, and blood shall pursue it, since it has not hated blood. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: Thus God will make Mount Seir most desolate, cutting off him that passes out and him that returns. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: He will fill its mountains with slain, in hills, valleys, and rivers falling sword-slain. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: He will make perpetual desolations, cities not returning, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Because Seir said, These two nations and two countries shall be mine, we will possess it, whereas the LORD was there. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: As God lives, he will do according to Seir's anger and envy used out of hatred, and make himself known among them when he judges her. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Seir shall know he is the LORD, and that he has heard all its blasphemies against Israel's mountains, saying, They are laid desolate, given us to consume. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: Thus with mouth they boasted against God and multiplied words against him, and he has heard them, for not one idle word is unknown to him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: When the whole earth rejoices, God will make Seir desolate. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: As Seir rejoiced at Israel's inheritance because it was desolate, so God will do to her: desolate, O Mount Seir, and all Idumea, even all of it, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
