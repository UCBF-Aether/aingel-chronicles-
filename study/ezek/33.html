Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 33

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Again the word of the LORD comes, renewing the prophet as watchman to Israel. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Speak to thy people: when God brings the sword on a land, if the people take a man of their coasts and set him for watchman. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: If, seeing the sword come, he blows the trumpet and warns the people, his duty is done. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Whoever hears the trumpet and takes not warning, if the sword takes him, his blood is on his own head. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: He heard and took not warning, his blood is on him, but he that takes warning shall deliver his soul. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: But if the watchman sees the sword, blows not, and the people are unwarned, the taken person dies in iniquity, but his blood is required at the watchman's hand. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: So thou, son of man, art set a watchman to Israel, to hear the word at God's mouth and warn them from him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: When God says to the wicked, Thou shalt surely die, if thou warnest not, the wicked dies in iniquity, but his blood is required at thine hand. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: If thou warn the wicked to turn and he turns not, he dies in iniquity, but thou hast delivered thy soul. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Speak to Israel, who say, If our transgressions and sins are upon us and we pine away in them, how should we then live? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: As God lives, he has no pleasure in the wicked's death, but that the wicked turn and live: turn ye, turn ye from evil ways, for why will ye die, O house of Israel? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: The righteous' righteousness shall not deliver him in his transgression day, nor shall the wicked fall by wickedness in the day he turns from it, nor can the righteous live for righteousness in his sinning day. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: When God says to the righteous he shall surely live, if he trusts his own righteousness and commits iniquity, all his righteousnesses shall not be remembered, and he shall die for his iniquity. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Again, when God says to the wicked, Thou shalt surely die, if he turns from sin and does lawful and right, he shall live. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: If the wicked restores the pledge, gives again what he robbed, walks in life's statutes without iniquity, he shall surely live and not die. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: None of his committed sins shall be mentioned to him, for he has done lawful and right and shall surely live. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: Yet thy people say, The way of the Lord is not equal, but their way is not equal. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: When the righteous turns from righteousness and commits iniquity, he shall die thereby. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: When the wicked turns from wickedness and does lawful and right, he shall live thereby. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: Yet they say, The way of the Lord is not equal, but God will judge each Israelite after his ways. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: In the twelfth year of captivity, tenth month, fifth day, one escaped from Jerusalem came, saying, The city is smitten. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: God's hand was upon Ezekiel in the evening before the escaped one came, opening his mouth until morning, and he was no more dumb. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: Then the word of the LORD came, against the waste-dwellers' presumption. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: Waste-inhabitants say, Abraham was one and inherited the land, but we are many, the land is given us for inheritance. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: Say to them: ye eat with blood, lift eyes to idols, and shed blood, and shall ye possess the land? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: Ye stand upon your sword, work abomination, defile neighbours' wives, and shall ye possess the land? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: As God lives, waste-dwellers shall fall by sword, field men given to beasts, fort and cave men dying of pestilence. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: God will lay the land most desolate, pomp of strength ceasing, Israel's mountains desolate, none passing through. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

29 COMMENTARY: Then they shall know he is the LORD, when the land is laid most desolate because of all their abominations. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

30 COMMENTARY: Also, thy people still talk against thee by walls and house doors, saying, Come hear what word comes forth from the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

31 COMMENTARY: They come as the people come, sit before thee as God's people, hear thy words, but will not do them, mouth showing much love while heart goes after covetousness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

32 COMMENTARY: Lo, thou art to them as a very lovely song of pleasant voice and well-played instrument, for they hear thy words but do them not. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

33 COMMENTARY: When this comes to pass, lo, it will come, then they shall know a prophet has been among them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
