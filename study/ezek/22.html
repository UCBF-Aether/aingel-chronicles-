Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 22

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Moreover the word of the LORD comes, to judge the bloody city and show her all her abominations. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Wilt thou judge the bloody city? Yea, show her all her abominations, for Jerusalem is called bloody because of her crimes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: The city sheds blood in her midst that her time may come, and makes idols against herself to defile herself. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Guilty in shed blood and defiled by made idols, she has caused her days to draw near, made a reproach to heathen and a mocking to all countries. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Near and far shall mock her, infamous and much vexed. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Behold, Israel's princes, every one to his power, shed blood. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: In her they set light by father and mother, oppressed the stranger, and vexed fatherless and widow. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: She despised God's holy things and profaned his sabbaths. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: In her are tale-carriers shedding blood, mountain feasters, and lewdness in her midst. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: In her they discovered fathers' nakedness and humbled her set apart for pollution. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: One committed abomination with his neighbour's wife, another defiled his daughter-in-law, another humbled his sister, his father's daughter. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Gifts taken to shed blood, usury and increase taken, neighbours gained by extortion, and God forgotten, at the bottom of all wickedness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: Therefore God has smitten his hand at her dishonest gain and at her blood in her midst. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Can her heart endure or hands be strong in the days God deals with her? The LORD has spoken and will do it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: God will scatter her among heathen, disperse her in countries, and consume her filthiness out of her. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: She shall take her inheritance in herself in the heathen's sight, left to her own portion, and know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD comes again, with the furnace similitude. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: Israel has become dross to God, brass, tin, iron, and lead in the furnace, even dross of silver, once gold and silver among baser metals. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: Because all are become dross, God will gather them into the midst of Jerusalem. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: As metals are gathered into the furnace to blow fire and melt them, so God in anger and fury will gather, leave, and melt them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: Yea, he will gather them and blow upon them in wrath's fire, and they shall be melted in its midst. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: As silver melts in the furnace, so shall they melt, and know the LORD has poured out his fury upon them, the refiner separating dross from good metal. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD comes again, against all orders of men who filled the nation's guilt. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: Say to her, Thou art the land not cleansed nor rained upon in the day of indignation. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: A conspiracy of her prophets, like a roaring lion ravening prey, devoured souls, took treasure and precious things, and made many widows. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: Her priests violated God's law and profaned holy things, putting no difference between holy and profane, clean and unclean, and hiding eyes from sabbaths. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: Her princes are like wolves ravening prey, shedding blood and destroying souls to get dishonest gain. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: Her prophets daubed them with untempered mortar, seeing vanity and divining lies, saying Thus saith the Lord GOD when he has not spoken. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

29 COMMENTARY: The people of the land used oppression and robbery, vexed poor and needy, and oppressed the stranger wrongfully. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

30 COMMENTARY: God sought a man to make up the hedge and stand in the gap before him for the land, that he should not destroy it, but found none. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

31 COMMENTARY: Therefore he poured out indignation, consumed them with wrath's fire, and recompensed their own way upon their heads. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
