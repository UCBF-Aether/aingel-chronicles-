Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 30

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD comes again, with a full prophecy of Egypt's destruction. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Prophesy, Howl ye, Woe worth the day! — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: The day is near, the day of the LORD near, a cloudy day, the time of the heathen. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: The sword shall come upon Egypt, great pain in Ethiopia, slain falling, multitude taken, foundations broken down. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Ethiopia, Libya, Lydia, mingled people, Chub, and league men shall fall with Egypt by the sword, for those taking lot with God's enemies share their punishment. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Egypt's upholders shall fall, pride of her power coming down, from Syene's tower falling by the sword. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: They shall be desolate among desolate countries, her cities among wasted cities. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: They shall know he is the LORD when he sets fire in Egypt and all her helpers are destroyed. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: In that day messengers in ships shall make careless Ethiopians afraid, great pain coming as in Egypt's day, for lo, it comes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: God will make Egypt's multitude cease by Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, one wicked man made a scourge to another. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: He and his terrible people shall be brought to destroy the land, drawing swords against Egypt and filling it with slain. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: God will make rivers dry, sell the land to the wicked, and make land waste by strangers' hands. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: He will destroy idols, cause images to cease from Noph, leave no prince in Egypt, and put fear in the land. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: He will make Pathros desolate, set fire in Zoan, and execute judgments in No. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: He will pour fury upon Sin, Egypt's strength, and cut off No's multitude. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: He will set fire in Egypt: Sin in great pain, No rent asunder, Noph in daily distresses. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: Aven's and Pibeseth's young men shall fall by the sword, and these cities shall go into captivity. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: At Tehaphnehes the day shall darken when God breaks Egypt's yokes there, pomp of strength ceasing, a cloud covering her, daughters going captive. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: Thus God will execute judgments in Egypt, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: In the eleventh year, first month, seventh day, the word of the LORD came again. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: God has broken Pharaoh's arm, and it shall not be bound up to be healed, no roller to bind it to hold the sword. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: God is against Pharaoh and will break both arms, strong and broken, causing the sword to fall from his hand. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: God will scatter Egyptians among nations and disperse them through countries. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: God will strengthen Babylon's king's arms and put his sword in his hand, but break Pharaoh's arms, who shall groan like a deadly wounded man. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: Babylon's arms strengthened and Pharaoh's fallen, they shall know he is the LORD when Babylon's sword is stretched over Egypt. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: God will scatter Egyptians among nations and disperse them among countries, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
