Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 25

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD comes again, now against Israel's neighbours who rejoiced at her fall. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Set thy face against the Ammonites and prophesy against them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Because Ammon said Aha against God's sanctuary profaned, Israel's land desolate, and Judah captive, judgment is set. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: God will deliver Ammon to the men of the east for a possession, setting palaces and dwellings there, eating fruit and drinking milk. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Rabbah shall become a stable for camels and Ammon a couching place for flocks, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Because Ammon clapped hands, stamped feet, and rejoiced in heart with all despite against Israel's land, it is wicked to be glad at God's people's calamities. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: God will stretch out his hand, deliver Ammon as spoil to the heathen, cut it off from the peoples, and destroy it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: Because Moab and Seir say, Behold, Judah is like all the heathen, they deny God's distinct people and promise. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: God will open Moab's side from frontier cities, Bethjeshimoth, Baalmeon, and Kiriathaim, the glory of the country. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Moab is given to the men of the east with Ammon, that Ammon may not be remembered among nations. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: God will execute judgments upon Moab, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Because Edom dealt against Judah by taking vengeance, greatly offending and revenging himself, judgment is set. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: God will stretch out his hand on Edom, cut off man and beast, make it desolate from Teman, and Dedan shall fall by the sword. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: God will lay vengeance on Edom by Israel's hand, according to his anger and fury, and they shall know his vengeance. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: Because the Philistines dealt by revenge with a despiteful heart to destroy for old hatred, judgment is set. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: God will stretch out his hand on the Philistines, cut off the Cherethims, and destroy the remnant of the sea coast. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: He will execute great vengeance with furious rebukes, and they shall know he is the LORD when he lays vengeance upon them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
