Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 28

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD comes again, against Tyre's prince, lifted up with excessive pride to claim divine honours. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Say to Tyre's prince: because his heart is lifted up, saying, I am a God, sitting in God's seat amid the seas, yet he is a man, not God. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Behold, thou art wiser than Daniel, no secret hidden from thee, in his own proud estimation. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: With wisdom and understanding he got riches, gold and silver into his treasures. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: By great wisdom and traffick he increased riches, and his heart was lifted up because of his riches. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Therefore, because he set his heart as the heart of God, judgment follows. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: God will bring strangers, the terrible of nations, drawing swords against the beauty of his wisdom and defiling his brightness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: They shall bring him down to the pit, dying the deaths of those slain in the midst of the seas. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Wilt thou yet say before thy slayer, I am God? Thou shalt be a man, no God, in the slayer's hand. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: He shall die the deaths of the uncircumcised by strangers' hands, for God has spoken it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: Moreover the word of the LORD comes, with a lamentation upon Tyre's king. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Thou sealest up the sum, full of wisdom and perfect in beauty, the height from which pride fell. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: In Eden, God's garden, every precious stone was his covering, sardius, topaz, diamond, beryl, onyx, jasper, sapphire, emerald, carbuncle, and gold, with tabrets and pipes prepared in his creation day. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Anointed cherub that covereth, set so by God, upon the holy mountain, walking amid stones of fire. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: Perfect in his ways from creation day, till iniquity was found in him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: By multitude of merchandise his midst was filled with violence, and he sinned, so God will cast him profane from God's mountain and destroy the covering cherub. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: His heart lifted up because of beauty, wisdom corrupted by brightness, God will cast him to the ground and lay him before kings to behold. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: Having defiled sanctuaries by multitude of iniquities and traffick's iniquity, fire from his own midst shall devour him to ashes before beholders. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: All who know him shall be astonished, and he shall be a terror and never be any more, for spiritual pride must expect to perish. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: Again the word of the LORD comes, against Zidon, Israel's borderer who seduced her to idols instead of glorifying God. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: Set thy face against Zidon and prophesy against it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: God is against Zidon and will be glorified in her midst, known as the LORD when judgments are executed and he is sanctified in her. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: Pestilence and blood in her streets, wounded judged by sword on every side, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: There shall be no more a pricking brier or grieving thorn to Israel from round-about despisers, and they shall know he is the Lord GOD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: When God gathers Israel from scattered peoples and is sanctified in them before the heathen, they shall dwell in Jacob's land. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: They shall dwell safely, build houses, plant vineyards, and dwell with confidence, once judgments are executed on despisers round about, knowing he is the LORD their God. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
