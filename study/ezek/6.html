Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 6

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD comes to Ezekiel with a new commission against the land's idolatry. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: He must set his face toward the mountains of Israel and prophesy against them, the high places of their worship. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Mountains, hills, rivers, and valleys must hear that God himself will bring a sword and destroy the high places. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Altars desolate, images broken, and slain cast before idols show war desolating even things esteemed most sacred. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Carcases laid before idols and bones scattered round altars make the idol's impotence visible. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Cities laid waste and high places desolate abolish idols, images, and works, for God ruins idolatries even by idolaters' hands. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: The slain falling in their midst will teach them that he is the LORD, whom their idols could not save. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: Yet God will leave a remnant, some escaping the sword among the nations when scattered through the countries. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: The escaped will remember God in captivity, broken by their whorish hearts and idol-chasing eyes, and loathe themselves for their evils. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: They shall know God has not said in vain that he would do this evil to them, and true penitents give glory to God by repentance. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: Smite the hand, stamp the foot, and cry Alas for all the evil abominations, for sword, famine, and pestilence are coming. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Far off dies by pestilence, near falls by sword, and the besieged remnant dies by famine, accomplishing God's fury. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: Slain among idols round altars, on every high hill, mountain top, green tree, and thick oak, will prove the LORD where they offered sweet savour to idols. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: God stretches out his hand and makes the land more desolate than the wilderness toward Diblath, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
