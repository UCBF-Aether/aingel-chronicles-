Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 20

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: In the seventh year, fifth month, tenth day, certain elders came to inquire of the LORD and sat before Ezekiel. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Then the word of the LORD came, answering seekers who ask leave to go on in sin even while suffering for it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Are ye come to inquire of me? As God lives, he will not be inquired of by them, hardened hearts asking while clinging to idols. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Wilt thou judge them, son of man? Cause them to know the abominations of their fathers, that God's righteousness in cutting them off may be seen. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: In the day God chose Israel, lifted up his hand to Jacob's seed, and made himself known in Egypt, saying, I am the LORD your God. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: He lifted up his hand to bring them from Egypt into a land espied for them, flowing with milk and honey, the glory of all lands. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: Then he said, Cast away every man the abominations of his eyes, and defile not yourselves with Egypt's idols, for he is the LORD their God. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: But they rebelled, would not hearken, cast not away eye-abominations, nor forsook Egypt's idols, and God said he would pour out fury in Egypt. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: But God wrought for his name's sake, that it should not be polluted before the heathen, in whose sight he had made himself known in bringing them out. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Wherefore he caused them to go forth from Egypt and brought them into the wilderness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: He gave them statutes and showed judgments, which if a man do, he shall even live in them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: He gave them sabbaths as a sign between him and them, that they might know he is the LORD that sanctifies them, sabbaths being privileges, not burdens. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: But Israel rebelled in the wilderness, walked not in statutes, despised judgments, and greatly polluted sabbaths, and God said he would pour out fury to consume them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: But he wrought for his name's sake, that it should not be polluted before the heathen who saw him bring them out. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: Yet he lifted up his hand in the wilderness that he would not bring them into the milk-and-honey land, the glory of all lands. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Because they despised judgments, walked not in statutes, and polluted sabbaths, for their heart went after idols. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: Nevertheless his eye spared them from destroying them, and he made no end of them in the wilderness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: To their children in the wilderness he said, Walk not in your fathers' statutes, observe not their judgments, nor defile yourselves with their idols. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: I am the LORD your God; walk in my statutes, keep my judgments, and do them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: Hallow my sabbaths, a sign between God and them, that they may know he is the LORD their God. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: Notwithstanding, the children rebelled, walked not in statutes, kept not judgments, and polluted sabbaths, and God said he would pour out fury in the wilderness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: Nevertheless he withdrew his hand and wrought for his name's sake, that it should not be polluted before the heathen who saw him bring them forth. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: He lifted up his hand also in the wilderness to scatter them among the heathen and disperse them through the countries. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: Because they executed not judgments, despised statutes, polluted sabbaths, and their eyes were after their fathers' idols. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: Wherefore he gave them statutes that were not good and judgments whereby they should not live, giving them up to their own evil desires, sin becoming its own punishment. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: He polluted them in their own gifts, causing all that opens the womb to pass through fire, to make them desolate, that they might know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: Therefore speak to Israel: yet in this your fathers blasphemed God, committing trespass against him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: Brought into the promised land, they saw every high hill and thick tree and offered sacrifices, sweet savour, and drink offerings there. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

29 COMMENTARY: Then God said, What is the high place whereunto ye go? And its name is called Bamah unto this day. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

30 COMMENTARY: Are ye polluted after your fathers' manner and committing whoredom after their abominations? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

31 COMMENTARY: Offering gifts and making sons pass through fire, they pollute themselves with idols even to this day, and God will not be inquired of by them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

32 COMMENTARY: What comes into their mind shall not be at all, that they say, We will be as the heathen, serving wood and stone, for sinful compliance gains nothing. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

33 COMMENTARY: As God lives, with mighty hand, stretched-out arm, and fury poured out, he will rule over them, for there is no shaking off his dominion. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

34 COMMENTARY: He will bring them out from the peoples and gather them from scattered countries with mighty hand, stretched-out arm, and fury poured out. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

35 COMMENTARY: He will bring them into the wilderness of the people and plead with them face to face. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

36 COMMENTARY: As he pleaded with their fathers in Egypt's wilderness, so he will plead with them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

37 COMMENTARY: He will cause them to pass under the rod and bring them into the bond of the covenant. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

38 COMMENTARY: He will purge out rebels and transgressors, bringing them from their sojourning country, yet they shall not enter Israel's land. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

39 COMMENTARY: As for Israel, God says, Go serve your idols if ye will not hearken, but pollute my holy name no more with gifts and idols. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

40 COMMENTARY: For in his holy mountain, the mountain of Israel's height, all Israel in the land shall serve him, and he will accept and require their offerings and firstfruits. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

41 COMMENTARY: He will accept them with sweet savour when he gathers them from scattered countries, and be sanctified in them before the heathen. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

42 COMMENTARY: They shall know he is the LORD when he brings them into Israel, the country sworn to their fathers. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

43 COMMENTARY: There they shall remember their ways and doings wherein they were defiled, and loathe themselves for all their evils, overcome by his kindness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

44 COMMENTARY: They shall know he is the LORD when he works with them for his name's sake, not according to their wicked ways. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

45 COMMENTARY: Moreover the word of the LORD came, now against the south, Judah full of people as a forest, yet empty of fruit. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

46 COMMENTARY: Set thy face toward the south, drop thy word toward the south, and prophesy against the forest of the south field. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

47 COMMENTARY: God will kindle a fire in the forest devouring every green and dry tree, a flame unquenched, burning all faces from south to north. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

48 COMMENTARY: All flesh shall see the LORD has kindled it, and it shall not be quenched. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

49 COMMENTARY: Then Ezekiel said, Ah Lord GOD! they say of me, Doth he not speak parables? For the plainest truths were as parables to those who would not be wrought upon. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
