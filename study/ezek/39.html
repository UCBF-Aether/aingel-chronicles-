Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 39

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Therefore prophesy against Gog: God is against him, chief prince of Meshech and Tubal, and weapons formed against Zion shall not prosper. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: God will turn him back, leave but the sixth part, cause him to come from the north parts, and bring him upon Israel's mountains. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: God will smite his bow from his left hand and cause arrows to fall from his right hand, disarming him by miracle. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: He shall fall on Israel's mountains, he, bands, and peoples with him, given to ravenous birds and beasts to be devoured. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: He shall fall upon the open field, for God has spoken it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: God will send fire on Magog and among careless isle-dwellers, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: So God will make his holy name known amid Israel, not letting them pollute it any more, and heathen shall know he is the LORD, the Holy One in Israel. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: Behold, it is come and done, this is the day whereof God has spoken, certain though in latter days. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Israel's city-dwellers shall go forth and burn the weapons, shields, bucklers, bows, arrows, handstaves, and spears, with fire seven years. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: They shall take no wood from field or forest, burning weapons instead, spoiling spoilers and robbing robbers. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: In that day God will give Gog a grave place in Israel, the valley of passengers east of the sea, stopping passengers' noses, burying Gog and multitude, called the valley of Hamongog. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Seven months shall Israel be burying them, that they may cleanse the land, for times of deliverance should be times of reformation. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: All the land's people shall bury them, and it shall be their renown in the day God is glorified. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Men of continual employment shall be severed out, passing through the land to bury remains with passengers, cleansing it, searching after seven months' end. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: Passengers seeing a man's bone shall set up a sign by it till buriers bury it in Hamongog's valley. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Also the city's name shall be Hamonah, thus cleansing the land. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: Speak to every feathered fowl and beast: assemble and come to God's sacrifice on Israel's mountains, to eat flesh and drink blood. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: They shall eat flesh of the mighty and drink blood of earth's princes, rams, lambs, goats, bullocks, fatlings of Bashan. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: They shall eat fat till full and drink blood till drunken, of God's sacrificed sacrifice. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: Thus filled at God's table with horses, chariots, mighty men, and all men of war, judgments become a sacrifice to justice and a feast to faith. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: God will set his glory among heathen, and all heathen shall see his executed judgment and his laid hand. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: So Israel shall know he is the LORD their God from that day and forward. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: Heathen shall know Israel went captive for iniquity, because they trespassed, God hid his face and gave them to enemies, falling by sword. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: According to uncleanness and transgressions God did to them and hid his face from them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: Now God will bring again Jacob's captivity, have mercy on the whole house of Israel, and be jealous for his holy name. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: After they have borne shame and all trespasses, when they dwelt safely with none making them afraid. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: When God brings them again from peoples and gathers them from enemies' lands, sanctified in them before many nations. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: Then they shall know he is the LORD their God, who led them captive among heathen but gathered them to their own land, leaving none there. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

29 COMMENTARY: Neither will he hide his face any more, for he has poured out his Spirit upon Israel, a pledge his favour will continue. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
