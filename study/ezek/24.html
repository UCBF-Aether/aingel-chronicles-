Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 24

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Again in the ninth year, tenth month, tenth day, the word of the LORD came, on the very day the siege began. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Write the name of this same day, for the king of Babylon set himself against Jerusalem this same day. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Utter a parable to the rebellious house: Set on a pot, pour water into it, Jerusalem besieged as a pot on fire. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Gather every good piece into it, thigh and shoulder, filled with choice bones, all orders and ranks within the walls as prey. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Take the choice of the flock, burn bones under it, make it boil well, and seethe its bones therein. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Woe to the bloody city, the pot whose scum is therein and not gone out, brought out piece by piece with no lot falling. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: Her blood is in her midst, set on a rock's top, not poured on ground to be covered with dust, openly crying for vengeance. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: That fury might come up to take vengeance, God set her blood on the rock's top, that it should not be covered. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Woe to the bloody city! God will even make the pile for fire great. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Heap on wood, kindle fire, consume flesh, spice it well, and let bones be burned. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: Then set the pot empty on coals, that its brass may burn, filthiness molten, and scum consumed. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: She wearied herself with lies, and her great scum went not forth, so her scum shall be in the fire. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: In her filthiness is lewdness: purged yet not purged, she shall not be purged any more until God's fury rests upon her. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: The LORD has spoken it, it shall come to pass, and he will do it, not going back, sparing, or repenting, judging according to her ways. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: Also the word of the LORD came, with a second, personal sign. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Behold, God takes away the desire of Ezekiel's eyes with a stroke, yet he must neither mourn nor weep nor let tears run down. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: Forbear to cry, make no mourning for the dead, bind his tire, wear shoes, cover not lips, eat not the bread of men, mourning kept under by religion. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: He spoke in the morning, at even his wife died, and in the morning he did as commanded. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: The people asked, Wilt thou not tell us what these things are to us, that thou doest so? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: He answered, The word of the LORD came to me, to explain the sign. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: God will profane his sanctuary, the excellency of their strength, desire of their eyes, and pity of their soul, and their left sons and daughters shall fall by the sword. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: They shall do as he has done, not covering lips nor eating the bread of men. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: Tires on heads, shoes on feet, they shall not mourn nor weep, but pine away for iniquities and mourn one toward another. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: Thus Ezekiel is a sign to them: as he has done, so shall they do, and when it comes, they shall know he is the Lord GOD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: Also, in the day God takes from them their strength, joy of glory, desire of eyes, and what their minds are set on, their sons and daughters. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: That he that escapes in that day shall come to Ezekiel, to cause him to hear it with his ears. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: In that day his mouth shall be opened to the escaped one, he shall speak and be no more dumb, a sign to them, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
