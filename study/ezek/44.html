Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 44

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Then brought back by the outward sanctuary's east gate, and it was shut. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: This gate shall be shut, not opened, no man entering by it, because Israel's God entered by it, therefore shut, for Christ alone entered heaven by his own holiness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: It is for the prince, who shall sit in it to eat bread before the LORD, entering and going out by the gate porch's way. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Brought by the north gate before the house, he looked, and the LORD's glory filled the house, and he fell on his face. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Mark well, behold with eyes, hear with ears, all ordinances and laws of God's house, marking house enterings and sanctuary goings forth. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Say to the rebellious house of Israel: let it suffice you of all your abominations. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: Ye brought into God's sanctuary strangers uncircumcised in heart and flesh, polluting his house when offering bread, fat, and blood, breaking covenant by abominations. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: Ye kept not the charge of holy things, but set keepers of God's charge in his sanctuary for yourselves. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: No stranger, uncircumcised in heart or flesh, among Israel's children, shall enter God's sanctuary. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Levites gone far from God when Israel went astray after idols shall bear their iniquity. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: Yet they shall be sanctuary ministers, in charge at house gates, ministering to the house, slaying burnt offering and sacrifice for the people, standing before them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Because they ministered before idols and caused Israel to fall into iniquity, God lifted his hand against them, and they shall bear iniquity. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: They shall not come near to do priest's office, nor near holy things in the most holy place, but bear shame and committed abominations. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: But God will make them keepers of the house's charge, for all its service and all done therein. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: But Zadok's Levite priests, who kept God's sanctuary charge when Israel went astray, shall come near to minister, standing before him to offer fat and blood. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: They shall enter his sanctuary, come near his table to minister, and keep his charge. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: Entering inner court gates, they shall be clothed with linen garments, no wool upon them while ministering in inner court gates and within. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: Linen bonnets on heads, linen breeches on loins, girded with nothing causing sweat. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: Going forth to the utter court to the people, they shall put off ministering garments, lay them in holy chambers, put on other garments, and not sanctify the people with their garments. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: Neither shave heads nor let locks grow long, only poll their heads. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: Neither shall any priest drink wine when entering the inner court. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: Neither take a widow or put-away woman for wife, but maidens of Israel's seed, or a widow who had a priest before. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: They shall teach God's people the difference between holy and profane, and cause discernment between unclean and clean. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: In controversy they shall stand in judgment, judging by God's judgments, keeping laws and statutes in all assemblies, hallowing sabbaths. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: They shall come at no dead person to defile themselves, except father, mother, son, daughter, brother, or sister who had no husband. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: After he is cleansed, seven days shall be reckoned to him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: In the day he goes into the sanctuary, to the inner court to minister, he shall offer his sin offering. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: It shall be their inheritance: God is their inheritance, no possession in Israel, God is their possession. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

29 COMMENTARY: They shall eat meat, sin, and trespass offerings, and every dedicated thing in Israel shall be theirs. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

30 COMMENTARY: First of all firstfruits and every oblation shall be the priest's, with first dough, that blessing may rest in thine house. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

31 COMMENTARY: Priests shall not eat anything dead of itself or torn, whether fowl or beast. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
