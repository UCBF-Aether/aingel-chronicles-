Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 37

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The LORD's hand carried Ezekiel in the Spirit and set him in a valley full of bones. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Passing round about, he saw very many bones in the open valley, and lo, they were very dry. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Son of man, can these bones live? He answered, O Lord GOD, thou knowest, for no created power could restore them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Prophesy upon these bones, and say, O ye dry bones, hear the word of the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: God will cause breath to enter into them, and they shall live, for God alone can cause them to live. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: He will lay sinews, bring up flesh, cover with skin, and put breath in them, and they shall live and know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: As he prophesied, there was a noise and a shaking, and bones came together, bone to his bone. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: Sinews and flesh came up and skin covered them above, but there was no breath in them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Prophesy to the wind: Come from the four winds, O breath, and breathe upon these slain, that they may live, the wind as emblem of God's Spirit and quickening power. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: The breath came into them, they lived, and stood upon their feet, an exceeding great army. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: These bones are the whole house of Israel, saying, Our bones are dried, our hope is lost, we are cut off, the vision to encourage desponding Jews. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Prophesy: God will open their graves, cause them to come up, and bring them into Israel's land, predicting restoration after captivity and recovery from long dispersion. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: They shall know he is the LORD when he opens their graves and brings them up. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: He shall put his Spirit in them, they shall live, and he shall place them in their own land, and they shall know he has spoken and performed it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD came again, with the two sticks emblem of unity. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Take one stick, write For Judah and Israel's companions, then another, For Joseph, Ephraim's stick, and all Israel's companions. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: Join them one to another into one stick, and they shall become one in his hand. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: When thy people ask, Wilt thou not show us what thou meanest by these? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: God will take Joseph's stick in Ephraim's hand and put it with Judah's stick, making one stick, one in his hand. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: The sticks whereon thou writest shall be in thine hand before their eyes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: God will take Israel's children from among the heathen, gather them on every side, and bring them into their own land. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: He will make them one nation on Israel's mountains, one king over all, no more two nations nor divided into two kingdoms. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: Neither shall they defile themselves any more with idols, detestable things, or transgressions, but God will save and cleanse them, so they shall be his people and he their God. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: David his servant shall be king over them, all having one shepherd, walking in judgments, observing statutes, and doing them, Christ the true David. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: They shall dwell in Jacob's land, wherein fathers dwelt, they, children, and children's children for ever, and David their prince for ever. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: God will make an everlasting covenant of peace, place and multiply them, and set his sanctuary among them for evermore. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: His tabernacle shall be with them, he their God, and they his people. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: Heathen shall know the LORD sanctifies Israel, when his sanctuary is among them for evermore. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
