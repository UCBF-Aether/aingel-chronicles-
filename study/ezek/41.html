Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 41

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Afterward brought to the temple, he measured its posts, six cubits broad each side, the breadth of the tabernacle. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: The door breadth ten cubits, sides five cubits each, length forty cubits, breadth twenty cubits. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Going inward, he measured the door post two cubits, the door six cubits, its breadth seven cubits. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: He measured its length twenty cubits and breadth twenty cubits, before the temple, and said, This is the most holy place. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: After he measured the house wall, six cubits, and every side chamber breadth, four cubits, round about the house. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Side chambers three, one over another, thirty in order, entering into the house wall for hold, but having no hold in the house wall itself. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: An enlarging and winding still upward to the side chambers, the house breadth increasing from lowest to highest by the midst. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: The house height round about, foundations of side chambers a full reed of six great cubits. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: The outer side-chamber wall thickness five cubits, and what was left was the place of the inner side chambers. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Between the chambers a wideness of twenty cubits round about the house on every side. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: Side chamber doors toward the place left, one north, another south, and the left place breadth five cubits round about. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: The building before the separate place at the west end, seventy cubits broad, wall five cubits thick round about, length ninety cubits. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: He measured the house a hundred cubits long, and the separate place and building with walls a hundred cubits long. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Also the breadth of the house face and separate place toward the east, a hundred cubits. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: He measured the building over against the separate place behind it, with galleries on each side, a hundred cubits, with inner temple and court porches. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Door posts, narrow windows, and galleries round about on three stories, over against the door, ceiled with wood, from ground to windows, windows covered. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: To above the door, even to the inner house and without, by all the wall round about within and without, by measure. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: It was made with cherubims and palm trees, a palm tree between cherub and cherub, every cherub having two faces. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: A man's face toward the palm tree on one side, and a young lion's face toward the palm tree on the other, made through all the house round about. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: From ground to above the door were cherubims and palm trees made, on the temple wall. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: The temple posts were squared, and the sanctuary face, appearance of one as of the other. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: The wooden altar three cubits high, length two cubits, corners, length, and walls of wood, and he said, This is the table before the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: The temple and sanctuary had two doors. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: Doors had two leaves apiece, two turning leaves, two for one door and two for the other. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: On temple doors were made cherubims and palm trees, as on walls, and thick planks upon the outer porch face. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: Narrow windows and palm trees on each side, on porch sides, upon house side chambers, and thick planks, closing the temple's measured order. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
