Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 45

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: When dividing the land by lot for inheritance, offer an oblation to the LORD, a holy portion, twenty-five thousand reeds long, ten thousand broad, holy in all borders. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Of this, for the sanctuary five hundred square, with fifty cubits suburbs round about. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Of this measure, twenty-five thousand by ten thousand, shall be the sanctuary and most holy place. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: The holy portion shall be for sanctuary priests ministering to the LORD, a place for their houses and a holy place for the sanctuary. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Twenty-five thousand by ten thousand also for Levites, house ministers, for themselves, a possession for twenty chambers. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: The city's possession, five thousand broad, twenty-five thousand long, over against the holy oblation, for the whole house of Israel. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: A portion for the prince on each side of the holy oblation and city possession, westward and eastward, length over against one portion, west to east border. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: In the land shall be his possession in Israel, and princes shall no more oppress God's people, giving the rest to Israel by tribes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Let it suffice you, O princes of Israel: remove violence and spoil, execute judgment and justice, take away exactions from God's people. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Ye shall have just balances, a just ephah, and a just bath, for princes rule with justice under Christ. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: Ephah and bath of one measure, each a tenth of a homer, measure after the homer. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: The shekel twenty gerahs, twenty, five-and-twenty, and fifteen shekels your maneh. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: This is the oblation: a sixth of an ephah of a homer of wheat, and a sixth of an ephah of a homer of barley. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Concerning oil's ordinance, a tenth of a bath out of the cor, a homer of ten baths. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: One lamb out of two hundred, from Israel's fat pastures, for meat, burnt, and peace offerings, to make reconciliation. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: All the land's people shall give this oblation for the prince in Israel. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: The prince's part is to give burnt, meat, and drink offerings in feasts, new moons, sabbaths, and all solemnities, preparing sin, meat, burnt, and peace offerings to reconcile Israel. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: In the first month, first day, take an unblemished young bullock and cleanse the sanctuary. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: The priest shall take sin-offering blood and put it on house posts, altar settle's four corners, and inner court gate posts. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: So do on the seventh day for every erring and simple one, reconciling the house. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: In the first month, fourteenth day, ye shall have the passover, a seven-day feast, unleavened bread eaten, Christ our Passover sacrificed for us. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: Upon that day the prince shall prepare for himself and all the land's people a bullock for a sin offering. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: Seven feast days he shall prepare daily a burnt offering, seven unblemished bullocks and rams, and a kid of goats daily for sin offering. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: He shall prepare a meat offering, an ephah for a bullock, an ephah for a ram, and a hin of oil for an ephah. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: In the seventh month, fifteenth day, he shall do likewise in the seven-day feast, according to sin, burnt, and meat offerings, and oil. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
