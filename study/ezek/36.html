Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 36

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Also prophesy to Israel's mountains, hear the word of the LORD, for God promises favour to his Israel and contempt turned on despisers. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Because the enemy said, Aha, even the ancient high places are ours in possession. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Therefore prophesy: because they made you desolate, swallowed you on every side to be a possession of the heathen residue, taken up in talkers' lips and an infamy. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Therefore, mountains, hills, rivers, valleys, desolate wastes, and forsaken cities, prey and derision to the heathen residue round about, hear the Lord GOD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: In jealousy's fire God has spoken against the heathen residue and all Idumea, who appointed his land their possession with joyful despiteful minds, to cast it out for prey. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Prophesy concerning Israel's land: God has spoken in jealousy and fury, because ye have borne the heathen's shame. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: God has lifted up his hand: surely the surrounding heathen shall bear their shame. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: But ye, O mountains of Israel, shall shoot forth branches and yield fruit to God's people, for they are at hand to come. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Behold, God is for you and will turn to you, and ye shall be tilled and sown. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: He will multiply men upon you, all Israel, even all of it, cities inhabited and wastes builded. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: He will multiply man and beast, increase and bring fruit, settle you after old estates, and do better than at beginnings, and ye shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: He will cause men, even his people Israel, to walk upon you, possess you as inheritance, and you shall no more bereave them of men. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: Because they say to you, Thou land devourest up men and hast bereaved thy nations. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Therefore thou shalt devour men no more, neither bereave thy nations any more. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: Neither shall heathen shame be heard in thee any more, nor people's reproach borne, nor nations caused to fall any more. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Moreover the word of the LORD came, on restoration as typical of redemption by Christ, aimed at God's glory. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: When Israel dwelt in their own land, they defiled it by their way and doings, their way before God as a removed woman's uncleanness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: Wherefore God poured fury for blood shed on the land and idols polluting it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: He scattered them among heathen and dispersed them through countries, judging according to their way and doings. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: Entering among heathen, they profaned God's holy name, when men said, These are the LORD's people, gone forth from his land. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: But God had pity for his holy name, profaned by Israel among the heathen. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: Therefore say to Israel: I do not this for your sakes, but for my holy name's sake, profaned among the heathen. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: God will sanctify his great name profaned among heathen, and heathen shall know he is the LORD when sanctified in Israel before their eyes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: For he will take them from heathen, gather them from all countries, and bring them into their own land. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: Then he will sprinkle clean water upon them, and they shall be clean, cleansed from all filthiness and idols, water as sacramental sign of the Spirit's sanctifying influence. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: A new heart will he give, a new spirit put within, taking the stony heart and giving a heart of flesh, soft and tender to God's will. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: He will put his Spirit within, as Teacher, Guide, and Sanctifier, causing them to walk in statutes and keep judgments. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: They shall dwell in the land given to fathers, be his people, and he their God. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

29 COMMENTARY: He will save them from all uncleannesses, call for corn, increase it, and lay no famine upon them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

30 COMMENTARY: He will multiply tree fruit and field increase, that they receive no more famine reproach among heathen. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

31 COMMENTARY: Then they shall remember evil ways and not-good doings, and loathe themselves for iniquities and abominations. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

32 COMMENTARY: Not for your sakes do I this, be it known: be ashamed and confounded for your own ways, O house of Israel. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

33 COMMENTARY: In the day he cleanses them from all iniquities, he will cause them to dwell in cities, and wastes shall be builded. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

34 COMMENTARY: The desolate land shall be tilled, whereas it lay desolate before all passers-by. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

35 COMMENTARY: They shall say, This desolate land is become like Eden's garden, and waste, desolate, ruined cities are fenced and inhabited. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

36 COMMENTARY: Then surrounding heathen left shall know the LORD builds ruined places and plants the desolate, for he has spoken and will do it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

37 COMMENTARY: Yet for this God will be inquired of by Israel to do it for them, increasing them with men like a flock. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

38 COMMENTARY: As the holy flock, as Jerusalem's flock in solemn feasts, so waste cities shall be filled with flocks of men, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
