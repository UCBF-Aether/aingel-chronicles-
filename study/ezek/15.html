Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 15

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The word of the LORD comes with a similitude applied to Jerusalem, an unfruitful vine. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: What is the vine tree more than any tree, or a branch among forest trees, if it bears no fruit? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Shall wood be taken from it for any work, or a pin to hang a vessel on? No, for fruit is the vine's sole use. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Cast into the fire for fuel, both ends devoured and the midst burned, is it meet for any work? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: When whole it was meet for no work, how much less when fire has devoured and burned it? — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: As the vine among forest trees given to fire for fuel, so God will give Jerusalem's inhabitants. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: God will set his face against them, going out from one fire only to be devoured by another, and they shall know he is the LORD. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: God will make the land desolate, because they have committed a trespass, an unfruitful profession ending in destruction. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
