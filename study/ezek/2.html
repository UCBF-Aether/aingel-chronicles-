Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 2

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Called son of man, a weak mortal creature, Ezekiel is told to stand, a posture of readiness for the business God gives him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Having no strength of his own, the Spirit entered him and set him on his feet, for God works in us whatever he requires of us. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: He is sent to Israel, a rebellious nation whose fathers and they themselves have transgressed against God to this very day. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: To impudent, stiffhearted children he must say, Thus saith the Lord GOD, speaking God's words whether they are welcome or not. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Whether they hear or forbear, they shall know a prophet has been among them, and God's word will be made honourable either way. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: He must not fear their words or looks, though briers and thorns and scorpions surround him, for wicked men are near to cursing and burning. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: He must speak God's words whether they hear or forbear, faithful to their souls, for they are most rebellious. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: He himself must not be rebellious like that rebellious house, but open his mouth and eat what God gives him, obeying God's voice first. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: A hand was sent to him holding a roll of a book, the written message he was to receive and deliver. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: The roll, written within and without with lamentations, mourning, and woe, shows God's word is filled with woe to impenitent sinners. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
