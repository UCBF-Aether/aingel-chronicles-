Study Notes — Ezekiel

Chapter 43

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Afterward he brought him to the east gate, after surveying the temple, to see God's glory return. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Behold, Israel's God's glory came from the east, voice like many waters, earth shining with his glory. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: It was according to the vision when he came to destroy the city, and like the Chebar vision, and he fell on his face. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: The LORD's glory came into the house by the east gate's way. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: The Spirit took him into the inner court, and behold, the LORD's glory filled the house. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: He heard him speaking from the house, and the man stood by him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: Son of man, this is my throne's place and my feet's soles' place, where I will dwell amid Israel for ever, and my holy name Israel shall no more defile, neither they nor kings, by whoredom or kings' carcases in high places. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: Setting their threshold by God's thresholds and post by posts, with only a wall between, they defiled his holy name by abominations, wherefore he consumed them in anger. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Now let them put away whoredom and kings' carcases far from him, and he will dwell among them for ever. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Show the house to Israel, that they may be ashamed of iniquities, and let them measure the pattern, for shown grace makes us ashamed for sins. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: If ashamed of all they have done, show them the house's form, fashion, goings out, comings in, forms, ordinances, and laws, written in their sight, that they may keep and do them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: This is the house's law: upon the mountain top its whole limit round about shall be most holy. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: These are the altar's measures: a cubit and handbreadth, bottom a cubit, breadth a cubit, border a span round about, this the altar's higher place. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: From ground bottom to lower settle two cubits, breadth one cubit, from lesser to greater settle four cubits, breadth one cubit. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: So the altar shall be four cubits, and from the altar upward four horns. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: The altar twelve cubits long, twelve broad, square in its four squares. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: The settle fourteen cubits long and broad in four squares, border half a cubit, bottom a cubit about, stairs toward the east. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: These are the altar's ordinances in the day they make it, to offer burnt offerings and sprinkle blood thereon. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: Give to Zadok's Levite priests, approaching to minister, a young bullock for a sin offering. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: Take its blood and put it on the four horns, four settle corners, and border round about, thus cleansing and purging the altar. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: Take the sin-offering bullock and burn it in the house's appointed place, without the sanctuary. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: On the second day offer an unblemished kid of goats for a sin offering, cleansing the altar as with the bullock. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: When cleansing ends, offer an unblemished young bullock and ram from the flock. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: Offer them before the LORD, priests casting salt upon them, offering them as burnt offering, for services are accepted only as sprinkled with cleansing blood. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: Seven days prepare daily a goat for sin offering, with unblemished bullock and ram. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: Seven days they shall purge and purify the altar and consecrate themselves. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: When these days expire, from the eighth day forward priests shall make burnt and peace offerings on the altar, and God will accept them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
