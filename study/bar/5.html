Study Notes — Baruch

Chapter 5

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

2 COMMENTARY: The garment is in the Hebrew a cloak, such as were worn by people under no disgrace, as Thamar's costly robe, and justice or mercy means God is bound to fulfil his gracious promises, so the double garment is righteousness that comes from God. — George Leo Haydock

3 COMMENTARY: His is in the Greek thy, and Christ, the sun of justice, would effect this showing of brightness to every one under heaven, so the glory shown in Jerusalem is the brightness of the Sun of justice. — George Leo Haydock

4 COMMENTARY: Piety is the distinction Jerusalem shall bear after the captivity, but still more when Christ shall appear and give peace to his Church after the ages of persecution, so the name, peace of justice and honour of piety, looks beyond the return. — George Leo Haydock

5 COMMENTARY: Arise repeats the earlier call to stand on high, and God means they remember his law with pleasure, so the gathering from east to west is a gathering of those who rejoice in remembrance of God. — George Leo Haydock

6 COMMENTARY: The children are in the Greek the throne, and the return of the captives was a triumph, as Isaias describes, with abundance of horses, and Darius giving a convoy of a thousand horsemen, so the exalted return is a royal procession. — George Leo Haydock

7 COMMENTARY: Diligently means without stumbling under the pillar of God's protection, as the way is made straight in Isaias, so the levelled mountains and filled valleys make a road on which Israel walks without stumbling. — George Leo Haydock

8 COMMENTARY: The woods are a poetical description, for the returning people shall pass through the sands of Arabia as if under delightful shade, since the trees overshadow Israel by God's commandment. — George Leo Haydock

No note for verse(s) 1, 9 — the public-domain sources consulted give no distinct explanation for these verses beyond the list itself, so nothing was invented for them.
