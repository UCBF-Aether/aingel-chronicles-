Study Notes — Sirach

Chapter 49

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The remembrance of Josias is like the composition of the perfume made by the art of the apothecary; it is sweet as honey in all mouths, and as music at a banquet of wine. Josiah's remembrance is like apothecary's perfume, sweet as honey in every mouth and like music at a wine banquet. — W. O. E. Oesterley

2 COMMENTARY: He behaved himself uprightly in the conversion of the people, and took away the abominations of iniquity. Josiah acted uprightly in converting the people, removing iniquity's abominations. — W. O. E. Oesterley

3 COMMENTARY: He directed his heart to the Lord, and in the time of the ungodly he established the worship of God. Josiah directed his heart to God and established divine worship in ungodly times. — W. O. E. Oesterley

4 COMMENTARY: All, except David, Ezekias, and Josias, were defective, for they forsook the law of the Most High, even the kings of Judah failed. All kings except David, Hezekiah, and Josiah were defective, forsaking God's law; Judah's kings failed. — W. O. E. Oesterley

5 COMMENTARY: Therefore he gave their power to others, and their glory to a strange nation. Their power was given to others, and their glory to a strange nation. — W. O. E. Oesterley

6 COMMENTARY: They burnt the chosen city of the sanctuary, and made the streets desolate, according to the prophecy of Jeremias. The chosen sanctuary city was burnt and its streets made desolate, as Jeremiah prophesied. — W. O. E. Oesterley

7 COMMENTARY: For they entreated him evil, who nevertheless was a prophet, sanctified in his mother's womb, that he might root out, afflict, and destroy, and that he might build up also, and plant. Jeremiah, ill-treated though sanctified from his mother's womb, was appointed to root out, afflict, destroy, and also to build and plant. — W. O. E. Oesterley

8 COMMENTARY: It was Ezekiel who saw the glorious vision, shown him upon the chariot of the cherubims. Ezekiel saw the glorious vision upon the cherubim's chariot. — W. O. E. Oesterley

9 COMMENTARY: For he made mention of the enemies under the figure of the rain, and directed those who went right. Ezekiel mentioned enemies under rain's figure, and directed the upright. — W. O. E. Oesterley

10 COMMENTARY: Of the twelve prophets let the memorial be blessed, and let their bones flourish again out of their place, for they comforted Jacob, and delivered them by assured hope. The twelve prophets' memorial is blessed, their bones flourishing again, since they comforted Jacob and delivered by assured hope. — W. O. E. Oesterley

11 COMMENTARY: How shall we magnify Zorobabel? Even he was as a signet on the right hand. Zerubbabel's magnification is as a signet on the right hand. — W. O. E. Oesterley

12 COMMENTARY: So was Jesus son of Josedec, who in their time built the house, and set up a holy temple to the Lord, which was prepared for everlasting glory. Joshua son of Josedech, with Zerubbabel, built the house and set up a holy temple prepared for everlasting glory. — W. O. E. Oesterley

13 COMMENTARY: Among the elect was Neemias, whose renown is great, who raised up for us the walls that were fallen, set up the gates and bars, and raised up our ruins again. Nehemiah, among the elect of great renown, raised fallen walls, set gates and bars, and restored ruins. — W. O. E. Oesterley

14 COMMENTARY: But upon the earth was no man created like Enoch, for he was taken from the earth. No man on earth was created like Enoch, for he was taken from the earth. — W. O. E. Oesterley

15 COMMENTARY: Neither was there a young man born like Joseph, a governor of his brethren, a stay of the people, whose bones were regarded of the Lord. No young man was born like Joseph, governor of his brethren, stay of the people, whose bones God regarded. — W. O. E. Oesterley

16 COMMENTARY: Sem and Seth were in great honour among men, and so was Adam above every living thing in creation. Shem and Seth were greatly honoured among men, and Adam above every living thing in creation. — W. O. E. Oesterley
