Study Notes — Sirach

Chapter 15

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: He who fears the Lord will do good, and he who has the knowledge of the law will obtain her. Fear of God produces good deeds, and knowledge of the law obtains wisdom. — W. O. E. Oesterley

2 COMMENTARY: As a mother she will meet him, and receive him as a wife married of a virgin. Wisdom meets her seeker with a mother's welcome and a bride's faithfulness. — W. O. E. Oesterley

3 COMMENTARY: With the bread of understanding she will feed him, and give him the water of wisdom to drink. Wisdom nourishes like bread of understanding and water of wisdom, food and drink for the mind. — W. O. E. Oesterley

4 COMMENTARY: He will be stayed upon her and will not be moved; he will rely upon her and will not be confounded. Leaning on wisdom gives stability and reliance without confusion. — W. O. E. Oesterley

5 COMMENTARY: She will exalt him above his neighbours, and in the midst of the congregation she will open his mouth. Wisdom exalts above neighbours and opens the mouth in public assembly, giving a voice among the people. — W. O. E. Oesterley

6 COMMENTARY: He will find joy and a crown of gladness, and she will cause him to inherit an everlasting name. Wisdom's portion is joy, a crown of gladness, and a name that lasts for ever. — W. O. E. Oesterley

7 COMMENTARY: But foolish men will not attain to her, and sinners will not see her. Fools cannot reach wisdom, and sinners cannot even see her. — W. O. E. Oesterley

8 COMMENTARY: She is far from pride, and men who are liars cannot remember her. Wisdom stands far from pride, and liars cannot hold her in memory. — W. O. E. Oesterley

9 COMMENTARY: Praise is not seemly in the mouth of a sinner, for it was not sent him of the Lord. A sinner's mouth is no fit place for praise, since praise was not given to him by the Lord. — W. O. E. Oesterley

10 COMMENTARY: Praise will be uttered in wisdom, and the Lord will prosper it. True praise is spoken in wisdom, and the Lord makes such praise prosper. — W. O. E. Oesterley

11 COMMENTARY: Do not say, it is through the Lord that I fell away, for you ought not to do the things he hates. Falling away must not be blamed on God, since the fallen things are what he hates. — W. O. E. Oesterley

12 COMMENTARY: Do not say, he has caused me to err, for he has no need of the sinful man. God does not cause error, and he has no need of a sinful man to serve his purposes. — W. O. E. Oesterley

13 COMMENTARY: The Lord hates all abomination, and those who fear God do not love it. God hates every abomination, and the God-fearing share that hatred rather than loving it. — W. O. E. Oesterley

14 COMMENTARY: He himself made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his counsel. Man was made by God and left to his own counsel, free to choose. — W. O. E. Oesterley

15 COMMENTARY: If you will, keep the commandments, and perform acceptable faithfulness. Keeping the commandments is a matter of will, and faithfulness so performed is acceptable. — W. O. E. Oesterley

16 COMMENTARY: He has set fire and water before you: stretch forth your hand to whichever you will. Fire and water are set before man as the great alternatives, and the hand chooses. — W. O. E. Oesterley

17 COMMENTARY: Before man is life and death, and whichever he likes will be given him. Life and death lie before man, and his own liking decides which is given. — W. O. E. Oesterley

18 COMMENTARY: The wisdom of the Lord is great, and he is mighty in power, and beholds all things. God's wisdom is great, his power mighty, and nothing escapes his sight. — W. O. E. Oesterley

19 COMMENTARY: His eyes are upon those who fear him, and he knows every work of man. God's eyes watch the God-fearing, and he knows every human work. — W. O. E. Oesterley

20 COMMENTARY: He has commanded no man to do wickedly, neither has he given any man licence to sin. God never commands wickedness nor licenses sin; sin is man's own choice, not God's order. — W. O. E. Oesterley
