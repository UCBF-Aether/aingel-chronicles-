Study Notes — Deuteronomy

Chapter 7

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: When the Lord brings you into the land you are going to possess, and has cast out many nations before you, the Hittites, Girgashites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, seven nations greater and mightier than you. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: And when the Lord delivers them before you, you shall smite them and utterly destroy them, making no covenant with them nor showing them mercy; this strict order was limited to these nations and is no precedent for later ages. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Neither shall you make marriages with them, giving your daughter to his son nor taking his daughter for your son. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: For they will turn away your son from following me, that they may serve other gods, and so the anger of the Lord will be kindled against you and destroy you suddenly; thousands, Henry warns, have been undone by ungodly marriages. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: But thus shall you deal with them: destroy their altars, break down their images, cut down their groves, and burn their graven images with fire, dealing decidedly with the instruments of idolatry. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: For you are a holy people to the Lord your God; the Lord has chosen you to be a special people to himself, above all people on the face of the earth. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: The Lord did not set his love upon you nor choose you because you were more in number than any people, for you were the fewest of all people. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: But because the Lord loved you, and because he would keep the oath sworn to your fathers, he brought you out with a mighty hand and redeemed you from the house of bondmen, from Pharaoh king of Egypt. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: Know therefore that the Lord your God, he is God, the faithful God, who keeps covenant and mercy with those who love him and keep his commandments to a thousand generations. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: And repays those who hate him to their face, to destroy them; he will not be slack to him who hates him, but will repay him to his face. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: You shall therefore keep the commandments, statutes, and judgments which I command you this day, to do them. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: Wherefore if you hearken to these judgments and keep and do them, the Lord your God shall keep to you the covenant and mercy which he swore to your fathers. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: And he will love you, bless you, and multiply you, blessing the fruit of your womb, the fruit of your land, corn, wine, oil, the increase of cattle and flocks, in the land sworn to your fathers. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: You shall be blessed above all people; there shall not be male or female barren among you, or among your cattle. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: And the Lord will take away from you all sickness, and will put none of the evil diseases of Egypt, which you know, upon you, but will lay them upon all who hate you; diseases are God's servants, going where he sends them. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: And you shall consume all the people which the Lord your God shall deliver to you; your eye shall have no pity upon them, nor shall you serve their gods, for that would be a snare to you. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: If you say in your heart, These nations are more than I; how can I dispossess them? the question of fear is answered by remembrance, not by counting armies. — Matthew Henry

18 COMMENTARY: You shall not be afraid of them, but shall well remember what the Lord did to Pharaoh and to all Egypt. — Matthew Henry

19 COMMENTARY: The great temptations which your eyes saw, the signs, wonders, mighty hand, and stretched-out arm, by which the Lord brought you out, are the pattern for what he will do to all the peoples you fear. — Matthew Henry

20 COMMENTARY: Moreover the Lord will send the hornet among them, until those left and hiding themselves from you are destroyed, God fighting by means beyond human armies. — Matthew Henry

21 COMMENTARY: You shall not be affrighted at them, for the Lord your God is among you, a mighty God and terrible. — Matthew Henry

22 COMMENTARY: And the Lord will put out those nations before you by little and little; you may not consume them at once, lest the beasts of the field increase upon you, and because sanctification itself proceeds gradually. — Matthew Henry

23 COMMENTARY: But the Lord shall deliver them to you, and destroy them with a mighty destruction until they are destroyed, in his own method and time, which are always best. — Matthew Henry

24 COMMENTARY: And he shall deliver their kings into your hand, and you shall destroy their name from under heaven; no one shall be able to stand before you until you have destroyed them. — Matthew Henry

25 COMMENTARY: The graven images of their gods you shall burn with fire; you shall not desire the silver or gold on them, nor take it to yourself, lest you be snared in it, for it is an abomination to the Lord. — Matthew Henry

26 COMMENTARY: Neither shall you bring an abomination into your house, lest you be a cursed thing like it, but you shall utterly detest it and utterly abhor it, for it is a cursed thing; whatever brings us into a snare brings us under a curse. — Matthew Henry
