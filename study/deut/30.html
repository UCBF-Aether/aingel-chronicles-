Study Notes — Deuteronomy

Chapter 30

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: And it shall come to pass, when all these things are come upon you, the blessing and the curse, which I have set before you, and you shall call them to mind among all the nations, where the Lord your God has driven you. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: And shall return to the Lord your God, and obey his voice according to all that I command you this day, you and your children, with all your heart, and with all your soul. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: That then the Lord your God will turn your captivity, and have compassion upon you, and will return and gather you from all the nations, where the Lord your God has scattered you; JFB notes this promise looks beyond the Babylonian return, to a full restoration when hearts are circumcised to love the Lord. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: If any of yours are driven out to the outmost parts of heaven, from there will the Lord your God gather you, and from there will he fetch you. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: And the Lord your God will bring you into the land which your fathers possessed, and you shall possess it, and he will do you good, and multiply you above your fathers. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: And the Lord your God will circumcise your heart, and the heart of your seed, to love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, that you may live; by the Word and Spirit, hearts are touched and purified from superstition and unbelief. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: And the Lord your God will put all these curses upon your enemies, and on those who hate you, who persecuted you. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: And you shall return and obey the voice of the Lord, and do all his commandments which I command you this day. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: And the Lord your God will make you plenteous in every work of your hand, in the fruit of your body, and in the fruit of your cattle, and in the fruit of your land, for good, for the Lord will again rejoice over you for good, as he rejoiced over your fathers. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: If you shall hearken to the voice of the Lord your God, to keep his commandments and statutes written in this book of the law, and if you turn to the Lord with all your heart, and with all your soul. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: For this commandment which I command you this day, it is not hidden from you, neither is it far off; the law of loving and obeying God was well known to Israel, and they could not plead ignorance of it. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: It is not in heaven, that you should say, Who shall go up for us to heaven, and bring it to us, that we may hear it, and do it? It was not concealed as an impenetrable mystery in heaven, for it had been revealed. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: Neither is it beyond the sea, that you should say, Who shall go over the sea for us, and bring it to us, that we may hear it, and do it? No long journey or distant voyage was needed, as ancient sages undertook in quest of knowledge. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: But the word is very near to you, in your mouth, and in your heart, that you may do it; it was a subject of common conversation, engraven on their memories, and frequently inculcated on their hearts, and Paul applies this passage to the gospel, whose law is substantially the same, only more clearly exhibited. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

15 COMMENTARY: See, I have set before you this day life and good, and death and evil, the alternative of a good and happy, or a disobedient and miserable life. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

16 COMMENTARY: In that I command you this day to love the Lord your God, to walk in his ways, and to keep his commandments and statutes and judgments, that you may live and multiply, and the Lord your God shall bless you in the land where you go to possess it; love of God and compliance with his will are the only ways of securing the blessings and avoiding the evils. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

17 COMMENTARY: But if your heart turns away, so that you will not hear, but shall be drawn away, and worship other gods, and serve them. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

18 COMMENTARY: I denounce to you this day, that you shall surely perish, and that you shall not prolong your days upon the land, where you pass over Jordan to go to possess it. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

19 COMMENTARY: I call heaven and earth to record this day against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing, therefore choose life, that both you and your seed may live. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

20 COMMENTARY: That you may love the Lord your God, and that you may obey his voice, and that you may cleave to him, for he is your life, and the length of your days, that you may dwell in the land which the Lord swore to your fathers, to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, to give them; Moses urges the choice with solemn earnestness, as Paul did to the elders of Ephesus. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
