Study Notes — Deuteronomy

Chapter 12

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: These are the statutes and judgments which you shall observe to do in the land which the Lord God of your fathers gives you to possess, all the days you live upon the earth, beginning with laws of worship. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: You shall utterly destroy all the places where the nations you shall possess served their gods, upon the high mountains, hills, and under every green tree. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: And you shall overthrow their altars, break their pillars, burn their groves with fire, hew down the graven images of their gods, and destroy their names out of that place. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: You shall not do so to the Lord your God, bringing idolaters' rites into his worship under colour of making it better; we cannot serve God and idols together. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: But to the place which the Lord shall choose out of all your tribes to put his name there, even to his habitation shall you seek, and there you shall come. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: And there you shall bring your burnt offerings, sacrifices, tithes, heave offerings, vows, freewill offerings, and firstlings of herds and flocks. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: And there you shall eat before the Lord your God, and rejoice in all that you put your hand to, you and your households, in which the Lord has blessed you; religion's services are to be a pleasure, not a drudgery. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: You shall not do after all the things that we do here this day, every man whatever is right in his own eyes, the unsettled worship of the wilderness being no pattern for the settled land. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: For you are not as yet come to the rest and to the inheritance which the Lord your God gives you. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: But when you go over Jordan, and dwell in the land which the Lord gives you to inherit, and he gives you rest from all your enemies round about, so that you dwell in safety. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: Then there shall be a place which the Lord shall choose to cause his name to dwell there, and there you shall bring all that I command you, your offerings, sacrifices, tithes, and vows. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: And you shall rejoice before the Lord your God, you, your sons and daughters, menservants and maidservants, and the Levite within your gates, who has no part nor inheritance with you. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: Take heed to yourself that you offer not your burnt offerings in every place that you see, but only where God appoints. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: But in the place which the Lord shall choose in one of your tribes, there you shall offer your burnt offerings, and there you shall do all that I command you. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: Notwithstanding, you may kill and eat flesh in all your gates, whatever your soul lusts after, according to the blessing of the Lord, which he has given you; the unclean and the clean may eat of it, as of the roebuck and the hart. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: Only you shall not eat the blood; you shall pour it upon the earth as water. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: You may not eat within your gates the tithe of your corn, wine, or oil, or the firstlings of herds and flocks, nor any vow, freewill offering, or heave offering of your hand. — Matthew Henry

18 COMMENTARY: But you must eat them before the Lord your God in the place which the Lord shall choose, you, your son and daughter, manservant and maidservant, and the Levite within your gates, rejoicing before the Lord in all you put your hand to. — Matthew Henry

19 COMMENTARY: Take heed to yourself that you forsake not the Levite as long as you live upon the earth; it is the people's duty to be kind to ministers who teach them well. — Matthew Henry

20 COMMENTARY: When the Lord enlarges your border, as he promised, and you say, I will eat flesh, because your soul longs to eat flesh, you may eat flesh, whatever your soul lusts after. — Matthew Henry

21 COMMENTARY: If the place which the Lord has chosen to put his name there is too far from you, then you shall kill of your herd and flock, which the Lord has given you, as I commanded, and eat in your gates whatever your soul lusts after. — Matthew Henry

22 COMMENTARY: Even as the roebuck and the hart is eaten, so you shall eat them; the unclean and the clean shall eat of them alike, the common meal being distinguished from the sanctuary meal. — Matthew Henry

23 COMMENTARY: Only be sure that you eat not the blood, for the blood is the life, and you may not eat the life with the flesh. — Matthew Henry

24 COMMENTARY: You shall not eat it; you shall pour it upon the earth as water, keeping the sign of life sacred to God. — Matthew Henry

25 COMMENTARY: You shall not eat it, that it may go well with you and your children after you, when you do what is right in the sight of the Lord. — Matthew Henry

26 COMMENTARY: Only your holy things which you have, and your vows, you shall take, and go to the place which the Lord shall choose. — Matthew Henry

27 COMMENTARY: And you shall offer your burnt offerings, the flesh and the blood, upon the altar of the Lord, and the blood of your sacrifices shall be poured out upon the altar, and you shall eat the flesh. — Matthew Henry

28 COMMENTARY: Observe and hear all these words which I command you, that it may go well with you and your children after you for ever, when you do what is good and right in the sight of the Lord. — Matthew Henry

29 COMMENTARY: When the Lord cuts off the nations from before you, where you go to possess them, and you succeed them and dwell in their land. — Matthew Henry

30 COMMENTARY: Take heed to yourself that you be not snared by following them, after they are destroyed from before you, and that you enquire not after their gods, saying, How did these nations serve their gods? Even so will I do likewise. — Matthew Henry

31 COMMENTARY: You shall not do so to the Lord your God, for every abomination to the Lord, which he hates, have they done to their gods, even burning their sons and daughters in the fire to their gods. — Matthew Henry

32 COMMENTARY: What thing soever I command you, observe to do it; you shall not add to it, nor diminish from it, the law being complete as God gave it. — Matthew Henry
