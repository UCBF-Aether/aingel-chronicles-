Study Notes — Deuteronomy

Chapter 6

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Now these are the commandments, statutes, and judgments which the Lord commanded Moses to teach them, that they might do them in the land they were going to possess; commandments denote the moral law, statutes the ceremonial, judgments the civil decisions. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: That you might fear the Lord your God, to keep all his statutes and commandments, you and your son and your son's son, all the days of your life, and that your days may be prolonged. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Hear therefore, O Israel, and observe to do it, that it may be well with you, and that you may increase mightily in the land flowing with milk and honey, as the Lord God of your fathers promised. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Hear, O Israel: the Lord our God is one Lord, a brief summary of religion: Jehovah is the only living and true God, and he is but one. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: And you shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, and might, the first and great commandment, love sincere, strong, superlative, intelligent, and entire, giving him your all. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: And these words which I command you this day shall be in your heart, laid up by meditation so that your thoughts are daily employed about them. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: And you shall teach them diligently to your children, talking of them when you sit in your house, walk by the way, lie down, and rise up, with careful and exact religious education. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: And you shall bind them for a sign upon your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes, making the word familiar and ready to use, to restrain from sin and direct in duty. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: And you shall write them upon the posts of your house and on your gates, owning your religion openly and keeping it constantly before your household. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: And when the Lord brings you into the land sworn to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, with great and goodly cities you did not build. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: And houses full of all good things you did not fill, wells dug you did not dig, vineyards and olive trees you did not plant, and you shall have eaten and be full. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: Then beware lest you forget the Lord who brought you out of Egypt, from the house of bondage; prosperity is the time when people grow secure and unmindful of the Giver. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: You shall fear the Lord your God, and serve him, and swear by his name, giving him the worship and the oath that belong to him alone. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: You shall not go after other gods, of the gods of the peoples round about you, exchanging the one Lord for the idols of neighbours. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: For the Lord your God is a jealous God among you, lest his anger be kindled against you and destroy you from off the face of the earth. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: You shall not tempt the Lord your God, as you tempted him in Massah, neither by despairing of his power while keeping in the way of duty, nor by presuming on it when turning aside. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: You shall diligently keep the commandments of the Lord, his testimonies, and his statutes, which he has commanded you; negligence will ruin us, but diligence is our life. — Matthew Henry

18 COMMENTARY: And you shall do what is right and good in the sight of the Lord, that it may be well with you, and you may go in and possess the good land sworn to your fathers. — Matthew Henry

19 COMMENTARY: To cast out all your enemies from before you, as the Lord has spoken, the promised possession following obedient conduct. — Matthew Henry

20 COMMENTARY: And when your son asks in time to come, What mean the testimonies, statutes, and judgments which the Lord our God has commanded you? the question itself is part of the teaching office of parents. — Matthew Henry

21 COMMENTARY: Then you shall say to your son, We were Pharaoh's bondmen in Egypt, and the Lord brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand. — Matthew Henry

22 COMMENTARY: And the Lord showed signs and wonders, great and sore, upon Egypt, upon Pharaoh, and upon all his household, before our eyes. — Matthew Henry

23 COMMENTARY: And he brought us out from there, that he might bring us in, to give us the land which he swore to our fathers. — Matthew Henry

24 COMMENTARY: And the Lord commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear the Lord our God, for our good always, that he might preserve us alive, as it is this day. — Matthew Henry

25 COMMENTARY: And it shall be our righteousness, if we observe to do all these commandments before the Lord our God, as he has commanded us; it is only through the Mediator, Henry notes, that we can be righteous before God, and the law shows sinful man his need of a Saviour. — Matthew Henry
