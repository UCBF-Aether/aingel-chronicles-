Study Notes — Deuteronomy

Chapter 28

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: And it shall come to pass, if you hearken diligently to the voice of the Lord, to observe and do all his commandments commanded this day, that the Lord will set you on high above all nations of the earth; this chapter is a large exposition of two words, blessing and curse, blessings being put first, for God is slow to anger and swift to show mercy. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: And all these blessings shall come on you, and overtake you, if you hearken to the voice of the Lord your God. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Blessed shall you be in the city, and blessed shall you be in the field, blessing covering town and country alike. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Blessed shall be the fruit of your body, and the fruit of your ground, and the fruit of your cattle, the increase of your kine, and the flocks of your sheep. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: Blessed shall be your basket and your store, provision in the house and in the field. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: Blessed shall you be when you come in, and blessed shall you be when you go out, blessing on every journey and return. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall cause your enemies who rise up against you to be smitten before your face; they shall come out against you one way, and flee before you seven ways. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall command the blessing upon you in your storehouses, and in all that you set your hand to, and he shall bless you in the land which the Lord gives you. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall establish you a holy people to himself, as he has sworn to you, if you keep his commandments and walk in his ways. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: And all people of the earth shall see that you are called by the name of the Lord, and they shall be afraid of you. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: And the Lord shall make you plenteous in goods, in the fruit of your body, ground, and cattle, in the land sworn to your fathers to give you. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall open to you his good treasure, the heaven to give rain to your land in its season, and to bless all the work of your hand, and you shall lend to many nations, and shall not borrow. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: And the Lord shall make you the head, and not the tail, and you shall be above only, and not beneath, if you hearken to his commandments, to observe and do them. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: And you shall not go aside from any of the words commanded this day, to the right hand or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: But it shall come to pass, if you will not hearken to the voice of the Lord, to observe and do all his commandments and statutes commanded this day, that all these curses shall come upon you, and overtake you; disobedience brings not only loss of blessing but the curse, which includes all misery as the blessing includes all happiness. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: Cursed shall you be in the city, and cursed shall you be in the field, the mirror image of the blessing, wherever the sinner goes the curse follows. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: Cursed shall be your basket and your store. — Matthew Henry

18 COMMENTARY: Cursed shall be the fruit of your body, and the fruit of your land, the increase of your kine, and the flocks of your sheep. — Matthew Henry

19 COMMENTARY: Cursed shall you be when you come in, and cursed shall you be when you go out. — Matthew Henry

20 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall send upon you cursing, vexation, and rebuke, in all that you set your hand to do, until you are destroyed, and until you perish quickly, because of the wickedness of your doings, by which you have forsaken me. — Matthew Henry

21 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall make the pestilence cleave to you, until he has consumed you from off the land you go to possess. — Matthew Henry

22 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall smite you with consumption, fever, inflammation, extreme burning, the sword, blasting, and mildew, and they shall pursue you until you perish. — Matthew Henry

23 COMMENTARY: And your heaven over your head shall be brass, and the earth under you shall be iron, rain withheld and ground unyielding. — Matthew Henry

24 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall make the rain of your land powder and dust, coming down from heaven upon you until you are destroyed. — Matthew Henry

25 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall cause you to be smitten before your enemies; you shall go out one way against them, and flee seven ways before them, and shall be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth. — Matthew Henry

26 COMMENTARY: And your carcase shall be meat to all fowls of the air, and beasts of the earth, and no man shall frighten them away. — Matthew Henry

27 COMMENTARY: The Lord will smite you with the botch of Egypt, emerods, scab, and itch, whereof you cannot be healed. — Matthew Henry

28 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall smite you with madness, blindness, and astonishment of heart. — Matthew Henry

29 COMMENTARY: And you shall grope at noonday, as the blind gropes in darkness, and shall not prosper in your ways, and shall be only oppressed and spoiled evermore, and no man shall save you; those who walk by sight, not faith, are in danger of losing reason itself when everything looks frightful. — Matthew Henry

30 COMMENTARY: You shall betroth a wife, and another man shall lie with her; you shall build a house, and not dwell in it; you shall plant a vineyard, and not gather its grapes. — Matthew Henry

31 COMMENTARY: Your ox shall be slain before your eyes, and you shall not eat of it; your ass shall be violently taken away from before your face, and shall not be restored; your sheep shall be given to your enemies, and you shall have none to rescue them. — Matthew Henry

32 COMMENTARY: Your sons and daughters shall be given to another people, and your eyes shall look and fail with longing for them all the day long, and there shall be no might in your hand. — Matthew Henry

33 COMMENTARY: The fruit of your land, and all your labours, shall a nation which you know not eat up, and you shall be only oppressed and crushed always. — Matthew Henry

34 COMMENTARY: So that you shall be mad for the sight of your eyes which you shall see, the afflictions themselves driving the sufferers to distraction. — Matthew Henry

35 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall smite you in the knees and legs, with a sore botch that cannot be healed, from the sole of your foot to the top of your head. — Matthew Henry

36 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall bring you, and your king whom you shall set over you, to a nation which neither you nor your fathers have known, and there you shall serve other gods, wood and stone. — Matthew Henry

37 COMMENTARY: And you shall become an astonishment, a proverb, and a byword, among all nations where the Lord shall lead you. — Matthew Henry

38 COMMENTARY: You shall carry much seed out into the field, and gather but little in, for the locust shall consume it. — Matthew Henry

39 COMMENTARY: You shall plant vineyards, and dress them, but shall neither drink of the wine, nor gather the grapes, for the worms shall eat them. — Matthew Henry

40 COMMENTARY: You shall have olive trees throughout all your coasts, but shall not anoint yourself with oil, for your olive shall cast its fruit. — Matthew Henry

41 COMMENTARY: You shall beget sons and daughters, but shall not enjoy them, for they shall go into captivity. — Matthew Henry

42 COMMENTARY: All your trees and fruit of your land shall the locust consume. — Matthew Henry

43 COMMENTARY: The stranger within you shall get up above you very high, and you shall come down very low. — Matthew Henry

44 COMMENTARY: He shall lend to you, and you shall not lend to him; he shall be the head, and you shall be the tail, the reversal of the blessing of verse 13. — Matthew Henry

45 COMMENTARY: Moreover all these curses shall come upon you, and pursue and overtake you, till you are destroyed, because you hearkened not to the voice of the Lord, to keep his commandments and statutes which he commanded you. — Matthew Henry

46 COMMENTARY: And they shall be upon you for a sign and for a wonder, and upon your seed for ever, a lasting testimony to the justice of the curse, which is not causeless or for a light cause. — Matthew Henry

47 COMMENTARY: Because you served not the Lord your God with joyfulness, and with gladness of heart, for the abundance of all things. — Matthew Henry

48 COMMENTARY: Therefore you shall serve your enemies whom the Lord shall send against you, in hunger, thirst, nakedness, and want of all things, and he shall put a yoke of iron upon your neck, until he has destroyed you; if they would not serve God with cheerfulness, they should be compelled to serve their enemies. — Matthew Henry

49 COMMENTARY: The Lord shall bring a nation against you from far, from the end of the earth, as swift as the eagle flies, a nation whose tongue you shall not understand. — Matthew Henry

50 COMMENTARY: A nation of fierce countenance, which shall not regard the person of the old, nor show favour to the young. — Matthew Henry

51 COMMENTARY: And he shall eat the fruit of your cattle, and the fruit of your land, until you are destroyed, leaving neither corn, wine, oil, increase of kine, or flocks of sheep, until he has destroyed you. — Matthew Henry

52 COMMENTARY: And he shall besiege you in all your gates, until your high and fenced walls come down, in which you trusted, throughout all your land, and he shall besiege you in all your gates throughout all your land which the Lord has given you. — Matthew Henry

53 COMMENTARY: And you shall eat the fruit of your own body, the flesh of your sons and daughters, whom the Lord has given you, in the siege and straitness with which your enemies shall distress you. — Matthew Henry

54 COMMENTARY: So that the man who is tender among you, and very delicate, his eye shall be evil toward his brother, toward the wife of his bosom, and toward the remnant of his children which he shall leave. — Matthew Henry

55 COMMENTARY: So that he will not give to any of them of the flesh of his children whom he shall eat, because he has nothing left him in the siege and straitness with which your enemies shall distress you in all your gates. — Matthew Henry

56 COMMENTARY: The tender and delicate woman among you, who would not adventure to set the sole of her foot upon the ground for delicateness and tenderness, her eye shall be evil toward the husband of her bosom, toward her son, and toward her daughter. — Matthew Henry

57 COMMENTARY: And toward her young one that comes out from between her feet, and toward her children which she shall bear, for she shall eat them for want of all things secretly in the siege and straitness with which your enemy shall distress you in your gates. — Matthew Henry

58 COMMENTARY: If you will not observe to do all the words of this law written in this book, that you may fear this glorious and fearful name, THE LORD THY GOD. — Matthew Henry

59 COMMENTARY: Then the Lord will make your plagues wonderful, and the plagues of your seed, even great plagues, and of long continuance, and sore sicknesses, and of long continuance. — Matthew Henry

60 COMMENTARY: Moreover he will bring upon you all the diseases of Egypt, which you were afraid of, and they shall cleave to you. — Matthew Henry

61 COMMENTARY: Also every sickness, and every plague, which is not written in the book of this law, them will the Lord bring upon you, until you are destroyed. — Matthew Henry

62 COMMENTARY: And you shall be left few in number, whereas you were as the stars of heaven for multitude, because you would not obey the voice of the Lord your God. — Matthew Henry

63 COMMENTARY: And it shall come to pass, that as the Lord rejoiced over you to do you good, and to multiply you, so the Lord will rejoice over you to destroy you, and to bring you to nought, and you shall be plucked from off the land you go to possess. — Matthew Henry

64 COMMENTARY: And the Lord shall scatter you among all people, from one end of the earth even to the other, and there you shall serve other gods, which neither you nor your fathers have known, even wood and stone. — Matthew Henry

65 COMMENTARY: And among these nations you shall find no ease, neither shall the sole of your foot have rest, but the Lord shall give you there a trembling heart, failing of eyes, and sorrow of mind; no rest of body, and worse, no rest of mind, banished from city to city. — Matthew Henry

66 COMMENTARY: And your life shall hang in doubt before you, and you shall fear day and night, and shall have no assurance of your life. — Matthew Henry

67 COMMENTARY: In the morning you shall say, Would God it were evening! and at evening you shall say, Would God it were morning! for the fear of your heart, and for the sight of your eyes which you shall see. — Matthew Henry

68 COMMENTARY: And the Lord shall bring you into Egypt again with ships, by the way of which I spoke to you, You shall see it no more again, and there you shall be sold to your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and no man shall buy you; these are but the beginning of sorrows to those under the curse, from which Christ has redeemed us by being made a curse for us. — Matthew Henry
