Study Notes — Mark

Chapter 16

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: When the sabbath was past, Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James, and Salome had bought sweet spices, that they might come and anoint him. After the sabbath, three women bought spices to anoint him, for love's last service was prepared as soon as the holy day allowed. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Very early in the morning, the first day of the week, they came to the sepulchre at the rising of the sun. At sunrise on the first day, the week's first morning, they came to the tomb, and the rising sun matched the rising they would find. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: They said among themselves, Who shall roll us away the stone from the door of the sepulchre? Their question about the stone shows they expected a closed tomb, for love went without a plan for the obstacle, trusting to find help at the place. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: When they looked, they saw that the stone was rolled away, for it was very great. The very great stone was already rolled away, for the obstacle they feared had been removed before they arrived, and looking up they saw what they had not dared hope. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Entering into the sepulchre, they saw a young man sitting on the right side, clothed in a long white garment, and they were affrighted. A young man in white, sitting on the right, affrighted them, for an angel in the tomb is a more startling sight than an empty one. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: He said to them, Be not affrighted: You seek Jesus of Nazareth, who was crucified: he is risen; he is not here: behold the place where they laid him. 'He is risen, he is not here' answers their seeking, and the empty place is shown as proof, for the tomb they came to anoint held no body to anoint. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: But go your way, tell his disciples and Peter that he goes before you into Galilee: there shall you see him, as he said to you. Tell his disciples, and Peter by name, for the denier is named to assure him he is not cast off, and Galilee is the appointed meeting, as he had said. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: They went out quickly, fled from the sepulchre, for they trembled and were amazed, neither said they anything to any man, for they were afraid. Trembling, amazement, and fear fled from the tomb in silence, for the news was too great for immediate speech, and fear held their tongues on the road. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: Now when Jesus was risen early the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, out of whom he had cast seven devils. Risen early, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, once possessed by seven devils, for grace honours a forgiven sinner with the first sight of the risen Lord. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: She went and told them that had been with him, as they mourned and wept. She told the mourning company, for the first witness preached to weepers, and her tidings met grief, not expectation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: They, when they heard that he was alive and had been seen by her, believed not. Hearing he was alive and seen, they believed not, for grief and the strangeness of resurrection overcame a woman's testimony, though it was true. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: After that he appeared in another form to two of them, as they walked and went into the country. In another form he appeared to two walking into the country, for the risen Lord was not always recognised at once, and the road became a place of revelation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: They went and told it to the residue, neither believed they them. The two told the rest, and again belief failed, for the disciples' unbelief persisted through a second witness, and their hardness is recorded without excuse. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: Afterward he appeared to the eleven as they sat at meat, and upbraided them with their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they believed not those who had seen him after he was risen. Appearing to the eleven at meat, he upbraided unbelief and hardness, for refusing witnesses was itself a fault, and rebuke preceded commission. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

15 COMMENTARY: He said to them, Go into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature. Go into all the world and preach to every creature is the commission after rebuke, for the gospel's scope is the world, and its hearers are every creature. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

16 COMMENTARY: He that believes and is baptized shall be saved, but he that believes not shall be damned. Believing and baptized shall be saved, and not believing shall be damned, for faith and its sign belong together, and unbelief alone condemns. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

17 COMMENTARY: These signs shall follow them that believe: In my name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues. Signs follow believers, not as their seeking, but as Christ's confirmation: devils cast out and new tongues spoken in his name. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

18 COMMENTARY: They shall take up serpents, and if they drink any deadly thing, it shall not hurt them; they shall lay hands on the sick, and they shall recover. Serpents taken up, deadly drink harmless, and hands healing the sick are the apostolic signs, given to confirm the word in its first publishing. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

19 COMMENTARY: So then after the Lord had spoken to them, he was received up into heaven, and sat on the right hand of God. After speaking, he was received up and sat at God's right hand, for the ascension crowns the commission, and the seated Lord rules the work he sends them to do. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

20 COMMENTARY: They went forth and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them, and confirming the word with signs following. Amen. They went forth everywhere, the Lord working with them, and signs confirmed the word, for the gospel's spread was the Lord's work through his preachers, and Amen seals the book. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
