Study Notes — Amos

Chapter 1

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The words of Amos, a herdman of Tekoa, which he saw concerning Israel in the days of Uzziah of Judah and Jeroboam of Israel, two years before the earthquake: God employed a shepherd to reprove and warn, and those given abilities for his service ought not to be despised for their origin or employment. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: The Lord will roar from Zion and utter his voice from Jerusalem, and shepherds' habitations shall mourn and Carmel's top wither, judgment announced from God's chosen city and royal seat, from which the ten tribes had revolted. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord: for three transgressions of Damascus, and for four, he will not turn away punishment, because they threshed Gilead with iron threshing instruments: the number means not exactly three or four but many, the measure of sins filled and ripe for vengeance. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: God will send fire into the house of Hazael, devouring Benhadad's palaces, the Syrian royal house itself judged for its cruelty. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: He will break Damascus's bar, cut off the inhabitant from the plain of Aven and the scepter-holder from the house of Eden, and Syria shall go captive to Kir, the method of judgment partly the same for each nation yet peculiar in each. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord: for three transgressions of Gaza, and for four, he will not turn away punishment, because they carried away the whole captivity to deliver them to Edom, Philistia handing God's people to their most inveterate enemies. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: God will send fire on Gaza's wall, devouring its palaces, desolating judgment on Philistine power. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: He will cut off the inhabitant from Ashdod, the scepter-holder from Ashkelon, and turn his hand against Ekron, and the remnant of the Philistines shall perish, the whole Philistine coast judged city by city. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord: for three transgressions of Tyre, and for four, he will not turn away punishment, because they delivered up the whole captivity to Edom and remembered not the brotherly covenant, the covenant between Hiram and David and Solomon broken for profit. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: God will send fire on Tyre's wall, devouring its palaces, the merchant city's strength consumed. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord: for three transgressions of Edom, and for four, he will not turn away punishment, because he pursued his brother with the sword, cast off all pity, and his anger tore perpetually, keeping wrath forever, bitterness against God's people shown in every age. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: God will send fire upon Teman, devouring the palaces of Bozrah, Edom's strength and glory threatened with utter overthrow. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: Thus says the Lord: for three transgressions of Ammon, and for four, he will not turn away punishment, because they ripped up the women with child of Gilead to enlarge their border, cruelty used to empty land of future claimants. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: God will kindle fire in Rabbah's wall, devouring its palaces with shouting in battle and tempest in whirlwind, judgment coming with irresistible force and surprising swiftness. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: And Ammon's king shall go into captivity, he and his princes together, says the Lord, so that when the Lord reckons with his enemies, how tremendous are his judgments. — Matthew Henry
