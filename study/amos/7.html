Study Notes — Amos

Chapter 7

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Thus the Lord God showed Amos: behold, he formed grasshoppers at the beginning of the latter growth's shooting up, after the king's mowings, the second crop, the people's sustenance, threatened at its most vulnerable moment. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: And when they had made an end of eating the land's grass, Amos said, O Lord God, forgive, I beseech thee, by whom shall Jacob arise, for he is small, the prophet most earnest for forgiveness of sin, since sin will soon make a great people small. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: The Lord repented for this: it shall not be, says the Lord, showing how ready and swift God is to show mercy and how he waits to be gracious, and what a blessing praying people are to a land. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Thus the Lord God showed Amos: behold, the Lord God called to contend by fire, and it devoured the great deep and ate up a part, judgment by fire consuming even the sea's depths. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: Then Amos said, O Lord God, cease, I beseech thee, by whom shall Jacob arise, for he is small, repeated prayer meeting repeated mercy. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: The Lord repented for this also: this also shall not be, says the Lord God, God bearing long, though he will not bear always with a provoking people. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: Thus he showed Amos: behold, the Lord stood upon a wall made by a plumbline, with a plumbline in his hand, Israel being a wall God himself reared as defense to his sanctuary, now measured and found bowing and bulging. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: And the Lord said, Amos, what seest thou, and he said, a plumbline, then the Lord said, behold, I will set a plumbline in the midst of my people Israel, I will not again pass by them any more, the time coming when those spared often shall be spared no longer. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: And Isaac's high places shall be desolate, Israel's sanctuaries laid waste, and God will rise against Jeroboam's house with the sword, the idolatrous sanctuaries and the royal house both falling. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: Then Amaziah, priest of Bethel, sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, Amos has conspired against thee in Israel's midst, the land not able to bear all his words, accusers misrepresenting God's friends as traitors and troublers of the land. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: For thus Amos says, Jeroboam shall die by the sword, and Israel shall surely be led captive out of their own land, Amaziah's report distorting the prophecy into a personal conspiracy charge. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: Also Amaziah said to Amos, O seer, flee away to Judah, eat bread and prophesy there, those who make gain their godliness thinking wealth and preferment the most powerful motives with others too. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: But prophesy not again any more at Bethel, for it is the king's chapel and the king's court, the priest defending a royal sanctuary against God's word. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: Then Amos answered Amaziah: I was no prophet, neither a prophet's son, but a herdman and gatherer of sycamore fruit, the Lord often choosing weak and foolish things to confound the wise and mighty. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: And the Lord took him as he followed the flock, and said, go, prophesy to my people Israel, a warrant from God that ought not to fear the face of man, and which, if God had not strengthened him, could not have set his face as flint. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: Now therefore hear the word of the Lord: thou sayest, prophesy not against Israel, and drop not thy word against Isaac's house, Amaziah's prohibition itself becoming the text of judgment. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: Therefore thus says the Lord: thy wife shall be a harlot in the city, thy sons and daughters shall fall by the sword, thy land shall be divided by line, thou shalt die in a polluted land, and Israel shall surely go into captivity forth of his land, since all who oppose or despise God's word must expect fatal effects unless they repent. — Matthew Henry
