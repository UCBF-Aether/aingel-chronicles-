Study Notes — Amos

Chapter 6

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Woe to those at ease in Zion and trusting in Samaria's mountain, named chief of the nations, to whom Israel came, ease and vain confidence in privilege being the greatest danger, especially for those at ease in Zion. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: Pass to Calneh and see, then to great Hamath, then down to Gath of the Philistines: are they better than these kingdoms, or their border greater, the examples of others' ruin forbidding security. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: You who put far away the evil day and cause the seat of violence to come near, by delaying judgment in thought while hastening oppression in deed. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: You who lie on ivory beds, stretch on couches, and eat lambs from the flock and calves from the stall, luxury described as the mark of those doing well for their bodies but not for their souls. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: You who chant to the viol and invent instruments of music like David, turning worship's art into self-indulgence. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: You who drink wine in bowls and anoint with chief ointments, but are not grieved for Joseph's affliction, set upon pleasures and careless of others' troubles, a great offense to God. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: Therefore now they shall go captive with the first captives, and the banquet of those stretched out shall be removed, pleasures they set their hearts on being taken away. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: The Lord God has sworn by himself, says the Lord God of hosts, that he abhors Jacob's excellency and hates his palaces, therefore he will deliver up the city with all therein, dreadful being the case of those whose ruin the Lord himself has sworn, since none can alter his purpose. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: And if ten men remain in one house, they shall die, death reaching even a remnant household. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: And a man's uncle shall take him up, and he who burns him, to bring bones out of the house, and shall ask one by the house's sides if any remain, and hearing no, shall say, hold thy tongue, for we may not mention the Lord's name, hearts so hardened they will not worship God even when sickness and death are in their families. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: For behold, the Lord commands, and he will smite the great house with breaches and the little house with clefts, great and small alike broken. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: Shall horses run upon rock, or will one plow there with oxen, for they have turned judgment into gall and righteousness' fruit into hemlock, services soured with sin making God's providences bitter in return. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: You who rejoice in a thing of nought, saying, have we not taken horns by our own strength, boasting in power that is nothing. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: But behold, God will raise up a nation against Israel, says the Lord God of hosts, and they shall afflict them from Hamath's entering to the wilderness river, pride being destroyed by the very nation God raises. — Matthew Henry
