Study Notes — Amos

Chapter 4

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Hear this word, you kine of Bashan in Samaria's mountain, who oppress the poor, crush the needy, and say to your masters, bring and let us drink: wealth got by extortion used to provide for the flesh and fulfill its lusts. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: The Lord God has sworn by his holiness that days shall come when they will be taken away with hooks, and their posterity with fishhooks, oppressors dragged off like caught fish. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: They shall go out at the breaches, each straight before her, and cast themselves into the palace, says the Lord, escape routes becoming routes of exile. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Come to Bethel and transgress, at Gilgal multiply transgression, bringing sacrifices every morning and tithes after three years, the prophet ironically bidding them do what they love, since confidence in unscriptural observances only proves they believe a lie. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: Offer thanksgiving with leaven and proclaim and publish free offerings, for this pleases you, O children of Israel, says the Lord God, worship performed to please themselves rather than warranted by God's word. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: God also gave them cleanness of teeth in all cities and want of bread in all places, yet they have not returned to him, says the Lord, famine as a first corrective refused. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: God also withheld rain when three months remained to harvest, raining on one city and not on another, one piece rained on and another withering, yet they have not returned, carnal hearts wandering from creature to creature for satisfaction but not inclining to him in whom all wants could be met. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: So two or three cities wandered to one city to drink water but were not satisfied, yet they have not returned to him, says the Lord, thirst itself not teaching them. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: God smote them with blasting and mildew, and when gardens, vineyards, fig trees, and olives increased, the palmerworm devoured them, yet they have not returned, preaching being as rain and everything withering where that rain is wanting. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: God sent pestilence after Egypt's manner, slew their young men with the sword, took away horses, and made camp stink rise to their nostrils, yet they have not returned, repeated strokes not producing repentance. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: God overthrew some of them as Sodom and Gomorrah, and they were as a firebrand plucked from the burning, yet they have not returned, deliverance itself not softening them. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: Therefore thus will God do to Israel, and because he will do this, prepare to meet thy God, O Israel, since ere long all must meet God in judgment and cannot stand if tried according to their doings. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: For he who forms mountains, creates wind, declares to man his thought, makes morning darkness, and treads on earth's high places, the Lord, the God of hosts, is his name, and to meet him with comfort they must now meet him in Christ, who came to save lost sinners. — Matthew Henry
