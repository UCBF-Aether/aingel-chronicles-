Study Notes — Amos

Chapter 9

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Amos saw the Lord standing upon the altar, saying, smite the lintel that the posts may shake, cut them in the head, all of them, and slay the last with the sword, none fleeing or escaping delivered, the Lord standing on the idolatrous altar at Bethel itself. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: Though they dig into hell, God's hand shall take them there, and though they climb to heaven, he will bring them down, wherever sinners flee from God's justice, it will overtake them. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Though they hide in Carmel's top, God will search and take them out, and though hid in the sea's bottom, he will command the serpent to bite them, no depth hiding from the Judge. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Though they go into captivity before enemies, God will command the sword to slay them there, setting his eyes upon them for evil and not for good, wretched being those on whom the Lord looks for evil. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: The Lord God of hosts is he who touches the land and it shall melt, all dwelling therein mourning, rising wholly like a flood and drowned as by Egypt's flood, the Creator's touch unmaking what he made. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: It is he who builds his stories in heaven, founded his troop in the earth, calls the sea's waters and pours them on the earth's face: the Lord is his name, the same power that orders heaven and earth ordering judgment. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: Are you not as Ethiopians' children to me, O children of Israel, says the Lord, have I not brought up Israel from Egypt, Philistines from Caphtor, and Syrians from Kir, God's migrations of other nations showing Israel's election did not make them exempt, and professors made like the world being leveled with the world. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: Behold, the Lord God's eyes are upon the sinful kingdom, and he will destroy it from the earth's face, saving that he will not utterly destroy Jacob's house, says the Lord, judgment on the kingdom but preservation of a remnant. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: For God will command and sift Israel among all nations like corn sifted in a sieve, yet not the least grain shall fall to the earth, the astonishing preservation of the Jews as a distinct people foretold, the sieve scattering chaff while keeping grain. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: All the sinners of God's people shall die by the sword, who say, the evil shall not overtake nor prevent us, self-flattering profession not protecting those who presume on it. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: In that day God will raise up David's fallen tabernacle, close its breaches, raise its ruins, and build it as in days of old, Christ dying to gather God's scattered children, those called by his name. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: That they may possess Edom's remnant and all the heathen called by God's name, says the Lord who does this, who can do it, who has determined to do it, grace's power engaged for doing it. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: Behold, days come, says the Lord, when the plowman shall overtake the reaper, and the grape-treader him who sows seed, mountains dropping sweet wine and hills melting, harvests so abundant that sowing and reaping overlap. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: And God will bring again his people Israel's captivity, and they shall build waste cities and inhabit them, plant vineyards and drink their wine, make gardens and eat their fruit, restoration as tangible as the ruin was. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: And God will plant them upon their land, and they shall no more be pulled up out of their given land, says the Lord their God, God marvelously preserving his elect amid confusions and miseries, reviving his church, and leaving not one good thing promised unfulfilled. — Matthew Henry
