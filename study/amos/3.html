Study Notes — Amos

Chapter 3

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Hear this word the Lord has spoken against you, O children of Israel, against the whole family brought up from Egypt, a word of reproof and threatening now that words of counsel and comfort were disregarded. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: You only have God known of all the earth's families, therefore he will punish them for all their iniquities, distinguishing favors not restraining from sin and therefore not exempting from punishment, the nearer to God in profession, the more surely he reckons. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Can two walk together unless agreed, for they could not expect communion with God unless they first sought peace with him, where there is no friendship there can be no fellowship. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Will a lion roar in the forest when he has no prey, or a young lion cry from his den if he has taken nothing, the prophet's warnings having a cause as certain as a lion's roar has prey. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: Can a bird fall in a snare where no gin is, or a snare spring up having taken nothing, effects not arising without causes, so judgment does not come uncaused. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: Shall a trumpet be blown in a city and the people not be afraid, or shall evil be in a city and the Lord not have done it, the evil of trouble being from God, whoever the instruments, while the evil of sin is from ourselves. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: Surely the Lord God will do nothing but reveal his secret to his servants the prophets, warnings preceding judgments so that none can say they were unwarned. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: The lion has roared, who will not fear, and the Lord God has spoken, who can but prophesy, the prophet compelled to speak once God has spoken. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: Publish in Ashdod's and Egypt's palaces and say, assemble on Samaria's mountains and behold the great tumults and oppression in her midst, inviting even foreign witnesses to see Israel's disorders. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: For they know not to do right, says the Lord, storing up violence and robbery in their palaces, power used as an instrument of unrighteousness. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: Therefore an adversary shall be round about the land, bringing down Israel's strength and spoiling her palaces, power got and kept wrongfully not being kept long. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: As a shepherd takes from a lion's mouth two legs or a piece of an ear, so shall Samaria's children be taken out, in a bed's corner and in Damascus on a couch, yet a remnant according to election of grace will be secured by the great Shepherd from destruction's jaws. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: Hear and testify in the house of Jacob, says the Lord God of hosts, the charge formally witnessed before judgment. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: In the day God visits Israel's transgressions he will also visit Bethel's altars, and the altar's horns shall be cut off and fall to the ground, the idolatrous sanctuary judged with the people. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: God will smite winter house with summer house, houses of ivory shall perish, and great houses shall have an end, says the Lord, the pomp and pleasantness of houses not fortifying against judgment but making sufferings more grievous. — Matthew Henry
