Study Notes — Malachi

Chapter 4

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven, he said, and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble, and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the LORD of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch, for this refers to the first and second coming of Christ, and God has fixed the day of both, and it is certainly to be applied to the day of judgment, when Christ shall be revealed in flaming fire to execute judgment on the proud and all that do wickedly. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: But unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings, he said, and ye shall go forth, and grow up as calves of the stall, for by the Sun of Righteousness we understand Jesus Christ, through whom believers are justified and sanctified and brought to see light, and his influences render the sinner holy, joyful, and fruitful, and that day which to the wicked will burn as an oven, will to the righteous be bright as the morning. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: And ye shall tread down the wicked, he said, for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet in the day that I shall do this, saith the LORD of hosts, for the saints' triumphs are all owing to God's victories, it is not they that do this, but God who does it for them. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Remember ye the law of Moses my servant, he said, which I commanded unto him in Horeb for all Israel, with the statutes and judgments, for this is a solemn conclusion, not only of this prophecy, but of the Old Testament, and conscience bids us remember the law, and though we have not prophets, yet as long as we have Bibles, we may keep up communion with God. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the LORD, he said, for they must keep up a believing expectation of the gospel of Christ, and look for the beginning of it, and John the Baptist preached repentance and reformation, as Elijah had done. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers, he said, lest I come and smite the earth with a curse, for the turning of souls to God and their duty is the best preparation for the great and dreadful day, and John shall preach a doctrine that reaches men's hearts and works a change in them, preparing the way for the kingdom of heaven, while those who turn not to him who smites them with a rod must expect to be smitten with a curse, and none can escape the curse of God's broken law unless their hearts are turned from sin and the world, to Christ and holiness. — Matthew Henry
