Study Notes — Malachi

Chapter 2

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: And now, O ye priests, this commandment is for you, for what is said of the covenant of priesthood is true of the covenant of grace made with all believers, as spiritual priests. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: If ye will not hear, and if ye will not lay it to heart, to give glory unto my name, saith the LORD of hosts, I will even send a curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings, yea, I have cursed them already, because ye do not lay it to heart, for the covenant is a covenant of life and peace, assuring believers of all happiness in this world and the next. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Behold, I will corrupt your seed, and spread dung upon your faces, even the dung of your solemn feasts, and one shall take you away with it, for those who despise God shall be lightly esteemed, while those who honour him, he will honour. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: And ye shall know that I have sent this commandment unto you, that my covenant might be with Levi, saith the LORD of hosts, for the warning itself proves God's faithfulness to his covenant. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: My covenant was with him of life and peace, he said, and I gave them to him for the fear wherewith he feared me, and was afraid before my name, for Levi's covenant is the pattern of the minister's walk with God in peace and righteousness. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: The law of truth was in his mouth, and iniquity was not found in his lips, he said; he walked with me in peace and equity, and did turn many away from iniquity, for those ministers, and those only, are likely to turn men from sin, who preach sound doctrine and live holy lives according to Scripture. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: For the priest's lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth, he said, for he is the messenger of the LORD of hosts, and it is an honour to God's servants to be employed as his messengers, and the people are all concerned to know the Lord's will, not only from the written word but from his messengers' instruction. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: But ye are departed out of the way, he said; ye have caused many to stumble at the law; ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi, saith the LORD of hosts, for many departed from this way, and thus they misled the people. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: Therefore have I also made you contemptible and base before all the people, he said, according as ye have not kept my ways, but have been partial in the law, for those who despise God shall be lightly esteemed. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: Have we not all one father, he asked, hath not one God created us, why do we deal treacherously every man against his brother, by profaning the covenant of our fathers, for corrupt practices are the fruit of corrupt principles, and he who is false to his God will not be true to his fellow mortals. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: Judah hath dealt treacherously, he said, and an abomination is committed in Israel and in Jerusalem, for Judah hath profaned the holiness of the LORD which he loved, and hath married the daughter of a strange god. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: The LORD will cut off the man that doeth this, the master and the scholar, out of the tabernacles of Jacob, and him that offereth an offering unto the LORD of hosts, for treachery against God cuts a man off from God's people whatever his standing. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: And this have ye done again, he said, covering the altar of the LORD with tears, with weeping, and with crying out, insomuch that he regardeth not the offering any more, or receiveth it with good will at your hand, for they pretended tenderness while making their wives' lives bitter. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: Yet ye say, Wherefore, he said, because the LORD hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth, against whom thou hast dealt treacherously, yet is she thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant, for the wife is to be looked on, not as a servant, but as a companion, and there is an oath of God between them not to be trifled with. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: And did not he make one, he asked, yet had he the residue of the spirit, and wherefore one, that he might seek a godly seed; therefore take heed to your spirit, and let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth, for God made one Eve for one Adam, that the children might be a seed to serve him, and husbands and wives must live in the fear of God, that their seed may be godly. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: For the LORD, the God of Israel, saith that he hateth putting away, he said, for one covereth violence with his garment, saith the LORD of hosts, therefore take heed to your spirit, that ye deal not treacherously, for those who would be kept from sin must take heed to their spirits, for there all sin begins, and wrong conduct in families springs from selfishness. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: Ye have wearied the LORD with your words, he said, yet ye say, Wherein have we wearied him, when ye say, Every one that doeth evil is good in the sight of the LORD, and he delighteth in them, or, Where is the God of judgment, for it is wearisome to God to hear people justify themselves in wicked practices, and those who think God can be a friend to sin affront him and deceive themselves, but the day of the Lord will come to answer the scoffers. — Matthew Henry
