Study Notes — John

Chapter 17

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: These words spoke Jesus, and lifted up his eyes to heaven, and said, Father, the hour is come; glorify thy Son, that thy Son also may glorify thee, for our Lord prayed as a man and as Mediator, yet spoke with majesty as one with and equal to the Father. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: As thou hast given him power over all flesh, that he should give eternal life to as many as thou hast given him, for eternal life could not be given unless Christ, their Surety, both glorified the Father and was glorified of him. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent, for this is the sinner's way to eternal life, and when this knowledge is made perfect, holiness and happiness will be fully enjoyed. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: I have glorified thee on the earth, he said; I have finished the work which thou gavest me to do, for the holiness and happiness of the redeemed are that glory of Christ and the Father which was the joy set before him, for which he endured the cross. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: And now, O Father, glorify thou me with thine own self with the glory which I had with thee before the world was, for he asked for the glory that was his before his humiliation. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: I have manifested thy name unto the men which thou gavest me out of the world, he said; thine they were, and thou gavest them me, and they have kept thy word, for Christ prays for those that are his, given as sheep to the shepherd to be kept, as patients to the physician to be cured, as children to a tutor to be taught. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: Now they have known that all things whatsoever thou hast given me are of thee, for he, all he is and has, and all he said and did, are of God, and this is our satisfaction in relying on Christ. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: For I have given unto them the words which thou gavest me, he said, and they have received them, and have known surely that I came out from thee, and they have believed that thou didst send me. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: I pray for them, he said; I pray not for the world, but for them which thou hast given me, for they are thine, for Christ offered this prayer for his people alone as believers, yet no one who desires to come to the Father need be discouraged, for he saves to the uttermost all who come unto God by him. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: And all mine are thine, and thine are mine, he said, and I am glorified in them, for this speaks Father and Son to be one, and the Son owns none for his who are not devoted to the Father's service. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: And now I am no more in the world, he said, but these are in the world, and I come to thee; Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are, for he prayed not that they might be rich and great, but kept from sin, strengthened for duty, and brought safe to heaven, united as Father and Son are united. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: While I was with them in the world, I kept them in thy name, he said; those that thou gavest me I have kept, and none of them is lost, but the son of perdition, that the Scripture might be fulfilled. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: And now come I to thee, he said, and these things I speak in the world, that they might have my joy fulfilled in themselves, for the prosperity of the soul is the best prosperity. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: I have given them thy word, he said, and the world has hated them, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world, for the Spirit of God in true Christians is opposed to the spirit of the world. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, he said, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil, for they had a great work to do for God's glory and mankind's benefit, and must pass through the world as through an enemy's country, kept from corruption, remaining sin, and Satan's power and craft. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: They are not of the world, even as I am not of the world, he repeated, for they are not left here to pursue the world's objects, but to glorify God and serve their generation. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: Sanctify them through thy truth, he prayed; thy word is truth, for Christ prayed not only that they be kept from evil, but made good, and the means of this grace is through God's truth. — Matthew Henry

18 COMMENTARY: As thou hast sent me into the world, even so have I also sent them into the world, for their mission follows his. — Matthew Henry

19 COMMENTARY: And for their sakes I sanctify myself, he said, that they also might be sanctified through the truth, for Jesus entirely devoted himself to his undertaking, especially offering himself without spot unto God, and the real holiness of Christians is the fruit of his death, by which the Holy Ghost was purchased. — Matthew Henry

20 COMMENTARY: Neither pray I for these alone, he said, but for them also which shall believe on me through their word, for his prayer extends to all future believers. — Matthew Henry

21 COMMENTARY: That they all may be one, he prayed, as thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us, that the world may believe that thou hast sent me, for believers are to be one body under one head, animated by one soul, through union with Christ and the Father in him. — Matthew Henry

22 COMMENTARY: And the glory which thou gavest me I have given them, he said, that they may be one, even as we are one, for unity is the gift that accompanies his glory. — Matthew Henry

23 COMMENTARY: I in them, and thou in me, that they may be made perfect in one, he said, and that the world may know that thou hast sent me, and hast loved them, as thou hast loved me, for the more believers dispute about lesser things, the more they throw doubts upon Christianity, and unity convinces the world. — Matthew Henry

24 COMMENTARY: Father, I will that they also, whom thou hast given me, be with me where I am, he said, that they may behold my glory, which thou hast given me, for thou lovedst me before the foundation of the world, for Christ, as one with the Father, claimed that the redeemed be brought to heaven to behold his glory as their beloved Friend and Brother. — Matthew Henry

25 COMMENTARY: O righteous Father, the world has not known thee, he said, but I have known thee, and these have known that thou hast sent me. — Matthew Henry

26 COMMENTARY: And I have declared unto them thy name, and will declare it, he said, that the love wherewith thou hast loved me may be in them, and I in them, for he had declared and would further declare God's name by his doctrine and Spirit, that, joined to him by one Spirit, they might be filled with all the fullness of God. — Matthew Henry
