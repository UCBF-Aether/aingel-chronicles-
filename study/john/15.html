Study Notes — John

Chapter 15

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: I am the true vine, he said, and my Father is the husbandman, for the union of human and divine natures and the fullness of the Spirit in him resemble the root of a vine made fruitful by rich soil. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: Every branch in me that bears not fruit he takes away, and every branch that bears fruit, he purges it, that it may bring forth more fruit, for the unfruitful are taken away, and even fruitful branches need pruning of notions, passions, and humours. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Now you are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you, for there is a cleansing virtue in Christ's word, as it works grace and works out corruption. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Abide in me, and I in you, he said; as the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, except it abide in the vine, no more can you, except you abide in me, for fruitfulness requires constant dependence and communion. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: I am the vine, you are the branches, he said; he who abides in me, and I in him, the same brings forth much fruit, for without me you can do nothing, for believers are weak branches borne up only by the root, and all supports and supplies are in Christ. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: If a man abides not in me, he is cast forth as a branch, and is withered, and men gather them, and cast them into the fire, and they are burned, for those who abide not, though they flourish awhile in outward profession, come to nothing, and the fire is the fittest place for withered branches. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: If you abide in me, and my words abide in you, you shall ask what you will, and it shall be done unto you, for prayer rooted in his word is answered. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: Herein is my Father glorified, that you bear much fruit, so shall you be my disciples, for the more fruit we bring forth, the more our Lord is glorified. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: As the Father has loved me, so have I loved you, he said; continue you in my love, for those whom God loves as a Father may despise the hatred of all the world. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: If you keep my commandments, you shall abide in my love, even as I have kept my Father's commandments, and abide in his love, for they are to show love by keeping his commandments. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: These things have I spoken unto you, that my joy might remain in you, and that your joy might be full, for the joy of those who abide in Christ's love is a continual feast, not the momentary joy of the hypocrite. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: This is my commandment, that you love one another, as I have loved you, for Christ's love to us should direct us to love each other, and this one command includes many duties. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: Greater love has no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends, the measure of Christ's own love. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: You are my friends, if you do whatsoever I command you, for friendship with Christ is shown by obedience. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: Henceforth I call you not servants, he said, for the servant knows not what his lord does, but I have called you friends, for all things I have heard of my Father I have made known unto you. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: You have not chosen me, but I have chosen you, and ordained you, that you should go and bring forth fruit, and that your fruit should remain, that whatsoever you shall ask of the Father in my name, he may give it you, for election precedes our choosing, and fruitfulness is the purpose of it. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: These things I command you, that you love one another, he repeated, for love is the sum of his charge to them. — Matthew Henry

18 COMMENTARY: If the world hate you, you know that it hated me before it hated you, so hatred for Christ's sake should not surprise his disciples. — Matthew Henry

19 COMMENTARY: If you were of the world, the world would love his own, he said, but because you are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, therefore the world hates you. — Matthew Henry

20 COMMENTARY: Remember the word I said unto you, The servant is not greater than his lord, he said; if they have persecuted me, they will also persecute you, and if they have kept my saying, they will keep yours also. — Matthew Henry

21 COMMENTARY: But all these things will they do unto you for my name's sake, because they know not him who sent me, for the world's ignorance of the Father is the true cause of its hatred to Christ's disciples. — Matthew Henry

22 COMMENTARY: If I had not come and spoken unto them, they had not had sin, he said, but now they have no cloak for their sin, for the clearer the discoveries of grace and truth, the greater the sin of refusing them. — Matthew Henry

23 COMMENTARY: He who hates me hates my Father also, for to oppose Christ as Prophet, Priest, and King is to prove ignorance of the one living and true God. — Matthew Henry

24 COMMENTARY: If I had not done among them the works which none other man did, they had not had sin, he said, but now they have both seen and hated both me and my Father. — Matthew Henry

25 COMMENTARY: But this comes to pass, that the word might be fulfilled that is written in their law, They hated me without a cause, for his rejection was foretold. — Matthew Henry

26 COMMENTARY: But when the Comforter is come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, which proceeds from the Father, he shall testify of me, for the blessed Spirit will maintain Christ's cause in the world despite opposition. — Matthew Henry

27 COMMENTARY: And you also shall bear witness, because you have been with me from the beginning, for believers taught and encouraged by the Spirit bear testimony to Christ and his salvation. — Matthew Henry
