Study Notes — John

Chapter 14

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Let not your heart be troubled, he said; you believe in God, believe also in me, for the remedy against trouble of mind is believing in Christ as Mediator, and disciples more than others should keep their minds quiet when all else is unquiet. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: In my Father's house are many mansions, he said; if it were not so, I would have told you, for heaven is a father's house, with many lasting dwellings for the many sons to be brought to glory. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and receive you unto myself, that where I am, there you may be also, for Christ will be the Finisher of that of which he is the Author, and if he prepares the place, he will prepare us for it. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: And whither I go you know, and the way you know, he said, for they had heard enough of his way to follow it. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: Thomas said, Lord, we know not whither thou goest, and how can we know the way, confessing their ignorance plainly. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: Jesus said, I am the way, the truth, and the life; no man comes unto the Father but by me, for he is the Way in his person as God manifest in the flesh, in his atoning sacrifice, and as Advocate, the Truth fulfilling all prophecies of a Saviour, and the Life by whose Spirit the dead in sin are quickened. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: If you had known me, you should have known my Father also, he said, and from henceforth you know him, and have seen him, for all who saw Christ by faith saw the Father in him. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: Philip said, Lord, show us the Father, and it suffices us, asking for a sight of God himself. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: Jesus said, Have I been so long time with you, and yet hast thou not known me, Philip, for he who has seen me has seen the Father, and how sayest thou then, Show us the Father. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: Believest thou not that I am in the Father, and the Father in me, he asked; the words I speak unto you I speak not of myself, but the Father dwelling in me does the works, for in Christ's doctrine men saw God as Father of lights, and in his miracles God as God of power. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: Believe me that I am in the Father, and the Father in me, he said, or else believe me for the very works' sake, for the works of the Redeemer show forth his own glory and God in him. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: Verily, verily, he who believes on me, the works that I do shall he do also, and greater works than these shall he do, because I go unto my Father, for the apostles' wider ministry would follow his departure. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: And whatsoever you shall ask in my name, that will I do, that the Father may be glorified in the Son, for to ask in Christ's name is to plead his merit and intercession and depend upon that plea. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: If you shall ask anything in my name, I will do it, he repeated, for whatever we ask in his name that is for our good and suitable to our state, he will give. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: If you love me, keep my commandments, he said, for the surest evidence of love to Christ is obedience to his laws. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you forever, for the gift of the Spirit is a fruit of Christ's mediation, bought by his merit and received by his intercession. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: Even the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it sees him not, neither knows him, but you know him, for he dwells with you, and shall be in you, for the Spirit is given to Christ's disciples and not to the world, and as source of holiness and happiness he abides with every believer forever. — Matthew Henry

18 COMMENTARY: I will not leave you comfortless, he said; I will come to you, for though he leaves them, he leaves this comfort, that he will come speedily at his resurrection, daily in his Spirit, and certainly at the end of time. — Matthew Henry

19 COMMENTARY: Yet a little while, and the world sees me no more, but you see me, because I live, you shall live also, for only those who see Christ with an eye of faith shall see him forever. — Matthew Henry

20 COMMENTARY: At that day you shall know that I am in my Father, and you in me, and I in you, mysteries fully known in heaven and already comforted by grace on earth. — Matthew Henry

21 COMMENTARY: He who has my commandments and keeps them, he it is who loves me, he said, and he who loves me shall be loved of my Father, and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him, for love is a commanding, constraining principle, and where love is, duty follows from gratitude. — Matthew Henry

22 COMMENTARY: Judas, not Iscariot, said, Lord, how is it that thou wilt manifest thyself unto us, and not unto the world, wondering at a private manifestation. — Matthew Henry

23 COMMENTARY: Jesus answered, If a man love me, he will keep my words, and my Father will love him, and we will come unto him, and make our abode with him, for God will be with obedient believers as his home. — Matthew Henry

24 COMMENTARY: He who loves me not keeps not my sayings, he said, and the word you hear is not mine, but the Father's who sent me, so disobedience proves the absence of love. — Matthew Henry

25 COMMENTARY: These things have I spoken unto you, being yet present with you, he said, summing up his farewell teaching. — Matthew Henry

26 COMMENTARY: But the Comforter, the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you, for we must pray for and depend on the Spirit's teaching, that Christ's words may be brought to remembrance and difficulties cleared. — Matthew Henry

27 COMMENTARY: Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you, he said; not as the world gives, give I unto you, for his peace is peace of mind from justification before God, and he himself is our Peace, widely different from the peace of Pharisees or hypocrites. — Matthew Henry

28 COMMENTARY: You have heard how I said, I go away, and come again unto you, he said; if you loved me, you would rejoice, because I said, I go unto the Father, for my Father is greater than I, for he raised their expectations to something beyond what they thought their greatest happiness. — Matthew Henry

29 COMMENTARY: And now I have told you before it comes to pass, that when it comes to pass, you might believe, for foreknowledge strengthens faith. — Matthew Henry

30 COMMENTARY: Hereafter I will not talk much with you, he said, for the prince of this world comes, and has nothing in me, for Satan has something in us to perplex us, since we have all sinned, but when he would disturb Christ, he found nothing sinful to help him. — Matthew Henry

31 COMMENTARY: But that the world may know I love the Father, he said, and as the Father gave me commandment, even so I do; arise, let us go hence, for the best evidence of love to the Father is doing as he commanded, and we should rejoice in the Saviour's victories and copy his love and obedience. — Matthew Henry
