Study Notes — 1 Chronicles

Chapter 6

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Levi was first among the tribes, dignified with an honour more valuable than precedency or the double portion: the priesthood. Henry notes Levi's sons Gershom, Kohath, and Merari were the families marshalled in Numbers, and that Moses and Aaron from this tribe were figures of him that was to come. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Amram's children were Aaron, Moses, and Miriam, and Aaron's sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. Henry notes the mention of Nadab and Abihu, who had no children, reminds readers of divine justice against them for offering strange fire. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: Johanan begat Azariah, who executed the priest's office in Solomon's temple. Henry supposes this is the Azariah who bravely opposed King Uzziah's invasion of the priest's office, venturing his neck to defend its barriers. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: Jehozadak went into captivity when the Lord carried away Judah and Jerusalem by Nebuchadnezzar. Henry frames the priestly line as beginning with Eleazar, who came out of Egypt's bondage, and ending with Jehozadak, who went into Babylon's, a sign the Levitical priesthood made nothing perfect. — Matthew Henry

31 COMMENTARY: These are they whom David set over the service of song after the ark had rest. Henry notes David, the sweet psalmist of Israel, appointed singing in God's house once the ark was settled, after harps had hung idle while it was unsettled. — Matthew Henry

32 COMMENTARY: They ministered with singing before the tabernacle until Solomon built the house of the Lord, then waited on their office according to order. Henry stresses they kept as close to office and order in the temple as in the tabernacle, and preferment must not make Levites remiss. — Matthew Henry

33 COMMENTARY: Heman the singer, of the Kohathites, waited with his children. Henry notes Heman, grandson of Samuel, did not think it below him to be precentor, and that blessing on the upright sometimes passes over one generation and fastens on the next. — Matthew Henry

39 COMMENTARY: His brother Asaph stood on his right hand. Henry notes Asaph, of Gershom, was called brother because he shared the office and tribe, and several psalms bear his name as penman or chief musician, and he was a seer as well as a singer. — Matthew Henry

44 COMMENTARY: The sons of Merari stood on the left hand: Ethan son of Kishi. Henry notes Ethan, of Merari, was posted on Heman's left, completing three masters, one from each Levitical house, so the work and honour were equally distributed. — Matthew Henry

48 COMMENTARY: Their brethren the Levites were appointed to all manner of service of the tabernacle. Henry lists serving-work: providing water and fuel, washing, sweeping, carrying ashes, and preparing sacrifices, for those not fit to sing, since every gift should minister. — Matthew Henry

49 COMMENTARY: Aaron and his sons offered on the altar of burnt offering and incense, and were appointed for the most holy work, to make atonement for Israel. Henry stresses their end was to mediate between people and God, not to enrich themselves, and their rule was to do all as God commanded. — Matthew Henry

54 COMMENTARY: These are their dwelling places in their castles, of Aaron's Kohathite families, for theirs was the lot. Henry calls the Levites' cities castles, because God was both their portion and their protection, and notes the list matches Joshua 21 apart from spelling changes as cities change names. — Matthew Henry
