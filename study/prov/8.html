Study Notes — Proverbs

Chapter 8

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Wisdom crying and understanding putting forth her voice is Christ as Wisdom calling to the sons of men, for God's will is made known by creation and conscience, but more clearly by Moses and the prophets. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Standing in high places, by the way, in the places of the paths, Wisdom makes her call public, and the chief difficulty is only getting men to attend to instruction. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Crying at the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors, Wisdom meets men where they gather, so none can plead the call never reached them. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Unto you, O men, I call, and my voice is to the sons of man: the call is universal, and attention to the words of Christ guides even the most ignorant into saving knowledge. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Simple ones are bid to understand wisdom and fools to be of an understanding heart, for where there is willingness to receive truth in love, wisdom is valued above silver and gold. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: Hearing, for Wisdom will speak excellent things and her lips right things, is the attention Henry names as the chief difficulty and the chief remedy. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: Her mouth speaking truth, with wickedness an abomination to her lips, shows the words of Christ guiding into saving knowledge of the truth. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: All her words in righteousness, nothing froward or perverse, are the plain words of Christ that an understanding heart receives in love. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Her words plain to him that understands and right to them that find knowledge confirm that attention to Christ's words guides the ignorant into truth. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: Receiving instruction and not silver, knowledge rather than choice gold, is valuing wisdom above silver and gold where the heart is willing to receive truth. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: Wisdom better than rubies, with nothing desired comparable to it, is the valuation Henry sets on an understanding heart receiving truth in love. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Wisdom dwelling with prudence and finding out knowledge of witty inventions is Christ, in whom are all treasures of wisdom and knowledge, and from whom all prudence and skill come. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: The fear of the LORD hating evil, pride, arrogancy, evil ways, and froward mouth, shows why pride and froward conversation render men unwilling to hear humbling, awakening, holy instructions. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: Counsel and sound wisdom, understanding and strength, are Wisdom's own, for true religion gives the best counsel in difficult cases and makes the way plain. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: By Wisdom kings reign and princes decree justice, for all prudence and skill are from the Lord, and his wisdom orders the affairs of rulers. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: By Wisdom princes rule and nobles, even all judges of the earth, for through Christ's wisdom rulers are guided, and his counsel makes their way plain. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: Loving them that love her, and being found by those who seek early, is Christ never saying seek in vain: seek him early, earnestly, before anything else. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: Riches and honour with Wisdom, durable riches and righteousness, are the lasting goods of grace, unlike wealth gotten by vanity, which soon diminishes. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: Her fruit better than gold and fine gold, her revenue than choice silver, are goods laid up not for days and years but for eternity. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: Leading in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment, is Christ by his Spirit guiding believers into all truth. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: Causing those who love her to inherit substance and filling their treasures is the happiness of those who receive Wisdom in the love of Christ Jesus. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: The LORD possessing Wisdom in the beginning of his way, before his works of old, is the Son of God engaged in creation, ordained before the world to his great work. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

23 COMMENTARY: Being set up from everlasting, from the beginning, before the earth was, shows how able and fit the Son, who was Creator, is to be Saviour of the world. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

24 COMMENTARY: Brought forth when there were no depths and no fountains abounding with water, Wisdom predates creation, ordained before the world to save. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

25 COMMENTARY: Brought forth before mountains were settled and before the hills, Wisdom was with the Father before any part of the world was made. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

26 COMMENTARY: While he had not yet made earth, fields, or the highest dust of the world, the Son was already engaged in the work of creation. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

27 COMMENTARY: When he prepared the heavens, Wisdom was there, setting a compass upon the face of the deep, the Creator present at his own work. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

28 COMMENTARY: Establishing clouds above and strengthening fountains of the deep are the Creator's acts in which the Son, Wisdom, was engaged from the beginning. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

29 COMMENTARY: Giving the sea its decree and appointing the foundations of the earth are the works of the Son who was ordained before the world to save it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

30 COMMENTARY: Then Wisdom was by him, as one brought up with him, daily his delight, rejoicing always before him, the Son rejoicing in his work for the salvation of man. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

31 COMMENTARY: Rejoicing in the habitable earth, with delights among the sons of men, shows the Son delighting in saving wretched sinners, and we should delight in his salvation. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

32 COMMENTARY: Hearkening, O children, for blessed are they that keep her ways, is the readiness of children with which we should hearken to Christ's voice. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

33 COMMENTARY: Hearing instruction, being wise, and refusing it not is the exhortation to hear Christ's word and not refuse such mercy. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

34 COMMENTARY: Blessed is the man who hears, watching daily at the gates, waiting at the posts of the doors, for blessed are those who wait on the Saviour with daily reading, meditation, and prayer. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

35 COMMENTARY: Whoso finds Wisdom finds life and obtains favour of the LORD, for Christ is Wisdom and Life to believers, and God's favour comes only by finding Christ and being found in him. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

36 COMMENTARY: He that sins against Wisdom wrongs his own soul, and all who hate her love death, for sinners die because they will die, which justifies God when he judges. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
