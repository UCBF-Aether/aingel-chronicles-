Study Notes — Proverbs

Chapter 2

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Receiving Wisdom's words and hiding the commandments within is the earnest seeking that Henry promises will never be complained of as lost labour. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

2 COMMENTARY: Inclining the ear to wisdom and applying the heart to understanding is the diligence that goes with the freeness of the gift: seek and find, ask and receive. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

3 COMMENTARY: Crying after knowledge and lifting up the voice for understanding marks those whom God favours: the righteous, on whom his image is renewed. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

4 COMMENTARY: Seeking wisdom as silver and searching for it as hid treasures is the earnestness that God answers, without doing away with our diligence. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

5 COMMENTARY: Then the seeker shall understand the fear of the LORD and find the knowledge of God, for if we depend on God and seek wisdom from him, he will enable us to keep the paths of judgment. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

6 COMMENTARY: For the LORD gives wisdom, and from his mouth come knowledge and understanding, the gift that rewards earnest seeking. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

7 COMMENTARY: He lays up sound wisdom for the righteous and is a buckler to them that walk uprightly, keeping them in the paths of judgment. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

8 COMMENTARY: He keeps the paths of judgment and preserves the way of his saints, so that those who depend on God are enabled to walk uprightly. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

9 COMMENTARY: Then the seeker shall understand righteousness, judgment and equity, every good path, since wisdom renewed in righteousness guides the whole life. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

10 COMMENTARY: When wisdom enters into the heart and knowledge is pleasant to the soul, it not only fills the head but rules the heart and preserves from within and without. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

11 COMMENTARY: Discretion shall preserve and understanding shall keep the wise, guarding against corruptions within and temptations without. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

12 COMMENTARY: Wisdom delivers from the way of the evil man who speaks froward things, for the wise man will shun such company and its ways of darkness. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

13 COMMENTARY: Those who leave the paths of uprightness to walk in darkness choose ways that are uncomfortable and unsafe, leaving plain, pleasant, lightsome paths for folly. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

14 COMMENTARY: They rejoice to do evil and delight in the frowardness of the wicked, taking pleasure both in committing sin and in seeing others commit it. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

15 COMMENTARY: Their ways are crooked and they froward in their paths, the very darkness from which true wisdom preserves its possessor. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

16 COMMENTARY: Wisdom also delivers from the strange woman who flatters with her words, a snare of fleshly lusts that defile the body, the living temple, and war against the soul. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

17 COMMENTARY: She forsakes the guide of her youth and forgets the covenant of her God, and Henry adds that this caution also warns against idolatry and subjecting the soul to the body. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

18 COMMENTARY: Her house inclines unto death and her paths unto the dead, a fatal snare from which it is very rare that any caught recover themselves. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

19 COMMENTARY: None that go unto her return again or take hold of the paths of life, so hardened is the heart and blinded the mind by the deceitfulness of this sin. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

20 COMMENTARY: The purpose of this deliverance is that the wise may walk in the way of good men and keep the paths of the righteous. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

21 COMMENTARY: The upright shall dwell in the land and the perfect shall remain in it, for to the righteous the earth is a place of preparation for heaven. — Matthew Henry (Concise)

22 COMMENTARY: But the wicked shall be cut off from the earth and transgressors rooted out of it, for to the wicked this earth is all the heaven they shall ever have, and they must share in the miseries of their latter end. — Matthew Henry (Concise)
