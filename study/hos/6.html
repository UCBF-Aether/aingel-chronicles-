Study Notes — Hosea

Chapter 6

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Come, and let us return unto the Lord, for he has torn, and he will heal us, he has smitten, and he will bind us up, because the same hand that tore is the hand that heals, and return is the proper answer to chastisement. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: After two days will he revive us, in the third day he will raise us up, and we shall live in his sight, because the revival is pictured as rising after a short burial, which Henry reads as Israel's restoration and, beyond it, as resurrection hope. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Then shall we know, if we follow on to know the Lord, his going forth is prepared as the morning, and he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter and former rain unto the earth, because knowledge of God grows by following on, and his coming is as certain as dawn and as fruitful as rain. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: O Ephraim, what shall I do unto thee, O Judah, what shall I do unto thee, for your goodness is as a morning cloud, and as the early dew it goes away, because their repentance evaporates like cloud and dew, promising much at dawn and gone by noon. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Therefore have I hewed them by the prophets, I have slain them by the words of my mouth, and thy judgments are as the light that goes forth, because the prophets' words cut like an axe, and the judgments they declared are as plain as daylight. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice, and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings, because God values the heart's mercy and knowledge above the ritual that is offered without them. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: But they like men have transgressed the covenant, there have they dealt treacherously against me, because the covenant was broken as Adam broke his, by men who knew the terms and broke them anyway. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Gilead is a city of them that work iniquity, and is polluted with blood, because the city beyond Jordan, meant as a refuge, has become a workshop of iniquity stained with blood. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: And as troops of robbers wait for a man, so the company of priests murder in the way by consent, for they commit lewdness, because the priests, banded like robbers, waylay travellers on the road to Shechem, turning the way to worship into a road of murder. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: I have seen a horrible thing in the house of Israel, there is the whoredom of Ephraim, Israel is defiled, because the idolatry of the whole house is summed up as one horrible thing, seen plainly by God. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: Also, O Judah, he has set a harvest for thee, when I returned the captivity of my people, because Judah too has a harvest of judgment appointed, to be reaped when the captivity of God's people is turned. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
