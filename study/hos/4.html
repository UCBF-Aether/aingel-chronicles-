Study Notes — Hosea

Chapter 4

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Hear the word of the Lord, ye children of Israel, for the Lord has a controversy with the inhabitants of the land, because there is no truth, nor mercy, nor knowledge of God in the land, because the prophet opens a lawsuit, and the charge is the absence of the three things that make a people God's. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: By swearing, and lying, and killing, and stealing, and committing adultery, they break out, and blood touches blood, because the sins of the second table run together, and violence follows violence without interval. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Therefore shall the land mourn, and every one that dwells therein shall languish, with the beasts of the field, and with the fowls of heaven, yea, the fishes of the sea also shall be taken away, because the curse on man's sin reaches the whole creation under his dominion. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: Yet let no man strive, nor reprove another, for thy people are as they that strive with the priest, because the people are so hardened that reproof is useless, and they contend even against the priest who should teach them. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Therefore shalt thou fall in the day, and the prophet also shall fall with thee in the night, and I will destroy thy mother, because priest and prophet fall together, and the mother, the nation itself, is destroyed with them. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge, because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt be no priest to me, seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I will also forget thy children, because the priests rejected the knowledge they were charged to keep, and the people perish with them. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: As they were increased, so they sinned against me, therefore will I change their glory into shame, because increase of numbers and wealth only increased sin, and the glory they boasted in will be turned to shame. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: They eat up the sin of my people, and they set their heart on their iniquity, because the priests live off the sin offerings and therefore profit from the very sins they should have prevented. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: And there shall be, like people, like priest, and I will punish them for their ways, and reward them their doings, because priest and people have become alike, and judgment falls on both without distinction. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: For they shall eat, and not have enough, and they shall commit whoredom, and shall not increase, because they have left off to take heed to the Lord, because appetite pursued apart from God never satisfies and never multiplies. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: Whoredom and wine and new wine take away the heart, because sensual indulgence, whether idolatrous or drunken, robs a man of understanding, which is why the nation cannot see its own ruin. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: My people ask counsel at their stocks, and their staff declares unto them, for the spirit of whoredoms has caused them to err, and they have gone a whoring from under their God, because they consult wooden idols and divining rods instead of God, led astray by the very spirit of idolatry. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: They sacrifice upon the tops of the mountains, and burn incense upon the hills, under oaks and poplars and elms, because the shadow thereof is good, therefore your daughters shall commit whoredom, and your spouses shall commit adultery, because the high places, chosen for their pleasant shade, become schools of both idolatry and immorality. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: I will not punish your daughters when they commit whoredom, nor your spouses when they commit adultery, for themselves are separated with whores, and they sacrifice with harlots, therefore the people that does not understand shall fall, because the fathers' example excuses nothing, and a people without understanding falls by its own leaders' sins. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

15 COMMENTARY: Though thou, Israel, play the harlot, yet let not Judah offend, and come not ye unto Gilgal, neither go ye up to Bethaven, nor swear, the Lord lives, because Judah is warned not to follow Israel's example nor to join the pilgrimages to the corrupt shrines. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

16 COMMENTARY: For Israel slides back as a backsliding heifer, now the Lord will feed them as a lamb in a large place, because the nation, stubborn as a heifer that will not be led, will be left to wander in a wide and empty place. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

17 COMMENTARY: Ephraim is joined to idols, let him alone, because the attachment to idols has become so fixed that God gives the nation over to its choice, which is the heaviest judgment of all. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

18 COMMENTARY: Their drink is sour, they have committed whoredom continually, her rulers with shame do love, give ye, because even their drink is corrupted, and their rulers love the shameful exactions that feed their revels. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

19 COMMENTARY: The wind has bound her up in her wings, and they shall be ashamed because of their sacrifices, because idolatry, like wind, carries them away, and the sacrifices they trusted in will end in shame. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
