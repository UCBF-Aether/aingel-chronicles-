Study Notes — Hosea

Chapter 2

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Say ye unto your brethren, Ammi, and to your sisters, Ruhamah, because after the sentences of Lo-ammi and Lo-ruhamah, the restored people are addressed again by the names of mercy, as a promise to be proclaimed to one another. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Plead with your mother, plead, for she is not my wife, neither am I her husband, and let her put away her whoredoms out of her sight and her adulteries from between her breasts, because Israel, as the unfaithful wife, is charged to abandon the idolatry she flaunts. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Lest I strip her naked, and set her as in the day that she was born, and make her as a wilderness, and set her as a dry land, and slay her with thirst, because the punishment answers the sin, stripping the adulteress of the gifts she spent on her lovers. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: And I will not have mercy upon her children, for they be the children of whoredoms, because the children share the mother's character, having been bred in the idolatry they now practise. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: For their mother has played the harlot, she that conceived them has done shamefully, for she said, I will go after my lovers, that give me my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, mine oil and my drink, because Israel credits Baal and the nations with the very provisions that came from God. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: Therefore, behold, I will hedge up thy way with thorns, and make a wall, that she shall not find her paths, because God will block the road to idols with affliction, so that the way to sin becomes impassable. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: And she shall follow after her lovers, but she shall not overtake them, and she shall seek them, but shall not find them, then shall she say, I will go and return to my first husband, for then was it better with me than now, because disappointed idolatry drives her back to the husband she left. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: For she did not know that I gave her corn, and wine, and oil, and multiplied her silver and gold, which they prepared for Baal, because every gift of harvest and wealth was spent upon the idol, while the Giver was forgotten. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: Therefore will I return, and take away my corn in the time thereof, and my wine in the season thereof, and will recover my wool and my flax given to cover her nakedness, because God reclaims the gifts that were abused, withdrawing them in their very seasons. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: And now will I discover her lewdness in the sight of her lovers, and none shall deliver her out of mine hand, because the shame she hid in prosperity will be exposed when her protectors fail her. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: I will also cause all her mirth to cease, her feast days, her new moons, and her sabbaths, and all her solemn feasts, because even the festivals, corrupted into idol feasts, will be taken away with the idolatry they served. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: And I will destroy her vines and her fig trees, whereof she has said, these are my rewards that my lovers have given me, and I will make them a forest, and the beasts of the field shall eat them, because the rewards of idolatry will be laid waste and given to wild beasts. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: And I will visit upon her the days of Baalim, wherein she burned incense to them, and she decked herself with her earrings and her jewels, and she went after her lovers, and forgat me, says the Lord, because the whole calendar of Baal worship will be reckoned against her, down to the ornaments worn for it. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: Therefore, behold, I will allure her, and bring her into the wilderness, and speak comfortably unto her, because after judgment God woos rather than drives, leading her to a place where the lovers cannot follow, to speak to her heart. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

15 COMMENTARY: And I will give her her vineyards from thence, and the valley of Achor for a door of hope, and she shall sing there, as in the days of her youth, and as in the day when she came up out of the land of Egypt, because the valley of trouble, where Achan was judged, becomes the doorway back to the joy of the exodus. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

16 COMMENTARY: And it shall be at that day, says the Lord, that thou shalt call me Ishi, and shalt call me no more Baali, because Ishi, my husband, replaces Baali, my lord, and the very name of mastery gives way to the name of marriage. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

17 COMMENTARY: For I will take away the names of Baalim out of her mouth, and they shall no more be remembered by their name, because restoration includes a cleansing of speech, so that the idols are not even named again. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

18 COMMENTARY: And in that day will I make a covenant for them with the beasts of the field, and with the fowls of heaven, and with the creeping things of the ground, and I will break the bow and the sword and the battle out of the earth, and will make them to lie down safely, because peace is restored to the whole creation around the restored people. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

19 COMMENTARY: And I will betroth thee unto me for ever, yea, I will betroth thee unto me in righteousness, and in judgment, and in lovingkindness, and in mercies, because the new marriage is founded on God's own character, not on Israel's merit. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

20 COMMENTARY: I will even betroth thee unto me in faithfulness, and thou shalt know the Lord, because faithfulness on God's side produces knowledge of God on Israel's side, which is the heart of the covenant. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

21 COMMENTARY: And it shall come to pass in that day, I will hear, says the Lord, I will hear the heavens, and they shall hear the earth, because prayer is answered through a chain of obedience, heaven answering earth and earth answering man. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

22 COMMENTARY: And the earth shall hear the corn, and the wine, and the oil, and they shall hear Jezreel, because the harvest itself answers the sown people, and Jezreel, once a name of judgment, is heard again as the people God sows. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

23 COMMENTARY: And I will sow her unto me in the earth, and I will have mercy upon her that had not obtained mercy, and I will say to them which were not my people, thou art my people, and they shall say, thou art my God, because the three names of judgment are all reversed in one sentence of restoration. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
