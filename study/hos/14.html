Study Notes — Hosea

Chapter 14

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: O Israel, return unto the Lord thy God, for thou hast fallen by thine iniquity, because the final call names the cause plainly, that Israel fell by its own iniquity, and therefore return must be to God himself. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Take with you words, and turn to the Lord, say unto him, take away all iniquity, and receive us graciously, so will we render the calves of our lips, because repentance is to be spoken, not merely felt, and the offering of the lips replaces the offerings of calves. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Asshur shall not save us, we will not ride upon horses, neither will we say any more to the work of our hands, ye are our gods, for in thee the fatherless finds mercy, because the three false trusts, Assyria, cavalry, and idols, are all renounced together, and mercy is found in God by those who have no other father. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: I will heal their backsliding, I will love them freely, for mine anger is turned away from him, because healing is promised for the very disease of backsliding, and love is free, since anger itself has turned away. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: I will be as the dew unto Israel, he shall grow as the lily, and cast forth his roots as Lebanon, because God as dew gives silent, nightly refreshment, and Israel grows like a lily above and roots like Lebanon below. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: His branches shall spread, and his beauty shall be as the olive tree, and his smell as Lebanon, because the restored tree spreads wide, is beautiful as the olive, and fragrant as the cedars of Lebanon. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: They that dwell under his shadow shall return, they shall revive as the corn, and grow as the vine, the scent thereof shall be as the wine of Lebanon, because those sheltered under Israel's shade revive like corn and grow like the vine, with a scent like Lebanon wine. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Ephraim shall say, what have I to do any more with idols, I have heard him, and observed him, I am like a green fir tree, from me is thy fruit found, because Ephraim renounces idols, and God answers as a green fir, evergreen and fruitful, from whom all Ephraim's fruit is found. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: Who is wise, and he shall understand these things, prudent, and he shall know them, for the ways of the Lord are right, and the just shall walk in them, but the transgressors shall fall therein, because the book closes by appealing to the wise, and the same ways that guide the just cause the transgressors to fall. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
