Study Notes — Hosea

Chapter 10

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Israel is an empty vine, he brings forth fruit unto himself, according to the multitude of his fruit he has increased the altars, according to the goodness of his land they have made goodly images, because the vine, fruitful only for itself, turns every blessing into another altar and every good land into another image. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Their heart is divided, now shall they be found faulty, he shall break down their altars, he shall spoil their images, because a divided heart cannot stand, and God himself will break the altars and spoil the images it built. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: For now they shall say, we have no king, because we feared not the Lord, what then should a king do to us, because when the king is lost, the people confess that a king who did not fear God could do them no good anyway. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: They have spoken words, swearing falsely in making a covenant, thus judgment springs up as hemlock in the furrows of the field, because false oaths in covenants make judgment grow like poisonous hemlock across the furrows. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: The inhabitants of Samaria shall fear because of the calves of Bethaven, for the people thereof shall mourn over it, and the priests thereof that rejoiced on it, for its glory, because it is departed from it, because the calf of Bethel, once the glory of the shrine, will depart, and priests who rejoiced in it will mourn with the people. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: It shall be also carried unto Assyria for a present to king Jareb, Ephraim shall receive shame, and Israel shall be ashamed of his own counsel, because the idol itself will be carried as tribute to Assyria, and the counsel that trusted it will end in shame. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: As for Samaria, her king is cut off as the foam upon the water, because the king, like foam on a stream, is carried off without resistance and without trace. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: The high places also of Aven, the sin of Israel, shall be destroyed, the thorn and the thistle shall come up on their altars, and they shall say to the mountains, cover us, and to the hills, fall on us, because the shrines of iniquity will be overgrown with thorns, and the people will prefer burial under mountains to the sight of judgment. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: O Israel, thou hast sinned from the days of Gibeah, there they stood, the battle in Gibeah against the children of iniquity did not overtake them, because the sin that began at Gibeah has continued unbroken, and the battle that should have overtaken evildoers there did not overtake them. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: It is in my desire that I should chastise them, and the people shall be gathered against them, when they shall bind themselves in their two furrows, because God will gather the nations against Israel for chastisement, binding them as oxen are bound in their furrows for the yoke. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: And Ephraim is as a heifer that is taught, and loves to tread out the corn, but I passed over upon her fair neck, I will make Ephraim to ride, Judah shall plough, and Jacob shall break his clods, because Ephraim, a heifer taught only to tread out corn and eat, will be put to the yoke and made to plough, with Judah alongside. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: Sow to yourselves in righteousness, reap in mercy, break up your fallow ground, for it is time to seek the Lord, till he come and rain righteousness upon you, because the fallow heart must be broken up and sown with righteousness, and seeking the Lord is the work of the season until he rains righteousness. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: Ye have ploughed wickedness, ye have reaped iniquity, ye have eaten the fruit of lies, because thou didst trust in thy way, in the multitude of thy mighty men, because the harvest answers the sowing, and trust in one's own way and in many mighty men reaps the fruit of lies. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: Therefore shall a tumult arise among thy people, and all thy fortresses shall be spoiled, as Shalman spoiled Betharbel in the day of battle, the mother was dashed in pieces upon her children, because the spoiler's tumult will wreck every fortress, as Shalman wrecked Beth-arbel, where mothers were dashed with their children. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

15 COMMENTARY: So shall Bethel do unto you because of your great wickedness, in a morning shall the king of Israel utterly be cut off, because Bethel, the house of the calf, will do to Israel what it has always done, and the king will be cut off at morning, suddenly, at the day's beginning. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
