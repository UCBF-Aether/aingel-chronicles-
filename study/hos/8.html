Study Notes — Hosea

Chapter 8

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Set the trumpet to thy mouth, he shall come as an eagle against the house of the Lord, because they have transgressed my covenant, and trespassed against my law, because the alarm is sounded, and the invader swoops like an eagle upon a house that has broken covenant and law. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Israel shall cry unto me, my God, we know thee, because in distress the nation claims knowledge of God, though the claim comes only when judgment has arrived. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: Israel has cast off the thing that is good, the enemy shall pursue him, because the good that was cast off was God himself and his covenant, and the enemy's pursuit is the consequence of that casting off. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: They have set up kings, but not by me, they have made princes, and I knew it not, of their silver and their gold have they made them idols, that they may be cut off, because kings made without God and idols made from wealth are both rebellions, and both end in cutting off. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: Thy calf, O Samaria, has cast thee off, mine anger is kindled against them, how long will it be ere they attain to innocency, because the calf at Bethel, which Samaria trusted, is itself cast off, and anger burns until the people learn innocence they have never learned. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: For from Israel was it also, the workman made it, therefore it is not God, the calf of Samaria shall be broken in pieces, because an idol made by a workman in Israel cannot be God, and the calf will be smashed as the work of hands deserves. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: For they have sown the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind, it has no stalk, the bud shall yield no meal, if so be it yield, the strangers shall swallow it up, because sowing empty wind reaps a destroying whirlwind, and even the crop that seems to grow will be eaten by strangers. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Israel is swallowed up, now shall they be among the Gentiles as a vessel wherein is no pleasure, because the nation, swallowed by exile, lies among the nations like a vessel no one wants. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: For they are gone up to Assyria, a wild ass alone by himself, Ephraim has hired lovers, because Ephraim, like a wild ass that loves solitude yet runs to others for help, has hired allies instead of trusting God. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: Yea, though they have hired among the nations, now will I gather them, and they shall sorrow a little for the burden of the king of princes, because the hired alliances will not prevent the gathering for judgment, and the tribute to Assyria's king will become their sorrow. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: Because Ephraim has made many altars to sin, altars shall be unto him to sin, because altars multiplied for atonement became altars multiplied for sin, and the remedy became the disease. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: I have written to him the great things of my law, but they were counted as a strange thing, because the law, written at length for Israel, was treated as foreign, as if it belonged to strangers. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: They sacrifice flesh for the sacrifices of mine offerings, and eat it, but the Lord accepts them not, now will he remember their iniquity, and visit their sins, they shall return to Egypt, because sacrifices eaten without obedience are not accepted, and the iniquity they were meant to cover will be remembered, returning the people to an Egypt of bondage. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: For Israel has forgotten his Maker, and builds temples, and Judah has multiplied fenced cities, but I will send a fire upon his cities, and it shall devour the palaces thereof, because forgetting the Maker leads to trusting in buildings, and fire will devour the temples and palaces alike. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
