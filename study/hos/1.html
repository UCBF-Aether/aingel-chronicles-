Study Notes — Hosea

Chapter 1

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The word of the Lord came to Hosea the son of Beeri, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash, king of Israel, because Hosea's ministry spans the last flourishing and the decline of both kingdoms. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: The beginning of the word of the Lord by Hosea commands him to take a wife of whoredoms and children of whoredoms, because the land has committed great whoredom, departing from the Lord, and the prophet's own house is made a living parable of Israel's unfaithfulness. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: So he went and took Gomer the daughter of Diblaim, who conceived and bare him a son, because the prophet obeys the hard sign, and the marriage itself becomes the sermon. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: God said, call his name Jezreel, for yet a little while and I will avenge the blood of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu, and will cause the kingdom of the house of Israel to cease, because Jehu's massacre at Jezreel, though used as judgment, was itself bloodguilt that would be repaid. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: At that day I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel, because the valley that gave Israel its military strength will become the place where that strength is broken. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: She conceived again and bare a daughter, and God said, call her name Lo-ruhamah, for I will no more have mercy upon the house of Israel, but I will utterly take them away, because the name declares that the season of sparing mercy is ended. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: But I will have mercy upon the house of Judah, and will save them by the Lord their God, and will not save them by bow, nor by sword, nor by battle, by horses, nor by horsemen, because Judah's deliverance will be God's own work, not the work of armies. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: Now when she had weaned Lo-ruhamah, she conceived and bare a son, because the signs follow in order, each child adding a further sentence upon the nation. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: Then said God, call his name Lo-ammi, for ye are not my people, and I will not be your God, because the covenant bond is declared broken from Israel's side, and the name pronounces the divorce. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: Yet the number of the children of Israel shall be as the sand of the sea, which cannot be measured nor numbered, and it shall come to pass that in the place where it was said unto them, ye are not my people, there it shall be said unto them, ye are the sons of the living God, because judgment does not cancel the promise, and restoration reverses the very sentence. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: Then shall the children of Judah and the children of Israel be gathered together, and appoint themselves one head, and they shall come up out of the land, for great shall be the day of Jezreel, because the divided kingdoms are reunited under one head, and Jezreel, the name of scattering, becomes the day of sowing again. — Matthew Henry; Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
