Study Notes — Leviticus

Chapter 20

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The LORD speaks to Moses, giving penal sanctions for the crimes of chapter 18, beginning with giving seed to Molech. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

2 COMMENTARY: Whoever of Israel or sojourning strangers gives seed to Molech shall surely be put to death, the people of the land stoning him, the stoning being carried out by witnesses pushing him from an eminence onto a stone, then completing the death with stones. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

3 COMMENTARY: God will set His face against that man and cut him off, because he has given seed to Molech, defiling the sanctuary and profaning God's holy name. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

4 COMMENTARY: If the people of the land hide their eyes from the man and do not kill him, that is, connive at countrymen practising Molech's horrid rites, God denounces severest penalties against the connivers and their families. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

5 COMMENTARY: God will set His face against that man and his family and cut off him and all who go a-whoring after him to commit harlotry with Molech, the family sharing the penalty for connivance. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

6 COMMENTARY: The person who turns to mediums and familiar spirits to go a-whoring after them, God will set His face against him and cut him off, as with Molech harlotry. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

7 COMMENTARY: Therefore they shall sanctify themselves and be holy, for the LORD is their God, the minute specification of crimes here showing their sad prevalence among idolatrous neighbours and Israel's proneness to follow them. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

8 COMMENTARY: They shall keep His statutes and do them, for He is the LORD who sanctifies them, holiness being both command and gift. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

9 COMMENTARY: Everyone who curses father or mother shall surely be put to death, his blood being upon him, and where no mode is described, stoning is meant. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

10 COMMENTARY: The man who commits adultery with his neighbour's wife, adulterer and adulteress, shall surely be put to death, stoning being the implied mode. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

11 COMMENTARY: A man lying with his father's wife has uncovered his father's nakedness, and both shall surely be put to death, their blood upon them. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

12 COMMENTARY: A man lying with his daughter-in-law, both shall surely be put to death, for they have wrought confusion, their blood upon them. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

13 COMMENTARY: A man lying with mankind as with womankind has committed abomination with the other, and both shall surely be put to death, their blood upon them. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

14 COMMENTARY: A man taking a wife and her mother commits wickedness, and they shall be burnt with fire, the only instance of another capital form, probably stoning first and the body afterwards consumed by fire. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

15 COMMENTARY: A man lying with a beast shall surely be put to death, and the beast shall be slain, the unnatural crime being purged with both parties. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

16 COMMENTARY: A woman approaching any beast to lie down thereto shall be put to death, woman and beast, for they have wrought confusion. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

17 COMMENTARY: A man taking his sister, father's daughter or mother's daughter, and seeing her nakedness, it is a wicked thing, and they shall be cut off in the sight of their people, the public excision affixing indelible ignominy. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

18 COMMENTARY: A man lying with a woman in her sickness and uncovering her fountain shall both be cut off from among their people, the same public cutting off as for sister incest. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

19 COMMENTARY: He shall not uncover the nakedness of his mother's sister or father's sister, for he uncovers his near kin, and they shall bear their iniquity, the minute enumeration showing the prevalence of such crimes among neighbours. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

20 COMMENTARY: A man lying with his uncle's wife has uncovered his uncle's nakedness, and they shall bear their sin and die childless, either having no children by God's judgment or their offspring being denied ordinary privileges in Israel. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

21 COMMENTARY: A man taking his brother's wife, it is an unclean thing, he has uncovered his brother's nakedness, and they shall be childless, the lesser grade of penalty for this alliance. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

22 COMMENTARY: They shall keep all His statutes and judgments and do them, that the land where He brings them to dwell does not spew them out, as it spewed out the nations for these crimes. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

23 COMMENTARY: They shall not walk in the manners of the nation God casts out before them, for they committed all these things, and therefore God abhorred them. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

24 COMMENTARY: God has said He will give them the land flowing with milk and honey for an inheritance, and He has separated them from other people, their selection being to preserve knowledge and worship of the true God amid universal apostasy, with clean-unclean distinction as a great means of separation. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

25 COMMENTARY: They shall put difference between clean beasts and unclean, and between unclean fowls and clean, and not make their souls abominable by beast, fowl, or creeping thing separated as unclean, the meat distinction being repeated here with emphatic solemnity. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

26 COMMENTARY: They shall be holy unto the LORD, for He is holy, and He has severed them from other people, that they should be His. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

27 COMMENTARY: A man or woman with a familiar spirit, or a wizard, shall surely be put to death, the people stoning them with stones, their blood upon them, stoning being the mode where none is described. — Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
