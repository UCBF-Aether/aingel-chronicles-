Study Notes — 1 Enoch

Chapter 80

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

2 COMMENTARY: Here the tone alters: in the days of the sinners the years shall be shortened and all things on earth shall alter and not appear in their season. The luminaries themselves are made to mislead, because of the sin of men. — W. O. E. Oesterley

4 COMMENTARY: The moon shall alter her order and not appear at her time — the ordered heavens of the earlier chapters now disordered by human sin. — W. O. E. Oesterley

No note for verse(s) 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 — the public-domain sources consulted give no distinct explanation for these verses beyond the list itself, so nothing was invented for them.
