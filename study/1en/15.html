Study Notes — 1 Enoch

Chapter 15

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

2 COMMENTARY: The intercession is reversed: the Watchers should intercede for men, not men for them. — W. O. E. Oesterley

3 COMMENTARY: God charges them with leaving the high, holy, eternal heaven, lying with women, defiling themselves with the daughters of men, and begetting giants. — W. O. E. Oesterley

8 COMMENTARY: The giants produced from spirits and flesh shall be called evil spirits upon the earth, and on the earth shall be their dwelling. — W. O. E. Oesterley

No note for verse(s) 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12 — the public-domain sources consulted give no distinct explanation for these verses beyond the list itself, so nothing was invented for them.
