Study Notes — 2 Thessalonians

Chapter 1

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Paul writes with Silvanus and Timothy to a church that stands in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ, not merely in a city or a sect. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: Grace comes first and peace follows it, both from the Father and from Christ, so the greeting itself teaches the order of salvation. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Thanksgiving is a debt here, because their faith grew exceedingly and their love abounded, and where grace truly is, it increases. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Paul glories in them before other churches, since their patience and faith held under every persecution and tribulation they endured. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: Their patient suffering is a manifest token of God's righteous judgment, fitting them to be counted worthy of the kingdom for which they suffer. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: It is righteous with God to repay tribulation to those who trouble his people, since his justice will not leave wrongdoing unanswered. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: Rest is promised to the troubled, together with the apostles, at the revelation of the Lord Jesus from heaven with his mighty angels. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: That day brings vengeance in flaming fire on those who know not God and do not obey the gospel, so ignorance and disobedience are both judged. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: Their punishment is everlasting destruction from the presence of the Lord, a ruin made dreadful above all by separation from his glory and power. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: On that day Christ will be glorified in his saints and admired in all who believe, his grace and power shining in what he has wrought in them. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: Paul therefore prays always that God would count them worthy of their calling and fulfil all the good pleasure of his goodness in them with power. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: The end of it all is that Christ's name be glorified in them and they in him, according to the grace of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ. — Matthew Henry
