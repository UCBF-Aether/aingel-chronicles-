Study Notes — Joshua

Chapter 3

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: Joshua rose early in the morning and removed with all Israel from Shittim to Jordan, lodging there before passing over, and Henry notes his early rising shows how little he sought his own ease. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: After three days the officers went through the host to give orders for the crossing. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: The people were commanded that when they saw the ark of the covenant and the priests bearing it, they were to remove from their place and go after it, walking after the rule of the word and the direction of the Spirit. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: A space of about two thousand cubits was to be kept between them and the ark, and they were not to come near it, for they had not passed this way before, an untrodden path through Jordan. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: Joshua told the people to sanctify themselves, for tomorrow the Lord would do wonders among them, since to experience God's love and power we must put away sin and not grieve the Holy Spirit. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: Joshua spoke to the priests to take up the ark of the covenant and pass over before the people, and they did so. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: The Lord told Joshua that this day he would begin to magnify him in the sight of all Israel, that they might know that as he was with Moses so he would be with Joshua. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: Joshua was to command the priests bearing the ark that when they came to the brink of Jordan's waters they should stand still in Jordan. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: Joshua called the children of Israel to come and hear the words of the Lord their God. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: Hereby they would know the living God was among them and would without fail drive out the Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites, and Jebusites, for God's appearances for his people ought to encourage faith and hope. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: The ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth would pass over before them into Jordan, and the word of the Lord was as truly with Joshua as with Moses. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: They were to take twelve men out of the tribes of Israel, out of every tribe a man, in preparation for the memorial to come. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: As soon as the soles of the priests' feet rested in Jordan's waters, the waters coming down from above would be cut off and stand upon a heap, a miracle never done before except in the dividing of the Red Sea. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: When the people removed from their tents to pass over Jordan, the priests bearing the ark went before the people. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: As those bearing the ark came to Jordan and their feet were dipped in the brim of the water, for Jordan overflowed all its banks in harvest time, the power of God was magnified. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: The waters from above stood and rose up upon a heap far from the city Adam beside Zaretan, and those coming down toward the salt sea failed and were cut off, so the people passed over right against Jericho. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: The priests bearing the ark stood firm on dry ground in the midst of Jordan while all Israel passed over clean, and Henry notes this passage shadowed the believer's passage through death to heaven, with Jesus, typified by the ark, gone before. — Matthew Henry
