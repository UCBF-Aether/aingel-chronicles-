Study Notes — Joshua

Chapter 14

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: These are the countries Israel inherited in Canaan, which Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of Israel's tribes distributed for inheritance to them. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: By lot was their inheritance, as the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses, for the nine tribes and the half tribe, and Henry notes God shall choose our inheritance for us, better by his infinite wisdom than by our own ignorance. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: For Moses had given the inheritance of two tribes and a half tribe on the other side of Jordan, but to the Levites he gave no inheritance among them. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: For Joseph's children were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim, therefore they gave no part to the Levites in the land, save cities to dwell in with their suburbs for cattle and substance. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: As the Lord commanded Moses, so Israel did, and they divided the land. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: Then Judah's children came to Joshua in Gilgal, and Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenezite said to him, Thou knowest the thing the Lord said to Moses concerning me and thee in Kadeshbarnea. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: Caleb recalled he was forty years old when Moses sent him from Kadeshbarnea to espy out the land, and he brought Moses word again as it was in his heart. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: His brethren who went up with him made the people's heart melt, but Caleb wholly followed the Lord his God. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: Moses swore that day that surely the land whereon Caleb's feet had trodden would be his inheritance and his children's forever, because he had wholly followed the Lord. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: Now the Lord had kept Caleb alive, as he said, these forty-five years since the Lord spoke this word to Moses while Israel wandered in the wilderness, and now Caleb was eighty-five years old. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: As yet Caleb was as strong that day as in the day Moses sent him, his strength then as now, for war, both to go out and to come in. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: Now therefore Caleb asked, Give me this mountain whereof the Lord spoke in that day, for he had heard the Anakims were there and the cities great and fenced, and if the Lord would be with him he would drive them out as the Lord said, valuing the promise far above mere providence. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: Joshua blessed him and gave to Caleb son of Jephunneh Hebron for an inheritance. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: Hebron therefore became Caleb's inheritance to this day, because he wholly followed the Lord God of Israel, and Henry notes singular piety shall be crowned with singular favour, for Caleb answered to his name, which signifies all heart. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: The name of Hebron before was Kirjatharba, and Arba was a great man among the Anakims, and the land had rest from war. — Matthew Henry
