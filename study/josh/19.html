Study Notes — Joshua

Chapter 19

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The second lot came forth to Simeon, for Simeon's tribe according to their families, and their inheritance was within Judah's inheritance. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: Out of Judah's portion was Simeon's inheritance, for Judah's part was too much for them, and therefore Simeon had their inheritance within Judah's inheritance, and Henry notes Judah did not oppose taking away cities within their border when convinced they had more than was right, since love seeketh not her own. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: The third lot came up for Zebulun's children according to their families, and the border of their inheritance was unto Sarid. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: This is the inheritance of Zebulun's children according to their families, these cities with their villages, and Henry notes in the division to each tribe the prophetic blessings of Jacob were fulfilled, in the manner and places he foresaw. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: The fourth lot came out to Issachar, for Issachar's children according to their families. — Matthew Henry

23 COMMENTARY: This is the inheritance of Issachar's tribe according to their families, the cities with their villages. — Matthew Henry

24 COMMENTARY: The fifth lot came out for Asher's tribe according to their families. — Matthew Henry

31 COMMENTARY: This is the inheritance of Asher's tribe according to their families, these cities with their villages. — Matthew Henry

32 COMMENTARY: The sixth lot came out to Naphtali's children, even for Naphtali's children according to their families. — Matthew Henry

39 COMMENTARY: This is the inheritance of Naphtali's tribe according to their families, the cities with their villages. — Matthew Henry

40 COMMENTARY: The seventh lot came out for Dan's tribe according to their families. — Matthew Henry

47 COMMENTARY: The coast of Dan's children went out too little for them, therefore they went up and fought against Leshem, took it, smote it with the sword, possessed it, and dwelt therein, calling Leshem Dan after their father's name. — Matthew Henry

48 COMMENTARY: This is the inheritance of Dan's tribe according to their families, these cities with their villages. — Matthew Henry

49 COMMENTARY: When they had made an end of dividing the land by their coasts, Israel gave an inheritance to Joshua son of Nun among them, and Henry notes Joshua waited till all the tribes were settled before asking any provision for himself, preferring the common welfare before private advantage. — Matthew Henry

50 COMMENTARY: According to the word of the Lord they gave him the city he asked, even Timnathserah in mount Ephraim, and he built the city and dwelt therein. — Matthew Henry

51 COMMENTARY: These are the inheritances Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of Israel's tribes divided by lot in Shiloh before the Lord, at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and so they made an end of dividing the country. — Matthew Henry

No note for verse(s) 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 25, 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 33, 34, 35, 36, 37, 38, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, 46 — the public-domain sources consulted give no distinct explanation for these verses beyond the list itself, so nothing was invented for them.
