Study Notes — Joshua

Chapter 4

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: When all the people were clean passed over Jordan, the Lord spoke to Joshua, commanding a memorial of the crossing. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: Joshua was to take twelve men out of the people, out of every tribe a man. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: They were commanded to take from the midst of Jordan, where the priests' feet stood firm, twelve stones, carry them over, and leave them in the lodging place for the night. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Joshua called the twelve men he had prepared, out of every tribe a man. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: He told them to pass over before the ark into the midst of Jordan and take up every man a stone upon his shoulder, according to the number of the tribes. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: This was to be a sign among them, so that when their children asked in time to come what these stones meant, they would have an answer. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: They were to answer that the waters of Jordan were cut off before the ark of the covenant when it passed over, so these stones would be a memorial forever, since the works of the Lord are worthy of remembrance and man's heart is prone to forget them. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: The children of Israel did as Joshua commanded, taking twelve stones from the midst of Jordan as the Lord had spoken, and carrying them to the lodging place. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: Joshua also set up twelve stones in the midst of Jordan, in the place where the priests' feet stood, and they remain there to this day. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: The priests bearing the ark stood in the midst of Jordan until everything was finished that the Lord commanded Joshua to speak to the people, according to all Moses commanded, and the people hasted and passed over. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: When all the people were clean passed over, the ark of the Lord passed over with the priests in the presence of the people. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: The children of Reuben, Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh passed over armed before the children of Israel, as Moses had spoken to them. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: About forty thousand prepared for war passed over before the Lord to battle, to the plains of Jericho. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: On that day the Lord magnified Joshua in the sight of all Israel, and they feared him as they had feared Moses all his life, and Henry notes those are feared in the best manner who make it appear that God is with them. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: The Lord then spoke to Joshua, giving the next command concerning the ark. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: Joshua was to command the priests bearing the ark of the testimony to come up out of Jordan. — Matthew Henry

17 COMMENTARY: Joshua commanded the priests, saying, Come ye up out of Jordan, and the priests with the ark did not stir till ordered to move. — Matthew Henry

18 COMMENTARY: When the priests came up out of Jordan and their feet were lifted to dry land, the waters of Jordan returned to their place and flowed over all its banks as before. — Matthew Henry

19 COMMENTARY: The people came up out of Jordan on the tenth day of the first month and encamped in Gilgal, on the east border of Jericho. — Matthew Henry

20 COMMENTARY: The twelve stones taken out of Jordan Joshua pitched in Gilgal, as a standing memorial. — Matthew Henry

21 COMMENTARY: He told Israel that when their children asked their fathers in time to come what these stones meant, they were to be taught. — Matthew Henry

22 COMMENTARY: They were to let their children know that Israel came over this Jordan on dry land, since it is the duty of parents to tell their children betimes of the words and works of God. — Matthew Henry

23 COMMENTARY: For the Lord their God dried up the waters of Jordan before them until they were passed over, as he had done to the Red Sea before their fathers. — Matthew Henry

24 COMMENTARY: This was done that all the people of the earth might know the hand of the Lord, that it is mighty, and that Israel might fear the Lord their God forever. — Matthew Henry
