Study Notes — Joshua

Chapter 16

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The lot of Joseph's children fell from Jordan by Jericho to the water of Jericho on the east, to the wilderness going up from Jericho throughout mount Bethel, and Henry notes Joseph's children, next to Judah, had the post of honour and the first and best portion in the north, as Judah had in the south. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: So Joseph's children, Manasseh and Ephraim, took their inheritance, and Henry notes these settled boundaries may remind us that our situation and provision in this life, as well as our future inheritance, are appointed by the only wise and righteous God, with whom we should be content. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: They drove not out the Canaanites that dwelt in Gezer, but the Canaanites dwell among the Ephraimites to this day and serve under tribute, since God's people, now as of old, suffer his enemies to remain. — Matthew Henry

No note for verse(s) 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 — the public-domain sources consulted give no distinct explanation for these verses beyond the list itself, so nothing was invented for them.
