Study Notes — Joshua

Chapter 18

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: The whole congregation of Israel assembled at Shiloh and set up the tabernacle of the congregation there, and the land was subdued before them, and Henry notes Shiloh was in Ephraim's lot, Joshua's tribe, and its name is that by which Jacob prophesied of the Messiah, the resting-place of the ark typifying our great Peace-maker. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: There remained among Israel seven tribes which had not yet received their inheritance. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: Joshua asked Israel, How long are ye slack to go to possess the land the Lord God of their fathers had given them, and Henry notes that as God has given us title to the heavenly Canaan, we are slack to take possession of it by faith, hope, and holy joy. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: They were to give out three men for each tribe, whom Joshua would send to walk through the land and describe it according to their inheritance, and return to him. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: They were to divide the land into seven parts, with Judah abiding in his coast on the south and Joseph's house in their coasts on the north. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: They were to describe the land into seven parts, bring the description to Joshua, and he would cast lots for them there before the Lord in Shiloh. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: But the Levites had no part among them, for the priesthood of the Lord was their inheritance, and Gad, Reuben, and half Manasseh had received theirs beyond Jordan eastward, which Moses gave them. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: The men arose and went away, and Joshua charged those going to describe the land to walk through it, describe it, and come again to him, that he might cast lots for them before the Lord in Shiloh. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: The men went and passed through the land, describing it by cities into seven parts in a book, and came again to Joshua to the host at Shiloh. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: Joshua cast lots for them in Shiloh before the Lord, and there divided the land to Israel according to their divisions. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: The lot of Benjamin's tribe came up according to their families, and the coast of their lot came forth between Judah's children and Joseph's children. — Matthew Henry

20 COMMENTARY: Jordan was the border of Benjamin's inheritance on the east side, and Henry notes the boundaries of each portion were distinctly drawn and each tribe's inheritance settled, preventing all contests and selfish claims by God's wise appointment, who allotted hill and valley, corn and pasture, brooks and rivers, towns and cities. — Matthew Henry

21 COMMENTARY: Now the cities of Benjamin's tribe according to their families are listed, beginning with Jericho, Bethhoglah, and the valley of Keziz. — Matthew Henry

No note for verse(s) 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28 — the public-domain sources consulted give no distinct explanation for these verses beyond the list itself, so nothing was invented for them.
