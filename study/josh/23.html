Study Notes — Joshua

Chapter 23

Everything below is COMMENTARY on the chapter — it explains the verses; it is not Scripture, and it never changes the Scripture text. Notes are condensed from public-domain commentators, and each note names its source.

Read the chapter itself: Standard · Modern

1 COMMENTARY: It came to pass a long time after the Lord had given rest to Israel from all their enemies round about, that Joshua waxed old and stricken in age. — Matthew Henry

2 COMMENTARY: Joshua called for all Israel, their elders, heads, judges, and officers, and said to them, I am old and stricken in age. — Matthew Henry

3 COMMENTARY: He reminded them they had seen all the Lord their God had done to all these nations because of them, for the Lord their God was he that had fought for them. — Matthew Henry

4 COMMENTARY: Behold, he had divided to them by lot these nations that remained, to be an inheritance for their tribes, from Jordan, with all the nations he had cut off, even to the great sea westward. — Matthew Henry

5 COMMENTARY: The Lord their God would expel them from before them and drive them from their sight, and they would possess their land, as the Lord had promised them. — Matthew Henry

6 COMMENTARY: They were therefore to be very courageous to keep and do all written in the book of the law of Moses, turning not aside to the right hand or to the left, keeping with care, doing with diligence, and regarding with sincerity what was written. — Matthew Henry

7 COMMENTARY: They were not to come among these nations remaining among them, neither make mention of the name of their gods, nor cause to swear by them, neither serve them, nor bow themselves unto them. — Matthew Henry

8 COMMENTARY: But they were to cleave unto the Lord their God, as they had done unto this day. — Matthew Henry

9 COMMENTARY: For the Lord had driven out from before them great nations and strong, but as for them, no man had been able to stand before them unto this day. — Matthew Henry

10 COMMENTARY: One man of them would chase a thousand, for the Lord their God was he that fought for them, as he had promised them. — Matthew Henry

11 COMMENTARY: They were to take good heed therefore unto themselves, that they loved the Lord their God, since to cleave to the Lord they must always stand upon their guard, for many a soul is lost through carelessness. — Matthew Henry

12 COMMENTARY: Else if they did in any wise go back and cleave to the remnant of these nations, marrying with them and going in unto them and they unto Israel. — Matthew Henry

13 COMMENTARY: They were to know for a certainty that the Lord would no more drive out any of these nations from before them, but they would be snares and traps unto them, scourges in their sides, and thorns in their eyes, until they perished from off this good land the Lord had given them. — Matthew Henry

14 COMMENTARY: Behold, that day Joshua was going the way of all the earth, and they knew in all their hearts and souls that not one thing had failed of all the good things the Lord had spoken concerning them, for all had come to pass unto them, and not one thing had failed thereof. — Matthew Henry

15 COMMENTARY: Therefore it would come to pass that as all good things had come upon them which the Lord had spoken, so the Lord would bring upon them all evil things, until he had destroyed them from off this good land he had given them. — Matthew Henry

16 COMMENTARY: When they transgressed the covenant of the Lord their God which he commanded them, and went and served other gods and bowed themselves to them, then the anger of the Lord would be kindled against them, and they would perish quickly from off the good land he had given them, and Henry notes the way of sin is downhill: friendship with idolaters, then marrying with them, then serving their gods. — Matthew Henry
