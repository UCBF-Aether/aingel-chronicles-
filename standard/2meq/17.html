2 Meqabyan

Chapter 17

1 And the grain of, if it dieth not, is not eaten and beareth not [fruit]; for if it dieth, the grain of maketh root in the earth, and bringeth forth stalk, and becometh, and beareth [fruit] — and know.

2 For you, that there should be many sins — one grain of.

3 And likewise this grain is eaten of wind and water and earth; and the sun the fire; for that which [is] without the sun cannot bear the fruit of.

4 And the wind; and that which [is] without wind cannot bear the fruit of; and the water which watereth and the earth.

5 And as the moist ashes and earth maketh root, and upward, and beareth [fruit] in the measure of the blessing of God.

6 For the grain of [is] in the likeness of Adam, upon whom the Spirit of God breathed —; and the tree of the vine likewise is watered with water, and maketh root, and the water ascendeth the fine root.

7 And it ascendeth unto heaven, in the city; for the dew of mercy from God shall them, in the city; in the flame of the sun, and it maketh fruit which [is] from God.

8 And it maketh a goodly which gladdeneth the heart; and when they eat it, it satisfieth as water and bread which hungereth not and thirsteth not; and when it, it maketh blood.

9 And when they drink it, it gladdeneth the heart of man, as is said in the Psalm: Wine gladdeneth the heart of man; and it maketh drunk, when his mouth, and and in his, and his blood in his heart.

10 And it him that [is] without understanding; for the drunkenness of wine exceedingly; and it him for and for, as the wide earth, and he knoweth not; and that [is] in his hands and his feet.

11 And thus God made the tree of the vine, and its fruit, that His name should be for them that do His will, and for them that believe the resurrection of the dead.

12 And He shall gladden them in the abode of life, for them that believe the resurrection of the dead.
