2 Meqabyan

Chapter 1

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Concerning what Meqabis did unto the Jews in Mesopotamia of Syria: and he slew them, with all those of them, unto the environs of Jerusalem — from Iyaboq and from Ṭəfʿa — unto the holy precinct.

2 For they marched forth from Samaria unto the environs of Jerusalem, and all its villages; and they slew with the edge of the sword, until there remained not save one; for the Edomites joined — and the Syrians, and the Amalekites — with Meqabis the Moabite, who destroyed the city of Jerusalem.

3 And when the children of Israel rebelled, Meqabis the Moabite sent unto them, and slew them — all of them — with a slaughter.

4 And concerning this thing, the wrath of God went up against His holy city; and they arrayed themselves in their camp.

5 And the Edomites marched forth, and the Elofəli; and they seized [them], to take vengeance, [upon] the city of God — for of His own will He sent them, because they transgressed the word of God.

6 But Meqabis — his city was Remat of Moab — rose up mightily from his city, and arrayed himself with those that were with him.

7 And there gathered all the multitudes of Mesopotamia unto Syria, that they might destroy the city of God. And from thence he summoned the Elofəlawəyan and the Amalekites, and gave them much gold and silver, and horses and chariots, that they might be with him in the camp.

8 And they came together, and besieged the city; and they poured out their blood like water — of those who dwelt therein.

9 And they forgot Jerusalem — like — and he wrought therein filth and refuse, and all that pleaseth not God; by the doing of they defiled the city of God, which was full of righteousness and holiness.

10 And they left their slain — O Thou that doest — as food for the birds of heaven, and their flesh — O Thy righteous — for the beasts of the desert.

11 And they were shut in, the multitudes; and the pangs of death — and as for our enemies, they brought forth that which was in the belly, until God shall have mercy — [God] who trieth the heart and the kidneys — for those without the fear of God did as Satan taught them.

12 And they repented in their streets, while they rejoiced because of the evil which they had done against the people of God; and He took away grace and mercy which I shewed, from the holy city.

13 And when they entered and returned into their dwellings, they made joy, and rejoicing, and encampment.
