2 Meqabyan

Chapter 2

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And thus said the prophet, the seer, unto him that spake: "Now rejoice alone in the light of the hour; for behold, the Lord, the Holy One of Israel, shall avenge thee, for thou hast not taken heed"

2 my horses — and concerning this.

3 "But as for me, I will look from the eagles — they shall rush that assail thee — and I will not from them; and the wrath of God is upon thee."

4 "And we: 'I shall gird my sword, and it shall not be withheld from me — arrows, bow, and därəbəyo, kwəyanəwə, and ʾäkəkoki — likewise with därəbəyo the sword, which thou hast not avenged,' said the Lord, the Holy One of Israel. But I will bring evil plague into thy heart — bow, and därəbəyo, kwənatə, and, siḥə, and ʾäbäqə.

5 "And I will bring evil plague into thy heart, and thou shalt be pained — who shall help thee? — and thou shalt not find [help]; for behold, my wrath [is kindled], and I will not from my hand until I destroy thy memorial from the earth."

6 "And Thou hast shewn me that I am the Lord thy Maker. When I do this thing, O my creature, in the twinkling of an eye — for thou hast exalted thy neck and lifted up thy head against my city — for thou art like grass before the face of the wind, which the fire devoureth before mine eyes; and like ashes which the winds carry away from before the face of the earth — likewise thou, by my "

7 "For behold my wrath; and I know thee not, O my Maker — and I will not spare any of these, and there shall not remain that shall lament in all thy mountains.

8 "And now repent from all the evil which thou hast done; but if thou repentest not and humblest not thyself before God with weeping and with wailing, and beseechest Him with a pure heart — God shall forgive thy sins in all wherein thou hast transgressed before Him."

9 And at that time Meqabis wept before God because of his sin, and put on ashes; for God had mercy.

10 And he declared that God will not turn aside the evil which He spoke by the mouth of His prophet; for His eyes are open and slumber not, and His ears are uncovered and are not deaf, and not the word which He spoke; and He shall perform it in one moment, in the twinkling of an eye.

11 And he rent his garments, and put on sackcloth, and cast ashes upon his head; and he wept and cried out before the Lord his God, because of the evil which he had done.
