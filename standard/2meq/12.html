2 Meqabyan

Chapter 12

1 Woe unto you, O mighty men of Israel, who kept not what I commanded you in the tabernacle, and did not my will — besides your own will: pride and and fornication and and and and in falsehood.

2 And because of this my wrath burneth against you, as which [is] before the face of fire, and as fire which burneth the, and as the wind of the whirlwind which lifteth the of the from the earth, and scattereth it in the height of the air, whither there is not found from its deed.

3 And likewise I will destroy them all, who do from before my face, said God, the Holy One of Israel; and remember God, who ruleth all, and there is none who is like Him.

4 And He shall love them who love Him, and He shall unto them their sins and their transgressions — unto those who walk in His commandment; and be not and of heart, without faith.

5 And make straight your souls unto God, and it shall seem good that ye may deliver your souls, and I will deliver you from the hand of your enemies, in the day of your.

6 And when ye cry unto Me, I will say unto you: Behold, I am with you; and I will deliver you from the hand of your enemies, and ye shall not in the day of your; for ye have trusted in Me, and done My commandment, and transgressed not My law; and ye have loved what I love, said God, who ruleth all.

7 And them that love Him He shall love, and them that keep His commandment He shall comfort; for merciful and is He.

8 And He shall the of His wrath, and shall not kindle with all His chastisement; for merciful is He, for He remembereth that they are flesh and blood; and when their souls go forth from their flesh, they shall in their.

9 And they know not where they are, until the time God desireth, that He should bring them from whence they were not; for He created them from whence they were not; and again He took their souls, and the earth in their.

10 And again He shall bring them from whence they were not, into being, as He desireth.

11 But Sirusaydan — his unto God — he multiplied boasting before God; and when he had until the time he desired, he lifted up his head.

12 And he said: My time is become as the time of heaven; and I am he who the sun, who dieth not for ever.

13 And while this word was not [yet] finished, there came down the angel of death, whose name is Ṭələməyakos, and smote his heart; and he died in his time; and he was from his, and from the multitude of his pride, and from his evil deed; for he knew not his Maker.

14 And the army of the king of the Chaldeans — while they were at the mouth of his city, and while they desired his; and when he died, and they went forth, and destroyed the land, and took away all his cattle, and there remained not in.

15 And all his property they took away, and they took, and burned with fire his city; and they entered into their land.
