2 Meqabyan

Chapter 19

1 O earth, which hast gathered unto thee kings and rulers and great ones and honoured ones, and women — and goodly virgins.

2 And for the strong ones, in whom [there is] and goodly; and in whom [there is] knowledge and understanding; and in whom [there is] a goodly word — as the word of the organ, and as the of the harp and the lyre.

3 And in whom [there is] a goodly song, they who gladden as the wine of Adam; their eyes bright as the star of morning.

4 And their right hand strong, as that which giveth and and; and their feet goodly to behold; and for them that run as the righteous.

5 I know thee, O death, that thou hast taken their souls, for the goodly; for by the will of the good God thou hast been sent.

6 And thou, O earth, for thou hast gathered many bodies, which from thee God brought forth and caused to enter unto thee — from thee, and, and unto thee we entered; by the will of God we rejoiced; by the will of God, upon thee.

7 And thou becamest a burying for our; we upon thee, and we in thy; we ate thy fruit, and upon thee our flesh.

8 We drank of the dew of thy, and thou drankest of the of our blood; we ate of the of thy earth, and upon thee of the of our bones.

9 We ate of the taste of ashes; thy dew good, as God commanded thee for our; and thou tookest our flesh, and thou didst, as God commanded thee for thy.

10 O, from thee, O death, that thou hast gathered many strong kings and rulers; and thou fearedst not their might and their greatness, as He commanded thee who created them; and thou didst not the poor.

11 And thou didst them [that are] goodly [in] their; and thou didst not leave the strong and the mighty; and thou didst not leave and; the and the goodly — the, and the children, the and the women.

12 And thou didst not leave them [that are] goodly [who] walk; and they transgressed not His commandment; and thou didst not leave them [that are] evil [who] walk. They who became as the beasts in their works; and they that [are] perfect in the of their and their word, and the sweetness of their speech.

13 And thou didst not leave them whose word [is] bitter, and [whose] mouth [is] full of cursing; and thou hast gathered their souls in thy treasuries — that [is] of light, and that [is] of darkness.

14 And the earth gathered their flesh — in the, and in the earth — until the blowing of the trumpet, when the dead shall rise.

15 And they shall be judged, the doers, until as their works which they did; for at the blowing of the trumpet, and at the commandment of God, the dead shall rise, as the twinkling of an eye; and then they shall rejoice, they who did good.
