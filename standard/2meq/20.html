2 Meqabyan

Chapter 20

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Believe that there shall not be left and there shall not be hidden all our works which we did upon the earth, when we shall stand before Him in fear and trembling.

2 And if there be not provision for our way, and if we have no garment for our nakedness.

3 And if we have no for our hands, and for our feet.

4 And we know not the way whither they shall take us — whether, and whether, and whether darkness, and whether; and in the midst, whether the sea, and Sheol.

5 And they that shall take us — we know them not, and we hear not their speech.

6 And we see not their faces; for darkness are they, and in darkness they shall lead us.

7 As David the prophet said, when upon me: Thou, O my Lord, know my way; in this way I walked, they hid for me a snare; and I the way of my right, and I saw and I that which knoweth me; and whither I shall escape I have not.

8 And this, when he said, for he knoweth that the angels shall him, and shall lead him whither he knoweth not the way; and he shall right and left, and there is none that shall find that which knoweth him.

9 And for him there is none that knoweth, but by himself in the midst of the angels of darkness.

10 And the spiritual angels of light who were sent unto the latter, that they should their souls for the righteous, and that they should unto the of the light of life.

11 And they that shall be sent — the angels of darkness and the angels unto the souls of the sinners, that they should receive them and take them into the judgment which is for ever, which [is] meet for them, that they should be judged as their works which they did.

12 Woe unto them, for the souls of the sinners whom they shall take into; and no rest, and no, and no escape, and no for ever, from that which found them.

13 Woe unto them, for the sinners; for in the way of Cain they walked, and in the counsel of the violence of Balaam they were; and the sin which they do, for in the they went down, they that they should take a strange, in violence which was not their.

14 They shall be judged as their works which they did, in the judgment of fire.
