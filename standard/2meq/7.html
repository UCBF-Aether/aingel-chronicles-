2 Meqabyan

Chapter 7

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Woe unto thee and those like thee who know not God who created you, and know not His commandment and His word; for ye have exchanged repentance for that wherein there is no profit — when ye were chastised by the of Sheol.

2 And ye shall have no escape from henceforth for ever — thou and thy gods and those whom thou makest to sacrifice and to bow down unto them as God your Maker; for they have no soul and no breath, and they profit not them that unto them, and not them that unto them.

3 For the work of the hand of man are they, in whom Satan speaketh for the destruction of the heart of such as thee, O perishing ones — that he may bring you down into Gehenna; [there shall be] fire for you and for the ministers of your gods, and for those who you — demons, unto you.

4 For ye know not that there is none who shall profit you: təgegəyu and ye err.

5 And better than you are the beasts which God created for your food — and and; for they have no second judgment besides one death.

6 But ye shall die, and be gathered again in the of Gehenna, wherein there is no escape for ever.

7 And when they had said this, they departed and were hidden from him.

8 But Sirusaydan remained in great fear and trembling, and departed not until the morning.

9 And from thence he went unto the of their corpses — O righteous ones — and found them not; for He desired to with them, and them; for God kept them, that they should not be from their flesh.
