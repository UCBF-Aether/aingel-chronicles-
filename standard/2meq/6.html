2 Meqabyan

Chapter 6

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And Sirusaydan made a great [image] — against God — the priesthood of falsehood and the ministers of the gods.

2 And they sacrifice unto him, and offer unto him.

3 And he imagineth that they eat and drink.

4 And he giveth them for a morning [offering] — wheat and firstfruits and and; and they sacrifice a ram and; and he eateth of that impure sacrifice.

5 And it is not for themselves alone that he doeth [it], but unto the stones he them, and them, that they may cry unto their gods.

6 And when they saw the children of Meqabis, that they were good, and worshipped God their Maker, the ministers of the gods desired to destroy them, that they might cry and eat of that impure sacrifice. And these blessed ones refused them.

7 And He asked them — O — for the commandment of your fathers they keep, and are zealous in good work, and fear God exceedingly.

8 And when they grew wroth with them, and were enraged against them, and oppressed them.

9 And they told Sirusaydan the king, that they refused to bow down and to cry unto his gods.

10 And because of this the king was wroth, and was grieved, and commanded to bring them; and they brought them, and set them before him; and he said unto them: Cry out, and bow down unto my gods.

11 And they answered and said unto him: We will not obey thee in this matter, and we will not cry unto thy impure gods.

12 And he threatened them with much torment, and it availed not; for they had hardened their heart unto God.

13 And he kindled a fire, and cast them [therein]; and they committed their souls unto God.

14 And He shewed them their life after death, while they held their swords full of — while they in the midst of his kingdom by night — and great fear.

15 And he said unto them: What shall I do unto you? My, and my soul shall not be lifted up, that I should do all which I commanded you; and I feared exceedingly.

16 And they told [him] all which they desired, while they said: Remember that God is thy Maker, and there is One who shall thee from this thy pride, and bring thee down into Gehenna beneath, with Diabolos thy father; and there thou shalt finish all thy being as He thee in the fire, while there is none who thee; and while we worship God our Maker, and bow down unto Him in His fear — unto God.

17 For He is the Maker of all, who made heaven and earth and the sea, and all which is therein.

18 The sun and the moon and the stars, and all the creatures which God made for Himself.

19 And there is no other god besides Him, in heaven above and in earth beneath, who is able [over] all; and there is none who is like Him — who killeth and giveth life, who chastiseth and sheweth mercy.

20 And there is none who is delivered from His hand; for the King of heaven and earth is He.

21 And there is none who transgresseth His commandment; from Him who created [thee] — besides thee and and those like thee — those whom thy father Satan their heart — they and thou and thy gods shall go down together into Gehenna, wherein [there is] no for ever.

22 For it is not thou alone who doest this, but Satan thy teacher, who taught thee this evil deed, that thou shouldest against us.

23 And thou knowest not God thy Maker, and thou hast lifted up thy head, that God is thy Maker.

24 And thou shalt be requited by the work of thy hands and by thy idols, until God shall thee, and bring thee down to the mouth of Gehenna; and He shall avenge thee because of all the transgression and sin which thou hast done upon the earth.
