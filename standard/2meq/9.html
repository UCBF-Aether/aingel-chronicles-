2 Meqabyan

Chapter 9

1 And thou, O man — and thou art not God — wherefore art thou proud? [Thou art] a man of today, and dust of the earth and ashes; and thou shalt become wood and in thy grave.

2 And unto Sheol thou shalt be rendered; and unto those who do thy deed; for thy teacher Seblianos, who upon his head the sin of all the world, because he destroyed our father Adam in his pride.

3 For he refused to bow down unto the work of his God, in the greatness of his head and in the of his.

4 And thou, as thy teacher, hast refused to unto God thy God.

5 And thou, walk as walked thy predecessors who knew not God their God — kingdoms.

6 And they were avenged and went down into Sheol, because of their evil deeds which they did upon the earth.

7 And thou likewise shalt go down into Gehenna.

8 It seemeth unto thee that thou shalt escape from the hand of God; for and and [is] the worship of God who gave thee dominion over

9 And He hath tried thee, whether thou doest His will or not; and if thou makest straight thy way upon the earth, God shall thy way, and all which thy hand bringeth He shall, and bless thee; and all thine enemies and thy foes He shall unto thee.

10 And thou shalt rejoice in thy and in thy and in the fruit of thy and in thy storehouse and in thy pasture; and wheresoever thou with thy fingers, and in thy heart — all shall be commanded unto thee; for from God it is unto thee, that thus thou shouldest do and plant and and.

11 And if thou refusest to hear His voice and His law — O God — and walkest not in His judgment, thou shalt have no to escape from the hand of God, like all the rebellious who [were] before thee, who worshipped not God in purity, and trusted not in His upright judgment; for righteousness [is] all His judgment.

12 And there is none who is hidden from before His eyes; and all [is] naked and before Him.

13 Now the dominion of kings is His, and the of the dwelling of the mighty is His.

14 And the exalter of the humble is He, and the raiser of the fallen is He.

15 And the opener of the bound is He, and the raiser of the dead is He; and as for those who their flesh, and become as dust, He shall them in the time He willeth; for a shadow of mercy is He from Him.

16 And as for those who did evil, He shall raise them — a resurrection of judgment; for they spurned [Him], and He shall avenge them.

17 And upon the earth He shall their seed; for the rebellious are they — against the law and the ordinance of God.

18 And the sinners shall not desire to in their counsel unto the righteous; for narrow [is] their unto the righteous, more than the ways of the sinners.

19 And as the heaven is high above the earth, so far [are] the ways of the righteous from the ways of the sinners.

20 But the ways of the sinners [are] the doing of darkness and təʿəgələt and violence and fornication and deceit and səsəʿət; and he his property and in violence.

21 And in the of blood, and in that which profiteth not; and for death, and the eating of dead flesh and blood, and the eating of the flesh of and camel and; in a woman and which [is] without purity.

22 And all these [are] the deeds of the sinners — a broad way, and a of the of Satan, which leadeth into darkness and the being which [is] for ever.

23 But the ways of the righteous [are] and, which lead into life: humility and, love and peace, fasting and prayer and of the flesh, and keeping from the eating which profiteth not: dead flesh and and fornication, and woman to woman.

24 And they keep themselves from all deed, and from impure foods which are not commanded in the Law, and from deed which God loveth not; for all this do the sinners.

25 But the righteous shall be hidden from all evil which God loveth not.

26 And He shall love them, and comfort them, and deliver them from all their tribulations.

27 But as for the sinners, Satan shall them; for they keep his law and his ordinance, and all which he loveth.
