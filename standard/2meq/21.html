Chapter 21

1 Where were they that gathered a strange in violence which was not their, and the work of their hands.

2 But for they shall take a strange which [is] without, and they shall gather while they know not the day of their death — thou, thou shalt come upon them, and they shall leave for another their.

5 As David said: I saw the sinner great and exalted as the cedar of Lebanon; and when I passed, I him, I sought and I found not his place.

6 And as their for the sinners, in, that there should not be they that their neighbours, while they that they die not; because they gathered a strange in.

7 O servants, remember that ye shall pass away; and your shall pass away with you; and in the multitude of your gold and your shall become that which was.

8 And ye multiply children, there shall become many graves; and ye multiply houses, there shall become.

9 And ye multiply cattle, there shall become for your enemies; for ye did not the will of the Lord your God; and all that your hands, there shall not be found, for it was not blessed.

10 of the house, and of the desert; in the and, and in and in the of food, and and wine.

11 And in the fruit of your, ye shall not rejoice; but sorrow shall become unto you from all your enemies; for God shall not you with all the men of your house; for ye kept not His commandments, [even] of the Lord.

12 But for them that kept His law and His commandment and His ordinance, He shall not them of His blessing; and He shall give them all that they asked; and He shall bless for them the fruit of their land and the fruit of their.

13 And He shall them above all the peoples that surround them, that they should be; and that they should be; and He shall give them all blessing, in the and their fruit.

14 And all that their hands, He shall bless for them the fruit of their works; and the of their cattle; and He shall gladden them in the fruit of their.

15 And He shall for them their cattle, and He shall save them from all their tribulations; and sickness and and from that which they know and they know not.

16 And He shall for them in righteousness, and He shall save them from and from evil speech, and from all that; for before He made the tabernacle the Levite who and keepeth the work of the tabernacle; and he walketh by the will of God; by the former law He shall give him the tithe in all that [is] in his; and all, in the firstborn of man and unto cattle.

17 And the city of refuge in all their lands, as Moses commanded Joshua the son of Nun, that there should be saved the slayer who in ignorance killed; and if with knowledge.

18 Until there shall enter for him judgment for him that judgeth and for him that justifieth.

19 And as, he shall say unto them: with him; and he shall say: For in ignorance there fell from my hand a stone, or a stone, or a tree, and there died he that fell upon him; I make known unto you, save him, for in ignorance he did, he shall be saved.

20 But if with knowledge he did, he shall be judged as his transgression; and there is none that shall him; but if in ignorance he did and killed, him, that he should not die, for in ignorance he did.

21 And thus Moses made the children of Israel, that they should not transgress the commandment of the good God; but that they should from all evil, He made them.

22 And He commanded them that they should walk in all His judgment; and righteousness exceedingly, they shall be far from and and death, and from all that [is] not meet for the children of men; that they should do they that walk by the commandment of God, and they that cease from all evil, as He made them.

23 And He commanded them that they should not transgress His commandment which He ordained for them, in the vision which the waters; which is the tabernacle, that He should save their souls, and they should find their dwellings with their fathers who did good.

24 For they that believed in the word of God, and walked by His commandment — these shall be called the generation of the righteous; for from Adam and Seth [are] they, they who did the will of God, they shall not.

25 For the seed of Adam [are] we, and in His likeness He us, and in His image He created us, that we should good; and all that loveth God.

26 And if we did good, we shall inherit the land of life, with them that did good; for He changeth not and He not for them that love Him.

27 And exceedingly He loveth them, and He heareth their prayer, for them that call upon Him in purity; and He their repentance for them that repent in; and He giveth them might and for them that keep His law and His ordinance and His commandment.

28 And they shall rejoice with Him in His kingdom which [is] forever, they who did His will — first and last; to Him they shall ascribe glory now and until forever. Amen.
