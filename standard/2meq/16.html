2 Meqabyan

Chapter 16

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Behold thou — for thyself — that which is in thy flesh, in the labour of thy hands and thy feet, and the of thy head; for in their time thou shalt them, and and; and in this know, and if thou hast understanding and knowledge, unto faith.

2 And from whence goeth forth, thou shalt say: These [are] the labours of thy hands and thy feet and the of thy head — it is God who made them, that they should; that thou shouldest know, thou, the resurrection of the dead which shall be — that thy flesh in flesh another, that thou shouldest know for thyself that thou shalt rise from the dead.

3 And thou shalt be judged, thou, as thy works which thou hast done — transgression and sin — in the resurrection of the dead; because thou didst cause to err the people, when thou saidst: There is no resurrection of the dead.

4 And now thou seest, when there cometh unto thee the day of thy judgment; for thou that sowedst shall not that there should be eaten the fruit and the.

5 But thou that plantedst a tree shall not in the fruit the tree of the vine, and the tree of the fig — there shall not be changed its and its fruit.

6 And if thou plantest the vine, there shall not be changed that it should become the fig; if thou plantest the fig, there shall not be changed that it should become the vine; and if thou sowest, there shall not be changed that it should become.

7 And if thou sowest, there shall not be changed that it should become; all these — in their seed, and in their, and in their fruit, and in their tree, and in their, and in their — shall receive blessing, in the dew of mercy which [is] from God, and shall bring forth fruit.

8 Likewise the earth shall bring forth, in that which God sowed — there shall eat soul and flesh, the doers of good, while they shall not be changed by the evil doers; and the evil doers, while they shall not be changed by the doers of good — these whom God sowed shall eat soul and flesh.

9 And as there cometh his time for the blowing of the trumpet, the dead shall rise, in the dew of mercy which [is] from God — they who did good, in the resurrection of life; and their [shall be] the garden of rejoicing, a dwelling for the pure, which He prepared for the latter, where there is no sickness and no toil, where they shall not die thence again.

10 But as for the evil doers, in the resurrection of judgment, with the devil who deceived them.

11 And his evil angels who desire not that they should be saved from all the children of men, in the judgment which is for ever.

12 Cast down beneath, in the of darkness, where [there is] weeping and; where there is no and no mercy, and where there is no escape for ever; for they did not good in their life upon the earth, while they were in their flesh.

13 And because of this they shall be in the resurrection of soul and flesh.

14 For others, for them who believed not the resurrection of soul and flesh together — thou likewise — God shall shew the greatness of His knowledge.

15 And He shall judge all — [TRUNCATED in the source; the verse breaks off mid-word.]

16 As his work, and as the of his hand.
