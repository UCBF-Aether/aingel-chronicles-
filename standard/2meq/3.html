1 And the prophet spoke; for Remat of Moab was near unto Syria.

2 And he dug the ground and went down unto his neck; and he wept a bitter weeping, and repented because of the sin which he had done before God.

3 And thus said the Lord unto the prophet: "Enter thou into the land of Judah, unto the land of Remat, unto Meqabis the prince of Moab; and say unto him: Thus said the Lord: For it was not thou that didst this thing, that thou shouldest swallow [it] up by the strength of my might and by my; thou destroyedst the holy city — but I, even I, am God.; of my own will I sent thee, that thou shouldest destroy my city."

6 "And now repent with all thy heart, and; and be not slack; for those who are slack keep not unto repentance."

7 "And blessed are they who repent with all their heart, and turn not back unto filth and sin, in all wherein they repent, because of their evil.

8 "And let them keep with weeping and with tears, and with much supplication, and with prostration unto God; for He said unto the repentant: 'Ye — after your error ye repented, ye.'"

9 "And unto he said unto him: 'After the error, when he returneth unto Him in repentance, because of thy affliction and thy fear, I will forgive thy sins; for I am the Lord, a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and unto the fourth generation; but unto them that love me and keep my commandments I shew mercy with them unto [a thousand?] generations.'"

10 "And now, concerning these thy children whom thou hast begotten, I will keep my covenant which is with thee, and the repentance which I have made because of the evil of thy deeds, said the Lord, the Holy One of Israel,.

11 And at that time he went forth from and bowed down unto the prophet, while he said thus — and thus: "May God cause me to inherit — that it depart not from thee — but cause me to inherit that which thou commandest; for I have spurned God, and have not walked in His commandment as my fathers; for we have no law, but it was shewn unto us that we should worship idols, as our fathers taught us."

12 "But as for me, I did not hearken unto the voice of His prophets of God until now; for I have sinned — I who walked in my sins, and in the pride of my heart, and in the lifting up of my neck — wherein I spurned the commandment of God, and walked not in His statutes, and him which he commanded me.

13 And the prophet took up [his word] and said unto him: "I know that now thy repentance is accepted; for there is none before you who shall be reckoned for his sin of your kindred

14 "And from this time forsake the worship of idols, and turn thou into the counsel of God, that thy repentance may be in truth." And he bowed down at his feet unto the prophet; and the prophet raised him up, and commanded him all fitting good deeds.

15 And he did as God commanded him, and returned into his house.
