2 Meqabyan

Chapter 4

1 And [there arose one] who was faithful: Hosea.

2 And [there arose one] who was faithful: Gideon.

3 And [there was one] who was faithful: Samson, and Barak, and Deborah, and Judith — sometimes with women and sometimes with, He raiseth up for them judges, that they might deliver them from those who oppress them; and as He delivered them from the hand of all their enemies.

4 And as He willed to deliver them, God [delivered them] from those who oppress them.

5 And they rejoiced greatly in all which He made straight for them: in the seed of their land, and their cattle multiplied, and in all the pasture which is in the field and which is in the desert.

6 And He blessed their cattle, and their crops, and all which they possessed, of house and of field; He blessed them, and He diminished not their cattle, for in His sight He watched over them; and again He loved them, for they are a righteous generation.

7 And when their became evil in their works, He gave them into the hand of their enemies.

8 And when He slayeth them, then the Prince them; and they turn and unto God their Maker.

9 And when they turn with all their heart, He heareth them; and their former transgressions He remembereth not unto them; for He remembereth that they are flesh and blood, and among them is the counsel of this world and of demons.

10 But Meqabis, when he heard this word which God ordained in the mount of His sanctuary, strove in repentance.

11 And he turned not aside from doing; and he heard this word. And because of all the chosen who, the children of Israel — for God shewed mercy unto them — and when God chastiseth them when they transgress the commandment, they weep and cry out; and again He sheweth mercy unto them, and they do His commandment.

12 And likewise Meqabis established his way, and kept the commandment, and walked in His commandment — unto God, the God of Israel.

13 He, the Prince, from whom they heard; and in all wherein they him, the children of Israel — likewise let him, O priest, in the keeping of His commandment unto God.

14 And he shall order his house and his children, that they may walk in all his way and His commandments unto God.

15 And the devoted things of the Jews who are devoted — he himself shall; and the commandments which the Jews keep, when they hear, likewise he shall do; and the which the Jews, he shall, while he [is] another — his people [being] Moabite.

16 And the tithes he shall tithe; and the firstfruits, his firstborn, and his, and his, and all which I have chosen, he shall give; and the sacrifices which the Jews sacrifice, likewise he shall sacrifice — the way of the face unto Jerusalem.

17 And as for that which [is] blessedness; and as for that which [is] sin; and as for that which [is] will — and the sacrifice of salvation; and the sacrifice of.

18 And the first of the fruits, and the which the Jews bring forth, likewise he shall bring forth, and give unto; and all which the Jews do, likewise he shall do; and incense.

19 And he made, and, and, and, and, and; and for the holy ministrations, and, that they might do [them] in the holy of holies of Israel — when they keep [them] unto God.

20 For they abode in His law and in His ordinance; and when God them, and putteth them not into the hand of their enemies — likewise Meqabis in all his way.

21 And he shall pray at all times unto God, the God of Israel, that they may be and separate not from the congregation of Israel — [even] those who and did [His] will.

22 And again he shall pray that He give seed in Zion, and in Jerusalem; and that He deliver [them] from those [things] which by the mouth of the prophet; and that his repentance be accepted in all wherein he repented.

23 And he wept before God with weeping; and as He destroyeth not the seed from the earth; and as He keepeth him in His and in His.

24 And from the nations — the Moabites who [were] under his hand — unto Meqabis they, that they might believe; for upright [is] the way — they walk, their king; and they keep judgment, and do His will; and they establish judgment in their land; and in their land; and they see that and greatly, O Meqabis.

25 And they come unto him, and hear the judgment of his righteousness, and the chosen unto Meqabis.

26 And in him [is] much, and, and, and, and; and in him [are] horses which wear swords

27 And from he shall worship God; when he walketh in, there is none who except of himself who.

28 But they come with the might of their gods, that they might him and him in the name of their gods; and there is none who him, for he hath set his trust upon God his God.

29 And he dwelt while he did thus; and while he his enemy, and bringeth the nations under his hand.

30 And he shall take vengeance on the violent; and he shall vindicate the judgment of the children of death.

31 And he shall them unto the, and unto the pangs of death them; and he shall them unto the, from his; and he shall clothe them — the naked — from his garment.

32 And he shall rejoice in his work, and in the work of his hands; and he shall give from what he hath, while he withholdeth not; and the tithe he shall give unto the house of God. And this, while he did [it], Meqabis rested in goodness.
