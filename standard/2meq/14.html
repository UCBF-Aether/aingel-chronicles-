2 Meqabyan

Chapter 14

1 For me, it grieveth me exceedingly, and it me, and my heart me, because of the Jews and Samaritans and Pharisees and Sadducees, who believe not the resurrection of the dead. The Jews say: Let us eat and drink — dust we shall die, and there there is none who seeth.

2 But the Samaritans say: Our flesh shall not rise, for dust it shall become.

3 But if there be a resurrection — a resurrection of our souls we believe; for the soul, when the flesh dieth, dieth not; for as the wind is it, which is not seen, and as the sound of thunder — and they say not and it is not shewn.

4 But our flesh is seen in all, and becometh ashes and dust; for the beasts eat it, and eat it in the grave.

5 And they who eat it shall become ashes, and there shall not be found; for as they shall become, and as that which is not created they became ashes.

6 And in this shall be found the flesh which; and thus say the Pharisees: The resurrection of the dead we believe, but He shall clothe the souls in the heavens [with] another flesh, which is not that which [is] on earth.

7 And thus say the Sadducees: When our soul goeth forth from our flesh, we shall not rise from the dead; and there is not among them a resurrection of the dead, neither for soul nor for flesh; and when we die, we shall not rise.

8 And because of this they exceedingly, and it me because of them; for they speak against God and His praise.

9 And they have no hope of salvation; for they believed not God, who justifieth them; and only hope have they not of salvation and the resurrection of the dead.

10 But thou, O blind Jew, boast not against God, who brought thee from whence thou wast not; and when He raised thee He shall thee, [even] thy seed thy soul and thy flesh, in His and in His.

16 But thy flesh — and sevenfold, as the flesh of our father Adam — thou shalt rise the mercy of God; and there thou shalt be judged as thou hast done transgression and sin, as thou hast not believed.

17 And it shall not seem unto thee as that which thou hast besides, and thou shalt for thyself that which thou shalt abide in the grave, in thy; and thou shalt say: There is no resurrection of the dead — as they the commandment of God.

18 And who shall leave thee in the ashes and the earth? He shall raise thee, that He may judge thee as thy deeds which thou hast done.

19 And there shall come a time which [is] in the winds — thy spirit; and a time which [is] in the earth — thy dust; and a time which [is] in the fire — thy flame.

20 And a time — the spirit of life which breathed upon thee, in the of darkness.

21 Which — but the souls of the righteous in the Garden with rejoicing shall be.

22 But thou, O Jew and Samaritan and Pharisee and Sadducee — until the time of thy judgment thou shalt in darkness.

23 And then thou shalt see how God shall judge thee as thy deeds, because thou hast thy.

24 And thou shalt say unto them: The dead shall not live — let us eat and drink, for dust we shall die; and thou shalt say while thou abidest upon the seat of Moses: There is no resurrection of the dead.

25 And because of this thy while thou knowest the book of the Law, and thou learnest the word of the Scriptures; and from man, from thy when thou learnest not and knowest not.

26 The word of the Scriptures the people of God, in thy evil learning and in thy word.

27 But thou shalt be as thy deeds, and as the fruit of thy; for God is no respecter of persons; and them that learn good, He shall them from the good which He hath prepared for them that love Him.

28 But thou — thou hast no to escape from the hand of Him who thee; and there is He shall judge thee as thy deeds; for thou and they whom thou hast together shall be gathered.

29 Know that the dead shall rise — some — those who kept the commandment shall rise, and there shall not them; for the commandment shall bring them forth; and as the earth bringeth forth when.

34 And at the blowing of the trumpet the dead shall rise, as the twinkling of an eye; and they shall stand before God, and He shall them as the work of their hands.

35 Then thou shalt see that thou shalt rise from the dead, and thou shalt in all thy deeds which thou hast done, thou, upon the earth; and when thou seest them written before thine eyes — and this in that which profiteth thee not.

36 And thou shalt know that thou shalt rise from the dead, and thou shalt be judged as thy deeds which thou hast done.
