THE FIRST GENERAL EPISTLE OF PETER

1 Peter

First Peter is a letter naming Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, as sender, written to believers scattered through Asia Minor who were suffering slander and pressure for their faith. Peter calls them a chosen people and living stones built into a spiritual house, urges honorable lives among their neighbors, respectful households, and humility under God's hand, and promises that suffering for Christ is a sharing in his glory. Hope, he insists, is alive through the resurrection. In this edition the text is the King James Version of 1611, printed as received from the public domain. The Ethiopian Church keeps it in this canon among the general letters, where it has comforted persecuted and ordinary believers alike across the centuries. Its five chapters are warm, dignified, and steady. Watch for the call to always be ready to give a reason for your hope.
