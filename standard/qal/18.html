Chapter 18

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And exalt not thyself before the king, and make not a show in his house, and boast not over him in thy good deeds; but when thou doest all good deeds, and fulfillest all the commandment, say: "I have done " — as our Lord says in the book of life — "for thou shalt not be saved by the multitude of thy evil deeds."
