Qalementos

Chapter 10

1 My son, do as he does to thee, and be not as they are not. And if thou believest in the name of God, and thou art a true man, with justice and righteousness as our law speaks — then contend with the sword the deniers and the proud who believe not in Christ, and fill thy sword with perfect faith. And be not a respecter of persons, and take not in judgment; and let there be feast, and the poor, with thee, as one.
