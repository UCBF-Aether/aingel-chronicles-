Qalementos

Chapter 20

1 Multiply peace to the children of the believers, and to the priest who to God and does according to His law; and beware the snare of Satan — for he ensnared Simon the sorcerer, after he had been a helper to my brother Philip, and after he had shown by his hand many wonders of divinity. And the cause of his error was that he sought from me the name of priesthood and the gift of the Holy Spirit in haste; and our Lord told me that he was; and I drove him from me, and he became a to his.
