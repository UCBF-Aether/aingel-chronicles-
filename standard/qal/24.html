Qalementos

Chapter 24

1 And anoint him with the oil of gladness who desires baptism, before thou baptizest him with water; and after baptism seal him with the oil of divinity which I have given thee — that is the oil of myron, in which is the fulfillment of all. And let the believers stand on their feet to hear the holy books of divinity.
