Chapter 27

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And as for those who and — the grace of divinity — let them pray as we have ordered in the book of the ordinance. For my God Christ taught me; and I tell thee, O my son Clement, that prayer is fitting for them: at the first hour of the day, and at the third hour, and at the sixth hour, and at the ninth hour, and in the evening, and at the time of, and at the time of midnight.
