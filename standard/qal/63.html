Qalementos

Chapter 63

1 (1) And let the priest wear the stole like a maʿtab upon his shoulders; for it is a figure of the rope which they put upon the neck of our Lord Christ, and dragged him unto the court of Pilate. (2) And let its border, which is red, be wide.
