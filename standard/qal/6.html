Qalementos

Chapter 6

1 And he who gives and who takes up in the priesthood in haste — he has no priesthood; and stern is the by me, I Peter, with the sternness with which I sternly judged Simon the sorcerer; and his portion shall be with Judas the despised, and his soul is far from the of God, as the devil him.
