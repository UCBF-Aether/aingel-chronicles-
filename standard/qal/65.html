Qalementos

Chapter 65

1 (1) I say unto thee, my son Clement, and unto those like thee, in: that thou mayest open the midst of the believers, and command them that which is fitting, in that which the Holy Spirit hath shewed thee, which fulfilleth (2) And whoso transgresseth and turneth aside — let him be accursed, until the day of judgment, when he standeth before Christ.
