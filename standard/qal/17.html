Qalementos

Chapter 17

1 Drive not the poor from the gate of thy dwelling, and mock him not, and him not, and him not; but let the of thy heart be, that thou mayest pity him and forgive him, as God forgives thee. And set him at thy table with thee, and of the cup which thou drinkest give him to drink; and remember not against him — for Nebuchadnezzar the king remembered and found the of God.
