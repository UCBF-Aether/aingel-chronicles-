Qalementos

Chapter 42

1 (1) And know thou, my son, that incense is frankincense — for frankincense is a great incense which is burned before God; for our Lady, the Mother of Light, spake unto me and gave it me from him. (2) And it is what men bring as sägälə, a present [unto God]. (3) But let its fragrance be for the burial of the bodies of the priests, that they rot not; and let them not bury them in the earth, but with longing above the earth.
