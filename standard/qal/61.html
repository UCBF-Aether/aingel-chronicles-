Qalementos

Chapter 61

1 (1) And let him not send unto the altar of God him who is a leper, whose uncleanness is no more — for there is no uncleanness after he hath received baptism — but lest he profane the priesthood of God. (2) And likewise said they unto the man with a discharge: for let him not trouble the angels of the house of God.
