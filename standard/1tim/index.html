THE FIRST EPISTLE TO TIMOTHY

1 Timothy

First Timothy is a personal letter naming Paul as sender, written to Timothy, his younger coworker left in charge at Ephesus. Paul instructs him to stop false teaching, order the church's prayers and worship, and appoint overseers and deacons of proven character. Along the way come practical words about widows, elders, money, and contentment, plus warm charges to guard the deposit of faith and fight the good fight. Think of it as a mentor's handbook for a young pastor. In this edition the text is the King James Version of 1611, printed as received from the public domain. The Ethiopian Church keeps it in this canon among the pastoral letters, where it has guided church order for centuries. First-time readers may be surprised how concrete and household-level its concerns are. Watch for the great confession that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners.
