Sinodos

Chapter 22

1 The forty-eighth canon: concerning the times at which they ought to pray apart, and hearken unto teaching, and by the sign of the cross.

2 And let all of them — the faithful men and the faithful women — rise in the morning; before they do anything, let them wash their hands, and pray unto God.

5 And if a word of teaching is told them [too fragmentary]

6 [too corrupt]

7 And let him ponder this in his heart, and keep all that he hath heard; for God is spoken by the mouth of him that teacheth. And he that in the church, and, shall be destroyed for the fear of the great God.

8 [fragment]

11 And again, for him that is able: reading. And if there be one that teacheth, let him not tarry from the church; and the place where teaching is — to him that speaketh, let there be given the word which is spoken by him, which is profit to him that heareth it. And thou shalt hear what thou hast not, and thou shalt profit by what the Holy Spirit hath given thee from him that teacheth; and thus thy faith shall be strong concerning what thou hearest.

12 And again they shall tell thee in that place what thou desirest to do in thy house.

13 And therefore let a man hasten unto the church; and in the secret place the Holy Spirit.

14 And if it be a day wherein there is no teaching, let them all in his house, and let him lift up the Holy Spirit, and read as much as he is able; for it is good.

15 And if thou art in thy house, pray at the hour.

16 And praise God; and if thou art in another place, and if this hour cometh upon thee, pray in thy heart unto God; for at this hour they lifted up Jesus Christ, and bound Him upon the wood of the cross.

17 And therefore it was commanded of old in the Law that they should offer the bread which they bring, as the likeness of His body — Christ's — and His precious blood.

18 And let them offer the sheep which is the likeness of the perfect Lamb; for He is Christ, and He is the bread which came down from heaven.

19 And again pray at the sixth hour; for at this hour the of our Lord Christ upon the wood of the cross was, and and there was darkness. And let them pray at this hour a strong prayer, and be likened by the word which our Lord Christ prayed; and darkness covered the whole world.

20 And let the Christians make great prayer.

21 And at this hour prayer, and prayer of praise, that ye may join in praising with the souls of the righteous, while they praise the living God.

22 who remembered His saints, and sent unto them His Son, which is His Word.

23 unto them; for at this hour was pierced His side — Christ's — and there came forth blood and water.

24 [fragment]

25 And therefore thou, when thou keepest the first and the second day as the likeness of the resurrection, pray; and and at midnight arise from thy sleep, and wash with water; pray [much lost]

26 And if be separated from it by thyself, and pray, and enter into thy chamber.

27 for bound art thou; and neglect not prayer, for pure are ye, and ye are not defiled.

28 They that are baptized desire not again washing, for pure are they.

29 And if thou hast breathed into thy hands, and with thy spittle which cometh forth from thy mouth, thou shalt be pure for the Holy Spirit.

30 And the water of baptism, which is and purifieth them for the faithful at this hour when they pray.

31 For these are the priests; thus they taught us: that at this hour all creation prayeth unto God — the stars and the plants stand.

32 And the waters the hour; and all the hosts of heaven — the angels — while they at this hour, with the souls of the righteous, praise God.

33 And therefore it is fitting for them that believe to observe this hour for prayer.

34 Hearken our Lord; for thus He said: "Behold at midnight, saying: Behold, the Bridegroom cometh; arise, go forth unto " — and again saying.

35 for ye know not at what hour your Lord cometh (Matt 24:42); and at the time of the cock-crowing arise and pray likewise.

36 For at this hour, at the cock-crowing, the children of Israel denied our Lord Jesus Christ.

37 we in faith have known the light of the world.

38 And for the resurrection of the dead, when we the day.

39 All this therefore while ye do, and while ye, and remember among yourselves.

40 Learn ye this wisdom, O little Christians [much lost] and be not, and, while Christ ye remember.

41 And continually thy perfection; for this is the sign of upon Satan. Know; and show, if in faith it is done — not for a man by himself that is known — but know ye that the breastplate of righteousness (Eph 6:14) ye shall put on [much lost]

42 For His creature after Him, if the Holy Spirit.

43 which is in the man which Moses first, and taught us in it, by the lamb of the Passover which was slain, and commanded [much lost] our faith which He gave us, by the pure and perfect Lamb.

44 And in this, if we we shall be saved from them that desire to kill us.

45 And this, if ye have learned and in upright faith, ye shall be strong, and He shall give you life everlasting.

46 This is the canon that ye keep it if ye have heard and received all, and all that followeth the teaching of the apostles. Let him[the heretic?] not hinder him, nor anything, from the heretics.

47 These are the which and corrupted the teaching of the apostles.

48 And if a man cometh unto them, thus let them teach him. But as for these heretics, and those that hear them — they desire not to be taught the commandments of the apostles.

49 But by themselves, by their own will, they do what they choose; and what was fitting for their heart. And if our brother whom the teaching, God shall reveal unto them — to them that desire the holy church.

50 [a Jew?] denied and believed, as God willed: who from before the world is the Only Son, in the latter days born of a virgin, without mingling of man, and who lived with men without sin.

51 He fulfilled all the righteousness of the Law; and as God willed, He was crucified, and was buried, and rose on the third day.

52 And after He rose from the dead while He was with the apostles, and completed all the ordinance, He ascended before them [much lost]

53 [fragment]

54 Is there not therefore among them that do signs and wonders.

55 For this is that which was given by the grace of God in Christ.

56 [fragment]

57 And whether it be a word of wisdom, or knowledge, or the of the Spirit.

58 [fragment]

59 And whether it be, or patience in the Law.

60 For Moses, the man of God, in Egypt while he did signs, was not exalted over the people of Israel.

61 and he was not exalted, and did not over this prophet Aaron Joshua; and after him the people, while they fight.

62 And the moon at Aijalon (Josh 10:12); he was not exalted over Phinehas and over Caleb.

63 And Samuel, who signs did, did not him unto David, the beloved of God, while they two were prophets.

64 [fragment]

65 And while there were in Israel saints who bowed not down.

66 therefore demons he casteth out.

67 And the dead he raiseth, and, and tongues he speaketh; but that which distributeth grace, as it was, for what is fitting, for the healing of while he forgiveth them.

68 for a sign, but that which help which for salvation.

69 As He said in the Law: "By another tongue I will speak unto this people, and by lips, and they shall not believe" (Isa 28:11); for the Egyptians believed not God — Moses the prophet, who signs and wonders did.

70 The Jews believed not in Him, because of their evil.

71 And our Lord Christ, unto the Jews as Moses: all disease and all sickness among them He healed and they believed not in Him.

72 [fragment]

73 and in this they believed not while the blind see, and the lame walk, and the dead are raised; and unto this, when they him and for this Annas and Caiaphas asked him; and this sufficed them not, nor did they him, while he did signs and wonders — but by themselves.

74 God shall keep

75 And by it there is made for them power — as the wise — not by the power of man, but by this will. This therefore we say: let them that have received such grace not be exalted over them that have not received grace.

76 Therefore I say by this sign: for there is no man that believeth in Christ the Son of God who hath not received the grace of the Holy Spirit.

77 For therefore he that turneth from the many evil gods of evil worship, and believeth in God the Father and in Christ His Son — grace it is from God, if he believeth in God denied and believed, as God willed: who from before the world is the Only Son, in the latter days born of a virgin, without mingling of man, and who lived with men without sin.

78 He fulfilled all the righteousness of the Law; and as God willed, He was crucified, and was buried, and rose on the third day.

79 And after He rose from the dead while He was with the apostles, and completed all the ordinance, He ascended before them [much lost]

80 [fragment]

81 Is there not therefore among them that do signs and wonders.

82 For this is that which was given by the grace of God in Christ.

83 [fragment]

84 And whether it be a word of wisdom, or knowledge, or the of the Spirit.

85 [fragment]

88 And whether it be, or patience in the Law.

89 For Moses, the man of God, in Egypt while he did signs, was not exalted over the people of Israel.

90 and he was not exalted, and did not over this prophet Aaron Joshua; and after him the people, while they fight.

91 The sun he made to stand at Gibeon (Josh 10:12).

92 And the moon at Aijalon (Josh 10:12); he was not exalted over Phinehas and over Caleb.

93 And Samuel, who signs did, did not him unto David, the beloved of God, while they two were prophets.

94 [fragment]

95 [much lost] while there were in Israel saints who bowed not down and Elijah was among them, and Elisha helped him; workers of wonders they were. And Elijah as a keeper of the Law and a fearer of God he was.

96 he did signs; and Elisha helped him, while he feared. And Daniel the wise, again, who was saved from the mouth of lions; and who went forth from the furnace of fire.

99 That by their own power they not, but by the strong God; and signs they do, and from tribulations they are saved. Therefore there is none among you that exalteth himself over his brother, if he be a prophet.

100 And if he be a worker of wonders, and if there be given unto him much, let there be nothing; therefore again.

101 All these are of signs, a help.

102 For the fear of God is from faith; and the worker of signs, from Him that sendeth power from above and again God helpeth concerning that which we have before told.
