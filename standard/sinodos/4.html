Sinodos

Chapter 4

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 The thirty-sixth commandment, the eighteenth: said Matthew: Deacons are appointed, according as it is written: "By the mouth of two or three shall every word stand."

2 And they take counsel concerning every charge

3 While, all the people, and in purity — even they that are merciful and humble.

4 And let them not be murmurers.

5 And be not double-tongued; and swear no oaths; for oath-swearing corrupteth [even] the wise man.

6 And.

7 For the day of the Lord is near; and it shall sweep away all, both seen and hidden; and the Lord shall come, and His reward with Him.

8 Be ye doers of the law for yourselves, and be ye learners, according as God hath taught you; and keep that which ye have been taught.

9 And add not unto it, neither take thou aught therefrom.

10 And Peter said: O brethren, the books shall teach you concerning the remaining words and commandments; and we will declare unto you that which we were commanded; and they all say: Let Peter speak.

11 The fourteenth commandment, the twelfth: concerning the appointing of a bishop. If: it is a town wherein are few believing men, and if the people are not many, so that they for a bishop, and there are not so many as twelve — they send to the churches near their town, where there are many believers, that they may bring three believing, pure, and elect men from that town; and them concerning.

12 If he be a man good, from the people, without sin, and without oath, and a lover of the poor, and, who is not a drunkard, and not, and not a lover of exceedingly for himself, and not, and not, and not like unto this.

13 And; and if he be without a wife, and if he take, be appointed bishop, he shall dwell with her.

14 And let him that is [have] good teaching; and let him be able to interpret the books.

15 And if he be able to interpret the books, let him be humble, and with all men.

16 So that there be no bishop, nor anything.

17 John said: [as for] a bishop who is appointed — if he hath known, and the love of God.

18 Who dwell with him, they appoint two priests; and they all say:, but; for are the priests: twelve on the right hand, and twelve on the left.

19 And John said: Well, ye have put me in remembrance, O brethren. Behold, the cups that are on the right are lifted up from of the angels, and they present them to the Lord; and they that are on the left become over all the angels. And he would have them to be priests, after the likeness of the elders who have passed the time of; and they with the bishops, and are helped in all that is done, and in love.

20 And the priests that are on the right shall rejoice, and be aided in the sacrifice, that they may be unto glory; and they shall.

21 And the priests that are on the left shall perish; and they shall not, and they are rebuked with all rebuke, and commanded with all commandment.

22 The fifteenth commandment, the twentieth: concerning the appointing of a reader, said James: he that appointeth a reader, after.

23 Let him not be of many words, nor, nor; but let him be, good, and a lover of good.

24 toward the house of the Christians, which is named therein

25 And let him be, and he that readeth well, and he that,; and he that giveth ear

26 before God.
