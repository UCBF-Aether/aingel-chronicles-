Sinodos

Chapter 3

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 The eighth commandment, the twentieth: said James: O my son, be not a diviner by signs, and practise not augury by birds, for all this leadeth toward the worship of idols.

3 And observe not, and choose not from the hour, and choose not days, and be not a star-gazer, and be not a sorcerer; and believe not men of omens, and desire not to believe them; for all this maketh thee a worshipper of idols.

4 The ninth commandment: said Nathanael: O my son, be not a liar; for falsehood leadeth to theft.

5 And be not a lover of gold, and be not a lover of vain; for all this becometh sin, and draweth toward theft.

6 The tenth commandment, the tenth: said Judas: O my son, be not a murmurer; for murmuring draweth a man toward.

7 And be not arrogant; and be not proud; and be not hasty to evil; for by all this cometh denial.

8 Be meek; for the meek shall inherit the kingdom of heaven.

9 Be, and merciful, and a peacemaker, with a pure heart, from all evil, without sin.

10 And be, and humble, and keep thyself in trembling at the word which thou hast heard.

11 And exalt not thyself, and join not thy soul with the proud; but with righteous and humble men. And in all that befalleth thee, trust in the of God; and know that there is nothing which cometh to pass, nor anything, except from God.

12 The tenth commandment, the eighteenth: said Thomas: O my son — as for him that speaketh to thee the word of God, which is become to thee a cause of life, and hath given thee of glory — love him as the pupil of thine eye; and remember; for are the priests: twelve on the right hand, and twelve on the left.

13 And John said: Well, ye have put me in remembrance, O brethren. Behold, the cups that are on the right are lifted up from of the angels, and they present them to the Lord; and they that are on the left become over all the angels. And he would have them to be priests, after the likeness of the elders who have passed the time of; and they with the bishops, and are helped in all that is done, and in love.

14 And the priests that are on the right shall rejoice, and be aided in the sacrifice, that they may be unto glory; and they shall.

15 And the priests that are on the left shall perish; and they shall not, and they are rebuked with all rebuke, and commanded with all commandment.

16 The fifteenth commandment, the tenth: concerning the appointing of a reader, said James: he that appointeth a reader, after.

17 Let him not be of many words, nor, nor; but let him be, good, and a lover of good.

18 toward the house of the Christians, which is named therein.

19 And let him be, and he that readeth well, and he that,; and he that giveth ear.

20 before God.
