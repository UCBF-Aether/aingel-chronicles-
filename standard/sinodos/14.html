Sinodos

Chapter 14

1 The thirty-fifth canon: concerning deposits which a man leaveth [in trust]: the disposal thereof [is] as he willeth; let a man keep his deposit.

2 And if death come upon a man, and if he hath no children, and if not to another: let it be entrusted to him that; and if it be brought to another man, let her be cast out.

3 And a man with whom there is a deposit: let him not withhold it, but let him restore it according to the law; and if he desire it to remain, let him not be cast out.

4 And for him that leaveth [a deposit] as he willeth: for the Spirit of God is in us all.

5 [canon 36] concerning the time [of instruction] in which they hear teaching: after [their] conduct if he be a Christian, while they hear the word of instruction; and if he be taught and know good deeds, let no [further] time be appointed him, but his conduct alone, which openeth [the way] for him.
