Sinodos

Chapter 10

1 The thirty-fifth canon: concerning the putting away [of a wife]. As for him that hath put [her] away — judge him as he willeth; a man may put away his kept one.

2 And if a man have a wife, and she have not borne children [much lost] let her be kept; but if he take another wife, let her be sent away.

3 And the man that hath a kept [wife] — let him put her away, or let him keep her according to the law; and if he be not willing, let him put her away, let him send her away.

4 And if there be any that hath put [her] away, judge him as he willeth; for the Spirit God [fragment].

5 [canon number lost] concerning the time wherein they hear the teaching [much lost] while they hear the word of instruction; and if there be a catechumen, and he knoweth good works, let days not be granted unto him, but his work alone, which is manifest concerning him.
