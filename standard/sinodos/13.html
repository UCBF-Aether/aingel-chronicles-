Sinodos

Chapter 13

1 The twenty-ninth canon: concerning a man, or another, who sendeth messengers from among the faithful and the believers; or a star-gazer, or a sorcerer, and such like.

2 And the deacons who are in the diaconate, or the chief of the deacons, who practiseth these things: let him not remain, or be cast out.

3 And if a Christian or a believer desireth it, and would be their agent: let them be cast out; for they are far from God.

4 a man without mercy, or a man who doeth what is not fitting: let them be cast out; for they are aliens and unclean, and it is not fitting that enter into their congregation of the believers.

5 And if there be a star-gazer, or a diviner by the sun, or a soothsayer, or an interpreter of dreams, or a seducer of the people, or one who weareth a bridal garment, or a worker of: let him not remain, or be cast out.
