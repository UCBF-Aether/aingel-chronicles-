Sinodos

Chapter 16

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [canon 38?] And when the bishop speaketh to all; and if there be no bishop, let them take the bread of blessing from the priest or from the deacon.

2 And when the bishop speaketh to them all, and asketh not a word to another.

3 For the bishop shall them; and if the bishop be not present, but the faithful alone are at the liturgy, let them take the eulogia from the hand of the priest, if there be one; and if there be no priest, let them take from the hand of the deacon; and he that becometh a Christian shall take his bread apart.

4 And if they be laymen by themselves, let them in silence.

5 And for the laymen, men, let them not make the eulogia.
