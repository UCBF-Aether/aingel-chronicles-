Sinodos

Chapter 12

1 Canon three hundred and thirty-five: concerning the laying of the hand upon the Christians.

2 And after the prayer, when his hand the Christians, let him pray and dismiss them.

3 And if there be and he heareth not the word of instruction concerning conduct, and that which is seemly let them bring them before; and if there be any that let him be sent away.

4 And if there be any that maketh idols, or that let them teach him that he make not idols; and if he be not willing, let him forsake the idols, let him be sent away. And if there be any that goeth unto the circus — let him forsake it or be sent away. And if there be any that teacheth of this world

5 And it is good if he forsake it; and if there be no second deed let be granted unto him.

6 And he that goeth unto the sacrifice of the idols — let him forsake it or be sent away; and if there be

7 he that teacheth — or he that teacheth killing

8 Or or the charioteer — let him forsake it or be sent away.

9 And if there be a craftsman of idols, or a keeper of idols — let him forsake it or be sent away. Unto the rulers he shall not be committed; but if he be committed — if he was commanded concerning killing, let him not do it; and if he forsake not, let him be sent away.
