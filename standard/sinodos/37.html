Chapter 37

1 Canon 30: Concerning the order of the priesthood. These things ye know, which we ordained for you; and ye know as those whom we named bishops.

2 And others we named presbyters, and others we named deacons, with prayer and laying on of hand; and we appointed for each an order this is their name and there is none.

3 which they did before God.

4 And if there were no law of difference of orders, it would suffice to perfect all the world with one name; but we have been taught by the Lord a series of functions.

5 And it is not proper for the deacons to offer the oblation, nor to baptize, nor to give the eulogia to great or small.

6 Nor for a presbyter to sign, that is, to ordain, anyone, for it is not proper without order.

7 For God is not the author of confusion.

8 But let not seize with audacity and make for themselves a new law to their own destruction in their ignorance.

9 but they are in opposition to the bishop of all the earth, the Son of God, the great Priest, Jesus Christ our Lord.

10 And there were appointed by Moses, the lover of God, the chief of the priests, and the priests, and the Levites.

11 And we were appointed — we and the apostles — by our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.

12 And we appointed for ourselves Clement and James; and after them that we might send them; and we appointed also.

13 all of us — the presbyters and the deacons.

14 And the first priest, the chief of the priesthood, who is one — He is our Lord Jesus Christ.

15 Who took not honour unto Himself, but the Father and He became the chief of priests for our sake, as He offered a spiritual sacrifice to God the Father, before He was crucified; and He commanded us to do likewise.

16 But it is not for all of them that believe in Him, to whom He giveth, to be priests; and they have not received the order of the priesthood like us.

17 And after His ascension, we offered according to the order the holy oblation without blood.

18 And we appointed bishops and presbyters and deacons; and Stephen the first martyr, who was not inferior to us in his love for God.

19 But he showed to God, in his faith and his love for Jesus Christ our Lord, who until the laying down of his soul for His sake.

20 And the evil Jews stoned him with stones; and for His name, and they killed him.
