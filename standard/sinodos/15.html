Sinodos

Chapter 15

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [canon 36] concerning the prayer of them that hear instruction and believe.

2 When the teacher finisheth his discourse, let them pray: if a Christian by themselves, separated from the faithful; and let the women stand apart in a place in the church, and let the believing women pray by themselves.

3 And the women, if they be Christians.

4 And when prayer is finished, let him not mingle among them — if a Christian with the faithful; for his is not pure.

5 And let the faithful greet one another: a man who believeth, and a believing woman; and let not the hands of the women, and let the women cover their heads with their veils or with their headcloths, and not with alone; for this is not for them that are united.

6 [canon 37] concerning the laying of the hand upon the catechumen.

7 And after prayer, when he hath laid his hand — the teacher — if he becometh a Christian, let him pray, and dismiss them.

8 And if be of the church that teacheth, or a layman, let him do thus.

9 And if they seize him that becometh a Christian for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, let him that is bound be for the name.

10 For if they find him, and bring him, and kill him, before he receiveth baptism: for the remission of his sins let him be justified, for he is baptized in his own blood.

11 [canon 38: concerning baptism] from the age of twenty; or those appointed for baptism, let them examine their: if they live in the fear of God let them be baptized; if they have kept the fast, or if they have endured sickness, or if they have done all good, and if they have heard these things, let them bring them; and if they have done thus, let them hear the Gospel apart from the rest; and let them lay the hand upon them, and every day let them teach them; and on the day of the approach, on the day whereon they are baptized apart, let the bishop make them known, that he may know whether they be pure; and if there be found twenty who are not pure, let him set them apart by themselves.

12 For he heareth not the word of instruction with "Amen".

13 For they desire it not; for a stranger let him do.

14 And let them teach those who are to be baptized, that they be watchful and pure, in five weeks.

15 And if there be among them a woman who is, let her be set apart, that she be baptized on another day.

16 And let them fast, those who desire it; let them be baptized on Friday, and let the bishop gather them, those who are baptized on Friday, in, and command them prayer and.

17 And upon the laying of his hand on them, let him adjure the unclean spirit, that it depart from them and return not again upon them; and when he hath finished his adjuration, let him breathe upon them, and read over them; and afterwards let them enter into the church, and let the bishop lay his hand upon them and pray.

18 And let him say: O God, who hast appointed these worthy ones for the adoption of the second birth, and for the remission of sins: appoint them worthy, that they may be filled with the Holy Spirit; and send upon them the grace of the Holy Spirit, that they may be sent to do Thy will. Glory to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, in the holy church, now and unto ages of ages.

19 And afterwards the priest, taking in his hand the which is, shall pour it upon their heads, saying: I anoint thee with holy oil.

20 In the name of God the Father Almighty, and of Christ Jesus His only Son, and of the Holy Spirit and let him say: God be with you all.

21 And he that shall answer and say: And with thy spirit.

22 And after these are finished, let them not pray with all the people, nor go before in prayer with the elder Christians, but after they have done the work which is ours.

23 And after prayer is finished, let them give the peace to one another with their mouths.

24 And let the deacons bring the offering to the bishop; and let the bishop take the bread and the cup; and the bread, that it may be the body of our Lord Christ.

25 And the cup of wine mixed, that it may be the blood of our Lord Christ, which was shed for us and for all of us, we that have believed.

26 And milk and honey mixed together, and let them give them to drink thereof, for the fulfilment of the promise.

27 Which He promised to our fathers, saying: I will give you a land flowing with milk and honey. This is the body of our Lord Christ, which He gave us, we that have believed.

28 And thus let them bring to the bishop him that is to be baptized; and then for the priest that standeth the water of baptism a deacon over the water, and let him say his faith: in God the Father Almighty, and in His only Son, our Lord and our Saviour Jesus Christ, and in the Holy Spirit, the giver of life to all creation; the Trinity, pure in His Godhead; and the kingdom; and one faith, and one baptism, in the holy church which is above all, and the life of the world to come. Amen.

29 And he that baptizeth shall say thus: I believe. And thus let him baptize, laying his hand upon him, and upon; and let him baptize at the time this is he that baptizeth at all times baptized.

30 And afterwards let him say to him his faith: in our Lord Jesus Christ, the only Son of God the Father of the Holy Spirit, and of the Lady Mary the Virgin, without seed of man.

31 And He was crucified in the days of Pontius Pilate.

32 And He died willingly for our salvation, and rose from the dead on the third day, and opened; and ascended into the heavens, and sat at the right hand of the Father; and He cometh to judge the living and the dead in His manifestation and in His kingdom.

33 And his faith: in the Holy Spirit and in the holy church.

34 And his faith: the resurrection of the flesh which shall be for all men, and the kingdom of heaven, and of eternity.

35 And he that baptizeth shall ask him concerning all this, saying: "I believe in this."
