Sinodos

Chapter 19

1 The forty-ninth canon: concerning the fruits which ought to be brought. Let them give the first of the produce, and let them all rejoice with him, for the bishop; and he shall come, blessing and proclaiming, while he said:

2 God hath commanded thee, and we offer unto thee the firstfruits which thou hast given us for our delight May [the earth] bring forth food for man and for every beast.

3 In that we praise thee, O God, in all wherein thou hast made fruitful for every creature that bringeth forth fruit, through thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord; to whom with Him be glory, and with the Holy Spirit, for ever and ever. Amen.

4 Concerning fruits: let the priest bless these fruits — clusters, wine, figs, and pomegranates, and fruits [much lost] and let them not bless upon.

5 And he shall not add nor anything from the.

6 And of the fruits they shall not bring, except of grace, a helper.

7 And those who eat in the of God shall say, "to whom be glory,", while ye lift up the fruits.

8 [canon number lost] concerning that they ought not to eat, nor anything from man, at the of the Pascha.

9 Except those who desire: at the proper time they may eat.

10 For such a one, his fast is not reckoned.

11 [too corrupt — likely concerning husband and wife] that they be not two.

12 On the vigils of the fast, on the Sabbath they shall fast; for when one fasteth, with bread and water.

13 [too corrupt]

14 after Pentecost the fast is released; but not the Pascha which followeth — but after it hath passed.

15 Concerning this, in the second month he shall not, and therein he shall do, while he believeth. Amen.
