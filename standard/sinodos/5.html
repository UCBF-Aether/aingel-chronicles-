6 And; and give to the poor; and drink not much wine; and fast concerning the hidden mystery.

7 And he commandeth them from the brethren, that they may give to him that hath not.

8 And let them be that they may share with them in their goods.

9 And they honour all with all honour, in and in fear; and they keep themselves in all purity.

10 And there is among them he that teacheth them.

11 And there is among them he that asketh them; and there is among them he that them; and there is he that admonisheth them, and bringeth them forth; and.

12 The seventeenth commandment, the fifteenth: concerning the ma'asab, said Cephas: there are appointed three ma'asabat; two of them for prayer, concerning all that is within.

13 As for the women who in sickness, that she may be lifted up well.

14 And keep thyself, and know the charge to the priests; and be not a lover of; and be not drunken; and pray by night.

15 And if, do.

16 The eighteenth commandment, the twentieth: concerning the deacons, that they be doers of good works, said Andrew: Let the deacons be doers of good work, by night and by day; and let them not exalt themselves above the poor and needy.

17 And.

18 And them, and he giveth them from that which remaineth; and he commandeth them, that they may, that they may do that which our Lord said: 'For I was an hungred; for he that a good charge without sin shall inherit.'

19 , that he may offer thine oblation in thy holy church, in the Holy Spirit, sins according to thy commandment.

20 thine ordinance, and he looseth all the bonds of iniquity, according to the authority which thou gavest to thy apostles; and with a pure heart, while he offereth unto thee a sweet savour by thy Son Jesus Christ, to whom be glory and power and honour, to the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, in thy holy church.

21 Now and ever, and unto ages of ages, Amen.

22 And after the bishop is ordained, they all salute him with the mouth, to him that is become bishop, and to him that.

23 And the deacon bringeth unto him the oblation, and, his hand upon the oblation, with all the saints and priests.

24 He said: ' God be with you all'; and the people say: ' with thy spirit.'

25 And the bishop said: ' your hearts.'

26 And all the people say: ' unto God our God.'

27 And the bishop said: ' unto God.'

28 And the people say: ' and.'

29 And then the bishop said.

30 'We thank thee, O God, by thy beloved Son Jesus Christ.'

31 '; and thou didst send from heaven thy Son into the womb of a virgin, who became flesh, and was conceived in the womb; and by the Holy Spirit; from a virgin he was born, that thy will might be fulfilled, and thy people.'

32 ' to the sick, that the sick may be.'

33 'To them that; that ye may hear and keep the commandments; and add not, and take not, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to whom be glory and praise, unto ages of ages, Amen.'
