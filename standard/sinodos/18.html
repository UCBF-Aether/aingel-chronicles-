Sinodos

Chapter 18

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [canon 43?] concerning.

2 let the deacons be appointed unto the bishop; and all of the deacons, let them be appointed unto the bishop; and let him admonish them concerning the sick, that they visit them; for the chief of priests, if he visiteth the sick let them be admonished, for they are remembered.

3 [canon 43?] concerning the time when they desire apart prayer: let the faithful at the time rise, from sleep, before they do anything of work, and wash their hands, and pray unto God.

4 And afterwards let them go to which floweth from the holy church, from that which we have received.

5 with thy washing which is sanctified from thee.

6 O my God, bless, blessing, and sanctify, sanctification, for those receive for the communion which is not; let mercy be in Thy fear and in Thy patience, and for of the body; for glorified is Thy glorious name in all, unto ages of ages. Amen.

7 And give from His body, and say: This is the body of Christ; and he that receiveth shall say Amen.

8 And give the cup, and say: This is the blood of Christ; and let him that receiveth say Amen, and Amen.

9 And thus thy hand upon their heads, and say: I bless thee, O my God, for Thou hast divided to Thy servants and to Thy handmaids for the washing.

10 Of the second birth, for the remission of sins, the grace of the Holy Spirit, His body and His blood, of Christ.

11 We beseech and implore Thee: Thy servants and Thy handmaids, by the power of Thy angels; and be to them a guardian in Thy Godhead which is in heaven; may they find.

12 Through our Lord Jesus Christ, to whom be with Him and with the Holy Spirit.

13 Glory now and ever and unto ages of ages. Amen.

14 Finished is the order of baptism.
