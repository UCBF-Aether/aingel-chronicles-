2 Statute 47: concerning burial. And let no man [provide?] at his own request for the burial of a man, in the burial that is performed for all.

4 But let him give to him that is bereaved, and to him that for him a place, and to him that abideth for a brother's sake — and the bishop, from this let him provide for the churches.
