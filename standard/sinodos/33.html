Sinodos

Chapter 33

1 Canon 24: Concerning and the works, of them that examine them: concerning their deeds with earnestness. If they be or; and if it be he that did it, let him be admonished, that there may be obedience.

2 And if he hath obedience, in good manner, let him be received.

3 But if he hath not obedience, let him be sent away, until he make himself worthy, that may be obedience.

4 And if he hath obedience, let him be received; and if it be he that did it

5 let them be taught to be at the pleasure of their masters, that the word of God be not blasphemed.

6 And if there be one who hath a wife, or a woman who hath a husband, let them be taught to commit no adultery and to abide; and if they be unmarried, let them be taught not to fornicate, but to marry according to the law.

7 And if his master be a believer, and he knew.

8 let them be examined by law; and if they be not examined by law by law let him be sent away his master from the work of the church, for.

9 And if there be a man who hath a devil upon him, let him be taught that he must be healed; let him not enter the church to partake of the mystery until he be purified; and if he be nigh unto death, let them bring him in; let him receive the oblation.

10 And if an adulteress desire to come in, let her forsake this practice, or let her be sent away.

11 And if there be a man who maketh idols, and he wisheth to come in, let him forsake his former deeds; and if not, let him be sent away.

12 And if there be a man or a woman, and they come from any people, if they wish to enter into our law.

13 Let them forsake their former deeds; and if not, let them be sent away.

14 And if there be a man or a woman of the people of the theatre, or.

15 a, or a charioteer, or.

16 Whether: or he that contendeth upon the lyre, or he that playeth upon the pipe, or he that teacheth dancing.

17 Or he that [fighteth?] unto death, and the theatre: let them forsake their deeds; and if not, let them be sent away.

18 Or the circus-man who performeth in the circus.

19 And our sister — if she be a watcher, or a guide of the dead, or a täwänayt, or a mäʿanzərt, or a zäfanit, or a ḫalayt, or one that teacheth.

20 Likewise let her forsake; and if not, let her be sent away. Or he that in the midst of evil men, and.

21 [Verse lost in the OCR; numbering skips 21.]

22 Or he that likeneth himself to these in deeds — let them forsake them, and and let them go in the way of the believers; and if not, let them not be received. If there be a man and if he wisheth to come, let him declare.

23 And if there be a man who marrieth a believing woman, and doeth evil in his flesh, or fornication, or.

24 Or he that consulteth spirits, or a star-gazer, or by the eye, or, or a drunkard, or whose deeds and words are evil, or who speaketh.

25 Or he that his hands and his feet and writeth upon him, or by divination concerning a voice heard or a man's word, that there may be upon him, or in a vision.

26 For all these are in error.

27 and it seemeth to them that speak concerning life and death; and let them not be received into our law until they be admonished and tried concerning their deeds, if they repent of what was theirs; and if they depart.

28 Canon 25: If there be a concubine of a man who believeth not — if she kept herself to him alone, let her be received.

29 But if she was taken by another, let her not be received.

30 And a woman who was as we are.

31 And if he enter into our law, then: let him and be separated from her.

32 For he is a Christian, and let him marry according to the law.

33 As we have commanded: if he love her, let a book of divorcement be written for her first, and afterwards let him marry [her].

34 And if she be a slave, let him marry her according to the law.

35 And if he do not so, let him be sent away.

36 And if there come a man from us and doeth evil, or.

37 who believeth, after the likeness of the heathen, and doeth like them: let him forsake, and depart; and if not, let him be sent away.

38 And whosoever desireth to be a believer in Christ, let him be; let them do good, and.

39 And if there be a man in whom is grace, spiritual, let him be received.

40 For this work is not in its time, but with good conscience.

41 And if a layman teacheth men with good [words], let him be in his word, and in his conduct, and in his tongue; then let him teach men and let him be their teacher from God.
