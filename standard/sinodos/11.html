Sinodos

Chapter 11

1 The fifty-fifth canon: concerning the prayer which is heard teaching.

2 When the teacher hath finished his discourse, let the Christians pray apart, the believers being separated from the unbelievers; and let the women stand in the in the church, and let the believing women pray apart.

3 and the women, the Christian women [fragment].

4 And when the prayer is finished, let them not assemble [the rest is lost to severe script corruption — Hebrew and Arabic characters intrude in the source; reproduced byte-exact above].

5 And let the believers assemble among themselves: a believing man with a man, and a believing woman with a woman; and the women, yea those women, shall cover their heads — with their veils or with their — and not with their alone; for this is not [the custom] which was given unto us.
