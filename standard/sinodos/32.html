Sinodos

Chapter 32

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Statute 61: Concerning what remaineth from the oblations, or the eulogiae which remain from the mystery, of that which was not offered.

2 Let the deacons divide them among the clergy, with the oversight of the bishop or the presbyter. He shall give to the bishop four portions, and he shall give to the presbyter three portions, and he shall give to the deacon two portions.

3 And for the rest — for the subdeacon, and for the reader, and for the singers, and for the deaconess — one portion.

4 This is good and acceptable before God. Let every man be honoured according to his rank; and let there be no confusion in the church, but good and honourable order.

5 Statute 62: Concerning him who is willing to partake of the holy mystery, and who is willing to partake of the holy mysteries.

6 This let them bring as a report before God, from the deacons unto the bishop.

7 Or from among the priests; and let them admonish them concerning the work whereunto they were called, that they may stand, and minister, and hear the word of God.

8 And let obedience be upon them that brought them; and let them rebuke them sharply with severity concerning their deeds.
