Chapter 6

1 The twenty-second commandment, the twenty-second: concerning the appointment of bishops, and the order of the right oblation. A bishop is appointed, chosen from all the people with the priests and deacons, on the Sabbath morning; and they lay their hands upon him; and the priests, while they stand, and all of them; and they pray in their hearts, and the Holy Spirit descend upon him.

2 And they pray, and all of them, while they stand, they lay their hands upon him that is appointed bishop; and they pray over him, while they say: 'O God.'

3 'Thou, and thou gavest an ordinance to the church.'

4 'By the word of thy grace, which the righteous.'

5 'From Abraham, and the priesthood, and thy holiness; and from before the creation of the world, thou art praised.'

6 'And now, from thee, the power of the Holy Spirit.'

7 'Which thou gavest to thy beloved Son Jesus Christ; to thee be glory and praise.'

8 ', O Holy Spirit, upon thy servant whom thou hast chosen for, that he may feed thy flock, and minister unto thee without.'

9 'While he unto thee, by day and by night, O God our God.'

10 The deacon said: Pray ye. And the bishop said: 'O God, but in Christ unto thee.'

11 ', and with the Holy Spirit, glory and, now and ever, and unto ages of ages, Amen.'

12 The deacon said: ', bow down your heads.'

13 The bishop said: 'O God; to thee thy people bow down their heads; incline unto them thine ear, and hear their prayer; and by the power of thy right hand.'

14 ', and with the Holy Spirit, glory and, now and ever, and unto ages of ages, Amen.'

22 'We thank thee, for thou.'

25 And the priest said: ', after.'

28 'And likewise the cup, he said: This is my blood, take, and drink; and when ye do this.'

29 'While we remember thy death and thy resurrection, we offer unto thee this bread and this cup, while, that we may stand before thee, and minister unto thee.'

30 ' unto thee, O Lord, and we beseech thee, that thou wouldest send thy Holy Spirit.'

38 The bishop said: ', O God, and our Saviour Jesus Christ, we receive the holy mystery.'

39 the holy mystery, his flesh and his blood; and he layeth his hand upon his head.

40 Concerning, a bishop, who layeth his hand upon him.

41 Concerning, for he is not appointed to the priesthood, that he may do the commandment which was commanded.
