Sinodos

Chapter 2

1 The sixth commandment, the fifth: said Philip: O my son, be not lustful; for its lust draweth toward fornication, and after it bringeth upon man laughter and derision altogether.

2 For lust is a deceitful snare; for when the Deceiver joineth an oath with lust — for it is folly to him that doeth it. And where there is an unclean spirit, there is sin to the soul: if it find a woman wherein to dwell, for it prepareth for itself a place, and taketh with it all the unclean spirits, and they dwell upon that soul.

3 And let not a man leave him to it; and be not; let him lift up prayer that he may see righteousness.

4 Forsake your oaths; do that ye may; and add not oath upon your oath, lest Satan cast you into evil doing; for oath and lust are utterly evil.

5 And when they dwell the time.

6 their oaths, they become demons.

7 And when the demons envy a man, they trouble their souls; and when they bring them to the doing of sin, they mock them, and they rejoice over their souls.

10 The seventh commandment, the fifth: said Simon: O my son, be not an evil speaker, and be not haughty of eye.

11 For by this cometh fornication.
