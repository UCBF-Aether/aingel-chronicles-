Ezra

Ezra tells the story of a people coming home. After long years of exile in Babylon, the Persian king allows the Jews to return to Jerusalem, rebuild the Temple, and start again. Ezra himself is a scribe and priest who leads a later company home and helps the people renew their covenant, sometimes through hard and painful choices. In its 10 chapters you will find royal decrees, lists of returning families, prayers of confession, and a community learning how to be faithful on its own land once more. In this edition Ezra is printed from the King James Version of 1611, received as a public-domain text. It belongs here as part of the common history every Bible shares, the hinge between exile and restoration. Watch for Ezra's long prayer in chapter 9, where grief over the people's failures turns into hope.
