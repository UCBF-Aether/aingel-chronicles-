1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 27

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 After this let the prayer be completed, and let the reader then read the Prophets and the rest; let the presbyter or deacon read the Gospel; and then let the bishop or presbyter teach those things which are convenient and profitable. After that let there be a prayer, and let the catechumens receive a laying on of the hand.
