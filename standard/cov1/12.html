1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 12

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Therefore I say unto you, [ye] children of the light, that the time is at hand, and the harvest is ripe that sinners should be harvested in judgment. And to many the Judge shall arise as one who is kind,’ and shall impute to them their own works.’ But when He shall be at hand, a sign shall be given to the elect, who keep the law of My Father.
