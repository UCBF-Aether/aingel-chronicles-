1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 44

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

OF SUBDEACONS

1 Similarly let a subdeacon be appointed who is chaste, the bishop praying over him. Let the bishop say over him on the first day of the week, in the hearing of all the people, thus:

2 Thou, O N., minister and hear the Gospel in the fear of God. Cultivate holily the knowledge of thy soul; keep pureness; discipline thyself; observe and obey and hear meekly; neglect not prayers and fasts, so that the Lord may give thee rest and make thee worthy of a higher degree.

3 And let all the priests say: So be it, so be it, so be it.
