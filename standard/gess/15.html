Gessew

Chapter 15

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [Topic:] Concerning eunuchs.

2 If he has become a eunuch by men by force, or if they have cut off his organ in war, or if he was born thus — and they deem him worthy — let him be ordained as bishop.

3 But if he castrated himself of his own will, he cannot become one of the ordained; for he became a slayer of himself and an enemy of the creature of God.

4 And if one of the ordained cuts off his organ, let him be deposed; for he is a slayer of himself, he alone.

5 And a layman who castrates himself, let him be expelled three years; for he is an enemy of his own life.
