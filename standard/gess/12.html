Gessew

Chapter 12

1 If an elder or a deacon, or any of the grade of the priesthood, leaves his post and goes to another place and dwells in that place a long time, without permission — we, the bishops, command that he never again minister. And moreover, if the bishop sent to him to return to his place and he did not listen to him, let him be expelled from his office, and let him approach the place where he was as a layman.

2 And if the bishop with whom he was receives him as one of the priests, and regards this ordinance which we established as void, let this bishop also be expelled, as a teacher of the transgression of the law.
