Gessew

Chapter 17

1 [Topic:] And also with reference to the rest of the ordained.

2 Those who are readers and psalmsingers: if they enter into the priesthood and desire to marry, let them marry.

3 But we command them only: while they are readers, let them marry.

4 But then it shall not be possible for them afterwards to be ordained.
