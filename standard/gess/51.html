Gessew

Chapter 51

1 [Topic:] Concerning the bishop whom they have accused.

2 When men — believing, truthful and good — have accused a bishop, it is necessary that he be called before the bishops.

3 And if he comes and confesses his sins, they shall again take him and pass over him a judgment which is just.

4 And if they call him and he does not come, they shall call him a second time, and two bishops shall go as messengers to him.

5 And if he does not obey, they shall call him a third time and shall again send two bishops to him as messengers.

6 And if he does not obey, and is rebellious and does not come — those who have assembled shall pass judgment over him according to what is right, that he shall be as one whom they have convicted, because he has fled from judgment.
