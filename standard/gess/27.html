Gessew

Chapter 27

1 And let there be a meeting of the bishops twice in a year; and let them examine concerning the ordinance of the divine office, and let them explain concerning the heresies and the offences which are in the church.

2 And the first meeting shall be in the middle of Pentecost; and the second meeting on the 12th of Tekemt.
