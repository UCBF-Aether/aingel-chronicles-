Gessew

Chapter 43

1 If a bishop or an elder or a deacon, or one of the ordained clergy, eats meat in which there is the blood of its life, or which has not been slaughtered, or of which a beast has bitten, or which has been found dead — let him be deposed, as the law commands in regard to this.

2 And if he be a layman, let him be expelled.
