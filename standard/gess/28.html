Gessew

Chapter 28

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And let the bishop take thought concerning the property of the church, and let him manage it as one upon whom God looks.

2 And it shall not be his privilege to take profit from them for himself alone.

3 And let him not give the property of God to the children of his kindred, even if they are poor.

4 And it shall not be his privilege to sell the property of his church on these pretexts.
