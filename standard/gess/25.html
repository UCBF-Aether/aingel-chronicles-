Gessew

Chapter 25

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [Topic:] It is fitting that the chief of the metropolitans know the bishops of the whole province.

2 And it is their duty to know who is the first among them, and they shall call him the metropolitan. And they shall not undertake an important work without the consent of the great one; and they shall do which is their duty according to their dioceses and according to the people who are in their dioceses.

3 But he who has been ordained as metropolitan over them, let him not do anything at all without the consent of all the bishops; and thus let them all be one association, and praise God through Christ Jesus and the Holy Spirit.

4 And a bishop shall not dare to ordain anyone outside of his diocese, in localities that do not belong to him.

5 And if he is found to have done this without the consent of the head — of this place and these villages and cities — let him be deposed, he and those whom he ordained.
