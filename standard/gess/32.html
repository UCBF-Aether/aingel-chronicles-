Gessew

Chapter 32

1 [Topic:] Concerning those who frequent the theatre.

2 A bishop or an elder or a deacon who frequents the theatre, or wanders around, or becomes intoxicated — let him cease; but if not, let him be expelled.

3 Sub-deacons, readers, and psalmsingers — if they do this deed, let them cease; but if not, let them be expelled.

4 And thus also the laymen. A bishop or an elder or a deacon who knows of one that frequents and does not cease, is in duty bound to prevent him; but if not, let him be deposed.
