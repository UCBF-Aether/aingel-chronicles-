Gessew

Chapter 37

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 If one of the priests has been found eating or drinking in a place of public resort, let him be separated; except if it be in an inn for strangers on account of the needs of travel.

2 And if one of the ordained curses a bishop, let him be deposed; for it is said: Thou shalt not curse the leader of thy people.

3 And if one of the lower clergy curses an elder or a deacon, let him be expelled.

4 And if one of the priests ridicules a deaf person, or a lame one, or a blind one, or one deformed in his foot, let him be expelled.

5 And thus also a layman, if he does this.
