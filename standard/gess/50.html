Gessew

Chapter 50

1 [Topic:] Concerning him who steals the oil or the wax or anything that is the property of the church.

2 If any of the clergy steals the wax belonging to the church, or the oil, let him be deposed; and what he has stolen he shall refund fivefold.

3 Silver vessels and clothes — if they have been seen a few days — no man shall take from them anything for himself alone; for this action is a sin.

4 And he who does this shall be expelled, and they shall punish him.
