Gessew

Chapter 3

1 [Topic:] A bishop, an elder, and a deacon shall not reject [his wife] under pretext for the sake of the service of the Lord.

2 And if they have rejected [her], let them be separated [from the church]; and if they do not restore her and bring her back, let them be deposed.
