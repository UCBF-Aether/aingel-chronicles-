Gessew

Chapter 39

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 If a bishop or an elder is neglectful in reference to him who is poor from among the clergy, and does not make him his equal by giving to him according to his desire — let him be removed.

2 And if he continues in his neglect, let him be deposed like him who slays his brother.
