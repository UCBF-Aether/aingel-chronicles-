Gessew

Chapter 57

1 [Topic:] Concerning the Canons of the Apostles.

2 This we command you, O bishops, in reference to the Synodus: for if ye listen to it, ye shall be saved and delivered, and there shall be peace unto you forever.

3 But if ye do not obey this and do not receive what is in this, [the Lord] will scorn you, and there shall be sadness in your midst forever.

4 And afterwards ye shall meet with the judgment which ye deserve on account of your transgression.

5 And may God, the only one, and his only Son Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit, the Maker of all creation, give unto each one of you peace, and may he help you with all good things; and may ye not faint, but be ye without stain, having no sin.

6 And may he make you worthy of a place in eternal life, through his Son whom he loves, our Lord Jesus Christ, the Living one, the Savior — to whom is glory with the Father and with the Holy Spirit to all eternity. Amen.

7 This is the Synodus of the apostles which they sent with Clement; and glory to God forever and ever. Amen.
