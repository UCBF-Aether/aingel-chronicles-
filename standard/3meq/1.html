3 Meqabyan

Chapter 1

1 Concerning what befell them at the hand of Meqabis among the Jews in Mesopotamia of Syria; and he slew them utterly, unto the border of Jerusalem, from Jabbok and from Ṭəfʿa, unto the holy precinct.

2 For they rose up from Samaria, even unto the border of Jerusalem and all its environs, and slew with the edge of the sword, until none remained except the transgressors. For there joined the Edomites, and the Tyrians, and the Amalekites, with Moab — Meqabis, who destroyed the city of Jerusalem.

3 And when the children of Israel sinned, He sent against them the Moabite, Meqabis, and he slew them, all of them, with the sword.

4 And because of this the anger of the Lord was kindled against His holy city, and they mustered in their camp.

5 And the Edomites and the Elofəli rose up, and seized and took vengeance upon the city of God; for by night He sent them, because they had despised the word of the Lord.

6 And as for Meqabis — his city was Remat of Moab — and he rose up with strength from his city, and mustered with those who were with him.

7 And they saw all the hosts of Mesopotamia unto Syria, [come] to destroy the city of God. And from there he sought the Elofəlians and the Amalekites, and gave them much silver and gold, and horses and chariots, that they might be with him in the camp.

8 And they came in force, and besieged the city, and shed blood like water — the blood of those who dwelt within her.

9 And he made Jerusalem like a miller's trough, and made within her filth and dung and every thing displeasing to God — a deed of affliction — and defiled the city of God, which was full of righteousness and holiness.

10 And they made the corpses of Thy servants food for the birds of heaven, and the flesh of Thy saints unto the beasts of the desert.

11 And they mocked the meek, O ye children of death; and they ripped forth what was in the womb, until God — who trieth the heart and the reins — shall have mercy [and intervene]. For they that have no fear of God did according as Satan taught them.

12 And they returned into their tents, rejoicing over the evil they had done unto the people of God; and He took away the shelter and the refuge which I had chosen, from the holy city.

13 And when they entered and came into their dwellings, they kept Passover and faḥəl and encamped.
