3 Meqabyan

Chapter 3

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And the prophet spoke, for Remat of Moab was near unto Syria.

2 And he tore at the ground, and came even unto his loins, and wept with bitter weeping, and repented because of the sin which he had done before the Lord.

3 And thus said the Lord unto the prophet: Go to the land of Judah; enter; go to the land of Remat, unto Meqabis the prince of Moab, and say unto him: Thus saith the Lord: For it was not of thine own hand that thou didst this — that thou shouldest devour by the strength of My might and by My təʿyənət — thou hast destroyed the holy city; but I am the Lord; My messenger is with thee; of Myself, by My will, I sent thee, that thou shouldest destroy My city.

4 For from My ʿəʿat with all səsəʿət and fornication and deceit.

5 And I took hold and preserved thee alive, and set thee in thy place; and not by thyself — wherein thou girdedst thy loins — and thou saidst: "I will rodək it, the city, by the strength of my hand." And now, because of thy children whom thou hast begotten, the Lord will forgive thy sin.

6 And now repent with all thy heart, and be steadfast, and be not negligent; for the negligent do not attain unto repentance.

7 And blessed are those who repent with all their heart, and do not return unto affliction and sin, in all their repentance for their evil.

8 And they attain with weeping and sighing, and with much prayer, and with worship unto the Lord who zäfä ṭäromu ; for He saith unto the penitents: Ye — after ye have erred and have repented — ʾəliʿayä , ye.

9 And to the proud He saith: After he hath sinned, when he cometh unto Him in repentance, because of thy contrition and because of thy fear, He will forgive thy sin. For I am the Lord, a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and unto the fourth generation; but unto them that love Me and keep My commandments I do mercy with them, unto a thousand generations.

10 And now, because of these thy children whom thou hast begotten, keep My covenant which is with thee; and I have accepted the repentance which I have wrought, because of the evil of thy deeds, saith the Lord, the Holy One of Israel, who kəluyəmäləkə .

11 And at that time he went out from gəb , and worshipped the prophet, saying: "Such and such — may the Lord grant me to inherit, that I be not cut off from thee; but grant me what thou wilt. For I have despised God, and have not walked in His commandment, as my fathers [did not]; for we have no law. And moreover thou knowest that we serve idols, as our fathers taught us."

12 And I — I have not heard the word of His prophets of God until now; for I have sinned, who have walked in my sin, and in the pride of my heart, and in the girding of my loins, wherein I despised the commandment of God; and I have not walked in His commandment, nor kept that which He commanded me.

13 And the prophet took up [his word] and said unto him: I have understood that now thy repentance is accepted; for there is none among you of your kindred who any longer trusteth in his sin.

14 And from henceforth forsake the worship of idols, and return thou into the counsel of God, that thy repentance may be in truth. And he worshipped at the prophet's feet; and the prophet raised him up, and commanded him all that is fitting — good deeds.

15 And he did according as God commanded him, and entered into his house.

16 And Meqabis set himself aright, and removed from his house all sägäl , and idols, and marəyan , and məṭəʿawəyan , and mäsägəlan .

17 And the children of Ṣewan, whom he had brought from Jerusalem — he examined them śärkä , and declared unto them all the commandment of God, and His law, and His ordinance, according as their fathers had done them.

18 And he set over his house wise men, from the children of Ṣewa.

19 And moreover, from the young among their wise men he appointed [teachers] [supplied] — those who keep — that they might teach them the way of God, according as the children of Israel did His law and His ordinance and His statutes, and all their abominations which the Moabites yətəḥeräm — let him hear from the children of Ṣewa.

20 And he shattered their abominations, and all their sägälat , and all their idols, and all their mäwaṣəḥt , and their məśəwaʿ , from their səbuḥ , from the ḥäragit of his märaʿəy , and from the mäqwəʿal of ṭäli , which he sacrifices to his gods — śärkä ; and he declared.

21 And he sacrificeth unto them, and worshippeth them in every deed, continually; and he asketh of them, and believeth in them, and doeth their will in all that the ministers of his gods tell him.

22 And he turned not aside from their words in all that they told him; for it pleased him in all that they had done for him.

23 But as for Meqabis, who was among them — he forsook the way.

24 And when the seer heard the word which the prophet had spoken unto him, he set his way straight into repentance; and all his household set straight their way, [learning] from the children of Israel, at that time. For the children of Israel — at one time they show mercy unto him, and at another they chastise him; they teach him, and turn him unto the Lord.

25 And when He saw that they were humbled under the hands of those that oppressed them, and they cried out before Him — then the Prince teacheth them, remembering the covenant of their fathers, when they cry unto Him, for the sake of Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, their fathers.

26 And after He had saved them, they forgot Him who had saved them, and returned unto the worship of idols.

27 And the Prince sendeth against them peoples that oppress them; and when they oppress them and afflict them, they cry out unto the Lord. And at that time He teacheth them, and forgiveth them; for He loveth them, for they are the work of His hands.

28 And again, when He giveth them rest, they return to seek favor from it, with the worship of the idols which ʿawədomu ; and the work of their hands was evil.

29 And He raiseth up against them the peoples of Elofəli, and Moab, and Midian, and Syria, and Egypt; and they weep and cry out when their məʿw oppresseth them, and he afflicteth them, and subdueth them, and taketh possession of them. And He raiseth up for them judges, that the judges might save them.
