3 Meqabyan

Chapter 12

1 Woe unto you, mighty men of Israel, who have not kept what I commanded you in the tabernacle, and have not done [His] will — but your own will: pride and təzəḫərt and fornication and səsəʿət and drunkenness and drink and mäḥali in ḥasät .

2 And therefore shall My wrath burn upon you, like stubble before the face of fire, and like fire which burneth the mountains, and like a whirlwind which lifteth the ṣäbäl dəkum from the earth and scattereth it to the height of the air, where no ʿaśər of it shall be found from its deeds.

3 And likewise will I destroy them, all of them that do evil from before My face, saith the Lord, the Holy One of Israel. And remember ye God, who ruleth over all, and there is none who resisteth Him.

4 And He loveth them that love Him, and forgiveth them their sins and their evils, for those who walk in His commandment. And be not dənəṣəwan and dənəzuzan of heart, in unbelief.

5 And make straight your souls unto God, and be like unto Him, that ye may save your souls; and I will save you from the hand of your enemies in the day of your tribulation.

6 And when ye are täṣewəʿu-ed, I will say unto you: "Behold, I am with you; and I will save you from the hand of your enemies, and ye shall not be təḥeyäy-ed in the day of your tribulation" — for ye have trusted in Me, and have done My commandment, and have not transgressed My law, and have loved what I love, saith the Lord who ruleth over all.

7 And those who love Him, He loveth them; and those who keep His commandment, He hath mercy upon them — for He is merciful and forgiving.

8 And great is the mäyəṭ of His wrath, yet He burneth not with all His mäqəśäfət ; for He is merciful. For He knoweth that they are flesh and blood; and when their souls go forth from their flesh, they enter into their earth.

9 And they know not where they were, until when God willed to bring them from where they were not; for He created them from where they were not. And again He took their souls, and brought the earth into its märsət .

10 And again He will bring them from where they were not into the being of His will.

11 And Ṣiruṣayədan, his ʿaḏlawi unto God, greatly täʿabəyo before God; and when he ḫaḏdägo until the time of His will, he lifted up his head.

12 And he said: "Let my days be as the days of heaven; and I am he who maketh the sun to rise, which shall not die for ever."

13 And while this word was not yet finished, the angel of death came down, whose name is Ṭələmyakos; and he smote his heart, and he died in that hour. And he was täʾagʷälä from the beauty of his dwelling, and from the greatness of his pride, and from the evil of his deeds — because he knew not his Creator.

14 And the army of the king of the Chaldeans, while they were at the entrance of his city, and təʿəyənt while they desired his ṣäbiʿot — and when he died, they went forth and destroyed his city, and mahərəku-ed all his ʾənsas , and left not until he found no rest in ʿarəfət .

15 And all his goods they mahərəku-ed, and qwäsaqwəs they took; and they burned his city with fire, and entered into their own land.
