3 Meqabyan

Chapter 21

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Where are they that gathered up others' goods by violence, which were not their goods, and the work of their hands?

2 But — for they take others' goods, which are without śiṭ , and gather them up — while they know not the day of their death. Thou shalt overtake them, and they shall leave to another their bəʿəl .

3 And their children shall not rejoice in the bəʿəl of their fathers; for they are houses of sinners — whether yamänäzəzəwomu , or by theft, or by təʿəgələy ; those like them are sinners, like their fathers; or by ḥäyəd .

4 And there is none to preserve for them the goods of their fathers; for in violence they gathered them for themselves. And like the śaḥəw of a cloud, and like smoke which the wind lifteth, and like the fruit of grass which is shaken, and like wax which melteth before the face of fire — so shall perish the glory of the sinners.

5 As said David: I saw the wicked great and exalted, like the cedar of Lebanon; and when I returned, I ḫaṭaʾəkəwo-ed; I sought him, and found not his place.

6 And likewise is the destruction of the sinners, in məʿər : that they shall not be established, those who oppress their neighbours — though they are not as those who die not — because they gathered others' goods by ʿa mäṣ́a .

7 O servants, remember that ye shall pass away, and your bəʿəl also shall pass away with you; and though [it be] with much of your silver, and your gold also, there shall be that which is left.

8 And if ye multiply children, there shall be many graves; and if ye multiply houses, there shall be mäzəbär .

9 And if ye multiply cattle, they shall be a spoil to your enemies; for ye have not done the will of the Lord God your God. And all that your hands have produced shall not be found, for it is not blessed.

10 Whether of the house or of the field; whether with ʾäʿəʿod and fruit, or with ḥätəl ; or with the produce of ʿəkəl and məkəyad and wine.

11 And if ye rejoice not in the fruit of your wombs, but sorrow shall be unto you from all your enemies; for God shall not prosper you with all the men of your house; for ye have not kept the commandments of the Lord God.

12 But unto those who keep His law and His commandment and His statute, He shall not withhold them from His blessing; and He shall give them all that they ask; and He shall bless for them the fruit of their land and the fruit of their wombs.

13 And He shall cause them to inherit the exaltation above all the nations which ʿawədu them, that they may be [exalted?], and not that they be brought low; and He shall give them all blessing, in their increase and their fruit.

14 And all which their hands have produced, He shall bless for them the fruit of their tillage, and the increase of their cattle; and He shall make them rejoice in the fruit of their wombs.

15 And He shall multiply for them their cattle, and save them from all their tribulations, from disease and dəkam , and from the mänəsut of those who know and of those who know not.

16 And He shall stand for them in the midst of judgment, and save them from tribulation and from evil speech and from all that riseth up; for at the first he made the tabernacle. If a Levite, who is blameless and strong, keepeth the service of the tabernacle and walketh in the will of the Lord God — by the former law he [the Israelite] shall give him the tithe of all that cometh in, and every firstborn from man even unto cattle.

17 And a city of refuge in all their lands, as Moses commanded Joshua the son of Nun, that the slayer might be saved who slew unwittingly — or whether wittingly.

18 Until they bring him unto judgment, to condemn him or to justify him.

19 And by your inquiry they shall say unto them: "If there was hatred with him—" And he shall say: "For unwittingly there fell from my hand an axe, or a stone, or a tree, and he died, he upon whom it fell." Know ye: save him, for unwittingly he did it; he shall be saved.

20 But as for him who did it wittingly, he shall be judged according to his sin, and there is none to pity him. But as for him who did it unwittingly — though he killed — by your inquiry, spare him, that he die not; for unwittingly he did it.

21 And thus Moses commanded the children of Israel, that they should not transgress the commandment of the Lord God, but that they should keep themselves from all evil — he commanded them.

22 And he commanded them also, that they should walk in all His judgments; and the righteous shall fädəfad put far from themselves ʾägəʿ and bətək and death, and from all that is unseemly for the children of men — that those who walk in the commandment of the Lord God may do them, and those who cease from all evil, as he commanded them.

23 And he commanded them, that they should not transgress the commandment which he ordained for them, according to the pattern which came mayat — which is the tabernacle — that their souls might be saved, and they might find their rest with their fathers who did good.

24 For those who believed in the word of the Lord God, and walked in the commandment — they shall be called təwə lədä of the righteous; for from Adam and Seth are they, who did the will of the Lord God — we ʾiyəmenənə .

25 For we are the seed of Adam; and in His likeness we were läḥäkwänä ; and in His image He created us, that a goodly dawn might shine; and all who keep unto the Lord God.

26 And as for us, we have done good; we shall inherit the land of the living with those who did good — for there is no destruction and no night — for those who love Him.

27 And fädəfad He shall love them, and hear their prayer — for those who call upon Him in purity — and He shall yətəwe * käfə their nəsəḥ — for those who are pure in ṭəbuʿ — and He shall give them might and ṣ́änəʿ , to those who keep His law and His statute and His commandment.*

28 And they shall rejoice with Him in the kingdom which is for ever — those who did His will — whether first or last; unto Him they shall offer the sacrifice of praise, and unto ages of ages. Amen.
