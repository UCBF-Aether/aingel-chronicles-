3 Meqabyan

Chapter 16

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 According to his work, and according to the cleanness of his hands.

2 And from whence cometh it that thou sayest: "These ṣəfər of thy hands and thy feet, and the ṣägur of thy head"? Did not God make them, that they should yəṣaʿ — that thou mayest know the resurrection of the dead which shall be not in another flesh — that thou mayest know for thyself that thou shalt rise from the dead.

3 And thou shalt be judged according to thy deeds which thou hast done — evil and sin — at the resurrection of the dead; because thou hast caused the people to err, while thou sayest: "There is no resurrection of the dead."

4 And now thou shalt see, when the day of thy requital cometh unto thee; for what thou hast sown shall not ʾäʿabi that it should profit — whence grain and whence root.

5 And again, thou who hast planted a tree — it shall not ʾäʿabu Whence the vine, and whence the fig tree? Its shoot and its fruit shall not be changed.

6 And if thou hast planted a vine, it shall not be changed to become a fig tree; and if thou hast planted a fig, it shall not be changed to become a vine; and if thou hast sown a root, it shall not be changed to become sägäm .

7 And if thou hast sown sägäm , it shall not be changed to become a root. All these — in his seed and in his bäzämädu and in his fruit and in his tree and in his bäqʷäṣəl and in his bämərəw — he shall receive blessing by the dew of mercy which is from God, and shall bring forth fruit.

8 Likewise the earth shall bring forth that which God hath sown; it shall profit soul and flesh — the doers of good, while they are not changed into doers of evil; and the doers of evil, while he is not changed into a doer of good. Those whom God hath sown shall profit in soul and flesh.

9 And when the time cometh for the blowing of the horn, the dead shall rise by the dew of mercy which is from God — who did good — in the resurrection of life; and their ʿəśet — Paradise of joy — a dwelling for the pure, which He hath prepared for the chosen, where there is no disease and no sickness, where they shall not die again.

10 But those who did evil — in the resurrection of judgment — with the devil who ʾaləḥat them.

11 And his evil angels who desire not to be saved, from all the servants of the living — in the judgment which is for ever.

12 Underneath, beneath, in the ṣənaf of darkness, where is weeping and ḥaqəyäsənän , where there is no forgiveness and no mercy, and where there is no escape for ever and ever — because they did not good in their life upon earth, while they were in their flesh.

13 And therefore they shall be judged at the resurrection of soul and flesh.

14 For lelomu — for those who believe not the resurrection of soul and flesh — with thee thus God shall show great is His täʾaməri .

15 And all shall be requited
