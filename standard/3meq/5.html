3 Meqabyan

Chapter 5

1 And he forsook his ʿəgwäl ; and the young children grew, according as their father had ordained for them, and kept the ordinance of their house; and all that was among them they observed, and they cried not nädäy — O ye children of death, and ye meek.

2 And they feared the Lord, and gave their goods to the poor, and were entreated in all wherein their father had entreated them; and they comfort the meek and the children of death in their affliction, and are to them father and mother, and bear them from the hand of their oppressors, and comfort them in all that befell them — trouble and sorrow.

3 And they continued, while thus they did.

4 And after him came Ṣiruṣaydan, king of the Chaldeans, and he destroyed all their cities, and stirred them up against the children of Meqabis, and destroyed all their districts.

5 And he maḫräk all their goods, and kiyahomuni ; he seized them and carried them off into his districts — the mäməlakəya of idols — who walk not in His commandment nor in His statutes of God, but in all evil and fornication and defilement and səsəʿət and ṣərəfät ; and they remembered not their God.

6 And they devoured all carrion and blood, and the food of wild beasts, and bətək , and everything displeasing to God — [the Ge'ez has a negation here, ʾiyəbäləʿu "they did not devour", which contradicts the vice-catalogue context and is probably corrupt,?] — and they have no law from any of the righteous commandments which are written in the Orit.

7 And they knew not the Lord, that He is their Creator and their Savior, who brought them out from their mother's womb, and nourished them as is fitting.

8 And they have no law in judgment, who walk in affliction and təʿgələt and zəmut and defilement, while they take their father's wife, and their mother's sister; and they do all evil, and take their sisters and their kinswomen; and they have no law.

9 And all their ways are gloom and darkness and defilement and zəmut .

10 But as for the children of Meqabis — they keep, in all that is among them, the təḫrəmət ; and they eat not carrion nor bətək , nor any of their defilement and their sin, [abstaining] from the children of the Chaldeans. For evil and many are their deeds which are not written in this book, which the children of the ʾÄräbamayan do — sinners, and the rebellious, hypocrites, and the deceitful, and deceitful ṣiʿ, and defilement, and təʿəgələt.

11 And there is nothing upon them that pleaseth God their Creator.

12 And again they worship idols whose name is Beelphegor; and they worship him and believe in him as God their Creator. For he is deaf and dumb, and seeth not nor heareth; for a graven image is he, the work of men's hands, which they have säbäk — nähäbe silver and gold — the work of the children of ʾəmäḥəyaw , that hath no knowledge nor breath.

13 And he eateth not, and he drinketh not.

14 And he killeth not, and he maketh not alive.

15 And he planteth not, and he plucketh not up.

16 He yäḥäsəm not, and he yaśeni not.

17 He yanäṣ not, and he yabəʿl not.

18 He rebuketh not, and he forgiveth not; but baḥətu — ʿəqəfət shall be — for the destruction of the ʿabədan of the Chaldean people.
