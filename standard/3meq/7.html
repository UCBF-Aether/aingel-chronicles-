3 Meqabyan

Chapter 7

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 "Woe unto thee, thou, and those like thee, who have not known Him — the Lord who created you — and have not known His commandment and His word; for ye shall be cut off, whence no repentance availeth, when ye are punished in the straits of Sheol."

2 "And ye shall have no escape from it for ever — thou and thy gods, and those who sacrifice and worship unto them as [if they were] God your Creator — unto those which have neither soul nor breath, and which avenge not them that ʾäḥsämä them, and show no mercy to them that ʾäśänäyä them."

3 "For the work of man's hand are they, in whom Satan speaketh, to destroy the heart of fools like thee, O fool! — that he may bring down you and the ministers of your gods, and those demons that command you, into Gehenna of fire."

4 "For ye have not known that none profiteth you; ye err and are destroyed."

5 "And better than you shall be the beasts which God created for your food — and the oxen witəni and the dogs käläbatəni — for they have no judgment again, save one death."

6 "But as for you, ye shall die, and be judged again in the terror of Gehenna, whence there is no escape for ever."

7 And when they had said this, they departed and were hidden from him.

8 And Ṣiruṣaydan was in great fear and trembling, and it left him not until morning.

9 And then he went to where he had cast the bodies of the righteous, and found them not; for he desired to yəqä with them and səʿän them. For God had hidden them, that he should not təḫaśəś from their flesh.
