3 Meqabyan

Chapter 9

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And thou, O man — and God is not [with thee?] — why dost thou exalt thyself, thou who today art a man, and tomorrow shalt be earth and ashes, and shalt become wood and boards in thy grave?

2 And the [depths?] of Sheol shall be given unto thee, and unto those who do thy works; for thy teacher Säbəlyanos hath revealed, who brought upon his head the sin of all the world, because he caused our father Adam to err through his pride.

3 For he who refused to bow down to the work of his God, in the pride of his head and in the loftiness of his kəśad .

4 And thou, like thy teacher, hast refused to bow to the Lord thy God.

5 [Kingdoms.]

6 And vengeance was taken upon them, and they went down into Sheol, because of the evil of their deeds which they did upon the earth.

7 And thou, like them, shalt go down into Gehenna.

8 Thou imaginest to snatch from His hand — from God! — for mäʿato and häk and tähäk : the worship of God, which gave thee dominion over [all?].

9 And He proved thee, whether thou wouldest do His will or not; and if thou hast made straight thy way upon the earth, God shall prosper thy paths, and all that thy hand hath gotten He shall prosper, and shall bless thee; and all thine adversaries and thine enemies He shall destroy for thee.

10 And thou shalt rejoice in thy ṣ́aʿat and in thy bäʿat , and in the fruit of thy belly, and in thy storehouses, and in thy pastures; and whithersoever thou puttest forth thy fingers, and all that He hath put in thy heart shall be commanded unto thee — for from God it was commanded unto thee. Thus shalt thou do, and build, and plant, and tənəśət **

11 But if thou hast refused to hear His word and His law of God, and hast not walked in His judgment, thou hast nowhere to snatch from His hand — from God! — like all the rebellious who were before thee, who worshipped not God in purity, and trusted not in His righteous judgment; for righteousness is all His judgment.

12 And there is none that hideth himself from before His eyes; and all is naked and manifest before Him.

13 Now His is the dominion of kings, and He is the overturner of the thrones of the mighty.

14 And He is the exalter of the humble, and the raiser of the fallen.

15 And He is the looser of the prisoners, and the raiser of the dead; and those whose flesh is worn out and become as dust, He shall awaken them at the time He willeth — for He is an overshadowing of mercy from Him.

16 But those who did evil, He shall raise them — a däyən resurrection — for they despised [God], and He shall requite them.

17 And upon the earth He shall yamasən their seed; for the rebellious are they, against the law and the ordinance of the Lord God.

18 And let not sinners plot in their counsel against the righteous; for strait is their path for the righteous, [far] from the ways of sinners.

19 And as heaven is high above the earth, so far are the ways of the righteous from the ways of sinners.

20 But as for the ways of sinners: the doing of ṣug and təʿəgələt and iniquity and fornication and deceit and səsəʿət ; and going after strange property, and drunkenness in iniquity.

21 And räwiṣ in the käʿəwät of blood, and ʾamasən in that which is unclean; and səʾanəbəʿa the children of death; and eating carrion and blood, and eating the flesh of ḥarawəyan ; and the camel and the ḥawir in a woman ḥaräsä , and təkət which is not cleansed.

22 And all these are the deeds of sinners: broad is the way, and wide is the net of Satan, which leadeth into ḫaʾgʷäl and into eternal judgment.

23 But as for the ways of the righteous: narrow and mäqəʿan , which lead unto life — humility, and yähawət , love and peace, fasting and prayer, and purity of flesh, and abstinence from unclean foods: carrion and bətk and fornication, and ḥawir

24 And they abstain from every foul deed, and from foul foods which are not [permitted?] in the law, and from foul deeds which God loveth not — for all these do the sinners.

25 But the righteous are set apart from all evil ways, which God loveth not.

26 And He loveth them, and hath mercy upon them, and shall deliver them from all their tribulations.

27 But as for sinners, Satan shall qänəy them; for they have transgressed His law and His ordinance, and [all] that He loveth.
