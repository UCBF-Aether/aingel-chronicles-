3 Meqabyan

Chapter 20

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Believe me, that He will spare none and will hide none — [even] all our deeds which we did upon the earth — when we stand before Him in fear and trembling.

2 And when we shall be filled with shame for our way, and when we have no garment for our nakedness.

3 And when we have no mərəguz for our hands, nor ʿaśaʿ for our feet.

4 And I know not his way, whither they shall lead us — whether däḫəṣ́ , or gwäṣ́agwəṣ́ , or darkness, or śok ; and moreover, whether the qälay of the sea or the qälay of Sheol.

5 And those who shall lead us, we shall not know them, and we shall not hear their speech.

6 And we shall not see their faces, for they are darkness; and in darkness they shall lead us.

7 As said David the prophet: When my soul was troubled within me — Thou, O Lord, didst shew my way. In this way I went; I hid my testimony; and I turned aside from the path of my right [hand]; and I saw, and I sinned against him who warned me; and in him I have no ʾämäśəṭ .

8 And this he saith: for he warneth that the demons yəśaläqəwo him, and lead him unto him that knoweth not the way; and he turneth aside to the right and to the left; and there is none to find him who would make known.

9 And for me there is none to make known, but only in the midst of the angels of darkness.

10 And the spiritual angels of light, who are sent unto the ḫeran , that they may convey their souls unto the righteous, and lead them unto the ʿaṣ́äd of the light of life.

11 And those — the angels of darkness and the demons — are sent unto the souls of sinners, that they may receive them and lead them away into the eternal judgment which is prepared for them, that they may be judged according to their deeds which they did.

12 Woe unto them, the souls of sinners, who are led into ḫagwəl ; and there is no rest, and no ʾädəḫin , and they shall not ʾäməśəṭ , and they shall not escape, for ever, from the tribulation which hath found them.

13 Woe unto them, the sinners! For in the way of Cain they went, and by the counsel of his error they täḫagulu-ed unto Balaam; and the sins which they commit; for bänäśiʾo * hələyanə they went down, they yəmäkänəyu , that they might take others' goods by violence, which were not their goods.*

14 They indeed shall be judged according to their deeds which they did, in the judgment of burning fire.
