3 Meqabyan

Chapter 19

1 O earth, that hast gathered kings and rulers, and the great and the honoured, and beautiful women, and fair virgins.

2 And strong hands, in whom was beauty, and fair ʾaqʷayaṣ ; and in whom was knowledge and ləbuna ; and in whom was a fair voice, like the voice of ʾarəganon näbabit , and like the zəbəṭät of the harp and ʿənəzira .

3 And in whom was fair song, who make rejoice as the drinking of wine, O son of Adam their eyes [were] shining like the morning stars.

4 And their yəman was strong as he who yəhub and he yənäśəʾ and yäḥayəṣ and their feet [were] fair to behold; and for those who yərəwəṣa like mäkʷərakʷər * rətuʿat .*

5 I know thee, O Death, who hast taken their souls — of the fair hands; for by the will of the Lord God thou art täfänäwəkä .

6 But thou, O earth — for thou hast gathered many bodies, whom God brought forth from thee, and He shall bring them in unto thee. From thee we went forth, and unto thee we shall enter. By the will of God we täfäśaḥənä ; by the will of God, upon thee.

7 And thou art mändäf for our limbs; we ʾansosäw upon thee, and we nom in thy wətit . We have eaten thy fruit, and thou hast eaten our flesh.

8 We have drunk from the dew of thy näqəʿ , and thou hast drunk from the näqəʿ of our blood. We have eaten from the ʾanəgʷaʿ of thy earth, and thou hast eaten from the ʾanəgʷaʿ of our bones.

9 We have eaten from the taste of ashes; thy dew is fair, as God commanded thee, for our sisit . And thou hast taken the laḥəy of our flesh, and thou hast räsäyəki-ed dust, as God commanded thee, for thy sisit .

10 O Death, from thee who hast gathered many of us — our mighty ones, kings and rulers — and thou fearedst not their gərəma nor their greatness, as He commanded thee who created them; and thou ʾimänänəkä the poor.

11 And thou ʾeməḥərəkä those whose laḥəy is fair; and thou sparedst not the mighty and the strong; and thou sparedst not the ʾabəʿəlt and the nədayyan , the ḫaśuman and the fair ʾaʿərug, and children of ʿədəw, and women.

12 And thou sparedst not those who think good and transgress not His commandment; and thou sparedst not those who think evil — who became as beasts in their deeds — nor those who are perfect in the beauty of their laḥəy , and their words, and the taste of their nägär .

13 And thou sparedst not those whose words are bitter, and full of märəgäm in their mouths; and thou hast gathered their souls into thy treasuries — whether of light or whether of darkness.

14 And the earth hath gathered their flesh — whether in the kʷäkʷəḥ or whether in the earth — until the blowing of the horn, when the dead shall rise.

15 And the doers shall be judged even according to their deeds which they did; for at the blowing of the horn and at the commandment of God the dead shall rise, in the twinkling of an eye; and they shall rejoice who did good.
