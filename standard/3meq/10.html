3 Meqabyan

Chapter 10

1 And ye, O kings and rulers, walk not in his ways, but fear ye the Lord God, who created you and hath kept you until now.

2 Hear, O kingdom.

3 For Balak, son of Zippor, when the children of Israel came by the way of Amalek, to inherit the land of the Canaanites and the Hittites and the Perizzites.

4 And he said to Balaam: "Curse thou them for me; for whom thou cursest is cursed, and whom thou blessest is blessed. And I will give thee much gold and silver, which shall honour thee, that thou curse them for me."

5 And Balaam came, while he looked for his iniquity; and Balak son of Zippor showed him where the children of Israel were encamped

6 And he performed divination, and offered sacrifices, and slew bulls and rams, from their səbuḥ ; and he desired to curse the children of Israel.

7 But God willed not that he should curse them; but He turned the curse into blessing, by His own word.

8 And He said: "Those who bless thee shall be blessed, and those who curse thee shall be cursed; for thou art the people of God, whom He hath chosen; and thou art the dwelling of the Beloved who cometh from on high."

9 Then Balak son of Zippor was grieved and angry and enraged, when he [Balaam] blessed them before him, though he had commanded him to curse them.

10 And Balaam said unto him: "I will not curse those whom God hath blessed; for the people whom God hath chosen is come into this land."

11 And Balak son of Zippor said to Balaam: "I desired that thou shouldest curse them for me, but thou hast not cursed them; but blessing, thou hast blessed them exceedingly before me. And I — the fullness of my house, gold and silver, would I have given thee, if thou hadst said 'Give me,' and hadst cursed them for me. But thou, blessing, hast blessed them, and hast done me no good; and I will do thee no good."

12 And Balaam said: "I cannot transgress nor alter the word of God; but what God hath set as a word in my mouth, that will I speak."

13 And I will not stake my goods upon my life; for had I coveted my goods, God had mercy upon me, that I should not curse the blessed people.

14 For God said to Jacob their father: "Those who bless thee, blessed let them be; and those who curse thee, cursed let them be. For every one that blesseth thee is blessed."

15 But he who curseth thee without cause is cursed. Make straight thy deeds and thy ways, that God may love thee.

16 And be not like the former people, who despised God by their evil; and He täḥäyäyomu them — and there are those whom He destroyed by the waters of ʾAyəh [the Flood?].

17 And there are those whom He destroyed by the hand of their enemies; and He brought an enemy, an evil people, who took them captive, and led away their kings with their prophets and their Levites.

18 And He brought them unto a strange land which they knew not, and scattered them, and maḫarək-ed them, and qäśäf-ed them, and ʾamasän-ed their land.

19 And they made Jerusalem a ruin, as a field; for they took the ṭəqom and qəṣər from the holy city.

20 And the priests were led away, and the law was rent, and they fell into the hands of kwənat .

21 And the ʿəberat were led away, and none wept for their dead that died; for they were carried off, and [it was] upon their heads

22 And the children wept, and the ʾäʿərug were täḫaḏfəru ; and they [the invaders] pitied not the face of the elder nor the aged.

23 And not those in the assembly, and not dənagəl — and they ʾamasänu-ed all that they found within their cities, and ʾafəläs-ed them unto a strange land which they knew not. For when God willed, wrath came upon His people; He yaqädəm-ed the näśət of His sanctuary.

24 And therefore, when God täḥäyäyomu the children of Israel, God made Jerusalem a ruin; it shall be plowed as a field — for at all times they despise their Creator.

25 But He will not destroy them utterly; for He will spare them for the sake of their fathers — and not for their own sake doth He spare them — but because He loveth their fathers: Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, who reigned in righteousness, and ḥoru * dəmeyəhu in righteousness toward their Creator.*

26 And He hath set them in the məkəʿəbit of glory, both in heaven and on earth.

27 And likewise ye, O kings and rulers, who are in this passing world — remember ye those before you, who ḥoru in purity, that they might inherit the kingdom of heaven; and their name is comely from generation to generation.

28 And thou, that thy kingdom may be established and thy name prosper, like those before thee, the good kings, who pleased God by the goodness of their lives.
