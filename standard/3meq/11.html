3 Meqabyan

Chapter 11

1 From among them — and not their ʾaṣəmət and not their ʾaləba — that which is not a brazen censer, which they brought into the house of God by the command of God; for He said: "Their censers are hallowed, by the offering of their bodies."

2 And for me, O sinner, grant me forgiveness; for I have done evil before Thee. And for these, śəräy to them; for Thou art merciful and forgiving.

3 And thus He ʾastäśərəyä to them, to those who ḥämäyəwo .

4 And for this, Phinehas was zealous.

5 And God loved him exceedingly above all the sons of the Levites his brethren; and he was tämasälo as God [was] among them; for to the priesthood He appointed them.

6 But those who transgressed, the sons of Korah — the earth swallowed them, and brought them down alive into Sheol, in soul and in flesh, with their households and their cattle; and all, every one, according as he spoke, it was done unto him — for he loved the Lord his God, and transgressed not His commandment.

7 And likewise thou — if thou transgress not the commandment of God, God will do thy desire for thee, and will love thy word, and will keep thy kingdom.

8 And the sons of Korah and Asaph, who transgressed the commandment, against Moses, and murmured against him, because he said unto them: "Make straight your souls unto God."

9 And they said unto him: "Are we not sons of Levi, that we should do the work of the priesthood in the chosen tabernacle?"

10 And they took their censers to burn incense; and they went and offered incense, but God accepted not their prayer. And by the fire of their censers they were burned and consumed, like wax that melteth in the fire; and none was left

11 And therefore God said to Moses and to Aaron: "Gather ye their censers into the tabernacle; and let it be for My house, its gəbərih ; and all that I have appointed for it, from its entrance to its interior."

12 And the ʿaśänäy of its gəbərih for the holy tabernacle: məsəḥal , ḫoṣadät , kirub , ʾabəḥərt .

13 And mäqdəḥ , and mäsawər , and mäwadəd ; and he bound the gädəgädən for the ṣaḥərat tabernacle; and mäś waʿət , which they offer in the chosen tabernacle.

14 And the freewill sacrifice, and the mädəḫanit sacrifice, and the bəṣəʿat sacrifice, and that for sin, and the sacrifice according to the law.

15 And all that He commanded Moses in the chosen tabernacle, that they should do — thus He commanded them.

16 And let them not provoke jealousy in their God, that His name may be glorified over them, in the tabernacle of His law, of the Lord their God, who ʾasäfəwomu , that He might give them the inheritance of their fathers — a land wherein floweth milk and honey, which He sware unto Abraham.

17 And He täkayädä unto Isaac, and established a covenant unto Jacob.

18 And unto Moses and unto Aaron, in the tabernacle, säməʿ .

19 And unto Samuel and unto Elias, in Jerusalem, in the tabernacle, in the house of the sanctuary which Solomon built — that it should be the house of God, and the resting-place of His name for the Most High, and the dwelling of the Holy One of Israel.

20 For an example and a məstəsəraəy is this sin, where He showeth to His priests, to those who walk in yəwaḥat .

21 And where He heareth the prayer of those who do His will.

22 Where He appointeth His law — of the Lord, the Holy One of Israel.

23 And where there is made ṣənəḥəḥ sacrifice, and incense for a sweet savour, unto the Lord, the Holy One of Israel.

24 And it is proclaimed upon the height of məsəḥal , in the chosen tabernacle; and He showeth His praise to God, to His chosen, the sons of Jacob, and His holy ones who walk in His commandment; and His judgment is God's.

25 But those who yətəʿeyäru shall be as the sons of Korah, whom the earth swallowed; and likewise the sinners who are like them shall be cast into the judgment of the burning fire, from which there is no escape for ever.
