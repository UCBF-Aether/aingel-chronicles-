3 Meqabyan

Chapter 6

1 And the proud Ṣiruṣaydan appointed — to the anger of the Lord — lying priests and ministers of idols.

2 And they sacrifice unto them, and bring [offerings] forth unto them.

3 And it seemeth to him that they eat and drink.

4 And he giveth them, unto those who ṣäbəḥ , flocks and cattle and ḥäragit and ʾäṭäli ; and they sacrifice śärkä , and he declareth; and he eateth of that defiled sacrifice.

5 And not of their own will did they do this unto him; but läʾäbəʾəni they were given and made, that they might eat [of the sacrifices] unto their gods.

6 And when he saw the children of Meqabis, that they are righteous and worship the Lord their Creator, he desired them; [and] the ministers of the idols yasəḥət them, that they might eat of the sacrifices and eat from that defiled sacrifice. But these blessed ones refused them.

7 And he asked them why they refused; for they keep their father's commandment, and are steadfast in good deeds, and fear the Lord exceedingly.

8 And when he was wroth against them, and was stern against them, and afflicted them.

9 And they told Ṣiruṣaydan the king that he refuseth to worship and to sacrifice unto his gods.

10 And because of this the king was moved with pity, and grieved, and commanded that they bring them; and they brought them, and he set them before him, and said unto them: "Eat ye, and worship ye my gods."

11 And they took up [their word] and said unto him: "As for us, we will not obey thee in this matter, and we will not sacrifice unto thy defiled gods."

12 And he threatened them with many tortures, and prevailed not against them; for their hearts were steadfast unto the Lord.

13 And he kindled the fire, and cast them in; and they delivered up their souls unto the Lord.

14 And He showed him their life after their death — while they held their swords, məlu ḥätä — while he slept upon his royal bed by night; and great fear [came upon him].

15 And he said unto them: "What shall I do for you? My ʾägaʿəzt — bəqwəʿuni — and I will not lift it for myself, that I may do all that ye commanded me; and I will fear Him exceedingly."

16 And they told him all that is fitting, saying: "Remember that the Lord thy Creator is He; and there is One who shall shatter thee from this thy pride, and bring thee down into Gehenna below, with Diabolos thy father. And there thou shalt fulfill all thy judgment, according as thou burnedst us with fire — while there was none that had sinned against thee — and while we worship the Lord our Creator, and worship Him in fear of the Lord."

17 For He is the Creator of all, who made heaven and earth and sea, and all that is in them.

18 The sun and the moon and the stars, and all creatures which God created lälihu .

19 And there is no other god besides Him, in heaven above nor in earth beneath, who is able for all; and there is none like unto Him, who killeth and maketh alive, who rebuketh and forgiveth.

20 And there is none that escapeth from His hand; for He is the mälak of heaven and earth.

21 And there is none that transgresseth His commandment [who escapeth?] from Him who created him, save thou and thy məʿlay and those like thee — mäməʿləyan who have blinded their hearts — they, and Satan thy father; and thou and thy gods shall go down together into Gehenna, whence there is no escape, for ever.

22 For it was not thou by thyself who didst this, but Satan thy teacher, who taught thee this evil deed, that thou mightest be put to shame before us.

23 And thou hast not known the Lord thy Creator, and thou hast lifted up thy head as [though thou wert] the Lord thy Creator.

24 And thou hast boasted in the work of thy hands and in thy idols, until God humble thee, and bring thee down to the mäfəḥt of Gehenna; and He will avenge Himself upon thee for all the iniquity and sin which thou hast done upon the earth.
