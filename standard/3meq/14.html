3 Meqabyan

Chapter 14

1 As for me, it grieveth me exceedingly, and it yatekəz me and yəqäləy my heart, concerning the Judahites and Samaritans and Pharisees and Sadducees, who believe not the resurrection of the dead. The Judahites say: "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die; and thereafter we shall see none."

2 And the Samaritans say: "Our flesh shall not rise, for it shall become dust."

3 But if there be a resurrection — we believe in the resurrection of our souls; for the soul, when the flesh dieth, dieth not. For it is as the wind, which is not seen, and as the sound of thunder; and none can speak it nor show it.

4 But our flesh is seen by all, and becometh ashes and dust; for the beasts devour it, and the ʿəṣeyat devour it in the grave.

5 And as for them that devoured it, they become ashes, and no ʿaśər of them is found; for they become as grass, and as though they had never been created, they become ashes.

6 And by this are found the bodies which bäləyu And thus say the Pharisees: "We believe in the resurrection of the dead; but He will clothe the souls in the heavens with another flesh than that which was on earth."

7 And thus say the Sadducees: "When our soul goeth forth from our flesh, we shall not rise from the dead; and they have no resurrection of the dead — neither for soul nor for flesh. And when we die, we shall not rise."

8 And for this they err exceedingly, and it yatikəz me concerning them; for they speak ṣərəfät against God and His praise.

9 And they have no hope of salvation, for they believed not in God who would justify them; but they have no hope of salvation and of the resurrection of the dead.

10 And O blind Judahite, thou hast tasätäʿabəyinu against God, who brought thee from where thou wast not who sowed thee, He ʿabəqulo thy soul and thy flesh, in His likeness and in His image.

11 And think thou not thus; for thou shalt not escape from His hand. Unto God thou shalt rise, how while thou desirest not; and thou shalt be judged while thou lovest not. For the cord of Sheol is in thy kəśad , which seizeth thee until the day of thy death.

12 For in the belly thou shalt təśärəf for thyself; from the gunəd of Sheol is this cord, which thou lettest down into thy kəśad ; and when thou tələḥəq , so too it tələḥəq .

13 And when thou diest, thou lettest it down into thy kəśad ; and when the gunəd draweth it, thou bringest them

14 And thou drawest its ʾaʿəṣuq ; for all the sinners are seized within it, as the cord is in their kəśawəd .

15 And He will bring all the souls of the sinners from where they were, from the ends of heaven; and likewise thy cord shall draw thee, that thou mayest bring thy soul from where thou wast, and thou shalt enter into the fäläg of Sheol.

16 And thy flesh, täʾatito and wino sevenfold — as the flesh of our father Adam — the dew of His mercy shall raise thee, of God; and thereafter thou shalt be judged according as thou hast done evil and sin, according as thou hast not believed.

17 And it shall not profit thee and thou errest in thy night, who dwellest in the grave in thy error, and sayest: "There is no resurrection of the dead" — that they may err from the commandment of God.

18 And who shall yäḫaḏəg thee in ashes and earth, that he may raise thee, that he may judge thee according to thy deeds which thou hast done?

19 And then He shall come — whence thy spirit which is in the winds, and whence thy dust which is in the earth, and whence thy flame which is in the fire.

20 And whence the spirit of life which was breathed upon thee, in the ʿaṣäd of darkness.

21 Which täʿaʿəwaq but the souls of the righteous in Paradise with joy are yətəʿaṣəwa .

22 But thou, O Judahite and Samaritan and Pharisee and Sadducee — until the time of thy judgment, thou shalt tətə ʿaśər in darkness.

23 And then thou shalt see how God shall judge thee according to thy deeds, because thou hast ʾasə ḥatäkä luboʾo .

24 And thou shalt say unto them: "The dead shall not live; let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die." And thou sayest in thy kal , while thou sittest upon the seat of Moses: "There is no resurrection of the dead."

25 And therefore thou hast erred, while thou knowest the book of the Law, and teachest the word of the books; and thou causest men to err from ḫaḏsäyäkä , whereas thou hast not learned and dost not know.

26 The word of the books — thou exaltest the people of God with the evil of thy teaching, and with thy word which profiteth not.

27 But thou shalt be requited according to thy deeds, and according to the fruit of thy kännafr ; for God is no respecter of persons. And to those who learn good, He will reward them with good things, which He hath prepared for those who love Him.

28 But thou, thou hast nowhere to escape from His hand, who shall judge thee; and behold, He shall judge thee according to thy deeds. For thou and those whom thou hast mäharək-ed shall be judged.

29 I have told you that the dead shall rise. Then those who kept His commandment shall rise; and none shall yətəhakäl to stand. For His commandment shall bring them forth — as the earth bringeth forth grass when the rain yəzäbəṭ .

30 And as the moist tree bringeth forth its shoot when the rain watereth the earth, and as it bringeth forth fruit — its root or the ear of grain — and it cannot withhold its fruit in the year God hath śämər-ed

31 And as conception, when ḥəmam cometh, the womb cannot restrain it but when it heareth the voice of God, it beareth them for the dew which is from God shall come down upon it.

32 And likewise the earth shall bring forth it cannot restrain but when it heareth the voice of God, it beareth them for the dew which is from God shall come down upon it.

33 And the bodies shall be gathered into their places, where their ʾabədənət fell; and one house of the treasuries of souls shall remain. And the souls shall enter into the bodies, from whence they went forth before.

34 And at the blowing of the horn, the dead shall rise in the twinkling of an eye; and they shall stand before God, and He shall requite them according to the work of their hands.

35 Then thou shalt see that thou shalt rise from the dead; and thou shalt be tanəkər-ed in all thy deeds which thou didst upon earth. And when thou seest them written before thine eyes — and this ʾamirä thou shalt tənäsəḥ — where it shall not profit thee.

36 And thou shalt know that thou shalt rise from the dead; and thou shalt be judged according to thy deeds which thou hast done.
