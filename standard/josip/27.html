Josippon

Chapter 27

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Their going forth from Sheol is by Jesus, for Christ.

2 And the Church is in the likeness of the tent. And in it — that which was baptized in it: the manna which came down from heaven: for Christ — they live by it, them. As the children of Israel were in it, in the tents they ate the manna. And the prophet — the rock which was cleft, which no cut: men Gabriel — for Christ was born of a Virgin, without the seed of man, and He became a great mountain, and filled all the world, and the were. And as for the Church, it is likened unto the tent. And the wilderness, in truth, is also filled with Paradise, as we aforetime. For in Paradise, in it, one rivers flow and water the thirst of the whole earth. And so also the Church: in it one rivers — one rivers of life — flow in it, and they water the thirst of men in all the earth, who thirst for the word of God, who love it.

3 As David the prophet saith in the Psalm of the hundred and fourth: As the hart longeth for the springs of water. And he saith in the Psalm of the one and seven: Sweet unto my throat is Thy speech; more than honey, and the honeycomb is its taste unto my mouth. And in Paradise [there are] many trees; and in the Church [there are] many books, which are in Paradise. He said [concerning] the days: He said: There shall be in that day a great famine — not of bread and water; God saith: [It is] of the word. I show [thee] that His word is bread and water; and — now the hunger is from Him; for they speak, and they understand not its interpretation; for God closed their eyes and their hearts and their ears, as the prophet said concerning them.

4 And in Paradise [is] the tree of life, of which God said — He who saith concerning it: from it: they are of God, who protect them for the souls, and they them.

5 As Moses said in the Law: — For not by bread alone doth man live, but by every word which proceedeth from the of God. I show [thee] that the word of God is the bread of life — He is; and so also Isaiah the prophet moreover named the word of God bread and water, and he said: concerning the Jews for ever.

6 And in the Church [is] the tree of life, which in truth is the flesh and the blood of Christ.

7 Of which Christ said: He that eateth My flesh and drinketh My blood liveth for ever.

8 And as for the Church, which in is the Paradise of God, He saith the earth from. — Adam was in Paradise, and he until the world; and — Sheol; and when Christ raised [him] from he entered into Paradise — moreover men who shall be — from, and he is born afresh, and he falleth into and he shall be in judgment; and when — Christ with judgment — entereth into them — anew, as He brought in Adam Paradise — anew he is born the new birth — born from God: he shall be with Him in for evermore; for he is an infant, a babe, who hath no strength to feed himself the food. Save: they anoint him with his mother, until he becometh like her, and he eateth food.

9 So also he who is born of God by Baptism — they anoint him [with] the flesh, and they him for Christ, when he abideth in this world; for he is fleshly, and there is not unto him strength, that he should eat food: Christ is spiritual altogether.

10 Save those who suck the milk of Christ — which is His flesh and His blood — until they become as Christ after death, and they live in this life; and they receive that which is given; and the receivers distant; and they are by them; far; and they suck them the milk of and the blood of Christ: — a pool and from milk — it is fleshly — for them milk which floweth from Christ, in a fullness which passeth not, and a which passeth not away.

11 For thus He said unto you: And — I am with you — that ye may eat and drink with Me at My table in My kingdom. Gabriel: that they may dwell so, as giveth birth to a child, and he becometh like them. And by the Baptism of holy Christianity a man becometh [free] from the power of the Devil, and he becometh — who was born, who was born without desire — as the incarnation was for Christ; for the Spirit of Christ from the birth of desire, and the shall have no authority over him, when the Spirit of Christ is in it.

12 For the Spirit of Christ — who is the Holy Spirit — is a likeness of Joshua the son of Nun; He is He who them —, the believers — He shall reign in the kingdom of heaven; He shall reign in the kingdom of heaven, from which the Devil and his host fell.

13 And He saveth them from him, and He overcometh for them, as Joshua the son of Nun did with the children of Israel.

14 And the believers shall bring the children of Israel from the fruit and the land. So also the priests, who show — [if] they know the kingdom of heaven — they shall bring unto the believers the fruit of the kingdom of heaven: the flesh and the blood of Christ. He is a sweet fruit, which men brought forth [before] the eye; He is from the whole earth, that the children of Israel may see it, and taste it, and know the taste, and desire that land, that they may rejoice in its many fruits.
