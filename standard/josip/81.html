Josippon

Chapter 81

1 Concerning the love of God: let it shine forth by the light of the Holy Spirit the mystery of the cross unto them that it may be made known.

2 mysteries unto in our Lord Christ, and the faith, and their communion.

3 and that unto thee henceforth which truly in their swaddling — which truly in their swaddling — on the eighth day from the birth: so unto thee Thomas; and unto thee Thomas, from, on the eighth day from. And as Jerusalem, and into the temple, and he was united from on the Thursday, after the birth, with: so into the heavenly Jerusalem, and the holy shall be saved, and he was united as he said.

4 [The text of this verse stands in chapter 80, verses 11–20; it is not duplicated here.]

5 The Almighty removeth sorrow and their wealth — the cause of this is by the help of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the living God which he mentioned for in the Book of the Interpretation of the Incarnation of the Son of God and his crucifixion: that the cause of his crucifixion and his sufferings was for judgment; and he it is who for judgment was made man and was likened, without, unto salvation from by judgment, without corruption — for.

6 concerning Adam his father, as I have made known unto thee in the Book of the Interpretation of the Incarnation and the Crucifixion; and likewise Adam also dwelt in Sheol; and Noah, without the word of the house of his children, dwelt in Sheol; and spake with God as a friend speaketh with his friend: he dwelt in Sheol until the incarnation of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, the Son of God, and.

7 For the believers concerning the judgment, again — and this is it: for before the coming of our Lord Christ the garden of the tree was, because of the transgression of Adam; and upon the earth was death from sin; and from it the righteous man descendeth into Sheol.

8 That they should not see Sheol, nor descend unto it: for at the time when their soul is separated from their bodies, it ascendeth unto paradise, and they rejoice with Adam and Noah and Abraham and Moses; and he gave them this joy for the believers, without toil and sorrow.

9 the Jews.

10 Job the prophet.

11 Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

12 And he joined unto them blessing, with fasting, with sorrow and weeping, and with in this world, that they might choose this for their souls, and be joined by their desire.

13 The strait [gate]: and the Lord bade them to enter in at the strait; for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that is given unto destruction, and many enter into it; and the gate and the hard way are not taken into life.
