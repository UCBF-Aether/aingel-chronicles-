Josippon

Chapter 90

1 He is he that overcometh the world, [even] he that believeth that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.

2 And he said: He that believeth in the Son of God — [the witness is] in life upon him; and he that believeth not in the Son of God hath not the life of the Son. I believed in the Son of God.

3 And he said: Who is [He], Lord, that I should believe in Him? And He said unto him: Thou hast seen Him, and He that talketh with thee, He is [He].

4 And again He said unto them:, when your father: Thou art the Son of God.

5 That when, thou mayest believe in Me.

6 And, when, you, that ye may believe and understand that I am in the Father, and the Father in Me.

7 And again He said: For this cause my Father loveth Me, because I lay down my life, that I may take it again.

8 And there is none who,; and I; and again,.

9 And again He said: Father, glorify Thy Son.

10 And the word from the Father, from heaven, I glorified Thee; and again.

11 And He said unto them again, unto His disciples: His time shall come, and now it is, that ye shall be scattered, every man to his own, and shall leave Me alone; and I am not alone, because the Father is with Me. And Mark the Evangelist said: When the high priest said unto them: Are ye the Son of the Blessed? And Jesus Christ said unto him: Yea, I am He; and ye shall see the Son of Power, from life, sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming in the clouds; and the high priest rent his garments, and he said: What [need] have we further of witnesses?

12 Behold, you, a witness, that [it is] blasphemy: what do ye say.

13 And he rendered judgement [unto] them all; and they said: Let Him be; and they asked Pilate that He should be; and he said unto them: What sin and evil hath He done, that He should be crucified?

14 And when they came together against Him, spake concerning the Son, whom He appointed and named for, a man.

15 And they said unto him: Let be upon us, and take us away.

16 And Pilate desired to let Him live; and the of the Jews [cried out], and they said unto him: If thou lettest [Him] live, for thou art not Caesar: whosoever maketh himself a king.

17 And they that they should crucify Him, their; and when they had crucified Him, from Him — hours, on Friday, according to the hour — Thou didst eat from the until the ninth hour.

18 And at the hour [when] the went forth from Paradise, He cried with a great voice, in Hebrew, and said: Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani.

19 And this He said, that they might know [even] the Jews, and that it might be fulfilled which was prophesied concerning Him by David the prophet in the psalm, which saith: My God, My God, hast Thou forsaken Me? and, hast Thou forsaken Me.

20 For he said in this psalm: The assembly of the wicked have laid hold upon Me; they pierced My hands and My feet; and all My bones.

21 And He rose on the third [day] and appeared unto His disciples; and Thomas was not with them. And when the report of the resurrection went forth, [they told him]; and he said: As for me, I will not believe, I see His hands and His feet; and I put my [fingers] into the, and into His feet, and all My bones.

22 And they parted My garments for themselves, and cast lots upon My vesture; and they clothed not, they reached not; and they hearkened not to a word, and they returned not unto Him; nay, they were not ashamed in concerning Him.

23 And before the the Sun cried out and said: Father, into Thy hands I commend My spirit. And after eight days our Lord showed Himself unto them, and said unto Thomas: Bring thy finger hither, the marks of the nails; and reach hither into My side; and be not faithless, but believing. And Thomas answered and said: Thou art my Lord and my God. And thou hast seen Me with thine eyes, these who have not seen Me shall believe; and thus it was, John the Evangelist.

24 And he said: Many other signs did our Lord Jesus Christ in the presence of His disciples, which are not written in this Book.

25 And these things are written of Him, that ye may believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, and that believing ye may obtain life in His name, for ever.

26 This is the of the apostles',, in the Christian faith: that He is the Son of God,.

27 And He shall be born of thee, Holy, in; and He shall be called the Son of God.

28 And when John the Evangelist said: And He — the Word became flesh — knoweth not the Son of God, that He became flesh, and not by a which is beside Him.

29 And again he said: No man hath ascended and into heaven, but He that came down from heaven.

30 This he said concerning which came down from heaven, not flesh; and He who ascended into heaven, made flesh, a man with Him.

31 And keep the of the land, as the prophets taught them; and received the Holy Spirit from the prophesying of the prophets, and He spake by their mouths.

32 And this is their teaching, which they preached abroad in the world: that He is the Son of God, who in truth was made flesh.

33 And after He was made flesh — and as Gabriel the angel said unto the Virgin, before God — after He was made flesh, at the stream of Jordan, said: This is My beloved Son; and so was our Lord Jesus Christ in Himself, that He is the Son of God; as they preached in the of, before kings and peoples, and in the books.

34 Holy: that through Him all who believe may be saved, unto the age of the ages, Amen.

35 The third discourse of the assembly: how there came to be in it an occasion of in the council; and men erred not in this orthodox faith, which is of the apostolic fathers, for years, until the time when Diocletian reigned, and Maximian, and Magnentius, — the reign of the Arameans.

36 And there was persecution the gods; and they destroyed the churches of the Christians; and there were slain in the many of the Christian people — martyrs whose number [is not told] — in their days, at their hands, of His, until the time when Constantine reigned; he also likewise; and the Christian concerning the cross.

37 And the faith in Christ: they brake the idols, and destroyed their houses, and built the houses of the Christians; and the faith was established and prospered in his days,, when there appeared Arius,, the denier of Christ, who was a priest in Alexandria; he said: Our Lord Jesus Christ is a created being.

38 And they in the evil faith, and many men.

39 And in there assembled the council of the fathers,, in the city of, in the year of Diocletian, in the reign of Constantine the king.
