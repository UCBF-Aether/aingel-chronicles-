Josippon

Chapter 16

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 holy Eve. And as for the pretext: for God remembered the serpent with and remembered not Satan concerning the desire that was joined; He remembered them.

2 And when Adam and Eve went forth into the earth — for Satan knew that God had cast them down in judgment.

3 And from then he plotted his deceit and in the earth and in Sheol, when he had become fallen.

4 And he set upon the tree from them an unclean spirit from the hosts, who made them do deeds of after the manner of beasts, until the day that God willeth; shortly He shall appear unto him in the vision which he sought, and darkness; and he feareth him — until the Devil — and from the smell of his vision; and he set darkness within, as Ezekiel the prophet saith; and he taketh the spirit and bringeth it down into; for a spirit of the heart, speaking, worketh not — it worketh in the garden; it worketh not in the flesh, which is clothed with the mist of blood, as light worketh not in a lamp without the mist of oil.

5 And when, O Devil, the spirit is separated from the flesh, as the light is separated from the lamp when the oil is spent — and as for the pretext, why did the Devil set himself upon the children of Adam and Eve? For this cause he made them do the work which they all desire; he is the doer of their bondage.

6 And as God was over Adam in the commandments given unto him, so also was God over the children, their seed; and when they slew them, they slew their seed.

7 And so said God concerning them, unto Adam: ye desired to slay them; and your father Satan; ye run after their footprints.

8 And he is the breath of men; he is from of old, and they know not; for he lied unto Adam and Eve, and said unto them: Ye shall become angels, and ye shall not die. for ever against them, when they obeyed him; for the Apostle John said: For the work of is he.

9 The Apostle also said: Ye are the servants of him whom ye obey. And by his victory over Adam and Eve, in that they obeyed him, they became his servants, and their seed was bound unto the ages.

10 As the Apostle saith in the Epistle to the Romans: For death came by the transgression of Adam, and reigned from Adam unto Moses; and over them that sinned not after the similitude of the sin of Adam are all they that die; from all the seed they go down into Sheol, even unto the infants that sinned not, and the prophets and the righteous from the fathers.

11 As Job the righteous said: For there is none born without the sin of Adam; it became a unto the Devil in the earth for one hour; for by the transgression of Adam Satan was made for one hour; for by the transgression of Adam Satan made them his own, and man — Satan made him his own; upon the infant, a child, an unclean spirit.

12 And when he dieth in that hour, he bringeth him down into Sheol, unto Adam; and men dwelt thus: over him that is born an unclean spirit until the day of his death; and they slay and bring down into Sheol, for from the time they sinned like the Devil, this followeth them with the Devil unto the ages. And if this be taken from upon them, and they leave unto the Devil his plot, his hosts are in this from working with him without judgment; and he lied unto him from this face of the judgment of God, and it came to pass that he hindered their salvation.

13 And another aspect: that the Devil took them not by violence, nor by compulsion, but by a pretext; and if God had taken them from him by violence or by compulsion, he would have and lied against Him; for He named Himself Righteousness, and this is His judgment, by all the prophets.

14 And shall be wisdom until He save them from these straits, without; and there is no wisdom with men concerning this. God restraineth all; He is able; and He is as God, concerning the righteous and the prophets.

15 But He was well pleased to choose from the seed of Adam those over whom the Devil and set his upon them, but from the time —

16 Abel, and Seth, and Enoch, and Noah;

17 And the angels: Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, and Job, and Moses, and Aaron;

18 And Joshua, and Samuel, and David, and Nathan, and Gad, and Elijah, and Elisha;

19 And Isaiah, and Jeremiah;

20 And Ezekiel, and Daniel, and the three youths, and Ezra;

21 And Hosea, and Micah;

22 And Amos, and Joel, and Nahum;

23 And Obadiah, and Jonah, and Haggai;

24 And Obadiah, and Zechariah, and and Malachi, and Esther;

25 And Jonith, and Judith, and her brethren, and her offspring.

26 And many others, righteous and holy.

27 Concerning them God was pleased to give them the smiting and the affliction and this and death, as He said unto him: From the judgment that is fitting over them in judgment, like the Devil, unto the ages; for from the time they were not all born a son, and their was not, a name of a son concerning them; and this of them all unto the ages was not a name a son concerning them: one sin. For the son did in his hiding-place the command of his; and he is as, when the twelve angels who made them not for who from the nights a son; and in the garden of the work God was bound with judgment together with mercy.

28 And the Son was the ransom in place of this that is fitting over Adam and his seed unto the ages, forasmuch as they sinned like the Devil and his.

29 And as the Devil hid his head from them and [set] them the serpent, and made a pretext over them, until he took them without violence:

30 So also God wrought in His wisdom and in His mercy, and was incarnate and became man, and hid His divinity from the Devil within the incarnation, as he was hidden within the serpent; and He did all the works of man that are weak, until the Devil supposed that He was a man like all men; and He wrought many of His miracles, and descended and went down into Sheol, and rested from him like the rest; and they set over Him guards, and He did unto him the sufferings that have in them, concerning Adam and his seed — the that is fitting over them unto the ages.

31 And further, when the Devil came that he might frighten Him — until the Devil — and He dieth and taketh the soul and bringeth it down into Sheol; death, while he visiteth and goeth down into Sheol unto the Devil, his friend — the slaying, in place of a ransom, without violence; for this was His wisdom from before the world was created, as we have spoken before from the Apostle Paul.

32 And so he saith in the holy Gospel: Glorify me, my Father, with Thy glory which I had with Thee before the world was created.

33 He wrought the slaying of the enemy and the victory over him, and revealed judgment and mercy and and glory; for all this glory is His, and it was fitting that by this He should do it from before the world was created.

34 For He saith in the Book of Creation: God made the heaven and the earth.

35 And the earth was invisible and was not made ready; and darkness was over the deep; and the Spirit of God moved upon the waters of the deep; and God said: Let there be light.

36 And God divided between the light and the darkness.

37 And God called the light Day, and the darkness Night.
