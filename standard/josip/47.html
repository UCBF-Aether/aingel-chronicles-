Josippon

Chapter 47

1 And now I love, O beloved, as the word of God, which saith: men shall rest on the day of the Sabbath — this which saith that he be joined unto His glory and unto His holiness and unto the hearing of His word, every day, both of them; and if he hath been made from this, in the life of the flesh of men, and concerning death, before the day of the Sabbath, the other — he shall perish from the people of God; and they which is gathered together for his flesh, beastly, from destruction; behold, he slept, that and a dead soul; and he caused to sleep, and God, salvation, who chose life from the life of his soul.

2 And if he concerning that which he did, and God shall quicken [him] until the day of the Sabbath, the other; and [he] shall be joined unto that which is commanded therein: the life of his soul, rest from the sin which was fitted upon him on the day of his being joined, for the servant of God, on the day which He chose for his soul from the seven days, the other.

3 God saith: men shall rest therein, and shall be joined unto the glory and unto the holiness and unto the reading, and shall not be made from Him in the work of the world.

4 He saith: and if he hath been made from Him in the work of destruction, and hath been from Him, he shall perish from the people of God, as God saith: Every soul which resteth not on that day shall be separated from His people — this which saith, as a cup they shall the of the people of strangers.

5 And concerning this it is fitting for them, that they on the day of the Sabbath unto the house of the Christians, the Paradise of God, and be joined unto the glory and the holiness which is without toil, and [that] which is without work, for the servant of destruction.

6 And from the ordinance of the offering, he shall be joined unto the reading of the books of God every day, both, for it is the day of God.

7 God saith by the tongue of David the prophet in the Psalm, the account of two and two: this is it — the day which God made; let us rejoice and be glad in it.
