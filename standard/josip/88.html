Josippon

Chapter 88

1 The second discourse: the exposition of their faith, of the apostolic fathers, which they proclaimed abroad in the house with.

2 And when our Lord Jesus Christ ascended into heaven in the glory of, and in the power of His authority, He gave to His disciples and told them that He would send unto them the Paraclete, that He might strengthen them and help them in the glory of the Gospel unto the ends of the earth; and He gave them authority, that they should cast out demons.

3 And they shall heal all [kinds of] sicknesses, and shall do wonders, and their deeds shall be marvellous and amazing; and by their hands all kings believed, and all the peoples, by this, [believed] their word and in all their mysteries; and they baptized them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, one God; and they told them that they should believe in God, that He is one God, and that there are not two gods; and the Son is one from the Father, and He is our Lord Jesus Christ; and He came down from heaven, while He was not separated from heaven, for He filleth all things, and there is nothing that cometh to an end.

4 And He became flesh of the Virgin, and she bore Him while she was a virgin, and He brake not the seal of her virginity, that men might believe that He who was born of her is God in truth, who was clothed, as Isaiah the prophet prophesied.

5 And he said: the which, and everything is with a seal; and that which is therein, and that which is without, except the LORD; and He entered in and went out, without being seen therein and without the seal being opened. And as Isaiah the prophet prophesied and said: Behold, the virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and they shall call His name Emmanuel, which is interpreted: God with us.

6 A child is born unto us, a son is given unto us, whose dominion is written upon His shoulder; and He is God, the Mighty One,, the messenger of great counsel. And again Isaiah the prophet prophesied and said: Behold My servant, whom I love, in whom I have chosen; I will put My Spirit upon Him, and all the peoples shall trust in Him.

7 shall follow the girdles of their loins; and they shall pray unto Thee, and worship before Thee, because the LORD hath dwelt upon Thee.

8 And Thou art God, and we knew Thee not. And as Jeremiah the prophet prophesied and said: The LORD shall descend, said, and in the midst of men.

9 And as Ezekiel the prophet prophesied and said: They shall know that I am, that the LORD is their God, when He appeared in the midst of men, and His openly.

10 And as David the prophet prophesied and said: God of gods in Zion.

11 And again he said: Of Zion shall it be said by men: A man, yea a man, was born in her; and He, the Most High, established her.

12 truly the LORD shall dwell with men, said the earth. And again David the prophet said: He shall descend as rain upon the fleece, and as the dropping which droppeth upon the earth.

13 The prophet [said]: The LORD shall come in truth, and shall appear upon the earth.

14 And Michael the prophet said: the word of the LORD from Jerusalem, and from Zion shall the law go forth. And Nahum the prophet said: Behold, He cometh and dwelleth upon thee, saith the LORD, who holdeth all.

15 And Zephaniah said: Rejoice, O Zion, and thy feasts.

16 For the LORD our God is mighty, and He shall dwell upon thee, and shall thee.

18 And Micaiah the prophet said: Behold, the LORD shall come and shall shine upon those who fear Him; the Sun of righteousness is His name. And the prophet said: the LORD, and He shall appear on high. And Job the righteous said: The LORD of righteousness shall appear upon the earth, and shall arise upon the sea, as — the apostles the faithful — God, who was made flesh of the holy Virgin, and became man, and was crucified, and died, and rose from the dead on the third day, and ascended into the heavens, from whence He was not separated, in, and was at rest.

23 And Isaiah the prophet prophesied and said concerning His death, that no deceit was found in His mouth; and He came that He might die for the sin of the people.

24 And again he said: He took, and bore sorrows.

25 And he said: Sorrows were laid upon Him for our sins, and for us He suffered, and by His stripes we were healed.

26 And moreover He came as a lamb to be slaughtered; and he said: They counted Him with the transgressors, and they delivered Him unto death.

27 And David the prophet prophesied and said concerning His crucifixion: The assembly of the wicked encompassed Me.

41 And our Lord, with the voice of the trumpet, sing praises to our God unto Thee; the LORD shall sit, saith he: His throne is holy. And again he said unto Him: The LORD said unto my Lord: Sit at My right hand, until I make Thine enemies beneath the footstool of Thy feet.

42 And, one of the apostles, testified and said unto: Christ, Thou art the Son of God, the one God. And He answered and said unto him: In this thou art blessed: flesh and blood hath not revealed this unto thee, but My Father who is in heaven.
