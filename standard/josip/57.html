Josippon

Chapter 57

1 The house of the Christians; and they did not bring them at the time of the Mass the offering alone — thus God commanded: the law, and his wealth, the causes; and that which could be, from the, and not from; and they them at the time of the Mass, in his causes, the causes.

2 One he loved: What? unto us mercy, ordinances in; when we fasted, we unto the and unto the; and therefore he commanded in the books: within, from this, desire, that we should abstain from, the flesh of a beast desire; and that which cometh forth from every beast — a beast shall be within the desire that is mingled; for he who ate the flesh of a beast — desire that is mingled, and desire within his flesh from the eating of that which he ate; and if desire be mingled within the flesh with the fellowship of Satan, men are conquered, and he cannot contend according to his nature which is within.

3 And if he abstain from eating that whose desire is within it, and abstain from eating and drinking in the daytime, his flesh is weakened, and Satan cannot contend against him, because his flesh is weakened; and there is no mingling, save the mingling of nature.

4 And the mingling of nature — the fear of God contendeth not against him, nor is it joined with Satan; for it is a seeking in its head, but it is conquered, and the fear of God contendeth if another mingling be added unto it, from that which they eat of beast; and concerning this he commanded in the book of fasts, that we eat not the flesh of a beast whose desire is, or a wife; and all the kindred of beasts — within them is desire, save alone.

5 And therefore he commanded that we eat not that which cometh forth from a beast; and he commanded that we eat the honey of bees; and that which cometh forth from a beast — but it is a beast which hath no desire.

6 And we are asked, that we abstain not from eating that which cometh forth from a beast which hath no desire, except because of desire alone; for we fasted for her sake, and not for the sake of another; another was unto me, before, from the causes.

7 And for her sake we know not, and not for the sake of another; for God made known, that a man, if he lie with a woman in one house, while the mingling of desire is conquered in, which is because of the food of beast, he cannot abstain from the woman; for the flame is conquered because of the food of beast; and therefore he commanded that we should abstain from eating of beast, that we may be able to bear the desire of the natures in the fear of God, if the mingling of beast be not added unto it.

8 And if a woman be present, and a woman in her in the days of fasting, and they eat the food of beast, they are able, likewise, to eat in desire.

9 and if, likewise, he him in the food of beast, he shall not be likewise a sinner; they lie in wait for him, and he cannot lie in wait in desire, sleeping, except at a time after a time; and he shall be one who knoweth when he; and desire is not unclean; and after baptism there is no lying with a woman in unclean desire,, like food alone; and when they eat and are not unclean, sin is not accounted unto them, if the food is not that which they eat on a day of fasting: he shall be dry, according as Adam sinned when he ate the fruit; he commanded that they should not eat.

10 And therefore the apostle Paul commandeth the man and the woman, that they abstain not from one another, except they consent together for a season, for the offering.
