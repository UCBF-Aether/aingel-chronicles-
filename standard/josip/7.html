Josippon

Chapter 7

1 the Son: the Son is as his Father; the Son is God in the Father.

2 the Holy Spirit, for he is the Spirit of the Father the Holy Spirit: the Spirit is God, for he is the Spirit of the Father.

3 the Holy Spirit from the Father they say the angels: as they say: We believe in one Father.

4 the Only Son by his blood and his sufferings: and he redeemed them from him.

5 the interpretation of the Son of God by the blood: the interpretation of the mystery of the incarnation of the Son of God and of his crucifixion.

6 And he is our Lord and his Father is Lord the Son is Lord.

7 and the Spirit and the Son — he is Lord it is fitting for him that he reign over sin.

8 the Father is Lord the Son is God in the Father and the Holy Spirit is Lord, for he is the Spirit of the Son.

9 the Son Jesus Christ, the only Son of God and the Holy Spirit Mary: We believe in one Lord Jesus Christ, the only Son of God.

10 life in the baptism of Christianity and the oblation and the priesthood the houses of the Christians baptism and oblation and priesthood: for one Spirit descends upon every baptism and oblation and priesthood.

11 the Christians one Church, a congregation of the apostles the holy apostles and in one baptism they covenanted with them it goes forth from this.
