Josippon

Chapter 20

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 the tomb; for the being of the divinity of the Son was one with the breath of the incarnation; and the being of the Holy Spirit is not removed: He abideth with the flesh, that He may anoint it with unction.

2 And by this He was called Messiah; and God hath revealed unto us in the Gospel that His being — and the being of the Holy [Spirit] — was not separated from the breath of the incarnation, nor from His body, at the time of death, until it is He who cried, saying: My Father, into Thy hands I commend my spirit.

3 This saying — Into the hands of the Father are the Son and the Holy Spirit — showed that the divinity of the Son was one with the incarnation, and was not separated from it; and the being of the Holy Spirit was not separated from the flesh; and moreover: that the flesh drieth not up — for He is He who was slain by violence.

4 That by His will it flowed, after death — water and blood from His side — to show that He dried not up: that He is He who was slain by violence.

5 And so, when they pierced Him, the Trinity not His body; for He gushed forth of Joseph; and the price of death had no over Him; then He made manifest in their faces, and they glorified Him with the glory which is among all Christians; and Holy is He: God holy, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal.

6 And that which followeth it showed that He dried not up when they pierced Him; and He is He who was slain by violence.

7 Those who did this deed [showed] that He said unto Adam and unto his seed — who are fitting for them, from the of concerning them — the bonds and the beatings and the and the scourging and the death.

8 For He is He who, when He knew that He feareth not death, nor tasteth the bitterness of death, as all the sons of Adam drank and at the time of death — instead of the bitterness of death, He could not — for He feareth not.

9 And so the Gospel said: For He is One, for whom all were created; and One is He of whom it is written.

10 And further, our God feared not, and He gave up His breath; and on account of death He flowed, and [it entered] into His side; and He is the Dead.

11 This saying: that the water came forth from His side — His — for Adam; and it is a sleep; and God made manifest from the side of Adam, and took from him one of his ribs; from it was made the of Adam: death.

12 And so the side of Christ was opened, and He is death; and it was not a for Adam and for his seed — from Him; and our Lord Jesus Christ revealed that the Devil should against Him: by the tree of the Cross; and he hath no authority over Him; thus [He said] unto His disciples in the third.

13 For the prince of this world cometh unto Me; he hath no authority over Me; but that the world may know that I love the Father; and as My Father said unto Me, so I do.

14 And for me of the world — this saying: that the Devil was over all the sons, from before the incarnation was created; the became — they became servants unto him, Adam and Eve — and which came forth from the desire of Adam.

15 And as for this which He said: He hath no authority over Me.

16 This saying: that I was not born from the — and he is Adam; and he hath no hold over Me, nor have we heard from Him concerning sin; who in the Gospel [saith]: I die by My will, that I may fulfil Thy word.

17 This saying: the will which He willed therein, that He should all the seed of Adam by Me; so also again He said: And the prince of this world they shall cast out and drive forth outside; and I, if I be lifted up from the earth, I will draw all men unto Me.

18 That which we do: when is drawn from them instead of the price of My death, when he cometh to kill Me; and I draw from his glory, and I cast down that which is the price of Mine.

19 As He said in the second Psalm: And the envy of the kings of the earth, and the princes were gathered together with My Father — with them together, together, — against the LORD and against His Anointed.

20 And therein, this which He said: He that dwelleth in heaven shall laugh them to scorn.

21 And God shall mock them, when He speaketh unto them in wrath; and in wrath He shall trouble them.

22 The prophet showed the of the Devil and his hosts, who became kings and princes over all the earth, and their gathering against Christ, to kill Him; this saying, when He said: Against the LORD and against His Anointed.

23 This saying: that by Him they were gathered together — the sons.

24 He is He who was gathered against the Glorious One, from His firstborn Son.

25 He is He who was gathered against the Glorious One, from His firstborn; and He shall mock them.

26 This saying: that He shall mock them, when they are gathered in power to kill Him, as He mocked in power to kill Him: over the which was desired, they slew the lions; for she hath no over Him; and concerning this: that He is He who, when He speaketh in wrath, shall trouble them.

27 And as for this which He said then — this saying — when He completeth in him, and their power is spent, He shall speak unto them in wrath; and in wrath He shall trouble them.

28 For the Devil, when he wrought with — as the sons of Adam — and they slew them.

29 And Christ died willingly, and seized the Devil by the power of His divinity, which was One in the Holy Spirit; and He spake in wrath, and in wrath He troubled them; and He bound him in judgment, in place of the price of his slaying; and He brought him down into Sheol, and there, as a; He is the slaying; for He slew for the spirits, their prison.

30 And our Lord slew him, concerning the price of His own.

31 As he said, in the Psalm of the hundred-and-third: Thou, O LORD, us as the grass.

32 And in the Psalm of, He scattered, as for gold.

33 It showed that the is from — or the slaying.

34 And so also the prophet speaketh concerning Christ: for He is He who shall smite his mouth.

35 This saying: that He smote His mouth, when He said: My God is from My side. He said: My God is from that which is upon Me — My God; from my hiding place: this is it — He smote His mouth, when He said: My God, My God; and He said: My God, hear me, from my hiding place.
