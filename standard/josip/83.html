Josippon

Chapter 83

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And we also shall receive rest; and it is not too much for us: sorrow and tribulation and our adversaries; and he said: in the midst of sins.

2 And they shall seize you, and persecute you, and deliver you unto councils and unto synagogues and unto kings and unto governors, for my name's sake; and ye shall be witnesses.

3 And be not anxious what ye shall say, and what ye shall answer: for utterance and wisdom shall be given you from the Holy Spirit, which ye shall speak at that time; and your adversaries shall not resist.

4 rejoice and be exceeding glad, for great is your reward in heaven. And he said again: Blessed are they which are persecuted for righteousness' sake: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

5 And he said again: Whosoever would save his life shall lose it; and whosoever loseth his life for my sake shall find it.

6 And what is a man profited, if he shall gain the whole world, and lose his own soul? And he said again: He that loveth his life shall lose it; and he that loseth his life shall lose it.

7 the master of the house; and if they have called me, they shall persecute you also; and if they have called your elders Beelzebub, and your envoys unto the councils.

8 Blessed are ye, when they shall persecute you and revile you, and shall say against you every evil word falsely.

9 and shall follow me; and he said: As I suffered in the world, they that strive: I for the world; and ye shall be from this.

10 be ye not troubled; and in your patience possess ye your souls.

11 And this he said unto them, not as the prophet spake concerning that which shall come upon them: for his word is truth, because he is the herald thereof; as he said unto them, unto the martyrs and unto the earth: for he created all the works by his word.

12 And so it shall be in the saying of the completion of: and ye shall have tribulation in the world; He laid tribulation upon us by this word, and made us out of all, that in patience in this.

13 He taught us His glory; and after the tribulation upon us, and if from men — as we bear this reproach — joy everlasting; and if we keep His commandment, from hunger and thirst and nakedness and weeping.

14 And afterwards [they besought] Abraham, that he would send Lazarus the poor man, that he might cool his tongue with a drop of water on the tip of his finger. Abraham said unto him: Remember that thou receivedst thy good things in thy lifetime in the world, and Lazarus in his tribulation.

15 the life everlasting, which is not taken, neither except by fasting; and He wrought this for us, for He knoweth that we are and mindful; and we are in our, that we should go willingly in fasting and thirst and sorrow and; and be ye not troubled: joy in.

16 for the son; and it is not received from the stranger, as the apostle said: despise not the chastening of the LORD, and be chastened: for whom the LORD loveth, He chasteneth.

17 We believe that no chastisement shall come upon him — neither from man, nor from tribulation, nor from sickness, nor from death — who keepeth the commandment and knoweth: he is in hunger and thirst and nakedness and weeping.

18 And whosoever of us doeth not this, and chastisement cometh upon him — whether from himself, by sin, or by sickness, or by loss of goods, or by the shortening of his life —: [know] that God loveth him, and therefore He chastened him, and that he might be saved from the chastisement that cometh upon him.

19 All of them, after death, are in everlasting judgement, as it is said in the holy Gospel: for the rich man that glorieth in the world — woe unto him! — after death is in condemnation for ever.

20 And when he asked Abraham that he would send Lazarus the poor man, that he might cool his tongue with a little water on the tip of his finger, Abraham said unto him: Remember that thou receivedst thy good things in thy lifetime in the world, and Lazarus in tribulation; and now here he resteth.

21 He resteth after death in the everlasting rest; and it behoveth us that we be not ashamed, if we be tried — we who receive this in faith — and joy, that we may know that it is in the which leadeth us into eternity.

22 And we beseech our Lord Jesus Christ, who our Saviour, that He would make us worthy to receive fasting for His name's sake, and, and give us patience over this, for His sake; and let him that is holy be counted worthy of.

23 and glory, with His Father and the Holy Spirit, who is equal with the Father and with the Son, now and for ever, and unto the age of ages. Amen.
