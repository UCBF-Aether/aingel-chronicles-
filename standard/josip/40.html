Josippon

Chapter 40

1 And if thou knowest not, thou shalt not do; go not unto another city, until thou shalt see that which abideth for thee; as thou goest, thou seekest the work of this world in the district which hath no sacrifice, unto the other which hath a sacrifice.

2 And the day of the life of the Spirit passeth from thee without work; because thou knowest not, that thou — until the other day — shalt not, that thou mayest find the life of thy spirit; verily God commanded that He do not the work of this world on the day of rest; therefore that men should forsake every thing, and abide in the holy books, which are the spiritual life, and should pray over it without delay, and be occupied in labours, as he doeth the border of eternal life; for the reading of the books of God cleanseth the soul and the body, and they are cleansed from sin, in the portion for His disciples, in the holy Gospel; verily it is a vineyard, and its fathers withered, and every ordinance that beareth not fruit and dieth is done; and that which beareth fruit is it that is cleansed, as He created many, and it was fulfilled.

3 Behold, I have shown thee in this word which I have told you therein.

4 It is shown: they shall spring up; men shall be cleansed from all of it, and shall bear fruit, the fruit of eternal life.

5 And he who heareth not the word, and speaketh not His books continually, shall be without fruit; and his Father cutteth him off from the burden of the Son, as they cut off the branch from the vineyard.

6 And he who speaketh the books continually on the holy day, he abideth in Christ, as the branch abideth in the vineyard; for the cleansing of Christ cleanseth him, and sanctifieth him from his sin, and causeth him to bear — he beareth — the fruit of eternal life.

7 Beloved, this — and for the rest which we have mentioned, the hours of the faithful; and in them they shall overcome the satans — which is our going unto the house of the Christians in the morning and in the evening, at every time; and we tarry at the time of the Holy One without ceasing, from the, until it is fulfilled.

8 And the hearing of the books of God on the holy day — all of it, and unto the rest; if men work continually, He abideth, in it the Holy Spirit; and he shall be continually who is born of God; and there shall not be over him the hosts of sin.

9 As John the evangelist said in the epistle: For everyone who is born of God sinneth not; for his seed abideth firm, and he cannot sin; for he is born of God.

10 And again he said in it: He who is born of God keepeth himself, and the evil one draweth not near to touch him.

11 And all the faithful who are baptized — they are born of God — as the seed of the Holy Spirit abideth firm in them, by which they are born of God; and He keepeth them from the evil one and from the hosts; and they cannot sin; but they do good, without their desire; for the Holy Spirit worketh for them over him; for He is firm in them; and He loveth to abide in them; if he keepeth these commandments, He abideth in them, and keepeth them from the evil one; and they have no power to sin, for the Holy Spirit hindereth them from sin, and keepeth them from the evil one who draweth them into the doing of sin.

12 And if they sin by weakness, then the Holy Spirit who dwelleth in them taketh them, and draweth them unto shame by leave; and likewise if they forsake one of these three commandments, the Holy Spirit forsaketh them, and an unclean, evil spirit overcometh them, and draweth them unto the doing of sin, by work and by violence; and God shall not be in them, if it be so; knowledge: he who is born of God sinneth not.

13 As John the apostle said: Behold, what is the greatness from these commandments, by which it shall be that men delight with Christ, the Son of God, in the glory of the kingdom; and he shall be who is born of God.

14 And they are not fat, and are not drunk, and have no weariness; and sweat perisheth not as and thirst; and sweat perisheth not; for he is he who from of old goeth to the work of this world; he goeth forth unto the house of the Christians; and after its fulfilment, work — he goeth unto her in the evening; and sweat perisheth not.

15 And at the time of the Holy One, if all — all — at every time, its measure is as one hour.

16 From seven and seven hours — and there is not in this again a — in those festivals they shall find the seeking of eternal life in the kingdom of heaven; and God giveth a doubled [reward] instead of that which they have left in those two other days, concerning the trading of the holy day; for fatness is grace; it is in His fatness.

17 As He said in the tongue of the prophet: I who slew, and I make alive; I am He who burneth; I who smote, and I heal; whom I heal.

18 But He giveth unto men a double [reward] instead of that which they have left from the work of this world, as He said in the holy Gospel — He said: Be ye not anxious for that which ye shall eat, and for that which ye shall drink; for all this do the nations seek; and your Father who is in heaven knoweth that ye are in need of all this, of which ye are needy; but this [seek ye] first: the kingdom and the righteousness; and it shall be given unto you a double [reward] instead of that which was left from us, for the sake of this seeking, that took the kingdom and the righteousness; and it shall be given unto us a double [reward] instead of that which is without the reading of the word of God, first; for He is the Father of us all, and He is He who giveth us that which is fitting, and that which is fitting, and that which is fitting; for He knoweth that we desire — we seek all this; He giveth unto us the kingdom; and He reckoneth unto us all this; He is He who giveth unto us; for He is He who, as we are humbled, strengtheneth us for this temporal world. Behold, beloved: these three commandments, keep them; for they are the salvation of everyone who believeth in Christ; and in them they overcome the evil enemy, who is he who prevaileth over them; and they have no power over him, without these three commandments.

19 For in them the Holy Spirit abideth firm in them, and strengtheneth them against the evil host, and they overcome him, while they are upon the earth in the flesh.

20 And after their going forth from the flesh, He giveth them victory over the hosts of the Devil in the air, and saveth them — joy and life for ever.

21 And the evil one, moreover — when he knew that these three commandments are they, a fence for the faithful, and in them they overcome him — and he proceedeth against them in their, and hindereth the enemy, and troubleth them from them, until he separate them from her, and the Holy Spirit forsaketh them, and he prevaileth over them, and destroyeth them.

22 And they, moreover, beloved, shall burn in faith; readiness shall be with them, as a wall, that they may receive in it the buckler, the darts of Satan and his spears shall trouble them from them — the commandments.

23 As the apostle Paul said in the epistle to the people of Ephesus: Take ye the sword of the Holy Spirit, which is the word of God, in all those [times], and in all, while ye pray at every time in the Holy Spirit; and be watchful over this, at every time.

24 I show that the word of God, which abideth in it — or which abideth upon him — and His glory and His holiness continually, is the sword of the Holy Spirit, which is in truth.
