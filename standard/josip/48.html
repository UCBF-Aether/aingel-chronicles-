Josippon

Chapter 48

1 This which saith, in joy: spiritual is he who is in the reading of the books of God, which is the rest of the soul, and life, and thy joy: as God said — for man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word which proceedeth out of the mouth of God; for the word of God, it is the life of thy spirit, as the bread is the life of the flesh.

2 And if there be a man who loveth a companion for him who heareth the reading of God, God giveth the portion of the righteous [in] His reading in the kingdom of heaven; and the portion shall be that which is greater than the portion which taketh up the dead; and in the first [place] he is greater than in the first [place], he.

3 For he who taketh up the dead shall take up the body which is present [and] shall die.

4 And as for him who readeth the word of God on the day of the Sabbath, he shall take up souls from their sins, that they may live unto everlasting; and the alms of this are greater than the alms of him who spendeth upon the orthodox; and He satisfieth them, for He is [He who] filled and watered the perishing flesh and the perishing

5 And this, moreover, satisfieth the soul and watereth [it], which liveth for ever.

6 How much is he who taketh up, who readeth on the day of God the word of God, for him who causeth [others] to hear the reading.

7 And as for him who knoweth not the reading, it is fitting for him to go unto him who causeth [others] to hear the reading, without toil.

8 And if he be not in the city in which he dwelleth, [in] that [place] where [one] causeth [others] to hear the reading, he shall go unto another city where [one] causeth [others] to hear the reading, as he goeth for the sake of a perishing work, from a city which hath no market unto another city in which there is a market; and he who from the life of the soul shall perish from God, as his flesh perisheth, which in the work of the life of his flesh, from the life of this world.

9 This is the reason of the Sabbath.

10 And as for him who doeth not a spiritual work, he shall be lost from the life everlasting, and from the life of this world.

11 For he who abideth in a perishing work, from a spiritual work, on the day of the Sabbath, hath destroyed the soul, and shall be lost from the life everlasting; and God shall judge him on the day of the third and in sorrow, in earth and in heaven, as He said: By the hand of; and it was not.

12 If ye keep the commandment on the day of the Sabbath, God giveth life and good, and much is His power; and He unto him always in earth and in heaven; and God giveth on two days great things, the which they on that day; and He blesseth, and therein.

13 God saith: men shall rest on this day; and men shall rest with Him; and the son and the daughter and the manservant and the maidservant shall rest.

14 And concerning this, it is fitting for a man that he work not, for a woman, the work of the world, and that he not diminish the glory of God and the holiness of God, but that he go with the woman unto God and unto the house of the Christians; and he shall rest in the voice of God; and so shall he do for our sons and our daughters and our servants and our maidservants and the stranger who dwelleth in the house; and he shall not make them [do] the work of the world. And as for him who resteth not, he [is] in the hearing of the word of God alone; and God shall seek blood from his hand; for he is a good rest for the soul alone; but it is fitting for him that he be grieved concerning their former state, as a soul; and He shall make good unto them in the being joined unto the glory of God and unto the holiness and unto the hearing of His word; and they shall not be made in the work of: the work of the flesh, except that which they keep for food alone.

15 And as for that which is food and drink, which the flesh cannot [do] without it, they shall do this in the night, before the glory and the holiness.

16 And if they are able, they shall do this on the day before; for God them in this until the evening.

17 For He saith: I rested, and men rested; and our sons and our servants and our maidservants and the stranger, who is his house, rested; and our asses rested; and the rest of the asses — which He saith — is the rest of His flesh; that is: our asses are the soul.

18 And He commanded that [a man] rest not with food and drink on the day of the Sabbath, which is the day of rest, that the being joined unto the reading every day, both of them; and the righteous and joy shall; for He is [He] who, when He was hungry, for the souls in hunger, and shall from life.
