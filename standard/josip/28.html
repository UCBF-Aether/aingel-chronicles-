Josippon

Chapter 28

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 This was likened, concerning the world, as a type of the children of Israel: when they were in the wilderness of Mount Sinai, they ate manna — they all ate of it — which is the body of Christ; and they drank water from the rock, which is the blood of Christ.

2 And they shall keep the law of Christ and His commandments, which we keep, and shall speak unto the men of the land of Canaan. And as were those honoured men who were sent unto the land of Canaan, so is the kingdom of the heavens, wherein they shall dwell who have therein life eternal.

3 For they here are refreshed but little, according to the measure of their understanding, because they believed as children who suck milk; and likewise they are refreshed, and they rejoice in it with perfect joy by the sight of His countenance, when they glorify Him and hallow Him, and they dwell with Him in the kingdom; and their life with Him shall be in life, as in the life of their fathers, when them.

4 And the offering, and the kingdom of the heavens, which the priests believed: they are the men — the son of Nun is that company who believed and the Holy Spirit, for whoever in truth [believed] the kingdom of the heavens; and it is with us, those riches which make us taste the taste of the kingdom with us. And their fruits from its fruit. And whoever tasteth this taste of that kingdom from all the priests — and they taught the people, and made them taste it, and strengthened them, that they should love and do works of goodness, wherein they shall inherit that kingdom, and made strong their faith against the hosts of the demons who envy him on account of that kingdom — he shall be as Caleb the son of Jephunneh, who strengthened the children of Israel together with Joshua the son of Nun, and inherited with them the land of Canaan.

5 Likewise is the priest who strengtheneth the people and teacheth them a good work in baptism, and he shall go up in his heart with that kingdom of the heavens, as the spies entered with Joshua the son of Nun into the land of Canaa. Baptism is that kingdom in the Holy Spirit, with Him; and they shall inherit the kingdom of the heavens with

6 The Holy Spirit, which is the type of Joshua the son of Nun.

7 And as for the priests who knew the taste of that kingdom of the heavens, and taught not the people, and strengthened them not, and made not their faith strong: they shall perish from before God, as there perished one man — the seventy spies, who saw the taste of the land of Canaan and encouraged not the people [to go] from it. And they said unto them: For the land is good, flowing, wherein those, those who dwell in it, are giants, mighty ones, before whom their eyes are as locusts, and its fortifications are lifted up unto heaven. And they perished by this word, and He diminished the faith of the people, and there perished of them two hundred hundred men, who came forth from the land of Egypt.

8 And now it is fitting unto all the priests that they strengthen the peoples, the believers, for the war of the demons — they who were kings in the kingdom of the heavens, in the type of the land of Canaan, which was promised to her, He being able to fight for them on account, and to save us from their hands. To Thee, Amen: thus we glorify Thee and hallow Thee continually, and we beseech Thee, that Thou save us from them, and give us victory over them, and bring us unto the order of the height, the heavenly, wherein they became kings, as He brought those who are of the children of Israel unto the land of Canaan.

9 And He gave them victory over those who dwell in it. Therefore it is fitting for you, that ye strengthen the faith of the people against their hosts.
