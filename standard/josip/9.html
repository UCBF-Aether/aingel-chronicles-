Josippon

Chapter 9

1 The second discourse: concerning the becoming-man of the Son of the LORD, and His crucifixion. beloved of the LORD: may He enlighten the eyes of thy heart by the dawn of the Holy Spirit the Paraclete, that thou mayest follow the mystery of the divinity.

2 First: that which thou askedst me — that I should write for thee a book, and reveal to thee in it the being of the LORD and His union, and the reason of the incarnation of the Son of the LORD, and His crucifixion, and. That which thou askedst, I have written for thee a book, and I have revealed to thee therein, and thee, the being of the LORD and His union; and I have made known to thee that the LORD is a being without division, and, and I do not ask of thee [anything] from the word of the LORD, by Himself alone.

3 For our Lord Jesus Christ said in the holy kingdom: There is none that knoweth the Son except the Father, and there is none that knoweth the Father except the Son, and him to whom He willeth to reveal [Him] to him.

4 Behold, the Son rejoiced, and revealed His knowledge, and the knowledge of His Father and of the Holy Spirit, to the holy prophets and apostles, and to our fathers the teachers of the holy Church; and they revealed that to the believers, because in them is the Holy Spirit through the holy baptism, and their eyes are shining; and they understand this Holy Spirit who dwelleth in them.

5 As Paul the apostle said: — there is none that can say that Jesus is Lord except by the Holy Spirit. And this also is as a word that proceedeth from a father: a tongue from a heart, a fleshly speaking spirit — they understand not its interpretation except by the flesh, which is, a human speaking spirit, as the spirit that proceedeth from it is a word.

6 beasts, and all the creeping things: but there is not in them a rational speaking breath; they understand not the interpretation of the word that proceedeth from a speaking, rational spirit, for they have hearing.

7 And so also bodies: but in them are rational spirits.

8 And so also were they baptized: but there is not in them the Spirit of the LORD, the Holy One; they understand not the word of this Spirit of the LORD, the Holy One; and when a man cometh to be, there cometh in him a rational and speaking spirit.

9 But when there is not in them the Spirit of the LORD, the Holy One — who is [in] all of them that were baptized — they understand not the word of the LORD, as the beasts understand not the word of man, for there is not in them the Spirit of the LORD.

10 And so also the apostle Paul said concerning them: For they who are of the breath, in whom there is not the Holy Spirit, receive not that which is of the Spirit of the LORD; for it is in his speech foolishness, because the Spirit of the LORD is not in them — as [a word] is spoken in it, and as a man speaketh unto those in whom there is not the spirit of man.

11 And they that were baptized, all of them — the Spirit of the LORD is in them; and if not in their faith, they understand not fully the interpretation of the word of the LORD, concerning the knowledge of the righteous in it. They are not naked from the Spirit of the LORD; but they are as a child that discerneth not the interpretation of the word of man because of [his] youth; and he is not naked from the human speaking spirit, but because of [his] youth, for he is a child: he understandeth not that.

12 And when he groweth up in it in years, and knoweth, all the believers understand likewise, in whom is the holy Spirit of the LORD from His Christian baptism; but they are too small for the teaching of the word of the LORD, and in it sin then, they understand by the Spirit of God, who dwelleth in them,.

13 They are taught and instructed, and believe His exposition, as a child [learneth] the interpretation of the speech of man, when he is taught and learneth in it, and receiveth and believeth by the spirit of man that is in them. And to our fathers, the apostles and the prophets, the Son revealed the mystery of the LORD, which was hidden in the Holy Spirit; and they revealed it to the believers, in whom is the Holy Spirit.

14 And behold, I have revealed to thee in the former book, from the words of those who spake in their books interpretations good and great — know — and to the knowing ones among all the believers, in whom is the Spirit of the LORD.

15 And behold, I reveal to thee in this book, by the help of the Holy Spirit, the reason of the incarnation of the Son of the LORD and His crucifixion, and again the mention of His exposition, from the fourth section.

16 The first section: know that in it His exposition [is] the end of Adam — a word, that its interpretation may reach in fullness unto the few in understanding; and know that the word shall not tarry over them, and they shall not forget its interpretation.

17 And the second section mentioneth: the end — its interpretation, in a few words, that he who asketh may understand its interpretation in few words.

18 The first [section] saith: that which the LORD made — heaven and earth — before He made [them]; and then He saith: on the earth He created them, the angels, in the midst of heaven: spirits without bodies.
