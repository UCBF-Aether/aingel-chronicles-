Josippon

Chapter 3

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 It was separated from the Father, and is not separated from the Son — the Spirit; for he is.

2 For the Son the Spirit and the Father is not separated from them.

3 From the Father, and power and and work: understanding.

4 The question of this word: the Holy Spirit, who is in the Father and in the Son, proceeds from the bosom of the Father unto the Son.

5 The Son and the Spirit the LORD a likeness that has no likeness.

6 the LORD: it befits him — peace the first part.

7 As the Son descended from heaven and was incarnate, so the Holy Spirit descended with the Son, as John saw.

8 and he, from the Father, descends and is not separated: he that has the wing descends and ascends, and is not separated from his fellow.

9 So the Holy Spirit is not separated from the Father [and] the Son; and so the Son was incarnate, but the Father and the Holy Spirit were not incarnate.

10 and he the LORD the Son and the Spirit.
