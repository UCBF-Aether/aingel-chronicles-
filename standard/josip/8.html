Josippon

Chapter 8

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 they shall rise from the dead life: all of them shall rise from the dead, and all of them shall live one life without death, for ever — the world to come.

2 the world to come in the Holy Spirit one holy Church: as they say, after their confessing in the Holy Spirit, they say: One holy Church, the congregation of the apostles.

3 of the apostles of baptism in the confession of the baptism the life everlasting.

4 in the Holy Spirit the Holy Spirit is one all of them: and they are all one body for Christ, though distinct in their members, in their body; and gathered in the Spirit for Christ the Holy One, who dwells upon all of them, and their body is for Christ.

5 for all of them the holy [Church] for the Christians who.

6 the three in their members: distinct in their members in their body, as the members of the body are distinct in their members — the hand distinct from the foot, and the eye distinct from the ear; and all of them are a congregation in one body.

7 all of them in the Spirit for Christ, the Holy One.

8 Who dwells for Christ and all of them: for Christ the Holy One, who dwells upon all of them.

9 the three that they shall rise from the dead: and in all of them is one covenant, that they shall rise from the dead, as the resurrection of Christ shall raise them — the Holy Spirit who dwells in them.

10 from the dead the resurrection of Christ the Holy Spirit in the holy baptism and the oblation.

11 And he life the Holy Spirit: he is the life of the Father and of the Son: for the Holy Spirit is the life of the Father and of the Son, and he shall be the life of all.

12 he shall be the life of all who proceeds from the Father and the Son.

13 Christ John the Baptist.

14 baptism for all Christ.

15 Without [separation] from the first Christ, and it befits and he.
