Josippon

Chapter 34

1 And water and wine — [for] our Lord [is] the glory: flesh within the belly; and [when] she bare Him — the breasts, the milk which is from that — again.

2 And when His flesh grew — as our flesh groweth, by the eating of bread and the drinking of water which is mixed with wine — [He likened] it unto us in every deed, without sin. And when the thief saw, he believed; and and — as He was with them — death, on account of us; as death on account of men: it was the days of, in the and within the; as they it in, into the; they it, on account of us, into the cup; His blood upon the; and we saw him in this; and we believe in, in the greatness, as of faith in them; and from Him the forgiveness of many; and on account of the in them, as he who believeth in truth him — and we believe in Him — remember us, O Lord, when Thou comest into Thy kingdom. And He said unto the thief: Verily as ye are today the drinking of blood; but beautifully He said: Thou shalt be with Me today in My paradise.

3 And so it behoveth all who to arrive, and at the time of the Mass, at every time; and whoso doeth this in this faith — if death come upon him on that day — is with Christ in paradise; for our Lord said: In My hand is righteousness. And therefore it behoveth every believer who standeth at the time of the Mass to in fear and in trembling, and to ask, and to beseech toward Him who [standeth] before Him, that He make him meet for paradise, and forgive him, as [He forgave] in My paradise; which He said unto all the, when they look upon Him in them; and we believe in, at every time of the; and from they have offered in Him.

4 And in Thy kingdom, at Thy time, that he should eat for the thief; and sins were multiplied in the faith; and they were sanctified, as he said: Remember me, O Lord, when Thou comest into Thy kingdom. And he mocked not, and he thought not the light of the world; and he was not troubled, as; and he spake not one word without for who thee, or for the Devil who was sent with him; and he went not forth from the house of the Christians, until all His flesh from upon the Cross.

5 And after the Ascension of all, He [sought] His and heaven, that they should ask [earnestly] that there should be in it [room for] the Holy Spirit, as [He rested] upon the apostles after His Ascension; and it behoveth [them] to read the five psalms — of the forty and the seven and the ten — as in them [is told] His Ascension; and to ask that the Holy Spirit depart not from upon him. This behoveth all the believers: [what] they do after His Ascension [at] the altar; for [then] the holy [sacrifice] was renewed in them at that time.

6 And [whoso] readeth the five psalms: he readeth thus: O my Lord Jesus Christ, who ascendedst into the heavens, fill me with the fullness of the Holy Spirit, which Thou didst [appoint] for Thy apostles after Thy ascension; and bringeth forth from on high [unto] the world; and cometh.

7 For unto Thee is the glory, and the honor, and the thanksgiving, with Thy Father, the merciful, and the Holy Spirit, who is equal with Thee in, and for ever, in the Name, Amen. [rubric] The Fifth Discourse, concerning the priests: a war in which the demons shall not prevail against the believers, and truly they shall overcome them. 1 [/rubric] O thou beloved of God, may He enlighten the eyes of thy heart with the light of the Holy Spirit; be comforted, that thou mayest [search] the mysteries in fullness. First, He revealed unto thee in the Book the incarnation of the Son of God, and His crucifixion, as God [prepared] for Adam and for his seed, that He might raise them up into the ordinance of exaltation, from which fell the Devil and his hosts.

8 And when they sinned, they believed; and the Devil reigned over them; and He slew them, and brought them down into Sheol, by their obedience unto him, God in His soul, and concerning him; and He brought up his soul from Sheol, unto paradise, which was the first, until should be loosed unto them from the of Adam; as the hosts with the Devil, until their from beneath them.
