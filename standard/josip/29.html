Josippon

Chapter 29

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 These giants are without number, and the believers are as locusts.

2 And the place of those who sought them: the believer is he who is exalted above all the heavens. Those who were asked of us concerning the believers — as Christ, who was for their sakes upon the wood, and was lifted up, and saved them, all of them, and bound [him] in Sheol, from their hands: He is the Victor, He is the Arab. And the demons are giants, abundant, and all men are before them as locusts; but those who believed in Christ glorify and hallow continually. He is who giveth us victory over them; and if we love the word, we cannot fight them, and we cannot prevail against their kingdom; it perished. And we depart, as there perished those who loved Israel, and were not healed, and drank of the flesh of Christ, as that which he ate of the water — the water which flowed — of God; and it is fitting, when every one, as he neither denied nor sinned, and the kingdom; but they rejoice because of their sins unto Christ, and continually. And they declare His scriptures, that He is who giveth them rest.

3 And the Holy Spirit; and they dwell with Him in the kingdom of the heavens. And the priest who teacheth not the people, his brethren, concerning Christ and His liturgy — know thou that he shall perish from before them, as one spy perished from before the people, and he shall inherit his eternal judgment with the Devil and his hosts, and shall be far removed from that eternal kingdom.

4 And behold, I have brought and shown thee, O beloved, the seasons which were ordained for us unto glory and liturgy in the book of the hosts of the demons against the Christians — the seasons which were ordained unto death, that they, in the kingdom which in truth they believed; and the Holy One giveth victory over that which is the type of Joshua the son of Nun. And let every of the people know, and their names be written for Christ. Lo, this is the book which thou hast completed.

5 Unto Thee; and know thou that this is His book, wherein is a great and glorious word, for He maketh to taste the faith of the Christians, and those who are fitting for the believers against the hosts of the demons who were humbled unto them from the kingdom, with Christ, unto eternity. Unto Him be glory, and praise, and greatness, and glory, for ever and ever, Amen. [Rubric] Fourth Discourse, concerning the priests: an interpretation of joy, and concerning the lamb; and there shall be revealed unto thee the bread and the wine, His body and His blood, of Christ. Behold, we have revealed unto thee, O beloved: may God enlighten the eyes of thy heart with the light of the Holy Spirit, the Comforter, that thou mayest understand the mystery of the Godhead in the book which is from the beginning concerning it. Every interpretation [is] many, which are from the Law, and Joshua the son of Nun. And I show thee that they all were types, and that which He showed unto Moses, and that which smote Pharaoh and his hosts with ten plagues, which I have revealed unto thee in the book which is from the beginning concerning it. And Pharaoh wished to send them away. God said unto Moses: One ordinance — this smote, by it, Pharaoh and his hosts; and He sendeth them away. And He commanded the children of Israel that they should keep, each one, forty-four houses of them, at the side of the completion, without disgrace; one man of a house shall not eat alone at the side, but they shall share with another house therein; and they shall keep concerning it the lamb
