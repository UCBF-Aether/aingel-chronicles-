Josippon

Chapter 82

1 for the poor, concerning their souls; and there abideth from him that which sufficeth them for their day, for they know that they shall live until its morrow.

2 And again he bade them that they should leave their servants; for if it were not given unto them — and he left the judgment, and he is borne among them, and sufficeth them.

3 If a man marrieth a wife in this world, and she is pure in every deed except the sin of adultery, and he becometh not an adulterer who looseth the world — nay, rather let him bear patiently, in paradise.

4 And there shall be seen from him, according to the measure of his power, as our Lord Jesus Christ ordained.

5 For years he appointed unto the believers: they seek by this fasting of the flesh which those took upon themselves in that long fast; and it shall be reckoned unto them.

6 As they fasted the whole day, so shall it be reckoned unto them; as he fasted one hour and took his reward, so took they who fasted the whole day.

7 As it is said in the holy Gospel: The kingdom of heaven is like unto a man, the master of a vineyard, who went out early in the morning to hire labourers for his vineyard.

8 And they agreed with them for a wage of a denarius for the day; and again he went out.

9 These are the straits and hardships in this world: that when they have laboured they may receive paradise, the gladness, by his judgment; for he reckoneth this their weariness and their suffering unto them — by their will it shall become rest.

10 How long Adam and Noah, Abraham and Moses laboured in Sheol — many [years].

11 and he found others, and said unto them: Why stand ye idle all the day? And they said unto him: We have found no man who giveth us our hire.

12 And do that which I command: go ye into my vineyard, and work, and go into my vineyard. And again, at the time of evening, he called unto his steward, and commanded him to call them that had laboured, and to give them their hire.

13 And they came each, from the last unto the first; and when he gave unto those of the morning a denarius each, they supposed that he would give them more than those; and he gave them each a denarius.

14 Together with those who fasted in Sheol many years — for he wrought and fasting, because they heard and obeyed — paradise, the gladness, shall appear unto them, which he prepared for Adam and for his seed.

15 For Adam was created for this cause, the first; and he it is who laboured from the morning in the length of his fast which is in Sheol, for the length of the days that he dwelt in Sheol.

16 And they murmured against him, and said: These last have laboured one hour; thou hast made them equal unto us who have borne the burden of the day, and.

17 the saints; and they who suffered in this world in the straitness of his commandments received as those received who were in that gladness without labour in Sheol, and fasted not.

18 And therein, for their fasting of one hour, they received their reward first; and they seek those, as they received their reward in paradise, the gladness.

19 Blessed are your eyes, for they see the things that ye see, and they saw not; and they hear the things that ye hear, and they heard not; for the faithful shall receive the reward in the kingdom of heaven.

20 And those who found not, and he that liveth in this world — and concerning this God saith unto them that believed on him in the holy Gospel: Blessed are the eyes which see the things that ye see; for many prophets.

21 For he gave them the reward first, to choose it before them; and he it is who gave them this in this world before those; and again he giveth them from before him — — behold this great bounty which he gave unto the believers for the fasting of the flesh.
