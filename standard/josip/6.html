Josippon

Chapter 6

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 the Holy Trinity Isaiah the prophet: he revealed the Holy Trinity, which was from of old, through Isaiah the prophet.

2 and he commanded them to baptize them, and to sanctify them and to praise him.

3 And the prophecy of Isaiah the prophet was fulfilled concerning the manifestation of the Holy Trinity, and the earth is full of his praise.

4 the Gospel the Son — and the Son in many places with the Father and the Holy Spirit.

5 and they saw him in his flesh; and the Holy Spirit descended upon him in the likeness of a dove; and the Father from heaven said: This is my Son, whom I love.

6 This is my Son, whom I love, the delight of my soul on Mount Tabor.

7 Isaac the word of the book of the LORD and in the prophets.

8 the holy [writings] and the books of Christ and the Gospels.

9 the Father from heaven, while he says: I have glorified it, and again I will glorify it — the Son the Holy Spirit.

10 in the Gospels and the apostles a book behold.

11 from the book of the LORD behold.

12 the Holy Spirit the word and the Spirit God the Paraclete.

13 The Father the Son and the Spirit: the Father is the Living, the Speaking; and so the Son is the Living, the Speaking, and so the Holy Spirit is the Living, the Speaking in each of them.

14 their union in will and power and work the Holy Trinity: and in him is their joining together and their union, in will and in power and in work — the Holy Trinity.
