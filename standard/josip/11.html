Josippon

Chapter 11

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 In weakness, that He might hide that from Satan, until Satan should know that He is a weak man. And Satan was astonished at the abundance of his wonder, and said in his soul: This [man] — since He hearkeneth not unto me, and will not as Adam hearkened, that He should be — I will slay Him, and bring Him down into Sheol; for it is not fitting for us that we should reign over Him, because He is of the seed of Adam. For Satan thought that Christ was born by the desire of Adam.

2 And this [is the matter]: for our Lord hid from him His incarnation from Mary the Virgin, without the seed of man; He hid that from him, when He made [her] His mother,.

3 And when Satan saw the women, he likened [them] from.

4 And therefore he said in his spirit:

5 When he was astonished at the abundance of the wonders of our Lord, and saw that He hearkened not unto me, and did no sin, he said: This is of the seed of Adam; and when I slay Him, I will bring Him down into Sheol, unto Adam His father, and I will rest from Him — as all the prophets and the righteous who were sent before Him.

6 For Satan supposed that He was a naked man, like all the seed of Adam; and they set upon Him — the priests of the Jews, and their priests — and they conspired against Him, and smote Him, and reviled Him, and cast spittle into His face, and crucified Him upon the tree, and pierced His side. And [He endured] all this enmity by [His] will, because of the judgment that is fitting upon Adam and his seed, because of their disobedience, as the devil [did], unto the ages.

7 He shall see, for them, suffering, and scourging, and judgment, and crucifixion upon the tree — His side — and death upon it, as Adam died from the life of the LORD, and became naked the second time when he ate of the tree; and He was crucified upon the tree — His side — instead of the nakedness of Adam. And when it came to pass the devil His side; the devil supposed that He was a weak man; he came unto Him with a face of fear, and waxeth strong, that he may be feared, until he groweth strong and His body dieth, and He goeth down into Sheol, as his host used to do unto Adam in the former time.

8 And then Christ died by [His] will, and gave up the spirit without; and the devil willed to bring down His soul into Sheol; and Christ the Lord seized [him] by the power of the divinity, and him. He came that His body might be buried: Christ was buried instead of the burial and the prison-house of Adam and all his seed, from the bond of him [the devil], by justice, without violence, for the ransom of the slaying, as the devil took them without violence.

9 And Adam and all his seed were not [raised], as was promised to the host; but [they remained] with the devil; and therefore it cannot be that He should take their bodies from the dead, and raise their souls unto the high order, before there be fulfilled the number of those who fell from it.

10 And He took their spirits into the garden, where Adam and Eve were at the first, and left them therein, until there should be according to the number of the host who fell with the devil.

11 And He rose in His body, on the third day from the crucifixion — that day — He shall seek their bodies when their number is fulfilled; and He appeared to His disciples, who believed in Him, the Jews; and He made known to them from the garden the mystery which was from before the crucifixion, hidden, that the devil might not know [it]; and He made known to them that He is the fulfillment of the number of the former Adam.

12 And He commanded them that they should go into all nations, and preach His name in the garden of the heavenly way, which His name, and they shall in it. And whoso loveth this, and taketh up the transitory works of the ages, they shall bring him in, and the unclean spirit shall depart from him; [then is he] in it a Christian; and the water of the Christian baptism is fitting upon him; and they shall name him for the Father, and for the name of the Son Jesus — Jesus Christ — that the unclean spirit may be driven out from him, which the devil set upon him on the day of his fleshly birth.

13 And again He exalteth [him] upon the water, and they shall ask of the LORD that the holy kingdom may be opened upon him, which came down upon the only Son, upon the water of Jordan.

14 And when the holy kingdom descendeth upon the water, they baptize him; and then [at that] time, as the Holy Trinity, the Holy Spirit dwelleth upon him, and they feed him His body, and [he becometh] for Christ a good pledge, which liveth in it in the heavenly kingdom.

15 In the life of Christ; and He reigneth unto the ages.

16 And they know him when they baptize him, that the host of the devil envy him, because of the heavenly way, which he willeth to ascend unto; and they prepare for him fleshly works, that there may be worked in them, from the time of his glorifying, the glory of the LORD and His holiness; and they cast him down, as they fell.

17 For they, when they forsook the glorifyings and the sanctifyings, fell from heaven.

18 And when they weary the believer, and do unto him with good works, he passeth from the time of glory, and they reach death in that time, and fell from the kingdom of the ages; and their came to be in the judgment of the ages.

19 And if he is patient, and riseth up because of that fall, one time — who passeth from the time of glory and sanctification in his time which was prepared, and reacheth death, while he forsaketh not the time of glory and sanctification — the host of the devil shall not be upon him, neither shall they be able to draw near unto his spirit; but the holy angel of the LORD, whom Christ appointed, keepeth [him] from the day that he was baptized at his birth: he taketh the spirit with glory and, which liveth in it, into the garden; and there cometh to be a [place] of spirits, until Christ shall raise them from Sheol; and all who were baptized
