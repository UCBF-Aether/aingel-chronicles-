Josippon

Chapter 10

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [in Him] they live, bodies without food; and when the LORD created them, they glorified Him, all of them, and sanctified Him.

2 And when Satanael saw the host, he became proud in himself, and thought that he was the LORD; he glorified not, nor sanctified [Him]; and he all the host that were in the order, and kept them from glorifying, and of the LORD with him unto the earth, all of them; and he was called Satan. And the LORD created our father Adam a spirit, like the angels, in an animal body, that he might dwell in the order from which Satan and his host fell.

3 And again He made for him a garden of delight in the east, [in] a land under heaven, and left him in it, until He should do with him according to the number of the host who fell with the devil, and should, and dwell in that order which had become void of glorifying and sanctifying.

4 And then He took from the side of Adam a bone, and created from that [bone of] Adam spirit and flesh, as there is no tree; and the females, in his likeness for the angels.

5 And it came to pass: Adam took from Eve — He made from him another; and there was another and another; and He worketh with them, until there should be in the city and in the the host; but there came forth among them the devil.

6 And when the devil saw, and knew that Adam was within, concerning his orders — his creation

7 He spake against them, and said to him: I was the head; I became proud, and I was likened unto the LORD, and He cast me down.

8 And when he entered into the devil, he said in himself: I became proud, and I made myself like unto the LORD, and He cast me down.

9 And when he entered these, and they were likened in it,, as He cast them down — as He cast down [me].

10 I desired them against them: they did in it my work by their own will, without any compelling them; and sin is fitting upon them, and they shall fall, as He cast me down.

11 And then he took occasion against them unto sin, as [was done] unto him, by their will, without violence; and he — — hid himself from them in the serpent, and said to them by the mouth of the serpent: hath the LORD forbidden you, that ye eat not of this tree.

12 And Eve said, As; and the devil said to her: Nay — ye shall die when ye eat of it? No: He knoweth that ye, when ye eat, shall become like unto the likeness of gods. And then, from the tree, they made the LORD a liar, and desired to become gods, and ate of it, that they might become in the likeness of the LORD, as the devil became proud and made himself like unto the LORD — He doeth glory and sanctification.

13 And then the LORD judged them, as He judged the devil, and stripped them of the robe of the angels, and their shame was revealed, as the beasts, and He cast them down.

14 And they came together, and brought forth children, and in the garden, as the beasts.

15 And their children grew many; the devil feared lest they should return unto the LORD, and He should teach them, and them.

16 For the devil — he was the cause of their error; and he set, with the day, from among them an unclean spirit from his host, that they might [work against] them in fleshly works, that they might not look unto the LORD, and might not return unto Him.

17 And so it came to pass that they did unto all that are born of the seed of Adam, as he set upon Adam an unclean spirit from his host, with the men, while he doeth fleshly, beastly works, from the day of his birth until the day that the LORD willeth; and when he dieth, that spirit appeareth to him — the unclean spi—

18 —rit, in a face of fear; and he waxeth strong, and his body dieth, and he seizeth the spirit, and goeth down into Sheol beneath the earth. And all the sons of Adam came to be thus: sin and sinfulness — for they sinned as the devil [sinned], and judgment is fitting upon them likewise.

19 Unto the ages; and it cannot be that judgment depart from them, and leave the devil in the judgment, when He dealt with him without justice; and justice from their condemnation, and in the garden — the cause.

20 And He withheld the face of the of unity, that He might save them; for the devil took them not from the LORD by violence, but by a cause; and it cannot be that the LORD should take [them] from him by violence, and do wrong.

21 From the place of the wisdom of the LORD, [He purposed] to fulfill justice and mercy, and to do all that is fitting for Him upon them, from the judgment — from the Son of; for they sinned as the devil [sinned], and judgment is fitting upon them likewise, unto the ages.

22 And if He judged them all unto the ages, the judgment of the Son for them in one hour would not be equal [thereto]; and if they were all condemned for their sins, it would not be equal to the suffering of the death of the Son for them; for the judgment of the Son of one hour is greater, and exceedeth all of them, for all of them were not equal unto Him.

23 And this was by the counsel of the Son, and by [His] will, as I have shown thee in the book that is joined; for the will of the Father and of the Son is one, and their counsel and their work is one. And the likeness of this is as the Son who said to His Father: O my Father, Thy judgment which is fitting — when upon Adam and his seed is judgment unto the ages, for they sinned as the devil and his host —

24 And again: I [have counselled] that I should my soul for those who through me from among them; and He made my counsel from their seed: Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Behold, O my Father, all that is fitting for Thy judgment upon them — from the hidden things, and judgment, and scourging, and again, and the death of Sheol, and — lay it upon me, and I will pay Thee it all for them in one day; know that their judgment, for all of them unto the ages, is not equal to my judgment for them in one hour.

25 As this likeness was the rising and the counsel of the Father and the Son concerning the condemnation of Adam and his seed — for their rising is one, and their counsel is one — therefore the Son became flesh; for He is Light, as the LORD His Father; He was begotten of His Father, as the begetting of the radiance of the sun from, which abideth in it always, inseparably — as the joining of the radiance in the sun: light without.

26 And when He counselled that He should concerning Adam and his seed the judgment that is fitting upon them, by counsel; and as the devil hid his head from them in the serpent, and took occasion against them unto sin, without violence — so also the Son of the LORD hid the divinity from the devil, and took the flesh of man, and did all that man doeth, unto the suffering of the devil, that He is man in truth, alone.

27 And at times He doeth a work of power, that the divinity may be known in it — of the Man who, without then doing a weak [work], hid the divinity from the devil; and this [Man] is found in unity, and sin [was] before thee.

28 At a time when He speaketh a word, the divinity is known in it, that He is the God of unity; and at another time He hasteneth, and speaketh a word in weakness, that He may hide the divinity from Satan.
