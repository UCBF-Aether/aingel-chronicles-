Josippon

Chapter 2

1 And his Son [is] light from light; and he is thy likeness, who in truth [is] as his Father.

2 And

3 an assembly: Christ was born of God.

4 That: there is a Father and a Son, One, without another Father, and without another Son; and the Son is not without the Father.

5 He said to him:; and we ask.

6 he is created.

7 the LORD a Son from the Father: God did not create, but [begot] a Son.

8 And he from before the ages, without joining.

9 One: one life, one power, and one, and one work of the creators.

10 That the LORD asks — behold.

11 Jesus Christ: he baptized them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.

12 And I spoke: the Holy Spirit; we ask.

13 One Spirit for the Father and the Son: the Holy Spirit is in the Father and in the Son; one Spirit, and he is the Spirit of the Father, and he is the Spirit of the Son.

14 The interpretation of the word: that the Son our LORD, who says.
