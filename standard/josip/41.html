Josippon

Chapter 41

1 For men — if he keepeth the three commandments — but they are fat; and the giving of the Mass and the work shall be, by the Holy Spirit, firm in him; and he is the sword of God: when the hosts of Satan see him, they flee from him, and cannot draw near unto him; and they resist, and they accuse men, those who transgress one of these three commandments and forsake it; the Holy Spirit is resisted over him; as with a man who hath in his hand a sharp sword, polished: his enemies cannot draw near unto him, from fear of the sword; and they stand from afar, and they cast at him with stones and sling-stones; and the sword falleth from his hand; and they cry out unto him; and he also desireth that there be in his hand a shield, with the sword; and when they cast at him with stones, he receiveth them therein, and it falleth from his hand.

2 And thy shield is faith, by which a man can keep these three commandments; the satans shall not draw near unto it, who trouble them from her.

3 As Paul saith in his epistle to the men of Ephesus:

4 Seek the shield of faith, which is the sword of the Holy Spirit, by which the quenching of all his arrows of the evil one, which are full of fire, is possible.

5 It is shown that men cannot be joined unto the glory and the holiness and the speaking of the word of God — which is the sword of the Holy Spirit — if there be not with him the shield of faith, by which he destroyeth the arrows of the evil one.

6 And I also show thee the arrows, that by them he overcometh, if he believeth, if he keepeth the three commandments. The first arrow: when a man who believeth goeth unto the house of the Christians for the glory and for the Mass and for the speaking of the word of God, Satan aimeth at him the work of this world, and stoppeth [it] in his breath, that he is he who, if he went unto the house of the Christians and tarried for the passing, shall pass from him the likeness, thus and thus, and causeth him to lose the profit.

7 And when he heareth from Satan this counsel, and transgresseth that which is meet for him, the sword falleth from his hand, which is the Holy Spirit; and if it be that his possession is the shield of faith, it is not bent for Satan, a portion for that which he adorned for him from the work of this world, and that which he strengthened in his handful from the lost profit of the likeness — those who give him say: Christ, thy fathers, righteousness is departed from thee; he saith: I will not forsake my likeness to pass away, and I will not let my work perish, that which passed me. For he is he who saith: Seek first my kingdom and my righteousness, and all this shall be added unto you. And if he believeth thus, he overcometh the thought of Satan by the shield of faith; and he is joined unto the glory and unto the Mass and the reading, firm — the sword in his hand — and overcometh thereby his enemy; and he shall be a counsel that is greater than the counsels of the martyrs, who endured their strife with the flesh, or in their likeness. And his strife, this one, is with the hosts of Satan, spirits of fire, who have dominion over all the earth, as Paul saith; and his counsel, at that time, is greater than the counsels of the martyrs; for he stood firm and burned with the helps of the satans, and the roughness of those who burned the martyrs.

8 Behold, I have shown thee the first arrow; and this is the second arrow: If a man wisheth to be joined in the speaking on the holy day, and goeth unto the house of the Christians for the glory and for the Mass, every time, and feareth Satan, and speaketh: Let us not find in the way, one draweth near unto thee thus and thus, lest he delay thee, or crush thee, or kill thee; and if he hearkened from him, and despiseth that which is meet for him, he forsaketh the Holy Spirit, who is the sword, and Satan his enemy resisteth over him, and destroyeth him. If there was with him the shield of faith, he giveth him and saith unto him: The lying Satan cannot at all delay me, and crush me, and torment me, and kill me, without the will of Christ, my strength; and if He willed it, He would do it by me.

9 And when thou didst hide thyself in a cave of the earth, he cometh not unto thee, and my doth not rise to work by thee; he cannot at all work evil by thee. And when it was thus, I was lifted up above the beasts; for the hairs of my head are numbered.

10 And he is in this [way]; and he is he who saith thus: For two sparrows are sold for two farthings; and one of them falleth not, saith He, to the earth, without the will of your Father who is in heaven.

11 And unto you also, all the hairs of your head are numbered; and he is, by this shield of faith, he who overcometh his enemy, and is resisted over him; and the sword abideth in his hand, which is the Holy Spirit; and there shall be a reward in this.

12 The second is greater than the first.

13 For he is he who gave over his head unto Christ, exceedingly, more than the martyrs; and he endured the tree of His cross, and followed Him, and believed; for they who kill him and destroy him are those who are without Him; and behold, I have shown thee the second arrow. The arrow of the veil: and when it was for a man — from a work that is done by it, from the keeping of these three commandments, and the fear which they fear for him by it, for Satan, until they are cut off from the veil.

14 And Satan cometh upon him secretly, and presseth upon him, until the sword falleth from his hand, and he resisteth over him; and the sin of this boaster, from sinners, is a sin; for he is he who was exalted, and was lifted up from the speaking and the glory and the Mass, without a cause.
