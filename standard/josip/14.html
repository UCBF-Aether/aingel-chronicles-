Josippon

Chapter 14

1 Concerning their disobedience unto Him, in that which pleaseth not the LORD their Creator; and He them unto the earth; for because of their fall He created [them]; for He knoweth that which shall be, before it cometh to be.

2 And there was a hearing concerning the devil, that he was the chief of the angels, and fell by his girding.

3 Against his Creator.

4 Elijah the prophet, when he said thus: The star of the morning fell, which shone in its time at dawn, which was from all the stars, as he said: I will set my throne above, and I will become in the likeness of the Most High.

5 Of heaven, the star of the morning; for he was a luminous angel, and he was created when the LORD created the light at the dawn of the first day.

6 And therefore he said that he shone in his time at dawn, for he was, and he was the chief of the angels unto them.

7 The prophet revealed in me his name, and the reason of his; and again he maketh known: it was in the name of the LORD; He createth instead of us, as.

8 And when he fell, and his host, and was stripped of the light, and became darkened in the time of his creation.

9 For in the time of his creation he was withheld from glorifying and sanctifying; and in its time he fell, on the first day, the day of, the head of days.

10 In its time was made the high heaven and its orders, and for its angels, and the four natures which are beneath.

11 And when he fell, he said in his thought: I know that the LORD will not leave the order which fell from it empty, and it from all their orders; and there is [a purpose], that He may fill [it] with those who glorify Him and sanctify Him: either He will have mercy on us and turn us back into it, or He will create in it others, strangers,, to return us into it, while it abideth empty; and, when He createth instead of us a host instead of a host, that He may His: in this [way], when he fell on the day of.

12 And when the day of came to be, there was created the dwelling of the angels, all the glory of the LORD; and all those men hear them, and men; and there is none that knoweth until: your knowledge is from Him, O host.

13 While the teachers say to them the teaching.

14 And then He created the angels in the holy kingdom.

15 And the angels of men in the holy kingdom, as the prophets called them witnesses; and the devil and his host were not made, but fell — this is the word, for glory; and is not known, as he said, that they are host upon host, and with them the devil and his host; but they: this is the word — the sowing and the names of the Holy Trinity.

16 For the Satans are the host, they, and with them the devil and his host, sinners in Jerusalem: the women of Israel, who was the first keeper of the throne, the LORD, Egypt. And when Satan beheld the, who draw [men] away, he thought that a rational spirit, like the angels; and he thought that God created His spirits, that He might find them in the order, and hid them from him in these animal bodies; and he began to fashion them and to test them, that he might know from the place in which there is a rational spirit in them.

17 And then the LORD did with him according to his thought, — that should return upon his head; for he thought that the LORD made His spirits as the likeness, and the likeness of the host, and hid them from them.

18 And He created Adam on the day of Friday; in it He created who was the devil; and He createth Adam, and testeth him; He created for Adam a rational [spirit] in the likeness of the angels, and hid [it] in flesh — as the flesh of a beast, flesh and blood — and He created, that He might set him in the high order from which the devil fell; and He made for him a garden in the east, a land under heaven, its beginning on the earth and its head in heaven, for heaven; and the angels in the east; and He left him in it, until He should create with him according to the number of the host who fell with Satan, and they should ascend into the order.

19 And then He took from the rib of Adam and filled its place with flesh; He made from that bone Eve for Adam, and filled its place with flesh; and Adam shall be in life. He made from that bone Eve for Adam; for the LORD is able to make from a bone, to create another, and from it another and another, for Adam; He did thus, until there should be according to their number of the host of the devil.
