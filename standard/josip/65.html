4 And when they cried out, and he saw him: their appearing unto the unclean spirits — when he filleth the air, he feareth them, and he them; and the breath is altered from his; and he wisheth not to return unto him by himself; he wandereth, and other spirits are sent with him, which are worse than he; and he saith unto him, and dwelleth in that denier, and becometh, which is worse than before him, by the teacher, and toward the holy of heaven.

5 He loved from the prophets and the holy ways; it is fitting, as he is trusted; and they desire him not: the men; it is fitting: Jordan; it is he, and this heart; and the holy angels knew it; and he believeth not that the angels of the LORD are holy indeed; and they are turned back; and that angel [cometh] unto the water which is with her on the day of baptism, and descendeth unto where it befitteth her.

6 And when the Son of God is baptized, the Holy Spirit parteth not from him, neither the heavenly angels who guard him; and that unclean spirit which went out from him on the day of baptism is not pleased: its suffering is fear and.

7 When he said: unto me — for the fear of death descended upon me; the holy angels of the LORD — there is no fear in them; and he feareth him not from; when he removed the fear from the, which appeareth unto him, as our father: the Spirit — the father was sent; and he said that the holy ones fear not the way: my God shall appear unto him; his eyes — fear [is taken] from him, as did the angels who appeared unto him: Christ; and as he said unto them: Fear ye him not —.

8 And thus he said unto them: Fear not — unto the shadows; fear him not, for I know joy within me; and thus he said unto the women who came unto the tomb of our Lord Jesus Christ: Fear not, ye.

9 And the holy one who was sent said: For death is fear; and.

10 But death and fear are from the unclean spirits; but from that which [is] are the enemies, and from them the Son of God, when he came forth and was made man and died in, and rose again from death, and raised us up from them by the power of his.

12 And Paul the apostle [saith] unto the Hebrews: thus he saith — He is the power of death; and Christ saved souls by death, without fearing him — all — for him that had dominion over death, by fearing him not; and he saith thus, and released the fear from the unclean spirits; now he shewed plainly and revealed that the holy angels of the LORD have no fear in them.

13 And the prophet crieth: For death is fear; and he — and whosoever hath no fear in the of the angels — not they that die, saith the angel of the LORD; not unto them that have the fear of death in their days.

14 And whosoever it befell: thus is the devil received — for the devil, he is the power of death.

15 And our Lord by his death abolished [death], and saved those who were his — who — from his dominion and his death; and he is now a fear unto men: he waxeth evil, who saith: Death the angel; and the apostle Paul saith: There is not among men; he knew: death was over him for Christ; and he shewed them the resurrection when.

16 Christ saith: [I am] the firstfruits of thy resurrection; and again: in Christ shall he rise.

17 And again the hosts of Satan — their thoughts, when he returneth unto the kingdom of the LORD the Father: when he shall abolish every place, and every dominion, and every of the service of power; it befitteth: he shall reign; he feareth; and, but in the which destroyed their power; and they shall take the kingdom of the LORD the Father, who made them to reign — those whom he shewed plainly; he shall raise them as he saith; he knew that their shall destroy in the, which is death; and it came to pass that Paul saith: He is death; Christ; who saith that he hath no heart; for he is of the holy angels; behold, it was revealed that Satan, in his entering, and he is where he is; and he from him upon men, that they may enter unto him, that he may be slaughtered for him; for that which he saith, and death; for the Satanic ones shall rise, when they return unto the kingdom of the LORD the Father; and the principalities and powers shall be destroyed; for the principalities and powers — they are the hosts of Satan, from men.

18 And when the LORD sent them forth — those who [are] from, from the way — he shall enter in the name of the angel of death.

19 And the angel of death gathered Satan; and Satan wrought this denial by him who was entrusted over men in the city; from he wrought in the city of the Christians; and which wrought this; and they know not this, in the of tongues: he is in the houses of the Christians;, as the tongue of fearful, as which took a vision; and their unclean ones, which are fit for judgement; and when the faithful behold them, they fear them, and they, and they do good, that he may from among them; and they glorify the LORD, who redeemed them from them, in glory and honour; and and the holy — the giver of life, which thy heart speaketh: the house of the Christians — in these days, these their therein, concerning the faith.

20 And from them [is] the province therein; and he another province; and he; and thou shalt dwell from, for the province — their greatness is in their mouth, for the tongue of the Arab; and there remained of them that which is known, that which is spoken in the Church in; and they were named, and they did not.

21 And in this province from them, from the province in the daughter: he me and me, unto the ages of ages. Amen.
