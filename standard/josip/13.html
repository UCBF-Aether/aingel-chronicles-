Josippon

Chapter 13

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Therefore the LORD left the mention of the creation of the angels.

2 And He kept [it] not in the Book of Creation, lest Israel should and their worship.

3 And that they might not make for themselves a bow, which pertaineth to the Son and to His Holy Spirit.

4 And the mention of their creation was hidden, and the fall of the devil and his host, until the LORD Jesus Christ revealed [it] in the holy Gospel.

5 And the holy fathers revealed it in the holy kingdom, and made it known from and the prophets, with much witness and knowledge.

6 And it was thus: at the first the LORD made heaven and earth, as Moses wrote in the Book of the Creation of heaven, who saith that He is the First who made that high heaven, in which are gatherings in it; and then He made the order which is in it, and in it the angels.

7 And when He had made the heaven, then He made the water and; and He the four natures, from which He created you.

8 He created the earth, sunk in the water, which was not seen, and was not — its seed — for there was an abyss of water; its was covered by water, above and below, and on all its sides.

9 As David the prophet said in the psalm which: the earth, and established it, that it should not be moved for ever and ever; and it was adorned as a garment; and shaketh in the water on all its sides, as the water shaketh upon the earth.

10 And therefore Moses the prophet said: And darkness was upon the abyss; for darkness in its likeness, because of the of darkness.

11 And again He created while it shook in the water, as the wind shaketh in, as it shaketh in the water.

12 He created the natures, others; and from above He made, when He said, Let there be light; He made then, and from above, host and night; and light; and it is of the kindred of light.

13 And in its time He created the angels in the holy kingdom.

14 He created them from the light of the kingdom-dwellers, without flesh; for He created them in the holy kingdom.

15 And by the Son He made the heavens and the four natures; in the holy kingdom He created all the angels, spiritual ones.

16 As David the prophet said in the psalm of the fifty-second: the LORD: Rejoice, ye heavens; and in the kingdom of His brother all. He maketh known that the host — the LORD created them in the holy kingdom, and our heaven by His word, which is the Son, who was with Him; He created the angels, hosts upon hosts, and [gave] to the host an order in the high heaven, which is the first creation of the angels; and the fall of the devil, as the Book of Moses, and their number of the host, unto the high ones, and unto the host.

17 As our Lord Jesus Christ said in the holy Gospel concerning the three sheep: While I am [here], I remember that in its time — for men know not of the host, except the thirty hosts whose heaven — the prophets, lo, they are angels.

18 And the chiefs of the angels, and the rulers.

19 And the host of the ways, and the thrones.

20 And the dominions, and the powers.

21 And the cherubim, and the seraphim; and the prophets: He created the angels.

22 He said that they are three hosts; and men know not, except the thirty and three that fell, alone.

23 And when He created the angels at the beginning of the creation of light, in the first of the days, which is the day

24 And in its time they glorified, all of them, and sanctified [Him]; for He created them that they should sanctify Him and glorify Him; and they have no life, neither delight, except His glory.

25 And they sanctify Him, and live in the garden; for they are spiritual.

26 And they delight in it — the life of the ages, and the grace of the world — with God, who is, who.

27 As the good ones live in the garden of darkness, and delight in food that passeth away, and a life that passeth away — and again it passeth away — there came to be a high order, an order which is numbered from all the orders.

28 And that order was among the orders above: a hundred orders; for all the orders are a hundred, as thee.

29 And therefore our Lord said in the Gospel: In the house of my Father are many mansions; it maketh known its interpretation to the sheep.

30 And when all the host had glorified, and had sanctified their Creator, Satanael that high order — that he was above all of them — and he like them who were with all the host, who were with him; and it was not fitting likewise with them, the host who were with him; and it was not fitting likewise with them, the host who were with holiness.
