Josippon

Chapter 78

1 Together with the creation that came forth, the heavenly and the earthly, and they said that they should glorify and bless the LORD: let all creation glorify and bless: Bless ye the LORD.

2 the LORD

3 O all ye waters that be above the heavens, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

4 O all ye powers, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

5 O sun and moon, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

6 O all ye stars of heaven, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

7 O showers and dew, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

8 O clouds and winds, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever. O all ye spirits, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

9 O all ye fire and flame, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

10 O hail and, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

11 O nights and days, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

12 O light and darkness, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

13 O Israel, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

14 O, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

15 O, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

16 O spirits and souls of the righteous, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever.

17 O Hananiah and Azariah and Mishael, bless ye the LORD; praise him and exalt him for ever: for he hath delivered us from Sheol.

18 Bless ye the LORD, the God of gods; praise him, for he is good; for his mercy is for ever.
