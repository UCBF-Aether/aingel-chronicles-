THE FIRST GENERAL EPISTLE OF JOHN

1 John

First John is a warm, searching letter in five chapters about knowing God for real. Its tests are simple and searching: walk in the light, obey Christ's commands, and love one another. Anyone who claims to know God while hating a brother or sister, John says, is still in darkness. It also gives tender assurance to ordinary believers: your sins are forgiven, you know the Father, and eternal life is in his Son. Few books speak so plainly about love being the mark of God's children.

In this edition the text is the King James Version of 1611, received as public-domain text. It belongs in this canon because the Ethiopian Church keeps the letters of John together, and 1 John has long been read as the companion to John's Gospel — the same voice, now teaching the churches how to live what they believe. It is pastoral, not distant, and meant to steady your confidence.

Watch for the beloved phrase God is love, and notice how John ties love of God to love of people every time.
