THE SECOND BOOK OF SAMUEL

2 Samuel

2 Samuel follows David as king — his victories, his capital at Jerusalem, and the bringing up of the ark, but also his grievous sin with Bathsheba and the sorrow that follows in his own house. It is a portrait of greatness and failure in the same man, told throughout with honesty, warmth, and deep sorrow. In this edition 2 Samuel is printed from the King James Version of 1611, given here as a public-domain text received just as it has come down to us. It belongs here because God's promise to David's house in these 24 chapters becomes a thread the rest of the Bible never lets go. Watch for Nathan the prophet confronting David with a simple story that turns back on the king himself.
