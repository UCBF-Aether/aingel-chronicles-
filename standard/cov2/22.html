2 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 22

THE PSALMS

1 In answer to him who singeth the psalms in the church, - let the virgins and boys respond and sing. But if they sing the psalms in a house privately, if they be two or three, let them respond to one another, singing the psalms. Similarly the men.
