THE FIRST BOOK OF MOSES

Genesis

Genesis is where everything begins. It tells how God made the world and all that is in it, how people first turned away, and then how God began again with one family — Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Joseph — whose story carries the promise forward. In this edition Genesis is printed from the King James Version of 1611, given here as a public-domain text received just as it has come down to us. It belongs at the very front of this canon because every later book looks back to it: the creation, the covenant, and the choosing of Israel all start here, and the Ethiopian church, like churches everywhere, has always opened its Bible with these 50 chapters. Watch for Joseph in Egypt, forgiving the brothers who sold him and seeing God's hand in it all.
