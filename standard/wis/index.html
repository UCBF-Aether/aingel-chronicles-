THE WISDOM OF SOLOMON

Wisdom of Solomon

The Wisdom of Solomon is a song in praise of Wisdom herself — pictured as a bright, pure spirit who was with God from the beginning and who guides honest lives. Over 19 chapters it contrasts the way of the righteous with the way of those who live only for pleasure, looks hard at death and judgment, and retells Israel's story from Egypt onward as Wisdom's own work. Tradition links the book to Solomon, the wise king, and it speaks in his voice, though it was written in Greek long after his day. The text here is the King James Version of the Apocrypha, printed as received. The Ethiopian canon keeps Wisdom among its scriptures, as do the Catholic and Orthodox churches, even though it stands outside the Hebrew Bible. It belongs here because this edition follows the Ethiopian church's wider canon whole. Watch for the portrait of Wisdom in chapters 7 and 8 — radiant, kind, and closer to God than anything else made.
