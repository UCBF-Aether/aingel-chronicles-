4 Baruch

Chapter 8

1 And when the day came wherein God brought the people out of Babylon, and the Lord said unto Jeremiah: Arise, thou and thy people, and come unto Jordan, and say unto the people: Let him that loveth the Lord forsake the works of the people of Babylon.

2 And as for the men that took wives from among you, and the women that took [husbands] from them, let us prove them.

3 And them that hearken unto thee, bring thou into Jerusalem; but them that hearken not unto thee, suffer them not to enter with thee.

4 And Jeremiah thus read unto them all this, and brought them unto Jordan to prove them.

5 And as he told them this word which the Lord spake, they that had taken wives murmured, and would not hearken unto Jeremiah; and there were some that said: We will not forsake our wives for ever; we will take them with us into our city.

6 And they crossed over Jordan, and came into Jerusalem.

7 And Jeremiah and Baruch and Abimelech stood up, saying: Let no man that took [a wife] from Babylon enter into our city.

8 And they said unto them that had taken wives for themselves: Arise, let us go into Babylon. And they departed and went.

9 And when the men of Babylon saw them, they went forth, that they should not receive them, and suffered them not to enter into Babylon, saying: Ye are our enemies from of old; and secretly ye departed from us. And for this cause ye shall not enter into our city; for we have sworn by the name of our god that ye and your children shall not, for secretly ye departed from us.

10 And when they heard this, they returned into Jerusalem, and built for them a town in the districts of Jerusalem; and they called that city Samaria.

11 And Jeremiah sent unto them, saying: Repent; and behold, the angel of righteousness shall come, and shall bring you into your place.
