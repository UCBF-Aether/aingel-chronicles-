4 Baruch

Chapter 9

1 And they abode rejoicing and offering sacrifice seven days for the people.

2 And when the tenth day was come, after this was done, Jeremiah offered sacrifice alone.

3 And Jeremiah prayed, saying: Holy, holy, holy art thou, the sweet savour unto men, and the light of truth, which enlightenest me until I come before thee.

4 I beseech thee for thy people, and I ask thee for the sake of the voice of the seraphim, and for the sake of the sweet savour of the cherubim.

5 I ask thee, yea, Michael, the angel of righteousness, who I beseech thee, O Lord of all, and Lord of all, who and didst create all, who and, who didst finish all, and all creation before they were made in secret.

6 And when he had prayed this, and finished his prayer, Jeremiah stood up in the temple, and with him Baruch and Abimelech; and Jeremiah became as one whose soul had gone forth from him.

7 And then fell Baruch and Abimelech, and cried with a loud voice, saying: Woe unto us, our father Jeremiah, the priest of God, is departed from us.

8 And when the people heard this, they ran unto him, and found Jeremiah fallen and dead; and they wept, and rent their garments, and cast ashes upon their heads, and wept with bitter weeping.

9 And after they had prepared that wherein they should bury him, there came a voice saying: Bury him not; he is alive, and his soul shall enter into his body again.

10 And when they heard this voice, they buried him not; but abode watching him three days, until his soul should enter into his body.

11 And there was a voice in the midst of them all, saying: Praise ye with one voice, praise God; and praise ye all the Messiah, the Son of God, who you and you, Jesus, the Son of God, the light of all the world, and the that faileth not, and the life of faith.

12 And it shall be after three hundred and three weeks of time for his coming into the land, the tree of life that was in the garden, and was not planted, shall unto every tree that bringeth forth fruit.

13 that they may come unto him, and them, that they may bring forth fruit, and; and their fruit shall abide with the angels.

14 And for the planting of his tree, that they may and, unto the air, that their roots may not wither, as a plant whose root hath not taken hold of the earth.

15 And the red shall ascend as wool; and sweet water shall become bitter, and bitter shall become sweet, with great gladness.

16 And the rejoicings of God for the that they may bring forth fruit by the word of his mouth, his Son.

17 And he in the night shall enter into the world, and shall choose unto him twelve apostles, that they may see that I have seen which was sent from his Father, who cometh into the world, and upon the mount of Olives he shall and the soul.

18 And thus spake Jeremiah concerning the Son of God, that he should come into the world.

19 And when the people heard this, they marvelled concerning this, and said: This is the word of Isaiah the son of Amoz, who saith: I have seen God, the Son of God.

20 Now arise, let us do unto him as we did unto Isaiah. And they said:; with a stone let us stone him.

21 And Baruch and Abimelech cried unto them, saying: With this death ye shall not kill him.

22 And Baruch and Abimelech mourned for Jeremiah, and they suffered not the hidden things which he saw.

23 And Jeremiah said unto them: weep not; for they cannot kill me, until I make known unto you all that I have seen.

24 And now bring ye unto me one stone, and bring ye unto him one stone; and he set it up and said: O light of eternity, this stone, that the likeness of man may be.

25 And then the stone became in the likeness of Jeremiah.

26 And they took and stoned the stone, supposing it was Jeremiah.

27 And Jeremiah made known unto Baruch and Abimelech all the hidden things which he saw; and after he had finished telling them, he went and stood in the midst of the people that he might finish.

28 And then that stone cried unto them, saying: O ye lost children of Israel, wherefore do ye stone me, supposing me to be Jeremiah? And Jeremiah, behold, standeth in the midst of you.

29 And when they saw, they ran with many stones, and upon him, and buried him; and they took that stone, and placed it in his tomb, and it as a door, and wrote therein, saying: Behold, this is the helper of Jeremiah.
