4 Baruch

Chapter 6

1 And Abimelech arose, and went forth out of the city, and prayed unto the Lord; and behold, an angel came and led him unto Baruch, and he found him abiding in the tomb.

2 And when they knew each other, and had wept between themselves, and kissed one another, he saw the figs in his basket; and he lifted up his eyes unto heaven, and prayed, saying:

3 Great is God, who giveth their reward unto his righteous. Rejoice, my soul, and be glad, saying unto the tabernacle of flesh, O holy house; and thy shall be turned into light; and after this the Faithful One cometh, and shall bring thee into thy tabernacle. Abide in thine own virgin faith, and believe, and thou shalt live.

4 Behold these figs: lo, sixty and six years since they were gathered, and they are not withered nor rotten, but their milk drippeth until this day. So shall it be done unto thee, my flesh, for thou hast kept thy commandment from the angel of righteousness; who kept the basket of figs, the same shall again keep thee by his power.

5 And when Baruch had thus spoken, he turned unto Abimelech and said: Arise, let us pray again, that the Lord may shew us the words which we shall write unto Jeremiah in Babylon, concerning the shelter wherewith he sheltered me.

6 And Baruch prayed, and said: My strength, O God, the Lord, and the light that proceedeth out of his mouth: I beseech thee for thy chosen. Great is thy name, and there is none that can know it. Hearken unto the prayer of thy servant, that may be manifest in my heart, thy will to do; and prosper thy priest Jeremiah in Babylon.

7 And while he was praying this, an angel came and said unto him: Baruch, minister of light, be not anxious how thou shalt send unto Jeremiah; for at the hour of light an eagle shall come unto thee, and do thou send him concerning Jeremiah; and write in a book, and say unto them thus:

8 Unto the children of Israel: as for the stranger that is among you, let him be set apart until fifteen days; and after this I will bring you into the city, saith the Lord.

9 He that is not set apart in fifteen [days] from Babylon Jeremiah into the city; and the men of Babylon, saith the Lord.

10 And when the angel had said this, he departed from Baruch. And Baruch sent unto the market, and brought papyrus and ink, and wrote, saying:

11 By Baruch the servant of God. He writeth a book unto Jeremiah in the captivity of Babylon.

12 Rejoice and be glad; for God hath not forsaken us in affliction and desolation; wherefore the Lord remembered us for our tears, and remembered the covenant which he made of old with our fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

13 And he sent his angel unto me, and told me these words which I send unto thee. These are the words which the Lord, the God of Israel, spake, who brought us out of the land of Egypt: Because ye kept not all his righteousnesses, but lifted up your glory and were haughty before him, he delivered you into the furnace of Babylon, because ye hearkened not unto my word, saith the Lord God, by the mouth of Jeremiah his prophet.
