1 Meqabyan

Chapter 4

1 And he,, when he saw that they were dead, commanded that their bodies be burned with fire till they became as dust, and be scattered to the wind; but he was not able to burn, and he brought them forth from.

2 And again they kindled a fire upon them from noon till dawn, but it consumed them not. And again he said: " cast their bodies into the sea."

3 And they did as the king commanded them, and cast them into the sea; but the sea swallowed them not, and great stones, and they sank not into the sea, but floated; for the Spirit of God was upon them, and bore them.

4 While eagles and birds shaded them with their wings from the heat of the sun, and them —

5 And they did so, but not; and the beasts and the birds their bodies — and their bodies remained.

6 And when they saw the shining of their bodies — like the of the sun, and like the tabernacle of light — the angels kept their bodies.

7 And; and then their bodies, for the five martyrs.

8 And they appeared unto him by night — unto that wicked king —

9 And when he awoke, he was afraid, and sought to flee; for; and his knees trembled, and he feared, for it seemed to him that they would kill him.

10 And concerning this he said: "What do ye desire, and what shall I do for you?"

11 And they answered and said unto him: "We are not those whom thou slewest, and burnedst, and commandedst to be cast into the sea; but, for God guarded our bodies, for we trusted in Him; for he who trusteth in Him shall not be moved, and we who trusted in Him."

12 And he said unto them: "What gift shall I give you which I did unto you, evil — for I knew not that such punishment would befall me."

13 "And now seek for me; I will give you a gift, that my soul not in death, and bring me not down alive into Sheol."

14 "For; for the law of your fathers is mercy."

15 And the blessed martyrs answered and said unto him: "But as for us, we will not ransom thee; [but?] there is God, our God, who will redeem thee — for."

16 "But we appear unto thee, that we are alive — because of the perversity of thy heart; but as for us, though thou thinkest that thou slewest us, unto us life."

17 "But thou and thy gods shall go down into the burning judgment of fire, whence there is no escape, for ever."

18 "Woe unto you — thou and thy gods, whom ye worship and to whom ye bow down. Forsake; bow down to God who created you."

19 And the king took up [his word] and said unto them: "What do ye command me, that I may do all that ye desire?"

20 And they said unto him: "Not for our sake, but for thy sake — that thou go not down into the burning judgment of fire."

21 "For these your gods — gold and silver, wood and stone, the work of men's hands, which have neither soul, nor heart, nor mind."

22 "For they do but kill, and give not life; they smell not, and perceive not; but they lead you astray by the spirit of demons, which desire not that any man be saved."

23 "And especially those like you — O blind — who make it seem to you that they are the ones who created you; for it was ye yourselves who made them!"

24 "For the spirits of demons and of satans gather, to you into the sea of fire."

25 "But forsake thou this error, and worship God, our God; and this shall be our death, a gift, that we may profit our souls."

26 But he and feared; for —

27 And he said: "Now I know that truly the dead shall rise — they who have become ashes; and I."

28 And then they vanished from the eyes of that king; and from that day he forsook; and he — Sirusaydan the mighty and.

29 And he had delighted in the error of his heart and in his gods — for they led him astray many times; and not he alone erred, but as he did, so he led astray many, until they forsook their Maker.

30 And they sacrificed their children and their offspring to demons, and did the desire of their heart, which their father Satan taught them, that he might work which God not.

31 And, and they defiled, and all; and they defiled their souls, for Satan hardened their hearts.

32 But Sirusaydan the mighty and, who knew not his Maker, prided himself in his gods.

33 "How should God give the kingdom to those who know Him not?" And He saith:, if they repent and come unto Him.

34 "But if they repent and come unto Him, He will love them; but if they refuse, He will punish them with the trembling of Gehenna for ever."

35 Let a king — as his kingdom is great — fear God; and.

36 And the — as their honour is great — let them their Maker.

37 For He created all creation, and He is the Lord of heaven and earth, who; for there is no other God but Him, in heaven above and on earth beneath.
