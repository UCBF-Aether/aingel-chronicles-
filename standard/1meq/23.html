1 Meqabyan

Chapter 23

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And they walk not in the way of Cain, who slew his brother with, though it pleased him — who loved his brother.

2 And he himself slew his brother through jealousy; and likewise they that do iniquity, and anger and hatred against their neighbour.

3 And he went in the way of Cain, and not in the way of Abel; but he rejoiced — as of, as blood pure — which was offered up to God with a pure heart.

4 And woe to them that walk in the way of Abel — they that love God; for they were as Abel. But unto all that walk in truth God showeth love, as unto the righteous Abel, and.

5 But the wicked shall God rebuke, and their judgment, in their books of shame, with their coverings; and he shall read it in the day of judgment, before the angels, and men, and all the creation.

6 Then shall the arrogant and the unrighteous be ashamed — they that do not the will of God.

7 And there shall be given unto them a voice, and a thunder which saith: Their judgment in the day of judgment — fire is kindled, whence there is no escape, unto eternity.
