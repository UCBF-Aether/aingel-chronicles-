1 Meqabyan

Chapter 2

1 And there was a man of the tribe of Benjamin, whose name was Meqabyos.

2 And he had —

3 and he found them, and said to them: "Bow not down, and sacrifice not, to the gods of Sirusaydan the king, as he hath commanded."

4 "But if ye refuse, we will seize you, and lead you away unto the king, and destroy all your goods, as the king hath commanded."

5 And they took up [their voice?] and said unto him: "These are good and upright; but as for us, there is [One] to whom we bow down — the God of our fathers, the Maker of heaven and earth, and of the sea and all that is therein, who made the sun and the moon and the stars, and the clouds; He is the God in whom we trust, whom we worship, and in whom we believe."

6 But as for these men — there went up to the king against him four commanders, and their carriers who bore the goods of their fields.

7 And when he desired to seize these holy ones, they escaped from their hands; and there was none who touched them. And they went, carrying the goods of their fields; for they were upright, and strong, and exceeding mighty.

8 And there was among them one who slew a bear by throttling, and strangled it, like.

9 And there was among them one who slew a lion with a single cast of a stone, or with a stroke of wood, at once.

10 And there was among them in battle one who slew a hundred at once with a single sword; and thus their name became renowned throughout all the land of Moab, and among the Chaldeans.

11 And they were strong in might; and in them were comeliness, and grace, and speech.

12 And a marvel was the comeliness of; but above all, that which gave strength to the of their heart was that they toward God, and feared not death.

13 And when they terrified them with the, there was none who was able to seize them; but they, the strong ones, escaped into a mountain.

14 But the entered into the city, and the of the city, and threatened them, saying: "If ye bring not unto these the Meqabyan, we will burn your city with fire, and send [word?] unto the king, and destroy your city."

15 And at that time there went out the people of the city — the poor, and the, and the Hebrew women, and; and they all cried out together, and lifted up their eyes unto the mountain, and cried unto them, saying: "Destroy us not — neither us nor our city."

16 And when together, they sought mercy from God.

17 And they turned their faces toward the east, and prayed unto God, together their hands, and said: "We cry unto Thee, O Lord, against these transgressors of Thy law and of Thy commandment."

18 "But as for us, we will not consent to hear the word of that proud one who believeth not in Thy law — for [these are but?] stone and silver, and wood and stone, the work of men's hands."

19 "And he boweth his head, as though these had made him — while Thou, Thou art their Maker, who killest and makest alive; but he is a pourer-out of the blood of men, and a devourer of the flesh of men."

20 "But as for us, we will not consent to look upon the face of that proud one, nor to hearken unto his word."

21 "But if Thou commandest us, unto him we will go, and our souls unto death for the sake of; yet we will not hearken to the word of that proud one, when he saith: 'Eat unto my gods!'"

22 "But as for us, O Lord, with all our heart we trust in Thee — the God of our fathers, who do Thy will and walk in Thy law — the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who triest the heart and the reins."

23 "And Thou to the righteous and to the sinner, and searchest that which is in the heart of man; and nothing is hidden from before Thee."

24 "But as for us, we have no God but Thee."

25 "But grant us might and strength, and in this work which we unto Thee, that we may give our souls unto death for Thy holy name's sake."

26 "And as Thou unto Israel in the land of Egypt — Thou heardest the prayer of Jacob — so now we ask Thee, O holy God."

27 And when Thou didst appear unto them —

28 And; and they fled from the sun from the east.
