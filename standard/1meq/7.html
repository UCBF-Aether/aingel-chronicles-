1 Meqabyan

Chapter 7

1 And if thou be king, and if thou be a ruler — what art thou? Thou art not God, who created thee.

2 And He brought thee forth from whence thou wast not, that thou shouldest do His will, and walk in His commandment, and fear His judgment; as thou thy servants, and the dominion that is over them — even so there is unto thee One who thee, and dominion over thee.

3 As thou withholdest the measure of mercy from them that do evil, even so there is unto thee God, who shall withhold thee and bring thee down into the judgment whence there is no escape, for ever.

4 And as thou punishest him that trespasseth against thee, and grantest him no pardon — and thou, why wilt thou not seek pardon of God?

5 And as thou desirest that they should fear thee — why wilt thou not fear God thy God? For He it is who created thee, and made thee king over all His work, that thou shouldest shepherd His people and feed them in righteousness.

6 And respect not the person of the great and of the small; but judge in righteousness and in equity, as God appointed thee. Of whom art thou afraid — beside whom thou keepest neither His way nor His commandment?

7 And turn thou not aside to the right hand nor to the left — as Moses commanded the children of Israel, and said unto them: "Behold, I have set before thee fire and water; stretch forth thy hand unto that which thou wilt." (cf. Deut 30:15; Sir 15:16-17)

8 And hear the word which I have appointed unto thee, that thou mightest hear His word, and do His commandment. And lest thou say: "There is no crossing — who shall bring it, that I may behold and hear His word, and do the commandment of God?"

9 "And if thou sayest again: 'Who shall go up for us into heaven, and bring down unto us this word — O God — that we may hear it and do it?' — And He saith: 'Behold, the word is nigh unto thee, in thy mouth; and behold, in thy hand.'" (Deut 30:12-14)

10 And if thou hast not hearkened unto the book, thou hast not hearkened unto God thy God; and if thou hast not kept my word, thou shalt not prosper thereby; and thou hast not done His commandment.

11 And thou — thou didst seek after the eternal estate, because thou lovedst not His commandment, and didst not do His will — His, who magnified thee and honoured thee above all thy people, that thou mightest shepherd them in righteousness.

12 And He set thee on high, and as head over all His people, while thou rememberedst the name of thy God who created thee, and gave thee [all] that thou shouldest shepherd the people in righteousness and in equity.

13 And from these [things] evil [cometh] unto thee through thy teaching and through thy learning — while thou rememberest the way of God, and while thou mendest thy mind, and openest in uprightness.

14 And when they are judged before thee, respect not the person, and take no bribe from a man, that thou mightest acquit him; and from a pure man, that thou mightest condemn him — for this earth, it is all His.

15 And if thou hast done His will, He shall prolong thy days.

16 Remember that thou shalt rise from the dead before His face, and shalt be examined in all that thou hast done — whether of evil, and whether of good.

17 And if thou hast done good, thou shalt dwell in the dwelling of the good kings — thou shalt abide in the houses of light; but if thou hast done evil, thou shalt dwell in the dwelling of the evil kings — and God shall not spare thy glory.

18 But thou, when thou seest the greatness of thy glory, and the expanse of thy hosts, and the produce of thy field gathered, and thou beholdest thy horses, and the messenger that is under thy hand, and them that blow the horn and make melody before thee on the pipe —

19 But thou, when thou seest all this, thy heart is lifted up, and thou stiffenest thy neck, and rememberest not Him who gave thee all this glory. Is it not when He saith unto thee "Forsake [it]," that thou forsakest — even thou?

20 And thou givest thy glory to another; for thou hast turned away from the food wherewith He fed thee.

21 And hereby thou art chastened, and thou art reckoned, and thou art ruled; for suddenly death shall come upon thee, and in the resurrection there shall be judgment, and the work of man shall be examined.

22 And unto the kingdoms of the earth there is none that giveth honour; and unto the rulers of this earth the crown shall fall from them. For master and judge shall stand together in the condition of judgment; for a righteous ruler is He.

23 In the condition of the soul shall tremble, and the books shall be opened, and the work of man shall appear; and there is none that hideth himself.

24 When the resurrection of the dead [cometh]; for the earth shall give forth her womb which was sealed upon her — as a woman holdeth not [back] her womb — when the time is ripe for bearing, and she hasteneth to bring forth that which is in the belly.

25 And as the clouds withhold the appointed rain — that they go not whither God commanded them, that they come not down — even so it is not possible that the dead should not rise, [when] the time is ripe for the resurrection; for the word of God brought forth all from whence there was not, and the word of God brought all into being.

26 And again the word of God shall bring forth all from beneath, as Moses saith: "Not by bread alone doth man live, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God." (Deut 8:3)

27 Behold, thou knowest that by the word of God the dead shall live.

28 And again He saith in the second law, concerning them that shall be kings and rulers, and them that do the will: "In the affliction of [this] life thou shalt keep them; and in due time their foot shall slide — for the day of their calamity is nigh." (cf. Deut 32:35)

29 And again God saith unto them — unto those who embitter His — "Know ye, know ye, that I, even I, am He — God: I kill, and I make alive." (Deut 32:39)

30 "I wound, and I heal; I bring down to Sheol, and again I bring up to heaven; and there is none that delivereth out of My hand." (Deut 32:39)

31 Thus saith God concerning the kings and rulers who keep not His commandment: "Keep My law and My statutes, that ye may enter into My kingdom which is for ever; for the kingdom that is on earth is passing, and it fleeteth from noon till dawn."

32 For is God's — for honour and for dishonour: this one He abaseth, and this one He honoureth. (cf. 1 Sam 2:7)

33 Him that doeth not the will He shall cast down; and him that doeth the will He shall set up.
