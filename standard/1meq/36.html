1 Meqabyan

Chapter 36

1 Pride not yourselves, O Macedonians, and lift not up your necks, O Amalekites; for there is God that against you.

2 For ye exalt yourselves unto heaven, and ye go down into Sheol.

3 And aforetime, when they brought Israel into the land of Egypt, in the kingdom of the Medes, and the, and the Moabites, He said: "Pride not yourselves, for the of God is not against you, that ye should Him."

4 And thou, O son of, thou Ishmaelite, wherefore liftest thou thy neck against Him that is not? Remember that God shall thee in the day of judgment, when He ariseth to the earth.

5 Then shalt thou be recompensed according to the work of thy hands. Wherefore liftest thou thy heart, and liftest thou thy neck? saith God who ruleth over all.

6 And I will thee, as them that were not; and I sent thee, for thou becamest that which thou lovedst — thou doest evil.

7 And likewise I will against thee, saith God who ruleth over all. But as for thee, if thou doest good, and lovest that which I love, I will hear thee in all that thou askest Me.

8 And I will do thy will, if thou doest My will; and thine enemy shall not rise against thee; and I will bless thee — thy, and thy seed, and thy children.

9 And I will bless thy cattle and thy; and I will bless all that thy hand, if thou walkest in My commandment, and doest that which I love, saith God who ruleth over all.

10 But if thou doest not My will, and walkest not in My commandment, My judgment shall find thee — all this My judgment which I told aforetime; and thou shalt not be able to escape Mine anger that cometh upon thee at all times, for thou in My commandment, and walkest not in My judgment.

11 And I will not thee whom I; and thou from My praise and from My worship, whereas I created thee out of nothing.

12 And I gave thee honour and glory among men that are under thee, that thou shouldest do all that: thou slayest, and; thou, and; thou, and; thou — this is he.

13 If thou doest the will of God, and walkest in His commandments, He will love thee, that thou mayest rejoice with Him in His kingdom, and be a portion with them that inherit the holy city.

14 For He saith: "If they Me, I will cause them to inherit honour and favour, and make them rejoice in the house of My prayer; and they shall be chosen and, as the sacrifice of offering," saith God who ruleth over all.

15 Esteem not lightly the good deed, and the way of salvation, that ye may pass from death into life.

16 But as for them that keep the good deed, God them in all good, that they may become His servants — as Job, whom God kept from all evil.

17 And as Abraham, whom He delivered when he slew the kings; and as He delivered Moses from the hand of Pharaoh, and from the hands of the Canaanites that were with them — Abraham, when for his soul and by day and by night, that they should bring him down into their idols.

18 But as for him, he when they him into their, when they him.

19 And he, when they him, feared God that created him; for he was a believer — Abraham — in God, in whom he believed from his youth.

20 And he not his worship of God until the year of his death; for he loved God exceedingly, and transgressed not His commandment until the day of his death; and he taught his children that they should keep the commandment of God.

21 And they transgressed not His commandment, as their father did — the seed of Abraham; His servants transgressed not the commandment of God — Isaac and Jacob — for whose sake God spake, when He said unto His angels: "."

22 Upon the earth: "Abraham My beloved, and Isaac My, and Jacob, the of My soul — of them I am praised," saith God who ruleth over all.

23 And unto the children of Israel exceedingly, when them; and He, when them, fed them with manna in the wilderness.

24 And their garments waxed not old, and their not; for they were fed with manna, which is the bread of.

25 But as for their heart, is far from God; and they have no hope of, for the workers of evil.

26 And they were not as their fathers — Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob — that unto God in the of their life; but they were as a bow, and in their idols, and in their, and in their mountains; and they eat in the midst of the mountain, and in the midst of, and in the midst of.

27 And they sacrifice and and rejoice in the work of their hands; and they eat and drink; and their vessels, and, and with the demons.

28 And the demons unto them in all that them and them, and in all, and that hath no, and and, that not God.

29 And they unto the of Canaan, and unto Baal, and unto of Midian, and unto the of the ʾElofəli — Dagon, and Apollo, and unto Artemis, and unto.

30 And unto all their gods — unto the nations that them — they all, as the nations did, in that which I saw and in that which I heard; and all their and that which them, and their that the nations do.

31 And likewise do the people of Israel that say: "We will worship God," and do not His law and commandment which Moses told them in the Orit, that they should keep the commandments of God, and from the worship of idols.

32 And they worship strange gods that are not the God of their fathers, that fed them, and them upon the of the earth, and them from, and fed them with manna.

33 And them not that which they and that which they loved; for He is their God, and feedeth them that which they love.

34 But as for them, they forsook not unto God; and when He maketh them rejoice, unto God.

35 And when He them, they unto Him; and He delivered them from their tribulation that found them; and again they rejoice and dwell days.

36 And then they turn their heart, and return that unto God; and He raiseth up against them nations that them, that they should slay them and them and them.

37 And again they turn and and unto God their Lord.

38 And He them — not for their own sake, but for the sake of their fathers, unto whom He promised and established His covenant — with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, that from of old unto God in the of their life.

39 And unto them that loved Him and kept His commandment He loved them, that their seed should be multiplied as the sand of the sea, and as the stars of heaven.

40 And as the sand of the sea, saith He — the souls of sinners at the resurrection of the dead, that shall be cast into the lake of fire — from the children of Israel.

41 But as for the souls of the righteous — as the bright stars in the height of heaven — from the seed of unto the children of Israel; for God said unto Abraham: "Look at the heaven by night, and count the stars of heaven, if thou art able to count them." And likewise thy seed, the righteous, shall shine as the stars in the kingdom of heaven.

42 And again He said: "Look into the sea, and unto the; and see that which is in the sand, and count — if thou art able to count them. And likewise thy seed, them that go down into Sheol at the resurrection of the dead."

43 And Abraham believed in God, and righteousness was it; and his hope found upon the earth; and afterwards his wife Sarah found — Isaac.

44 And again in the heavens he shall find the kingdom for his faith; for he believed that the dead shall rise, and they that have done good shall walk into the life that is for ever.

45 But as for them that have done evil — in the judgment that is for ever, at the resurrection of the dead — and the righteous that have done good shall reign with Him for ever and ever.

46 And they that have done evil shall be judged for ever and ever. Amen and Amen.
