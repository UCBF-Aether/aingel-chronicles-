1 Meqabyan

Chapter 11

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Armon and Arefatiha and Daqa — and those who worship idols in Zebulon — and Edomyas shall be brought low; for God shall recompense them according to their evil, and according to the work of their hands. Tyre and Sidon shall weep; for near is that which shall befall them, because of their evil and their idols which they made in their youth, and they forsook [them] not until.

2 And therefore God shall them, because she wrought and defilement and and idols. And the children of Judah He shall; for they stood not in the commandment of God their Maker.

3 And after the resurrection of the dead, shame shall be uncovered unto Jerusalem; for she walked not in His law, nor in His judgment unto God, but walked in and in the slaying of the righteous.

4 This God shall with the of His wisdom; and she shall be in all the evil which she wrought in the days of her youth, and she forsook [it] not in the days of her until her.

5 And she entered into the grave, and became dust, like her former fathers who went in their and rejected the commandment of God — that the resurrection should befall them.

6 For Moses saith concerning them: "For as the vine of Sodom is the vine — [so is] their vine."

7 And them from Gomorrah; and them of gall; and them bitter.

8 serpents of the earth, and their wine [is] the of asps that slay.
