1 Meqabyan

Chapter 15

1 And at that time there rose up against them the men of Midian, and they raised their host to Israel; and the name of their king was Akrandes, and he assembled at dawn a great host of Syria and Cilicia and Damascus.

2 And he at the crossing of Jordan, and sent messengers unto Israel, saying: "Give me dinars; and if not, I am come to you, and to take your, and to carry off all your, and to your children."

3 And I will you into the sea which ye know not; and there I will you — and water.

4 And boast not, saying: "We are the people of God, and there is none that can [stand against] us"; for it is God that sent me to destroy you, and to take your, and all that is yours — as He assembled, He sent me.

5 Where are they whom their gods delivered — the strange people whom I judged, and took their and their, and slew their, and their and their children?

6 Likewise unto you also I will you, if ye give not unto me as I commanded you; and I will cross Jordan, to take their and their cattle, and to men.

7 And then the children of Israel wept unto God with bitter weeping, and cried and; and sinners, that them.

8 And therefore God gave power unto three brethren; and their names were these: Mebqeyus, and Meqabyos, and Judah — who were good and and strong in all their strength.

9 And when they heard that the children of Israel wept and, their heart was grieved at the deed of all the children of Israel — the and the children of death — and their priests and their princes, and all the tribe of Israel, man and woman and child — and all of them wept, and cast dust upon their heads, and put on sackcloth.

10 But as for them, the good and brethren, they that they should go and that they should deliver them; and they took counsel and said: "Let us go, and let us give our life unto death for these."

11 And they went, them, when in their their swords and their spears were in their hands; and they went, them, that they should unto.

12 And they came, when he was in the; and Mebqeyus cast at the and his bow with one, when into his mouth; and Judah and Meqabyos smote them with the sword, them that were on the right and on the left of their king.

13 And when their king fell, they and fled, all of them; and there entered into the heart of their; and their were, and their were broken.

14 But as for them, the good and brethren, they were delivered; and there was none that found them, not even one evil; but as for the evil, they were among themselves, for God brought punishment upon them.

15 And they fell and died; and all that were theirs they pursued until they crossed Jordan; and all that were remained. And when the children of Israel saw that they, they went with their camp; they lifted up unto their head.

16 And thus God delivered the children of Israel by the hand of Mebqeyus and his brethren.

17 And they dwelt days, when the children of Israel unto God.

18 But afterwards they returned again unto their evil, and the unto God did the children of Israel in; and He again them with a people whom they knew not, whom their, and who their vine and their wine, and lift up their, and their before them; and He could not deliver them — for a people without law are they; for unto God at all times.
