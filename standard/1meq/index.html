THE FIRST BOOK OF MEQABYAN

1 Meqabyan

1 Meqabyan is an Ethiopian book of courage under persecution, and it surprises many first-time readers. Despite the similar name, it is not 1 Maccabees: the Meqabyan books are entirely different works, and this canon carries no Maccabees at all. In 36 chapters, 1 Meqabyan tells of rulers who demand idolatry, of families and fellow believers who refuse to bow, and of God's judgment on proud and violent power. Its heroes suffer rather than compromise, and the book keeps returning to repentance, hope beyond death, and the danger of false teaching. The Ethiopian Orthodox Church alone preserved these books in its canon. For this edition, 1 Meqabyan was translated anew, directly from the Ge'ez text, verse by verse. It belongs here because without it, the Ethiopian Bible would be missing one of its own distinctive witnesses. Watch for the steadfast confessions of its sufferers, who answer power with simple trust in God.
