1 Meqabyan

Chapter 30

1 And the house of Saul shall be despised, for he provoked me concerning that wherein he transgressed my word.

2 And I will reject him, and the kingdom shall not again be from the house of this, for he kept not my word.

3 And likewise them that keep not my word, and walk not in my law and in my ordinance, I will withhold from them my gift and my kingdom, from their seed for ever.

4 And I will repent, that I honour them no more; but the nonentity, whom I have rejected: for when I honoured them, they honoured me not, and when I exalted them, they exalted me not.

5 And when I did good unto them, they did me no good; and when I committed unto them, they committed nothing unto me.

6 For I set them as head above all, and they set me not as head over them; and when I made them great above all, they made me not great, and kept not my commandment.

7 And I took away the gift which I gave them, and it shall not return unto them, as I sware by myself; and him that honoureth me I will honour, and him that loveth me I will love, so saith God of all who ruleth.

8 But them that honour me not, and keep not my commandment, I will cut them off from my gift which I gave them.

9 Saith God of all who ruleth: him that loveth me I will love, and him that honoureth me I will honour.

10 For when God who established all, and there is none that delivereth out of my hand, in heaven above and on earth beneath; for I am God, who killeth and maketh alive, who woundeth and healeth.

11 And great and glorious is he, this is he: him that loveth thee I will honour, and him that despiseth thee I will cut off.

12 And I am he that healeth them, for them that love me and remember my name at all times; for the poor and for the rich, I am the giver of food.

13 And not for man alone, but for the beasts, and for the birds, and for the wild beasts, and for the fishes which are in the sea, and for the ʾäkəyəsət.

14 And for the monsters, and for the ḥärəgäṣ́at, and for the ʾənəgwätat, and for the behemoth, and for the frogs.

15 And all that moveth in the water, and all that flieth above the earth — even this same is he.

16 And I am he that feedeth with that which they desire, and with that which is delightful, for all of them that seek it.
