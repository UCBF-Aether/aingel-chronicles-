1 Meqabyan

Chapter 33

1 And yet I fed them manna in the wilderness, bread as David saith, and the bread of his angels they ate — the children of ʾəmäḥəyawə; and all this I set before them, that they might serve me in righteousness and uprightness.

2 But they served me not; and I will visit them, saith God of all who ruleth; and they despised me, and walked in a trackless.

3 And I, according to their works will I recompense them, and according to the work of their hands will I visit them; for they forsook my worship, and endured not my ordinance and my counsel; and I will bring them down into the judgment of heaven which is in the heavens.

4 And upon the earth I will shorten their days, for they kept not my commandment; and I will despise them.

5 But thou, O king, art not; thou art a man, thou that diest and perishest; for to-morrow thou shalt become wood and trees and dust.

6 And now thou exaltest thyself, and art proud, as he that dieth not for ever.

7 For thou art a man who now showest thyself alive, and to-morrow is death, saith God of all who ruleth.

8 But if thou keep my word and my commandment, I will cause thee to inherit the holy city, with them that did my will — honoured kings, whose dwellings are bright, and beautiful are their crowns, and their thrones of gold and of silver; who are, who dwell upon them.

9 And thou shalt rejoice in the midst of his city, which is nigh, a dwelling for the good servants.

10 But for the evil servants, it shall not be permitted unto them to enter into this city, wherein dwell the honoured kings.

11 For they kept not my word and my ordinance, saith God of all who ruleth.
