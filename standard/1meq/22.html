1 Meqabyan

Chapter 22

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Remember — behold — the names of them that do good, and forget not their deeds.

2 And be zealous, that thy name may be as the name of their fathers — that in heaven thou mayest rejoice with them in the dwelling of light, which He prepared for the elect, [for] kings and princes that did the will of God.

3 Again, know thou and learn the names of the evil kings and princes — that they may be punished and put to shame among men after their death.

4 Who in heaven shall be punished with the wicked and the ʿalawəyanə — that did not the will of God; for though they see and hear, they make not their way straight.

5 But thou, go not in the way of the wicked, whom God rejected because of the evil of their deeds; but be thou elect, and integrity and uprightness.

6 And open unto righteousness, and justify the poor and the dying; and deliver [them] from the hand of them that oppress them — the sinners.

7 And stand for the dying as a father, that thou mayest deliver him from the hand of him that oppresseth him; and dread [shall fall] when he is cut off from among the dying in thy midst — that thou be not afraid in the sea of fire, wherein are punished the sinners that repented not.

8 Make straight thy steps in the way of peace, and seek peace, and follow after it — for the eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, and His ears unto their supplications.

9 Be holy unto the LORD against them that do evil, that He may root out their memorial from the earth; and there shall not remain of them any that is left — neither in the wilderness, nor in ʿarəfət.

10 And I will not turn away My face, until I destroy them that keep not My word — for I am a jealous God, who repayeth them that hate Me, and them that keep not My word.

11 And I will honour them that honour Me, and keep My word.
