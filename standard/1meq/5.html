1 Meqabyan

Chapter 5

1 The visionary exalted himself; and in the first fäṣän God planted him, that his belly should be filled from his foot unto his head; and in the second fäṣän he died.

2 And again Nimrod exalted himself, who made a bed of out of great splendour; to him also God gave glory.

3 And again Nebuchadnezzar exalted himself, and said: "There is no other king but me; and I am he who made the sun to rise upon the earth." And from much pride he spake thus.

4 And God drove him out from among men —

5 And when He said, "Again I will bring him into his kingdom" — who is he that could shut him out? was proud against God his Maker, who made not the earth.

6 And who is he that transgressed against Him — His law and His ordinance? And to whom did He not apportion the earth?

7 But as for thee, Sirusaydan — desirest thou to be proud against thy Maker? And again, it shall be unto thee as unto them: that He humble thee from thy pride, and bring thee down into the land of death.

8 And bring down into Sheol, of darkness, where there is weeping and gnashing of teeth, into a great pit and burning, where their flame is not quenched for ever.

9 But thou art man and earth; thou who art become flesh shalt die, like those proud kings like thee, who forsook the goods of this world.

10 But as for us, we say: thou art man, and not God; for God is the Maker of the heavens and the earth, and the Maker of thy flesh.

11 And He humbleth the proud, and exalteth the lowly; and He giveth strength to the weak, and giveth weakness to the strong.

12 And He giveth death to the living, and He raiseth the dead who sleep in the grave and are become dust.

13 And He setteth them apart for service in life, from the works of sin.

14 O king Sirusaydan, why dost thou pride thyself in thy defiled gods, which have no profit?

15 God made the heavens, and founded the earth; He created the great sea; He set the sun and the moon; the seasons and the years He ordained.

16 He sendeth man forth unto his work by day, and he toileth until the evening; and the stars of heaven are hidden at His word.

17 And He numbereth them all by their names; and there is none whom God knoweth not.

18 And the angels of heaven — He it is who that they should be sent unto Him and praise His holy name; and He sendeth him unto all the angels, because of those who shall inherit life.

19 The angel Raphael was sent unto Tobit, and delivered Tobiah in the land of Raguel.

20 And an angel was sent unto Gideon, that he might put to flight; and an angel was sent from before Moses when He divided for Israel the Red Sea.

21 For God saith: "I alone am their guide, and there is no strange god with them."

22 And He made them go up upon of the earth.

23 And He fed them, and satisfied them with honey from the rock; for He loved them exceedingly.

24 But as for thee, He made thee king over His people, and gave thee dominion over the four kingdoms, that thou shouldest keep the people, and shepherd [them] in righteousness, and do the will of Him that made thee.

25 And He made thee king a second time, that thou shouldest love God thy God; for — and He made thee king —

26 But as for thee, O righteous one, love thou God thy God a second time, as He loved thee and thou hast trusted in all His; and do thou the will of God, that thy days may be prolonged upon the earth, and with thee.

27 And He shall set thee above all thine enemies, and make thee dwell upon all thy thrones, and spread His wings over thy head.

28 If thou obeyest, God shall choose thee, as He chose Saul from the children of Israel, and made [him] king over Israel His people — when he sought the asses of his father — and he sat upon the throne with Samuel the prophet.

29 And He gave him a great portion above his brethren, and said unto him: "Behold, from this day God hath made thee king over His people — "

30 And show thou unto them that do good, good; and unto them that do evil, evil; for God hath set thee over them, that thou shouldest kill and make alive.

31 And unto them that do good, that thou render unto them good; and unto them that do evil, that thou render unto them evil; for unto thee hath God given all thou desirest, that thou mayest do His will — whether thou punish or whether thou make alive.

32 But as for thee, do the will of God at all times and in every hour, that He may do thy will in all thou hast and in all thou hast asked, when thou prayest before Him; for it is thou that hast made unto Him that ruleth all, unto Him that is above.

33 And as for him that hath none to establish him, He Himself establisheth all.

34 As for him that hath none to appoint him, He Himself appointeth all.

35 As for him that hath none to him, He Himself all.

36 As for him that hath none to set apart him, He separateth all.

37 As for him that hath none to bring him back, He Himself bringeth back all; and there is none that escapeth from His hand; for the judgment of heaven and earth is with Him.

38 And there is nothing hidden before His eyes, but [all is] naked before Him.

39 And He seeth all, but Him seeth none; He heareth the prayer of him that prayeth unto Him — and his prayer — for He formed man in His image and in His likeness.

40 And He feedeth all from His storehouse that faileth not; for He is King — He endureth for ever.
