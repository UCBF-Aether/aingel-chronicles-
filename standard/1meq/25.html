1 Meqabyan

Chapter 25

1 I say unto their souls: when I seize them in Mine anger, saith the Lord, whom all obey; for perverse is their way, and all that walk in the way of Satan are destroyed.

2 And the way of God they know not, and they have turned their backs, and turned away from the law.

3 And I will recompense them according to the evil of their works, afterward in the day of judgment; I will visit them, as it is written: I have kept the evil of their works, and they shall not escape from My hand; for I am the Lord, who killeth and maketh alive, who rebuketh and pardoneth.

4 And there is none that escapeth from My hand, neither under the earth, nor above the heavens, nor in the sea, nor in the depths of the waters; for I am the Lord, from the end unto the ends of the world, and every soul is in My hand.

5 He commanded under the earth, and to the fishes in the midst of the sea, and to the birds in the midst of the air, and to the in the wilderness, from the end unto the end, and unto the ends of the world — this is He.

6 And there is none that escapeth from My hand, in heaven above or in earth beneath; for I am He that did it, and was silent, and it was shown before Mine eyes. And there is none that saith unto Me: Whither hast thou turned? and, What doest thou?

7 And I have commanded above the angels and the elders, and every name that is named — this is He; and all the birds of heaven, and the beasts of the desert, and men — this is He.

8 And the breath of the hyena, and the of the evening, and the sea of is gathered, from the deep its, for him that shall come unto it in the latter days.

9 For He shall judge the living and the dead, in Noba and Saba and Ethiopia and India, and all that them, and their.

10 And every one seeth in truth, with the staff that is in his hand; and he seeth with the staff of his; for his staff is exalted above all staves.

11 And His horn is weighty above all horns, and His kingdom is exalted above all kingdoms, and His dominion hath seized all the world, which encompasseth all, and there is none that measureth it.

12 He commandeth the clouds above, and maketh the grass to spring for men beneath, and giveth fruit upon the field.

13 And He feedeth all according to His will, with His and with His fruit and with His, to all that He created, to cattle and to creeping things under the earth, and to the beasts and to men upon the earth — who giveth to him that prayeth, and rejecteth not their supplication, for and for of death.

14 And again their prayer is increased unto us — who pray unto Him at all times; but their is unto the wicked, as the soul of the wild ass, and their gathering unto the unrighteous, as our blood.

15 And their joy also is as the bird, and the beauty of their pasture is transient — of gold and silver upon the earth — and their gold also profiteth them not; but their is for the righteous, and in the day of their it shall be swallowed in.

16 And the store of our and our is known; and all that passeth, as the day that turneth — it hath passed; and like the word that goeth forth from the mouth, it returneth not; its is for sinners; and like the shadow that passeth, so the beauty of their dwelling; and like a garment, so their is for sinners before God.

17 But the righteous — but He saith: God rebuketh them not; but when they show mercy, and weep openly over the dead and the poor, and when they clothe the naked with their garments, and when they withhold not the seed of their house which God gave them, that they may give to the stranger and to the poor —

18 And they wrong not openly the faithful, and repay not evil for evil, and do no iniquity in the reckoning of their months, nor in the measure of their balances; for the word of God is righteousness and glory, as this.
