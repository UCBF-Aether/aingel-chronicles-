1 And He created the whole world out of nothing, and prepared and: the mountains, and; and He the earth upon the waters, that it should not be moved in the; for first of all God said: "Let there be light."

2 And there was light, whereas darkness [was] the world; and God wrought a work, and prepared and the world with that which and with that which; and He said: "Let there be evening and darkness."

3 And again God said: "Let there be light," and it shone, and there was light; and He lifted up the waters that are beneath above the heaven.

4 And He as, and with the breath of His; and unto the waters He the lower depths of the sea.

5 And He in the unto the sea, and them with His breath, that they should not the lower depths; and He them for the beasts and for the cattle therein, and for the great beasts therein — which have no number: them that are seen and them that are not seen.

6 And on the third day God made the herbs upon the dry land, and all and the trees, and the fruits that bear fruit.

7 And life that is good to see, and sweet to eat, and, and all the green of the earth, whose seed is in itself; and He appointed food for all cattle and beasts and birds, and gave to eat unto all that He made.

8 And there was evening and morning; and on the fourth night God said: "Let there be light in the firmament of heaven." And God made the sun and the moon and the stars, and set them in the firmament of heaven, that they should shine upon the earth, and rule it by day and by night.

9 And then were the sun and the moon and the stars, by day and by night.

10 And on the fifth day God created all beasts and cattle that dwell in the heavens, and all birds that fly above the air, and all that is seen and that is not seen — all this God created.

11 And on the sixth day He created all beasts and cattle and, and every creature; and, He created Adam in His image and in His likeness.

12 And He all the beasts and cattle that He created, that he should rule over them; and all cattle and beasts and and that are in the sea.

13 And, and all the that are upon the earth, and the cattle that are seen and that are not seen.

14 And He set the children of men whom He made in His image and in His likeness, and commanded that he should eat and the garden, and praise God therein.

15 And that he should not transgress the commandment of God, and that he should not eat of the tree that good and evil — the tree of death.

16 For He saith: "In the day thou eatest of this tree of death, thou shalt die."

17 And by his the serpent her from Eve; and she ate of that tree, and gave unto our father Adam.

18 And he ate of that tree, and death came upon him and upon his children.

19 And God upon our father Adam, for he transgressed His commandment, and ate of that tree which God commanded him that he should not eat thereof; and He drove him out from the garden, and gave him this earth and, wherein when he the commandment, that he should and eat the herb of his in the sweat of his face.
