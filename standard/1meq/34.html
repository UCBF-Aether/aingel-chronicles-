1 Meqabyan

Chapter 34

1 The kingdom of the Medes shall, and the kingdom of Rome shall exceedingly against the kingdom of Macedonia, and the kingdom of Nineveh, and against the kingdom of Persia.

2 And the kingdom of Ethiopia against the kingdom of Alexandria, and the kingdom of Moab against Amalek, for they shall rise up.

3 And brother shall rise up against his brother; and God shall, as He spake against them, that they should be destroyed.

4 And He saith: "Nation shall rise against nation, and people against people, and city against city."

5 And there shall be, and there shall be slaughter and famine and and and; for peace and love are taken away, and the scourge of God is come down upon her.

6 And when the day of God entereth, as the lightning that is seen from the east unto the west.

7 And then shall every one be recompensed according to his deeds, and according to the of his hands, and according to the fruit of his deeds; for saith God: "In the day of judgment them, and in their feet, for the day of their destruction is nigh."

8 At that time God shall destroy the workers of evil that walk not in His law, in the judgment that is for ever — the people of Judah, and Cyprus, and Samaria, and Damascus, and the people of Babylon.

9 And these that dwell — Arabia, and India, and Noba, and Saba, and Egypt, and Ethiopia — and all of them that dwell therein.

10 Then unto Me, for I am God, that established heaven and earth; and I am He that giveth honour and favour; and I am He that killeth and maketh alive.

11 And I am He that maketh the sun to rise and it in its setting; and I am He that bringeth good and evil.

12 And I am He that bringeth a people that, that shall eat the fruit of your, and your cattle and your.

13 And your children before you, and them; and their deliverance; and they shall eat your and your, for ye have not the commandment of God which ye heard, for the Spirit of God not you.

14 And he in whom the Spirit of God dwelleth all, as Nebuchadnezzar the said unto Daniel the prophet: "For I see the Spirit of God upon thee."

15 For he in whom is the Spirit of God all — hidden and manifest shall be unto him; and there is none that hideth from the Spirit of God, but all, hidden and manifest.

16 For we are men; we that shall die, and our deeds shall be revealed, that in secret every one that we did.

17 As they try gold and silver in the fire, so shall the sinners be tried in the latter day, for they kept not the commandment of God.

18 Then shall the deeds of all the children of Israel be tried, and of all the nations.
