1 Meqabyan

Chapter 24

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 But Gideon, when he believed God, with his small host — and the thousands of hosts which had no number, as locusts, and their host.

2 Trust ye not in your gods; for there is no god beside Me. I am King and Governor.

3 For I am He, the Lord your God, who brought you forth from the land of your bondage, and raised you up, and you, and you; and why have ye set up and made king another god beside Me?

4 And all this have I done for you; and what have I given you, and what have I desired of you, save the remedy of your salvation — that ye walk in My law and in My ordinances and in My commandment? And I have given you the remedy of your souls.

5 Saith the Lord, to whom all: I will bring your heads into humiliation and captivity and.

6 For the wrath of God shall come upon them that do this, and upon them that hearken unto them, and upon them that do their will, and upon them that go with them, and upon them that walk in their commandment.

7 For a people shall rise up against you, whom ye know not and who know you not, and they shall devour the fruit of your labour — because ye have not done the will of God, which He did for you, as His prophets spake, from Samuel the prophet onward; Ezekiel and Enoch spake, and Asaph sang.

8 And he said: The enemies shall come, their loins girt, tearing their garments, and there shall be no other law among them, save fornication and drunkenness; and they weave gold and silver, and every work of affliction which God hateth not.

9 But they shall prevail against the, in fornication and drunkenness; when they wake from their sleep, from morning until evening they run in evil works; and for their feet there is no way of peace, but their path is shame, and in their way.

10 And they know not the way of peace, and the fear of God is not before their eyes; they are a wanton people and, that have no salt, and no faith; drunkards and fornicators and winebibbers and drunkards; and without law they have corrupted in the pasture, and they pour blood, and and, and they seize the goods of their masters by iniquity.

11 And mockers without law, and dissolute, and there is no fear in their faces; for they fear not God who made them.

12 And they are not ashamed before the man that seeth them, nor before him that rebuketh them, nor before the elder; and when they hear of the goods of the world, they forget that which their eyes have not seen, and when they have seen with their eyes, it seemeth unto them what they have uttered with their mouths; for the fear of God is not before their eyes.

13 And their rulers also take counsel with fornicators; and what they have spoken in the morning, they undo in the evening; for they are liars, and there is no uprightness in their mouths.

14 And their kings also, the crafty, run unto evil; for they oppress the poor and needy, and they drive them not to the stranger from the hand of them that oppress them.

15 And therefore there shall be no kings that are; but they shall unto the stranger and unto.

16 But they are the murderers and the proud, who drink blood, and have no mercy, like a mother, and a bird like its young; for narrow is all their way that they have seen, and that which is theirs they forget.

17 And they have no mercy on the poor and the stranger; but they love to gather unto themselves, and seize the goods of their masters by violence; and all that they find they heap up, that they may store it in their treasuries, and rejoice therein.

18 Woe unto their souls, when God shall take them in His anger; because they established not their ordinance in their life; for they shall be suddenly extinguished, as he that goeth forth to the way and findeth nothing, and bringeth nothing into his house.

19 And likewise they shall be extinguished in the land in one, when God rebuketh them; they shall perish in their time. For God hasteneth not to quench him, but waiteth until he enter into repentance.

20 But if he enter not into repentance, God shall quench them, as of old them that were before them, who kept not the commandment of God in purity.

21 And the fornicators, the flesh of man, and the drinkers of blood — and the fear of God is not in them at any time; for quickly they pass unto the, when they rise from their, and they rest not from the work of iniquity.

22 Of fornication and drink and in evil, and ḫagwələ — that they may destroy many souls upon the earth.
