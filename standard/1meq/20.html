1 Meqabyan

Chapter 20

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Remember, my brethren, and forget not what the ancients told you: how God will keep the righteousness of them that do good.

2 And their fruit shall be multiplied upon the earth, and their remembrance shall be for good for ever; and their seed shall not be cut off from upon the earth.

3 And He shall deliver them from the hand of all their oppressors and their enemies who pursue them; for on their account it shall come to pass unto them, and He will not abandon them into the hand of their enemies.

4 And He shall be unto them a helper in their tribulation — unto them that love His name; and they shall be kept, and He will forgive them all their sins.
