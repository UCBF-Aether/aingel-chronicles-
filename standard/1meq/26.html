1 Meqabyan

Chapter 26

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 But the poor — now they reckon in their; but if the rich are increased, they are as dry wood, which hath no moisture; and he that hath no moisture putteth forth no root, and he that hath no root putteth forth no branch.

2 But if it putteth forth no branch, it beareth no fruit; for the branch is for the bearing of fruit; and he that hath no fruit hath no good work; for the head of man is the fruit of righteousness.

3 But he that doeth righteousness bringeth forth uprightness, and God rejoiceth over the man that doeth righteousness and uprightness.

4 And He giveth his prayer to him that prayeth, and his, and forsaketh not the righteous, because of the righteousness which he did.

5 But the sinner that is without repentance, because of the evil of his works which he did — for righteous is God, and He loveth righteousness; and He respecteth not the person of the mighty, nor the poor in judgment; for He is the King of heaven and earth, and every soul is in His hand.
