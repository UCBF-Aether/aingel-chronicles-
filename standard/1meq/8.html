1 Meqabyan

Chapter 8

1 And concerning the resurrection of the dead also — hear my word: they plant trees, and make hedges, and vineyards, and wine; and they make [wine] from it — for God bringeth forth the fruit from whence there was not.

2 The showers of water and of earth —

3 And God shall water its root from —

4 The winds shall gather the souls — of water, and of earth, and of the sun, and of the wind — for this he went, as the going-forth from the flesh.

5 And the Spirit of God shall give fruit in the midst of them; even so [shall be] the resurrection of the dead.

6 And He said: From —

7 The earth was where it was, and became ashes; and the water was where it was, and became dew.

8 And the wind also was where it was, and became wind; and the fire also was where it was, and became the burning of the sun.

9 And the Spirit of God shall return into the hand of its Maker, and shall abide in that which He willed; for when the time cometh, He willed.

10 And He shall set them in the houses of light — the souls of the righteous; but the souls of the sinners He shall shut up in the houses of darkness, until the appointed time He bringeth them forth.

11 And He said unto Ezekiel the prophet: "Call unto the four winds, that they may gather, and become one spirit." (Ezek 37:9)

12 And when he prophesied thus, with one word they were gathered from the four winds.

13 And He brought the water of dew; and again He brought the burning of the sun.

14 And again He brought the ashes of the earth; and the wind also He brought as breath.

15 And God also brought the soul from its dwelling — from the bones — and with one word they were gathered, and there was a resurrection.

16 And again I will show thee that which is a parable unto thee: there shall be evening, and thou shalt sleep; and there shall be morning, and thou shalt rise. But thou — when thou sleepest, thou art dead.

17 And when thou awakest, [it is] a parable of the resurrection; but the night also is a parable of this world, whose darkness is all of it — and the whole world sleepeth in it, for the darkness of night covereth them.

18 And the coming of the morning also is a parable of the resurrection of the dead: in it the darkness is scattered, and there shall be light in the whole world; and man shall rise, and be fruitful in his work.

19 And so this is the new world, and the resurrection of the dead; for this world is passing, like this night.

20 And when the sun cometh, and shineth — a parable of this world — as David saith: "And in the sun He set His tabernacle." (Ps 19:4)

21 And when He shall come in the new world — as the sun shineth now — when God shall come unto him over it, He shall shine in the new world; for He saith: "A sun that setteth not, and a lamp that is not quenched — God is light unto him." (cf. Isa 60:19-20)

22 And again He shall raise the dead in the twinkling of an eye; and again I will set before thee one parable from thy sowing which thou sowest — and it liveth in it — whether thou hast reaped wheat, or thou hast reaped barley, or thou hast reaped — and every work of man which is sown in all the earth: if it dieth not, there is none that liveth. (1 Cor 15:52, 36-37)

23 And when he dieth, his flesh is devoured in the dust with his, like the flesh of man which thou seest.

24 And his flesh is devoured in the earth; it —

25 Behold, it is — if it dieth not, it cannot live; and when thou bringest forth grain in abundance.

26 And He shall give from God fruit in this grain which was sown, and He shall clothe [it with] flesh anew. (cf. 1 Cor 15:38)

27 And, and, and, and — it shall not be hidden from thee; but behold, how abundant is the of that which thou sowedst!

28 Be not a fool, and behold thy seed — how abundant! Even so [shall be] the resurrection of the dead; and according as they shall be recompensed according to their works. (1 Cor 15:36)

29 And again hear my word: for seed is not changed unto another seed — for if thou sowedst wheat, it.

30 Barley — and if thou sowedst thus, it; And from among the trees: if thou plantest a fig, its fruit shall yield for; and if thou plantest a walnut, there shall be unto thee.

31 And if thou plantest sweet fruit, it shall yield bitter; and if thou plantest a bitter tree, they shall eat the of sweetness.

32 Even so: if a sinner dieth, they shall the of the righteous at the resurrection; and if a righteous man dieth, they shall the of the sinner at the resurrection — but all, according to his work, shall be recompensed; and according to the fruit of his doing, and according to the work of his hands, he shall be recompensed; and there is none that shall be in the sin of another.

33 The cedar of Lebanon is planted, and maketh long and great boughs — but if the dew falleth not upon it, its shall not; but it shall wither and be plucked up.

34 And is filled from its root — if the rain of cometh not down upon it.

35 And so, if it cometh not down upon them — and unto life from God — the dead shall not be raised.
