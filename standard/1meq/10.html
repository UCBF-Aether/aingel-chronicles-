1 Meqabyan

Chapter 10

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 The grave — and their bones.

2 Is it not [meet] that they should rise at the resurrection together with their companions; and their bones shall not be mingled with the bones of the wicked, and of the, and of the.

3 And let not thy mind err, while thou sayest: "By what shall the dead rise after death?" — those who (cf. 1 Cor 15:35)

4 And when thou seest in the grave, there is not found a full of dust; and "By what shall their bones rise for the dead?" — thou sayest this in the foolishness of thy heart.

5 "That which thou sowest shall not [live]," thou sayest; "it shall thee — thou, that which thou sowest." (cf. 1 Cor 15:36)

6 And even so God also, who sowed the souls — they shall rise; and He shall not hinder their being raised by His life-giving word; for He it is who brought man from whence he was not into being — who in truth.

7 And: from existence He brought him into death; and again, from death He shall bring him into existence.

8 God is able — He made him live, and raised him.
