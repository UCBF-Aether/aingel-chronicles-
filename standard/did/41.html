Didascalia

Chapter 41

OF THE PRAYER WHICH IS SAID OVER THE CHRISM

1 O Lord God, the Lord of the whole Creation, Who hast granted us the sweet savour of this chrism, even

2 to all the nations who believe in Thee, (grant) also

3 the efficacy of this chrism as a mystery to those that are baptized into Thy name.

4 And after this he shall stand

5 and pray this prayer which our Lord hath taught us: “Our

6 Father who art in heaven,” unto the end thereof. Then

7 let he Priest turn towards the East, and raise his eyes

8 to heaven, and say, “Glorify the Lord, for the Lord

9 is good; and sing to His name for it is yr i mercy is for ever.” Secs
