Jubilees

Chapter 42

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And in the first year of the third week of the forty-fifth jubilee the famine began to come into the land, and the rain refused to be given to the earth, for none whatever fell.

2 And the earth grew barren, but in the land of Egypt there was food, for Joseph had gathered the seed of the land in the seven years of plenty and had preserved it.

3 And the Egyptians came to Joseph that he might give them food, and he opened the storehouses where was the grain of the first year, and he sold it to the people of the land for gold.

4 (Now the famine was very sore in the land of Canaan), and Jacob heard that there

5 was food in Egypt, and he sent his ten sons that they should procure food for him in Egypt; but Benjamin he did not send, and (the ten sons of Jacob) arrived (in Egypt) among those that went (there.) 5.

6 And Joseph recognized them, but they did not recognize him, and he spake unto them and questioned them, and he said unto them: " Are ye not spies, and have ye not come to explore the approaches of the land? ' And he put them in ward.

7 And after that he set them free again, and detained Simeon alone and sent off his nine brothers.

8 And he filled their sacks with corn, and he put their gold in their sacks, and they did not know.

9 And he commanded them to bring their younger brother, for they had told him their father was living and their younger brother.

10 And they went up from the land of Egypt and they came to the land of Canaan; and they told their father all that had befallen them, and how the lord of the country had spoken roughly to them, and had seized Simeon till they should bring Benjamin.

11 And Jacob said: " Me have ye bereaved of my children!

12 Joseph is not and Simeon also is not, and ye will take Benjamin away.

13 On me hath your wickedness come." 11.

14 And he said: " My son will not go down with you lest perchance he fall sick; for their mother gave birth to two sons, and one hath perished, and this one also ye will take from me.

15 If perchance he took a fever on the road ye would bring down my old age with sorrow unto death." 12.

16 For he saw that their money had been returned to every man in his sack, and for this reason he feared to send him.

17 And the famine increased and became sore in the land of Canaan, and in all lands save in the land of Egypt, for many of the children of the Egyptians had stored up their seed for food from the time when they saw Joseph gathering seed together

18 and putting it in storehouses and preserving it for the

19 years of famine.

20 And the people of Egypt fed

21 themselves thereon during the first year of their

22 famine.

23 But when Israel saw that the famine was

24 very sore in the land, and there was no deliverance,

25 he said unto his sons: " Go again, and procure food

26 for us that we die not." 16.

27 And they said: " We

28 shall not go; unless our youngest brother go with us,

29 we shall not go." 17.

30 And Israel saw that if he did

31 not send him with them, they should all perish by

32 reason of the famine.

33 And Reuben said:

34 " Give him into my hand, and if I do not bring him

35 back to thee, slay my two sons instead of his soul."

36 And he said unto him: " He shall not go with thee."

37 And Judah came near and said: " Send him with

38 me, and if I do not bring him back to thee, let me

39 bear the blame before thee all the days of my life." 20.

40 And he sent him with them in the second year of this

41 week on the first day of the month, and they came to

42 the land of Egypt with all those who went, and (they

43 had) presents in their hands, stacte and almonds and

44 terebinth nuts and pure honey.

45 And they went

46 and stood before Joseph, and he saw Benjamin his

47 brother, and he knew him, and said unto them: " Is

48 this your youngest brother?

49 " And they said unto

50 him: " It is he."

51 And he said: " The Lord be

52 gracious to thee, my son!

53 And he sent him

54 into his house and he brought forth Simeon unto

55 them and he made a feast for them, and they presented

56 to him the gift which they had brought in their hands.

57 And they ate before him and he gave them all a

58 portion, but the portion of Benjamin was seven times

59 larger than that of any of theirs.

60 And they ate

61 and drank and arose and remained with their asses.

62 And Joseph devised a plan whereby he might

63 learn their thoughts as to whether thoughts of peace

64 prevailed amongst them, and he said to the steward

65 who was over his house: " Fill all their sacks with

66 food, and return their money unto them into their

67 vessels, and my cup, the silver cup out of which I

68 drink, put it in the sack of the youngest, and send them away."

69 Joseph finally tests his Brethren, and then makes himself known to them.
