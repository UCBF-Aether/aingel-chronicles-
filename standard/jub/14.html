Jubilees

Chapter 14

1 After these things in the fourth year of this week, on the new moon of the third month the word of the Lord came to Abram in a dream, saying: " Fear not, Abram; I am thy defender, and thy reward will be exceeding great." 2.

2 And he said: "Lord, Lord, what wilt thou give me, seeing I go

3 hence childless, and the son of Maseq, the son of

4 my handmaid, is the Dammasek Eliezer: he will be

5 my heir, and to me thou hast given no seed." 3.

6 And

7 He said unto him: " This (man) will not be thy heir,

8 but one that will come out of thine own bowels;

9 he will be thine heir." 4.

10 And He brought him

11 forth abroad, and said unto him: " Look toward

12 heaven and number the stars, if thou art able to

13 number them." 5.

14 And he looked toward heaven,

15 and beheld the stars.

16 And He said unto him:

17 " So shall thy seed be." 6.

18 And he believed in the

19 Lord, and it was counted to him for righteousness.

20 And He said unto him: "I am the Lord that

21 brought thee out of Ur of the Chaldees, to give thee

22 the land of the Canaanites to possess it for ever;

23 and I shall be God unto thee and to thy seed after

24 thee." 8.

25 And he said: "Lord, Lord, whereby

26 shall I know that I shall inherit (it)?

27 And he

28 said unto him: "

29 Take Me an heifer of three years,

30 and a goat of three years, and a sheep of three years,

31 and a turtle-dove, and a pigeon." 10.

32 And he took

33 all these in the middle of the month; and he dwelt

34 at the oak of Mamre, which is near Hebron.

35 And

36 he built there an altar, and sacrificed all these; and

37 he poured their blood upon the altar, and divided

38 them in the midst, and laid them over against each

39 other; but the birds divided he not.

40 And birds

41 came down upon the pieces, and Abram drove them

42 away, and did not suffer the birds to touch them.

43 And it came to pass, when the sun had set, that

44 an ecstasy fell upon Abram, and lo! an horror of

45 great darkness fell upon him, and it was said unto

46 Abram: " Know of a surety that thy seed shall be a

47 stranger in a land (that is) not theirs, and they will

48 bring them into bondage, and afflict them four

49 hundred years.

50 And the nation also to whom they will be in bondage shall I judge, and after that they will come forth thence with much substance.

51 And thou wilt go to thy fathers in peace, and be buried in a good old age.

52 But in the fourth generation they will return hither; for the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet full." 17.

53 And he awoke from his sleep, and he arose, and the sun had set; and there was a flame, and behold! a furnace was smoking, and a flame of fire passed between the pieces.

54 And on that day the Lord made a covenant with Abram, saying: " To thy seed will I give this land, from the river of Egypt unto the great river, the river Euphrates, the Kenites, the Kenizzites, the Kadmonites, the Perizzites, and the Rephaim, the Phakorites and the Hivites and the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Girgashites, and the Jebusites."

55 And the day passed, and Abram offered the pieces, and the birds, and their fruit offerings, and their drink offerings, and the fire devoured them.

56 And on that day we made a covenant with Abram, according as we had covenanted with Noah in this month; and Abram renewed the festival and ordinance for himself for ever.

57 The Birth of Ishmael.

58 And Abram rejoiced, and made all these things known to Sarai his wife; and he believed that he would have seed, but she did not bear.

59 And Sarai advised her husband Abram, and said unto him: " Go in unto Hagar, my Egyptian maid: it may be that I shall build up seed unto thee by her.'

60 And Abram hearkened unto the voice of Sarai his wife, and said unto her, "Do (so)."

61 And Sarai took Hagar, her maid, the Egyptian, and gave her to Abram, her husband, to be his wife.

62 And he went in unto her, and she conceived and bare him a son, and he called his name Ishmael, in the fifth year of this week; and this was the eighty-sixth year in the life of Abram.
