Teach

Chapter 2

9 We believe the suffering of him who cannot suffer, who was born in the flesh, who cannot be born; dead, living; the Son of the Father; he was contained, who cannot be contained; who took away our sins, whereas in him there is no sin; dead, he lived;; through the sufferings of his flesh he redeemed us; and he gave us his honoured blood, the spirit of life, and the sanctification of the Spirit, and the water of our purification, even this.

10 Who giveth light within their hearts unto them that fear him; for their likeness endureth for ever; who and chose us out of all the ways of Satan, the pilot of souls;; we this;; he was, from before the world, with the Father; he saw the world in the enclosure of sin, when by the power of wisdom; he showed;;; unto death he was made to reign.

11 It pleased [him] to heal mankind; he was contained in the womb of the virgin, hidden from all the powers whose dwelling is in the heavens; and unto them that.

12 Flesh he put on, corruptible and incorruptible; for the flesh of the dead; incorruptible, which in appearance was the flesh of the dead;; the appearance, the incorruptible which is his, by a corruptible appearance was abolished.

13 And orders of righteousness by the Gospel he gave, who is the caller, that we might live; and by this Gospel the bonds of Satan were broken, that instead of death we might receive life as our portion, and instead of ignorance take understanding.

14 And he became man, the Son of the Lord; who was born of mortal man; he took what was first; to be born he was conceived as man.

15 This; and by the apostles he was preached; and by the angels he was praised; and by the prophet, all he was glorified.
