Chapter 5

1 The dead arose; Sheol he trampled; by his death, death he destroyed. After he rose on the third day, he glorified the Father, saying: O God, the Word, undefiled, like as thou who in the Spirit didst call Adam, and madest him live. As saith Moses, and likewise the prophets — we also, in him have we trusted; unto him we give glory; unto whom be glory and power, for ever and ever. Amen.
