The Prayer of Manasseh

Appendix to the Psalter

The Prayer of Manasseh is a short, heartfelt prayer of repentance, printed here as an appendix to the Psalter. Tradition credits it to Manasseh, the king of Judah remembered in 2 Kings and 2 Chronicles as an idolater who later humbled himself and prayed. The prayer itself gives words to that turning: confession without excuse, wonder at God's patience, and a plea for forgiveness. It is a single short piece of 15 verses. In this edition the text is the King James Version, Apocrypha, printed as received. It is printed as an appendix to the Psalter (KJV text, 15 verses), following Psalm 151, not counted as a separate book of the canon. Generations of Christians have loved it as a model for anyone coming home to God. Watch for its closing turn from confession to praise, as sorrow gives way to trust.
