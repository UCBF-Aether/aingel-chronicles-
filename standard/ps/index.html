THE BOOK OF PSALMS

Psalms

The Psalms are Israel's prayer book and songbook, 151 songs that teach God's people how to pray in joy, grief, anger, guilt, and praise. Most English Bibles print 150 psalms, ending with the great call for everything that breathes to praise the Lord. The Ethiopian canon, following the Greek tradition, keeps one more: Psalm 151, David's own short song about his youth and his fight with Goliath. In this edition, Psalms 1 to 150 are printed from the King James Version of 1611. Psalm 151, which the King James Version does not include, is printed from Charles Thomson's English Septuagint of 1808. The Psalter belongs at the heart of this canon, as it has belonged at the heart of worship for centuries. Watch for how honestly the psalms speak, bringing even complaint and confusion into prayer.
