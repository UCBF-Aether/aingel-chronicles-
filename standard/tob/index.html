Tobit

Tobit is a warm, homely story about two families in exile who are joined by faithfulness, prayer, and a travelling angel in disguise. Old Tobit, blinded and mocked, prays for death; far away, young Sarah prays under a similar shadow. God answers both through Tobit's son Tobias, who sets out on a journey with a companion who is really the angel Raphael. Across 14 chapters there is adventure, a wedding, healing, and quiet lessons about almsgiving, honest work, and caring for the dead. In this edition Tobit is printed from the King James Version's Apocrypha, as received. The Ethiopian Church kept this book, as did the early church more widely, for its picture of everyday righteousness under foreign rule. Watch for Raphael's gentle guidance, and how ordinary kindnesses become the road to healing.
