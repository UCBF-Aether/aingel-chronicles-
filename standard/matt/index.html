THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO SAINT MATTHEW

Matthew

Matthew is the first Gospel in this collection, told in 28 chapters. Tradition ascribes it to Matthew the tax collector turned apostle, and it presents Jesus carefully as teacher and king: a genealogy and birth story, the Sermon on the Mount, parables of the kingdom, healings and controversies, and then the cross and the empty tomb. Long teaching sections — five of them — make this the most lesson-shaped of the Gospels, ending with Jesus sending his followers to teach all nations.

In this edition Matthew is printed from the King James Version of 1611, the public-domain text as received. It belongs in this canon at the head of the New Testament, where the Ethiopian Church, like the wider church, opens the story of Jesus. Its many quotations of the older Scriptures also bind the two testaments tightly together for Ethiopian readers, who keep both in one large canon.

Watch for how often Matthew pauses to say 'this was done, that it might be fulfilled' — and what each fulfilment shows about Jesus.
