Kebra Nagast

Chapter 119

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And after our Saviour was born, these kings reigned: Bazēn; Ṣənfä ʾAsgəd; Baḥrä ʾAsgəd; Gərma Säfər; Särʿada; Kʷəlu-lä-Ṣəyon; Särgʷay; Zärʾay; Bägamay; Zan ʾAsgəd; Ṣəyon Ḥəg; Maʾalbagad; Saf ʾArʿad; ʾAgdar; ʾAbrəha and ʾAṣbəḥa, the two beloved brothers; ʾAsfəḥa; ʾArfəd and ʾAmsi, brothers; ʾArʿada; Sälʿadoba; ʾAlʿamida; Tazena; Kaleb; Gäbrä Mäsqäl; Qʷäsṭänṭinos; Bäzagar; ʾAsfaḥ; ʾArmaḥ; Žän ʾAsfəḥ; Žan Sägäd; Frē Śänay; ʾAdrəʿaz; ʾAyzur; Maʿəday; Kəlaʾudəyos; Gərma ʾAsfa; Zəməz; Dəgna Mikaʾel; Bädägəz; ʾArmaḫ; Ḥəzbä ʾAnani; Dəgnazan; ʾAnbäsa Wädəm; Dəl Näʾad.

2 And after him the kingdom was taken away, and given to a people who were not of the tribe of David, nor the people of Israel; as God saith, "I will make them jealous by that which is not a people."

3 And after him He set over them: Yəkʷəno ʾAmlak; Yagba Ṣəyon; Baḥr Sägäd; Ḥəzbä ʾArʿad; Qədmä Sägäd; Žan Sägäd; Wədəm ʾArʿad; ʿAmədä Ṣəyon.
