Trail 1: The Missing Medicine Book of Noah

STATUS: TRAIL ENDS OPEN · NO MANUSCRIPT · TWO FRAGMENTS UNPUBLISHED

After the flood, Noah receives the angels' teaching on healing, writes it down, and hands it to Shem. Then the book vanishes. The chase runs through Enoch, fragments in the Dead Sea Scrolls, Tobit, a medieval Hebrew medical book that claims descent from it, and a king who hid a copy — and stops where the evidence stops.
