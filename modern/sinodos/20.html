Sinodos

Chapter 20

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 The forty-fifth canon: concerning that the deacons ought to be subject to the bishop [much garbled] Let the deacons be gathered to the bishop, and tell him concerning the sick, that they[the priests] may visit them; for the chief of the priests — if they visit the sick let them be admonished, for they are remembered.

2 Canon forty-three: concerning the time when they desire to pray apart. Let the faithful at the time awake, their rising from sleep; beforehand let them do nothing of work, and let them wash their hands, and pray to God.

3 And after it let them go into their work [much lost] and if there is a word of teaching that is done, let them to them where there is a word of teaching — which is the word of God that strengthens souls.

4 And let them hasten to the church, where the Spirit is; and let them there.

5 Canon forty-four: concerning that they ought to partake of the; beforehand, when they go up, beforehand let them taste nothing.

6 Let all the faithful be admonished with discipline, that they partake of the mystery; beforehand let them, and nothing.

7 And if there is faith in him that is baptized, and if he has given which kills, there is none that touches him.

8 Canon forty-five: concerning them that desire to abstain from the mystery, in penitence.

9 They ought not to be cut off, nor anything, from the cup [much lost] firmly.

10 And let him not partake, except the faithful, of the mystery.

11 And let him not from it, nor anything, and let him not cast it away, for it is His body — Christ's.

12 And let all these faithful eat of it; and they that desire not, let them not to it.

13 For they ought not to be separated, nor anything, from the cup; for the cup is blessed in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

14 That you may partake of it; for it is the blood of Christ.

15 [too corrupt]

16 Let him not be cut off, and let him not from within it, nor anything; that a strange spirit enter not into you from within it; that you are not as who denied and the blood of Christ you have and you have become for mercy.

17 And you will be as one that denied Christ, if you have fallen from His body; and if from the cup, you will find condemnation.

18 Canon forty-five: concerning the deacons and the priests — they ought all to gather in prayer to the bishop, where there is; as he commanded them, according to the canon; and the deacons and priests shall not that they gather to the bishop every day, if sickness of body hinder them not.

19 And when they gather by themselves, let them teach them that are in the churches; and thus their prayer in their work.
