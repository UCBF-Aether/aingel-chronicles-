Sinodos

Chapter 3

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 The eighth commandment, the twentieth: said James: O my son, do not be a diviner by signs, and practice not augury by birds, for all this leads toward the worship of idols.

3 And observe not, and choose not from the hour, and choose not days, and do not be a star-gazer, and do not be a sorcerer; and believe not men of omens, and desire not to believe them; for all this makes you a worshipper of idols.

4 The ninth commandment: said Nathanael: O my son, do not be a liar; for falsehood leads to theft.

5 And do not be a lover of gold, and do not be a lover of vain; for all this becomes sin, and draws toward theft.

6 The tenth commandment, the tenth: said Judas: O my son, do not be a murmurer; for murmuring draws a man toward.

7 And do not be arrogant; and do not be proud; and do not be hasty to evil; for by all this comes denial.

8 Be meek; for the meek shall inherit the kingdom of heaven.

9 Be, and merciful, and a peacemaker, with a pure heart, from all evil, without sin.

10 And be, and humble, and keep yourself in trembling at the word which you have heard.

11 And exalt not yourself, and join not your soul with the proud; but with righteous and humble men. And in all that befalls you, trust in the of God; and know that there is nothing which comes to pass, nor anything, except from God.

12 The tenth commandment, the eighteenth: said Thomas: O my son — as for him that speaks to you the word of God, which has become to you a cause of life, and has given you of glory — love him as the pupil of your eye; and remember; for are the priests: twelve on the right hand, and twelve on the left.

13 And John said: Well, you have put me in remembrance, O brothers. Behold, the cups that are on the right are lifted up from of the angels, and they present them to the Lord; and they that are on the left become over all the angels. And he would have them to be priests, after the likeness of the elders who have passed the time of; and they with the bishops, and are helped in all that is done, and in love.

14 And the priests that are on the right shall rejoice, and be aided in the sacrifice, that they may be to glory; and they shall.

15 And the priests that are on the left shall perish; and they shall not, and they are rebuked with all rebuke, and commanded with all commandment.

16 The fifteenth commandment, the tenth: concerning the appointing of a reader, said James: he that appoints a reader, after.

17 Let him not be of many words, nor, nor; but let him be, good, and a lover of good.

18 Toward the house of the Christians, which is named in it.

19 And let him be, and he that reads well, and he that,; and he that gives ear.

20 Before God.
