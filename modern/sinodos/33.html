Sinodos

Chapter 33

1 Canon 24: Concerning and the works, of them that examine them: concerning their deeds with earnestness. If they be or; and if it is he that did it, let him be admonished, that there may be obedience.

2 And if he has obedience, in good manner, let him be received.

3 But if he has not obedience, let him be sent away, until he make himself worthy, that may be obedience.

4 And if he has obedience, let him be received; and if it is he that did it

5 Let them be taught to be at the pleasure of their masters, that the word of God is not blasphemed.

6 And if there is one who has a wife, or a woman who has a husband, let them be taught to commit no adultery and to abide; and if they be unmarried, let them be taught not to commit sexual immorality, but to marry according to the law.

7 And if his master be a believer, and he knew.

8 Let them be examined by law; and if they are not examined by law by law let him be sent away his master from the work of the church, for.

9 And if there is a man who has a demon upon him, let him be taught that he must be healed; let him not enter the church to partake of the mystery until he be purified; and if he is nigh to death, let them bring him in; let him receive the oblation.

10 And if an adulteress desire to come in, let her forsake this practice, or let her be sent away.

11 And if there is a man who makes idols, and he wishes to come in, let him forsake his former deeds; and if not, let him be sent away.

12 And if there is a man or a woman, and they come from any people, if they wish to enter into our law.

13 Let them forsake their former deeds; and if not, let them be sent away.

14 And if there is a man or a woman of the people of the theatre, or.

15 A, or a charioteer, or.

16 Whether: or he that contends upon the lyre, or he that plays upon the pipe, or he that teaches dancing.

17 Or he that [fights?] to death, and the theatre: let them forsake their deeds; and if not, let them be sent away.

18 Or the circus-man who performs in the circus.

19 And our sister — if she is a watcher, or a guide of the dead, or a täwänayt, or a mäʿanzərt, or a zäfanit, or a ḫalayt, or one that teaches.

20 Likewise let her forsake; and if not, let her be sent away. Or he that in the midst of evil men, and.

21 [Verse lost in the OCR; numbering skips 21.]

22 Or he that likens himself to these in deeds — let them forsake them, and and let them go in the way of the believers; and if not, let them not be received. If there is a man and if he wishes to come, let him declare.

23 And if there is a man who marries a believing woman, and does evil in his flesh, or sexual immorality, or.

24 Or he that consults spirits, or a star-gazer, or by the eye, or, or a drunkard, or whose deeds and words are evil, or who speaks.

25 Or he that his hands and his feet and writes upon him, or by divination concerning a voice heard or a man's word, that there may be upon him, or in a vision.

26 For all these are in error.

27 And it seems to them that speak concerning life and death; and let them not be received into our law until they be admonished and tried concerning their deeds, if they repent of what was theirs; and if they depart.

28 Canon 25: If there is a concubine of a man who believes not — if she kept herself to him alone, let her be received.

29 But if she was taken by another, let her not be received.

30 And a woman who was as we are.

31 And if he enter into our law, then: let him and be separated from her.

32 For he is a Christian, and let him marry according to the law.

33 As we have commanded: if he love her, let a certificate of divorce be written for her first, and afterwards let him marry [her].

34 And if she is a slave, let him marry her according to the law.

35 And if he do not so, let him be sent away.

36 And if there come a man from us and does evil, or.

37 Who believes, after the likeness of the heathen, and does like them: let him forsake, and depart; and if not, let him be sent away.

38 And whoever desires to be a believer in Christ, let him be; let them do good, and.

39 And if there is a man in whom is grace, spiritual, let him be received.

40 For this work is not in its time, but with good conscience.

41 And if a layman teaches men with good [words], let him be in his word, and in his conduct, and in his tongue; then let him teach men and let him be their teacher from God.
