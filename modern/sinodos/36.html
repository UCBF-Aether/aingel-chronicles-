Sinodos

Chapter 36

1 Canon 27: And if you are not able to go to the church because of the unbelievers — you, O bishop, celebrate the liturgy in your house, that the servants of God are not gathered with the profane.

2 For it is not the place that sanctifies the man, but the man sanctifies the place.

3 And if the profane seize the place, depart from it, for they have defiled it.

4 As the holy man sanctifies the church.

5 And if you are not able to assemble among yourselves, in the house or in the church, let each one sing praise where he is by himself, and let him read the book, and pray, if there are two.

6 And let not the believers pray with the Christians in the house, for it is not right the mysteries which the saints partake.

7 And let not the of God pray with the godless.

8 What fellowship has light with darkness? And for the believers with the — if they consort with, let them be separated and go out from the church.

9 Statute 70: Concerning the commemoration of them that slept, which shall be kept with psalm and prayer, because of Him who rose our Lord, on the third day.

10 And let them keep the memorial of the living and the dead.

11 And let them keep also at the fulness of the month, after the likeness of the ancient practice.

12 And thus the people for Moses.

13 And let them keep also at the fulness of.

14 And let them keep at the half of the year.

15 And let them keep also at the fulness of the year, after the likeness of their memorial.

16 And let them give of their goods to the poor, of them that died, as a ransom for their souls, for their memorial. And this we say concerning the servants of God alone.

17 But as for the profane: if all the world were given to the poor, it would not profit them.

18 For while he was in his life, he was an enemy to God; and as he was His enemy, when he goes out from the world there is no mercy for him, for there is no with Him.

19 And behold the man and his work.

20 And behold the man whose words and deeds are good.

21 And if you are invited, eat orderly and in the fear of God; you shall pray for them that are removed from this world. And you, O presbyters and deacons of Christ.

22 And let them not for they are under God and His only Son, our Lord Jesus Christ — the presbyters and deacons and the authorities of the church.

23 Wine but let them not drink wine to be drunken.

24 The drunkard thorns spring up in the hand of the drunkard.

25 And this which we said is not for the priests alone, but for all the Christian people, who are called by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, who said: "Woe ".

26 To whom shall it be? Is it not to them that are assiduous in wine-drinking, and to them that and go after.

27 Canon 29: Concerning them that are cast out for the faith, and them that flee from city to city for the faith, and concerning the remembrance of the word of the Lord.

28 For the spirit is willing and the flesh is weak; and he flees from place to place.

29 And let them accept the spoiling of their goods, that they may keep the name of Christ and deny Him not; assist them and give them all that they need, and fulfill the statute of God our Lord.

30 And all this we command you and let every one stand in the order which was given him.

31 And go not beyond the statute which was ordained for him.

32 It is not ours, this statute, but God's the Father's; for He said: He that receives you receives Me, and he that receives Me receives Him that sent Me.

33 And he that despises you despises Me, and he that despises Me despises [My Father] that sent Me.

34 Are there not these without soul, yet they keep the statute which was given them — the day and the night, the sun and the moon and the stars, and the winds, and the changes of the seasons?

35 And the weeks and the hours and the days are subjected to that which was ordained for them, as it was said before.

36 And He made a statute and gave them, and they pass it not.

37 And concerning the sea He said: I have set for it a statute; and I have set for it a barrier and doors, and I said to it: Reach as far as this, and pass not beyond.

38 How much more is it not proper for you to move anything which was ordained for you according to the will of God? Yet there are some who reckon this as nothing, that they confuse the orders.

39 And let them not confuse the ordinations nor take for themselves a name which was not given to them; and go not beyond.

40 And therefore they provoke God.

41 Like the children of Korah, and Uzziah the king, who took by force the priesthood without the commandment of God.

42 And the former were consumed with fire, and the latter had his forehead marked with leprosy, for they provoked God, Jesus Christ, who commanded that it be so; and they pained the heart of the Holy Spirit, and despised His testimony.

43 For them that do this deed.

44 And they that offer the oblation and the bread of blessing, until they honor in the work of the priesthood this is the likeness of the great [priesthood].

45 And we declare that we may teach them this; and from now on we speak of Moses, the servant of God, to whom God spoke face to face.

46 As He speaks with His companion, who said: I know you more than all whom God spoke [with] face to face, in signs and wonders and dreams and angels and interpretation.

47 For the priests and not the work of the law.

48 And that which He commanded the priests, let not the Levites approach but.

49 And if he transgress and go out from his service which he received, death.

50 And moreover this makes clear to us what happened to Saul, when he thought to offer sacrifice by his own will, which the prophet had not given him.

51 And Samuel the high priest brought upon him sin, and cursed him for ever.

52 And the prophet feared not because of the anointing with which he had anointed him to be king.

53 And God has made us to know the great deed, revealing what happened to Uzziah the king; and vengeance waited not because of his pride, and He made him a stranger his kingdom.
