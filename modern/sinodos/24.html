Chapter 24

1 The fifty-third canon: concerning the death of bishops, and the canon of the mourning. It is fitting that the bishops be ordained as we have set forth before for all the people together.

2 And let him ask that are among them — priests and deacons — saying: "This is he whom you have chosen to be your chief." If they say together, "Yes, we ask him,"

3 And if they all answer together and say, "Yes," thus in truth.

4 And And God the Father, and Christ His Son, and the Holy Spirit — the Spirit of righteousness to this.

5 And let him ask them three times, and say: "Do you desire this in truth and uprightness "

6 From the mouths of two let every word be established (cf. Deut 19:15; Matt 18:16).

12 And the bishops shall sit upon the thrones and they shall greet them all in the Lord, and shall read the holy books.

13 And when they have finished the reading from the Gospel, let one bishop that all of the church.

14 And let him say: "The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Spirit be with you all" (2 Cor 13:14). And let them all say, "And with your spirit."

15 And when he has finished this, let him speak.

16 Let all the people say the word of gladness.

21 Men — let men greet men.

22 And women — let women greet women.

30 And let the deacons bring water to the priests, and let them wash their hands for their souls to God. And let the deacons and let no stranger enter.

31 And let there not stand, nor any but the faithful; nor let him not stand here.

32 And you women, watch that your children here and here.

33 And that they dispute not and let in his heart; and let there not stand that which is not pleasing to God.

34 And let them stand in fear and trembling, that they may be.

35 And if by this a bishop or a priest, to be ordained.

36 And thus — this is the canon, concerning him that believes. And if he keeps it not; for he that denies Christ, and becomes an enemy of the faithful.
