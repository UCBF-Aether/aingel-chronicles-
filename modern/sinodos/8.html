Sinodos

Chapter 8

1 The twenty-fifth canon: concerning them that have believed and are chastised for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ. If he that believes be in affliction, in the affliction of confession, for the name of Christ, let them not read the hand upon him for the laying on of hands, for this is the honor of the deacon.

2 But honor belongs to the priesthood; and this is the honor of the priesthood: for him that believes, if he die, let one priest read his hand upon him; but if he that believes live [breaks off].

3 Who comes not before the rulers, and if he was not chastised in affliction [fragment].

4 And he was not shut up in the house of affliction, and entered not into tribulation, but was set apart alone for the name of his Lord; and he was not chastised, neither with light chastisement; and let all acknowledge the work of the priesthood: as he wills, let them read the hand upon him.

5 And let them make him a deacon, and let one priest present him, as we commanded before — we have spoken. And do according to that which we commanded before, as we have spoken, that he may be examined openly, with observance, and let them present him to God, as they will. Let them pray; and if there is one that is able, let him pray or the prayer of the great and the exalted; and he is approved. And if he has prayed and said "Glory" as he prays; and he is right in faith.
