Sinodos

Chapter 12

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Canon three hundred and thirty-five: concerning the laying of the hand upon the Christians.

2 And after the prayer, when his hand the Christians, let him pray and dismiss them.

3 And if there is and he hears not the word of instruction concerning conduct, and that which is seemly let them bring them before; and if there is any that let him be sent away.

4 And if there is any that makes idols, or that let them teach him that he make not idols; and if he is not willing, let him forsake the idols, let him be sent away. And if there is any that goes to the circus — let him forsake it or be sent away. And if there is any that teaches of this world

5 And it is good if he forsake it; and if there is no second deed let be granted to him.

6 And he that goes to the sacrifice of the idols — let him forsake it or be sent away; and if there is

7 He that teaches — or he that teaches killing

8 Or or the charioteer — let him forsake it or be sent away.

9 And if there is a craftsman of idols, or a keeper of idols — let him forsake it or be sent away. To the rulers he shall not be committed; but if he is committed — if he was commanded concerning killing, let him not do it; and if he forsake not, let him be sent away.
