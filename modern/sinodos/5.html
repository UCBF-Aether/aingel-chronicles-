6 And; and give to the poor; and drink not much wine; and fast concerning the hidden mystery.

7 And he commands them from the brothers, that they may give to him that has not.

8 And let them be that they may share with them in their goods.

9 And they honor all with all honor, in and in fear; and they keep themselves in all purity.

10 And there is among them he that teaches them.

11 And there is among them he that asks them; and there is among them he that them; and there is he that admonishes them, and brings them forth; and.

12 The seventeenth commandment, the fifteenth: concerning the ma'asab, said Cephas: there are appointed three ma'asabat; two of them for prayer, concerning all that is within.

13 As for the women who in sickness, that she may be lifted up well.

14 And keep yourself, and know the charge to the priests; and do not be a lover of; and do not be drunken; and pray by night.

15 And if, do.

16 The eighteenth commandment, the twentieth: concerning the deacons, that they be doers of good works, said Andrew: Let the deacons be doers of good work, by night and by day; and let them not exalt themselves above the poor and needy.

17 And.

18 And them, and he gives them from that which remains; and he commands them, that they may, that they may do that which our Lord said: 'For I was hungry; for he that a good charge without sin shall inherit.'

19 , that he may offer your oblation in your holy church, in the Holy Spirit, sins according to your commandment.

20 Your ordinance, and he looses all the bonds of iniquity, according to the authority which you gave to your apostles; and with a pure heart, while he offers to you a sweet fragrance by your Son Jesus Christ, to whom be glory and power and honor, to the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, in your holy church.

21 Now and ever, and to ages of ages, Amen.

22 And after the bishop is ordained, they all greet him with the mouth, to him that has become bishop, and to him that.

23 And the deacon brings to him the oblation, and, his hand upon the oblation, with all the saints and priests.

24 He said: ' God be with you all'; and the people say: ' with your spirit.'

25 And the bishop said: ' your hearts.'

26 And all the people say: ' to God our God.'

27 And the bishop said: ' to God.'

28 And the people say: ' and.'

29 And then the bishop said.

30 'We thank you, O God, by your beloved Son Jesus Christ.'

31 '; and you did send from heaven your Son into the womb of a virgin, who became flesh, and was conceived in the womb; and by the Holy Spirit; from a virgin he was born, that your will might be fulfilled, and your people.'

32 ' to the sick, that the sick may be.'

33 'To them that; that you may hear and keep the commandments; and do not add, and do not take, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to whom be glory and praise, to ages of ages, Amen.'
