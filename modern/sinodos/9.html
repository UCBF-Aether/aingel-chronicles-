Sinodos

Chapter 9

1 The twenty-sixth canon: concerning the death of the maʿsəb. [a clause is lost to encoding damage] let her not; but let it be done in the name; and if there is one that is dead

2 [the opening is lost] let her appoint; and if there is one that died let but if she is she shall be remembered many days. For for them that appoint to the place; and let her appoint a maʿsəb by word alone, and let her be joined with the remaining maʿsəbs; and let them not read the hand upon her, for she offers not sacrifice [corrupt].
