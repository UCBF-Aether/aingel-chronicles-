Sinodos

Chapter 29

1 Concerning that which is left of the oblations, or the eulogia which is left of the mystery, besides that which they offered.

2 The deacons shall distribute among the priests, with the knowledge of the bishop or presbyter. Four parts shall be given to the bishop, and three shall be given to the presbyter, and to the deacon.

4 And to the rest — to the subdeacon, and to the reader, and to the singers, and to the deaconess, women — one part.

5 This then is good and acceptable before God; let every one perform his ordinance. And there is none in the church that works a sign, but he that performs the good and honourable ordinance.
