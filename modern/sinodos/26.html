Sinodos

Chapter 26

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Statute 54. Concerning the ordination of presbyters and deacons; concerning subdeacons; and concerning deaconesses, women; and subdeaconesses; and female readers.

2 And you, O bishop, ordain the presbyter, and lay hand upon his head, while the presbyters and all the deacons stand by.

4 And likewise the deacons: lay hand upon them.

5 Concerning the deaconesses and and female readers, women —

6 And aforetime we have spoken concerning them.

7 Statute 55. Concerning those who confess [the faith].

8 He who confesses shall not be ordained; this is of his own will, for great honor is fitting for him, for he confessed God, and before peoples and kings.

9 And if he were called to this ministry, [as] bishop or presbyter, let him be ordained.

10 And likewise, this is the ordinance concerning him who confesses: and if he finished not, but turned back and denied, let him be deposed; for not only did he deny Christ, but he has become an enemy, as one of the unbelievers.
