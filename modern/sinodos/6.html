Sinodos

Chapter 6

1 The twenty-second commandment, the twenty-second: concerning the appointment of bishops, and the order of the right oblation. A bishop is appointed, chosen from all the people with the priests and deacons, on the Sabbath morning; and they lay their hands upon him; and the priests, while they stand, and all of them; and they pray in their hearts, and the Holy Spirit descend upon him.

2 And they pray, and all of them, while they stand, they lay their hands upon him that is appointed bishop; and they pray over him, while they say: 'O God.'

3 'You, and you gave an ordinance to the church.'

4 'By the word of your grace, which the righteous.'

5 'From Abraham, and the priesthood, and your holiness; and from before the creation of the world, you are praised.'

6 'And now, from you, the power of the Holy Spirit.'

7 'Which you gave to your beloved Son Jesus Christ; to you be glory and praise.'

8 ', O Holy Spirit, upon your servant whom you have chosen for, that he may feed your flock, and minister to you without.'

9 'While he to you, by day and by night, O God our God.'

10 The deacon said: Pray. And the bishop said: 'O God, but in Christ to you.'

11 ', and with the Holy Spirit, glory and, now and ever, and to ages of ages, Amen.'

12 The deacon said: ', bow down your heads.'

13 The bishop said: 'O God; to you your people bow down their heads; incline to them your ear, and hear their prayer; and by the power of your right hand.'

14 ', and with the Holy Spirit, glory and, now and ever, and to ages of ages, Amen.'

15 And the deacon said: '.'

16 And the bishop said: 'Holy things to the holy.'

17 And the people say: ' holy, holy, Holy Spirit.'

18 The bishop said: 'The Lord be with you all.'

19 And the people [say]: 'And with your spirit.'

20 And then they lift up their hands; and the people, concerning the salvation of their souls their sins.

21 A prayer, after, O God, and our Savior Jesus Christ.

22 'We thank you, for you.'

23 ', from the holy mystery; let it not be, nor, but, soul and flesh and spirit, and with the Holy Spirit, glory and, now and ever, and to ages of ages, Amen.'

25 And the priest said: ', after.'

26 'O God.'

27 'On the night, he took bread, and said to them: Take, eat; this is my flesh, which for the remission of sins.'

28 'And likewise the cup, he said: This is my blood, take, and drink; and when you do this.'

29 'While we remember your death and your resurrection, we offer to you this bread and this cup, while, that we may stand before you, and minister to you.'

30 ' to you, O Lord, and we beg you, that you would send your Holy Spirit.'

31 ', to them that receive of it, that they may be to holiness, and to the fulness of the Holy Spirit, and to the of the faith.'

32 ', that may praise and by your Son, our Savior Jesus Christ.'

33 ' to you, glory and power, in the holy church.'

34 'Now and ever, and to ages of ages, Amen.'

35 Concerning the oblation, he offers bread and wine likewise.

36 And likewise, and all that taste, to them that receive.

37 And the people say: ', and to ages of ages, Amen.'

38 The bishop said: ', O God, and our Savior Jesus Christ, we receive the holy mystery.'

39 The holy mystery, his flesh and his blood; and he lays his hand upon his head.

40 Concerning, a bishop, who lays his hand upon him.

41 Concerning, for he is not appointed to the priesthood, that he may do the commandment which was commanded.

42 And he is not appointed, that he may be; but let him be to the bishop.

43 And not, that, but.

44 For the bishop, who lays his hand upon him.

45 And to the priests, for, and all the priests with him.

46 And they lay their hands upon him, for the Spirit which descends upon him.

47 And the priest; and are not appointed readers deacons a bishop, and he lays his hand upon him.

48 A prayer of the ordination of deacons.

49 'O God, and by your word.'

50 ', and our Savior Jesus Christ.'

51 'Give and grace, this your servant whom you have chosen, that he may be a deacon; and he may offer, to the glory of your name; and he may praise you, and our Savior Jesus Christ your angels upon them without, by your.'

52 ' to you, and with the Holy Spirit, glory and, now and ever, and to ages of ages, Amen.'

54 And the bishop said: 'The Lord be with you.'

55 And the people [say]: 'And with your spirit.'

56 And the deacon said: 'Depart in peace.'

57 And after the liturgy is ended.
