Sinodos

Chapter 1

1 The fourth commandment, the second: said Peter: You shall not kill a soul; and you shall not commit sexual immorality; and you shall not corrupt a young child.

2 You shall not steal; and do not be a soothsayer; and do not be a star-gazer; and do not be a sorcerer.

3 And you shall not kill the tiny babe in the womb; after it is born, you shall not kill it.

4 And you shall not covet your neighbor's goods, nor anything; and you shall not withhold mercy from man; and do not be a false witness.

5 And do not be an evil speaker against any man.

6 And do not be hasty to do evil; and do not be double of heart, and double of tongue; for the double-tongued is a snare of death.

7 And let not your word be vain; and do not be a liar.

8 And do not be contentious; and do not be a lover of much property; and do not be proud; and; and do not be, and do not be evil of heart.

9 And do not be proud; and do not take evil counsel against your neighbor; and do not withhold mercy from man; but rebuke: and hate some, and deliver some, and love some from your soul exceedingly.

10 The fifth commandment, the fifth: said Andrew: O my son, flee from all evil, and hate all evil; and swear no oath; for an oath leads into murder.

13 And do not be envious, and do not be, and do not be a lover of murder; for this corrupts and brings forth evil.
