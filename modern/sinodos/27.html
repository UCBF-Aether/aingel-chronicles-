Sinodos

Chapter 27

1 Statute 56. Concerning virgins and widows. And he shall not lay hand upon virgins, for there is no commandment concerning it from God.

2 For this work is of the nature of man, and it is not that she despises marriage, but that she may have time for the service of God. And concerning widows: he shall not lay hand upon a widow; but if she is a widow whose husband died many years ago, and if her widowhood be of long time —

3 And she lived diligently, and if no accusation be found against her, and if she was able to keep in good order the men of her house as is fitting, like Judith, and if she loved all good things like Judith — then let her be ordained into the order of widows.

4 And if her husband died but a short time ago, and it was not long — let them not trust her, and let her not be ordained into the order of widows; but let her remain a long time in good works, in fasting and prayer, keeping herself apart, that she may subdue herself with patience. For horse and mule are not controlled save by a strong bridle.

6 Concerning male virgins: they shall not ordain male virgins, for this work is from the will of the heart alone — it being his single state by the grace of God and by the gift of Christ Jesus.

7 For when the Holy Spirit abides upon a man, he obtains grace and healing, being known by the grace which is in him, which shall be known to all men.

8 And if he were called to this work, that he should be ordained — whether bishop, presbyter, or deacon — they shall lay hand upon them.

9 Statute 57. Concerning: it is seemly that the bishop who is with you be ordained by bishops — or by two.

10 And if one bishop alone laid hand upon him, let him be deposed — both he and he that ordained him.

11 And if it were by reason of the violence of men —

12 — the bishop being alone, if he was not able to gather an assembly by reason of the violence of men, and not through pride and unbelief —

13 And if it were thus, let him receive authority from many bishops; and this shall be fitting for him, and it shall be by their commandment; and neither he nor he that ordained him shall be deposed.
