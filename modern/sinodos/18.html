Sinodos

Chapter 18

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [canon 43?] concerning.

2 Let the deacons be appointed to the bishop; and all of the deacons, let them be appointed to the bishop; and let him admonish them concerning the sick, that they visit them; for the chief of priests, if he visits the sick let them be admonished, for they are remembered.

3 [canon 43?] concerning the time when they desire apart prayer: let the faithful at the time rise, from sleep, before they do anything of work, and wash their hands, and pray to God.

4 And afterwards let them go to which flows from the holy church, from that which we have received.

5 With your washing which is sanctified from you.

6 O my God, bless, blessing, and sanctify, sanctification, for those receive for the communion which is not; let mercy be in Your fear and in Your patience, and for of the body; for glorified is Your glorious name in all, to ages of ages. Amen.

7 And give from His body, and say: This is the body of Christ; and he that receives shall say Amen.

8 And give the cup, and say: This is the blood of Christ; and let him that receives say Amen, and Amen.

9 And thus your hand upon their heads, and say: I bless you, O my God, for You have divided to Your servants and to Your handmaids for the washing.

10 Of the second birth, for the remission of sins, the grace of the Holy Spirit, His body and His blood, of Christ.

11 We beg and implore You: Your servants and Your handmaids, by the power of Your angels; and be to them a guardian in Your Godhead which is in heaven; may they find.

12 Through our Lord Jesus Christ, to whom be with Him and with the Holy Spirit.

13 Glory now and ever and to ages of ages. Amen.

14 Finished is the order of baptism.
