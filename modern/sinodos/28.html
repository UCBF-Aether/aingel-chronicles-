Sinodos

Chapter 28

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Statute 58. The bishop shall bless, and they shall not bless him; and he shall bless the firstfruits himself; and he shall ordain men; and he shall offer the oblation; and he shall administer the eulogia; and from a bishop he shall receive — never from the presbyters at all. The bishop shall depose all the ordained whom it is right to depose, except a bishop, for he alone is not able.

2 The presbyter shall bless and shall be blessed; and he shall take the blessing from the bishop and from a presbyter like himself; and he ministers to a presbyter like himself. He shall ordain, but shall not depose. Moreover, let him reprove those who have sinned.

3 If they be in such a case.

4 The deacon shall not bless, nor give the blessing; he shall take the blessing from the bishop or from the presbyter.

5 He shall not baptize nor offer; but when the bishop has offered, or if the presbyter is not there, he himself shall minister to the people — not as one of the priests, but as a minister of the priests.

6 Statute 59. And afterward: the ordained presbyters shall not do the work of the deacons, nor anything which is not theirs.

7 The deaconesses, women, shall not bless, and shall do nothing of what the presbyters and deacons do.

8 But they shall keep the gates of the church; apart from this there is nothing at all else which they do.

9 Except the ministry to the presbyters, and the baptizing of women — for this is fitting for them. And the deacon shall reprove the subdeacon, and the reader, and the singers, and the deaconess.

10 Thus indeed it is fitting, if the presbyter is not present. But for the subdeacon it is not fitting to reprove, neither the readers, nor the ministers of the Scripture, nor the deaconess, women.

11 Neither those in orders nor the people — for they are the servants of the deacons.

12 Statute 60. Concerning firstfruits and tithes.

13 And all the firstfruits and the tithes they shall bring to the bishops, and to the presbyters, and to the deacons, for their sustenance; but all the tithes they shall bring for the sustenance of the rest of the ordained that remain, and for the virgins, and for the widows, and for them that are afflicted by poverty, for the firstfruits are for the priests and for their ministers.
