54 But if a brother from abroad, or a sister, come — let the deacon examine them; and if [they bring] an offering, let him receive it and seat them in their places.

55 And the priests — in their places let them sit.

56 But if another priest come from abroad, let him be received into his own honoured place.

57 But if it is otherwise, and if others come after the manner of the world — whether men of the city or a brother from abroad — you, O priest, when you are seated, speak the word of God.

58 But if not, when you hear or when you read, show no partiality, and forsake not your charge as you were commanded concerning them. [As for] places — but sit diligently, for brothers are to be received.

61 But if likewise, while the elders sit, [the others] having risen up, let them leave their places.

62 You, O deacon — have you observed among them that sit a young man or a virgin? Having risen up, cause that one to sit.

63 Or if you that others may be instructed and yield a place to them that are greater than you the great house; as you do to the poor.

64 Or if one from a foreign land, or one from abroad, come, and they have no lodging: you, O priest, make a place for them — for these likewise — with all your heart. And upon earth let there be no respect of persons, but to God.

65 And to him likewise who our brother God for these.

66 While the holy church [is] within this, and and is not hidden — this is the matter concerning [it].

67 The likeness of them that stayed [in it?] and they shall be shaken; concerning alien spirits — and God them. What men are they, to whom prophecy [came?] or wonders were done?

68 And now this matter leads us to that which is fitting for the order of the church. As for you, O bishops — we have entrusted you and sent you forth from us, by the commandment of our Lord Jesus.

70 Therefore we have made known to you this canon from us, that you may do all and forsake not anything, as our Lord Jesus Christ commanded, according to the canon which He gave to you.

71 And know that he that hears from us, he hears from Christ; he [that hears from Christ] hears from God the Father, to whom be glory for ever and ever; amen.
