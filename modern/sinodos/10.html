Sinodos

Chapter 10

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 The thirty-fifth canon: concerning the putting away [of a wife]. As for him that has put [her] away — judge him as he wills; a man may put away his kept one.

2 And if a man have a wife, and she have not borne children [much lost] let her be kept; but if he take another wife, let her be sent away.

3 And the man that has a kept [wife] — let him put her away, or let him keep her according to the law; and if he is not willing, let him put her away, let him send her away.

4 And if there is any that has put [her] away, judge him as he wills; for the Spirit God [fragment].

5 [canon number lost] concerning the time in which they hear the teaching [much lost] while they hear the word of instruction; and if there is a catechumen, and he knows good works, let days not be granted to him, but his work alone, which is manifest concerning him.
