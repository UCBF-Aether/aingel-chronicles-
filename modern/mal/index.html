Malachi

Malachi is the last voice among the Twelve Prophets, a book of 4 chapters shaped as a series of arguments. Again and again God makes a claim — I have loved you, I deserve honour, I remember what is written — and the people answer back: how? how have we done that? Through those exchanges the book addresses tired worship, careless offerings, broken promises in marriage, and withholding from God, and it ends with a promise: a messenger will come, and Elijah will be sent before the great day.

In this edition Malachi is printed from the King James Version of 1611, the public-domain text as received. In this canon it closes the prophetic books of the Old Testament, forming a natural bridge toward the New Testament that follows in the wider collection. The Ethiopian Church keeps it in that closing place, among the Twelve.

Watch for the book of remembrance in chapter 3 — God keeping a record of those who spoke of him with reverence.
