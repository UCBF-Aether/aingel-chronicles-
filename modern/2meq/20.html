2 Meqabyan

Chapter 20

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Believe that there shall not be left and there shall not be hidden all our works which we did upon the earth, when we shall stand before Him in fear and trembling.

2 And if there is no provision for our way, and if we have no garment for our nakedness.

3 And if we have no for our hands, and for our feet.

4 And we do not know the way where they shall take us — whether, and whether, and whether darkness, and whether; and in the midst, whether the sea, and Sheol.

5 And those who shall take us — we do not know them, and we do not hear their speech.

6 And we do not see their faces; for darkness are they, and in darkness they shall lead us.

7 As David the prophet said, when upon me: You, O my Lord, know my way; in this way I walked, they hid for me a snare; and I the way of my right, and I saw and I that which knows me; and where I shall escape I have not.

8 And this, when he said, for he knows that the angels shall him, and shall lead him where he does not do know the way; and he shall right and left, and there is none that shall find that which knows him.

9 And for him there is none that knows, but by himself in the midst of the angels of darkness.

10 And the spiritual angels of light who were sent to the latter, that they should their souls for the righteous, and that they should to the of the light of life.

11 And those who shall be sent — the angels of darkness and the angels to the souls of the sinners, that they should receive them and take them into the judgment which is forever, which [is] meet for them, that they should be judged as their works which they did.

12 Woe to them, for the souls of the sinners whom they shall take into; and no rest, and no, and no escape, and no forever, from that which found them.

13 Woe to them, for the sinners; for in the way of Cain they walked, and in the counsel of the violence of Balaam they were; and the sin which they do, for in the they went down, they that they should take a strange, in violence which was not their.

14 They shall be judged as their works which they did, in the judgment of fire.
