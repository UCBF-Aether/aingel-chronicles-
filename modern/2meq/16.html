2 Meqabyan

Chapter 16

1 Behold you — for yourself — that which is in your flesh, in the labor of your hands and your feet, and the of your head; for in their time you shall them, and and; and in this know, and if you have understanding and knowledge, to faith.

2 And from where goes forth, you shall say: These [are] the labors of your hands and your feet and the of your head — it is God who made them, that they should; that you should know, you, the resurrection of the dead which shall be — that your flesh in flesh another, that you should know for yourself that you shall rise from the dead.

3 And you shall be judged, you, as your works which you have done — transgression and sin — in the resurrection of the dead; because you caused to err the people, when you said: There is no resurrection of the dead.

4 And now you see, when there comes to you the day of your judgment; for you that sowed shall not that there should be eaten the fruit and the.

5 But you that planted a tree shall not in the fruit the tree of the vine, and the tree of the fig — there shall not be changed its and its fruit.

6 And if you plant the vine, there shall not be changed that it should become the fig; if you plant the fig, there shall not be changed that it should become the vine; and if you sow, there shall not be changed that it should become.

7 And if you sow, there shall not be changed that it should become; all these — in their seed, and in their, and in their fruit, and in their tree, and in their, and in their — shall receive blessing, in the dew of mercy which [is] from God, and shall bring forth fruit.

8 Likewise the earth shall bring forth, in that which God sowed — there shall eat soul and flesh, the doers of good, while they shall not be changed by the evil doers; and the evil doers, while they shall not be changed by the doers of good — these whom God sowed shall eat soul and flesh.

9 And as there comes his time for the blowing of the trumpet, the dead shall rise, in the dew of mercy which [is] from God — they who did good, in the resurrection of life; and their [shall be] the garden of rejoicing, a dwelling for the pure, which He prepared for the latter, where there is no sickness and no toil, where they shall not die there again.

10 But as for the evil doers, in the resurrection of judgment, with the devil who deceived them.

11 And his evil angels who do not desire that they should be saved from all the children of men, in the judgment which is forever.

12 Cast down beneath, in the of darkness, where [there is] weeping and; where there is no and no mercy, and where there is no escape forever; for they did not good in their life upon the earth, while they were in their flesh.

13 And because of this they shall be in the resurrection of soul and flesh.

14 For others, for them who did not believe the resurrection of soul and flesh together — you likewise — God shall show the greatness of His knowledge.

15 And He shall judge all — [TRUNCATED in the source; the verse breaks off mid-word.]

16 As his work, and as the of his hand.
