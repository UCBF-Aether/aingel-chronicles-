Chapter 14

1 For me, it grievs me exceedingly, and it me, and my heart me, because of the Jews and Samaritans and Pharisees and Sadducees, who do not believe the resurrection of the dead. The Jews say: Let us eat and drink — dust we shall die, and there there is none who sees.

2 But the Samaritans say: Our flesh shall not rise, for dust it shall become.

3 But if there be a resurrection — a resurrection of our souls we believe; for the soul, when the flesh dies, does not do dy; for as the wind is it, which is not seen, and as the sound of thunder — and they do not say and it is not shown.

4 But our flesh is seen in all, and becomes ashes and dust; for the beasts eat it, and eat it in the grave.

5 And they who eat it shall become ashes, and there shall not be found; for as they shall become, and as that which is not created they became ashes.

6 And in this shall be found the flesh which; and thus say the Pharisees: The resurrection of the dead we believe, but He shall clothe the souls in the heavens [with] another flesh, which is not that which [is] on earth.

7 And thus say the Sadducees: When our soul goes forth from our flesh, we shall not rise from the dead; and there is not among them a resurrection of the dead, neither for soul nor for flesh; and when we die, we shall not rise.

8 And because of this they exceedingly, and it me because of them; for they speak against God and His praise.

9 And they have no hope of salvation; for they did not believe God, who justifies them; and only hope have they not of salvation and the resurrection of the dead.

16 But your flesh — and sevenfold, as the flesh of our father Adam — you shall rise the mercy of God; and there you shall be judged as you have done transgression and sin, as you have not believed.

18 And who shall leave you in the ashes and the earth? He shall raise you, that He may judge you as your deeds which you have done.

19 And there shall come a time which [is] in the winds — your spirit; and a time which [is] in the earth — your dust; and a time which [is] in the fire — your flame.

20 And a time — the spirit of life which breathed upon you, in the of darkness.

21 Which — but the souls of the righteous in the Garden with rejoicing shall be.

22 But you, O Jew and Samaritan and Pharisee and Sadducee — until the time of your judgment you shall in darkness.

23 And then you shall see how God shall judge you as your deeds, because you have your.

24 And you shall say to them: The dead shall not live — let us eat and drink, for dust we shall die; and you shall say while you sit upon the seat of Moses: There is no resurrection of the dead.

28 But you — you have no to escape from the hand of Him who you; and there is He shall judge you as your deeds; for you and they whom you have together shall be gathered.
