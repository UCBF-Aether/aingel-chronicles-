2 Meqabyan

Chapter 9

1 And you, O man — and you are not God — why are you proud? [You are] a man of today, and dust of the earth and ashes; and you shall become wood and in your grave.

2 And to Sheol you shall be rendered; and to those who do your deed; for your teacher Seblianos, who upon his head the sin of all the world, because he destroyed our father Adam in his pride.

3 For he refused to bow down to the work of his God, in the greatness of his head and in the of his.

4 And you, as your teacher, have refused to to God your God.

5 And you, walk as walked your predecessors who did not know God their God — kingdoms.

6 And they were avenged and went down into Sheol, because of their evil deeds which they did upon the earth.

7 And you likewise shall go down into Gehenna.

8 It seems to you that you shall escape from the hand of God; for and and [is] the worship of God who gave you dominion over

9 And He has tried you, whether you do His will or not; and if you make straight your way upon the earth, God shall your way, and all which your hand brings He shall, and bless you; and all your enemies and your foes He shall to you.

10 And you shall rejoice in your and in your and in the fruit of your and in your storehouse and in your pasture; and wherever you with your fingers, and in your heart — all shall be commanded to you; for from God it is to you, that thus you should do and plant and and.

11 And if you refuse to hear His voice and His law — O God — and do not walk in His judgment, you shall have no to escape from the hand of God, like all the rebellious who [were] before you, who did not worship God in purity, and did not trust in His upright judgment; for righteousness [is] all His judgment.

12 And there is none who is hidden from before His eyes; and all [is] naked and before Him.

13 Now the dominion of kings is His, and the of the dwelling of the mighty is His.

14 And the exalter of the humble is He, and the raiser of the fallen is He.

15 And the opener of the bound is He, and the raiser of the dead is He; and as for those who their flesh, and become as dust, He shall them in the time He wills; for a shadow of mercy is He from Him.

16 And as for those who did evil, He shall raise them — a resurrection of judgment; for they spurned [Him], and He shall avenge them.

17 And upon the earth He shall their seed; for the rebellious are they — against the law and the ordinance of God.

18 And the sinners shall not desire to in their counsel to the righteous; for narrow [is] their to the righteous, more than the ways of the sinners.

19 And as the heaven is high above the earth, so far [are] the ways of the righteous from the ways of the sinners.

20 But the ways of the sinners [are] the doing of darkness and təʿəgələt and violence and fornication and deceit and səsəʿət; and he his property and in violence.

21 And in the of blood, and in that which does not do profit; and for death, and the eating of dead flesh and blood, and the eating of the flesh of and camel and; in a woman and which [is] without purity.

22 And all these [are] the deeds of the sinners — a broad way, and a of the of Satan, which leads into darkness and the being which [is] forever.

23 But the ways of the righteous [are] and, which lead into life: humility and, love and peace, fasting and prayer and of the flesh, and keeping from the eating which does not do profit: dead flesh and and fornication, and woman to woman.

24 And they keep themselves from all deed, and from impure foods which are not commanded in the Law, and from deed which God does not do love; for all this do the sinners.

25 But the righteous shall be hidden from all evil which God does not do love.

26 And He shall love them, and comfort them, and deliver them from all their tribulations.

27 But as for the sinners, Satan shall them; for they keep his law and his ordinance, and all which he loves.
