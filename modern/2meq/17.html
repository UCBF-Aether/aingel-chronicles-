2 Meqabyan

Chapter 17

1 And the grain of, if it does not do dy, is not eaten and does not do bear [fruit]; for if it dies, the grain of makes root in the earth, and brings forth stalk, and becomes, and bears [fruit] — and know.

2 For you, that there should be many sins — one grain of.

3 And likewise this grain is eaten of wind and water and earth; and the sun the fire; for that which [is] without the sun cannot bear the fruit of.

4 And the wind; and that which [is] without wind cannot bear the fruit of; and the water which waters and the earth.

5 And as the moist ashes and earth makes root, and upward, and bears [fruit] in the measure of the blessing of God.

6 For the grain of [is] in the likeness of Adam, upon whom the Spirit of God breathed —; and the tree of the vine likewise is watered with water, and makes root, and the water ascends the fine root.

7 And it ascends to heaven, in the city; for the dew of mercy from God shall them, in the city; in the flame of the sun, and it makes fruit which [is] from God.

8 And it makes a fine which gladdens the heart; and when they eat it, it satisfies as water and bread which does not do hunger and does not do thirst; and when it, it makes blood.

9 And when they drink it, it gladdens the heart of man, as is said in the Psalm: Wine gladdens the heart of man; and it makes drunk, when his mouth, and and in his, and his blood in his heart.

10 And it him that [is] without understanding; for the drunkenness of wine exceedingly; and it him for and for, as the wide earth, and he does not do know; and that [is] in his hands and his feet.

11 And thus God made the tree of the vine, and its fruit, that His name should be for those who do His will, and for those who believe the resurrection of the dead.

12 And He shall gladden them in the remained of life, for those who believe the resurrection of the dead.
