2 Meqabyan

Chapter 5

1 And [there] departed the children of the young generation, and they erred as their fathers had done; and they kept the ordinance of their house, and all which is theirs they held fast, and they did not poverty and death and.

2 And they feared God, and gave their property to the poor, and comforted themselves in all [the ways] their fathers had; and He comforted in their affliction; and He raised them up; and when He struck them from the hand of their oppressors, and comforted them in all the affliction and sorrow which befell them.

3 And they dwelt while thus they did years.

4 And after him came Sirusaydan, king of the Chaldeans, and destroyed all their cities, and led captive the children of Meqabis, and destroyed all their land.

5 And he took away all their property, and he seized them, and brought them into his land, filled with idols which do not go by the commandment, and with his being like to God — but in all evil and fornication and impurity and and, and they did not remember their God.

6 And they eat all dead flesh and blood, and the eating of beasts and — and all which God does not do love; and there is no law from all the commandments of righteousness which are written in the Orit.

7 And they do not know Him — God — that He is their Maker and their Savior, who brought them out from under them, and led them by that which He desired.

8 And there is no law among them in judgment — who go in darkness and and fornication and impurity, while they take the wives of their fathers and their sisters; and they do all evil, and take their brothers and their kinsfolk, and there is no law among them.

9 And all their ways [are] narrow and darkness and impurity and fornication.

10 But the children of Meqabis are kept in all which is theirs [as] devoted; and they do not eat dead flesh and, and all their impurity and their sins [are] upon the children of the Chaldeans; for evil and many are their deeds which are not written in this book — which the children of the Arabians do: sinners and the rebellious, hypocrites and the deceitful, and deceitful ṣiʿ, and impurity and təʿgələt.

11 And there is none over them — all — who loves God their Maker.

12 And again they worship idols whose name is Baal Peor, and worship him, and believe in him as God their Maker; for deaf and dumb is he, and there is none who sees and who hears, for a graven image, the work of the hand of man, is he — which the craftsman forged of brass and gold and silver, the work of the children of men, who have no knowledge and no breath.

13 And he does not do eat, and does not do drink.

14 And he does not do kill, and does not do give life.

15 And he does not do plant, and does not do strike.

16 He does not do think, and not.

17 He not, and not.

18 He does not do chastise, and does not do show mercy — but affliction is for the destruction of the perishing people of the Chaldeans.
