2 Meqabyan

Chapter 13

1 But they

2 And they committed their souls to the fire, that they might be delivered from the fire which [is] in the heavens; they knew that this my world passes away.

3 And he said: and there does not do dwell for; for they knew that the of God [is] more than the of the children of men, and the wrath of God [is] more than the wrath of the king.

4 And better [is] one day of rejoicing in the of the Garden, than a thousand years of life upon the earth; and for time, better [is] one hour of — this [is] yours, O my God.

5 The Meqabyan, the children of Meqabis, who believed and committed their souls to death — they refused the sacrifice of idols.

6 And what [is] the time of man? They have become as a shadow which passes away, and as which [is] before the face of fire.

7 Their hands [are] good, which — who together committed their souls to death; they knew that their life from this earthly passing [world].

8 But You, O my God, forever shall you remain, and Your years which do not perish, and Your memorial to generation and generation.

9 And all this, and that which seems the children of Meqabis; and and they trusted in God, and refused the eating of the impure sacrifice.

10 And because of this they gave their flesh to for God; they knew that the dead shall rise, and they knew that there shall be judgment after the resurrection.

11 But those who did not know and did not believe the resurrection of the dead — they knew from

12 And they saw that all passes away, and they committed their souls to death, that from God they might find because they believed and did not bow down to idols, and did not eat the sacrifice of the dead and impure.

13 And because of this they knew that He shall make them rejoice in the latter day, in soul and in flesh; for while with them [were] wife and children, they did not know the taste of this world, and to death; and they committed their souls to death; they knew that there shall be resurrection in the latter day, in soul and in flesh.

14 And they knew that they shall reign — those who kept His ordinance — O God — in the world to come, to generation and generation; and for time, in which there is no sickness and no sorrow.

15 there is no sickness and no sorrow, and there is no death; and like those who believed in the word of God — kings and rulers — and they committed their souls to death, as in the midst of fire, while they saw in the of their heart that which [is] to come.

16 And they shall with that which their faces, sevenfold more than the sun; and as they shall rejoice in His love — this — in the resurrection of all, in soul and in your flesh.
