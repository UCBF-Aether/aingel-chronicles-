2 Meqabyan

Chapter 19

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 O earth, which have gathered to you kings and rulers and great ones and honored ones, and women — and fine virgins.

2 And for the strong ones, in whom [there is] and fine; and in whom [there is] knowledge and understanding; and in whom [there is] a fine word — as the word of the organ, and as the of the harp and the lyre.

3 And in whom [there is] a fine song, they who gladden as the wine of Adam; their eyes bright as the star of morning.

4 And their right hand strong, as that which gives and and; and their feet fine to behold; and for those who run as the righteous.

5 I know you, O death, that you have taken their souls, for the fine; for by the will of the good God you have been sent.

6 And you, O earth, for you have gathered many bodies, which from you God brought forth and caused to enter to you — from you, and, and to you we entered; by the will of God we rejoiced; by the will of God, upon you.

7 And you became a burying for our; we upon you, and we in your; we ate your fruit, and upon you our flesh.

8 We drank of the dew of your, and you drank of the of our blood; we ate of the of your earth, and upon you of the of our bones.

9 We ate of the taste of ashes; your dew good, as God commanded you for our; and you took our flesh, and you did, as God commanded you for your.

10 O, from you, O death, that you have gathered many strong kings and rulers; and you did not fear their might and their greatness, as He commanded you who created them; and you did not the poor.

11 And you did them [that are] fine [in] their; and you did not leave the strong and the mighty; and you did not leave and; the and the fine — the, and the children, the and the women.

12 And you did not leave them [that are] fine [who] walk; and they did not transgress His commandment; and you did not leave them [that are] evil [who] walk. They who became as the beasts in their works; and they that [are] perfect in the of their and their word, and the sweetness of their speech.

13 And you did not leave them whose word [is] bitter, and [whose] mouth [is] full of cursing; and you have gathered their souls in your treasuries — that [is] of light, and that [is] of darkness.

14 And the earth gathered their flesh — in the, and in the earth — until the blowing of the trumpet, when the dead shall rise.

15 And they shall be judged, the doers, until as their works which they did; for at the blowing of the trumpet, and at the commandment of God, the dead shall rise, as the twinkling of an eye; and then they shall rejoice, they who did good.
