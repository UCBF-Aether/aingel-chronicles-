2 Meqabyan

Chapter 3

1 And the prophet spoke; for Remat of Moab was near to Syria.

2 And he dug the ground and went down to his neck; and he wept a bitter weeping, and repented because of the sin which he had done before God.

3 And thus said the Lord to the prophet: "Enter into the land of Judah, to the land of Remat, to Meqabis the prince of Moab; and say to him: Thus said the Lord: For it was not you that did this thing, that you should swallow [it] up by the strength of my might and by my; you destroyed the holy city — but I, even I, am God.; of my own will I sent you, that you should destroy my city."

4 "For from my with all and and.

5 "And I into your hand; and not by yourself — which you exalted your neck; and you said: 'I the city by the strength of my hand.' And now, because of your children whom you have begotten, God shall be gracious as for your sins."

6 "And now repent with all your heart, and; and do not be slack; for those who are slack do not keep to repentance."

7 "And blessed are they who repent with all their heart, and do not turn back to filth and sin, in everything in which they repent, because of their evil.

8 "And let them keep with weeping and with tears, and with much supplication, and with prostration to God; for He said to the repentant: 'You — after your error you repented, you.'"

9 "And to he said to him: 'After the error, when he returns to Him in repentance, because of your affliction and your fear, I will forgive your sins; for I am the Lord, a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children to the third and to the fourth generation; but to those who love me and keep my commandments I show mercy with them to [a thousand?] generations.'"

10 "And now, concerning these your children whom you have begotten, I will keep my covenant which is with you, and the repentance which I have made because of the evil of your deeds, said the Lord, the Holy One of Israel,.

11 And at that time he went forth from and bowed down to the prophet, while he said thus — and thus: "May God cause me to inherit — that it do not depart from you — but cause me to inherit that which you command; for I have spurned God, and have not walked in His commandment as my fathers; for we have no law, but it was shown to us that we should worship idols, as our fathers taught us."

12 "But as for me, I did not listen to the voice of His prophets of God until now; for I have sinned — I who walked in my sins, and in the pride of my heart, and in the lifting up of my neck — in which I spurned the commandment of God, and did not walk in His statutes, and him which he commanded me.

13 And the prophet spoke again and said to him: "I know that now your repentance is accepted; for there is none before you who shall be reckoned for his sin of your kindred

14 "And from this time forsake the worship of idols, and turn into the counsel of God, that your repentance may be in truth." And he bowed down at his feet to the prophet; and the prophet raised him up, and commanded him all fitting good deeds.

15 And he did as God commanded him, and returned into his house.

16 But Meqabis strengthened his soul, and removed from his house all, and the idols, and, and, and.

17 And the children of the ṣiwani, whom he brought from Jerusalem, he taught morning and midday — all the commandment of God, and His law, and His statutes — as their fathers did [them].

18 And he set, from among the children, Ṣewa — from among the wise — in his house.

19 And again, from the young men — from among their wise men — he set in his, who keep the young men, that they might teach them the way of God, as the children of Israel did: His law and His statutes, and, and, and all their which the Moabites; let him hear — from among the children, Ṣewa.

20 And he humbled their, and all their sägälat, and all their idols, and all their, and their — from their, from, and from which cries to his gods morning and noon.

21 And he cried to them, and worshipped them with all, and asked them, and trusted them; and he did their will in all which the ministers of his gods spoke to him.

22 And he did not depart from their words in all which he spoke to him; for it seemed in all which him.

23 But Meqabis — as for those — forsook this way.

24 And after the seer heard the word which the prophet spoke to him, he made his way straight in repentance; and all his house made their ways straight, after the manner of the children of Israel, at that time. For as for the children of Israel — at one time they spurn Him, and at [another] time He chastises them them, and they cry to God.

25 And when He saw them, that they were afflicted beneath their enemies, [and] to those who oppress them they cry before Him — then the Prince them, remembering the covenant of their fathers, when they cry to Him for the sake of Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, their fathers.

26 But He delivered them, [yet] they forget Him who delivered them; and they return to the worship of idols.

27 And the Prince sends among them the nations who oppress them; and when they oppressed them and grieved them, they cry to God; and at that time He them and them, for He loves them — for.

28 And again, when they have rest, they return, that they might spurn Him with the worship of idols which them; and their evil.

29 But He raises up against them the people of Elofəli, and Moab, and Midian, and Syria, and Gebṣ; and they weep and cry out. When their oppressors, their enemies, [oppress?] them and grieve them and them and them, then He raises up for them judges, that the judges might deliver them.
