THE SECOND BOOK OF MEQABYAN

2 Meqabyan

2 Meqabyan continues the Ethiopian Meqabyan witness, and like its companions it is not a Maccabees book at all, but a separate work the Ethiopian Church preserved when others did not. In 21 chapters it returns to a time of persecution, telling of believers, families, and companions who refuse to worship idols and who encourage one another to stand firm. There are accounts of suffering and of comfort, strong warnings against compromise, and tender scenes of people strengthening one another for what lies ahead. For this edition, 2 Meqabyan was translated anew, directly from the Ge'ez text. It belongs in this canon because the Ethiopian tradition kept the whole set of three Meqabyan books together, as a school of endurance for hard times. Watch for the way sufferers speak to one another here, turning private fear into shared courage.
