2 Meqabyan

Chapter 7

1 Woe to you and those like you who do not know God who created you, and do not know His commandment and His word; for you have exchanged repentance for that in which there is no profit — when you were chastised by the of Sheol.

2 And you shall have no escape from now on forever — you and your gods and those whom you make to sacrifice and to bow down to them as God your Maker; for they have no soul and no breath, and they do not profit them that to them, and not them that to them.

3 For the work of the hand of man are they, in whom Satan speaks for the destruction of the heart of such as you, O perishing ones — that he may bring you down into Gehenna; [there shall be] fire for you and for the ministers of your gods, and for those who you — demons, to you.

4 For you do not know that there is none who shall profit you: təgegəyu and you err.

5 And better than you are the beasts which God created for your food — and and; for they have no second judgment besides one death.

6 But you shall die, and be gathered again in the of Gehenna, in which there is no escape forever.

7 And when they had said this, they departed and were hidden from him.

8 But Sirusaydan remained in great fear and trembling, and did not depart until the morning.

9 And from there he went to the of their corpses — O righteous ones — and did not find them; for He desired to with them, and them; for God kept them, that they should not be from their flesh.
