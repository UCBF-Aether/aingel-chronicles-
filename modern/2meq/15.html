2 Meqabyan

Chapter 15

1 But as for them who have heard in their good works — when they rejoice; but as for them who were reckoned, when they say: The dead shall not rise — when they grieve, when they see how there rose from the dead, with their evil works, which is not profitable.

2 And there shall be their works which they did, while there is none who accuses them — for themselves they know their heads, how they shall be.

3 And they shall stand at the station of the wicked, in the day of weeping and of judgment, in the day of judgment, and in the day of God.

4 In the day of darkness, in the day of distress, in the day of, in the day of, in the day of lightning.

5 In the day of fear and trembling, in the day of and, in the day of.

6 In the day when the evil shall be judged as he did evil, and in the [day] in which the pure shall be judged as he did purity, and in the [day] in which the oppressed shall be judged as he did.

7 And in the [day] in which the Lord shall not be honored for [His] work, and in the [day] in which the congregation shall not be honored for truth.

8 And in the [day] in which the king shall not be honored for the poor, and the ruler for the child, and the father for his son, and the for his daughter.

9 And in the [day] in which the master shall not be honored above the servant, nor the proud above the humble, and in the [day] in which the great shall not be honored above the small; for it is the day of righteousness, and the day of requital and of judgment; and on that day He shall requite all according to what he did.

10 And on that day when the doers of good [shall do good], and in the [day] in which the evil shall be judged, [even] they who did evil.

11 And on that day when they shall rejoice, they who did good; and on that day when they shall grieve, they who did evil; and they shall see them who despised the Scriptures, when they say: The dead shall not rise.

12 When all the sinners of the earth shall weep, because of their sins which they did; because there found them sorrow which has no end, because they did not good upon the earth.

13 And likewise for the latter they who did good — their joy shall not perish until forever; for while they were upon the earth, they heard good works.

14 And they did not transgress the commandment of their Creator; for they knew that they shall rise from the dead.

15 And because they did not transgress His commandment, they shall inherit — [TRUNCATED in the source; the verse breaks off mid-word.]

16 And in the heavens He shall cause them to inherit the land of life, which He promised to their fathers, in the day of the resurrection, when they shall cast away the.

17 And woe to the evil doers who did not believe the resurrection of the dead, and the law of God; and the day of the resurrection they did not remember.

18 When you see that there finds them evil which has no passing away, and which has no healing, and no end; and in their hearts sorrow which has no end, and no rest.

19 And a worm which does not do dy, and a fire which is not quenched.

20 And upon their flesh fire, and smoke, and a tempestuous wind, and, and hail, and, which shall upon them.

21 A cutting off of fire for them who did not believe the resurrection of the dead.
