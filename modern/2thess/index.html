THE SECOND EPISTLE TO THE THESSALONIANS

2 Thessalonians

Second Thessalonians is a shorter follow-up letter, again naming Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy as senders, to the same church in Thessalonica. Some believers had been shaken by claims that the day of the Lord had already come, and others had stopped working while waiting. Paul steadies them: certain rebellion and lawlessness must come first, God restrains evil for now, and they should hold fast to what they were taught. He also gives a plain command about idleness and asks for their prayers. In this edition the text is the King James Version of 1611, printed as received from the public domain. In this canon it completes the Thessalonian pair among Paul's letters, kept by the Ethiopian Church with the apostolic writings. Its three chapters are brief but bracing. Watch for the mysterious man of sin and Paul's firm rule that those unwilling to work should not eat.
