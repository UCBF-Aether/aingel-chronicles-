THE GOSPEL ACCORDING TO SAINT JOHN

John

John is the most reflective of the four Gospels, told in 21 chapters. It begins before time — 'In the beginning was the Word' — and then presents Jesus through signs and long, searching conversations: with Nicodemus at night, with a Samaritan woman at a well, with crowds hungry for bread, and with his closest friends at a final meal where he washes their feet. Its purpose is stated plainly near the end: these things are written so that readers may believe, and believing, have life.

In this edition John is printed from the King James Version of 1611, the public-domain text as received. It stands in this canon as the fourth Gospel, completing the fourfold witness the Ethiopian Church keeps together. Where the other Gospels stress what Jesus did and taught on the road, John lingers on who he is.

Watch for the 'I am' sayings scattered through the book — each one opens a different window onto Jesus.
