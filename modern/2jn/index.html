THE SECOND GENERAL EPISTLE OF JOHN

2 John

Second John is the shortest letter in this group — a single chapter in the familiar form — written by the elder to a chosen lady and her children. In a few lines it holds two things together that are often pulled apart: truth and love. Walk in love, John says, and at the same time do not welcome teachers who deny that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh. Warmth and watchfulness belong in the same house.

In this edition the text is the King James Version of 1611, received as public-domain text. It is included because the Ethiopian canon preserves all three of John's letters, not only the longest one. Small books were kept on purpose: they show the apostle's care reaching into a single household, and they model how a local church should handle both hospitality and deception. You can read it in a minute, and think about it all day.

Watch for the balance John keeps — open-hearted love for the family of faith, and a closed door to teaching that denies Christ.
