THE SECOND EPISTLE TO TIMOTHY

2 Timothy

Second Timothy is the most personal of Paul's letters in this collection, naming Paul as sender and written to Timothy from prison, with death near. An older apostle urges a younger, perhaps timid, coworker not to be ashamed of the gospel, to endure hardship like a soldier, to handle the word of truth rightly, and to preach in season and out. Paul's own course, he says, is finished, and he expects the crown of righteousness. The letter feels like a farewell passed hand to hand. In this edition the text is the King James Version of 1611, printed as received from the public domain. It belongs in this canon among the pastoral letters preserved by the Ethiopian Church, a testament of courage at the end of a life of service. Watch for the famous charge about all Scripture being given by inspiration of God.
