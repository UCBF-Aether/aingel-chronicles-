Jubilees

Chapter 26

1 And in the seventh year of this week Isaac called Esau, his elder son, and said to him: "I am old, my son, and behold my eyes are dim in seeing, and I do not know the day of my death.

2 And now take your hunting weapons, your quiver and your bow, and go out to the field, and hunt and catch me (venison), my son, and make me savory meat, such as my soul loves, and bring it to me that I may eat, and that my soul may bless you before I die."

3 But Rebecca heard Isaac speaking to Esau.

4 And Esau went forth early to the field to hunt and catch and bring home to his father.

5 And Rebecca called Jacob, her son, and said to him: "Behold, I heard Isaac, your father, speak to Esau, your brother, saying: "Hunt for me, and make me savory meat, and bring (it) to me that I may eat and bless you before the Lord before I die." And now, my son, obey my voice in that which I command you: Go to your flock and fetch me two good kids of the goats, and I will make them savory meat for your father, such as he loves, and you shall bring (it) to your father that he may eat and bless you before the Lord before he dies, and that you may be blessed." And Jacob said to Rebecca his mother: "Mother, I shall not withhold

6 anything which my father would eat, and which would please him: only I fear, my mother, that he will recognize my voice and wish to touch me.

7 And you know that I am smooth, and Esau, my brother, is hairy, and I shall appear before his eyes as an evildoer, and shall do a deed which he had not commanded me, and he will be angry with me, and I shall bring upon myself a curse, and not a blessing." 9.

8 And Rebecca, his mother, said to him:

9 "Upon me be your curse, my son, only obey my voice." 10.

10 And Jacob obeyed the voice of Rebecca, his mother, and went and fetched two good and fat kids of the goats, and brought them to his mother, and his mother made them (savory meat) such as he loved.

11 And Rebecca took the fine clothing of Esau, her elder son, which was with her in the house, and she clothed Jacob, her younger son, (with them), and she put the skins of the kids upon his hands and on the exposed parts of his neck.

12 And she gave the meat and the bread which she had prepared into the hand of her son Jacob.

13 And Jacob went in to his father and said: "I am your son: I have done as you commanded me: rise and sit and eat of what I have caught, father, that your soul may bless me." 14.

14 And Isaac said to his son: "How have you found so quickly, my son?" 15.

15 And Jacob said: "Because (the Lord) your God caused me to find." 16.

16 And Isaac said to him: "Come near, that I may feel you, my son, if you are my son Esau or not." 17.

17 And Jacob went near to Isaac, his father, and he felt him and said: "The voice is Jacob's voice, but the hands are the hands "of Esau," and he did not discern him, because it was a dispensation from heaven to remove his power of perception and Isaac did not discern, for his hands were hairy as (his brother) Esau's, so that he blessed him.

18 And he said: "Are you my son, Esau?

19 " and he said: "I am your son": and he said, "Bring

20 near to me that I may eat of what you have caught, my son, that my soul may bless you." 20.

21 And he brought near to him, and he ate, and he brought him wine and he drank.

22 And Isaac, his father, said to him: "Come near and kiss me, my son."

23 And he came near and kissed him.

24 And he smelled the smell of his clothing, and he blessed him and said: "Behold, the smell of my son is as the smell of a (full) field which the Lord has blessed.

25 And may the Lord give you of the dew of heaven and of the dew of the earth, and plenty of grain

26 and oil: Let nations serve you, and peoples bow down to you.

27 Be lord over your brothers,

28 And let your mother's sons bow down to you; and may all the blessings with which the Lord

29 has blessed me and blessed Abraham, my

30 father be imparted to you and to your offspring forever: Cursed be he that curses you, and blessed be he that blesses you."

31 And it came to pass as soon as Isaac had made an end of blessing his son Jacob, and Jacob had gone forth from Isaac his father he hid himself and Esau, his brother, came in from his hunting.

32 And he also made savory meat, and brought (it) to his father, and said to his father: "Let my father arise, and eat of my venison that your soul may bless me." 27.

33 And Isaac, his father, said to him: "Who are you?" And he said to him: "I am your firstborn, your son Esau: I have done as you have commanded me." And Isaac was very

34 greatly astonished, and said: "Who is he that has hunted and caught and brought (it) to me, and I have eaten of all before you came, and have blessed him: (and) he shall be blessed, and all his offspring forever." 29.

35 And it came to pass when Esau heard the words of his father Isaac that he cried with an exceedingly great and bitter cry, and said to his father: "Bless me, (even) me also, father." 30.

36 And he said to him: "Your brother came with guile, and has taken away your blessing."

37 And he said: "Now I know why his name is named Jacob: behold, he has supplanted me these two times: he took away my birthright, and now he has taken away my blessing." 31.

38 And he said: "Have you not reserved a blessing for me, father?

39 " and Isaac answered and said to Esau:

40 "Behold, I have made him your lord,

41 And all his brothers have I given to him for servants,

42 And with plenty of grain and wine and oil have I strengthened him:

43 And what now shall I do for you, my son?

44 And Esau said to Isaac, his father:

45 "Have you only one blessing, O father?

46 Bless me, (even) me also, father": And Esau lifted up his voice and wept.

47 And Isaac answered and said to him:

48 "Behold, far from the dew of the earth shall be your dwelling,

49 And far from the dew of heaven from above.

50 And by your sword you will live,

51 And you will serve your brother.

52 And it shall come to pass when you become great

53 And shake his yoke from off your neck,

54 You will sin a complete sin to death,

55 And your offspring will be rooted out from under heaven." 35.

56 And Esau kept threatening Jacob because of the blessing with which his father blessed him, and he said in his heart: "May the days of mourning for my father now come, so that I may slay my brother Jacob."
