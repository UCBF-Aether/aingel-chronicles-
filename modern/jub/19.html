Jubilees

Chapter 19

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And in the first year of the first week in the forty-second jubilee, Abraham returned and dwelt opposite Hebron, that is Kirjath Arba, two weeks of years.

2 And in the first year of the third week of this jubilee the days of the life of Sarah were accomplished, and she died in Hebron.

3 And Abraham went to mourn over her and bury her, and we tried him [to see] if his spirit was patient and he was not indignant in the words of his mouth; and he was found patient in this, and was not disturbed. 4.

4 For in patience of spirit he conversed with the children of Heth, with the intent that they should give him a place in which to bury his dead.

5 And the Lord gave him grace before all who saw him, and he pleaded in gentleness with the sons of Heth, and they gave him the land of the double cave over against Mamre, that is Hebron, for four hundred pieces of silver.

6 And they pleaded with him, saying, "We shall give it to you for nothing"; but he would not take it from their hands for nothing, for he gave the price of the place, the money in full, and he bowed down before them twice; and after this he buried his dead in the double cave.

7 And all the days of the life of Sarah were one hundred and twenty-seven years, that is, two jubilees and four weeks and one year: these are the days of the years of the life of Sarah.

8 This is the tenth trial with which Abraham was tried, and he was found faithful, patient in spirit.

9 And he said not a single word regarding the rumor in the land that God had said that He would give it to him and to his offspring after him, and he begged a place there to bury his dead; for he was found faith-

10 ful, and was recorded on the heavenly tablets as the friend of God.

11 And in the fourth year thereof he took a wife for his son Isaac and her name was Rebecca [the daughter of Bethuel, the son of Nahor, the brother of Abraham] the sister of Laban and daughter of Bethuel; and Bethuel was the son of Melca, who was the wife of Nahor, the brother of Abraham.

12 And Abraham took for himself a third wife, and her name was Keturah, from among the daughters of his household servants, for Hagar had died before Sarah.

13 And she bore him six sons, Zimram, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah, in the two weeks of years.

14 And in the sixth week, in the second year thereof, Rebecca bore to Isaac two sons, Jacob and Esau, and Jacob was a smooth and upright man, and Esau was fierce, a man of the field, and hairy, and Jacob dwelt in tents.

15 And the youths grew, and Jacob learned to write; but Esau did not learn, for he was a man of the field and a hunter, and he learned war, and all his deeds were fierce.

16 Abraham loves Jacob and blesses him.

17 And Abraham loved Jacob, but Isaac loved Esau.

18 And Abraham saw the deeds of Esau, and he knew that in Jacob should his name and offspring be called; and he called Rebecca and gave commandment regarding Jacob, for he knew that she (too) loved Jacob much more than Esau.

19 And he said to her: "My daughter, watch over my son Jacob, for he shall be in my place on the earth, and for a blessing in the midst of the children of

20 men, and for the glory of the whole offspring of Shem.

21 For I know that the Lord will choose him to be a people for possession to Himself, above all peoples that are upon the face of the earth.

22 And behold, Isaac my son loves Esau more than Jacob, but I see that you truly love Jacob.

23 Add still further to your kindness to him, and let your eyes be upon him in love;

24 For he will be a blessing to us on the earth from now on to all generations of the earth.

25 Let your hands be strong

26 And let your heart rejoice in your son Jacob; for I have loved him far beyond all my sons.

27 He will be blessed forever,

28 And his offspring will fill the whole earth.

29 If a man can number the sand of the earth, his offspring also will be numbered.

30 And all the blessings with which the Lord has blessed me and my offspring shall belong to Jacob and his offspring always.

31 And in his offspring shall my name be blessed, and the name of my fathers, Shem, and

32 Noah, and Enoch, and Mahalalel, and Enos, and Seth, and Adam.

33 And these shall serve

34 To lay the foundations of the heaven,

35 And to strengthen the earth,

36 And to renew all the luminaries which are in the expanse." 26.

37 And he called Jacob before the eyes of Rebecca his mother, and kissed him, and blessed him, and said: "Jacob, my beloved son, whom my soul loves, may God bless you from above the expanse, and may He give you all the blessings with which He blessed Adam, and Enoch, and Noah, and Shem; and all the things of which He told me, and all the things which He promised to give me, may He cause to cleave to you and to your offspring forever, according to the days of heaven above the earth.

38 And the spirits of Mastema shall not rule over you or over your offspring to turn you from the Lord, who is your God from now on forever.

39 And may the Lord God be a father to you and you the first-born son, and to the people always.

40 Go in peace, my son."

41 And they both went forth together from Abraham.

42 And Rebecca loved Jacob, with all her heart and with all her soul, very much more than Esau; but Isaac loved Esau much more than Jacob.

43 Abraham's Last Words to his Children and Grandchildren.
