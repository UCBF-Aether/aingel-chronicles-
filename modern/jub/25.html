Jubilees

Chapter 25

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And in the second year of this week in this jubilee, Rebecca called Jacob her son, and spoke to him, saying: "My son, do not take yourself a wife of the daughters of Canaan, as Esau, your brother, who took himself two wives of the daughters of Canaan and they have embittered my soul with all their unclean deeds: for all their deeds are fornication and lust, and there is no righteousness with them, for (their deeds) are evil.

2 And I, my son, love you exceedingly, and my heart and my affection bless you every hour of the day and watch of the night.

3 And now, my son, listen to my voice, and do the will of your mother, and do not take yourself a wife of the daughters of this land, but only of the house of my father, and of my father's kindred.

4 You will take yourself a wife of the house of my father, and the Most High God will bless you, and your children will be a righteous generation and a holy offspring." 4.

5 And then Jacob spoke to Rebecca, his mother, and said to her: "Behold, mother, I am nine weeks of years old, and I neither know nor have I touched any woman, nor have I betrothed myself to any, nor even think of taking myself a wife of the daughters of Canaan.

6 For I remember,

7 mother, the words of Abraham, our father, for he commanded me not to take a wife of the daughters of Canaan, but to take myself a wife from the offspring of my father's house and from my kindred.

8 I have heard before that daughters have been born to Laban, your brother, and I have set my heart on them to take a wife from among them, 7.

9 And for this reason I have guarded myself in my spirit against sinning or being corrupted in all my ways throughout all the days of my life; for with regard to lust and fornication, Abraham, my father, gave me many commands.

10 And, despite all that he has commanded me, these two and twenty years my brother has striven with me, and spoken frequently to me and said: 'My brother, take as wife a sister of my two wives'; but I refuse to do as he has done.

11 I swear before you, mother, that all the days of my life I will not take myself a wife from the daughters of the offspring of Canaan, and I will not act wickedly as my brother has done.

12 Fear not, mother; be assured that I shall do your will and walk in uprightness, and not corrupt my ways forever."

13 And thereupon she lifted up her face to heaven and extended the fingers of her hands, and opened her mouth and blessed the Most High God, who had created the heaven and the earth, and she gave Him thanks and praise.

14 And she said: "Blessed be the Lord God, and may His holy name be blessed forever and ever, who has given me Jacob as a pure son and a holy offspring; for He is Yours, and Yours shall his offspring be continually and throughout all the generations forevermore.

15 Bless him, O Lord, and place in my mouth the blessing of righteousness, that I may bless him." 14.

16 And at that hour, when the spirit of righteousness descended into her mouth, she placed both her hands on the head of Jacob, and said:

17 "Blessed are you, Lord of righteousness and

18 God of the ages;

19 And may He bless you beyond all the generations of men.

20 May He give you, my son, the path of righteousness,

21 And reveal righteousness to your offspring.

22 And may He make your sons many during your

23 life, and may they arise according to the number of

24 the months of the year.

25 And may their sons become many and great

26 beyond the stars of heaven, and their numbers be more than the sand of

27 the sea.

28 And may He give them this good land — as He

29 said He would give it to Abraham and to his offspring after him always — and may they hold it as a possession forever.

30 And may I see (born) to you, my son, blessed

31 children during my life, and a blessed and holy offspring may all your offspring be.

32 And as you have refreshed your mother's spirit

33 during her life, the womb of her that bore you blesses you, [My affection] and my breasts bless you and my mouth and my tongue praise you greatly.

34 Increase and spread over the earth,

35 And may your offspring be perfect in the joy of heaven

36 and earth forever; and may your offspring rejoice, and on the great day of peace may it have

37 peace.

38 And may your name and your offspring endure to all

39 the ages, and may the Most High God be their God, and may the God of righteousness dwell with

40 them,

41 And by them may His sanctuary be built to all the ages.

42 Blessed be he that blesses you,

43 And all flesh that curses you falsely may it be cursed"

44 And she kissed him, and said to him:

45 "May the Lord of the world love you as the heart of your mother and her affection rejoice in you and bless you."

46 And she ceased from blessing.

47 Jacob obtains the Blessing of the Firstborn
