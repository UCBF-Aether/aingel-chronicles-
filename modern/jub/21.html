Jubilees

Chapter 21

1 And in the sixth year of the seventh week of this jubilee Abraham called Isaac his son and commanded him, saying: "I have become old, and do not know the day of my death and am full of my days.

2 And behold, I am one hundred and seventy-five years old and throughout all the days of my life I have remembered the Lord, and sought with all my heart to do His will, and to walk uprightly in all His ways.

3 My soul has hated idols, (and I have despised those that served them, and I have given my heart and spirit) that I might observe to do the will of Him who created me.

4 For He is the living God, and He is holy and faithful, and He is righteous beyond all, and there is with Him no accepting of (men's) persons and no accepting of gifts; for God is righteous, and execut-

5 es judgment on all those who transgress His commandments and despise His covenant.

6 And you, my son, observe His commandments and His ordinances and His judgments, and do not walk after the abominations and after the graven images and after the molten images.

7 And eat no blood at all of animals or cattle, or of any bird which flies in the heaven.

8 And if you slay a sacrificial animal as an acceptable peace-offering, slay it, and pour out its blood upon the altar, and all the fat of the offering offer on the altar with fine flour (and the grain offering) mingled with oil with its drink-offering — offer them all together on the altar of burnt-offering; it is a pleasing aroma before the Lord.

9 And you will offer the fat of the sacrifice of thank-offerings on the fire which is upon the altar, and the fat which is on the belly, and all the fat on the inwards and the two kidneys, and all the fat that is upon them, and upon the loins and liver you shall remove, together with the kidneys.

10 And offer all these for a pleasing aroma acceptable before the Lord, with its grain offering and with its drink-offering, for a pleasing aroma, the bread of the offering to the Lord, 10.

11 And eat its meat on that day and on the second day, and let not the sun on the second day go down upon it until it is eaten, and let nothing be left over for the third day; for it is not acceptable [for it is not approved] 7 and let it no longer be eaten, and all who eat of it will bring sin upon themselves; for thus I have found it written in the books of my forefathers, and in the words of Enoch, and in the words of Noah.

12 And on all

13 your offerings you shall sprinkle salt, and let not the salt of the covenant be lacking in all your offerings before the Lord.

14 And as regards the wood of the sacrifices, beware lest you bring (other) wood for the altar in addition to these: cypress, defran, sagad, pine, fir, cedar, savin, palm, olive, myrrh, laurels and 'citron, juniper, and balsam.

15 And of these kinds of wood lay upon the altar under the sacrifice, such as have been tested as to their appearance, and do not lay (thereon) any split or dark wood, (but) hard and clean, without fault, a sound and new growth; and do not lay (thereon) old wood, [for its fragrance is gone] for there is no longer fragrance in it as before.

16 Besides these kinds of wood there is none other that you shall place (on the altar), for the fragrance is dispersed, and the smell of its fragrance does not go up to heaven.

17 Observe this commandment and do it, my son, that you may be upright in all your deeds.

18 And at all times be clean in your body, and wash yourself with water before you approach to offer on the altar, and wash your hands and your feet before you draw near to the altar; and when you are done sacrificing, wash again your hands and your feet.

19 And let no blood appear upon you nor upon your clothes; be on your guard, my son, against blood, be on your guard exceedingly; cover it with dust.

20 And do not eat any blood, for it is the soul; eat no blood whatever.

21 And take no gifts for the blood of man, lest it be shed with impunity, without judgment; for it is the blood that

22 is shed that causes the earth to sin, and the earth cannot be cleansed from the blood of man except by the blood of him who shed it.

23 And take no

24 gift or present for the blood of man: blood for blood, that you may be accepted before the Lord, the Most High God; for He is the defense of the good: and that you may be preserved from all evil, and that He may save you from every kind of death.

25 I see, my son,

26 That all the works of the children of men are

27 sin and wickedness, and all their deeds are uncleanness and an

28 abomination and a pollution; and there is no righteousness with them.

29 Beware, lest you walk in their ways and tread in their paths,

30 And sin a sin to death before the Most High

31 God.

32 Otherwise He will [hide His face from you, and] 3 give you back into the hands of your

33 transgression, and root you out of the land, and your offspring

34 likewise from under heaven, and your name and your offspring will perish from

35 the whole earth'.

36 Turn away from all their deeds and all their

37 uncleanness, and observe the ordinance of the Most High God, and do His will and be upright in all things.

38 And He will bless you in all your deeds,

39 And will raise up from you the plant of righteousness through all the earth, throughout

40 all generations of the earth, and my name and your name will not be forgotten under heaven forever.

41 Go, my son, in peace.

42 May the Most High God, my God and your God,

43 strengthen you to do His will, and may He bless all your offspring and the remnant

44 of your offspring for the generations forever, with

45 all righteous blessings, that you may be a blessing on all the earth."

46 And he went out from him rejoicing.

47 Isaac, Ishmael and Jacob join in Festival with Abraham for the Last Time.

48 Abraham's Prayer.
