Jubilees

Chapter 18

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And God said to him, "Abraham, Abraham"; and he said, "Behold, (here) am I." 2.

2 And He said, "Take your beloved son whom you love, (even) Isaac, and go to the high country and offer him on one of the mountains which I will point out to you." 3.

3 And he rose early in the morning and saddled his donkey, and took his two young men with him, and Isaac his son, and split the wood of the burnt-offering, and he went to the place on

4 the third day, and he saw the place afar off.

5 And he came to a well of water, and he said to his young men, "Stay here with the donkey, and I and the lad shall go (yonder), and when we have worshiped we shall come again to you." 5.

6 And he took the wood of the burnt-offering and laid it on Isaac his son, and he took in his hand the fire and the knife, and they went both of them together to that place.

7 And Isaac said to his father, "Father"; and he said, "Here am I, my son."

8 And he said to him, "Behold the fire, and the knife, and the wood; but where is the sheep for the burnt-offering, father?

9 And he said, "God will provide for himself a sheep for a burnt-offering, my son."

10 And he drew near to the place of the mount of God.

11 And he built an altar, and he placed the wood on the altar, and bound Isaac his son, and placed him on the wood which was upon the altar, and stretched forth his hand to take the knife to slay Isaac his son.

12 And I stood before him, and before the prince Mastema, and the Lord said, "Tell him not to lay his hand on the lad, nor to do anything to him, for I have shown that he fears the Lord." 10.

13 And I called to him from heaven, and said to him: "Abraham, Abraham"; and he was terrified and said: "Behold, (here) am I."

14 And I said to him: "Do not lay your hand upon the lad, nor do anything to him; for now I have shown that you fear the Lord, and have not withheld your son, your first-born son, from me." 12.

15 And the prince Mastema was put to shame; and Abraham lifted up his eyes and looked, and, behold, a single ram caught by his horns, and Abraham went and took the ram and offered it for a burnt-offering in place of his son.

16 And Abraham called

17 that place "The Lord has seen," so that it is said "(in the mount) the Lord has seen": that is Mount Sion.

18 And the Lord called Abraham by his name a second time from heaven, as he caused us to appear to speak to him in the name of the Lord.

19 And He said: "By Myself have I sworn, says

20 the Lord, Because you have done this thing, and have not withheld your son, your beloved

21 son, from Me,

22 That in blessing I shall bless you,

23 And in multiplying I shall multiply your offspring

24 As the stars of heaven,

25 And as the sand which is on the seashore.

26 And your offspring will inherit the cities of its enemies,

27 And in your offspring will all nations of the earth be

28 blessed;

29 Because you have obeyed My voice,

30 And I have shown to all that you are faithful

31 to Me in all that I have said to you: Go in peace."

32 And Abraham went to his young men, and they arose and went together to Beersheba, and Abraham dwelt by the Well of the Oath.

33 And he celebrated this festival every year, seven days with joy, and he called it the festival of the Lord according to the seven days during which he went and returned in peace.

34 And accordingly it has been ordained and written on the heavenly tablets regarding Israel and its offspring that they should observe this festival seven days with the joy of festival.

35 The Death and Burial of Sarah.
