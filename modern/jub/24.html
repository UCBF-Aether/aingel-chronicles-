Jubilees

Chapter 24

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And it came to pass after the death of Abraham, that the Lord blessed Isaac his son, and he arose from Hebron and went and dwelt at the Well of the Vision 1 in the first year of the third week of this jubilee, seven years.

2 And in the first year of the fourth week a famine began in the land, besides the first famine, which had been in the days of Abraham.

3 And Jacob boiled lentil pottage, and Esau came from the field hungry.

4 And he said to Jacob his brother: "Give me of this red pottage." And Jacob said to him: "Sell to me your [primogeniture, this] birthright and I will give you bread, and also some of this lentil pottage." 4.

5 And Esau said in his heart: "I shall die; of what profit to me is this birthright?

6 " And he said to Jacob: "I give it to you." 5.

7 And Jacob said: "Swear to me, this day," and he swore to him.

8 And Jacob gave his brother Esau bread and pottage, and he ate until he was satisfied, and Esau despised his birthright; for this reason was Esau's name called Edom, on account of the red pottage which Jacob gave him for his birthright.

9 And Jacob became the elder, and Esau was brought down from his dignity.

10 And the famine was over the land, and Isaac departed to go down into Egypt in the second year of this week, and went to the king of the Philistines to Gerar, to Abimelech. And the Lord appeared to him and said to him: "Do not go down into Egypt; dwell in the land that I shall tell you of, and sojourn in this land, and I shall be

11 with you and bless you.

12 For to you and to your offspring shall I give all this land, and I shall establish My oath which I swore to Abraham your father, and I shall multiply your offspring as the stars of heaven, and shall give to your offspring all this land.

13 And in your offspring will all the nations of the earth be blessed; because your father obeyed My voice, and kept My charge and My commandments, and My laws, and My ordinances, and My covenant; and now obey My voice and dwell in this land."

14 And he dwelt in Gerar three weeks of years. 13.

15 And Abimelech charged concerning him, and concerning all that was his, saying: "Any man that shall touch him or anything that is his shall surely die." 14.

16 And Isaac grew strong among the Philistines, and he got many possessions, oxen and sheep and camels and donkeys and a great household.

17 And he sowed in the land of the Philistines and brought in a hundred-fold, and Isaac became exceedingly great, and the Philistines envied him.

18 Now all the wells which the servants of Abraham had dug during the life of Abraham, the Philistines had stopped them after the death of Abraham, and filled them with earth.' 17.

19 And Abimelech said to Isaac: "Go from us, for you are much mightier than we"; and Isaac departed from there in the first year of the seventh week, and sojourned in the valleys of Gerar.

20 And they dug again the wells of water which the servants of Abraham, his father, had dug, and which the Philistines had closed after the death of Abraham his father, and he called their names as Abraham his father had named them.

21 And the servants of Isaac dug a well in the valley, and found living water, and the shepherds of Gerar strove with the shepherds of Isaac, saying: "The water is ours"; and Isaac called the name of the well "Perversity," because

22 they had been perverse with us.

23 And they dug a second well, and they strove for that also, and he called its name "Enmity."

24 And he arose from there and they dug another well, and for that they did not strive, and he called the name of it "Room," and Isaac said: "Now the Lord has made room for us, and we have increased in the land." 21.

25 And he went up from there to the Well of the Oath in the first year of the first week in the forty-fourth jubilee.

26 And the Lord appeared to him that night, on the new moon of the first month, and said to him: "I am the God of Abraham your father; fear not, for I am with you, and shall bless you and shall surely multiply your offspring as the sand of the earth, for the sake of Abraham my servant." 23.

27 And he built an altar there, which Abraham his father had first built, and he called upon the name of the Lord, and he offered sacrifice to the God of Abraham his father.

28 And they dug a well and they found living water.

29 And the servants of Isaac dug another well and did not find water, and they went and told Isaac that they had not found water, and Isaac said: "I have sworn this day to the Philistines and this thing has been announced to us." 26.

30 And he called the name of that place the "Well of the Oath"; for there he had sworn to Abimelech and Ahuzzath his friend and Phicol the prefect of his host.

31 And Isaac knew that day that under constraint he had sworn to them to make peace with them.

32 Isaac curses the Philistines.

33 And Isaac on that day cursed the Philistines and said: "Cursed be the Philistines to the day of wrath and indignation from the midst of all nations; may God make them a derision and a curse and an object of wrath and indignation in the hands of the sinners the Gentiles and in the hands of the Kittim.

34 And whoever escapes the sword of the enemy and the Kittim, may the righteous nation root out in judgment from under heaven; for they will be the enemies and foes of my children throughout their generations upon the earth.

35 And no remnant will be left to them,

36 Nor one that will be saved on the day of the

37 wrath of judgment; for destruction and rooting out and expulsion from the earth is the whole offspring of the Philistines (reserved), and there will no longer be left for these Caphtorim a name or an offspring on the earth. For though he ascend to heaven, from there shall he be brought down, and though he make himself strong on earth, from there shall he be dragged forth, and though he hide himself among the nations, even from there shall he be rooted out; and though he descend into Sheol, there also shall his condemnation be great, and there also he shall have no peace. And if he go into captivity,

38 By the hands of those that seek his life will they slay him on the way,

39 And neither name nor offspring will be left to him on all the earth;

40 For into eternal malediction will he depart." And thus is it written and engraved concerning him on the heavenly tablets, to do to him on the day of judgment, so that he may be rooted out of the earth.
