Jubilees

Chapter 22

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And it came to pass in the first week in the forty-fourth jubilee, in the second year, that is, the year in which Abraham died, that Isaac and Ishmael came from the Well of the Oath to celebrate the feast of weeks — that is, the feast of the first-fruits of the harvest — to Abraham, their father, and Abraham rejoiced because his two sons had come.

2 For Isaac had many possessions in Beersheba, and Isaac used to go and see his possessions and to return to his father.

3 And in those days Ishmael came to see his father, and they both came together, and Isaac offered a sacrifice for a burnt-offering, and presented it on the altar of his father which he had made in Hebron.

4 And he offered a thank-offering and made a feast of joy before Ishmael, his brother: and Rebecca made new cakes from the new grain, and gave them to Jacob, her son, to take them to Abraham, his father, from the first-fruits of the land, that he might eat and bless the Creator of all things before he died.

5 And Isaac, too, sent by the hand of Jacob to Abraham a best thank-offering, that he might eat and drink.

6 And he ate and drank, and blessed the Most High God,

7 Who has created heaven and earth, who has made all the fat things of the earth, and given them to the children of men that they might eat and drink and bless their Creator.

8 "And now I give thanks to You, my God, because You have caused me to see this day: behold, I am one hundred seventy-five years, an old man and full of days and all my days have been to me peace.

9 The sword of the adversary has not overcome me in all that You have given me and my children all the days of my life until this day.

10 My God, may Your mercy and Your peace be upon Your servant, and upon the offspring of his sons, that they may be to You a chosen nation and an inheritance from among all the nations of the earth from now on to all the days of the generations of the earth, to all the ages."

11 Abraham's Last Words to and Blessings of Jacob.

12 And he called Jacob and said: "My son Jacob, may the God of all bless you and strengthen you to do righteousness, and His will before Him, and may He choose you and your offspring that you may become a people for His inheritance according to His will always.

13 And you, my son, Jacob, draw near and kiss me." 11.

14 And he drew near and kissed him, and he said:

15 "Blessed be my son Jacob

16 And all the sons of God Most High, to all the ages:

17 May God give to you an offspring of righteousness;

18 And some of your sons may He sanctify in the midst of the whole earth;

19 May nations serve you,

20 And all the nations bow themselves before your offspring.

21 Be strong in the presence of men,

22 And exercise authority over all the offspring of Seth.

23 Then your ways and the ways of your sons will be

24 justified, so that they shall become a holy nation.

25 May the Most High God give you all the blessings with which He has blessed me

26 And with which He blessed Noah and Adam; may they rest on the sacred head of your offspring from generation to generation forever.

27 And may He cleanse you from all unrighteous-

28 ness and impurity,

29 That you may be forgiven all (your) transgressions; (and) your sins of ignorance.

30 And may He strengthen you,

31 And bless you.

32 And may you inherit the whole earth,

33 And may He renew His covenant with you, that you may be to Him a nation for His

34 inheritance for all the ages, and that He may be to you and to your offspring a God in truth and righteousness throughout all the days of the earth.

35 And you, my son Jacob, remember my words, and observe the commandments of Abraham, your

36 father: Separate yourself from the nations, and do not eat with them: and do not do according to their works, and do not become their associate; for their works are unclean,

37 And all their ways are a pollution and an abomination and uncleanness.

38 They offer their sacrifices to the dead and they worship evil spirits,

39 And they eat over the graves

40 And all their works are vanity and nothingness.

41 They have no heart to understand

42 And their eyes do not see what their works are, and how they err in saying to a piece of wood:

43 'You are my God,' and to a stone: 'You are my Lord and you are my deliverer.' [And they have no heart.]

44 And as for you, my son Jacob, may the Most High God help you and the God of heaven bless you

45 And remove you from their uncleanness and from all their error.

46 Beware, my son Jacob, of taking a wife

47 from any offspring of the daughters of Canaan; for all his offspring is to be rooted out of the earth.

48 For, owing to the transgression of Ham, Canaan

49 erred and all his offspring will be destroyed from off the

50 earth and all the remnant thereof, and none springing from him will be saved on

51 the day of judgment.

52 And as for all the worshippers of idols and the

53 profane (b) There will be no hope for them in the land of the living;

54 (e) And there will be no remembrance of them on

55 the earth; (c) For they will descend into Sheol, (a) And into the place of condemnation will they go As the children of Sodom were taken away from

56 the earth So will all those who worship idols be taken away.

57 Fear not, my son Jacob,

58 And be not dismayed, son of Abraham: may the Most High God preserve you from

59 destruction, and from all the paths of error may He deliver

60 you.

61 This house I have built for myself that I might put my name upon it in the earth: [it is given to you and to your offspring forever], and it will be named the house of Abraham; it is given to you and to your offspring forever; for you will build my house and establish my name before God forever: your offspring and your name will stand throughout all generations of the earth;

62 And he ceased commanding him and blessing him.

63 And the two lay together on one bed, and Jacob slept in the bosom of Abraham, his father's father and he kissed him seven times, and his affection and his heart rejoiced over him.

64 And he blessed him with all his heart and said: 'The Most High God, the God of all, and Creator of all, who brought me forth from Ur of the Chaldees, that He might give me this land to inherit it forever, and that I might establish a holy offspring — blessed be the Most High forever." 28.

65 And he blessed Jacob and said: "My son, over whom with all my heart and my affection I rejoice, may Your grace and Your mercy

66 be lifted up upon him and upon his offspring always.

67 And do not forsake him, nor count him as nothing from now on to the days of eternity, and may Your eyes be opened upon him and upon his offspring, that You may preserve him, and bless him, and may sanctify him as a nation for Your inheritance; and bless him with all Your blessings from now on to all the days of eternity, and renew Your covenant and Your grace with him and with his offspring according to all Your good pleasure to all the generations of the earth."

68 The Death and Burial of Abraham.
