Jubilees

Chapter 1

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

This is the history of the division of the days of the law and of the testimony, of the events of the years, of 2 their (year) weeks, of their jubilees throughout all the years of the world as the Lord spoke to Moses on Mount Sinai when he went up to receive the tablets of the law and of the commandment, according to the voice of God as He said to him, "Go up to the top of the Mount."

1 And it came to pass in the first year of the exodus of the children of Israel out of Egypt, in the third month on the sixteenth day of the month, that God spoke to Moses, saying: "Come up to Me on the Mount, and I will give you two tablets of stone of the law and of the commandment, which I have written, that you may teach them." 2.

2 And Moses went up into the mount of God, and the glory of the Lord abode on Mount Sinai, and a cloud overshadowed it six days.

3 And He called to Moses on

4 the seventh day out of the midst of the cloud, and the appearance of the glory of the Lord was like a flaming fire on the top of the Mount.

5 And Moses was on the Mount forty days and forty nights, and God taught him the earlier and the later history of the division of all the days of the law, and of the testimony.

6 And He said: "Incline your heart to every word which I shall speak to you on this Mount, and write them 2 in a book in order that their generations may see how I have not forsaken them for all the evil which they have done in transgressing the covenant which I establish between Me and you for their generations this day on Mount Sinai.

7 And thus it will come to pass when all these things come upon them that they will recognize that I am more righteous than they in all their judgments and in all their actions, and they will recognize that I have been truly with them.

8 And you, write for yourself all these words which I declare to you this day, for I know their rebellion and their stiff neck before I bring them into the land of which I swore to their fathers, to Abraham and to Isaac and to Jacob, saying.

9 "To your offspring will I give a land flowing with milk and honey.

10 And they will eat and be satisfied, and they will turn to strange gods, to (gods) which cannot deliver them from any of their tribulation: "and this witness shall be heard for a witness against them.

11 For they will forget all My commandments, (even) all that I command them, and they will walk after the Gentiles, and after their uncleanness, and after their shame, and will serve their gods, and these will prove to them an offense and a tribulation and an affliction and a snare.

12 And

13 many will perish and they will be taken captive and will fall into the hands of the enemy, because they have forsaken My ordinances and My commandments, and the festivals of My covenant, and My sabbaths, and My holy place 2 which I have made holy for Myself in their midst, and My tabernacle and My sanctuary, which I have made holy for Myself in the midst of the land, that I should set My name upon it, and that it should dwell (there).

14 And they will make for themselves high places and groves and graven images and they will worship, each his own (graven image), so as to go astray, and they will sacrifice their children to demons and to all the works of the error of their hearts.

15 And I will send witnesses to them, that I may witness against them, but they will not hear and will slay the witnesses also, and they will persecute those who seek the law, and they will abrogate and change everything so as to work evil before My eyes.

16 And I shall hide My face from them, and I shall deliver them into the hand of the Gentiles for captivity, and for a prey, and for devouring and I shall remove them from the midst of the land, and I shall scatter them among the Gentiles.

17 And they will forget all My law and all My commandments and all My judgments, and will go astray as to new moons, and sabbaths, and festivals, and jubilees, and ordinances.

18 And after this they will turn to Me from among the Gentiles with all their heart and with all their soul and with all their strength, and I shall gather them from among all the Gentiles, and they will seek Me, so that I shall be found by them, when they seek Me

19 ' The Tabernacle is apparently thought of as still in existence (in Jerusalem) during the time of the monarchy.

20 with all their heart and with all their soul.

21 And I shall disclose to them abounding peace with righteousness, and I shall remove them, the plant of uprightness, with all My heart and with all My soul and they will be for a blessing and not for a curse and they will be the head and not the tail.

22 And I shall build My sanctuary 6 in their midst, and I shall dwell with them, and I shall be their God and they will be My people 6 in truth and righteousness.

23 And I shall not forsake them nor fail them; for I am the Lord their God." 19.

24 And Moses fell on his face and prayed and said, "Lord my God, do not forsake Your people and Your inheritance so that they should wander in the error of their hearts, and do not deliver them into the hands of their enemies, the Gentiles, lest they should rule over them and cause them to sin against You.

25 Let Your mercy, O Lord, be lifted up upon Your people, and create in them an upright spirit and let not the spirit of Beliar 10 rule over them to accuse them before You, and to ensnare them from all the paths of righteousness, so that they may perish from before Your face.

26 But they are Your people and Your inheritance, which You have delivered with Your great power from the hands of the Egyptians: create in them a clean heart and a holy spirit, and let them not be ensnared in their sins from now on until eternity." 22.

27 And the Lord said to Moses: "I know their contrariness and their thoughts and their stiff-neckedness, and they will not be obedient until they confess their own sin and the sin of their

28 fathers.

29 And after this they will turn to Me in all uprightness and with all (their) heart and with all (their) soul, and I shall circumcise the foreskin of their heart and the foreskin of the heart of their offspring, and I shall create in them a holy spirit, and I shall cleanse them so that they shall not turn away from Me from that day to eternity.

30 And their souls will cleave to Me and to all My commandments, and they will fulfill My commandments, and I shall be their Father and they will be My children.

31 And they will all be called children of the living God and every angel and every spirit will know, yes, they will know that these are My children, and that I am their Father in uprightness and righteousness, and that I love them.

32 And you, write down for yourself all these words 4 which I declare to you on this mountain, the first and the last, which shall come to pass in all the divisions of the days in the law and in the testimony and in the weeks and the jubilees to eternity, until I descend and dwell with them 5 throughout eternity."

33 God commands the Angel to write.

34 And He said to the angel of the presence: "Write for Moses from the beginning of creation until My sanctuary has been built among them for all eternity. 28.

35 And the Lord will appear to the eyes

36 of all and all will know that I am the God of Israel and the Father of all the children of Jacob and King on Mount Zion for all eternity.

37 And Zion and Jerusalem will be holy." 29.

38 And the angel of the presence who went before the camp of Israel 4 took the tablets of the divisions of the years 6 — from the time of the creation — of the law and of the testimony of the weeks, of the jubilees, according to the individual years, according to all the number of the jubilees [according to the individual years], from the day of the [new] creation when the heavens and the earth shall be renewed and all their creation according to the powers of the heaven, and according to all the creation of the earth, until the sanctuary of the Lord shall be made in Jerusalem on Mount Zion, and all the luminaries be renewed for healing 8 and for peace and for blessing for all the elect of Israel, and that thus it may be from that day and to all the days of the earth.
