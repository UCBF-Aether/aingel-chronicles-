Jubilees

Chapter 12

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And it came to pass in the sixth week, in the seventh year thereof, that Abram said to Terah his father, saying, "Father." And he said, "Behold, here am I, my son." 2.

2 And he said,

3 "What help and profit have we from those idols which you worship, and before which you bow yourself?

4 For there is no spirit in them

5 For they are dumb forms, and a misleading of the

6 heart.

7 Do not worship them:

8 Worship the God of heaven,

9 Who causes the rain and the dew to descend on the

10 earth and does everything upon the earth,

11 And has created everything by His word.

12 Why do you worship things that have no spirit in

13 them?

14 For they are the work of (men's) hands

15 And on your shoulders you bear them, and you have no help from them,

16 But they are a great cause of shame to those who

17 make them, and a misleading of the heart to those who worship

18 them: do not worship them."

19 And his father said to him, "I also know it, my son, but what shall I do with a people who have made me to serve before them?

20 And if I tell them the truth, they will slay me; for their soul cleaves

21 to them to worship them and honor them.

22 Keep silent, my son, lest they slay you." 8.

23 And these words he spoke to his two brothers, and they were angry with him and he kept silent.

24 And in the fortieth jubilee, in the second week, in the seventh year thereof, Abram took for himself a wife, and her name was Sarai, the daughter of his father, and she became his wife.

25 And Haran, his brother, took for himself a wife in the third year of the third week, and she bore him a son in the seventh year of this week and he called his name Lot.

26 And Nahor, his brother, took for himself a wife.

27 And in the sixtieth year of the life of Abram, that is, in the fourth week, in the fourth year thereof, (1936 AM) Abram arose by night, and burned the house of the idols, and he burned all that was in the house, and no man knew it.

28 And they arose in the night and sought to save their gods from the midst of the fire.

29 And Haran hastened to save them, but the fire flamed over him, and he was burnt in the fire, and he died in Ur of the Chaldees before Terah his father, and they buried him in Ur of the Chaldees.

30 The Family of Terah in Haran; Abram's Experiences there; his Journey to Canaan

31 And Terah went forth from Ur of the Chaldees, he and his sons, to go into the land of Lebanon and into the land of Canaan, and he dwelt in the land of Haran, and Abram dwelt with Terah his father in

32 Haran two weeks of years.

33 And in the sixth week, in the fifth year thereof, Abram sat up throughout the night on the new moon of the seventh month to observe the stars from the evening to the morning, in order to see what would be the character of the year with regard to the rains, and he was alone as he sat and observed.

34 And a word came into his heart and he said: "All the signs of the stars, and the signs of the moon and of the sun are all in the hand of the Lord.

35 Why do I search (them) out?

36 If He desires, He causes it to rain, morning

37 and evening; and if He desires, He withholds it, and all things are in His hand.'

38 And he prayed that night and said

39 "My God, God Most High, You alone are my God, and You and Your dominion I have chosen.

40 And You have created all things,

41 And all things that are are the work of Your hands.

42 Deliver me from the hands of evil spirits who have

43 sway over the thoughts of men's hearts, and let them not lead me astray from You, my

44 God.

45 And establish me and my offspring forever, that we may not go astray from now on and for

46 evermore."

47 And he said, "Shall I return to Ur of the Chaldees who seek my face that I may return to them, or am I to remain here in this place?

48 The right path before You — prosper it in the hands of Your servant that he may fulfill (it) and that I may not walk in the deceitfulness of my heart, O my God." 22.

49 And he made an end of speaking and praying, and behold the word of the Lord was sent to him through me, saying: "Get up from your country, and from your kindred, and from the house of your father, to a land which I will show you, and I shall make you a great and numerous nation."

50 And I shall bless you

51 And I shall make your name great,

52 And you will be blessed in the earth,

53 And in you will all families of the earth be blessed,

54 And I shall bless them that bless you, and curse them that curse you.

55 And I shall be a God to you and your son, and to your son's son, and to all your offspring: fear not, from now on and to all generations of the earth I am your God." 25.

56 And the Lord God said: "Open his mouth and his ears, that he may hear and speak with his mouth, with the language which has been revealed"; for it had ceased from the mouths of all the children of men from the day of the overthrow (of Babel).

57 And I opened his mouth, and his ears and his lips, and I began to speak with him in Hebrew in the tongue of the creation.

58 And he

59 took the books of his fathers, and these were written in Hebrew, and he transcribed them, and he began from now on to study them, and I made known to him that which he could not (understand), and he studied them during the six rainy months.

60 And it came to pass in the seventh year of the sixth week that he spoke to his father, and informed him that he would leave Haran to go into the land of Canaan to see it and return to him.

61 And Terah his father said to him: "Go in peace:

62 May the eternal God make your path straight,

63 And the Lord [(be) with you, and] protect you from all evil,

64 And grant you grace, mercy and favor before those who see you,

65 And may none of the children of men have power over you to harm you;

66 Go in peace.

67 And if you see a land pleasant to your eyes to dwell in, then arise and take me to you and take Lot with you, the son of Haran your brother, as your own son: the Lord be with you.

68 And Nahor your brother leave with me until you return in peace, and we go with you all together."
