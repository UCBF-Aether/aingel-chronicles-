Jubilees

Chapter 27

1 And the words of Esau, her elder son, were told to Rebecca in a dream, and Rebecca sent and called Jacob her younger son, and said to him: "Behold Esau your brother will take vengeance on you so as to kill you.

2 Now, therefore, my son, obey my voice, and arise and flee to Laban, my brother, to Haran, and stay with him a few days until your brother's anger turns away, and he removes his anger from you, and forgets all that you have done; then I will send and fetch you from there." 4.

3 And Jacob said: "I am not afraid; if he wishes to kill me, I will kill him." 5.

4 But she said to him: "Let me not be bereft of both my sons on one day." 6.

5 And Jacob said to Rebecca his mother: "Behold, you know that my father has become old, and does not see because his eyes are dull, and if I leave him it will be evil in his eyes, because I leave him and go away from you, and my father will be angry, and will curse me. I will not go; when he sends me, then only will I go." And Rebecca said to Jacob: "I will go in and speak to him, and he will send you away." And Rebecca went in and said to Isaac: "I loathe my life because of the two daughters of Heth, whom Esau has taken him as wives; and if Jacob takes a wife from among the daughters of the land such as these, for what purpose

6 do I further live; for the daughters of Canaan are

7 evil."

8 And Isaac called Jacob and blessed him,

9 and admonished him and said to him: "Do

10 not take yourself a wife of any of the daughters of Canaan;

11 arise and go to Mesopotamia, to the house of Bethuel,

12 your mother's father, and take yourself a wife from there

13 of the daughters of Laban, your mother's brother.

14 And God Almighty bless you and increase and

15 multiply you that you may become a company

16 of nations, and give you the blessings of my father

17 Abraham, to you and to your offspring after you, that

18 you may inherit the land of your sojournings and

19 all the land which God gave to Abraham: go, my

20 son, in peace." 12.

21 And Isaac sent Jacob away, and

22 he went to Mesopotamia, to Laban the son of Bethuel

23 the Syrian, the brother of Rebecca, Jacob's mother.

24 And it came to pass after Jacob had arisen to

25 go to Mesopotamia that the spirit of Rebecca was

26 grieved after her son, and she wept.

27 And Isaac

28 said to Rebecca: "My sister, do not weep on account

29 of Jacob, my son; for he goes in peace, and in peace

30 will he return.

31 The Most High God will preserve

32 him from all evil, and will be with him; for He will

33 not forsake him all his days; for I know that

34 his ways will be prospered in all things wherever he

35 goes, until he returns in peace to us, and we see him

36 in peace.

37 Fear not on his account, my sister,

38 for he is on the upright path and he is a perfect man:

39 and he is faithful and will not perish.

40 Do not weep."

41 And Isaac comforted Rebecca on account of her

42 son Jacob, and blessed him.

43 And Jacob went

44 from the Well of the Oath to go to Haran on the

45 first year of the second week in the forty-fourth

46 jubilee, and he came to Luz on the mountains, that is,

47 Bethel, on the new moon of the first month of this

48 week, and he came to the place at evening and turned from the way to the west of the road that night: and he slept there; for the sun had set.

49 And he took one of the stones of that place and laid it (at his head) under the tree and he was journeying alone, and he slept.

50 And he dreamt that night, and behold a ladder set up on the earth, and the top of it reached to heaven, and behold, the angels of the Lord ascended and descended on it: and behold, the Lord stood upon it.

51 And He spoke to Jacob and said: "I am the Lord God of Abraham, your father, and the God of Isaac; the land on which you are sleeping, to you shall I give it, and to your offspring after you.

52 And your offspring will be as the dust of the earth, and you will increase to the west and to the east, to the north and the south, and in you and in your offspring will all the families of the nations be blessed.

53 And behold, I shall be with you, and shall keep you wherever you go, and I shall bring you again into this land in peace; for I shall not leave you until I do everything that I told you."

54 And Jacob awoke from his sleep, and said, "Truly this place is the house of the Lord, and I did not know it."

55 And he was afraid and said: "Dreadful is this place which is none other than the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven." 26.

56 And Jacob arose early in the morning, and took the stone which he had put under his head and set it up as a pillar for a sign, and he poured oil upon the top of it.

57 And he called the name of that place Bethel; but the name of the place was Luz at the first.

58 And Jacob vowed a vow to the Lord, saying: "If the Lord will be with me, and will keep me in this way that I go, and give me bread to eat and clothing to put on, so that I come again to my father's house in peace, then shall the Lord be my God, and this stone which I have set up as a pillar for a sign in this place, shall be the Lord's house, and of all that You give me, I shall give the tenth to You, my God."
