4 Baruch

Chapter 3

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And when the sixth hour of the night was come, whereof the Lord had told Jeremiah, that he should go up with Baruch, they went up within the walls of the city, and abode watching.

2 And there was the sound of a trumpet, and angels came forth from heaven, holding torches of fire in their hands, and stood within the walls of the city.

3 And when Jeremiah and Baruch saw them, they wept, saying: Now we know that the word is true.

4 And Jeremiah besought the angels, saying: I beseech you all, destroy not the city, until I ask one word of the Lord;

5 And the Lord answered the angels, saying: "Destroy not the city, until I speak with Jeremiah my chosen."

6 Then spoke Jeremiah, saying: "I beseech you, my Lord, give me leave to speak with you. And he said to him: Speak, my chosen Jeremiah, whatsoever you will."

7 And Jeremiah said to him: "Behold, now we know, my Lord, that you will deliver the city into the hand of the enemy, and the people shall be carried away to Babylon. What will you that I do with the holy vessels of our ministry, which you have committed to us? and what will you that I do concerning them?"

8 And the Lord said to him: "Take them, and commit them to the earth, and to the house of the sanctuary, saying:"

9 And you, O earth, hear the voice of your Creator, who formed you in the abundance of waters, who sealed you with seven seals: take again your beauty, and guard the vessels of your ministry until the coming of the Beloved.

10 And Jeremiah answered and said: "I beseech you, my Lord, show me what I shall do for Abimelech the Ethiopian; for he has done many kindnesses to the people and to your servant Jeremiah: he was distinguished above all the men of the city, and he drew me out of the miry pit; and I would not that he should see the destruction and desolation of the city, lest he be grieved."

11 And the Lord said to Jeremiah: "Send him into the vineyard of Agrippa, by the mountain way; and I will hide him, until I bring the people again into the city;"

12 And you, Jeremiah, go with the people until you come to the land of Babylon, and abide prophesying to them, until I bring them into their city;

13 But leave you Baruch here in Jerusalem.

14 And when the Lord had spoken all this to Jeremiah, he departed from Jeremiah into heaven.

15 And Jeremiah and Baruch entered into the house of the sanctuary, and committed all the vessels of their ministry to the earth, as the Lord had commanded them; and straightway the earth swallowed them, and they twain abode and wept.

16 And in the morning Jeremiah sent Abimelech, saying: Take a basket, and go by the mountain way into the vineyard of Agrippa, and bring a few figs for the sick of the people; for the favor of the Lord is upon your head, and his glory. And he went as he commanded him.
