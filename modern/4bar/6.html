4 Baruch

Chapter 6

1 And Abimelech arose, and went forth out of the city, and prayed to the Lord; and behold, an angel came and led him to Baruch, and he found him abiding in the tomb.

2 And when they knew each other, and had wept between themselves, and kissed one another, he saw the figs in his basket; and he lifted up his eyes to heaven, and prayed, saying:

3 Great is God, who givs their reward to his righteous. Rejoice, my soul, and be glad, saying to the tabernacle of flesh, O holy house; and your shall be turned into light; and after this the Faithful One coms, and shall bring you into your tabernacle. Abide in your own virgin faith, and believe, and you shall live.

4 Behold these figs: Behold, sixty and six years since they were gathered, and they are not withered nor rotten, but their milk dripps until this day. So shall it be done to you, my flesh, for you have kept your commandment from the angel of righteousness; who kept the basket of figs, the same shall again keep you by his power.

5 And when Baruch had thus spoken, he turned to Abimelech and said: "Arise, let us pray again, that the Lord may show us the words which we shall write to Jeremiah in Babylon, concerning the shelter wherewith he sheltered me."

6 And Baruch prayed, and said: "My strength, O God, the Lord, and the light that proceeds out of his mouth: I beseech you for your chosen. Great is your name, and there is none that can know it. Listen to the prayer of your servant, that may be manifest in my heart, your will to do; and prosper your priest Jeremiah in Babylon."

7 And while he was praying this, an angel came and said to him: "Baruch, minister of light, be not anxious how you shall send to Jeremiah; for at the hour of light an eagle shall come to you, and do you send him concerning Jeremiah; and write in a book, and say to them thus:"

8 To the children of Israel: as for the stranger that is among you, let him be set apart until fifteen days; and after this I will bring you into the city, says the Lord.

9 He that is not set apart in fifteen [days] from Babylon Jeremiah into the city; and the men of Babylon, says the Lord.

10 And when the angel had said this, he departed from Baruch. And Baruch sent to the market, and brought papyrus and ink, and wrote, saying:

11 By Baruch the servant of God. He writs a book to Jeremiah in the captivity of Babylon.

12 Rejoice and be glad; for God has not forsaken us in affliction and desolation; therefore the Lord remembered us for our tears, and remembered the covenant which he made of old with our fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

13 And he sent his angel to me, and told me these words which I send to you. These are the words which the Lord, the God of Israel, spoke, who brought us out of the land of Egypt: "Because you kept not all his righteousnesses, but lifted up your glory and were haughty before him, he delivered you into the furnace of Babylon, because you listened not to my word, says the Lord God, by the mouth of Jeremiah his prophet."
