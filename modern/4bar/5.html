4 Baruch

Chapter 5

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And Abimelech took the figs at the time of midday, as Jeremiah had sent him; and he found a shady tree, and abode and rested, to take a little rest; and he leaned his head upon the basket of figs, and slept sixty and six years, and awoke not from his sleep.

2 And after a time he arose and awoke from his sleep, and said: "I have slept sweetly a little while; for my head is heavy, and I have not had my fill of sleep."

3 And he uncovered that basket of figs, and found the figs fresh, and their milk dripping.

4 And he desired to sleep again, for his head was heavy, and he was not sated with slumber; and he said: "I fear lest I sleep and be late, lest my father Jeremiah rebuke me; for he sent me early in the morning. And now I will arise and go; for is there not heat, is there not toil, every day?"

5 And he arose, and took up the basket of his figs, and entered into the city Jerusalem; and he knew not the city, nor his house. And he said: "Blessed are you, O Lord; for a great trance is fallen upon me."

6 And he said: "Is this indeed the city Jerusalem? I have surely gone astray, for I came by the mountain way; and if my head be heavy and I have not had my fill of sleep, my heart is perplexed within me; and how shall I declare this thing to Jeremiah, that the city is hidden from me?"

7 And he sought out all the landmarks that were in the city, to know whether this were Jerusalem. And he went again into the city, and sought if he knew any, and found none. And he said: "Blessed are you, O Lord; for a great trance is fallen upon me. And he went again out of the city afar off, and abode mourning; and where he would go, he knew not."

8 And he set down that basket of figs, and said: "I will abide here, until the Lord take this trance from me. And after he had sat, he saw an old man coming from the field. And Abimelech said to him: To you I speak, you old man: which is this city? And the old man said to him: This is Jerusalem."

9 And Abimelech said to him: "Where is Jeremiah the priest, and Baruch the Levite, and all the people of this city? for I have found none. And the old man said to him: Are you not of this city? and do you remember Jeremiah even now, that you ask concerning him after all this time?"

10 But Jeremiah is in Babylon with the people; for they were taken captive, and delivered into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Persia; and he went there to prophesy to them.

11 And when Abimelech heard this from the old man, Abimelech said to him: "If you were not an old man, I would laugh you to scorn; but it is not meet that men should mock an old man; and were it not so, [I would show you]."

12 But as for that you say, that the people were taken captive into Babylon — though the floods of heaven had descended upon them, there has not been time for them to go into Babylon; and you say the people were taken captive into Babylon!

13 And I, as my father Jeremiah sent me, went into the vineyard of Agrippa for a few figs, to give to the sick that are among the people. I went and came there, and took that which he commanded me, and returned; and as I went I found a tree, and abode under it to rest, for it was midday; and there I leaned upon the basket of figs, and slept; and I awoke, thinking I had been late, and uncovered this basket of figs, and found them dripping with milk, as when I gathered them, my chosen ones. And you say the people were taken captive into Babylon!

14 But behold, see that my figs are not withered. And he uncovered for him the basket of figs, and showed him; and the old man looked, and Behold, the figs were fresh, and their milk dripping.

15 Then that old man marvelled, and said to Abimelech: "You are righteous, my son; for God would not that you should see the destruction of the city, and God brought this trance upon you, and kept you alive. Behold, it is sixty and six years this day since the people were taken captive into Babylon."

16 And if you will know and be sure, my son, look and see in your field that the crops have not put forth their increase, and that it is not the season for my figs; and know that the time is not the time for all this.

17 Then Abimelech cried with a loud voice, saying: "I bless you, O Lord my God; the God of heaven and earth; the rest of the souls of the righteous in all the world."

18 And he said to the old man: "What month is this? And he said to him: The tenth and second of the month Nisan, which is Miyazya. And after this Abimelech gave to that old man of these figs, and said to him: God lighten your way to the city above, Jerusalem."
