4 Baruch

Chapter 2

1 And Jeremiah rent his garments, and cast ashes upon his head, and entered into the house of the sanctuary.

2 And Baruch saw Jeremiah [with] ashes strewn upon his head, and his garments rent; and he cried with a loud voice, saying: "My father Jeremiah, what ails you? and what sin have the people committed? For when the people sinned, Jeremiah mourned, and cast ashes upon his head, and prayed for the people, that their iniquity might be forgiven them."

3 And Baruch asked him, saying: My father Jeremiah, what ails you, and what has become of the people?

4 And Jeremiah said to him: "Take heed that we rend not our garments, but let us rend our hearts; and let us put no water into the basins, that we may weep bitterly until we fill them with our tears: for from henceforth he will have no mercy on this people."

5 And Baruch said: "My father Jeremiah, what ails you?"

6 And Jeremiah said to him: "God will deliver the city into the hand of the king of the Chaldeans; for he shall lead the people captive in affliction."

7 And when Baruch heard all this, he rent his garments, and said: "My father Jeremiah, who has showed you this?"

8 And Jeremiah said to him: "Abide with me until the sixth hour of the night, that you may know that the word is true."

9 And they abode in the house of the sanctuary, weeping.
