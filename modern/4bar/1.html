4 Baruch

Chapter 1

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And it came to pass, when the king of the Chaldeans had taken the children of Israel captive, that the Lord spoke to Jeremiah, saying:

2 Jeremiah, my chosen, arise and go forth out of this city, you and Baruch; for I will destroy it because of the multitude of the sins of them that dwell in it. For your prayer is as a strong pillar in the midst of the city, and as a wall of adamant round about it. Now therefore arise you, and go and depart, before the host of the Chaldeans come and compass the city.

3 And Jeremiah answered, saying: "I beseech you, my Lord, suffer your servant to speak before you."

4 And the Lord said to him: "Speak, my chosen Jeremiah."

5 And Jeremiah answered and said: "O Lord of all, will you deliver this chosen city into the hands of the Chaldeans, that the king with his people may boast and say, I have prevailed against the city of God?"

6 No, my Lord; but if it please you, destroy it with your own hands.

7 And the Lord said to Jeremiah: "Because you are my chosen, arise and go forth, you and Baruch; for I will destroy it because of the sins of them that dwell in it. And neither the king nor his host shall be able to enter into the city, except I first open and enlarge the gates of it."

8 Arise now, and go to Baruch, and tell him this word; and when the sixth hour of the night has come, enter you within the walls of the city, and I will show you: for except I first destroy the city, they shall not be able to enter into it.

9 And when the Lord had spoken these things, he departed from Jeremiah.
