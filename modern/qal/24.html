Chapter 24

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And anoint him with the oil of gladness who desires baptism, before you baptize him with water; and after baptism seal him with the oil of divinity which I have given you — that is the oil of myron, in which is the fulfillment of all. And let the believers stand on their feet to hear the holy books of divinity.
