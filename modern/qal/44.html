Qalementos

Chapter 44

1 Concerning the consecration of the tabot. (1) And sanctify every tabot of the Law, and seal it with the seal of God, which is the meron — the anointing of gladness. (2) And let the seven priests be with you at the time you consecrate it; for they are they that come after us (3) And seal the tabots and the altars with the seal of God, that there may be found in it — in which they consecrate upon it — the of God. (4) And seal with this holy anointing every one that is baptized; and they shall find in it priesthood and kingdom, and prophecy shall abound through the anointing of meron, and through its glory.
