Qalementos

Chapter 38

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 (1) And keep the feast, from his resurrection, in the eighth season, which is the New Sunday. (2) For in it he came to us — to the company of the twelve — after his resurrection from the dead, while we were gathered in the upper room in Zion, the doors being shut. (3) For on this day Thomas found and touched with his hand the place of the nails in the side of our Lord Christ, and the place of the wounds — even to the inward part, the tips of his fingers; and our God made them new and gave them life. (4) And he said to him: Doubt not, Thomas, but be believing.
