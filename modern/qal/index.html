THE ETHIOPIC CLEMENT

Qalementos

Qalementos, the Ethiopic Clement, in sixty-seven chapters, is a large collection of teaching presented in the name of Clement, the apostolic father, concerning the ordering of the church and the Christian life. It gathers instruction on ministry, worship, conduct, and community discipline, and it stands in the same broad family as the other church-order books in this part of the canon, while carrying its own Ethiopian voice and shape, preserved in Ge'ez when other versions were lost or scattered.

For this edition Qalementos was translated anew, directly from the Ge'ez, from the project's Ge'ez corpus at chapter level. Books like this survive in English only in rare, scattered, or long-unavailable editions, which is why a fresh translation from the Ge'ez was needed to place it here, complete and readable alongside its companions. It belongs in this canon because the Ethiopian Church counts Qalementos among its Scriptures — one of the clearest examples of how much wider Ethiopia's Bible is than the sixty-six books most readers know.

Watch for how Clement's name is used — not as decoration, but as a claim that this ordering reaches back to the apostles.
