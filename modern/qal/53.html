Chapter 53

1 (1) And let there be in every house of the sanctuary two altars: the one which they call ʾəmäkan within the place — like the stones of the children of Israel which was in the wilderness, which they call ʾəmäkan within the place — and the other, which they do not call by its ʾəmäkan.
