Chapter 11

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Teach the children of the dead, and guard the virgins and strengthen them; and make not a path for Satan over you, and thrust away his — accursed — by fasting and by prayer and nightly vigil. And let alms be your sign, and your house and your dwelling; and guard the children of baptism and of the faith.
