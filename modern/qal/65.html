Chapter 65

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 (1) I say to you, my son Clement, and to those like you, in: that you may open the midst of the believers, and command them that which is fitting, in that which the Holy Spirit has showed you, which fulfills (2) And whoever transgresses and turns aside — let him be accursed, until the day of judgment, when he stands before Christ.
