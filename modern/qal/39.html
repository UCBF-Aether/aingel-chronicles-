Qalementos

Chapter 39

1 (1) And keep the feast of his ascension into heaven, and the feast of the descent of the Holy Spirit upon us the apostles. (2) For he descended upon our mouths from heaven, like tongues of fire, and taught us the word which we knew not; and we had not heard it aforetime, neither did we understand. (3) And this was after the ascension of Christ into heaven — who returned not to us — in a chariot of light, from the Mount of Olives; which is forty days after his resurrection.
