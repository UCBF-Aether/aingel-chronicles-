Qalementos

Chapter 42

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 (1) And know, my son, that incense is frankincense — for frankincense is a great incense which is burned before God; for our Lady, the Mother of Light, spoke to me and gave it me from him. (2) And it is what men bring as sägälə, a present [to God]. (3) But let its fragrance be for the burial of the bodies of the priests, that they rot not; and let them not bury them in the earth, but with longing above the earth.
