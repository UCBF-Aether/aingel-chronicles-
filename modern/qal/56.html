Chapter 56

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 (1) And if a layman come to the holy altar of God at the time of the consecration — his seeking glory from his neighbor — let them rebuke him, and let him stand at the door of the house of the Christians seven years, and let him show his repentance to men.
