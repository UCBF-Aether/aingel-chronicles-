THE SECOND GENERAL EPISTLE OF PETER

2 Peter

Second Peter is a short, urgent letter in three chapters, written to believers who needed to hold fast while false teachers spread confusion and scoffed at Christ's return. It calls its readers to grow in goodness, knowledge, self-control, and love, and it insists that prophecy did not come from human ideas, but from people moved by the Holy Spirit. The warning is plain, and so is the hope: a day is coming when the Lord will make all things new, so live ready for it now.

In this edition the text is the King James Version of 1611, received as public-domain text. It stands here because the Ethiopian canon, like the wider church's, keeps 2 Peter among the New Testament letters, and its voice completes Peter's witness alongside 1 Peter. Read it slowly; its three chapters are dense and direct.

Watch for how often Peter tells you to remember — remembrance is his guard against drifting away.
