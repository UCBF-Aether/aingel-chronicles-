1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 32

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Let them say the daily hymn of praise in the Church, each of them at his own time, thus.

DAILY HYMN OF PRAISE

2 The grace of our Lord [be] with you all.

3 The people: And with your spirit.

4 The priest: Praise you the Lord.

5 The people: It is fitting and right.

6 The priest: You, O Father of incorruptibility, Deliverer of our souls, Confirmer of the thoughts, and Guardian of our hearts, who have illumined our hearts and have brought to an end the darkness of our intelligence, by the knowledge which is in You; who have by the cross of Your Only - begotten brought back anew to incorruptibility the old man which was given over to corruption; who have brought error to an end, and by Your commandments have made man‘ to pass to immortality; who sought that which was lost, we [Your] servants [and] also [Your] people praise.

7 The people: We praise You, and the rest.

8 The priest: We praise You, O Lord, whom continually the unceasing doxologies of the Archangels singing praise, and the hymns of praise of Glories and chants of Dominions praise.

9 We praise You, O Lord, who sent Your Thought, Your Word, Your Wisdom, Your Energy,’ [namely] Him who [is] of old, and was with You before the worlds, the uncreated Word of the Uncreated one, but appeared, incarnate, in the end of times, for the salvation of created man, Your beloved Son Jesus Christ, who made us free from the yoke of slavery.

10 Therefore we also, as we are accustomed, [we] Your servants, O Lord [and] also [your] people, praise You.

11 The people: We praise You, and the rest.

12 The priest: We sing to You a triple hymn of praise from our hearts, O Lord who give life, to You who visit the souls of the poor, and neglect not the spirits of those who are afflicted, the Assister of those who are persecuted, the Helper of those who are tossed on the sea, the Deliverer of those who are buffeted, the Provider for those who are hungry, who take vengeance for those who are wronged, the Lover of the faithful, the Companion of the saints, the Habitation of the pure, the Dwelling-place of those who call on You in truth, the Protector of widows, the Liberator of orphans, who give to Your Church a right government, and have founded in it love-feasts, ministrations, receptions’ of the faithful, the partaking of the Spirit, gifts of grace and powers.

13 We praise You; we cease not alway in our hearts picturing the image of Your kingdom in ourselves, for Your sake [and] also [for the sake] of Your beloved Son Jesus Christ, by whom [be] praise and might to You with the Holy Spirit, for ever and ever, Amen.

14 And let the people say: Amen.

15 But if also any one says prophetical words, let him say [them]; he has a reward.

16 But at midnight let the sons of priestly service, and those of the people who are more perfect, give praise by themselves. For also in that hour our Lord, rising, praised His Father.

17 Of the Lord, walks as He walked in this world, that where He is, there he may be also.
