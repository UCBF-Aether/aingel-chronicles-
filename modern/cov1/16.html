1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 16

1 Then Martha and Mary, and Salome, who were with us,‘ answered:—Yes, O our Lord, teach us to know what we ought to do, that our souls may live to You.

2 Then Jesus answered them: I will that, persevering in supplication,’ you should always serve My Gospel, and be examples of holiness, for the salvation of those who trust patiently’ in Me; and in all things be figures of the kingdom of heaven.
