1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 45

OF THE READER

1 A reader is instituted [who is pure, quiet, meek,’ wise, with much experience, learned and of much learning, with a good memory, vigilant, so that he may deserve a higher degree? First let the book be given him in the sight of the people, on the first day of the week. But a hand is not laid on him. But he hears from the bishop [the following].

2 You, O N., whom Christ has called to be a minister’ of His words, be careful, and strive that you may appear approved both in this rule and in a higher degree, even by our Lord Jesus Christ; so that He in His everlasting habitations may pay you a good reward for these things.

3 And let the priests say: So be it, so be it, so be it.
