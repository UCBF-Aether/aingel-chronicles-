THE FIRST BOOK OF THE COVENANT

1 Book of the Covenant

The First Book of the Covenant, in forty-seven chapters, is Book One of the work also known as the Testament of Our Lord. It presents itself as the risen Lord's ordering for his church: teaching on the end times, on bishops and presbyters, on prayer, the Eucharist, widows, virgins, and the care of the poor. It reads like a bridge between the Gospels and the church-order books that follow it here — Christ still speaking, now about how his people should be organized and how they should live together.

In this edition the text is the English of J. Cooper and A. J. Maclean, The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac and published in 1902, received as public-domain text with small repairs against the printed edition. That matters for honesty: the two Books of the Covenant have had a Syriac twin in English since 1902, so this is not a new discovery. They belong in this canon because the Ethiopian Church kept them as Scripture.

Watch for chapter nineteen — unnumbered in the Syriac, its content recovered here from the edition's own page headers.
