1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 41

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Let the appointment! Be thus. As she prays at the entrance of the altar and looks down, let the bishop say quietly, so that the priests may hear, thus.

PRAYER OF THE INSTITUTION? OF WIDOWS WHO SIT IN FRONT

2 O God, the Holy One, the Most High, who see the [things] that are humble, who have chosen the weak and the mighty; the Honoured One who have created also those [things] which are despised; give, O Lord, the spirit of power to this Your handmaid, and strengthen her with Your truth, so that doing Your commandment and serving in the house of Your sanctuary, she may be an honoured vessel to You, and may glorify!?

3 [You] in the day when You will glorify Your poor, O Lord. And grant to her power cheerfully to accomplish Your teachings which You have determined for a rule for Your handmaid. Grant to her, O Lord, the spirit of meekness and of power and of patience and of kindness, so that, bearing with ineffable joy Your burden, she may endure labours.

4 Yes, O Lord God, who know our weakness, perfect Your handmaid for the praise of Your house; strengthen her for edification and a good example,’ sanctify [her], make [her] wise; comfort [her] O God; for blessed and glorious” is Your kingdom, O God the Father.

5 And to You [be] praise, and to Your Only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, and to the Holy Spirit [who is] good and adorable and the Maker of life,” and of equal essence with You,” now and before all the worlds and for the ages and for ever and ever. The people: Amen.
