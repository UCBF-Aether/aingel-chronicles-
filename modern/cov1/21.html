1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 21

1 Being such as this, let him receive ordination” on the first day of the week, all consenting to his appointment, and bearing witness to him, with all the neighbouring presbyters and bishops. Let those bishops lay hands on him, having first washed their hands, but let the presbyters stand beside them, not speaking, in fear, lifting up their hearts in silence.

2 After [that], let the bishops lay hands on him, saying.

3 We lay hands on the servant of God, who has been chosen in the Spirit, for the true and pious disposing of the Church, which alone has the principality, and which is not dissolved, of the invisible [and] living God, and for the delivering of true judgment and divine and holy revelations, and of divine gifts and faithful doctrines of the Trinity, by the cross, by the resurrection, by the incorruptibility, in the holy Church of God.

4 After this, one bishop, commanded by the other bishops, shall lay hands on him, saying his calling of appointment, thus.

5 Were made by You; who have given to them to keep Your commandments in fear; who have bestowed upon us the understanding of the truth, and have made known to us that good Spirit of Yours; who sent Your beloved Son, the only Savior, without spot, for our salvation; O God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, Father of mercies and God of all comfort who in the pure heights dwell eternally, who are high and adorable, dreadful and great; who see all things, who know all things before they are, with whom all things were before they were [made]; who gave illumination to the Church by the grace of Your Only-begotten Son, having foreordained from the beginning those who delight in just things, and do those things that are holy, to dwell in Your habitations; who chose Abraham, who pleased You by his faith, and translated holy Enoch to the treasure-house of life; who have ordered princes and priests in Your upper” sanctuary; O Lord, who called [them] to praise and glorify the Name of You and of Your Only-begotten in the place of Your glory; O Lord God, who before the foundation of the world did not leave Your upper’ sanctuary without a ministry, and also, since the foundation of the world, have adorned and glorified Your sanctuaries [on earth] with faithful princes and priests, after the pattern of Yours [own] heaven; You, Lord, even now also are well pleased to be praised, and have vouchsafed that there should be princes for Your people: Cause to shine forth and pour out understanding, and the grace which comes from Your princely Spirit, which you did deliver to Your beloved Son Jesus Christ; give wisdom, O God, [give] reasoning,” strength, power, unity of spirit to do all things by Your co-operation.

6 Give the Spirit which is Yours, O holy God; send to Your holy and pure Church, and to every place which sings to You “Holy,” Him who was given to Your Holy One; and grant, O Lord, that this Your servant may please You for doxology, and for laud without ceasing, O God, for fitting hymns of praise, and for suitable times, for acceptable prayers, for faithful asking, for an upright mind, for a meek heart, for the working of life and of meekness and of truth, for the knowledge of uprightness.

7 O Father, who know the hearts, [grant] to this Your servant whom You have chosen for the episcopate, to feed Your holy flock, and to stand at the head of the priesthood without fault, ministering to You day and night; grant that Your face may be seen by him; vouchsafe, O Lord, that he may offer to You the offerings of Your holy Church carefully [and] with all fear; bestow upon him that he may have Your powerful’ Spirit to loose all bands, as you did bestow [Him] on Your apostles, to please You in meekness; fill him full of love, knowledge, understanding, discipline, perfectness, strength, and a pure heart, when he prays for the people, and when he mourns for those who commit folly and draws them to [receive] help; when he offers to You praises and thanksgivings and prayers for a sweet-smelling fragrance through Your beloved Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom [are given] to You praise and honor and might, with the Holy Spirit, both before the worlds, and also now, and at all times, and for ever and ever without end.

8 Amen.

9 And let the people say: Amen. And then let them cry out: He is worthy, He is worthy, He is worthy.

10 After he is [ordained], let the people keep the feast three days, according to the mystery that in three days [our

11 Lord] rose from the dead. And let every one give him the Peace.
