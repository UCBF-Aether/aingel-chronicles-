1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 1

1 And we returned answer, saying, Lord, what is the Holy Spirit, and what is His power, whom you bade us to ask for? And our Lord said to us, Truly I say to you that you shall not be the children of the light except by the Holy Spirit. And we returned answer to Him, and said, O our Lord, give us this [Spirit].

2 And immediately Jesus breathed on us.’ And after we had received the Holy Spirit, He said to us, Truly I say to you, that you who have been made disciples to the kingdom of heaven, and who have believed in Me with undoubting heart, and have cleaved to Me, shall be with Me; and all those who through you know and do the will of My Father, who keep My words and know My sufferings, shall be made holy, and shall dwell in the habitations of My Father, and shall be delivered from the evil days that are about to come; and I will be with them, showing them My ways in which they shall live.
