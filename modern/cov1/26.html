1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 26

1 At early dawn let the bishop assemble the people, so that the service may be finished before the rising of the sun.

2 When he says the First Hymn of Praise, of the Dawn, the presbyters and deacons and the rest, the faithful also, [standing] close by, let him say thus.

3 Praise to the Lord.

4 And let the people say: It is fitting and right.

HYMN OF PRAISE FOR THE DAWN

5 The bishop: It is fitting and right that we should praise and laud and give thanks to You, who made all, ineffable God.

6 Stretching forth! Our souls upward, we raise to You, O Lord, a hymn of praise for the morning,—to You who are all-wise, powerful, great in mercies, O God, the Confirmer and Raiser-up of our souls; we praise You, the Word who before the worlds were begotten of the Father, and rest alone with Your saints who are praised with the hymns of the archangels——You the Maker, who were not made with hands, and who make known holy things which are invisible, pure, and spotless——You who have made known to us the hidden mysteries of wisdom, and promised to us immortal light; we lift up praise to You in pure holiness, we Your servants, O Lord.

7 And let the people say: We praise You, we bless You, we give thanks to You, O Lord; and we beg you, O our God.

8 Also [let] the bishop say: O God, the Begetter of light, the Principle of life, the Giver of knowledge, the Gift of grace, the Maker of souls, who make things [that are] beautiful, the Giver of the Holy Spirit, the Treasure of Wisdom, and the Maker of good things, the Lord, the Teacher of holiness, who rule the worlds by Your will, the Receiver of pure prayers; we praise You, the Only-begotten Son, the First-born and Word of the Father, who bestowed all Your grace on us who call upon You, the Helper, and upon the Father who begat You; who have an essence that cannot be injured, where neither moth nor worm! Does corrupt; who give to all that with all their heart trust in You those things which the angels have desired to behold; who are the Guardian of light eternal and [of] treasures incorruptible; who have by the will of Your Father shed light on the darkness which [is] in us; who from the depth have raised us up to light; who have given us life out of death, and bestowed upon us freedom out of slavery; who by the cross have made us of the household of Your Father, and by Your gospel have guided us to the heights of heaven, and have comforted us by Your prophets; who in Your own Person have made us of the household of God the Father of lights; grant us, O Lord, that we may praise You, our God, so that always with unceasing thanksgiving’ we may speak praises to You, we Your servants, O Lord.

9 The people: We praise You, we bless You, we give thanks to You, we beg you, O our God.

10 Let the bishop also say: We sing to You with our mouths this triple hymn of praise as a figure of Your kingdom, O Son of God, who [are] by eternity; who [are] above all, with the Father; whom all creation praises, trembling with fear of Your Spirit; at whom all nature trembles in fear and [whom] every soul of the righteous blesses; with whom all we have taken refuge; who have made confusion, storms, [and] wind to cease from us; who have been to us an haven of rest, and a place to flee to from corruption; in whom we have hope of eternal salvation; who make the peacefulness of fine weather for those who are buffeted on the seas and with the tempests; who in sicknesses are

THE EUCHARIST

11 Entreated and heal without price; who are with those that are shut up in prison; who have loosed us from the bonds of death; [who are] the Comforter of the poor, and of those who mourn, and of those who have laboured and wearied themselves with the cross; who turn away from us every menace!; who for us have reproved the craft of Satan; who drive away his menaces, and give us courage; who thrust away all error from those that trust in You; whom the prophets and apostles praised secretly: we praise You, O Lord, we lift up to You a doxology, so that, having known You, we may rest in the habitations of life, doing Your will alway.

12 And grant to us, O Lord, to walk according to Your commandments, and in mercy visit us all, both small and great, the prince and his people, the shepherd and his flock; for You, O Lord, are our God, and blessed and praised [is] Your kingdom—the kingdom of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, both before the worlds, and now and always, and for the ages, and for ever and ever without end.

13 And let the people say: Amen.

14 Let them sing psalms and four hymns of praise; one by Moses, and of Solomon, and of the other prophets. Thus: little singing-boys; two virgins; three deacons; three presbyters. And so let the hymn of praise be said by the bishop, or by one of the presbyters.

15 Let wt be said thus: The grace of our Lord [be] with you all. And let the people say: And with your spirit.

16 And let the priest say: Also let us praise our Lord.

17 And let the people say: Meetly and rightly. Let the priest say: Let your hearts be fixed. And let the people say: We have [them fixed] with the Lord.

HYMN OF PRAISE OF THE SEAL

18 O Lord, the Father, the Giver of light, the Author of all power and of all spirits, the Sealer of eternal light, and the Guide of life, the Maker of felicity and immortality, who have made us to pass through material darkness, and have bestowed upon us immaterial light; who have loosed the bonds of disobedience and crowned us with the faith which is Yours; who do not keep far off from Your servants, but are in them always; who do not neglect those [souls] which with labour and in Your fear beg You; who know all things before they are thought, and search out all things before they are considered, and give what You will give before we ask You; who are well pleased to hear’ those who with heart undoubting serve You, O King of the highest lights and the soldiery of heaven, who hear the archangels when they praise You, and are pleased in them; Answer us, O Lord, we beg you.

19 Grant us with boldness [with] unceasing voice to praise You, to laud You, to lift up to You a doxology; so that being guarded by You and guided in light, we Your servants, O Lord, may constantly praise You.

20 The people: We praise You, we bless You, we give thanks to You, O Lord; and we beg you, O our God.

21 The priest: O Lord Jesus, hear us, O Holy One, who were the Voice of the dumb and the irrational, the Strength of the paralysed, the Giver of light to the blind, the Guide of the lame, the Cleanser of the lepers, the Curer of material fluxes, the Healer of the deaf and dumb, the Reprover of death, the Tormentor of darkness, the Ray of light, and the Lamp that is not quenched, the Sun that is not darkened [and] rests [not]; but who always give light to Your saints; who have established all things together for the good likeness of comeliness; who are the well-tempered Reason; who have plainly given light to all; who are the Savior of the sons of men, and the Converter of souls; who are the Provider of all things as is right, the Maker of the angels, who adorn all; the Thought of the Father; who found the worlds in prudence and wisdom, and establish them together; and were sent from Your eternal Father to us; the Intelligence of the Spirit who may not be apprehended or understood, the Maker-known of things invisible; You are glorious, and Your Name is Wonderful.’ Therefore we also, Your servants, O Lord, give praise to You.

22 The people: We praise You, we bless You, we give thanks to You, O Lord; we beg you, O our Lord.

23 The priest: We sing, O holy Lord, this threefold hymn of praise to You, who gave us a faith in You which cannot be loosed, by which you did make us to conquer the bonds of death; who created upright minds in them that trust in You, that they might be gods; who by the Spirit gave to us to tread under foot all the power of the enemy that we! May not profane those things which may not be profaned; who by Your mediation have made friendship for us with Your Father.

24 Answer us Your servants, O Lord, [You] whom without ceasing! We entreat, who at our supplication give [us] power against the adversary; whom alway we ask, as [it were], for the overthrow of the Evil one; Hear us, O King Eternal; comfort the widows, help the orphans; pity and cleanse those who are possessed with unclean spirits, give wisdom to the unwise; convert those who go astray; deliver those who are in prison; guard us all, for You, O Lord, are our God; blessed and glorious is Your kingdom.

25 The people: Amen.
