1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 43

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [Let her hymns of praise be said thus quietly:]

NIGHT HYMN OF PRAISE OF WIDOWS

2 Holy, holy, without spot, who have Your dwelling in the light, God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob, God of Enoch and David, of Elijah, of Elisha, of Moses, of Joshua, and of the prophets and of the others who in truth preached Your Name, God of the Apostles, the God who have guided all things by Your reason and have blessed them who lovingly trust in You; my soul praises You with the power of the spirit of my power, my heart praises You, O Lord, and Your might, alway.

3 Let all my power praise You, O Lord, for if You will, J am Yours, O God, the God of the poor; for You are the Helper of them that lack, and You are He that looks on the meek, and the Assister of the weak; assist me, O Lord, because by Your grace You were well pleased in me that I should be Your handmaid, for You have bestowed upon me a great name, that I should be called a Christian.

4 You who have freed me from servitude that I may serve a servitude to God, the Mighty One who [are] for ever, who see all, that I may praise You uncondemned. Yes, O Lord God, confirm my heart in You until it is perfected in the Holy Spirit.

5 Rejuvenate us for the edification of Your holy Church, Son and Word and Thought of the Father, the Christ who came for the salvation of the race of man, who suffered and were buried, and rose, [and] also were glorified by Him who sent You, turn, help, O Lord, set upright our thoughts by the strong faith of the Spirit. Glorify Your Name in us.

6 For in Your Father and in You and in the Holy Spirit is our hope for ever and ever.

7 With those who are with her let her say: Amen. But let her say the hymn of praise at dawn thus.

HYMNS OF PRAISE AT DAWN OF WIDOWS WHO SIT IN FRONT

8 O Eternal God, Guide of our souls, Maker of light, Treasure of life, who rest! In the praises and prayers of the holy ones, Lover of compassion merciful, kind, King of all, and God, our Lord, my spirit praises [You], sending [up] to You the unceasing voices of Your handmaid, O Lord, who beseeches You that You may perfect in Your handmaid the spirit of reason and of piety and of right knowledge.

9 I praise You, O Lord, who took away from our poverty all disturbance and confusion, wrath and all contention and evil habit, who prepared [and] changed the feelings of my understanding that I might serve You only, O God; who have adorned Your holy Church with various ministries, who drive away from Your handmaid all doubtfulness, fear, weakness; and hold the thoughts of those who rightly serve You; I praise You, O God, who have enlightened me with the light of Your knowledge, through Your Only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom [be] praise and might to You for ever and ever.

10 Amen?

11 And with those who are with her let her say: Amen.
