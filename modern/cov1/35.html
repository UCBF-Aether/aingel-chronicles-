1 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 35

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Let him be in everything as the eye of the Church, with fear admonishing, so that he may be an example to! The people of piety Let him admonish thus.

ADMONITION OF THE DEACON

2 Let us arise.

3 Let every one know his place.

4 Let the catechumens depart.

5 [Lift] up the eyes of your hearts.

6 The angels are looking on.

7 Let us beg in concord.

8 Let no sexually immoral person, no wrathful man? [remain]; if one who is a servant of evil be [here], let him withdraw.

9 Our Lord and our God and our Savior Jesus Christ.

10 When the presbyter or bishop begins the prayer, let the people pray and kneel.

11 Then let the deacon say thus.

12 For the peace which is from heaven let us beg, that the Lord in His mercy may give us peace.

13 For our faith let us beg, that the Lord may grant to us to keep truly to the end the faith which is in Him.

14 For harmony and concord let us beg, that the Lord may keep us together in concord of the Spirit.

15 For patience let us beg, that the Lord may bestow [upon us patience to the end in all afflictions.

16 For the Apostles let us beg, that the Lord may grant to us to please Him, as they also pleased Him, and may make us worthy of their inheritance.

17 For the holy prophets let us beg, that the Lord may number us with them.

18 For the holy confessors let us beg, that the Lord God may grant us to fulfill [our course] with the same mind [as they].

19 For the bishop let us beg, that our Lord may grant him to us for length of days’ in faith, rightly dividing the word of truth, and standing at the head of the Church purely and without blame.

20 For the presbyterate let us beg, that the Lord may not take away from them the spirit of the presbyterate, but bestow on them earnestness and piety until the end.

21 For the deacons let us beg, that the Lord may grant to them to run a perfect course, and to perfect holiness, and that He may remember their work and their love.

22 For the presbyteresses let us beg, that the Lord may hear their supplications and keep their hearts perfectly in the grace of the Spirit and help their work.

23 For the subdeacons, readers, deaconesses let us beg, that the Lord may grant to them to receive a reward in patience.

24 For the faithful laymen let us beg, that the Lord may grant to them to keep the faith perfectly.

25 For the catechumens let us beg, that the Lord may grant to them to be counted worthy of the laver of forgiveness, and may sanctify them with the seal of holiness.

26 For the kingdom let us beg, that the Lord may bestow upon it tranquillity.

27 For the exalted powers let us beg, that the Lord may grant to them prudence and the fear of Him.

28 For all the world let us beg, that the Lord may provide for each one such things as are fitting.

29 For those who travel by sea, and those who go on journeys let us beg, that the Lord may guide them with the right hand of mercy.

30 For those who are persecuted let us beg, that the Lord may grant to them patience and knowledge, and may bestow on them also a completed labour.

31 For those who have fallen asleep from the Church let us beg, that the Lord may bestow upon them a place of rest.

32 For those who have fallen let us beg, that the Lord may not remember their follies to them, but moderate [His] threats to them.

33 And let us all also, who need prayer, beg that the Lord may protect and keep us with the peaceful Spirit.

34 Let us persuade and beg the Lord, that He may receive our prayers.

35 After the deacon commemorates, let the bishop make a sign with his hand.

36 Let the deacon say: Let us arise in the Holy Spirit, that, being made wise, we may grow in His grace, boasting in His Name; being built on the foundation of the Apostles,’ let us beg [and] beg the Lord that, being persuaded, He may receive our prayers.

37 Then let the bishop complete [the prayer]. And let the people say: Amen.
