Sirach

Chapter 32

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 If you are made the master [of a feast,] lift not yourself up, but be among them as one of the rest; take diligent care for them, and so sit down.

2 And when you have done all your office, take your place, that you may be merry with them, and receive a crown for your well ordering of the feast.

3 Speak, you who are the elder, for it becomes you, but with sound judgment; and hinder not musick.

4 Do not pour out words where there is a musician, and do not show forth wisdom out of time.

5 A concert of musick in a banquet of wine is as a signet of carbuncle set in gold.

6 As a signet of an emerald set in a work of gold, so is the melody of musick with pleasant wine.

7 Speak, young man, if there is need of you: and yet scarcely when you are twice asked.

8 Let your speech be short, comprehending much in few words; be as one who knows and yet holds his tongue.

9 If you are among great men, do not make yourself equal with them; and when ancient men are in place, do not use many words.

10 Before the thunder goes lightning; and before a shamefaced man shall go favor.

11 Rise up early, and do not be the last; but get you home without delay.

12 There take your pastime, and do what you will: but sin not by proud speech.

13 And for these things bless him who made you, and has replenished you with his good things.

14 Whoever fears the Lord will receive his discipline; and those who seek him early shall find favor.

15 He who seeks the law shall be filled with it: but the hypocrite will be offended at that.

16 Those who fear the Lord shall find judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light.

17 A sinful man will not be reproved, but finds an excuse according to his will.

18 A man of counsel will be considerate; but a strange and proud man is not daunted with fear, even when of himself he has done without counsel.

19 Do nothing without advice; and when you have once done, repent not.

20 Do not go in a way in which you may fall, and do not stumble among the stones.

21 Do not be confident in a plain way.

22 And beware of your own children.

23 In every good work trust your own soul; for this is the keeping of the commandments.

24 He who believes in the Lord takes heed to the commandment; and he who trusts in him shall fare never the worse.
