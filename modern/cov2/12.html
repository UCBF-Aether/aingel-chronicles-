2 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 12

1 Let the end of Pascha be after the Saturday, at midnight.

2 [At] Pentecost let no one fast or kneel. For these are days of rest and joy.

3 Let those who bear the burdens of labour refresh themselves a little in the days of Pentecost, and on every first day of the week.

4 Let the bishop, before he offers the Offering, say what is fitting for the Offering, while those who are clothed in white receive from one another and say [to one another] Hallelujah.
