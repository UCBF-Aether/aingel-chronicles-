2 Book of the Covenant

Chapter 13

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

THE AGAPE

1 In the supper or feast, let those who have come together receive [a portion ] thus from the shepherd, as for a blessing. But let not a catechumen receive.

2 If any one be of the household of, or related to, one who is a teacher of heathenism,’ let him not accord with him and give praise with him, also let him not eat with him because of relationship or for concord, lest he deliver ineffable things to a wolf and he receive judgment.

3 Let those who are called with the bishop to the house of one who is faithful, eat with gravity and knowledge, not with drunkenness or to debauchery, and not so that he who is present may laugh, or so as to annoy the household" of him

FIRST FRUITS, PROPERTY

4 That called him; but so let them enter that he who called [them] may pray that the saints may enter into his house, For you are the salt of the earth,) [as] you have heard.

5 Because when they eat, let them eat abundantly, [but] so that there may be left over both for you [and] also for those to whom he that called you wishes [to] send, so that he may have them as foods‘ left over by the saints, and that he may rejoice at that which remains over.

6 Let those who come to a feast, being called, not stretch out” a hand before them that are elder. But let the last eat when the first shall have done.

7 Let not those who eat strive in speech, but let them eat in silence; but if any one desire, or the bishop or presbyter ask [a question], let him return answer.
