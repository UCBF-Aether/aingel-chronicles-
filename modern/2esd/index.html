THE SECOND BOOK OF ESDRAS

2 Esdras

2 Esdras is a book of hard questions asked after disaster. Writing in the name of Ezra, its author wrestles with why Jerusalem fell, why the righteous suffer, and how God can be both just and merciful, in conversations with the angel Uriel and in strange, vivid visions. Its 16 chapters include the famous vision of an eagle, a mourning woman who becomes a city, and a man rising from the sea. Western churches largely set it aside, but the Ethiopian Church kept it, and readers have turned to it for centuries in times of loss. In this edition the text is the King James Version's Apocrypha, printed as received. It belongs here as the Ethiopian canon's great book of lament and hope after catastrophe. Watch for the moment the mourning woman is transformed, when private grief opens into a vision of Zion restored.
