Teach

Chapter 3

16 He was crucified for our sake, and by his cross he became our life, our perfect [one]; by this, which is the hidden mystery, the perfect [one], which, which is in him.

17 No desire of man can remove the soul far from God; it cannot; whereas; glory; love does not turn away from God.

18 And by these lips the word cannot be precise [concerning] the things which diligently; hidden; now the mystery is revealed, to the believers; not that it may be seen, but that it may be meditated; this is the cross; and in it let us glorify, that we may exalt [him].

19 And by it they that are baptized are perfect believers; they have saved their souls from all that was said, from all that appears but does not exist in reality.

20 By this, you who shall become strong; examine yourselves, make deaf your ears; and for the things that appear make blind your eyes to what is manifest.

21 That you may know the will of Jesus Christ, and all the mysteries of your salvation, O men and women, saints who are among you, in glorification in the Lord.

22 Let the inward man hear this. Christ put us under the yoke; he brought us near; having come, after he had suffered, he went down into Sheol, of the dead he took captive to life. (the middle of the verse is textually corrupt; the edition declines to translate it) Who is this? Man he put on.
