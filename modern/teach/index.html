THE TEACHING OF MYSTERIES

Teach

Bonus Appendix

The Teaching of the Mysteries, Temherta Hebu'at in Ge'ez, is a short, hidden-feeling work in five chapters and twenty-seven verses, included here as a bonus appendix — a classic of Ethiopian Christian literature, not part of the eighty-one-book canon and not in the 365-day plan. It speaks of mysteries kept and revealed: deep teaching about God, creation, and the unseen order, in a voice meant for readers ready to sit quietly with things that are not explained quickly or carelessly.

For this edition it was translated anew, directly from the Ge'ez. Chapters two to four come from the printed Ge'ez of Lifchitz and Grébaut in 1940, pages forty-four to fifty-two; chapters one and five come from the project's Ge'ez corpus. That 1940 edition published the Ge'ez with a French translation only, and no earlier published English translation is known — this is the project's one verified whole-book first English translation, in that documented form. Its French was consulted as a reference, never republished.

Watch for the reverent restraint of the language — some things are named carefully, and left mysterious on purpose.
