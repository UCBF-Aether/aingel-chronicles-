Teach

Chapter 1

1 Concerning the hidden teaching, which was spoken of old by Peter Fora, to the hidden believers. The hidden teaching is as follows: He who was before, and is; who came; who suffered; and died; and was buried; and rose again. He broke [the gates] of death; he opened [them]; and the dead arose. Was it not he alone who became man — whose flesh is of the Holy Spirit — for Adam, with his soul; who did not leave his soul in death; who in the Spirit called to Adam, and made him live.
