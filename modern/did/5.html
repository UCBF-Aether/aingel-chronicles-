Didascalia

Chapter 5

THAT THE BISHOP OUGHT NOT TO HEARKEN TO THE WORD OF ACCUSATION FROM AN EVIL MAN WHO Speaks FALSELY AGAINST HIS Neighbor

1 But do you put your trust in God and order the people as is fitting. And let not the son magnify himself against his father, nor the servant against his lord,

2 Return on himself.’ And if he beget a son, and he see all the sin of his father that he commits, and fear, and do not likewise, and have not eaten upon the mountains, nor lifted his eyes towards the thoughts of the house of Israel, nor defiled his neighbor’s wife, nor oppressed any man, but have returned the pledge of his debt, and have not robbed (his neighbors) nor plundered, but have given of his food to the hungry, and clothed the naked with his garment, and have turned his hand from wrong, and have not taken interest or bribes wrongfully, but have done righteousness, and walked in my commandment, he shall not die in his father’s sin, but shall surely live for ever.!

3 But his father, if he oppress, and plunder, and commit injustice, and defraud, and do what should not be done in the midst of my people, shall die in his iniquity. And you say, Why is the son requited for his father’s sin? Because!’ the son is righteous and has given alms, and kept my law and done (it), he shall surely live. And the soul that sins, it shall die.

4 But the son shall not be requited for the sin of his father; the righteousness of the righteous (shall be) upon him, and the sin of the sinner on him.

5 If the sinner turn from his sin which he has done, and keep all my law and my commandment, and do righteousness, and (give) alms, he shall surely live, and shall not die; and all his sin which he has done I will not remember to him, but in his righteousness which he has done he shall live.

6 Do I at all desire that the sinner should die, says the Lord, (and not) rather that he should turn from his evil! Way and live? And if the righteous man turn from his righteousness, and commit injustice and sin,

7 In all his iniquity which he has done, the sinner shall die: all his righteousness which he has done I will not remember to him, and in his sin which he has sinned, in it he shall die. But you say, It is not right.. When the righteous man turns from his righteousness and commits sin, he shall die in his iniquity which he has done.

8 And if the sinner turn from his sin which he has done, and do righteousness and uprightness, such a one has kept his soul; and has seen, and turned from all his sin which he has done; he shall surely live, and shall not die. And the house of Israel says, The way of the Lord is not right. Is not my way right, O house of Israel?

9 Is it not your way (which) is not right And for this will I judge all of you according to your way, O house of Israel, says the Lord God. Turn, and repent of your evil, and let not the punishment of your sin come upon you.

10 Put away from you all your evil and your iniquity and your sin which you have sinned against me; and make you a new heart and a new spirit; and why will you die in your sins, O house of Israel? For I desire not that you should in any wise die, says the Lord God, but that you should repent and live.”
