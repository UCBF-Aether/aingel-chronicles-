OF THE SERVICE OF THE HOSTS OF SPIRITUAL ONES AND THE HOLY CHURCH

1 ALL the spiritual hosts of angels are a flame of fire, and the holy cherubim with six wings glorify You with a loud voice, and sing with a voice that is never silent, and say, ‘“ Holy, holy, holy, Lord of Hosts, the holiness of Your glory [is perfect and]! Fills the heavens and the earth.””?

2 The (whole) number of these angels, thrones and authorities, dominions and principalities, and powers, the cherubim and seraphim cry and say, Blessed be the Lord the God of Israel, and blessed be this Church of the nations (which) is like to the host of heaven.

3 Day and night with holy soul and perfect heart thus they sing,’ and say, “The Chariot, of God is ten thousand times ten thousand and thousands of thousands of them that glorify the Lord in the holy mount of Sinai.’ The heavens and all that is in it give humble thanks to You, for You have hung the heavens as a vault, and have laid the foundations of the earth, and established it on the waters; and You have separated darkness from light, and numbered the stars in their fulness, and called them all by their names.

4 The whole creation declares Your greatness, and the trees of the field also You have made to grow. Therefore the whole creation of mankind must necessarily offer up to You glory and thanksgiving, sacrifice and offering and incense through our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, Your only-begotten Son. You alone are Almighty, good, and loving mercy, and beneficent, for Yours is the glory and power for ever and ever. Amen.

5 You are in heaven, and You are on earth, and You

6 Are in the sea Who have gathered together the whole creation from the confines of the earth, and there is no end of Your greatness. For it is not we who have said this, but you did say, ‘‘! Am the Lord God who am above the heavens,” and am present in every place, and besides me there is no other god.

7 True is Your word, and there is none that is like to You, the holy God, the Lord of the Saints, and the souls of all are in Your hand. You are worthy of glory and exaltation, Whose throne is unchangeable, and (Whose) strength decays not, and (Whose) greatness none can declare, and the excellence of (Whose) beauty is marvellous.

8 There is none that dares to draw nigh to your habitation, and the knowledge of Your wisdom is without number. Your righteousness has no end, You need none to counsel You, nor seek any to help You; You have no beginning nor any end; Your kingdom passes not away, and there is none that can overcome Your power, and innumerable are the hosts of angels who minister to You.

9 You are the Father of wisdom, and the maker of the whole creation, and the framer of the law. You destroy the ungodly, and reward the righteous. (You are) God the Father of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, the Lord of those who give Him humble thanks, in Whom is no falsehood;. Who take no bribe in judgment, Whose praises are innumerable.

10 To you are due the honor, and glory, and worship which every rational soul sends up to You for ever and ever. Amen.
