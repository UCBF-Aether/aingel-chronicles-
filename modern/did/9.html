THAT THE DEACON OUGHT TO DO NOTHING EXCEPT BY PERMISSION OF THE BISHOP

1 Even-as the Son is the good pleasure of the Father,

2 And His angel of counsel

3 And His prophet, in like

4 Manner is the deacon the messenger! And prophet of

5 The bishop.: Therefore it

6 Is not right that he should

7 Do anything without the counsel of the bishop. And if he give in secret to one that is destitute without the knowledge of the bishop, he causes reproach, and imputes (to him) neglect and sloth as touching the poor, and needy,” and afflicted.

8 And he that speaks evil against the bishop, or does (him) wrong, has sinned against God, not understanding that which is said, “You shall not speak evil against the ruler of your people.’’ For this law was not ordained concerning stones and trees which the Gentiles worship, but rather with respect to priests and wise men. For to

9 These He said, “You are gods, and all of you sons of the Most High.”

10 Understand then, O deacon, if you see a destitute man, and he be ashamed to beg, speak concerning him to the bishop, and reveal not his poverty to another, nor speak of it, lest he be ashamed and murmur. But if you reveal his secrets, you will bring sorrow on your brother, and for his sake the Lord will be angry with you.

11 ‘And He said to them, Why were you not afraid to speak evil against my servant Moses? And the anger of the wrath of the Lord came (upon them).

12 And Moses said to those that murmured against him, And we, what are we and this your murmuring—it is not against me that you murmur, (but) against the Creator.” And if there is anyone that says to a layman, You fool he shall be condemned, for he has blasphemed against the name of Christ. And if this be so, much more then shall those (be condemned) that blaspheme against the bishop.

13 For when he stretches forth his hands the Holy. Spirit comes down'; and by him you are taught the spiritual commandment, and know God, and

14 Threaten,

15 Have believed in Christ, by whom you are saved by the oil of gladness and the holy chrism, and are made the sons of light. For when the bishop lays his hand upon you in holy baptism, the Lord sends His holy Word upon you, saying, ‘““You are my sons, and to-day have begotten you.’’

16 Understand, then, O man, that by the prayer of the bishop you are called a son of God. Now also know that!” which was granted to you; and love him that has loved you, and called you to this glory and honor. Revere the bishop! Who is under God a father to you.

17 For you have heard the word which says concerning those who begat you according to the flesh, “Honor your father and your mother, and for this you will find good"; and, “he that speaks an evil word against his father or his mother, let him die the death.” And if this be so, how then is it that you do not magnify and honor those who have begotten you again with water and the Holy Spirit, and have fed you with spiritual milk, and nurtured you with the word of admonition, and strengthened you by the eucharist, and made you meet to receive the holy Body and the precious Blood of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ?

18 These are they who have loosed you from the bond of sin, and made you partakers of the holy eucharist,! And of the love of God, that you may be partakers of the inheritance and joint-heirs with His Son.

19 Fear them, then, and honor them in every thing, for they have been granted life from God, that they may give judgment against them that have sinned, and save them from the fire of Gehenna, and pardon them that turn from their sins.

20 These then you ought to fear as chiefs and rulers; and obey them as kings, and receive them into your houses. Even as Samuel the prophet commanded the people concerning the king in the first Book of Kings, and Moses also commanded concerning the priests, we in like manner command you concerning the bishops, that you give them their due, even as is appointed. For of old Moses and Elijah and Samuel commanded the people to honor kings and priests, who do service in matters of this world; and if this be so, much more is it meet that you should give their due and their maintenance to bishops and priests who watch and do service on your behalf, and are your guides to the Kingdom of heaven. And if of old kings raised armies to fight and war, that they might guard and save men’s bodies, how much more are they (worthy of honor) who have received from God the office of the priesthood, (and) who preserve soul and body from condemnation. For the soul is of more account than the body, and the priestly office is of more account than the kingly, for this is the office which binds those that deserve condemnation, and looses those that seek for healing. Therefore it is right that you should honor and love the bishop as a father, and fear him as a king, and trust in him as (in) God. Bring to him your gifts, both the fruits of your ground, and the labour of your hands, and your first-fruits, and your presents, and your vows; even the first-fruits of your wheat and wine and oil and wool. And for this shall he pronounce a blessing upon you, that your oblation may be acceptable and well-pleasing as incense, even a sweet fragrance before the Lord your God; and that the Lord may bless the work of your hands, and multiply the good fruits of your land. For the blessing of the Lord is upon the heads of the righteous who show mercy.

21 Behold now, know that the Lord has freed us from the curse of the Law, and brought us

22 Them,

23 Back to life, when we were scattered abroad and lost as sheep without a shepherd, and as the beasts that cannot speak. He has turned us from our sins, and cleansed

24 Us from our iniquities, and commanded us to bring our

25 Sacrifices to the priests, and to give alms to the poor who have nothing.

26 For our Lord says in the Gospel, “Unless you abound more than the Scribes and Pharisees in doing righteousness you cannot enter into the Kingdom of heaven.” (This will you do) if you take care to give to the priests and the widows and the orphans; even as it is written, saying, ‘He has dispersed abroad and has given to the poor, and his righteousness remains for ever,” for ‘by righteousness and uprightness and mercy sin is pardoned.”

27 For a pure and blessed soul pleases God. Do, then, righteousness thus as the Lord has commanded; give to the priests as it befits, the first-fruits of your winepress and your threshing-floor, for they have authority to give to all the destitute, and power over every matter of the Church.

28 Strive not, then, against the bishops in anything that they do, saying, When did he do it or, To whom has he given or, When did he spend it or, Where did he send it? (Nor discuss) whether he has done good or evil, or acted justly or unjustly, for the Lord God will require it of them, who has given them the office of the priesthood, and granted to them the greater degree.

29 But do you set the fear of God before your eyes, and remember at all times the word that says, “Love the One Lord, your God with all your heart and with all your strength,” and follow not the way which leads to (false) gods, nor anything like those that have no life in them, nor the doctrine of demons, and other creatures () which Christ has not commanded.

30 But seek you to read the Scriptures

31 Which are the law of the Lord your God, and cease not to read His Scriptures at all times. And remove from you all evil that opposes itself, which is not commanded in the law, (even) mocking and anger. But honor your father and your mother who begat you according to the flesh, and love your neighbor as yourself, and show mercy to the poor, and keep far from false swearing in which is no profit.

32 But keep not far from the Church of Christ; resort there early at all times, and worship humbly with the lowly, who sleep not night or day, giving thanks to God for the life that He has bestowed upon them.

33 Live by (your) labour, the work of your hands, and offer to the Lord of what you have according to your power; and be a friend of the poor, and gain the Kingdom of heaven, where is no moth nor rust, neither does the thief find it. And if you desire to save yourself, strive not in judgment with the bishop, nor with a layman, your brother, nor with your fellow wrongfully.

34 And if you strive in judgment with him wrongfully, you have drawn on yourself transgression and sin, none constraining you. For judgment has been given to the priests, and not to the layman. For the Lord says (to them) “Judge in righteousness and uprightness ’’; and, “Be of understanding and judge the great ones of the people, each one of them.’’ But to you it is not permitted to judge any man.

35 For it is said to those that have not the office of the priesthood, “Judge not, for your judgment shall not be acceptable.”

36 But let the bishop judge in uprightness, even as it is written, saying, “Judge the poor and the

37 Orphan, do justice to the oppressed and the needy, and save the desolate and destitute.” And moreover it is said, “Therefore judge you not amongst you in uprightness, ’’
