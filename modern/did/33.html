Didascalia

Chapter 33

OF THE RIGHT FAITH AND THE HOLY TRINITY WHICH THE APOSTLES PREACHED TO US IN TRUTH

1 But we, (being) children of God and sons of peace, preach this holy word to those who are obedient in uprightness. We believe in One God, the Maker of the whole creation, the Father of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, whose existence is without cause (), even as we said before, but He is existent at all times without beginning or end. And in Him is light unquenchable, and to Him! None can approach.

2 He is not two, nor three, nor is He capable of increase, but He is One alone who abides for ever. Yet He is not a hidden (Being) who cannot be known, but we have known surely by the Law and the prophets that He is Almighty and the Ruler of the whole creation.

3 He is the One God, the Father of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, who was begotten before the world was created, the only-begotten Son, who is equal with Him, the maker of all hosts and powers and dominions; who was pleased to become man in the latter days, and took flesh of the holy Virgin Mary, without seed of man, and grew up” amongst men, without sin, or trespass, nor was there guile in His mouth; and afterwards He suffered and died without sin in the flesh, and rose from the dead on the third day, and ascended into heaven to the Father, who had sent Him, and sat down on the right hand of power; and He sent to us the Paraclete,

4 The Holy Spirit, who proceeds from the Father, and has saved the world; who is co-existent with the Father and the Son. We say then that every creature of God is good and there is nothing (to be) rejected, but that the spirit, the life of the flesh, is pure and holy in all things.

5 We say that marriage is pure, and the begetting of children not unclean, for God made Adam and Eve that man might be multiplied. We understand then that there is in our body a soul which does not die, nor perish with the body; but we abhor every deed of apostates from the faith, and all divisions, and transgression of the law, for to us such are unclean.

6 Again, we believe in the. Resurrection of the dead, both of the righteous and sinful in the day of judgment, when every man shall be requited according to his works. Moreover, we believe that Christ was in nothing subjected to loss by His incarnation, but that He is God the Word, who became man in truth, and reconciled the race of man with God, and (that He is) the High-priest of the Father.

7 We do not practice circumcision then like the Jews; we know that He has come who fulfilled the Law and the prophets, for whose coming all the nations hoped, Jesus Christ who sprang from Judah and from the root of Jesse, and whose government is on His shoulder.

8 For in those days ungodliness grew strong, and led many astray, by which the Church was disturbed.

9 Then we gathered together in Jerusalem, we the twelve apostles, and appointed Matthias in the place of Judas Iscariot, even as it is written, “And his office let another take "; and we were assembled together with James our Lord’s brother, the bishop of Jerusalem, and we took counsel with the presbyters to speak to the people words of admonition. For there

10 Were some who set forth from Judaea, and went to Antioch to teach the brothers who were in that place, saying, Unless you be circumcised according to the law of Moses, and walk according to his ordinance, you shall not be saved; and there arose enmity and strife.

11 Then the brothers who were in Antioch knew that we were gathered together and had enquired concerning this matter, and they sent to us faithful men, who had knowledge of the holy Scriptures, to search out this thing.

12 And they came to us in Jerusalem to declare to us all that had happened in Antioch; and there were some who said to them, You must necessarily be circumcised, and do what is in the ordinances of the Law.

13 But I Peter rose up and said to the people, Brothers, you know that God chose the Gentiles who took heed to the preaching of the Gospel, and believed, to whom God who knows the hearts bore witness. And to Cornelius the centurion an angel of the Lord appeared, and bade him send to me that he might hear from my mouth the word of life.

14 And he sent to me to the city of Joppa, and brought me to Caesarea. And before I came to him, I stood on a lofty place and prayed; and behold I saw the heaven opened, and a great sheet descend to the earth, held by its corners; and‘in it were creeping things and beasts and birds of the heaven.

15 And heard a voice which said, Arise, Peter, kill and eat; and I said, Never has there entered into my mouth anything defiled, which was not clean. And again a voice came which said, What God has cleansed, count you not unclean, and the whole (sheet) was drawn up again into heaven.

16 And while I thought thus, What thing is this that has appeared to me the Holy Spirit said to me, Behold (certain) men are seeking you; arise and go with them without doubting, for I have sent (them) to you. These were they whom the centurion had sent.

17 Then I thought in my heart, and believed what the Scripture says, ‘‘ Whoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved"; and again it is said, “Let Joel,.

18 All the ends of the earth remember and turn to the Lord, and let all the lands of the nations worship before him; for the kingdom is the Lord’s, and he shall judge the nations.”" Moreover, I remembered that which is written concerning the calling of the Gentiles, and [ went with them, and entered into the man’s house, and preached the word.

19 And at once the Holy Spirit descended upon him and upon all who were with him, even as He had descended upon us; and there was no distinction between us in faith, and in the purification of our hearts. And I perceived that God respects not persons, but that all nations that fear God and do righteousness are well-pleasing before Him.

20 Then the faithful rose up and said, How long tempt you God, and lay on the shoulders of the Gentiles a heavy burden which they cannot bear? But we believe that we shall be saved by grace even as (they). The Lord has loosed us from the bond of sin, and lightened (and removed) from off us the heavy burden. And when I had said this, all the people kept silence.

21 Then James the brother of our Lord answered them, Hearken to what I say to you: behold Simeon has before declared how God chose the Gentiles for His inheritance as it is written, “T will come and build the house of David which is in ruins, and I will raise up those that have fallen, that all the Gentiles on whom my name is named may turn to the Lord, says the Lord.” Therefore I say to you, Lay no burden on the Gentiles who have turned to God, but command them to abstain from all (that is) unclean, and not to eat that which dies of itself,!! Or which has been sacrificed to (false) gods, and (to abstain from) sexual immorality, (and from) what is not commanded in the law, and is not written in the book of the fathers, Enosh, Enoch and Noah and Melchizedek and Job and others such as they.

22 Then the apostles, and James,

23 The bishop of Jerusalem, and the presbyters of the Church agreed, and chose from amongst us men whom they should send with Barnabas and Paul the tentmaker,’ both Judas who was called Barsabbas,! And Silas, who were chosen from the brothers. Then we wrote and sent (a letter) to the brothers in the churches of Antioch and Syria, saying, Peace be to you.

24 Because we have heard (that there are) some who have troubled your souls with words, to whom we gave no (such) commandment, now also we having agreed together have chosen men to send to you with our brothers Barnabas and Paul, men who have surrendered their lives for Jesus Christ’s sake. And we have sent Judas and Silas that they may inform you (of these things).

25 For the Holy Spirit agreed with us that we should lay no burden (upon you) nor add any other (charge) save this only, that men should abstain from anything sacrificed to (false) gods, and from that which dies of itself, and from blood, and from sexual immorality; and that what they hate for themselves they should not do to their neighbors. Take heed to these things and peace be to you.

26 This letter we sent, (but) we laboured in Jerusalem for many days, desiring to do what was good for us and you ().

27 And after a few days we visited the brothers and confirmed ‘them with the word of admonition, and commanded them to keep far from those who blasphemed against the name of the Lord and against Moses. For these are ravening (wolves), and make themselves like to sheep, and strive against the Lord and Moses and the prophets, (being) false Christs and false

28 Prophets and lying apostles who destroy the nations, and bow down themselves to the earth, and think all (manner of) evil, and spoil the holy vineyard. And by reason of these the love of many shall wax cold;! But he who patiently endures to the end, the same shall be saved.

29 And concerning these our Lord commanded us, saying, “ Beware of false prophets who come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly are ravening wolves, and from their fruit you shall know them.” And because of this we all have assembled in one place—we are the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ, Peter and Andrew; James and John, the sons of Zebedee; Philip and Bartholomew; Matthew and Thomas; James the son of Alphaeus and Simon the Cananean; ‘Thaddaeus and Matthias who was numbered with us and appointed in place of Judas Iscariot; and James the brother of our Lord Jesus Christ, the bishop of Jerusalem and Paul the chosen (vessel) and teacher of the Gentiles—and being gathered together in one place we have sent to you this holy message.” You also, faithful brothers, have believed in Christ, by whom were all things: and now also we say to you that there is One God Almighty, and there is no other god besides Him; (and) we teach you to worship Him, and adore Him, and to serve Jesus Christ and the Holy Spirit, and to read the Holy Scriptures of the Law and the prophets.

30 Honor your parents, and abstain from all that contends against the law; and believe in the resurrection arid the day af judgment; and wait in hope for that time. When He shall render to every man according to his works.

31 For the whole creation is from God, and not from Satan; but all evil is from Satan. And in lawful marriage there is no defilement nor loss, but rather it is from God, for in the beginning He created (them) male and female; and for this cause it is said “Let a man leave his father and his mother, and cleave to his wife, and let them two be one flesh.”

32 And after a man is married, let him not put away his wife, but for sexual immorality only, for “she is your spirit and your flesh, for I created them and not another.” Again, it is said, “What God has joined together let not man separate.” For he that separates himself from his wife, dividing the one flesh, has transgressed the commandment of God. Le also that has married one that is divorced has defiled his flesh; and he that has cleaved to an adulteress has quite transgressed the law.

33 Cut off the adulteress from your flesh, for she is not yours. Defile you not then your flesh, for circumcision of the heart in a pure spirit is sufficient for the faithful.

34 ( Make no repetition of baptism: the first baptism which you have received suffices for you, for you were buried into the death of Christ; (a baptism) which has not been given to the ungodly and unbelieving, but to holy priests who have bestowed it upon you

35 Into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the.

36 Holy Spirit. Receive not baptism at the hands of apostates from the faith. For there is one baptism, and one God, the Father, and our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, and the Paraclete, the Holy Spirit: one is the death of Jesus in the flesh; and in like manner let your baptism which you have received into His death be one.

37 And he that believes not, and does not thus, his portion shall be with the ungodly in their evil doctrine. To those who do thus, God says, “ Because

38 You have rejected my doctrine, also will reject you and forsake you, and not make you priests to me. - And those who are baptized by them are not cleansed, but are unclean, and have no remission of sin.

39 So are they who receive a second baptism, and make the work of Christ a mockery, and despise holy things, and blaspheme against the Holy Spirit, and count unclean His precious blood, and set at naught His sufferings and His testimony. But those who believe not, and are not baptized into the right faith, are transgressors of the law and condemned, for they reproach Him, and give Him not thanks.

40 Our Lord says, “He that is not born again of water and the Holy Spirit cannot enter into the Kingdom of heaven,” but ‘he that believes and is baptized shall be saved, and he that believes not shall be condemned.” And he who says, Afterwards I will be baptized, and makes light of baptism, the same shall be condemned and be far from God.

41 Delay not to turn to the Lord for man does not know that which shall come upon him.

42 And baptize your children, while they are babes, and feed them with spiritual food, and bring them up with admonition and wisdom, for it is said, “ Allow the little children, and do not forbid them to come to me.” Behold we have written all this to you, beloved brothers, that you may understand wisdom and knowledge, and not receive idle words which the ungodly put forth in our name.

43 Say not, then, We have apostles who will pray for us, but rather see whether their deeds are right. For there are some that do many mighty works, which are full of the poison of death, in the name of Christ, like Simon the Sorcerer and Cleobius, and those with them in every place, who lead men astray. [You also are they that have los

44 Loved Christ (and) we are His servants.] And others also of the ancients have written apocryphal books of Moses, and Enoch, and Adam, and Seth, and Isaiah, and David, and Elijah, and the three patriarchs, who strove against those that were transgressors of the law. Moreover (certain) have written books that are vain; who account themselves elect and pure, and say, Our Scriptures are sure, though they are unclean.

45 From those who do thus keep far away, lest you be condemned with those that have written to lead astray the faithful—who are blameless, the disciples of our Lord Jesus Christ.

46 We said, then, concerning bishops and presbyters that they should live with one wife before ordination, but that after ordination it is net right that they should marry. And again we give commandment concerning the servants who are obedient to the Church, sub-deacons, and readers, and doorkeepers that they should live with one wife, and (in one) marriage.

47 But after they have received baptism and ordination, let them not commit sin, lest they fall into condemnation. Behold we give commandment that they marry not a harlot, nor a servant, nor a woman that is divorced, nor a widow, even as it is written in the law. Moreover let the deaconess be a pure virgin, or a faithful widow who has been married to one man. (Such) women ought to be appointed.

48 Those women also who turn from sin, receive. This is the will of God in Jesus Christ. And as touching the catechumens when they understand the teaching, baptize them into the name to the Father,

49 And of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit; and command the faithful to separate themselves from those that serve not God, (and not to have fellowship with them) in anything, whether counsel or word or prayer.

50 And put them forth from the Church of God, for they are strangers, and evil, and corrupt, who destroy the sheep of Christ's flock; concerning whom the wise Solomon says, ‘‘ Woe to the unbelieving who account themselves wise, and their latter end (comes) to the condemnation of Sheol." These are they that teach false doctrine, and blaspheme against the Holy Spirit, from whom has been taken the portion of the inheritance which they received from Him, and who have forgiveness neither in this world, nor in that which is to come.

51 For they have transgressed more grievously than the Jews and the heathen, who have blasphemed against God (who is) the Creator of all things and is above all; and they have risen up against His Son, and denied the Holy Spirit, and made void the holy Scriptures, and made blasphemy their portion,“ and spoiled the Church of Christ. These are the foxes that spoil the vineyard. From such we bid you keep far away.

52 For he that follows a wise man shall be wise like to him; but he that follows a fool shall be a fool like to him. You ought not to dwell with robbers, nor be partakers with adulterers. Hear what the holy David says, “I have hated, O Lord, them that hate you, and have been troubled because of your enemies.

53 I have hated them with a perfect hatred and they have become my enemies.’’ ‘For God cursed Jehoshaphat because of the love and help of King Ahab; and to King [Uzziah] ‘ he said by [Joel] the prophet, “You have been a help to a sinner; will you then save him whom the Lord has hated’ “ Therefore there came on him speedily the anger of the Lord; because his heart was not right with Him, his works were diminished in

54 The earth.

55 Keep, then, far from them, and receive not their greeting.” Concerning them says the prophet, The wicked have no rejoicing before God.” These are ravening wolves and dogs that have no mercy And when the day has drawn nigh and the hour has come, they will be evil, even as our Lord says in the Gospel, “In the latter days faith and love shall grow less, and sin shall be multiplied, and there shall come false Christs and false prophets, and they shall do signs in heaven and seduce many of the elect.’ Behold then let us pray to God to save us from their error by Jesus Christ, and to deliver us from the ungodly people, that we may confirm the Church and save many, and heal them by much admonition, and by the word of grace.

56 But those who received not our preaching could not be healed, and have gone forth from the flock, lest they should corrupt other undefiled sheep in whom was no blemish.

57 Thus, then, we have ordained in all lands, and have set forth for you this right doctrine of the Didascalia in truth and in purity as a memorial for those who believed in our Lord Jesus Christ; and we have sent (it) with Clement the beloved and the elect Barnabas, and our beloved son Timothy and the blessed Mark, and with these Titus and Luke, and Jason and Lucius, and Sosipater.

58 We beg you therefore: imitate these and hear their words, and shun your former manner of life, for the former things have passed away; and become new yourselves, for you have known our Lord Jesus Christ by whom are all things; and (who) has given to us a pure law (for) the salvation of our souls, in which He put His own perfect name, which is without division or defect, or change, unspotted and converting souls, When the Hebrews forgot this, He gave them a memorial by Malachi the prophet, who says, “ Remember the law of Moses the man of God who enjoined on you these commandments of righteousness and holiness.” But our Lord Jesus Christ did

59 Not abolish the ordinances of the Law, nor diminish (them), but rather said, “ One word shall not pass from the Law, till all this be fulfilled.” Again, He says, “T have not come to abolish the Law and the prophets, but to fulfill them.” or Moses the legislator, prophet and king, and Elijah the prophet, who appeared with our Lord when He was transfigured before them, bore testimony concerning His passion and His death and His incarnation as friends, and men of His household, and not as strangers.

60 ‘he Lord gave the ordinance, the ten words which are in the Law and the holy prophets, and they heard His voice continuously, before they made for themselves the calf which the Egyptians called Apis. For the ordinance is righteous, and therefore was it called the law; and He judges in uprightness. Simon and those with him hate this ordinance, and praise themselves, but they shall go down to Gehenna.

61 But the law is good and holy, for He says, “If you will make for me an altar, build it for me in the land,” and He says not, “Build for me,” except (it be) your pleasure, for God needs not sacrifices and offerings from us. But Abel, and Noah, and Abraham, the friends of God, offered up sacrifices of incense to Him according to their pleasure.

62 And in like manner the Hebrews did thus according to their pleasure. And it was not He who commanded them, but they themselves did His good pleasure, and He received their sacrifices which they offered up to Him with a pure heart.

63 Therefore He says, ‘I desire not your incense, nor do I need aught, for the world is my and all that is in it.” In days of old the people forgat God, and made for themselves a calf as a god instead of the true God. And when they came out of Egypt it is said, “Is this your god, O Israel, that brought you

64 Forth from the land of Egypt?” They worshipped a calf in (their) ungodliness, and forsook God who had sent Moses, and brought them out of sore affliction. And behold God worked signs by the hand of Moses his servant, and with the rod which destroyed the Egyptians with the ten plagues, and with which he struck the Red Sea, and made them pass through it on dry ground as it were a course for horses; but he overwhelmed his enemies in it; and he made sweet the fountain of bitter water; and he split the rock and made water to pour forth for them; he overshadowed them by day with a cloud, and by night with a pillar of fire; and he enlightened them and led them—but they knew not where they were going; he brought down from heaven manna for them to eat and (sent them) flesh as the dust; and he gave them the law in the desert, and made them ready to hear His voice. They forsook him who had done all this, and said to Aaron, “Make us gods who shall go with us.” And (Aaron) made a calf for them, and they bowed down before it.

65 And God knew that they felt no gratitude for all that, He had done for them, so they were bound with bonds that could not be loosed, and were cast into a net, from which there was no escape. (And God said to them), “When you make an altar, offer up sacrifices on it at all times.” For He says, you did forget God who saved you, and gave no. Thanks to my name.

66 Offer then your offerings that there you may remember to give thanks to my name. And substitute not an unclean beast for a clean, for if you do thus, I will bring upon you sore punishment as (on) an evil and slothful and foolish servant, and you shall [not] be bound with the bonds of the commandment.

67 Take heed then and fall not into the error of (having) many gods, lest there come upon you the word which says “Where is your God, O Israel”' And remember

68 What is written, saying, “lear, O Israel, the Lord your God is One.” And abide you in this ordinance, and know that there is no other god besides Him in heaven or in earth; love Him with all your heart, and with all your strength, and with all your mind; and fear Him alone, and set not in your heart the name of strange gods, nor make mention of them with your mouth, for because of the guile of their hearts God bound them.

69 But as for you, since you have known the One God with a pure heart, and a good understanding, and have heard the voice of the Savior, blessed are your eyes, for they have seen, and your ears for they have heard. Behold you are loosed from the bonds and are freed from the servitude.

70 Moreover He says, “! Will not call you my servants, for the servant does not know what his lord does to him; but you are my friends. Behold I have taught you all that I have heard from my Father.” But as for those who desire not to see nor to hear, their heart has been hardened, and they are far from Him.

71 But blessed are you from whom God has taken away His curse. For Christ the Son of God came, (and) established and fulfilled the law; and He removed the heavy burden that was in it, (and) saved us, and freed us from the service of sin, and had mercy on us, and gave us life eternal.

72 And again He says, “He that will come after me, let him await my second coming.” But He received not the sacrifices and offerings which the nations offered, for oftentimes they offered to Him; and they thought that it was by multiplying sacrifices that they would be saved, without repentance, from the scourge of God’s anger. Moreover it is said, “ Why bring you to me incense from Saba, and cinnamon from a far country?

73 I have no pleasure in your sacrifices, and will not receive your incense.” And again Isaiah says, ‘ Therefore offer

74 You to me your sacrifices; I am sated with the sacrifices of rams and oxen and with the fat of sheep. I desire not the blood of oxen and goats. If you bring (them), will you not show me who has required this at your hands? You shall not again tread the courts of my house.

75 And if you bring fine flour, it is in vain; your incense, and your feasts, and your sabbaths, and your great day I desire not; your fasts also and your prayers I desire not; my soul has hated (them) and you are an abomination to me.” Moreover the prophet says, “Remove from me the sound of your anger.” Again Samuel the prophet said to Saul, when he desired to sacrifice, ‘‘To hearken is better than to sacrifice and knowledge than the fat of sheep, for the Lord desires not that you should sacrifice to him, but rather that you should hearken to his word.” And David also says, “I will take no oxen from your house, nor rams from your flock, for all the beasts of the field are mine; the cattle of the field also, and the oxen; and I know all the birds of the heaven; and the beauty of field also is with me.

76 If I be hungry I will not ask of you, for the whole world is mine, with the fulness of it. I will not eat the flesh of oxen, nor drink the blood of goats.

77 Sacrifice to God the sacrifice of praise and send up your prayer to the Most High.” Moreover it is written in the Scripture that He desires not the incense of those that have sinned against Him, for unclean before Him is the sacrifice of the ungodly who offer contrary to the law: everyone who eats of it is unclean.

78 But He desires above all a meek heart and a humble spirit which are better than sacrifice. Behold, then, He came to bring to an end the sacrifices of blood which they offered.

79 For He said in the Law, “The Lord your God is One”; again He said in the Gospel, ‘This is life eternal that they should know the One true

80 God." Moreover He said, ‘Love your neighbor as yourself”; (and) repeated (it in the Gospel) and said, “T have given you a commandment that you love one another, as I have loved you.” For thus He said (in the law) You shall not kill, but He has commanded us never to hate anyone.

81 He who said You shall not commit adultery nor desire your neighbor’s wife, behold He has commanded (us not even to look upon a woman to lust after her) ft. He said (then), You shall not steal, but He has commanded us to abstain from every evil deed, and to love our enemies.

82 For He who said, You shall not render evil to him that does you injury, has commanded us to be at peace with all men; for mercy is better than those who desire vengeance unrighteously (). He said (then), “Honor your parents’; but He has commanded us to humble ourselves to all men.

83 He put an end to circumcision, and He Himself fulfilled circumcision: and He fulfilled the hope for which the whole world was hoping. He who said, “Perform to the Lord your oath,” has commanded us not to swear by anything.

84 Instead of sacrifices of blood He has given us a rational bloodless offering; and instead of sacrifices in one place, He has commanded us that our offering should be made in all the earth from the rising of the sun even to its setting.”

85 Moses says in the law, Read you the words which I have commanded you while you work, or while you sit, or while you stand, or while you walk in the way.” ” And David also says, “ But the law of the Lord is his delight, and in his law he will meditate day and night.” And everywhere He has commanded that they should do that which (is written) in the law, and

86 Not transgress the ordinance. And again it is said, “Blessed are they that are pure in their way, and that walk in his ways, and seek him with all their heart.” Moreover it is said in the Gospel, “ Blessed are you, if you do this.”

87 For on account of this is the law manifest in righteousness, and illumines all the world, (even) those who believe in our Lord, and have forsaken the worship of idols, and kept far from wickedness and received all that is good.

88 And the Jews also, who have rejected the covenants, whose hearts were blinded, and (who) followed the way of those who said, ‘We have no king but Caesar,” and said again, “If we let this man alone, the Romans will come, (and) will take away our nation and our land,” prophesied against Him and said, ‘It is better for us to put one man to death, than that the nation perish.” (Yet) all nations have believed in Him, and the Romans have taken away their authority and all their ordinances; and therefore are they accursed because they have not been able to do His commandment.

89 Moreover it is said, “Cursed is he that does not all the commandments which are written in the book of the law.”

90 But we follow the way of Christ, that we may receive a blessing: and let us receive the law, and the prophets, and the holy Gospel; and turn aside from those that worship many gods, and let us keep far from the murderers of Christ, and from them that killed the prophets. These are the unbelieving Jews.

91 And let us be humble and firm in faith in Christ the King who has authority over all things, and is the teacher of the law, and the guardian of the ordinances.

92 ( Keep far, then, bishops and priests and people, from (those that cause) division (even) from those that account unclean the Law and the prophets, being the enemies of God Almighty; and who believe

93 Not in Christ the Son of God, and separate () His birth in the flesh, and blaspheme against His sufferings in the flesh, and revile His cross, and mock at His death, and believe not in His resurrection, and make void His generation from the Father before the whole creation, [and say (that in Him) are flesh and soul and not the Godhead].

94 And there are others who say that Jesus Christ is above the whole creation, and glorify Him and say that He alone is the Father; and again they say that He is the Son and the Paraclete. Are not these then more impious than all? And there are others who say that the resurrection of the dead (is of) souls without bodies. To these He says, Depart into fire eternal.

95 Keep far (from such men), beloved brothers, lest you be condemned together with them. [And those that watch the stars, and worship the host of heaven, and observe days and hours —as for such, will He accept their prayers, and be pleased with their offerings and will they read the holy Scriptures? These then are void () of the Holy Spirit, which abides in the heart of the faithful.

96 Solomon says concerning the saints, “Let them be prepared, if they sleep, to watch; if they sleep and awake, to be wise in truth and love.” ] For the Holy Spirit is not far from faithful Christian people from the day of their birth, until they die. Let a woman after her child-bearing remain in her separation seven days.

97 But if she think in her heart, and say, How shall the Holy Spirit abide upon me, while I am unclean? And

98 If (her) faith grow weak, and she be without good works, the Holy Spirit will not abide upon her. For this matter is not a transgression of the law, but marriage, and the customary separation of women, and nocturnal pollutions hinder not the Holy Spirit. But with the ungodly and unbelieving who have transgressed the law and the ordinances, the Holy Spirit will not abide.

99 And those that have not the Holy Spirit are the habitation of unclean (spirits): but upon those that have believed and (that) hope in His second coming the Holy Spirit abides. [Tor the Paraclete hates lies and every (evil) work; and the devil hates works of righteousness and goodness.

100 And whoever is baptized in faith is separated from the spirit of Satan, and is the habitation of the Holy Spirit: and he that does His will is filled with wisdom and knowledge, and the unclean spirit cannot come nigh to him. The unclean spirit loves such as are ungrateful, slothful, and sinners and sorcerers, for the devil by his want of gratitude fell from his throne, and was deprived of the glory of God.

101 And he himself chose to be Satan rather than to be an archangel. Therefore women ought to beware of every evil and vain thing, and be mindful of God, who created all things, and to follow His law, (and) not work the pleasure of the flesh, except only in lawful wedlock. Beware then of the counsel of evil men, and of union with them.

102 Therefore separate yourselves, beloved brothers, from all the heathen who practice evil, and keep far from them; and count not as an abomination the flesh of men’s dead bodies. We believe that the dead shall live and rise again.

103 And in like manner lawful marriage is not an abomination, for the union of man and woman is righteous (before God) who created man and woman, and blessed them, and said to them, “Increase and multiply, and fill the earth.” Behold we understand that from God are all these things; without Him was not anything. She did not name his name, but said of him, My lord is old. And in like manner, you men, love your wives, for they are your members and sharers with you in the begetting of children. 105 The wife of your youth, as the hart loves the fountains of waters; for she is your and is with you at all times, and is a member of yours.” For it is said, “Take heed to yourselves and forsake not your wives.” And as touching the lawful union of man and wife, when they rise from sleep let them not give heed to ceremonial washings, but rather take thought for prayer and supplication to God.

106 But if a man lie with the wife of his neighbor, or defile himself with a harlot, and, when he rises, wash himself in the sea, and in all the waters of the rivers, he shall not be able to cleanse himself; but his portion shall be rather in eternal condemnation.

107 Take no heed then of laws relating to the flesh, for lawful marriage is not unclean; nor seek after the way of the Jews, nor use at all times (such ceremonial) washings, nor practice their customary purifications.
