Didascalia

Chapter 36

OF THE CHRISM

1 You will give thanks thus: We give thanks to You, Creator of all things, for the fragrance of this chrism, and for this oil of immortality, which You have revealed to us by Jesus Christ Your Son. For

2 Yours is the glory, and the kingdom, and the power for ever and ever. Amen.

3 And whoever comes to you, and gives thanks thus, receive him as a disciple of Christ; and if there is anyone that teaches contrary to the law which was given to you by the Lord, receive him not, and let him not have fellowship with you in thanksgiving.

4 Prove everyone that comes to you, and examine diligently his deeds, (and) appoint as is fitting for you have knowledge of those on the right hand and on the left.

5 And judge between those who rule justly and (those who) rule unjustly; and to your neighbor that judges justly do good according to his doings; to him also that judges unjustly render according to his doing; and pray not with him, lest you be defiled together with him. And every prophet and teacher that comes to you and teaches truly is worthy of his food, as a good workman who serves his lord.

6 To the priests also give the first-fruits that they may bless your labours; and give all the tithes

7 To the widows, and to the orphans, and to travelers,” and to strangers.

8 And on the Sabbath day, which is (the day of) the resurrection of our Lord, gather you together in one place, and give thanks to God for all the good things that He has done for you in Christ, that your sacrifice may be perfect and acceptable before God, for it is said concerning the Church that they shall assemble in it, and offer up incense and sacrifice, (for) ‘I, says the Lord, am a great King upholding all the world, and my name is terrible in all the world, and (among) the Gentiles.”

9 Appoint for yourselves bishops, presbyters, and deacons, meek men and humble, who are chosen from the people, pure and tried in every good work; who desire not money, but rather teach the word of the Lord and the doctrine, and discern in uprightness the commandments of Christ as father and lord in love without anger and impatience, with a humble heart and in meekness.

10 Observe all this that you have been commanded of the Lord for the life of your souls. Let your loins be girt, and your lamps burning, and be ready as men who wait for their Lord when he comes, whether at the first hour of the night, or at midnight, or at cock-crowing, or at break of day. The Lord will come at a time that you expect not.

11 Blessed are those servants whom their Lord, when he comes, shall find so doing. Behold, He will gird Himself, and go about, and serve them. Pray then and work, lest death come upon you suddenly.

12 And when the glory of the Son of Man shall appear in heaven, and the archangel shall blow the trumpet, and the dead shall be raised, and our Lord shall come with His Saints in the clouds of heaven, and all the angels of His power with Him, then shall He sit on the throne of His Kingdom, and destroy the devil, the deceiver, and shall render to every man

13 According to his works; and then shall the sinners go into eternal condemnation, but the righteous into life eternal, and (these) shall rejoice in the Kingdom of heaven through our Lord Jesus Christ.

14 Be, then, ready that you may receive His good things.

15 Let us pray and make supplication to Him, and say, O, our Savior; Who are before the world (was), God of gods, the Almighty, One God, the Creator of every creature, the Lord of our holy fathers, who were without blame before You, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob (Who are) merciful, and ready to forgive sins, faithful and kind, plenteous in compassion and mercy, from whom nothing is hidden, (but) all is open before You: in You the souls of the righteous trust, and in You the saints hope; (You are) the Father of them that are blameless, and hear the prayer of the pure in heart, and hearken to the supplication of them that are silent.

16 Make Your favor, O Lord, to dwell on every creature, for you know that which the heart thinks before the thought (comes); and in all the ends of the world they offer up to You sacrifice and incense with prayer and thanksgiving; Who have appointed days that men may do righteousness, and the gate of whose mercy is open to everyone that seeks You, and have appointed laws and ordinances, and caused us to understand that the riches of this world are transitory and that the ornament of beauty abides not for ever, and that the might of the strong decays speedily.

17 For our father Abraham was the first to walk’ in the way of righteousness, and you gave him honor and riches, and in faith he accomplished his life; and you said to him, “T will multiply your seed as the stars of heaven, and as the sand of the sea”; and you gave Isaac to him; you did bless him and say to him, “I will be with your seed after you.” For we have known Christ in

18 Our father Jacob, whom you did send to the land of Egypt,’ and said to him, “ Behold I will be with you, and will surely multiply your seed exceedingly.”' And in like manner you did say to Moses, Your holy and faithful servant at the bush, “I am from before the world (was), and my name shall be for ever”; and Your memorial also is for generations of generations; Who blessed the descendants of Abraham that they might be blessed for ever..

19 You did create man that he might be a rational (creature), and you did say in Your wisdom, ‘Let us make man in our image and in our likeness ”; and you did set Adam to be ruler over all, and gave him life eternal. And when he transgressed Your commandment, you did put him forth from the garden, and brought death on him.

20 Then you did send Your only-begotten Son and raised him from his fall, and restored him to the garden of delight.

21 You, O Lord, alone are great, and powerful, and terrible, merciful and kind, the Almighty who does wonders, and does not withdraw ‘Your compassion from any of Your works or creatures, good and a lover of men, and dealing gently with sinners, and bringing them back in the multitude of Your mercy, and though we are not worthy to look up to You, nor raise our eyes to heaven, You in the greatness of Your power regard our humility.

22 The heavens are supported by Your commandment, and by Your word the earth is established, for You, O Lord, are mighty, and the righteousness of Your judgment has filled the earth which You have created.
