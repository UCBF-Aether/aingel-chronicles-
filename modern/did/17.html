Didascalia

Chapter 17

OF WIDOWS

1 For Satan seeks an occasion by which to ensnare, as he did of old in the case of Cain. For there are some who say, We are widows, but do not do the

2 Works that befit widows, even as Cain! Did toward his brother that which befitted not. These then are they that have no knowledge. For it is not by being called widows that they shall enter into the Kingdom of heaven, but by a right faith and by good works.

3 But she that takes the name of a widow, and does the work of Satan, belies the name () so that she shall find eternal condemnation.’ Behold we hear that there are among the widows some that are evil and envious and slanderers, and that cause strife amongst the brothers. These are not of Christ, for they have not kept the word of admonition.

4 If there is one of the sisters, and (others of them) see men give her alms, whether money, or clothing, or food, or drink, and (if) she then rejoice and find rest, they ought to say thus.

5 Blessed be the Lord our God, Who has granted rest and joy to the widow, our beloved sister, O Lord send blessing and honor on him who has given to this widow, and increase his substance in righteousness and in purity, and remember him with Your goodness, in the greatness of Your compassion and mercy.

6 And to this bishop also give You a double honor, who has taught and exhorted those that have given to us: give him the crown of glory when he shall depart this life. And in like manner let this widow pray and entreat along with them for those that have shown mercy towards her.

7 And now’ also let her give to the destitute from that which she has received; and for this cause shall her works be well-pleasing before God, as it is written, saying, ‘Let not your left hand know what your right hand does, and let your alms be

8 Secret.” Thus let the widow pray for them that have given to her and bestowed alms upon her, for she is as the sanctuary, and your Father, who sees you in secret, shall reward you openly. But widows who walk not after the commandment of God are envious of him who has given alms to their sister, (and) they ask, and say, Who is it that has shown this mercy towards her? And then when they understand and know, they go to him that has given alms to their sister to trouble him, and say to him, Why have you given first to her, while we are far more destitute than she?

9 For they are foolish who say thus thinking it is a gift from man, whereas the gift of favor is from God. But if she was manifestly in want, they ought not to go to trouble! Him ‘that shows mercy, but rather is it right that they should remain at home, and fall down on their faces, and repent and Pray to God. For God shows mercy to them that Proclaim His goodness.

10 Our Lord says, “When you enter into a house, say, Peace be to this house, and if a son of peace be there, your peace shall rest upon it; and, if not, your peace shall return to you.” But if peace find not (a place) prepared (for her) where she may rest, she abides not there, but returns to him that sent her.

11 In like manner the unrighteous curse shall not come on him that has not sinned, but shall rather return on him that uttered it He that has cursed another without cause, has cursed himself, as Solomon says. In like manner you also shall be requited even as you have done to others.

12 For it is said, ‘“‘ He has digged a pit, and hollowed it out, and he shall fall into the pit which he made.

13 And his toil shall return on his own head, and his wickedness shall descend upon his own pate.” For he that digs a pit for his neighbor shall fall,into it, and he that fears a curse, let him not curse another; and what he hates for himself let him not do to his neighbor.

14 Therefore, O bishop, do you as a master admonish those women that despise widows, and bear patiently with the angry, and visit the sick, and rejoice with the elect who bless and curse not, and command them to do not that which is evil, but to make peace.

15 Whether it be a bishop, or a presbyter, or a deacon, or whoever it be of those set in authority, let there not proceed from his mouth cursing, lest he inherit a curse instead of a blessing. The bishop ought to admonish and teach and command the people that no word of cursing proceed from their mouths. But let him show care for priests, and deacons, and virgins, and widows, and the people.

16 Therefore, O bishop, prove the deacons who do service with you, and help them in doing good, that they may be ready to minister to the people, and let a deaconess also be appointed to minister to the women, an elect and pure woman who is blameless. For a deacon ought not to minister to the women, lest there arise an evil report from those that believe not.

17 Therefore let there be a deaconess as a minister to the women, and let her anoint them with oil, and clothe them after baptism. And do you, O bishop, anoint the foreheads of them that are baptized, men and women, with holy oil according to the ordinance of

18 Holy baptism. And after this, O bishops and presbyters,

19 Which is evil, but rather that

20 They do good and (make) peace.

21 Pray for them that are under you, and baptize them into the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. And then let the deacon take the men, and let the deaconess also take the women, that the seal which they receive, which no man may (break), may be holy and pure.

22 And after they have been baptized, let the bishop anoint them with the oil of balsam, for they have been baptized into the death of

23 The oil of balsam is the strength of faith, for when a man comes forth from the water,’ he is in the likeness of Christ whom the Father raised from the dead. This is He who is over all; our Lord Jesus Christ is the only-begotten Son the Lord of life, and the Paraclete is the Spirit of truth who was sent by the Father to teach all things.

24 But let them that are baptized be far from transgression and be pure from sin, and let them be friends of God and enemies of the devil, and fellow-heirs of Christ and adversaries of Satan and his devices. And they shall be pure without stain, and shall be called the sons of Christ.

25 Moreover let them pray and entreat the Father that they may be one congregation together with the faithful, and say thus, Our Father who are in heaven. Hallowed be your name. Your Kingdom come. And your will be done, as in heaven so also on earth. Give us to-day our daily bread. Forgive us our trespasses and our iniquities, as we also forgive him that has trespassed against us.

26 And bring us not into temptation, but save us and deliver us from all evil. For yours is the Kingdom, the power and the glory, for ever and ever. Amen.

27 The deacons then must necessarily be pure and good, without blame, as the bishop; and serve continually in the Holy Church, and minister to God

28 With a good ministry, and fulfill their office in wisdom with one -heart and one spirit, and understand the reward of their service, and not be puffed up in ministering to the destitute. Even as our Lord Jesus Christ came, not to be ministered to, but to minister, and to give His life « ransom for many. And moreover He refused not to give His life in place of His friends.

29 And if the Lord of glory and the Lord of heaven and earth endured all this patiently for our sakes, how then are you puffed up in ministering to the poor and needy? You ought in everything to be imitators of our Lord Jesus Christ, who endured to become a servant, and accepted poverty and want, and humbled Himself, and bore the sufferings of the Cross for our sakes.

30 In like manner do you also do service to the poor your brothers, and be imitators of Christ. But he among you that desires to be chief, let him be your servant. So our Lord Jesus Christ fulfilled in word and in deed all that was written, and became the servant of many.

31 He took a towel,'' and poured water into a bason, and first washed the feet of His disciples and (then) wiped them with the towel with which His loins were girded. He did thus that we might love‘ one another, and taught us to love our neighbors as ourselves.‘? And if our Lord and Savior humbled Himself, and did thus, how then are you puffed up and slothful to do good to the destitute and afflicted?

32 Do service, then, in perfect love without murmuring, or accepting of persons. However do not this for outward appearance, but for God’s sake, that you may receive the reward of your toil from Him at the last day. The deacons ought to seek to satisfy the desires of the poor, before they ask them.

33 Moreover let them do service to the bishop, and be of one mind ‘ (with him), and account him as father and teacher and monitor.

34 And behold we command that a bishop be ordained by three bishops, and if not, by two. But by

35 One bishop it is not possible to give ‘ ordination: for by the testimony of three or two ‘ (witnesses) let ’ every word be established. Presbyters and deacons and others set in authority in the Church shall be ordained by one bishop. To presbyters and to deacons it is not given to ordain a layman; but let the presbyter teach, and baptize, and bless the people, and burn incense, and offer up the sacrifice.

36 And as for the deacon there ‘is nothing that he may do except minister to the bishop and the presbyters;‘ and let him fulfill aright his ministry.
