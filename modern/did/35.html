Didascalia

Chapter 35

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

OF TOIL AND THANKSGIVING.!

1 Moses the prophet said plainly to the people, even to the people of Israel, ‘And behold show you plainly the way of death, and (the way of) life; choose life for yourselves, that you may live.” And Elijah the prophet said to the people, “How long halts your heart concerning the Lord—the Lord is One; seek Him.” Our Lord Jesus Christ said, “A servant cannot serve two masters; otherwise he will obey one, and disobey the other.

2 You cannot then serve God and wealth. We have followed our Lord Jesus Christ, who has mercy on the whole world.

3 We say then that there are two ways, one of death, and one of life: separate and having no connection one with the other; one which leads to death, and one (which leads) to life. The first is the way of life concerning which the Law has spoken,

4 And said, “Love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your strength and with (all) your soul, besides whom there is no other god,” and, “love your neighbor as yourself,” and “what you desire not for yourself, that do not to your neighbor,” “‘ Bless them that curse you, and pray for them that oppress you,” and “ Love your enemies,” “What then is your reward, if you do good to them that do good to you” Take heed then and hate no man.

5 And again it is said, “You shall not hate any man, neither an Egyptian, nor an Edomite "; for they are all creatures of God. Keep far from evil men and from the desires of this world. “And to him that strikes you on the right cheek, turn the other.”!!

6 For David says, “If I have rendered a return to them that rendered evil to me, let your enemies cause me to fall naked.” And again it is said, “If any man compel you to go one mile, go (with him) twain”; and, “If any man take your coat, leave to him your cloak (also); and, “Do not prevent him that takes your goods”; and, “Give to him that asks of you, and shut not your door against him that desires to borrow of you.” For God is merciful (and) a lover of men, and gives all things to him that asks of Him.

7 And the Father “makes the sun to rise on the evil and the good, and sends rain on the righteous and the sinful.” You ought to give to all men of the labour of your hands; and honor you the saints. For it is said, ‘You will do no murder.” (Everyman) is your image and your likeness; and there is nothing worse than murder.

8 And, “You shall not commit adultery,” nor separate (what is) your flesh, nor make two parts (of it); for it is said, “They twain shall be one flesh,” and one spirit; and in form (only) are they separate; and “You shall not steal,” for when Achan the Israelite stole, they stoned him with stones, and he

9 Perished; and in like manner.

10 Gehazi stole and lied and was clothed with the leprosy of Naaman the Syrian; and Judas Iscariot also stole the purse of alms (from) which alms were given to the poor, and betrayed the Lord of Glory into the hands of the Jews, and then repented, and hanged himself, and his bowels gushed out, and he died; ‘Ananias and Sapphira his wife kept back part of the price of their possessions, and lied to the Holy Spirit, and died by (the sentence of) our beloved brother Peter the Apostle.

11 _ You shall not practice magic, nor use divination, nor consult wizards, for it is said, ‘You shall not trust in wizards.” And, You shall not kill a child by abortion, for it is the image and spirit of God. And,

12 “You shall not covet the possessions of your brother,

13 Nor his wife, nor his servant, nor his maid, nor his donkey, nor his ox.” Moreover, you will never swear falsely, but if you swear, swear truly. And again it is said, “You shall not bear false witness,” for “he that speaks lies against the poor provokes his creator to anger.”

14 And the way of evildoers leads to death. You shall not be of a double heart, or of a double tongue, for “the snare of a man is the word of his mouth,” ” and, “a talkative man shall not prosper upon earth.” And let not your words be idle, for everyone that speaks thoughtlessly shall be condemned.

15 And, You shall not be rapacious, for it is said, “Woe to them that wrong their neighbors.”

16 And you shall not accept persons, nor be deceitful in heart, nor proud, for ‘God humbles the proud.” And, You shall not hate any man, nor curse your brother, nor do evil to him. And abstain from all evil, and do not be passionate, nor envious, lest that befall you that befell Cain, and Saul, and Joab. For Cain killed Abel his brother, and God chose Abel

17 And accepted his sacrifice; and Saul persecuted David, when he overcame Goliath the Philistine, (and) was envious of him because of the songs which the women sang; and Joab, also killed Abner the Israelite, and Amasa of Judah.

18 And, You shall not be an augur, for it leads to idolatry, and concerning this Samuel says, “This thing is sin,” and it shall not be thus in Israel, for all these things are rejected in the law.

19 And, You shall not be a murmurer, for you have heard what befell those who murmured against Moses. Be meek like Moses and David, for the meek shall inherit the earth.

20 Be therefore merciful, for they shall obtain mercy. Accept every toil that comes upon you, like Job and Lazarus, that you may receive a reward from God.

21 And honor him that teaches you the word of God, and be mindful of him day and night, for where instruction is, there God is.

22 And reconcile those who are at variance, and judge uprightly, for the judgment is the Lord’s.

23 And when you pray, do not be of a double heart. Peter said, He said to me while I] was on the sea, Why do you doubt, and make for yourself a double heart?

24 And if you give alms to the poor, Doubt not; know you then who it is that will render the reward; is it not the Lord? Give, then, to everyone that asks of you that which he needs.

25 And forget not any of the commandments of the Lord; and confess your trespass before Him, and sin no more, that you may find (that which is) good before the Lord your God, for He desires not the death of a sinner, but that he should turn to repentance.

26 And honor your father and your mother; forsake not your brothers and your kinsmen.

27 And fear the King, and know that his

28 Position is from the Lord; and honor rulers, for they are ministers of God.

29 And offer not a sacrifice while you do

30 Evil, before you repent of the evil of your doings.

31 This is the way of death and (the way) of life; and

32 May God make you worthy to follow the way of our

33 Lord Jesus Christ.
