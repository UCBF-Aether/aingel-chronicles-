Chapter 14

OF WIDOWS

1 (A widow) ought to be appointed at sixty years of age, in whom are good works, and (who is) elect and pure, and desires not another husband. Younger widows are not worthy of the office.

2 But if you appoint a young widow, as she is not able to have continency as the elder widows, but desires to marry again, trust her not, for she will bring a shameful report, and dishonour, and reproach on the Church, and shall be condemned before God, for she has married a second husband, and has not kept the command of the Scriptures.

3 Therefore they ought not to appoint (them) hastily, until they make enquiry as to their lives, and prove them (to see) if they have (the gift of) continence. But if they cannot have continency it is better not to appoint ()! Them, for they keep not that which they have promised.

4 But if there is a young widow, who has lived for a little while with her husband, and been separated from him by death, and then has lived alone, and observes the duties of the widows, she who does thus is blessed: for she is like to the widow

7 And let not younger widows be appointed to the order (of widows) of the Church, for they cannot have continency while they are in the vigour of their youth; and they will marry a second husband. And as for such as these their youth is (given over) to Satan; but let them take heed for their own salvation that they marry not a second husband.

8 And as for such as these you must necessarily understand this, that a first marriage is pure before God; but those that marry a second time are transgressors of the law; yet it is not on account of their union, but (because) they have proved false against God the Creator.

9 And those that marry a third time shall not be reckoned with the flock of the Lord Christ; and those that marry a fourth time, their wantonness is still more abundantly manifested, and they shall find shame and dishonour. For in the first creation God gave one woman to one man, and for this cause they two are one flesh.

10 Thus we command the younger women; if their first husbands die, let them marry a second husband, if they cannot have continency, lest they fall again into the snare and devices of Satan, and into evil desires that destroy

11 Souls, and go down themselves into the eternal condemnation of Gehenna, where is no “ rest.

12 But widows who are known for righteousness, women who have married one man, and concerning whom many bear witness that they have lived in purity, let such be reckoned with the faithful widows, and (those who) bring up their children without blame.! Those that are such you ought to help, and give them what they need, for their trust is in God.

13 And now also stretch forth your hand to give, and remember the poor,’ and give to each one according to his need, and visit the widows and the orphans and the afflicted.

14 The bishop, then, ought to help and save and supply the need of all the destitute and troubled, and help them, and feed in his good pasture all the people, who bring their offering of alms. From what they have brought to you, give what they have given to you,“ to each one, as is fitting, as a good and merciful steward, for this is well-pleasing before God.

15 Everyone that gives to the destitute shall find a good reward; and you, who have toiled for God, will obtain a recompense. And say thus to those to whom you have given, (It is) such a one who has offered (this), and I have given (it) to you, that you may pray for him,‘ and entreat on his behalf.

16 You ‘ ought to do good to all men, and not say, To this man will I give, and the other I will refuse. Do you make no distinction between any.

17 For our.Lord says, “Give to everyone that has asked of you,” that is, either your friend, or your enemy, or your kinsman, or a stranger, or one that is married, or one that has no wife, or a widow, or an orphan—even to every one that is destitute do you good. For our Lord has com-

25 Seek words of faith and deeds of righteousness, and a good hope before God. But let those (widows) that desire to learn that which is appointed, go to those in authority to learn and hear the word of admonition: and let them not hearken to slander, nor follow after other gods, but worship the One God who created all things.

26 For the Lord has likened the Kingdom of heaven to a grain of mustard seed, which is smaller than all seeds, and when a man tastes it, it burns him. And so the word of the doctrine of the faith burns Satan. We ought not to transgress the words of the prophets, nor reveal mysteries to unbelievers but we (ought) to be firm in faith.

30 For there stayed with us Mary Magdelene, and the sisters of Lazarus, Mary and Martha and Salome, and others also with them”: (and) since He commanded not them to teach along with us neither is it right for other women to teach. For the man is the head of the woman, so it is not right

31 That the member should be greater than the head.” Widows and virgins ought not to walk about in the streets, nor enter men’s houses, but they who do thus are not widows nor virgins, but rather become a stumbling-block to men.

32 And if they cleave not to the Church, they become slothful and insatiate, and covetous of the goods of others, and they lead men captive, and become seducers of others, and cause souls to fall; and they that follow them are far from the Lord.

33 But as for those that enter the Church and understand not that which is in the Scriptures, and hearken not to the word of the book of admonition, concerning them that do thus Isaiah the prophet says, “Hearing they shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing they shall see, and shall not perceive: for the heart of this people is darkened.”

34 And to widows such as these show you not the sacrifice of Christ. But let her that desires to please Christ dwell in her house in quietness, and glorify Him by day and by night with a mouth that keeps not silence, and let her pray to God with a pure heart like wise Judith who prayed to God for Israel day and night.

35 In like manner the merciful widow will pray to God, ceasing not to resort to the church every day,! For all her thoughts are ready continually for (His) service, and she turns not to pleasure; but her eyes are pure, and her ears holy, and she stretches not out her hands to covetousness, and her feet run in the way of peace, and her mouth speaks not lies, and her food is in moderation.

37 The widows, then, ought to be pure, and to be in subjection with fear and trembling, but above all to the bishop and to the presbyters and to the deacons and to the deaconesses. And let them do nothing without the counsel of the deacons, nor go after pleasant foods or wantonness. But if they be found doing thus, let them be admonished by the deacon, and let him command them to fast.

38 And in like manner let not the priests receive gifts from robbers and moneychangers and adulterers. Receive you nothing from those that do thus, nor offer (it) as an offering to the Lord your God. The widows ought, then, to be ready to hearken to the words of their elders, and to do all that they command them, and to observe the word of the bishop, and to honor him as God.

39 But if there is anyone that receives alms from the evil-doers and the ungodly, he shall fall into their error, and die in their iniquity. If he prays for them, he grieves Christ.

40 But let him turn them to repentance; and those that convert not’ the evil-doers and liars! And sinners are partakers with them in the evil of their deeds.
