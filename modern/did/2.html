THAT THE RICH OUGHT TO SEEK TO KNOW THE PROFIT OF THE SCRIPTURES AT ALL TIMES IN THE STRENGTH OF THE HOLY SPIRIT

1 But if you are rich, and have no need of the art and labour of your hand, with which to earn your bread and livelihood, do not be slothful, nor sit idle, but go to the faithful, and sit with them, (even) with them that are united with you in faith; and speak with another of the word of life, and read what is in the Book of Kings and the Prophets, and sing a psalm of praise, and hearken to the word of the Gospel, which is the fulfilment of the whole law.

2 And keep far from all service of the gods of the Gentiles; and take no heed of any words of vanity which profit not, and seek no other law, nor false prophets who change and corrupt the faith. What then are you unable (to find) in the law of God, that you

3 Should you seek after the learning of the Gentiles? But if you desire to know the power of the interpretation of the Scriptures, seek out the Book of Kings: and if you wish to find out the power of the wise and their teaching, you have the prophets, Job and Solomon. These are they that are full of wisdom, and you will find knowledge in them.

4 These, more than all teachers, shall make understanding to increase, for their word is the voice of the Lord God, who alone is all-wise. And if you desire a song, you have the Psalms of David.

5 And if you desire to seek to understand the creation of the world and the generation of the first fathers, you have the Law.’ And if you desire to know wisdom and doctrine, you have the book of the law, which is full of the glory of the Lord God.

6 Keep far, then, from works of evil, and from the doctrine of Satan, and the word of vanity, which is not in the law; but read what is in Deuteronomy, that you may understand and know the glory of God, who has saved you from many snares of Satan.

7 For the Savior came not to destroy the Law and the prophets, but to fulfill them, and to loose the bonds which are in Deuteronomy, and to bring men to a spiritual teaching. Therefore He calls us, saying, ‘‘Come to me all you that are troubled and heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”

8 And you, when you have read the Law and the prophets and (the Books of) the Kings and the Gospel, will understand how many were the righteous men who have reigned and pleased God in their lives, and for this have found life eternal; and also you will know how many evil kings have transgressed through lack of understanding.

9 These have perished and been destroyed by the stroke of the judgment of God,’ and have lost eternal life. And knowing this, you will be

10 Strong in the right faith, and will find grace and favor with our Lord Jesus Christ, for you are one of His members.

11 Moreover, when you desire to bathe, enter not into the bath where the women bathe, lest they see the beauty of your youth, and the charm of your flesh, and stumble because of you, or you yourself sin and fall by reason of them.

13 And again it is said, ‘Keep your way from her, and draw not nigh to the door of her house, lest you give your life to others, and your livelihood to them that are without mercy; lest strangers be filled with your strength, and your labour pass to the house of another; and you will repent afterwards when your flesh (and) your body are weakened, and you will say, How have I hated instruction, and my ears are turned from reproof, and I have not hearkened to the voice of them that gave me commandments,” who admonished me and taught me, nor caused my ear to attend: I have been well nigh in all evil.” And now also I say to you, Choose out all the good things which are written in the Holy Scriptures, that we may not neglect these witnesses,” and turn aside to a multitude of words, but may be strong in faith, and in doing good.

14 And let us keep far from all evil-doing, and be pure from all evil (and) uncleanness before God, that we may find life eternal.
