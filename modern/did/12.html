Didascalia

Chapter 12

THAT BISHOPS OUGHT TO BE PEACEMAKERS, MEN OF MERCY, WHO PARDON HIM THAT Has TRESPASSED AGAINST THEM, AND RECEIVE THE REPENTANCE OF THOSE THAT TURN TO GOD; AND IF THEY DO NOT THUS THEY SHALL NOT BE CALLED BISHOPS, BUT EVIL

1 But if the bishop pray for peace upon others, shall he not much more then be peaceable himself that he is not a son of darkness, and that he may be called a son of light‘ And if in himself there is no peace, how then can he give (peace) to others?

2 Therefore the beginning of all his work must necessarily be peace, without evil or hatred; but in love and in peace let him feed the sheep of Christ. Especially let him guard them as a faithful friend, that they may be saved together and become one flock. But let evil men, who work iniquity and (cause) hatred and trouble, be far from God.

3 Thus our Lord has commanded us, When we pray to the Father, saying, Your will be done, as in heaven, so also on earth; that as the hosts of heavenly and spiritual ones glorify (Him), so every creature on earth may glorify the One God by His only-begotten Son our Lord and our God and our Savior Jesus Christ.

4 This, then, is His good pleasure that we should glorify Him and bless Him with one mind, and worship Him with one spirit. This is the good pleasure of Christ, that those that are saved should be many, and that not one of them should perish, nor the Church be diminished. Kill not the soul which has been made alive through repentance, for it has been saved from indignation and anger.

5 But do you in the beauty of your patience fulfill every word of the Scriptures. But if you scatter the sheep of His flock, you shall be enemies of God. Destroy not the lambs that Christ has fed.

6 Be of good understanding, then, O bishop, lest by your occasion the sheep of Christ’s flock be scattered, whom we have gathered by the preaching of tongues from every quarter, by toil, and by weariness, and by sorrow, and by watching, and by hunger, and by thirst and labour (and) journeyings, and by persecutions, and by affliction, and by imprisonment.

7 And behold we have done the good pleasure of God, and have filled with plants the house which is the holy congregation, the Church of God. Those that are called into it rejoice and are glad, glorifying and blessing God, even those that have been called by you to life eternal.

8 And you also, O people, work amongst you (the works of) love and peace, that you may walk in the Church of God with one heart. And for this you shall obtain a good reward from God.

9 But be, O bishop, holy, without

10 Blemish, and do not that which is unseemly, nor be Passionate, nor deceitful, nor of evil-disposition, but be a teacher and helper of those that perish, and a counsellor of good; and confirm the people as a man of God. And when you are gathered together in the church, command them to walk in the good way as a wise ship-master.

11 And command the deacons to be as the sailors, that they may make the brothers to sit down each one in his own place, as befits in righteousness and purity, for they are together with you in one ship.

12 Let the length, then, of the church be towards the East; and (let there be) two porches in its sides after the likeness of a ship; and let the throne of the bishop be set in the midst. Then let the presbyters sit on his right hand and on his left: and let the deacons also stand ready to serve with their garments girt up like sailors.

13 But let the people sit in their places in fear and trembling; and in like manner let the women sit by themselves in silence. And let the reader also stand in (his) place, and read the books of Moses, and of Joshua the son of Nun, and of the Judges, and the Kings, and the Chronicles, and the books after the captivity (and) return from Babylon, (and) Job and Solomon, and the fifteen prophets.

14 And after they have read ” from the books two at a time, one shall rise up and sing praises in the words of David, and the people shall answer with praises.

15 Then they shall read the Acts of the Apostles and the -

16 Epistles of our brother Paul, our beloved fellowlabourer, which he wrote, and sent to all the churches by the help of the Holy Spirit. And after this let the presbyters or the deacons read the Gospel which we have given to you, even I Matthew and John, and which Luke and Mark, the fellow-labourers of the renowned Paul, have proclaimed, (and) which they have appointed for you. And when the Gospel is read, let

17 The presbyters and the deacons and all the people stand in great fear; for it is written, ‘Be silent, O Israel, and hearken.” Then let the presbyters stand one by one, and confirm the people; and afterwards the bishop that he may save them by (his) admonition.

18 And next let the doorkeepers stand where the men come in, and set them in their places: let the deaconess in like manner make the women stand in their places. If any man be found standing in a place which is not his, let the deacons put him to shame, and then bring him back to his own place; for the deacons are like the sailors, and the church like a ship.

19 However it is not a ship, but a flock of sheep. Now shepherds separate the sheep from the goats, and place them according to their kinds and according to their divisions; and in like manner let the people be found together in the church according to their classes.

20 Let the young men stand by themselves; but if there is no place to contain them, let them stand with (the rest of) the people, And let the old men sit by themselves, and let the children sit in front of their fathers, these taking care of them. And let the maidens sit by themselves; but if there is no place, let them stand behind the married women.

21 And let the virgins and the widows and the elder women stand before all the (other) women. And let those that have husbands and children sit by themselves. The deacons should arrange them one by one; and let no one pass over into his neighbor’s seat.

22 And let him mark the conduct and behavior of the people in church, lest they sleep or be slothful in prayer; but let him watch them, and if any be found laughing or talking foolishly, let him admonish them and cause them to be silent.

23 We ought, then, to stand in the church in fear and trembling, and to set our thoughts to hearken to the word of the Scriptures. And when the catechumens and those that are under penance have

24 Departed let all the people rise up and look towards the East, and pray to God, who is in every place. To bring them back to their former place, to the garden of joy, from which the first man went forth, when he consented to the counsel of the Serpent, and transgressed the commandment of God. And after this let the presbyters rise up and offer the sacrifice to God with fear and trembling.

25 Then let the ministers rise and command them to keep silence; and let the deacon who stands at the right hand ” of the bishop say thus to the people, Let no man harbour in his heart revenge, or hatred, or envy against his neighbor. Then let the men greet one another with a holy kiss; and in like manner let the women greet one another.

26 However greet not one another with guile like Judas Iscariot, who kissed our Lord and betrayed Him wickedly. And after this let the deacon pray, and make supplication for the whole world, and all that is in it, for the fruits of the ground, for the king, for the priests, for the rulers, for the chief priests, for the peace of the holy Apostolic Church.

27 And after this let the chief priest make supplication and entreaty that peace may be upon the people.

28 Then let him bless them, as Moses commanded the priests to bless the people, saying thus, “The Lord bless you, and keep you, and make his face to shine on you, and have mercy upon you,‘ and give you peace.” And after this let the bishop make supplication, and say, Save Your people, and bless Your heritage; feed them and lift them up for ever.

29 You have purchased and redeemed by Your precious Blood Your Holy Church

30 Wickedly.

31 Through our Lord Jesus Christ, Who has called her to be a congregation of kings and priests, a pure family, a holy people. And after that the priests have taken up the mysteries, let them take heed lest (any) enter who are not of the faithful.

32 And then let the men stand up, and the women also,‘ veiling their heads, (and) let them put forth their hands, and pray for pardon, (and) receive His holy ‘’ Flesh and precious blood.

33 And if any brothers or sisters come from a distant place, let the deacon enquire into their lives, and get knowledge of their faith. And if they have the tokens of the right faith, let him permit them to enter the church; but if they are unbelievers, let him not permit them to enter. And if a presbyter come from a distant place, let the presbyters receive him; and in like manner the deacons.

34 And if a bishop come, a stranger, let the bishop, who is like to him, receive him, and let him sit with him, and let (the bishop) show him honor. Then let him ask him to teach and admonish! The people; for it is said, “A prophet is not had in honor in his own city.” Entreat him to offer up the sacrifice.

35 But if, by reason of fear, he refuses, let them all the more entreat him to bestow a blessing on the people. And if there is one that comes to you, a stranger, or one of the men of the place, clothed in goodly apparel, full of grace, do you, O bishop, declare to him the word of God.

36 And when you hear them teaching or reading the Scriptures, cease not the work of your ministry. The brothers ought to receive strangers,

37 The deacons.

38 And magnify and honor them. But if there is one (who is) haughty, and (who) honors not a stranger, let the deacon coming admonish him, and set him behind (the rest of) the people, that others, when they see, may fear.

39 And if there come to you poor men, whether young or old, let the deacon receive them with a glad heart, and set them each one in his place, as befits, without respect of persons, but (so) that your service be that which is well pleasing to God. And let the deaconess also do in like manner with the women, whether rich or poor.

40 Admonish, then, O bishop, your people, and bid them come to the church day and night, and never absent themselves from it, that the congregation in it is not diminished, for they are members of Christ. And we say this not concerning the priests alone, but concerning all the people, that each one may understand the word of the Lord.

41 For our Lord says, “But he that is not with me is my adversary, and he that gathers not with me scatters.” Do not be slothful, then, for you are members of Christ; separate not yourselves from His Body and His Blood; nor choose the cares of this world before the commandments of God.

42 Gather yourselves together in the church in the evening and in the morning; glorify God, and sing, and read the Psalms of David, the sixty-second, and the hundred and fortieth as well.

43 And especially on the Christian Sabbath. Which is (the day of) His holy resurrection, offer praise and thanksgiving and honor to God, who created all things by His Son Jesus Christ, whom He sent to us; who was well pleased to suffer according to His will, and was buried in the earth, and rose again from the dead.

44 But if you come not to the church, what excuse, or what answer will you make to God? For on this day, the Christian Sabbath,

45 We ought to hear the preaching of His holy resurrection, and remember His sufferings, and make remembrance of Him, and read the Scriptures of the prophets, and the Gospel; and (celebrate) the eucharist, the sacrifice, and oblation, (our) spiritual food.

46 But if you watch not day and night,’ and do not according as we say, you are transgressors against God, and enemies to Him, for you have chosen pleasure, eating and drinking, and delights, that grow old and perish, and you have forsaken that which grows not old, nor perishes.

47 And to them that do such things God says, “The foolish people ’ are better than you”; as He reproached” Jerusalem, and said to her, “Sodom is better than you.” Behold, then, let us look at the foolish Gentiles.

48 Every day, when they awake and rise from sleep, they betake them at once to those gods that ’ profit not, and they worship them, and make supplication to them concerning their needs; and they gather themselves together, great and small, on their feast-days, and not these alone, but others also come from distant places, and worship and serve them.

49 And the foolish Jews, though ungodly (and) unbelieving, yet work six days, and rest on the seventh day. Then they gather themselves together in the synagogues, and keep a feast to idols, and magnify them, and honor them that have no life, nor spirit, and (that) profit not at all. However they vaunt themselves, and say, We are Jews.

50 But if these do thus, and gather themselves together at all times, and in ’ folly serve those in whom is no profit, how is it then that you who have received grace from the Lord God, are slothful, and neglect the service of the church? If you keep far from the church, you shall lose your hope, and abandon your salvation, and become ungodly,

51 Take heed, then, that you keep not far from the church of God, nor follow the steps of the ungodly and unbelievers, and the worshippers of idols, and the congregation of the Jews. And go not toa temple of demons, nor have fellowship with Christ’s murderers, nor

52 Enter into a congregation of the ungodly. Have you not heard that which is said, “I have not sat in the assembly of vanity, nor have I gone in with evil-doers: I have hated the congregation of the wicked, and I will not sit with the deceitful and evil-doers ’’?

53 And again it is said, “Blessed is the man that has not walked in the counsel of the ungodly, and that has not stood in the way of sinners, and that has not sat in the seat of the scornful.

54 But his delight is in the law of the Lord; and on his law he meditated day and night.” But behold you have left the congregation of the faithful, and have despised the Church of God, and have not kept His law and His ordinance; but have followed the way of robbers, and have counted the unclean as clean, and have become a companion of those that were separated from you, and have made your dwelling with the heathen, and have inclined your ear to the words of the theatre, which are full of all uncleanness and vanity, in which is no profit.

55 And you have refused to hearken to the words of Jeremiah the prophet, who says, ‘‘O Lord, I have not sat on the seat of judgment with injustice, but I am afraid before your face.” Therefore do you desire to hear the words of the dead heathen, who were wounded through sin, by the works of the devil, (and) kill the man who follows them, and draw him away from the right faith, and make him to serve many gods?

56 But do not you thus, but keep the commandments of God, and choose for yourselves life. And above all seek after the law of the Church of the Lord, which Christ, His beloved Son, who was before the world was created, has purchased with His Blood.

57 For His Church is the Daughter of the Most High, she who strengthens our hearts, and brings us near to God, that we may find a place with Him; for we are His members and His

58 Friends. Let us, then, be good and pure, without uncleanness, and perfect in the right faith and in every good work.
