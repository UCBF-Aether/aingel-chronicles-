Didascalia

Chapter 18

OF ORPHANS

1 CHRISTIAN people who have no children ought to educate orphans, whether youths or virgins, and adopt them as their own children, and love them yet more. And he that has a son who has attained the due age, let him marry the orphan virgin.

2 And if you do thus, you shall accomplish a great work and be the fathers of the orphans, (and) obtain a reward from the Lord God for the service you have done. But if there is one that is proud, and despises the orphan, and says, I will not marry a poor woman,’ let him understand then who is the father of the orphan, and the protector of the widow.

3 For he that thinks to do thus shall scatter his wealth to another, and shall fulfill in himself that which the Scripture says, “ What the saints have not eaten, the Syrians‘ shall eat,” even as Isaiah says, “Your land a foreign enemy shall devour it in your sight.”
