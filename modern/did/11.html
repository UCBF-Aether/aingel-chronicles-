Didascalia

Chapter 11

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

THAT CHRISTIANS OUGHT EVER! TO FORGIVE THE TRESPASSES OF THEIR Neighbors, AND NOT LET REVENGE DWELL IN THEIR HEARTS, NOR THINK EVIL

1 But if you desire to be Christians indeed, walk in the way of the Lord, and loose every band of sin; for the Savior has commanded you to forgive the trespasses of your neighbor (even to) seventy times seven. And if you desire not to forgive them, hear what Jeremiah the prophet says?

2 But if you think evil in your heart, and nurse hatred, and judge in anger, your prayer shall not be able to ascend to God, for you have not done the commandments of your Lord. But if you have forgiven‘ the trespass of your brother four hundred and ninety times, you have become slow of anger and great in mercy, that you may save yourself.

3 And if you do thus, you will be a son of your Father who is in heaven, and when you make supplication to Him, He shall hear your prayer as a faithful friend.

4 Therefore, O bishops, when you are gathered together for prayer and praise and worship, and to hear the reading of the word of admonition, read the Holy Scriptures. Let the deacon stand amongst you, crying with a loud voice, and saying, If there is any man that has’ in his heart revenge, or

5 Envy, or deceit, or any malice against his neighbor,‘ let him speedily be reconciled with him, and then God will receive their prayer and their supplication.! And when you enter into a house, say thus, The peace of God be in this house. And if there is there sons of peace, your peace shall rest upon them.

6 Even as it is written, “He is peace to them that are afar off, and'! To them that are nigh,” even to the people that believe in Christ. All who enter into the Church of God ought to pray to Him to send peace upon His people.
