THAT WIVES OUGHT TO OBEY THEIR HUSBANDS AND WALK IN TRUE WISDOM

1 Fi.,.] Ler the wife obey and be subject to her ‘husband, for ‘the head of a wife is her husband,” and the head of the man that walks in the

2 Commandments.

3 Way of righteousness is Christ; and “the head of Christ is God” the Father, who is above all, who was and is to come, the Creator of all, with His Son our Lord Jesus Christ, to whom be honor, greatness, and majesty and glory.

4 You women also obey your husbands, and be in subjection with humility and honor and the fear of God; and delight your husbands with good service, that you may find favor with them, as we said before. For thus says the wisdom of Solomon, “TE any man have found a brave woman, she is more precious than a jewel of great price.

5 She that is such— the heart of her husband trusts in her; she that is such-—good spoil‘ will not be wanting. She helps her husband with good in good, and not with evil in evil (in) her life; who cards wool and flax, (and) has worked with her hands (that which is) good. She is as a ship which trades afar; and she gathers for herself all riches.

6 And she rises up by night, and gives food to her household, and their tasks to her handmaidens. She saw a field and bought (it); with the fruit of her hands she planted a possession. She firmly girded her loins, and put forth her hands to the work. She tasted that labour is good, and her lamp goes not out all night long.

7 She stretches out her hands to what does profit, and her arm is strong to spin. And she opened’ her hands in mercy to the needy, and the palm of her hand is stretched out to the poor. And her husband has no care about his house, even if he tarries long anywhere.

8 All her household are clothed;' double cloaks of fine linen and purple she made for her husband, and for herself she made garments. And her husband becomes notable in the gates, when he sits in the assembly with the

9 Elders of the land. Linen garments she made,! And gave (them) away, and girdles for the Canaanites. And her mouth she opened according to the law, and set a fence for her tongue. With strength and beauty was she clothed, and rejoiced in the latter days. They that enter her house are many: she has not eaten the bread of idleness.

10 She opens her mouth in wisdom, and according to the ordinance of the law; and her almsgiving has brought up children and_ enriched (them), and her husband has praised her. Many daughters have obtained riches, and many have done mightily, but you excel and have surpassed them all. Flattery is deceitful, and beauty is despised, for a wise woman shall be blessed, and she that fears the Lord shall be extolled.

11 Give her of the fruit of her lips, and let her husband be praised in the gates,” ” that you, O women, may know that a woman who loves her husband: shall find honor and blessing and compassion with the Lord God.

12 Take heed and understand, O woman, that desire and wish to be faithful, that you may please the Lord, vaunt not yourself, nor adorn yourself for another man; and clothe not yourself with gorgeous apparel, nor put sandals on your feet for show, as the harlots do to lead (men) astray: but keep yourself from all evil-doing and adultery, lest by you other men offend. Obey your husband rather, and show kindness to him.

13 But if you desire to walk about in the assembly and the streets, to ensnare the souls of many, then judgment from God shall come upon you, and you will be as one that has no hope, as when a wicked man falls into great affliction, and brings on himself disgrace, and dishonour, and sorrow. In like manner a wicked woman brings on herself dishonour: and as,a lion that lies in wait

14 Knowledge.. Draws.

15 And rends, so she ensnares fools, even as it is written, saying, “As moths (destroy) a garment, and as decay a tree, So sorrow wastes the heart of a man.” And furthermore it is said, “It is better to dwell in the wilderness, than that you should dwell with a quarrelsome and talkative and passionate woman.” And now also I say to you, O Christian people, look not after nor follow adulterous women.

16 But it is right for faithful women to veil themselves in purity, not seeking to enhance the beauty of their faces with dyes and painting and adornment, which have no profit, on the form which God created. Let them not do thus, but when they walk in the way let them incline their heads, being veiled.

17 And take heed to yourselves, you faithful women, and speak no (word of) hate to anyone, but above all (not) to your husbands.

18 O you good and gentle women, worship God with joy and gladness; and you also, our children and our brothers’ and kinsmen and beloved, seek after the precious wisdom that adorns our life, and enlightens our -hearts, and brings us close to God, and wins for us an entrance into the Kingdom of heaven in life eternal and rest.
