Didascalia

Chapter 7

THAT WE OUGHT NOT TO ADMONISH ANY MAN EXCEPT IT BE PROVED BY TESTIMONY THAT HE Has SINNED

1 For he that thrusts forth an innocent person, is worse than a murderer, and perceives not the mercy of God, for he remembers not the love of his neighbor.' And he who does thus shall not find salvation, for he has slipped away, and fallen into grievous sin, because he has not admonished, nor converted those who have committed many sins, but has scattered the flock of God, and also fought against Him.

2 For God is righteous, and righteous is His judgment, with which He judges all, righteous men and sinners; (He is) merciful to those who turn to Him in righteousness and mercy and meekness, as David the beloved of God (says).‘

3 The bishop, then, ought to judge uprightly and follow after God and do His will, for God judges righteously those that turn to Him. Do you remember not David, when he sinned and Nathan said to him, Why have you done thus? And he said, I have sinned and I repent. And when he confessed his iniquity, he was saved from death.

4 And Nathan said to him, “Be of good cheer and doubt not; you shall not die."” Jonah the prophet, also, not wishing to preach to the men of Nineveh, was plunged in the sea, and a whale swallowed him up, but afterwards he came forth from the whale’s belly, and was saved from death. And Hezekiah, also—when he became proud, judgment came upon him.

5 And Hezekiah humbled himself, and forsook his pride, and wept before God, and (God) had mercy on him. Hear, you bishops, the profit that is in this matter; for it is written in the Fourth Book of Kings: ‘And Manasseh his son reigned in his stead.

6 Twelve years old was Manasseh when he began to reign, (and) fifty and five years reigned he in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Hephzibah; and he did evil in the sight of the Lord.” Moreover it is written in the Second Book of Chronicles, saying, “And he did evil in the sight of the Lord more than all the uncleanness of the nations whom the Lord rooted out from before the children of Israel.

7 And he turned and built in the high place the altar which Hezekiah his father had destroyed, and he set up pillars for the Baalim, and made groves, and worshipped all the host of heaven, and served them. And he built an altar in the house of the Lord, of which the Lord said,!! Let my name be in Jerusalem for ever.

8 And he built altars for all the stars of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord; and he himself made his own sons

9 To pass through the fire in the land of Benomnimén, and used signs, and [made himself like a donkey '], and practiced magic, and set up diviners and soothsayers, (and) worked much evil in the sight of the Lord to sin against Him.

10 And he set the graven and molten images which he made in the house of the Lord; (of which the Lord had said) to David and Solomon his son, In this house and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, will I set my name for ever; and I will not again move the foot of Israel from the land which I gave to their fathers.

11 However let them observe all that I have commanded them in all my law and my ordinances and my judgments by the hand of Moses. And Manasseh made Judah to sin, and them that dwelt in Jerusalem, by doing evil above'’ all the nations whom the Lord removed before the face of the children of Israel. And the Lord spoke against Manasseh and against his people, and they hearkened not to him.

12 And the Lord sent against them the captains of the host of the king of Assyria, and they took Manasseh in bonds, and bound him in chains, and brought him to Babylon.

13 And when he was in affliction, he sought the face of the Lord his God, and he humbled himself greatly before the face of the God of his fathers.’’' And Manasseh the son of Hezekiah the King prayed, and said thus: “O Lord, Ruler of all the world, the God of our fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and all their righteous race, who have made heaven and earth with all that in them

14 Is; who rebuked the sea with the word of your commandment; who shut up the depths, and seal them in terrible majesty, and that by your glorious name; before the face of whose might everything trembles and shakes, for the greatness of your glory is without end. Terrible is the plague of your wrath against sinners.

15 The mercy of your commandment has no number, nor measure, for you alone are the Lord most high, merciful, slow of anger, and bountiful in comPassion, and gentle towards the sins of the sons of men.

16 And now, O my Lord, the God of the righteous—for it is not for a righteous man that you have appointed repentance, not for Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, who sinned not against you—turn you to the repentance of mea sinner, for I have sinned beyond the number of the sand of the sea; and my sins are many.

17 And I am not worthy to look up, and behold the height of heaven, because of the multitude of my iniquities. I have toiled in chains of iron, that I might get me respite of my sins, and by this also I have had no respite, for I have provoked you to anger, and done evil in your sight, observing vanities and multiplying things that profited me not.

18 And now I bow the knees of my heart, entreating your mercy. I have sinned, O Lord, I have sinned; and I confess my sin. I make supplication and entreat of you; have compassion on me, O Lord, have compassion on me, and deliver me not over to my sin. Reserve not evil against me for ever; and take not vengeance upon me in the depths of the earth.

19 For you, O Lord, are the God of those that repent; and your goodness shall be manifest upon me, since while I was not worthy, you did save me in the multitude of your compassion. I will glorify you at all times and all the days of my life; for all the host of heaven glorify you, and yours is the glory for ever. Amen.”

20 And when the Lord saw that Manasseh repented of the evil ‘of his doings which he had done in his

21 Ignorance, the Lord pardoned him and heard his prayer and accepted the voice of his petition. And at once a flame of fire encircled him, and the bonds of his chains of iron were loosed. Then the Lord saved Manasseh from all his affliction, “and brought him back to Jerusalem to his kingdom.

22 And Manasseh knew that the Lord was his God,” and he served the Lord with all his heart and with all his soul, all the days of his life; and he was reckoned with the righteous.

23 Then he thrust forth from the house of the Lord the strange gods that were made by the hands of men, ‘and he raised high (the wall), and appointed captains of the host in every place over the walls of Judah.

24 And all the altars which he had built on the mountain of the house of the Lord and in Jerusalem, he utterly destroyed from out the city; and he erected the altar of the Lord, and sacrificed on the altar, and offered praise. And Manasseh spoke to Judah to serve the Lord, the God of Israel. And Manasseh slept with his fathers in peace; and Amon” his son reigned in his stead.

25 And he did evil in the sight of the Lord, as Manasseh his father had done, and provoked the Lord his God to anger.”

26 Behold then, you have heard, our children, how he served idols, and sacrificed to them, and in his lawlessness killed many. And the Lord God afflicted him for a while, but when he repented, He forgave his sins, and restored him to his kingdom. For God receives the repentance of them that turn to Him, and restores them again to their former honor.

27 For there is no sin greater than idolatry, since it is rebellion against God, yet men are saved from it by true repentance. But whoever transgresses and commits sin, and says in his heart, What will God do to me?—he is a rebel against God. For he does not first try himself, but walks in his own way, and does

28 The desire of his soul, and says, This is good. Whoever does thus, and repents not, shall not have forgiveness. Thus, then, did Amon, the son of Manasseh.

29 For the Holy Scripture says, “ Amon conceived rebellion in his heart, and did evil in the sight of the Lord, as Manasseh his father had done; and he multiplied proud words,! And said, My father sinned much in his youth, and when he was old, he turned and repented.

30 I also will do in like manner and will walk in my own way, and will follow the desire of my soul, and when I am old, I will turn from it, and repent before the Lord. Then Amon did evil in the sight of the Lord, and worked exceeding much evil, as those before him had done.

31 Then the Lord chastised him, and destroyed him from off the good land, and his servants [aid (hands) upon him, and killed him in (his) house. “And the people of the land killed those who had laid (hands) on King Amon. And he reigned two years.”

32 Understand then, you people, and think not to do evil as Amon did, lest you be destroyed. In like manner, the bishop ought to’’ feed and convert the people and admonish them, and heal them of their sickness and receive their repentance, so far as he is able.

33 But if he is lacking in mercy, and receive not the repentance of sinners who turn to him, he is despised and dishonoured, and shall be condemned before the Lord his God.

34 But if he receive the repentance of those that turn to him,! He shall find compassion and mercy with the Lord God, who sent His only-begotten Son into the world; and He was pleased to be born of a woman, and was made man for our sakes. And He endured suffering on the Cross, and

35 Was wounded, and died. He is God the Word, the Angel of His great and terrible counsel; He took our sicknesses and bore our sufferings, and by His wounds our wounds have been healed..In_ like manner, let us, too, patiently endure suffering and affliction, and receive the repentance of them that have sinned.

36 See then the mercy of the Lord towards Matthew, who was a tax collector—when he turned to Him, He made him an evangelist; and towards Peter, who denied Him thrice through fear—when he repented and wept bitterly, He received his repentance, and made him the shepherd of His sheep; and towards Paul, our brother, though he was formerly a persecutor, who blasphemed against the Holy Spirit, yet when he turned to Him, He made him an apostle.

37 And to the woman who was a sinner, who gave alms ‘ to many, He said, ‘Your sin has been forgiven you.” ‘ And the other woman also, the adulteress whom the priests set before Him to prove Him,‘ He saved, and said to her, ‘‘Go your way, from now on sin no more.” ” It is right for you, you bishops, to set your trust and hope in God, our Lord and our Savior, Jesus Christ the Savior of your souls.

38 Draw nigh to Him in meekness and in mercy, aS peacemakers without strife and anger: convert the sinful, and lift up the fallen; and do not render evil for evil. Moreover, do not be railers, nor passionate, nor blasphemers, nor boastful, nor accepters of persons, nor drinkers of much wine, nor drunkards, nor singers,‘ nor dancers, nor thieves, nor murderers, nor takers of bribes.

39 Neither seek. You honor and riches from strange gods, but keep far from them, for you have been called to this work, that you should serve God..

40 Thus let the life of the bishop be. (Let)

41 His clothing and his food (be) in moderation and purity, as much as is sufficient for him, that he transgress not in any other way or matter. For he that serves is worthy of his food, even the tithes and first fruits which men bring to the Church, as is commanded by our Lord.

42 The bishop may give what they need to the poor, and the needy, and the widows, and the orphans, and to the destitute, and to the Strangers, and to them that have nothing. But if the bishop exercise no care for them, God will require it of him on their behalf, for (the bishop) has authority to appoint for every man, as is meet.

43 And you also that are set in authority in the Church, if you have need, take from its store, but if you are not impoverished, it is not right that you should take from it. And if you act thus, you shall be saved before God.

44 But if the bishops take for themselves alone, and be insatiable in eating, and lay up store for themselves, and give not to the destitute, God will require this of them, and they shall be clothed with shame and dishonour.

45 Therefore it is said, “You have drunk the milk, and clothed you with the wool, and have killed the bulls; you have not lifted up that which had fallen, nor helped that which was lost.” “ Will you live alone upon the earth? ’’ Therefore has He commanded you in the law, saying, “Love your neighbor, as yourselves.” And moreover it is said, “You shall not muzzle the mouth of the ox when you tread out your corn.” And seek you not to take aught but that which is according to your service and your toil, as is right.

46 For the threshing-floor is the Holy Church of God. And do you also eat in it, as the Levites, who served in the tabernacle of the testimony, which is a type of the Holy Church. It is meet, then, that the priests eat the offerings with their wives, and their sons, and their daughters, from the offering that has been

47 Should you also save the people, even as Christ saved you. Think not that the burden is light which presbyters and ” bishops bear. As they have borne a

48 Heavy burden, so they shall receive the first-fruits, and

49 Give” of it to the destitute.” For One will call

50 You to account, with whom none can” dispute or contend.

51 It is right, then, that they who serve the Holy Church should have their needs supplied, and receive the offerings as priests, and Levites, and ministers; even as it is written in the Book of Numbers concerning the priests: “And the Lord said to Aaron, Behold, I have given to you to keep the first-fruits which they offer to me of all that is sanctified by the children of Israel; to you I have given it; [even all the first-fruits of corn that are in all their land and all that they offer to the Lord; to you have I given it all] until your old age,’ and after you to your children as an ordinance for ever.

52 And this shall be yours of that which is sanctified to the holy place, of the sacrifices, both of every oblation, and of all their sacrifices, and of their trespass-offerings, and of all their sin-offerings; whatsoever they bring to me of all their holy things, to you shall it be and to your children.

53 In the most holy place eat of it; let every male eat of it, you and your sons; for it is holy to you. And let this be yours of their first gifts, and of all that the children of Israel offer; to you have I given it, and to your sons and your daughters with you as an ordinance for ever. Let everyone that is clean in your house eat of it.

54 And all the first-fruits of oil, and all the first-fruits of wine and wheat, even their first-fruits, and all that they

55 Offer to the Lord, to you I have given it. All the first-fruits of corn that are in all their land, and all that they offer to the Lord, to you have I given it all. Let him that is clean in your house eat of it.

56 All that they dedicate among the children of Israel is yours, and everything that opens the womb of his mother of all flesh, everything that they bring to the Lord from man even to beast is yours. And those that are redeemed with a ransom—the first born of men, and the first born of beasts that are unclean, you shall redeem.

57 And for a ransom for each one that is a month old, the price shall be fifty shekels according to the

58 ’ shekel of the sanctuary, (that) is twenty obols. But

59 The first born of cows and the first born of sheep and the first born of goats, you shall not redeem, for they are holy; and you will pour out their blood at the altar, and their fat you will make a sacrifice to the Lord for a sweet fragrance. And its flesh shall be for you; even as the breast which they offer, and as the right shoulder, it shall be yours.

60 Every offering which is holy, even all that the children of Israel separate to the Lord, I have given it to your sons and your daughters with you as an ordinance for ever; and it is a perpetual decree for ever before the Lord to you and to your seed after you.”
