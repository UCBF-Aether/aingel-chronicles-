Didascalia

Chapter 23

THAT SERVANTS OUGHT TO BE SUBJECT IN ALL PURITY TO THEIR MASTERS WHETHER THEY BE UNGODLY OR FAITHFUL.

1 What say you then concerning servants? Let them be prudent and serve their masters in the fear of God. And if a servant be a Christian and his master an unbeliever, yet ought he to serve him in everything; but let him have no fellowship with him in faith. And in like manner let masters love their servants.

2 And if a man’s master be a believer, let him serve him, and fear him as God, and count him as a father and a good master. And for this he shall receive a good reward for the service with which he has served his master.

3 And if there is a master who has a servant, a believer, and he does his will, let him love him as his son, and count him as his brother for the love of the faith.

4 Humble yourselves and obey kings and” princes who fear God, for they are God’s ministers. Fear them then who are set in authority, as is fitting, and offer to them from that which you have with honor and obedience, for so has God commanded. Make for yourselves no excuses (), but rather love one another, even as the Lord God has commanded by His Son, our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.
