6 And if they teach not, and turn not the people to true godliness, they shall receive condemnation.

7 Now let us receive with joy and love the repentance of those that turn to God; and against those that have sinned let us give judgment with compassion and mercy, as is fitting, towards the penitent.’ But if you see a man who commits sin, and falls into error, lift him up again from his fall; but if you make his way of life pleasant to him, behold, then you have killed your brother.

8 But it is right that you should lift up again from his fall him that has fallen, that he perish not; and that others, when they see him, may learn, and understand, and receive the word of admonition.

9 It is not right for the bishop to be negligent as touching the sin of the people, nor, again, to disregard the desire of those who seek for repentance, lest by his slackness the flock of the Lord perish.

10 For concerning them God spoke by the mouth of Jeremiah the prophet, saying, “ Many shepherds have destroyed my vineyard, and defiled my portion, and have made my portion, which I love, a desert and a waste; and it has become a desolation and a ruin: for my sake all the land has perished and is destroyed.”

13 For the Savior prayed for sinners to His Father, even as it is written in the Gospel, “Father forgive them, for they do what they do not know.” And then let them examine him that has sinned, whether he forsakes his evil way, and when he confesses his sins, and turns to repentance, let them allow him to enter the Church, and bid him to fast according to the greatness of the sin he has committed, two weeks, or three weeks, or five weeks, or seven.

14 It is right that you should teach the sinner, and instruct him, an admonish him, without anger or favor, that he may humble himself, and pray to God, and find compassion and mercy when he stands before Him." For it is said, “If you, O Lord, should mark sin, O Lord, who should stand before you? For forgiveness is from you,” and mercy is in your hand. Thus, then, do, - make a difference.

15 For God said to Cain, "You have sinned: be silent then,” and lift not up your han - But it is right for the sinner to grieve and sorrow, and despise himself. Let us understand the word which God spoke to Moses concerning Miriam, “If her father had spit in her face, should she not have been ashamed seven days?

16 Let her remove outside the camp for seven days and then let her enter in. And Miriam removed outside the camp for seven days, and the people journeyed not until Miriam was purified. And after this they journeyed.” Let us do, then, thus to those who desire repentance for their sins. Let us appoint

17 Them a fixed time of years, according to the greatness of

18 Their sin, in which they shall repent: and when they repent, let us receive them, even as fathers show mercy towards their children.

19 But if bishops, and others in authority,

20 Have on them any defilement or stain, and on account of them the people are put to shame; and (if) they judge not uprightly for the orphan, and the poor, and the widow, but show favor in judgment, that they may get gifts and bribes, such shall be reckoned with the hypocrites. Even as it is written in the Gospel saying, “Why do you behold the mote that is in your neighbor’s eye, and see not the beam that is in your own eye? ’’ Let bishops and Priests then give heed that there is not found with them deeds of this sort, lest others be offended, when they see these evil doings.

21 For the wicked seek occasions to destroy others and to scatter the Lord’s flock. For a little leaven leavens much meal. In like manner sin follows after those that commit it, (and) thus destroys very many.

22 As when a thief is found in a treasure-house and brings an evil name on his kinsfolk by the evil of his doings; and as when the stinking flesh of a corpse is cast into ointment, the odour of the fragrance of it is tainted; and as when a king is evil and cruel (and) dispenses not justice, so, too, are his Princes; and as when a scabby sheep feeds with (other) sheep, they all become scabby, if it is not separated from them; and as, when a mad dog bites all whom it meets, except it be killed, all those whom it bites become mad together with it; in like manner if any man be a scorner, and a mocker, and contentious and a transgressor of the commandments which are in the law, let him be put forth from the Church, lest he corrupt the house of the Lord, and make (it) a den of

23 Robbers. It is not right that we should keep silent concerning sinners and wicked men, but we should admonish them, that they may forsake their doings, and put from them wicked deeds and habits. On the other hand, let us bid them fast and pray and give alms, that they may learn the fear of God.

24 For He said to the children of Israel, ‘Fear me, and keep my commandments.” The bishop ought to show anxious care concerning the sin of the people, and consider the life of each one of them, that he may be to them a guide and a savior ” to works of righteousness, that they may be prepared for God, and be saved from the condemnation which shall fall on sinners at the day of judgment.

25 But let such as are slothful hear what is said, “‘ Why have you kept silent concerning transgressors and gathered their fruits? ”

26 ‘It is right that the bishop should feed the people in peace; those that have not sinned let him not allow them to fall: and those also that have sinned let him turn to repentance. Hear, O bishops, what the

27 Lord says to you, ‘Despise not one of these little ones.” But they ought to forgive and pardon him that has sinned. And when a sinner confesses his sin, and cries to the Lord from the depth of his heart, humbling himself with tears and sighs, the Holy Spirit will come and say to him, The Lord pardon‘! You all your sin.

28 Know, then, O bishop, how much honor and glory God has bestowed upon you, and has given you authority to loose and to bind.. Take heed to yourself, and guard the flock of Christ. Walk in the way of righteousness, while you live in this world, for you will give account in the day of judgment. “For to whom much has been committed, of him

29 And. P he.

30 Who spare not, nor save the flocks, but follow the good shepherds who spare and save their flocks.

31 For the people love a good shepherd, and fear him as a father, and a lord, and as a priest, and as God’s high-priest, for he admonishes them, and guides them to true godliness. For he that obeys him, has obeyed Christ, and heard His voice, but he that rejects him and hears not his voice, has rejected and transgressed against Christ,’ as he that obeys Christ, has obeyed God.

32 Moreover it is said, ‘He that hears you, has heard me, and he that rejects you, has rejected me, and he that rejects me, has rejected him that sent me.” In like manner it is right for the bishop to love the people as his children, for they are his sons; and to nurture them with admonition as a bird which broods over her eggs, and nurtures her young ones until they fly.

33 In like manner let him teach the people the word of God, and feed them with spiritual doctrine, and give what they need to all who desire to receive of him; and let him not be burdensome to them, but admonish them with words of wisdom, and put them in fear, lest they turn again to sin.

34 Rather let him bid them walk in the way of righteousness, and keep the faith; and let him feed the people in peace. Moreover let him raise the fallen, and strengthen the weak, and gather together the scattered, and heal the wounded in heart, and console the faint-hearted, and strengthen the lame with the word of admonition; and let him lighten the heavy burden of their sin, that they may get them a good hope.

35 And as for them, let them learn the law of the Church, and turn to repentance. And if there is any man who is cast out through not understanding the

36 Words of the Scriptures, despise him not, nor allow him to fall into sin, but help him, and heal him from the wound of sin, and turn him to you, and restore him to the Christian flock.

37 But whoever refuses to turn again, he shall go forth from your flock, and be made food for the birds of the heaven and the beasts of the field; and his portion shall be with them that say, Let us eat and drink: to-morrow we die; and that think not on their latter end.

38 And if there is any man whom the bishop has seen doing thus, he ought to say to him, I will bear your sin, and die in your stead, even as Christ died for the sin of the world. Then let him toil and suffer with him, until he turn again to repentance.

39 For “the good shepherd gives up his life for the sheep; but the hireling who is not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf coming, and flees and leaves his sheep, and the wolf catches them and scatters them.

40 Because he is a hireling, he cares not for the sheep.” It is right that we should know that God is merciful, and desires not the death of a sinner, but his conversion and return to repentance. And if there is anyone that sins through understanding not the commandments which God has ordained

41 For repentance, but walks in the deceit of his or and remembers not his sin, and his evil which he hat

42 Done, remembering not the words of the Holy Scriptures which have proclaimed mercy to him, such

43 A one shall perish and be without hope. But search you out a good shepherd, and a meek and patient teacher

44 And monitor, and one that Is merciful and a peace maker, who goes about and. Feeds his sheep seeks that which is lost, like the Lord our God For our Father who is in heaven sent His belove

45 Son our Savior the Good Shepherd, our Teacher Jesus Christ, who left the ninety and nine in the wilderness and went to seek one sheep that was lost. And when

46 He found it He bore on His shoulder,’! And brought it to the flock with joy, because He had found that which was lost. Hear this, O bishop, guide them that have gone astray, and gather together them that have been scattered, and seek them that are lost, for you have authority to turn (to repentance), that you may bind and loose.

47 For our Savior” said to the man with the palsy, “I have forgiven you your sin; your faith has saved you; go in peace.” ’ For the peace which we have received is the Church of Christ, into which you should bring them that have repented of their sins, and are cleansed,’ and have done righteousness and goodness, and found the hope of life.

48 The bishop ought to heal the sick who are diseased through deeds of sin; to visit them and to console them and to heal their wounds, for “not the whole, but the sick are they that need the physician.

49 For the Son of man came to seek and to help that which was lost.” Since, then, you are a physician in the Church of the Lord, impart healing to each one as it befits, and feed them with spiritual food, that they may be strong in the Holy Church.

50 And feed the flock of Christ in humility and in love, and mild severity" without anger, not exalting yourself in your position of authority, for you are the shepherd of the sheep of Christ’s flock; and you will gather the lambs in your bosom.

51 Be, then,’ merciful and just, without guile, or revenge, or jealousy, or slandering, or calumny, or hatred, or partiality, or evil speaking, or haughtiness, or boasting. Despise not them ’ that are under you, nor hide from them the commandments of God, nor turn yourself away from their repentance, nor bring pretexts against them, nor drive them away from the Church.

52 Be of understanding, then, in judgment, and receive not the witness of one man against any, but only the witness

53 Of three good and faithful men,'! To whom others bear witness that they are good and faithful, who cherish not hatred, nor revenge, nor jealousy. For there are some that speak falsely, that have two tongues, or three, who rejoice in evil-doing, haters of the brothers, who seek to scatter the flock of Christ.

54 But if you hear their voice, and believe those that do thus, behold you have scattered your flock without judgment, and given them over to evil and ravening wolves, that is (to) evil men. Or rather, they are not men, but evil beasts who are in the likeness of men, ungodly and unbelieving and apostates, who do not know God.

55 Such are liers in wait in the way, and like a wolf that seizes and devours, they seize them that depart from the law of the Church; and when they see one that is lost, it seems a great thing for themselves. For their father, the devil, is a murderer.

56 And those that go astray ’ from the Church, and depart from the law, demons shall be their shepherds, and shall afflict them with torment and distress and sorrow of heart. And they shall follow the way of transgressors, and shall lose their reward, and shall depart farther and farther from God.

57 And as for the bishop, if he does not judge uprightly, his portion shall be with them.’ You ought not, then, to thrust away the sinner, nor to despise him that turns; neither be lacking in mercy, that you may heal the wound of sin, nor have you fellowship with the man of blood.

58 For concerning them the Scripture says, ‘Their feet are swift to shed blood; shame and affliction are in their way, and they do not know the way of peace; and there is no fear of God before their eyes.” The way of peace is our Savior Jesus Christ, who has taught us, saying, “Forgive, that he may forgive you; and give, (and) men shall give to you;” that is to say, Forgive men their trespasses, that your heavenly Father

59 Faithful. Gotten them great gain. Yoe

60 May forgive you your trespasses. Moreover He has taught us in the Prayer, saying,!

61 “Forgive us our trespasses and our iniquity as we also forgive him that has trespassed against us.” But if you cannot forgive the trespasses of him that has trespassed against you, how shall you find forgiveness for your trespasses And if there is anyone that has been thrust forth being innocent, and then returning has asked for pardon, and (the bishop) receive him not, he is a murderer of his brother, and a shedder of blood like Cain, who shed the blood of Abel his brother; and his blood cried to God.

62 And if any bishop thrust forth (a man) being innocent, he is, like Cain, a murderer.
