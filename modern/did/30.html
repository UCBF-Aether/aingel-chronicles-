Didascalia

Chapter 30

THAT WE CHRISTIANS OUGHT TO UNDERSTAND TO OBSERVE THE PASCHAL SEASON AND NOT KEEP IT IN ERROR ON ANY OTHER DAYS, BUT IN THE WEEK IN WHICH THE PASSOVER COMES ON THE FOURTEENTH DAY (OF THE

1 MONTH).!

2 You, beloved brothers, who have been bought with the precious blood of Christ, ought to keep the feast of the holy Passover with all exactness and understanding. For day and night are equal on the twenty-fifth? (day) of the month Magabit.? Make your reckoning sure, for our Lord did not suffer twice in one year. Do you also take heed to keep the memorial of His sufferings once only every year.

3 For our sakes He died, and (therefore) consent not to

4 Keep the feast along with the Jews, for there is no love between us and them. [or they in folly have erred in their reckoning, though they think they are exact. Therefore they are dishonoured in all the earth, and have departed from the truth.

5 But do you take heed to the reckoning of the day on the twenty-fifth (day) of Magabit, which is the twenty-second (day) of the month Magabit according to the reckoning of the Hebrews, and observe this reckoning until the twenty-first (day). And further make no mistake in the reckoning of the Passover, lest it be celebrated before the (festival of) Hosanna, that the Passover may not fall twice in one year through inaccurate reckoning.

6 But let the holy day of the resurrection of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ be celebrated on the holy Christian Sabbath.

7 And further let the fast of the holy Passover be from the second day of the week until the first Sabbath,‘ the vigil of the Passover. And in the week of the Passion cat nothing except bread, and water and salt. Abstain on these days of the Fast from eating flesh and drinking wine, for they are days of mourning and sorrow.

8 Above all in these six days, if any one be able to fast, let him patiently endure for two days; but if he is not able, let him fast on one day, and keep himself from evil-doing. For our Lord says, “The sons of the bridegroom cannot fast so long as the bridegroom is with them, but days will come when the bridegroom shall be taken from them; then shall they fast.’

9 For in these six days the Jews took our Lord from us, and crucified Him on the Cross, and reckoned Him with the transgressors. Therefore we command you to fast, even as we ourselves have done since He ascended into heaven. And as for the other days, let them fast on the fourth and sixth days of the week until the ninth hour, and whoever wishes let him fast till the sun set.

10 And keep vigil at night until the cock crow, being: gathered together in the church for

11 Said, “Thus do you in remembrance of me.” Finish the fast, then, with joy and gladness, and keep the feast, for our Lord Jesus Christ has risen from the dead, and His resurrection has given us hope. Let this ordinance f' be to you a law for ever until He come again in glory.

12 For the Jews have not believed in the resurrection of our Savior, but we Christians believe in His resurrection, for all who hope in His holy resurrection shall find life eternal.

13 Keep, then, a feast on the eighth day, for on it the Lord appeared. Thomas said, To me also He appeared, and made me glad, (showing me) the marks in His hands and His feet, and the wound in His holy side. Though before was faithless, and believed not in His resurrection, He showed me openly the wound in His right side.

14 On the fortieth day keep a feast, for on it He finished all (His) dispensation and ascended into heaven to the Father who had raised Him (from the dead), and He sat down on the right hand of power, and subjected His enemies under the footstool of His feet; and He shall come again in glory and great power to judge the living and the dead, and He shall render to everyone according to his works.

15 And behold the Jews who crucified Him shall see the beloved Son of God.

16 For even until now the Jews are gathered together on the tenth (day) of the ninth month, reading the Lamentations of Jeremiah the prophet (where it is said) “The spirit of our life, Jesus Christ they have taken from us, and have afflicted him.” And moreover they read what Baruch wrote, “This is our God; there is none other that is like to him.

17 He found every way of life, and gave it to Jacob his servant and to Israel his beloved. And after this he walked upon the earth.” And as they read this, they weep and mourn.

18 And thus they do, making it seem a mourning for the desolation which Nebuchadnezzar worked, and pretending it is the truth.

19 And ten days after He ascended (was) the fulfilment of Pentecost, for on that day at the third hour Jesus Christ sent to us the Paraclete, and filled us with His power, and we spoke with new tongues, (declaring) that Jesus Christ is the Messiah of God, who has authority to judge the living and the dead.

20 And concerning Him Moses bore witness, and said, ‘‘ The Lord our God took fire, and caused it to come down as rain.” And in like manner Jacob saw Him in the likeness of a man, and said, “I have seen the Lord our God face to face, and my life is preserved.” And Abraham also received Him as a stranger and believed that He was God.

21 This is the chief of the hosts of the armies of God whom Joshua the son of Nun saw; and moreover as a strong man in armor He helped him by Jericho, and then Joshua did reverence to Him as a servant does to his lord. And again, this is He of whom Samuel the prophet knew that He was Christ the Anointed of God. And further priests and kings called Him the Anointed.

22 And again, He it was whom David knew, and spoke with fear (concerning) the glory of the Beloved. ‘My heart has uttered a good word and I have made known my work to the king; my tongue is as the pen of a scribe that writes speedily. His beauty is fairer than the sons of men, grace has been poured forth from his lips; therefore God has blessed you for ever.

23 Gird you with your sword upon your thigh, O mighty one, in your beauty and your comeliness. Advance, prosper and reign, because of righteousness, and uprightness, and meekness, and the glory of your right hand shall lead you. Your arrows are sharp, O mighty one—the peoples shall fall under you—in the heart of the king’s enemies. Your throne,

24 O Lord, is for ever and ever, a sceptre of righteousness is the sceptre of your Kingdom. You have loved righteousness and hated iniquity; therefore the Lord your God has anointed (you) with the oil of gladness above your fellows.” And concerning Him Solomon says, ‘“‘The Lord created me in the beginning of his way for his works, before the world be founded me, in the beginning before he made the earth, and before he

25 Issued forth, and before he set in order the mountains; before all the hills he begat me.” And moreover Isaiah the prophet says, “A rod shall come forth from the root of Jesse, and a flower shall ascend from its stock, and shall be set as a ruler of the nations.” And again, Solomon says, “Wisdom has built herself a house.” And moreover, Zechariah says, “Rejoice greatly, daughter of Zion, and publish (it), daughter of Jerusalem; behold your King comes; he is righteous and brings salvation, and is meek, and rides on a donkey, and on the foal of a donkey.”

26 And moreover, Daniel says, ‘‘The Son of man came to the ancient of days. He then is the stone (that) came down from the mountain, (cut out) without hands.".

27 We also declare (and) proclaim to you that He is the Word of God that was before the world, and moreover is equal with Him in everything. Believe in Him that you may find life eternal; but if you believe not in Him, you shall find condemnation. He who believes not on the Son shall not see life eternal, and the wrath of God abides on him.

28 And after the feast of Pentecost keep a feast for seven days, and then fast; for we ought to rejoice and be glad because He has granted us that we should receive the gift of the Holy Spirit. Moses and Elijah fasted for forty days. And Daniel also fasted for three weeks, and ate no pleasant food, nor did flesh and wine enter into his mouth. And the blessed Hannah, when she

29 Made supplication to God to grant her a son [Samuel], said, “I have not drunk wine to be drunken, but my heart was strong in the Lord.” And the men of Nineveh fasted for three days and three nights. Esther, and Mordecai, and Judith were saved by fasting from the wicked Holofernes and from Haman.

30 And David also says, ‘My knees are weak through fasting, and my flesh is dried for want of oil.” Do you also

31 Fast and ask of Him pardon of sin. And fast besides.

32 On the fourth and sixth days of the week, giving alms to the poor; and honor the Sabbath being gathered together in the church with joy and gladness; and on the other Sabbaths fast not, but only on one day, the vigil of the Passover.

33 And if there is anyone who fasts on the Christian Sabbath which is (the day of) His holy resurrection, or at Pentecost, (that is) until the feast of Pentecost —they who do thus, and mourn on the feasts of the Lord, have no remission of sin.

34 Ne you keep on them a spiritual feast, and never appear sad.
