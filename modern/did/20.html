Didascalia

Chapter 20

THAT UNMARRIED WOMEN AND WIDOWS OUGHT TO RECEIVE WITH THANKSGIVING THAT WHICH IS BESTOWED UPON THEM

1 Ler us exhort widows and unmarried women to receive that which God has bestowed on them with thanksgiving in fear and_ trembling, and to give thanks to God who gives food to the hungry. Which of you are there that eats and drinks in moderation? Is it not God who gives to him?

2 He who stretches out His hand and multiplies good things according to His will; who gives corn‘ to the young men, wine to the maidens, and the oil of rejoicing to the living; abundance to the cattle, and liberty to servants, and flesh to the beasts, and fruits to the birds, and food

3 To all, as it befits. Therefore our Lord says, “ Be like to the birds of heaven which sow not, nor reap, nor gather into barns, and your heavenly Father feeds them: you are far better than many birds. Think not, saying, What shall we eat or, What shall we drink or, Wherewithal shall we be clothed?

4 For your Father who is in heaven knows that you seek all these things.””’ But if you desire to receive His good things, and to inherit His kingdom, offer to Him praise and thanksgiving and blessing. And to those that receive the widow and the orphan God the Father shall give gladness in the Kingdom of heaven, through His Son our Lord Jesus Christ, to whom be honor and glory for ever and ever. Amen.
