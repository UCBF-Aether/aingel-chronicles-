Didascalia

Chapter 4

OF BISHOPS, PRESBYTERS, AND DEACONS,

1 OF bishops we have heard thus from our Lord Jesus Christ, that the shepherd of the sheep who is ordained bishop of all the Churches must necessarily be blameless, pure and good,” who thinks not on the care of this world, who is full fifty years old, who has

2 Children.

3 Passed the strength of youth, who is not a slanderer, nor one that speaks lies amongst the brothers.

4 For the Gospel says, “For every word that a man speaks thoughtlessly, he shall give account in the day of judgment, that it may condemn him.’” And _ furthermore it is said, “For by your words you will be justified, and by your words you will be condemned.” Therefore it is meet that he abound in all good things. But if he is not learned, let him be prudent and intelligent, of middle age.

5 But if the city be a small one, and there is not found in it one (thus) fitted for the office of bishop and there is found among the young men one prudent and wise, though his years are (but) few, and if those who know him bear witness that he is worthy to be ordained bishop, if he has lived from his youth perfect in the works of the holy and humble fathers,’ and walks in the way of righteousness, tried in every work, and if (men) bear witness to him that he is perfect, let him be ordained in peace.

6 For Solomon also when but twelve years old became King over the people of Israel; and Joash too became ruler when he was seven years old and afterwards in the eighth year he reigned in righteousness.

7 For the Lord spoke by the mouth of Isaiah the prophet, and said, ‘‘To whom shall I look, but to him that is meek and humble and quiet, who trembles at my word" and again it is said in the Gospel, “Blessed are the meek, for they shall inherit the earth.” Let him therefore be merciful, for it is said, ‘Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy.” Let him therefore be a peacemaker, for it is said, ‘Blessed are the peacemakers; they shall be called the children of God.” Let him be pure of

8 Heart, without revenge and evil and unrighteousness, for it is said, “ Blessed are the pure in heart; they shall see God.”

9 Let him, then, be spiritual, and watchful to do good, neither insatiable nor passionate, nor a drunkard, nor vindictive, without hatred and strife, not haughty, nor a lover of money, nor a reviler; neither let him exalt himself, lest he fall into the snare of Satan; for everyone that has exalted himself shall be abased, and he that humbles himself shall be raised to honor.

10 And let such a one be ordained bishop who has married one elect wife, who is able to order his household aright, a teacher and monitor who has brought up his children in purity. And let his wife also be faithful, who trains her children in the true worship and in the fear of the Lord.

11 It is right that the men of his household should obey the bishop in humility and in fear, and let him be ’ master even as it is written.

12 And let them try him that he be blameless, not passionate, (for) thus says wisdom, “Anger destroys the wise man.” And let him be merciful and charitable, for our Lord says, ‘And by this shall all know that you are my disciples, if you love one another.”

13 Let him, then, be a lover of men, who receives strangers, who accepts not persons, a man of understanding, who shows favor neither to rich, nor to poor, one who has a craft,” whose hands are open to give, who receives widows, a lover of the poor and the orphans and widows. And moreover let him support those (widows) who have children.

14 But if there is a slothful man, or one who has spent all his substance in drunkenness and sexual immorality, and desires to receive alms, let not the bishop show any care for him, nor permit him to enter the church. For

15 The Scripture says to those who act thus, “ How long will you lie, you sluggard and when will you awake from' sleep? A while you sleep, and a while you sit; a while you slumber, and a while you fold your hand to your breast; and then as an evil messenger comes poverty upon you, and want as a swift) runner.”

16 It is not right for the bishop to judge, accepting the person of the rich, and to neglect the poor. For God said to Moses, “ Judge not, accepting the person of the rich, nor turn aside the judgment of the poor, for the judgment is the Lord’s.” But he ought to judge uprightly.

17 The food of the bishop ought to be food seasoned with moderation, that he may be able to teach and admonish the people, and that he may turn them to God.

18 And let him keep far from all evil, and read the Scriptures at all times and be a lover of wisdom, that he may be able to interpret the Scriptures in the Holy Spirit, (even) the words of the Gospel, the Law and the prophets.

19 The Lord’ says, ‘‘Understand you the words of the Scriptures, for they bear witness of me.” It becomes a bishop to search out and expound the Scriptures in order, and to satisfy the people with spiritual teaching, and fill them with the light of the law of God with much admonition. God says, “Make the light of wisdom to shine upon you, for it is time.”

20 ‘Thus, then, it is right for the bishop to suffer and toil and wrestle for righteousness’ sake, but above all before the people, that they, when they see his works, may receive his admonition. And, moreover, let him become surety for no man, nor associate with the great, nor be of a double tongue, nor follow after princes, but let him fear God; and let him not hearken to a false accusation against his

21 Neighbor.

22 And let him not be slothful in fasting, nor seek occasions for eating, nor say, To-day is a feast, as the Gentiles do, to eat and drink and be wanton: but let him remove from him every idle desire which has no profit, for all this is despised and hateful before God, and well-pleasing to demons.,It is meet for the bishop to command and admonish the people to remove from them all these works.

23 But let him teach them and turn them, that they may imitate him and follow (him) in righteousness. For God said to the children of Israel, “Observe my law and my commandment.” And Jesus Christ Himself also, our Lord and our God, first did all, and (then) taught us to imitate Him.

24 Therefore He says that he who shall teach and do, shall be great in the Kingdom of heaven." It is right for you, O bishops, to teach the people and save them from error, even as Christ has given you grace.

25 For the Lord spoke by the mouth of Ezekiel the prophet, and said, “Son of man, have made you a watchman to the house of Israel, and you will hear the word from my mouth, and admonish them by my word.

26 When I say to the sinner, You will surely die, and he has not repented, and you have not spoken to him, nor admonished him, that he may repent and turn from his (evil) way and live, that sinner shall die in his sin, and his blood will I require from you, says the Lord.

27 But if you have admonished the sinner, and he has not turned from his sin, he shall die in his guilt and you will save your soul.

28 And if the righteous man has turned from his righteousness and committed sin, while I show him punishment before his face, and you have not admonished him,‘! He shall surely die in his guilt, and his righteousness which he has done I will not remember, and his blood will I require from your hand. And if you have admonished the righteous man, so that

29 He sin not, that righteous man shall surely live, and you also have saved your soul.” ‘As when war and strife arise in the midst of a people, and they seek out a watchman to bring them tidings, that they may be on guard, and if he tell them not beforehand, that they may be saved from defeat, their blood shall be upon his head, for they heard not the sound of the trumpet.

30 But if he sees the sword which comes on the land, and blow with the trumpet a signal to the hosts, and (if) one who hearkens hear the sound of the trumpet and does not get away, and the sword come and find him, his blood shall return on his own head, for he heard the sound of the trumpet, and did not get away, and his blood shall be poured out on him; but he who got away saved his life.” In like manner the bishop, if he teaches the word of life, shall find life eternal.

31 The sword is eternal condemnation, and the sound of the trumpet is the Holy Gospel, and the watchman is the bishop of the Holy Church. The bishop ought to feed (the flock), and teach, and hold fast the word,’ and make men to fear concerning the judgment.

32 Therefore let him admonish those that walk without knowledge, and make them to see plainly; and let him teach those that do not know, and confirm those that know; and let him gather those that are lost and speak to them at all times concerning their salvation, that, when they hear these things, they may be admonished and do (that which is) good.

33 For God says by the prophet, ‘‘Testify against this people, and tell them their sins (to see) if they will turn, and repent, and be saved from their iniquity.” And Moses also said to the people, ‘“‘Hear, O Israel, the Lord our God is one.” And again, it is said in the Holy Gospel, ‘‘He that has ears to hear, let him hear.”

34 And Solomon also says, ‘ Hear, my son, the instruction of your father and disregard not the commandment of your mother.” Yet they have not heard to this day, but have forsaken the Lord the only true God, and He has called them a generation of transgressors and adulterers, concerning whom we have spoken before.

35 And now know, beloved, you who have been baptized into the death of our Lord Jesus Christ, that it is not meet that they should sin who have received such grace as this. Know, brothers, that such as these who, after they have received baptism, turn back to deeds of sin, shall go down to the condemnation of Gehenna.

36 And (there are) others also who have not been believers, but (who) afterwards when they have believed and ’ been baptized become blessed before God, even as our Lord says in the Holy Gospel, ‘Blessed are you when they revile you and mock you, and speak all (manner of) evil against you falsely for my sake.

37 Rejoice, for great is your reward in heaven.” And if there is anyone against whom they have spoken, blessed is he. For it is written, saying, “Every man who is not tried, is not elect before God.” If there is any man who has transgressed the law, and men curse him, he has brought the curse on his own head.

38 In like manner we also, if we hear not the commandments of God and do not His will, shall bring a curse upon ourselves, and moreover shall be far from God. For as for such as do not what they themselves teach, and turn not from their own ways, the bishop ought to admonish them, and put to shame those who do thus.

39 But it is right that he teach them in the love of Christ how they ought to serve..

40 It is right for the bishop to seek after

41 Righteousness, and not honor transgressors, nor be partial in judgment, nor take a bribe from any man, for a bribe blinds the eyes of the wise, nor turn aside a right cause. And moreover it is said somewhere, “Put away all evil from you.” And Solomon also says, “‘Come not into the place where they encamp; withdraw from them, and turn away. For they sleep not, unless they do evil.

42 Sleep is taken away from them, and they sleep not who feed on sin.”

43 And should order their manner of living, as is fitting, in peace; that the people may lead good lives and turn from their sins. This then is well-pleasing in God’s sight, for when a man turns from his sin and returns to repentance, others seeing him are taught the true service.

44 Therefore ought the bishop to be watchful to do good, that the beauty of his life may be known to all; for he is the guide of the people, and the shepherd of the elders’ and rulers and teachers, and of all who are under him.

45 The bishop ought to sit in the church with the priests, and they ought to judge each man, as is fitting; for to them has the Lord given authority, and has commanded them, saying, ““Whatsoever you have bound on earth, let it be bound in heaven, and whatsoever you have loosed on earth, let it be loosed in heaven.”

46 It is right for the bishop to turn the people to the knowledge of God, and to receive the repentance of those who have turned to God, (and) to teach them, and console them in meekness and in love and_ in humility, and with joy of heart; ’ for the Lord God is merciful.

47 For He says by Ezekiel the prophet, “Say to them,” as I live; says the Lord God, I desire not that the sinner should die,” but that he should repent, and forsake his evil way: and why will you die, house of Israel?

48 And you, son of man, say to the children of your people, the righteousness of the righteous shall not save him, when he commits iniquity, and the sin of the sinner shall not afflict him, if he repent of his iniquity: the righteous man also shall not be saved, when he has sinned.

49 When I speak to the righteous, if he trust in his righteousness and commit sin, all his righteousness I will not remember to him: in his sin which he has committed, he shall

50 Die.

51 And if T say to the sinner, You will surely die, and he repent of his sin and do justice and righteousness, and return the pledge of his debt and restore what he has robbed, and walk in the ordinances of life, and commit not sin, he shall surely live, and shall not die in his sin that he has sinned, neither will I remember to him his sin and his iniquity; for he has done justice and righteousness, and by it he shall live.” Therefore sinners shall hope; when they turn from their sins and repent, they shall find a good hope; and they shall inherit the earth.

52 But let them that have not transgressed take heed lest they fall into affliction and sorrow and weeping. Know, then, and understand, O man, if you transgress and commit sin in this world, and repent not, you have not life. Repent, then, while you live, for after death there is no repentance.

53 Even as David says, ‘‘For there is none in death who remembers you, and in Sheol who will confess to you” It is right, then, brothers, that we should be ready to do good, and should walk in the way of righteousness, in which there is no sorrow.

54 Let us hear, then, and understand that which is said, ‘ Direct my way and my going according to your word.” And let us not be wanting in any good work, but observe the holy word which the Holy Gospel says concerning the five wise virgins, and not (be) as the five foolish virgins, whose oil of good works failed, and they went forth from the marriage which is in heaven.

55 Therefore we ought to take heed to ourselves and fashion ourselves, and mark the time of the bridegroom, lest we fall into sin; but he that does righteousness let him take heed to do good even to the end. It is meet,

56 Then, that the bishop be of understanding and judge uprightly, for the judgment is the Lord’s. Let him admonish sinners, that they may walk in the right way; and, when they turn, let him receive their repentance in compassion and in mercy. Remember, then, what our Lord says, “There is great joy in heaven over one sinner that repents.”’

57 But if you receive not them that repent, but reject them, you have scattered your flock, and have not understood what David says, “Deliver not to the wild beasts the soul that confesses to you.” For thus says the Lord, “That which has fallen, shall it not arise and that which has erred, shall it not turn again?

58 Why has my people transgressed with an evil transgression, and held fast to their own counsel, and refused to leave it? Hearken now and hear me. Say they not thus? Is there not a man that repents of his evil, and says, What have I done?

59 Turn again, O my children that are far away, and I will heal your wounds.” Receive them that turn to you and repent, O bishop, and do not be of a double heart, nor make proud your heart as those who have no mercy. For he who does thus fears not God: but let him rather take thought to fulfill the needs of the people, as it is fitting.

60 Let us take heed, then, to ourselves, and have no fellowship with sinners and wicked men who do thus. The righteousness of the righteous (shall be) for himself, but he that commits sin without law, his iniquity shall return on his own head.

61 For it is said by Ezekiel the prophet, “If a land sin against me, and go astray, and transgress, and I lift up my hands against it, and break the strength of bread, and send famine against it, and destroy from off it man and beast, if these three men, Noah, Daniel, and Job, were in it, they should not save (it) by their righteousness, says

62 The Lord God. And if I send evil beasts against and upon that land, and punish it, and destroy it, and there is none left to go about (in it) because of those beasts, even if these three men were in it, as I live,

63 Says the Lord God, they shall not save their sons, nor

64 Their daughters,” but they alone shall be saved, and the land shall be destroyed.” As it is written, “If

65 ‘there is in this world a righteous man and a sinner, the

66 Righteous shall not perish with the sinner.” But He sends rain on the good!"' and the evil. And if there is anyone who thinks and says, Judgment is equal against all, it is not so; '" for in this warfare there is one that is strong, and again, there is another who is stronger than he: and he that has conquered shall receive the crown of victory openly.

67 For God condemns not the righteous with the sinner. Noah was not swallowed up in the waters of the flood: and Lot was not burnt in the fire: and Rahab the harlot perished not.

68 But if you desire to know what happened amongst us: Judas Iscariot perished, though he was together with us in one ministry; and Simon the sorcerer received the seal of the Lord; (yet) because these two were evil, their wickedness was made known openly. Judas hanged himself, and Simon flew up (into the air) and was dashed upon the ground.

69 And while Noah was in the ark with his sons, Ham by his wickedness brought a curse upon his children. And by this we understand that the fathers are not punished for the sin of their children, nor children for the sin of their fathers.

70 And this thing is quite manifest, that wives are not condemned for their husbands, nor young men for their wives, nor servants for their masters, nor masters for their servants, nor kinsmen for their kinsmen, nor friends for their friends, nor sinners for the righteous, nor the righteous for sinners, but to each one is rendered according to his deeds. For Noah was

71 Not recompensed for the sin of the people, and Lot was not consumed in the fire of Sodom, nor Rahab for the sake of Jericho, nor the children of Israel for the sake of the Egyptians. We ought not to follow
