THAT WE OUGHT TO REST ON THE SABBATH DAYS AND HEARKEN TO THE WORDS OF THE HOLY SCRIPTURES THAT WEMAY DO HIS WILL, AND REMEMBER THE SUFFERINGS WHICH GOD THE WORD ENDURED FOR OUR SAKES.?

1 O Lord Almighty, who created the world by

2 Jesus Christ our Savior, and appointed the Sabbath, and rested on it from all Your work, and have commanded us to rest (on it) from all the work of our hands, and to be ready to serve and do Your commandments; and have given us feast days (for) the joy of our souls; and that we should remember the skill of Your wisdom, that God the word was pleased to be born of a woman for our sakes, and appeared manifestly in this world (); and was baptized as man, though He was God, and endured patiently the sufferings of the Cross, died and rose from the dead in great might.

3 Therefore we keep as a feast the holy day of His resurrection, and honor the first day of the week, and g

4 Ive glory, and sing praise to Him who conquered death and gave light to the whole world, and has bestowed on us incorruptible honor and glory, and has gathered the scattered people, and made them to be one pure flock, and has blessed them, even as He blessed Israel.

5 (It was) You, O Lord, who brought our fathers from the land of Egypt, and delivered them from bitter bondage, and saved them from the sea, and from the labour of the brick-kiln, and brought them forth from the power of Pharaoh and his hosts; and led them through the sea on dry ground, and sustained them with all good things in the

6 Wilderness, and appoint for them the law of the ten words which you did utter with Your voice, and wrote with Your hands, and commanded them to rest on the Sabbath day, that they might give You humble thanks, and be safe from all evil; and bound them with words of admonition, and gather them and bring them into the house of Your sanctuary.

7 Therefore He has commanded us to rest on every Sabbath day, because on the Sabbath day our Lord rested from all His work. Let us, then, seek after all His law, and give thanks to Him, and bless God for all the good things that He has bestowed on us, the sons of men.

8 And greater than all these is (the day of) His holy resurrection which our Lord and Savior and Creator, God the Word, has taught us (to observe), He who was begotten before the world was created, the Son of the Father, and (was born) of the Virgin Mary without human father: He suffered and was crucified and died in the days of Pontius Pilate; and rose from the dead on the day of the Christian Sabbath.

9 Remember, O Lord, that which we offer to You on this day, the sacrifice, and the oblation, and the eucharist of Your holy peace,‘ this great portion which You have given to us, that we may offer () that which is good to the greatness of Your power.

10 And You have fulfilled the prophecy, and have shown mercy to Zion, and have had compassion on Jerusalem, and have exalted the throne of David your servant amongst us by the birth of Christ, who was born of a virgin of his seed according to the flesh.

11 Do You, O Lord, receive the prayer and supplication of Your people who call upon You in truth, even as you did receive the sacrifices of the righteous each in its own time: first of Abel, whose sacrifice you did respect and receive; of Noah when he was going forth from the Ark; of Abraham when he was going forth from the land of the Chaldees; of Isaac when he was going forth from the Well of the Oath; of Jacob in

12 To Your holy name.

13 Bethlehem; and of Moses in the mount; and of Aaron in the wilderness; and of Joshua the son of Nun in Gilgal; and of Gideon on the rock; and of Manoah and his wife in the plain; and of Samson when he was thirsty before he transgressed; and of Jephthah in war; and of Barak and Deborah over Sisera; and of Samuel in Mizpeh; and of David in the field of Ornan the Jebusite; and of Solomon in Gibeon and in Jerusalem; and of Elijah on Mount Carmel; and of Elisha at the fountains; and of Jehoshaphat in battle; and of Hezekiah in his sickness and concerning Sennacherib; and of Manasseh in the mount of the Chaldees after he had transgressed; and of Josiah in Pasa; and of Ezra when he was returning; and of Daniel in the den of lions; and of Jonah in the belly of the whale; and of the Three Children in the furnace of fire; and of Hannah in the tabernacle of testimony by the ark; and of Nehemiah when he was building the wall with Zerubbabel; and of Mattathias and his sons on the hilltop (); and of Joel in the blessing; now also in like manner receive the prayer and supplication of those who offer to You with knowledge through Jesus Christ.: We give thanks to You for all things.

14 You do not take from us Your compassion and Your mercy, for You are merciful and full of compassion in all generations, Who received Enosh, and Enoch, and Noah, (and helped) in the days of Moses and Joshua, (and) the Judges, and in the days of Samuel and Elijah and the other prophets, and in the days

15 And Mordecai, and in the days of Judith, and in the days of Judas Maccabaeus and his brothers: receive us also in like manner in our days through Your Son Jesus Christ the Great High-priest, who has saved us from the sword and famine, and has fed us and satisfied us, and healed us from (our) sickness, and protected us from evil tongues.

16 To You we give thanks at all times through Jesus Christ, who has bestowed on us a sweet and articulate voice, and a tongue fair as the sound of an instrument, and has made us to discern between sweet and bitter, (and given us) eyes to see, and ears to hear, and noses to smell, and hands to handle with, and feet to walk; and these You have created from a tiny drop that is gathered in the womb; and you did bestow (on man with it) a soul that shall not die, and make him a rational being.

17 And you did instruct him by Your law and adorn him by Your commandments; and after a while you made him mortal and granted him a resurrection. How then shall man repay You for all this that You have done for him? Now also we must necessarily give You thanks and glorify You according to the measure of our power.

18 Receive (us) that (our service) may be acceptable before You; and save us from the apostasy of the wicked whose gods are many, and deliver us from those who contend against Christ, and free us from the error of Satan, for you did send Christ Your beloved Son to become man, though He is God, and caused the Paraclete to dwell in us, and set the angels to bless us, and put Satan to shame.

19 Since You have created us and granted us life, preserve us, and give us sustenance, and turn us to repentance; for You are worthy of glory and worship for all that You have done for us through Your Son our Savior Jesus Christ for ever and ever. Amen. Follow after this at all times, brothers; the Lord be with you on earth and in heaven and in the Kingdom of His

20 Father, who sent Him to free us from deeds of sin, and the service of Satan, and (who) has given life to the sons of men, even to those that believe in the Lord God, the Creator, that those who believe in Christ may ive.

21 What thanks is it fitting that we should offer to God the Father through Jesus Christ? Behold we have told you before, and have taught you. Observe all this that you may find life eternal. Amen.
