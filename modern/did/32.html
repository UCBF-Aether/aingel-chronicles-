Chapter 32

HOW SATAN INTRODUCED DIVISIONS AND ENMITIES INTO THE CHURCH EVEN AS HE DID OF OLD IN THE CASE OF THE PEOPLE OF ISRAEL

1 Behold, then, see and understand the craft and deeds of Satan, the enemy of all good, how he caused divisions and unbelief and ungodliness in the midst of the people.

2 The first division which he caused was with Simon in the city called Gitthae Now this man was a sorcerer, and by his own impure craft, he clothed himself with Satan as with a garment, and served him as a slave, performing all the evil of his will.

3 And Philip the Apostle our brother worked mighty works and great signs, and healed the sick in the city of Samaria by the grace of the Lord and the doctrine of the Holy Spirit. And when the men of Samaria saw all his deeds, they became strong in their faith, and believed in God, and were baptized into the name of Jesus Christ.

4 And when Simon the sorcerer saw the signs and wonders which were not done by magic art, amazement seized him, and immediately he believed and was baptized, and after that he fasted and prayed. And when we heard this, we gave thanks to God for that the men of Samaria had believed by the preaching of Philip.

5 And then we arose and came to them, and taught them carefully the word of admonition; and on all those who had received baptism we laid hands; and bestowed on them by prayer the gift of the Holy Spirit.

6 And when Simon saw that when we laid on our hands the Holy Spirit descended on those who believed, he came to us, bringing bribes (and) saying, Give me also this authority, that when I lay on my hands the Holy Spirit may descend on them.

7 For Satan desired to seduce those who desired to believe, even as he seduced Adam—and he ate of the tree, and (God) put him forth from the garden and brought on him the judgment of death. And in like manner he laid a snare for us that we might take bribes.

8 And if we had taken (them) we should have been deprived of the Holy Spirit, which we had received without price, (and should have had) in exchange the wealth that grows old and perishes. Then we feared and were troubled. And when ft I, Peter, saw this evil deed, I said to Simon, Your gold and your silver be with you to perdition.

9 Do you think that with gold you may purchase the gift of God? For your heart is not right before God. Turn from the evil of your deeds, and pray to God that He pardon the sin on

10 Which your heart has thought, for I see that you are as bitter gall in doing evil. ‘Then Simon feared exceedingly, and said to us, Pray you for me to the Lord that there come on me nothing of all this that you have said to me.

11 Then we went forth to preach in all the world the word of life, but Satan sent after us false apostles, and he raised up also those that caused the preaching of the Gospel to cease, and that worked lies and vanity.

12 Then they set up one from among them whose name was Cleobius, and they joined him with Simon, and these became disciples of him who was called Dositheus, and they removed him from his place. And moreover they took counsel with others, and made a place, and assembled together in it to do what was full of blasphemy.

13 These were Cerinthus, Marcus, and Menander, and _ Basilides, and Saturnilus And again there are others who say that there are many gods, and others that (there are) three orders; (and others that they are gathered together’ and are at variance among themselves]; and there are some who worship many gods whom they do not know, and some who teach, saying that marriage is not from God; and others who despise and abhor the union of the sexes; and others who commit sexual immorality without shame; and others who deny () the law and their teacher is Nicolas..

14 I, Peter, to whom Simon came in Caesarea, the city of Cornelius, the faithful in Jesus Christ. But Simon took counsel to seduce the blessed and holy children, Zacchaus, who was once a tax collector, and Barnabas, and Nicetas, and Aquila, the brother of Clement, who was ordained bishop in the

15 City of Rome, (and) who was the disciple of Paul the righteous in the doctrine of the holy Gospel. And spoke to him before them thrice by the word of prophecy, and overcame him by the power of the Lord. Then he fled away, and went into Italy.

16 And when he came to the city of Rome, he disturbed the Church, and destroyed the faith of many, and perverted the Gentiles, and turned them to himself; and taught them to use sorcery and (do) the work of accursed demons. And after this he went to the house of their gods at midday, and made an oration to them looking up into the skies, and all the people did reverence to him.

17 Then he was carried up by the demons and flew up into the air. But I, Peter, stood, and prayed, and saw him flying in the air; and he said, I will ascend into heaven, and he promised the people every (kind of) benefit. And they believed (him) and accounted him as a god.

18 But I stretched forth my hands and prayed to God with all my heart to bring him down to earth, and to Break the power of the demons, who were seducing and destroying the people.

19 And again I prayed to God not to kill him, but to break him as a potter’s vessel.’ Then I turned to Simon, and said to him, If you are a man of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, and teach His commandments and His truth, come not down; but if you are

20 A seducer and a destroyer, O Simon—I command you.

21 A loud voice, and said, There is one Lord, the God of Peter, whom he has preached to us in truth, And at once the people forsook Simon, and from that day the ungodliness and divisions amongst the Simonians were increased.

22 Then Satan entered into the hearts of the false prophets who led men astray, and caused them to utter words of blasphemy against God Almighty, (saying) that He is not the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. Moreover, they said that He is not the Creator of the world, but that He is invisible and unknown and unnamed,'' and not begotten.

23 And again, they said that it was not He who had sent down the Law and the prophets; and they believed not in the resurrection of the dead, and (said that) there shall be no retribution at the last day, and that the soul will return to its nature; and they take pleasure in (their) lusts, and turn to evil habits.

24 And there are others who say that there are many gods, and say that there are three orders, and others that there are two orders. And others forbid to marry, and command to drink no wine, and count as impure and abhor marriage and the begetting of children, and the eating of fruits; and they make themselves out as pure, and receive an evil wisdom, and feign to be righteous and faithful.

25 And there are some of them who command (men) not to eat flesh, and say of the beasts [that they have no reason or soul]; moreover they say that whoever injures a beast is as a murderer.

26 And there are some who say that the flesh of a clean swine is not clean, but unclean, (and that) we ought to keep far from every such thing; but that men may eat of whatsoever was clean under the law. And there are some who practice circumcision, and believe that Christ was (but) a holy man, or as: one of the prophets. And there are some who without

27 Shame openly teach lust, and do the pleasure of the flesh, and are completely given over to every deed of uncleanness. Now these are as instruments (whom) the devil has led captive,'’ and children of wrath.
