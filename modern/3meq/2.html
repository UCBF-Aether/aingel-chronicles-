3 Meqabyan

Chapter 2

1 And thus says the prophet, the seer, who says to him: Now rejoice, O sinner, in the hour of light; but now the Lord, the Holy One of Israel, will avenge Himself upon you, because shame never touched your face.

2 Our couriers and my horses are worn out, and therefore I have fled to Bedir.

3 And I — through you — from ʾänəsərət they run who pursue you, and you shall not escape from them, nor from the stroke of the Lord which is upon you.

4 And they said to us: "Gird on the sword; and the drawing of the bow shall not hold me back, nor the därəb of my kwəyan; and I will ʾäkəkoki you; likewise with the därəb of the sword which shall not avenge you," says the Lord, the Holy One of Israel. But there shall come a severe disease into your heart, which is worse than the drawing of the bow, and than the därəb of kwənat, and than the stroke of siḥ — and it shall not ʾäbäq.

5 And a severe disease shall come into your heart, and you shall miss him who would help you, and he shall not be found; for I will lay my stroke upon you, and you shall not escape from my hand, until I destroy your remembrance from the earth.

6 And He said to me: I am the Lord your Creator. When I do this thing swiftly, like the twinkling of an eye — for you have girded your loins and lifted up your head against My city — for you are like stubble before the wind, which the fire devours before My eyes, and like dust which the winds take up and scatter from the face of the earth; so you are in My hand.

7 For I will lay my stroke upon you, and your Creator shall have no mercy upon you; and I will not spare anything that is in you, and none shall remain that hides itself in all your mountains.

8 And now repent from all the evil which you have done; and if you have repented and humbled yourself before the Lord with sighing and weeping, and have asked of Him with a pure heart — the Lord will forgive your sin in everything in which you have sinned before Him.

9 And at that time Meqabis wept before the Lord because of his sin, and clothed himself with dust; for the Lord had mercy upon him.

10 And I understood that God will not delay the evil which He spoke by the mouth of His prophet; for His eyes are watchful and do not sleep, and His ears are open and are not deaf. And I heard the word which He spoke, and He will do it at once, like the twinkling of an eye.

11 And he tore off his garment, and put on rough sackcloth, and cast dust upon his head, and wept, and cried aloud before the Lord his God, because of the evil which he had done.
