3 Meqabyan

Chapter 6

1 And the proud Ṣiruṣaydan appointed — to the anger of the Lord — lying priests and ministers of idols.

2 And they sacrifice to them, and bring [offerings] forth to them.

3 And it seems to him that they eat and drink.

4 And he gives them, to those who ṣäbəḥ , flocks and cattle and ḥäragit and ʾäṭäli ; and they sacrifice śärkä , and he declares; and he eats of that defiled sacrifice.

5 And not of their own will did they do this to him; but läʾäbəʾəni they were given and made, that they might eat [of the sacrifices] to their gods.

6 And when he saw the children of Meqabis, that they are righteous and worship the Lord their Creator, he desired them; [and] the ministers of the idols yasəḥət them, that they might eat of the sacrifices and eat from that defiled sacrifice. But these blessed ones refused them.

7 And he asked them why they refused; for they keep their father's commandment, and are steadfast in good deeds, and fear the Lord exceedingly.

8 And when he was wroth against them, and was stern against them, and afflicted them.

9 And they told Ṣiruṣaydan the king that he refuses to worship and to sacrifice to his gods.

10 And because of this the king was moved with pity, and grieved, and commanded that they bring them; and they brought them, and he set them before him, and said to them: "Eat you, and worship you my gods."

11 And they spoke again and said to him: "As for us, we will not obey you in this matter, and we will not sacrifice to your defiled gods."

12 And he threatened them with many tortures, and did not prevail against them; for their hearts were steadfast to the Lord.

13 And he kindled the fire, and cast them in; and they delivered up their souls to the Lord.

14 And He showed him their life after their death — while they held their swords, məlu ḥätä — while he slept upon his royal bed by night; and great fear [came upon him].

15 And he said to them: "What shall I do for you? My ʾägaʿəzt — bəqwəʿuni — and I will not lift it for myself, that I may do all that you commanded me; and I will fear Him exceedingly."

16 And they told him all that is fitting, saying: "Remember that the Lord your Creator is He; and there is One who shall shatter you from this your pride, and bring you down into Gehenna below, with Diabolos your father. And there you shall fulfill all your judgment, according to what you burned us with fire — while there was none that had sinned against you — and while we worship the Lord our Creator, and worship Him in fear of the Lord."

17 For He is the Creator of all, who made heaven and earth and sea, and all that is in them.

18 The sun and the moon and the stars, and all creatures which God created lälihu .

19 And there is no other god besides Him, in heaven above nor in earth beneath, who is able for all; and there is none like to Him, who kills and makes alive, who rebuks and forgives.

20 And there is none that escapes from His hand; for He is the mälak of heaven and earth.

21 And there is none that transgresses His commandment [who escapes?] from Him who created him, save you and your məʿlay and those like you — mäməʿləyan who have blinded their hearts — they, and Satan your father; and you and your gods shall go down together into Gehenna, from where there is no escape, forever.

22 For it was not you by yourself who did this, but Satan your teacher, who taught you this evil deed, that you might be put to shame before us.

23 And you have not known the Lord your Creator, and you have lifted up your head as [though you were] the Lord your Creator.

24 And you have boasted in the work of your hands and in your idols, until God humble you, and bring you down to the mäfəḥt of Gehenna; and He will avenge Himself upon you for all the iniquity and sin which you have done upon the earth.
