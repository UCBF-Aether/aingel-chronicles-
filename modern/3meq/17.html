3 Meqabyan

Chapter 17

1 And the grain of wheat: if it does not do dy, it does not do profit and bears no fruit; but if it dies, the grain of wheat makes root in the earth, and brings forth a shoot, and becomes an ear, and bears fruit; and täʾaməru .

2 For you, that there may be many grains — one grain of wheat.

3 And likewise this grain profits from wind and water and earth; and the sun fire. For that which is without the sun cannot bear fruit

4 And the wind həyənt nädəs ; and that which is without wind cannot bear fruit And the water which yaśäqəya and waters the earth.

5 And when it is watered, dust and earth, it makes root and yətəliḥal upward, and bears fruit by the ʾaməṭan of the blessing of God.

6 For the grain of wheat is in the likeness of Adam, upon whom was breathed the spirit of God and the vine. Likewise it yəśäqəy with water, and makes root, and lifts water the cord of fine root.

7 And it ascends toward heaven, in the kätämä of the shoot for the dew of mercy which is from God yaṭəbəw them in the kätämä * ʾaḥərug näwaḫatäwəyəbäsəl by the flame of the sun; and it brings forth fruit which is from God.*

8 And it makes a sweet fragrance which makes glad the heart; and in it, when they eat it, it satisfies as water; and bread — it shall not hunger nor thirst. And when yäʿaṣərəwa , it makes dämä ʾaskal .

9 And when they drink it, it makes glad the heart of man, as it says in the Psalm: "Wine makes glad the heart of man." And with śəmur it intoxicates when yabäqəʿ his mouth and he yəwəḫaṭ and yəmələʿ in his sänəbəʿ ; and his blood yətəkäʿaw in his heart.

10 And it yəresəy the one who is without heart; for the drunkenness of wine causes to err exceedingly. And it yəresəy him to ṣädəf and to gʷäṣagʷäṣ as the broad earth; and he does not do know ʿəqəfət , nor the śok that is in his hands and his feet.

11 And thus God made with the vine and its fruit, that His name may be yətəʾakʷat — for those who do His will, and for those who believe the resurrection of the dead.

12 And He shall make them rejoice in the dwelling of life — for those who believe the resurrection of the dead.
