3 Meqabyan

Chapter 16

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 According to his work, and according to the cleanness of his hands.

2 And from where comes it that you say: "These ṣəfər of your hands and your feet, and the ṣägur of your head"? Did not God make them, that they should yəṣaʿ — that you may know the resurrection of the dead which shall not be in another flesh — that you may know for yourself that you shall rise from the dead.

3 And you shall be judged according to your deeds which you have done — evil and sin — at the resurrection of the dead; because you have caused the people to err, while you say: "There is no resurrection of the dead."

4 And now you shall see, when the day of your requital comes to you; for what you have sown shall not ʾäʿabi that it should profit — from where grain and from where root.

5 And again, you who have planted a tree — it shall not ʾäʿabu From where the vine, and from where the fig tree? Its shoot and its fruit shall not be changed.

6 And if you have planted a vine, it shall not be changed to become a fig tree; and if you have planted a fig, it shall not be changed to become a vine; and if you have sown a root, it shall not be changed to become sägäm .

7 And if you have sown sägäm , it shall not be changed to become a root. All these — in his seed and in his bäzämädu and in his fruit and in his tree and in his bäqʷäṣəl and in his bämərəw — he shall receive blessing by the dew of mercy which is from God, and shall bring forth fruit.

8 Likewise the earth shall bring forth that which God has sown; it shall profit soul and flesh — the doers of good, while they are not changed into doers of evil; and the doers of evil, while he is not changed into a doer of good. Those whom God has sown shall profit in soul and flesh.

9 And when the time comes for the blowing of the horn, the dead shall rise by the dew of mercy which is from God — who did good — in the resurrection of life; and their ʿəśet — Paradise of joy — a dwelling for the pure, which He has prepared for the chosen, where there is no disease and no sickness, where they shall not die again.

10 But those who did evil — in the resurrection of judgment — with the devil who ʾaləḥat them.

11 And his evil angels who do not desire to be saved, from all the servants of the living — in the judgment which is forever.

12 Underneath, beneath, in the ṣənaf of darkness, where is weeping and ḥaqəyäsənän , where there is no forgiveness and no mercy, and where there is no escape forever and ever — because they did not good in their life upon earth, while they were in their flesh.

13 And therefore they shall be judged at the resurrection of soul and flesh.

14 For lelomu — for those who do not believe the resurrection of soul and flesh — with you thus God shall show great is His täʾaməri .

15 And all shall be requited
