3 Meqabyan

Chapter 18

1 From what error do you err, O you who do not believe the resurrection of the dead? And when they lead you where you do not know at this time tənəśəḥu where it shall not profit you; and when they cast you into judgment, because you did not believe the resurrection of the dead, in flesh and soul ḫəburä .

2 And you shall be requited according to your deeds, whether good or whether evil; for you have ʾaḥəsäm-ed the heart of your kaləʾan , while you say: "We believe that the dead shall not rise — who became ashes and earth."

3 And therefore their neighbors err; for there is no saḥət for their death, and they cannot məqəś * fətomu for they shall stand before the judge of God.*

4 And they shall be təḫaḏfəru-ed exceedingly, when He yəgeśəṣ them in His wrath, and He shall yətəmäʿaʿ them for all the evil which they did. For while they did not know the ways of God, they speak for ləlihomu they did not know from where they came.

5 And they did not know where they go, into the judgment of the burning fire. For according to the ṭəwəyät of their heart they teach their neighbors; for bitter are they, and gʷäṣagʷäṣ in their deeds, and ṣäwagan and those who teach ṭəway * nägär , while they say: "There is no resurrection of the dead."*

6 And this ʾamirä — they knew that the dead shall rise; and they shall be judged, because they did not believe the resurrection of the dead — for all the creatures, the servants of the living.

7 For we are all the fruit of Adam, and through Adam we die; for for us all, through the sin of our father Adam, there found us the judgment of death from God.

8 And again, as we shall be judged according to what we have done, we shall rise with him, Adam our father; for through our father Adam's not knowing, täqänəy entered the world to death.

9 And therefore we are judged by the transgression of Adam; and our flesh is consumed in the grave, and our bones are täqäṭəqäṭu .

10 And the earth sätäyətät our ʾanəgʷaʿ and our masän and our laḥəy is consumed in ashes; and our flesh is tädäfənä in the earth, and our good qal is tädäfənä in the earth.

11 And there went forth ʿəd from our ʾayənt shining and our masän in the mäkab ; and it became dust.

12 Where is the beauty of their laḥəy for the good wärazut whom Adam qomomu and śaʿəśaʿ the taste of their words? Where is their greatness for the ṣənəʿan ?

13 And where is their strength for kings, and their honor for rulers? And they were täräsəyo with horses, and täśärəgəwo with gold and silver, and täräśəyo with the goods of the field which yanəṣäbärəq .

14 Where is the taste of mäbaləʿət in sweet wine?
