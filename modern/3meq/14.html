3 Meqabyan

Chapter 14

1 As for me, it grievs me exceedingly, and it yatekəz me and yəqäləy my heart, concerning the Judahites and Samaritans and Pharisees and Sadducees, who do not believe the resurrection of the dead. The Judahites say: "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die; and thereafter we shall see none."

2 And the Samaritans say: "Our flesh shall not rise, for it shall become dust."

3 But if there be a resurrection — we believe in the resurrection of our souls; for the soul, when the flesh dies, does not do dy. For it is as the wind, which is not seen, and as the sound of thunder; and none can speak it nor show it.

4 But our flesh is seen by all, and becomes ashes and dust; for the beasts devour it, and the ʿəṣeyat devour it in the grave.

5 And as for those who devoured it, they become ashes, and no ʿaśər of them is found; for they become as grass, and as though they had never been created, they become ashes.

6 And by this are found the bodies which bäləyu And thus say the Pharisees: "We believe in the resurrection of the dead; but He will clothe the souls in the heavens with another flesh than that which was on earth."

7 And thus say the Sadducees: "When our soul goes forth from our flesh, we shall not rise from the dead; and they have no resurrection of the dead — neither for soul nor for flesh. And when we die, we shall not rise."

8 And for this they err exceedingly, and it yatikəz me concerning them; for they speak ṣərəfät against God and His praise.

9 And they have no hope of salvation, for they did not believe in God who would justify them; but they have no hope of salvation and of the resurrection of the dead.

10 And O blind Judahite, you have tasätäʿabəyinu against God, who brought you from where you were not who sowed you, He ʿabəqulo your soul and your flesh, in His likeness and in His image.

11 And think you not thus; for you shall not escape from His hand. To God you shall rise, how while you do not desire; and you shall be judged while you do not love. For the cord of Sheol is in your kəśad , which seizes you until the day of your death.

12 For in the belly you shall təśärəf for yourself; from the gunəd of Sheol is this cord, which you let down into your kəśad ; and when you tələḥəq , so too it tələḥəq .

13 And when you die, you let it down into your kəśad ; and when the gunəd draws it, you bring them

14 And you draw its ʾaʿəṣuq ; for all the sinners are seized within it, as the cord is in their kəśawəd .

15 And He will bring all the souls of the sinners from where they were, from the ends of heaven; and likewise your cord shall draw you, that you may bring your soul from where you were, and you shall enter into the fäläg of Sheol.

16 And your flesh, täʾatito and wino sevenfold — as the flesh of our father Adam — the dew of His mercy shall raise you, of God; and thereafter you shall be judged according to what you have done evil and sin, according to what you have not believed.

17 And it shall not profit you and you err in your night, who dwell in the grave in your error, and say: "There is no resurrection of the dead" — that they may err from the commandment of God.

18 And who shall yäḫaḏəg you in ashes and earth, that he may raise you, that he may judge you according to your deeds which you have done?

19 And then He shall come — from where your spirit which is in the winds, and from where your dust which is in the earth, and from where your flame which is in the fire.

20 And from where the spirit of life which was breathed upon you, in the ʿaṣäd of darkness.

21 Which täʿaʿəwaq but the souls of the righteous in Paradise with joy are yətəʿaṣəwa .

22 But you, O Judahite and Samaritan and Pharisee and Sadducee — until the time of your judgment, you shall tətə ʿaśər in darkness.

23 And then you shall see how God shall judge you according to your deeds, because you have ʾasə ḥatäkä luboʾo .

24 And you shall say to them: "The dead shall not live; let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die." And you say in your kal , while you sit upon the seat of Moses: "There is no resurrection of the dead."

25 And therefore you have erred, while you know the book of the Law, and teach the word of the books; and you cause men to err from ḫaḏsäyäkä , whereas you have not learned and do not know.

26 The word of the books — you exalt the people of God with the evil of your teaching, and with your word which does not do profit.

27 But you shall be requited according to your deeds, and according to the fruit of your kännafr ; for God is no respecter of persons. And to those who learn good, He will reward them with good things, which He has prepared for those who love Him.

28 But you, you have nowhere to escape from His hand, who shall judge you; and behold, He shall judge you according to your deeds. For you and those whom you have mäharək-ed shall be judged.

29 I have told you that the dead shall rise. Then those who kept His commandment shall rise; and none shall yətəhakäl to stand. For His commandment shall bring them forth — as the earth brings forth grass when the rain yəzäbəṭ .

30 And as the moist tree brings forth its shoot when the rain waters the earth, and as it brings forth fruit — its root or the ear of grain — and it cannot withhold its fruit in the year God has śämər-ed

31 And as conception, when ḥəmam comes, the womb cannot restrain it but when it hears the voice of God, it bears them for the dew which is from God shall come down upon it.

32 And likewise the earth shall bring forth it cannot restrain but when it hears the voice of God, it bears them for the dew which is from God shall come down upon it.

33 And the bodies shall be gathered into their places, where their ʾabədənət fell; and one house of the treasuries of souls shall remain. And the souls shall enter into the bodies, from where they went forth before.

34 And at the blowing of the horn, the dead shall rise in the twinkling of an eye; and they shall stand before God, and He shall requite them according to the work of their hands.

35 Then you shall see that you shall rise from the dead; and you shall be tanəkər-ed in all your deeds which you did upon earth. And when you see them written before your eyes — and this ʾamirä you shall tənäsəḥ — where it shall not profit you.

36 And you shall know that you shall rise from the dead; and you shall be judged according to your deeds which you have done.
