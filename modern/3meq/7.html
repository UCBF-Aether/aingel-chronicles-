3 Meqabyan

Chapter 7

1 "Woe to you, you, and those like you, who have not known Him — the Lord who created you — and have not known His commandment and His word; for you shall be cut off, from which no repentance avails, when you are punished in the straits of Sheol."

2 "And you shall have no escape from it forever — you and your gods, and those who sacrifice and worship to them as [if they were] God your Creator — to those which have neither soul nor breath, and which do not avenge them that ʾäḥsämä them, and show no mercy to them that ʾäśänäyä them."

3 "For the work of man's hand are they, in whom Satan speaks, to destroy the heart of fools like you, O fool! — that he may bring down you and the ministers of your gods, and those demons that command you, into Gehenna of fire."

4 "For you have not known that none profits you; you err and are destroyed."

5 "And better than you shall be the beasts which God created for your food — and the oxen witəni and the dogs käläbatəni — for they have no judgment again, except one death."

6 "But as for you, you shall die, and be judged again in the terror of Gehenna, from where there is no escape forever."

7 And when they had said this, they departed and were hidden from him.

8 And Ṣiruṣaydan was in great fear and trembling, and it did not leave him until morning.

9 And then he went to where he had cast the bodies of the righteous, and did not find them; for he desired to yəqä with them and səʿän them. For God had hidden them, that he should not təḫaśəś from their flesh.
