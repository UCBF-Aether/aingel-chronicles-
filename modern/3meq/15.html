3 Meqabyan

Chapter 15

1 But those who heard in their good deeds — then they shall rejoice. But those who täḥäkäyu , while they say "The dead shall not rise" — then they shall grieve, when they see that they are risen from the dead, with their evil deeds, which do not profit.

2 And their deeds which they did shall judge them, while there is none to yəwäqəs them They knew themselves how they shall be judged.

3 And they shall stand at the station of the wicked, in the day of weeping and of judgment, in the day of däyən , and in the day of God.

4 In the day of darkness, in the day of ṭaqa , in the day of gimi , in the day of ṣäʿəʿ , in the day of lightning.

5 In the day of fear and trembling, in the day of dädək and märəqi , in the day of ʾasäḥatəya .

6 In the day the evil shall be requited according to what he did evil; and in the day the pure shall be requited according to what he did purity; and in the day the ṣəwag shall be requited according to what he did ṣug .

7 And in the day the master shall not be honored above the servant, nor the maid above her mistress.

8 And in the day the king shall not be honored above the poor, nor the ruler above the child, nor the father above his son, nor the mother above her daughter.

9 And in the day the baʿəl shall not be honored above the näday , nor the proud above the humble, nor the great above the small; for it is the day of justice, and the day of requital and of judgment — the day when He shall requite all according to what he did.

10 And the day when the doers of good shall be rewarded; and in the day the evil, who did evil, shall be requited.

11 And the day, when they shall rejoice who were rewarded with good; and the day, when they shall grieve who were rewarded with evil. And they shall see those who ʾaḥäsäyu the books, while they say: "The dead shall not rise."

12 Then shall all the sinners of the earth weep for their sins which they did; for there found them a sorrow which has no comfort, because they did not good upon earth.

13 And likewise for the chosen also who did good, their joy shall not fail forever and ever; for while they were upon earth, they listened to good deeds.

14 And they did not transgress the commandment of their Creator; for they knew that they should rise from the dead.

15 And because they did not transgress His commandment, they shall inherit

16 And in the heavens He has given them to inherit the land of life, which He sware to their fathers, in the day of resurrection, in the day yənädəyu * ʾabəʿəlt .*

17 And they shall yäʾawəyaw — the doers of evil, who do not believe the resurrection of the dead, nor the law of God; and the day of resurrection they did not remember.

18 Then they shall see you shall find them evil, which has no maḫələqət , and which has no healing in it, and no nuzaʾze ; and in their hearts a sorrow which has no comfort, and no rest.

19 And ʿəṣe which does not do dy, and fire which is not quenched.

20 And upon their flesh: fire and täy , and a whirlwind, and ḥasätəya , and hail, and dädək which yəzonəm upon them.

21 Mäqaṣəḥ of fire for those who do not believe the resurrection of the dead.
