3 Meqabyan

Chapter 13

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And as for them —

2 And they gave their souls to the fire [martyrdom], that they might be saved from the fire of heaven; they knew that this world passes away.

3 And he said to me: "And no delight dwells for zəluf ; for they knew that more grievous is the təʿəyərt of God than the təʿəyərt of the servants of the living, and more grievous is the wrath of God than the wrath of the king."

4 And He reckons one day — they shall rejoice in the ʾaʿəṣədat of Paradise — than a thousand years of life upon earth; and for long days He reckons one hour. Forgive ziʾakä , O my Lord.

5 Their hands [are] good who ḫəburä ; they gave their souls to death. They knew that təśeniyuḫarit is their life, rather than this earthly, passing one.

6 But You, O my Lord, remain forever; and Your years which shall not fail, and Your memorial from generation to generation.

7 And all this, and the like, ḥaläyu the sons of the Meqabyan; and they ʾabdäru-ed and ʾabdäru-ed, trusting in God, and refused the polluted sacrifice of liḥa .

8 And therefore they gave their flesh as wax for God's sake. They knew that the dead shall rise, and they knew that there shall be judgment after the resurrection.

9 But those who do not know and do not believe the resurrection of the dead — they knew

10 And He showed them that all passes away; and they gave their souls to death, that from God they might find məzəgana — because they believed in Him, and did not bow to idols, and did not eat the sacrifice of the dead and of pollution.

11 And therefore they knew that He will make them rejoice in the latter day, in soul and in flesh. For while they were with wife and children, they did not know the taste of this world, nor the bitterness of death; and they gave their souls to death. They knew that there shall be a resurrection in the latter day, in soul and in flesh.

12 And they knew that they shall reign — who kept the ordinances of God — in the world to come, for generation to generation, and for long days, where there is no sickness and no sorrow.

13 There, there is no sickness and no sorrow, and no death; and with those who believed in the word of God — the chosen kings and rulers — and gave their souls to death, like wax in the midst of fire, while they beheld with the mind of their heart that which is to come.

14 And they shall shine, zäyəbərəh their faces, sevenfold more than the sun; and that they may rejoice in His love at the resurrection of all, in soul and in flesh.
