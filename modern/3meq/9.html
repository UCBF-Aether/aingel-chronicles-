3 Meqabyan

Chapter 9

1 And you, O man — and God is not [with you?] — why do you exalt yourself, you who today are a man, and tomorrow shall be earth and ashes, and shall become wood and boards in your grave?

2 And the [depths?] of Sheol shall be given to you, and to those who do your works; for your teacher Säbəlyanos has revealed, who brought upon his head the sin of all the world, because he caused our father Adam to err through his pride.

3 For he who refused to bow down to the work of his God, in the pride of his head and in the loftiness of his kəśad .

4 And you, like your teacher, have refused to bow to the Lord your God.

5 [Kingdoms.]

6 And vengeance was taken upon them, and they went down into Sheol, because of the evil of their deeds which they did upon the earth.

7 And you, like them, shall go down into Gehenna.

8 You imagine to snatch from His hand — from God! — for mäʿato and häk and tähäk : the worship of God, which gave you dominion over [all?].

9 And He proved you, whether you would do His will or not; and if you have made your way straight upon the earth, God shall prosper your paths, and all that your hand has gotten He shall prosper, and shall bless you; and all your adversaries and your enemies He shall destroy for you.

10 And you shall rejoice in your ṣ́aʿat and in your bäʿat , and in the fruit of your belly, and in your storehouses, and in your pastures; and wherever you put forth your fingers, and all that He has put in your heart shall be commanded to you — for from God it was commanded to you. Thus shall you do, and build, and plant, and tənəśət **

11 But if you have refused to hear His word and His law of God, and have not walked in His judgment, you have nowhere to snatch from His hand — from God! — like all the rebellious who were before you, who did not worship God in purity, and did not trust in His righteous judgment; for righteousness is all His judgment.

12 And there is none that hides himself from before His eyes; and all is naked and manifest before Him.

13 Now His is the dominion of kings, and He is the overturner of the thrones of the mighty.

14 And He is the exalter of the humble, and the raiser of the fallen.

15 And He is the looser of the prisoners, and the raiser of the dead; and those whose flesh is worn out and become as dust, He shall awaken them at the time He wills — for He is an overshadowing of mercy from Him.

16 But those who did evil, He shall raise them — a däyən resurrection — for they despised [God], and He shall requite them.

17 And upon the earth He shall yamasən their seed; for the rebellious are they, against the law and the ordinance of the Lord God.

18 And let not sinners plot in their counsel against the righteous; for strait is their path for the righteous, [far] from the ways of sinners.

19 And as heaven is high above the earth, so far are the ways of the righteous from the ways of sinners.

20 But as for the ways of sinners: the doing of ṣug and təʿəgələt and iniquity and fornication and deceit and səsəʿət ; and going after strange property, and drunkenness in iniquity.

21 And räwiṣ in the käʿəwät of blood, and ʾamasən in that which is unclean; and səʾanəbəʿa the children of death; and eating carrion and blood, and eating the flesh of ḥarawəyan ; and the camel and the ḥawir in a woman ḥaräsä , and təkət which is not cleansed.

22 And all these are the deeds of sinners: broad is the way, and wide is the net of Satan, which leads into ḫaʾgʷäl and into eternal judgment.

23 But as for the ways of the righteous: narrow and mäqəʿan , which lead to life — humility, and yähawət , love and peace, fasting and prayer, and purity of flesh, and abstinence from unclean foods: carrion and bətk and fornication, and ḥawir

24 And they abstain from every foul deed, and from foul foods which are not [permitted?] in the law, and from foul deeds which God does not do love — for all these do the sinners.

25 But the righteous are set apart from all evil ways, which God does not do love.

26 And He loves them, and has mercy upon them, and shall deliver them from all their tribulations.

27 But as for sinners, Satan shall qänəy them; for they have transgressed His law and His ordinance, and [all] that He loves.
