3 Meqabyan

Chapter 19

1 O earth, that have gathered kings and rulers, and the great and the honored, and beautiful women, and fair virgins.

2 And strong hands, in whom was beauty, and fair ʾaqʷayaṣ ; and in whom was knowledge and ləbuna ; and in whom was a fair voice, like the voice of ʾarəganon näbabit , and like the zəbəṭät of the harp and ʿənəzira .

3 And in whom was fair song, who make rejoice as the drinking of wine, O son of Adam their eyes [were] shining like the morning stars.

4 And their yəman was strong as he who yəhub and he yənäśəʾ and yäḥayəṣ and their feet [were] fair to behold; and for those who yərəwəṣa like mäkʷərakʷər * rətuʿat .*

5 I know you, O Death, who have taken their souls — of the fair hands; for by the will of the Lord God you are täfänäwəkä .

6 But you, O earth — for you have gathered many bodies, whom God brought forth from you, and He shall bring them in to you. From you we went forth, and to you we shall enter. By the will of God we täfäśaḥənä ; by the will of God, upon you.

7 And you are mändäf for our limbs; we ʾansosäw upon you, and we nom in your wətit . We have eaten your fruit, and you have eaten our flesh.

8 We have drunk from the dew of your näqəʿ , and you have drunk from the näqəʿ of our blood. We have eaten from the ʾanəgʷaʿ of your earth, and you have eaten from the ʾanəgʷaʿ of our bones.

9 We have eaten from the taste of ashes; your dew is fair, as God commanded you, for our sisit . And you have taken the laḥəy of our flesh, and you have räsäyəki-ed dust, as God commanded you, for your sisit .

10 O Death, from you who have gathered many of us — our mighty ones, kings and rulers — and you did not fear their gərəma nor their greatness, as He commanded you who created them; and you ʾimänänəkä the poor.

11 And you ʾeməḥərəkä those whose laḥəy is fair; and you did not spare the mighty and the strong; and you did not spare the ʾabəʿəlt and the nədayyan , the ḫaśuman and the fair ʾaʿərug, and children of ʿədəw, and women.

12 And you did not spare those who think good and do not transgress His commandment; and you did not spare those who think evil — who became as beasts in their deeds — nor those who are perfect in the beauty of their laḥəy , and their words, and the taste of their nägär .

13 And you did not spare those whose words are bitter, and full of märəgäm in their mouths; and you have gathered their souls into your treasuries — whether of light or whether of darkness.

14 And the earth has gathered their flesh — whether in the kʷäkʷəḥ or whether in the earth — until the blowing of the horn, when the dead shall rise.

15 And the doers shall be judged even according to their deeds which they did; for at the blowing of the horn and at the commandment of God the dead shall rise, in the twinkling of an eye; and they shall rejoice who did good.
