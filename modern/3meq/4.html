3 Meqabyan

Chapter 4

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And there was [one] who believed: Hosea.

2 And there was [one] who believed: Gideon.

3 And there was [one] who believed: Samson, and Barak, and Deborah, and Judith — whether among women, whether among the humble — He raises up for them judges, that they might save them from those that oppress them, and that He might save them from the hand of all their oppressors.

4 And according to what He willed, the Lord saved them from those that oppressed them.

5 And they rejoiced greatly in all that He set straight for them: in the sowing of their land, and He multiplied their cattle, and in all pasture that is in the field and in the desert.

6 And He blesses for them their cattle, and their plantings, and all that they hold, whether of the house or of the desert; He blesses for them, and their cattle do not miscarry, for with His eyes He watches over them, and He loves them exceedingly, for they are a generation of the righteous.

7 And when they became evil in their deeds, He cast them into the hand of their oppressors.

8 And when the Prince slays them, they bethink themselves, and return, and cry out to the Lord God their Creator.

9 And when they return with all their heart, He forgives them, and their former iniquity He does not do remember to them; for He remembers that they are flesh and blood, and in them is the anointing of the conscience of this world, and devils.

10 And Meqabis, when he heard this word which God ordained on the mount of His sanctuary, contended in repentance.

11 And he did not turn aside from doing what he had seen; and he heard this word. And because of all the ḫirut which the children of Israel yəgä bəru — for God forgave them; and when God chastises them, if they keep the commandment, they weep and cry out, and again He forgives them, and they do His commandment.

12 And likewise Meqabis made his way straight, and kept His commandment, and walked in the commandment of the Lord God of Israel.

13 The Prince, when he heard, and in all that the children of Israel counsel — likewise let him counsel with the keeper of His commandment, to the Lord.

14 And he established his house and his children, that they might walk in all his way and in His commandments, to the Lord.

15 And the ḥərmät of the Jews which they keep — he too shall keep; and the commandments which the Jews keep, when he hears, likewise he shall do; and the mäbaləʿt which the Jews keep he shall keep, though he is of another people — a Moabite.

16 And the tithe he shall tithe; and the firstborn of his cattle, and of his flock, and of his ʿədug, and all that is clean he shall give; and the sacrifices which the Jews sacrifice, likewise he shall sacrifice, facing the way toward Jerusalem.

17 And [the offering] for firstfruits, and [the offering] for sin, and the freewill [offering], and the sacrifice of salvation, and the sacrifice of zäləf.

18 And the first of the fruits, and the moṣaḥt which the Jews bring forth — likewise he shall bring forth, and give to the Levite whom he has appointed; and all that the Jews do, likewise he shall do. And the lifting of incense.

19 And he made märanat and milonat and ṭäräṗezat and däbatərat and baḥrəyat and ḥoṣadat, and kə bəʿa wəguʿä for the holy maḥətot and mänəṭolaʿt, as they do within the holy of holies of Israel, when they keep the feast to the Lord.

20 For they walked in His law and in His ordinance; and if God had not kept them alive, He would not have given them into the hand of their oppressors. Thus did Meqabis in all his way.

21 And he prays at all times to the Lord God of Israel, that they may be led, and that he may not separate from the congregation of Israel who have chosen and done [His] will.

22 And again he prays that He may give seed in Zion, and ʾäbəyat in Jerusalem; and that He may save them from those which He spoke by the mouth of the prophet; and that his repentance may be accepted in everything in which he has repented.

23 And he wept before the Lord with sighing, [praying] that He would not destroy his seed from the earth, and that He would keep him in his coming in and his going out.

24 And of the peoples of Moab who were under his hand, to Meqabis they listened, that they might believe; for in the right way walk their angels, and they keep judgment, and do [His] will; and they establish judgment in their lands, and their districts are at peace, and they see that he is upright and exceedingly wise, Meqabis.

25 And they come to him, and hear the judgment of his righteousness, and his ḫirut — to Meqabis.

26 And in it are many ṭərit, and ʾägəbərət, and ʾäʿəmat, and ʾäʿədug, and camels; and in it are horses that wear the sword.

27 And after this he worships the Lord; when he walks in ṣäbəʿ, there is none that moʿo except by night that moʿa.

28 But they come with the strength of their gods, that they may ṣäbəʿ him and curse him in the name of their gods; and there is none that yəmäwəʿ him, for he has founded his trust upon the Lord his God.

29 And it came to pass, while he did thus, and while he yəmäwəʿ the oppressor, that he brought the peoples under his hand.

30 And he avenges the oppressed, and justifies the judgment of the children of death.

31 And he receives the meek and the children of death in their affliction; and he satisfies the hungry with his food, and clothes the naked with his garment.

32 And he rejoices in his conduct and in the work of his hands; and he gives of that which he has, unstintingly; and the tithe he gives to the house of God. And while Meqabis did this, he rested well.
