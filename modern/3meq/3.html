3 Meqabyan

Chapter 3

1 And the prophet spoke, for Remat of Moab was near to Syria.

2 And he tore at the ground, and came even to his loins, and wept with bitter weeping, and repented because of the sin which he had done before the Lord.

3 And thus said the Lord to the prophet: Go to the land of Judah; enter; go to the land of Remat, to Meqabis the prince of Moab, and say to him: Thus says the Lord: For it was not of your own hand that you did this — that you should devour by the strength of My might and by My təʿyənət — you have destroyed the holy city; but I am the Lord; My messenger is with you; of Myself, by My will, I sent you, that you should destroy My city.

4 For from My ʿəʿat with all səsəʿət and fornication and deceit.

5 And I took hold and preserved you alive, and set you in your place; and not by yourself — in which you girded your loins — and you said: "I will rodək it, the city, by the strength of my hand." And now, because of your children whom you have begotten, the Lord will forgive your sin.

6 And now repent with all your heart, and be steadfast, and do not be negligent; for the negligent do not attain to repentance.

7 And blessed are those who repent with all their heart, and do not return to affliction and sin, in all their repentance for their evil.

8 And they attain with weeping and sighing, and with much prayer, and with worship to the Lord who zäfä ṭäromu ; for He says to the penitents: You — after you have erred and have repented — ʾəliʿayä , you.

9 And to the proud He says: After he has sinned, when he comes to Him in repentance, because of your contrition and because of your fear, He will forgive your sin. For I am the Lord, a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children to the third and to the fourth generation; but to those who love Me and keep My commandments I do mercy with them, to a thousand generations.

10 And now, because of these your children whom you have begotten, keep My covenant which is with you; and I have accepted the repentance which I have brought about, because of the evil of your deeds, says the Lord, the Holy One of Israel, who kəluyəmäləkə .

11 And at that time he went out from gəb , and worshipped the prophet, saying: "Such and such — may the Lord grant me to inherit, that I not be cut off from you; but grant me what you will. For I have despised God, and have not walked in His commandment, as my fathers [did not]; for we have no law. And moreover you know that we serve idols, as our fathers taught us."

12 And I — I have not heard the word of His prophets of God until now; for I have sinned, who have walked in my sin, and in the pride of my heart, and in the girding of my loins, in which I despised the commandment of God; and I have not walked in His commandment, nor kept that which He commanded me.

13 And the prophet spoke again and said to him: I have understood that now your repentance is accepted; for there is none among you of your kindred who any longer trusts in his sin.

14 And from now on forsake the worship of idols, and return into the counsel of God, that your repentance may be in truth. And he worshipped at the prophet's feet; and the prophet raised him up, and commanded him all that is fitting — good deeds.

15 And he did according to what God commanded him, and entered into his house.

16 And Meqabis set himself aright, and removed from his house all sägäl , and idols, and marəyan , and məṭəʿawəyan , and mäsägəlan .

17 And the children of Ṣewan, whom he had brought from Jerusalem — he examined them śärkä , and declared to them all the commandment of God, and His law, and His ordinance, according to what their fathers had done them.

18 And he set over his house wise men, from the children of Ṣewa.

19 And moreover, from the young among their wise men he appointed [teachers] [supplied] — those who keep — that they might teach them the way of God, according to what the children of Israel did His law and His ordinance and His statutes, and all their abominations which the Moabites yətəḥeräm — let him hear from the children of Ṣewa.

20 And he shattered their abominations, and all their sägälat , and all their idols, and all their mäwaṣəḥt , and their məśəwaʿ , from their səbuḥ , from the ḥäragit of his märaʿəy , and from the mäqwəʿal of ṭäli , which he sacrifices to his gods — śärkä ; and he declared.

21 And he sacrifices to them, and worships them in every deed, continually; and he asks of them, and believes in them, and does their will in all that the ministers of his gods tell him.

22 And he did not turn aside from their words in all that they told him; for it pleased him in all that they had done for him.

23 But as for Meqabis, who was among them — he forsook the way.

24 And when the seer heard the word which the prophet had spoken to him, he set his way straight into repentance; and all his household made their way straight, [learning] from the children of Israel, at that time. For the children of Israel — at one time they show mercy to him, and at another they chastise him; they teach him, and turn him to the Lord.

25 And when He saw that they were humbled under the hands of those that oppressed them, and they cried out before Him — then the Prince teaches them, remembering the covenant of their fathers, when they cry to Him, for the sake of Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, their fathers.

26 And after He had saved them, they forgot Him who had saved them, and returned to the worship of idols.

27 And the Prince sends against them peoples that oppress them; and when they oppress them and afflict them, they cry out to the Lord. And at that time He teaches them, and forgives them; for He loves them, for they are the work of His hands.

28 And again, when He gives them rest, they return to seek favor from it, with the worship of the idols which ʿawədomu ; and the work of their hands was evil.

29 And He raises up against them the peoples of Elofəli, and Moab, and Midian, and Syria, and Egypt; and they weep and cry out when their məʿw oppresses them, and he afflicts them, and subdues them, and takes possession of them. And He raises up for them judges, that the judges might save them.
