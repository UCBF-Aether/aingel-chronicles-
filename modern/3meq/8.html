3 Meqabyan

Chapter 8

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And he went in pride and in təməyənət of heart.

2 For the sun stood in the midst of heaven until he destroyed the armies of the Canaanites and the Hivites and the Hittites and the Perizzites and the Jebusites; and he slew them, and bound their feet in their fetters, and slew them with the edge of the sword, and wəsät in the bäʿat , and heaped stones upon them.

3 And he went about in all ʿəbäd and evil and hukät .

4 And what we told before them: he plows and he nagwʿ and he eats.

5 The kings in one bäʿat — and by his prayer he made the sun stand at Gibeon, that he might destroy their armies; and he slew in one moment.

6 And he did not know God, that He is his Creator — who says: "My time has become as the time of heaven."

7 And he thought in his heart that from him the sun rises.

8 And he rose up bägod and encamped in the land of Zebulon, and took ṣ́äbʿa and Macedonia, and took provisions from Samaria, and gave it to ʾäsäbäʾəməsorəya .

9 And he yäḫdr to the däwäl of Kedar, and extended to Sidon, and did gəbər in Achaia, and raised kəśad to the Sea of Tekeze, and yətmäyṭ , and sent his messengers to the Indian Sea.

10 And thus he raises kəśad to heaven.

11 And his head is not ʾätəḥt but [he is] in all evil and təzəḫrət .

12 And all his ways are in däḥṣ́ and darkness and pride and ʿələwät and qəṭəqaṭe and kəʿiw of blood.

13 And all his fəmot which God hats, he does — according to what Säbəlanəyos taught him, mämə həru for evil and təʿgələt and ṣug ; and he yagʿər the children of death, and does not do pity nädäy .

14 And the kings of the nations he brought under his hand, and slew them.

15 And the angels of the enemies he judged; and many nations he ʾägrär , and did with them as he willed.

16 And he did not spare, and there was none that escaped, from the Sea of Tarshish to the Sea of ʾIyariko .

17 And he worships idols, and eats dead things and blood and bətk , and sacrifices to the gods; and in his heart there is no justice, but in all his ways there is nothing except fətəḥ and mäʿat and qwəṭʿa and häkäk . For he became a mädänəgṣ to the nations under his hand, and did with them as he willed.

18 And there is no fear of God before their eyes; for all that he ʾädämo * yəgä bərə before him, and he walks in təməyənət before God his Creator.*

19 And he regards Him not, that his God, even the Lord, will requite him according to what he did evil against his neighbor, when the day shall seize him with his mäʿat .

20 And He will avenge Himself in His time, according to what He śäräw the former sinners; for He says: "I am the Lord, who avenge Me upon the proud, who do not walk in My commandment; and I will root out their memorial from the earth."

21 And the wicked shall be requited according to what they did evil.

22 But as for the doers of good, goodness shall be given to them from the Lord.

24 Thus shall He find those who despise God by their evil deeds.
