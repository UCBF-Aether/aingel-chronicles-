1 Enoch

Chapter 2

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Observe everything that takes place in the heaven, how they do not change their orbits, [and] the luminaries which are in the heaven, how they all rise and set in order, each in its season, and do not transgress against their appointed order.

2 Behold the earth, and give heed to the things which take place on it from first to last, [how steadfast they are], how [none of the things upon earth] change, [but] all the works of God appear [to you].

3 Behold the summer and the winter, [how the whole earth is filled with water, and clouds and dew and rain lie on it].
