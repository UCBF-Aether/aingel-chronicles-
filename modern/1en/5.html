1 Enoch

Chapter 5

1 Observe [you] how the trees cover themselves with green leaves and bear fruit; therefore give heed [and know] with regard to all [his works], and recognize how he who lives forever has made them so.

2 And [all] his works go on [thus] from year to year [forever], and all the tasks [which] they accomplish for him, and [their tasks] do not change, but as [God] has ordained, so it is done.

3 And behold how the sea and the rivers in like manner accomplish [and do not change] their tasks [from his commandments].

4 But you — you have not been steadfast, nor done the commandments of the Lord, but you have turned away and spoken proud and hard words with your impure mouths against his greatness. Oh, you hard-hearted, you will find no peace.

5 Therefore you will curse your days, and the years of your life will perish, and [the years of your destruction] will be multiplied in eternal cursing, and you will find no mercy.

6a In those days you will make your names an eternal curse to all the righteous,

6b and by you [all] who curse will curse.

6c [And all] the sinners [and godless] will curse by you,

7c and for you, the godless, there will be a curse.

6d [And all the shall rejoice,

6e and there will be forgiveness of sins,

6f and every mercy and peace and forbearance;

6g there will be salvation to them, a good light.

6i And for all of you sinners there will be no salvation,

6j but on you all a curse will abide.]

7a But for the elect there will be light and grace and peace,

7b and they will inherit the earth.

8 And then wisdom will be bestowed on the elect, and they will all live and never again sin, either through ungodliness or through pride; but those who are wise will be humble.

9 And they will not again transgress, nor will they sin all the days of their life, nor will they die of (the divine) anger or wrath, but they will complete the number of the days of their life. And their lives will be increased in peace, and the years of their joy will be multiplied, in eternal gladness and peace, all the days of their life.
