Ruth

Ruth is a short, tender story set in the hard days of the Judges. A widowed Moabite woman, Ruth, chooses to stay with her mother-in-law Naomi, gleans in the fields of Boaz in Bethlehem, and finds welcome, kindness, and a new family. In this edition Ruth is printed from the King James Version of 1611, given here as a public-domain text received just as it has come down to us. It belongs here because, in just 4 chapters, it shows ordinary faithfulness up close and quietly carries the family line that will lead to King David — an outsider welcomed into the heart of Israel's story. Watch for Ruth's promise to Naomi, a vow of loyalty that has been loved and quoted ever since.
