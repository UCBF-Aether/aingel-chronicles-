THE FIRST EPISTLE TO THE THESSALONIANS

1 Thessalonians

First Thessalonians is a letter naming Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy as senders, written to a young church in Thessalonica facing persecution and grief. Paul had been driven from the city quickly, and he writes with relief and affection after Timothy brings good news of their faith. He encourages their love and honest work, comforts them about believers who have died, and teaches that the Lord will return, so they should stay awake, sober, and hopeful. In this edition the text is the King James Version of 1611, printed as received from the public domain. It stands in this canon among the apostolic letters kept by the Ethiopian Church, precious for its tender pastoral tone and its clear teaching on Christ's coming. New readers will find it gentle, direct, and deeply encouraging. Watch for the promise that the dead in Christ will rise first and the living will meet the Lord together.
