116. CONCERNING THE CHARIOT OF ETHIOPIA

1 And the Archbishops answered to Gregory, the Worker of Wonders, "Behold now, you have told us concerning the vanquisher of the enemy of Rômê, and now [tell us] of the chariot of Ethiopia and whether it shall remain henceforward, to the Coming of Christ, as you have told us concerning Zion, and concerning the faith of the people of Ethiopia, and likewise if their chariot shall remain." And Gregory said to them, "It shall assuredly not disappear. And again, hearken you to me and I will declare this to you: A few Jews shall lift up their heads against our faith in Nâgrân and in Armenia in the days after this, and this God will do by His Will so that He may destroy them, for Armenia is a territory of Rômê and Nâgrân is a territory of Ethiopia."
