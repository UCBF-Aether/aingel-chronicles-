40. HOW ZADOK THE PRIEST GAVE COMMANDS TO DAVID THE KING

1 And Zadok the priest answered to the young man, "Hearken to what I shall say to you. And if you will perform it you will live to God, and if you do not God will punish you, and you will become the least of all the nations, and you will be vanquished by your foes. And God shall turn away His face from you, and you will be dismayed, and sad, and sorrowful in your heart, and your sleep shall be without refreshing and health. And hearken to the word of God, and perform it, and withdraw not yourself either to the right hand or the left, in respect of that which we command you this day; and you will serve no other god. And if you will not hear the word of God, then hearken to all the curses here mentioned which shall come upon you. Cursed shall you be in the field, cursed shall you be in the city. Cursed shall be the fruit of your land, cursed shall be the fruit of your belly, and the herds of your cattle, and the flocks of your sheep. And God shall send upon you famine and pestilence, and He shall destroy that to which you have put your hand, until at length He shall destroy you, because you have not hearkened to His word. And the heavens which are above you shall become bronze, and the earth which is beneath you shall become iron; and God shall make the rain [which should fall upon] your land to be darkness only, and dust shall descend from heaven upon

2 You until it shall cover you up and destroy you. And you will be struck in battle before your enemies. You will go forth to attack them by one road, and by seven ways shall you take to flight before their faces, and you will be routed; and your dead body shall become food for the birds of the heavens, and there shall be none to bury you. And God shall punish you with sores (or, leprosy), and with the wasting disease, and with the fever that destroys, and with the punishments (i.e., plagues) of Egypt, and with blindness and terror of heart; and you will grope about by day like a blind man in the darkness, and you will find none to help you in [your] trouble. You will marry a wife, and another man shall carry her away from you by force. You will build a house, and shall not dwell in it. And you will plant a vineyard and shall not harvest the grapes of it. Men shall kill your fat oxen before your eyes, and you shall not eat of their flesh. Men shall snatch away your donkey, and shall not bring him back to you. Your sheep shall run to the slaves and to your enemy, and you will find none to help you. And your sons and your daughters shall follow other people, and you will see with your own eyes how they are struck, and will be able to do nothing. An enemy whom you do not know shall devour the food of your land and your labour, and you shall not be able to prevent him; and you will become a man of suffering and calamity. When the day dawns you will say, 'Would that the evening had come!' and when the evening comes you will say, 'Would that the morning had come!' through the greatness of your fear.—[All these things shall come upon you] if you will not hearken to the word of the Lord. But if you will truly hearken to the word of the Lord—hear you—the goodness of God shall find you, and you will rule the countries of the enemy, and you will inherit everlasting glory from the Lord God of Israel, Who rules everything. For He honors

3 Him that honors Him, and He loves him that loves Him, for He is the Lord of death and of life, and He directs and rules all the world with His wisdom, and His power, and His [mighty] arm."
