Chapter 111

111. CONCERNING HIS ASCENSION AND HIS SECOND COMING

1 Thus all the Prophets and many of the early Fathers prophesied concerning His Ascension and His Second Coming to judge the living and the dead.

2 And David said concerning His Resurrection, "He has gone up into the heights. You have made captive captivity, and have given grace to the children of men." And he also said, "Having gone forth, I will

3 Come back, and I will return from the abyss of the sea." And again he said, "Sing you to God Who has gone up into the heavens, the heavens which are opposite the morning."

4 Thus Amos the Prophet prophesied and said, "The Messiah, Who made the time of the morning, has come and is exalted from the earth into the heights: and His Name is God Who rules all things."

5 Thus prophesied David the Prophet and said, "You are exalted, O Lord, by Your might, and we will praise and hymn Your strength."

6 Thus prophesied Zechariah the Prophet and said, "His foot stands on the Mount of Olives to the east of Jerusalem. And He rides upon the Cherubim, and He flies upon the wing of the winds."

7 Thus David said, "Open you the gates of the princes, and let the doors which were from the creation be opened, and the King of glory shall come! Who is this King of glory? God, the mighty and strong One, God, the mighty One in battle." And he also made known and said, "Open you the gates of princes, and let the doors which were from the creation be opened, and the King of glory shall come! Who is this King of glory? The Lord God of Hosts is this King."

8 And again concerning His coming—He shall judge the living and the dead—He to Whom belongs glory for ever and ever. Amen.

9 Thus Zechariah the Prophet prophesied and said, "That day the Lord my God shall come, and all His saints with Him."

10 Thus David the Prophet prophesied and said, "God spoke once, and this according [to what] I have heard: Compassion belongs to God. And Yours, O Lord,

11 Is the power, for You will reward every man according to his work."

12 Thus spoke Daniel the Prophet and said, "I saw in my vision by night, and behold, there came [one] like to the Son of man to the Ancient of Days, and there were given to him dominion, and glory, and sovereignty, and all the nations and peoples and countries shall serve Him, and His dominion shall have no end for ever and ever. Amen."

13 And all the Prophets prophesied, and nothing that has happened has been without the prophecy of the Prophets. And they have declared everything that has happened, and what shall happen, what has been done and what shall be done, and that which belongs to the times of old and that which belongs to the latter days up to His Second Coming. And this they have done not only by what they have prophesied and declared, but together with their prophecies they have given manifestations of Him in their bodies. And there was a famine in the land of Canaan, and our father Abraham went down to Egypt, and came back with much riches and honor without blemish. And in like manner our Redeemer went down and delivered the Church, the Assembly of the Nations, and He went up [again], having gotten honor and praise.
