67. CONCERNING THE LAMENTATION OF SOLOMON

1 And now I will tell you how he died. His days were sixty [years], when a sickness attacked him. And his days were not as the days of David his father, but they were twenty [years] shorter than his, because he was under the sway of women and worshipped idols. And the angel of death came and struck him [in] the foot, and he wept and said, "O Lord God of Israel, I am conquered by the terrestrial law, for there is no one free from blemish before You, O Lord, and there is no one righteous and wise before You, O Lord. For You do scrutinize and try the heart. Nothing is hidden from You. You look upon the hidden things [as if they were] revealed, and you search out the heart. Have mercy upon me, Lord. You examine the heart of man and do try the reins. Have mercy upon me, Lord. You hear both the whisper and the thunderclap. Have mercy upon me, Lord. And if You have mercy upon the righteous who have not transgressed Your commandments, what is there wonderful in Your mercy? Have mercy upon me, Lord. But if You should show mercy upon me, a sinner, Your mercy would be a marvellous and gracious thing. Have mercy upon me, Lord. And although I have sinned remember Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, my fathers who did not transgress Your commandment. Have mercy upon me, Lord, for You are merciful and forgiving; for the sake of David Your servant have

2 Mercy upon me, Lord. O Master of the world, and of kings and governors, have mercy upon me, Lord. O You who make fools to be wise, and the wise to be fools, have mercy upon me, Lord. O Turner of sinners and Rewarder of the righteous, have mercy upon me, Lord." And as he spoke these words tears streamed down his face, and he searched for his napkin.

3 And the Angel of God went down to him and said to him, "Hearken you to what I shall say to you, for the sake of which God has sent me. From being a wise man you have turned yourself into a fool, and from being a rich man you have turned yourself into a poor man, and from being a king you have turned yourself into a man of no account, through transgressing the commandment of God. And the beginning of your evil was the taking of many wives by you, for through this you did transgress His Law, and His decree, and the ordinance of God which Moses wrote and gave to you, to Israel, that you should not marry wives from alien peoples but only from your kinsfolk and the house of your fathers, that your seed might be pure and holy and that God might dwell with you. But you did hold lightly the Law of God, thinking that you were wiser than God, and that you would get very many male children. But the foolishness of God is wiser than the wisdom of men, and He has only given you three sons: the one who carried off your glory into an alien land, and made the habitation of God to be in Ethiopia; the one who is lame of foot, who shall sit upon your throne for the people of Israel, the son of the kin of your kin from Tarbâna, of the house of Judah; and the one who is the son of a Greek woman, a handmaiden, who in the last days shall destroy Rehoboam and all your kin of Israel; and this land shall be his because he believes in Him that shall come, the Savior. And the tribe of Rehoboam, and those who are left of Israel, shall crucify Him that shall come, the

4 Redeemer, and the memory of you shall be blotted out from the earth. For they shall think out a plan which they shall not be able to establish, and He will be angry with them and blot out the memorial of them.

5 "And as for you, Joseph, the son of Jacob, shall be a symbol of you. For his brothers sold him into the land of Egypt from Syria, the country of Lâbâ (Laban), and on his going down into the land of Egypt there arose a famine in Syria and in all the world. And through his going down he called his kinsfolk and delivered them from famine and gave them a habitation in the land of Egypt, the name of which is Gêshên (Goshen). For he himself was King under Pharaoh, King of Egypt. Similarly the Savior Who shall come from your seed shall set you free by His coming, and shall bring you out of Sheôl, where until the Savior comes you will suffer pain, together with your fathers; and He will bring you forth. For from your seed shall come forth a Savior Who shall deliver you, you and those who were before you, and those who shall [come] after you, from Adam to His coming in the kin of your kin, and He shall make you to go forth from Sheol as Joseph brought out his kinsfolk from the famine, that is to say the first Sheôl in the land of famine, so also shall the Savior bring out of Sheol you who are His kinsfolk. And as afterwards the Egyptians made [the kinsmen of Joseph] slaves, so also have the demons made you slaves through the error of idols.

6 "And as Moses brought his kinsmen out of the servitude [of Egypt], so shall the Savior bring you out of the servitude of Sheôl. And as Moses worked ten miracles and punishments (or, plagues) before Pharaoh the King, so the Savior Who shall come from your seed shall work ten miracles for life before your people. And as Moses, after he had worked the miracles, struck the sea and made the people to pass over as it were on dry

7 Land, so the Savior Who shall come shall overthrow the walls of Sheôl and bring you out. And as Moses drowned Pharaoh with the Egyptians in the Sea of Eritrea, so also shall the Savior drown Satan and his demons in Sheôl; for the sea is to be interpreted by Sheôl, and Pharaoh by Satan, and his hosts of Egyptians by demons. And as Moses fed them [with] manna in the desert without toil, so shall the Savior feed you with the food of the Garden (i.e. Paradise) for ever, after He has brought you out from Sheôl. And as Moses made them to dwell in the desert for forty years, without their apparel becoming worn out, or the soles of their feet becoming torn, so the Savior shall make you to dwell without toil after the Resurrection. And as Joshua brought them into the Land of Promise, so shall the Savior bring you into the Garden of Delight. And as Joshua killed the seven Kings of Canaan, so shall the Savior kill the seven heads of ’Iblîs. And as Joshua destroyed the people of Canaan, so shall the Savior destroy sinners and shut them up in the fortress of Sheôl. And as you have built the house of God, so shall churches be built upon the tops of the mountains."
