42. CONCERNING THE TEN COMMANDMENTS

1 "And hear you, Israel, that which God commands you to keep; He says, 'I am the Lord your God Who has brought you out of the land of Egypt and out of the house of bondage. There shall be no other gods besides Me, and you shall not make any god that is graven, and no god that is like what is in the heavens above, or in the earth beneath, or in the water which is under the earth. You shall not bow down to them, and you shall not serve them, for I the Lord your God am a jealous God. [I am He] Who visits the sin of the father on the children to the third and fourth generation of those who hate Me, and I perform mercy to a thousand (or, ten thousand) generations of those who love Me and keep My commandments.

2 "You shall not swear a false oath in the Name of

3 The Lord your God, for the Lord will not hold innocent the man who swears a false oath in His Name.

4 "And observe the day of the Sabbath to sanctify it, even as the Lord your God commanded. Six days you will do your work, and on the seventh day, the Sabbath of the Lord your God, you will do no work at all, neither yourself, nor your son, nor your daughter, nor your servant, nor your donkey, nor any beast, nor the stranger that abides with you. For in six days God made the heavens and the earth, and the sea and all that is in them, and rested on the seventh day, and because of this God blessed the seventh day and declared it free [from work].

5 "Honor your father and your mother so that may be good to you the many days that you will find in the land which the Lord your God has given you.

6 "You shall not go with the wife of [another] man.

7 "You shall not kill a life.

8 "You shall not commit sexual immorality. You shall not steal.

9 "You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor.

10 "You shall not covet your neighbor's wife, nor his house, nor his land, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his cattle, nor his donkey, nor any of the beasts that your neighbor has acquired."

11 This is the word which God has spoken, His Law and His Ordinance. And those who sin He rebukes, so that they may not be confirmed in error, and may restrain themselves from the pollution with which God is not pleased. And this is the thing with which God is not pleased, and it is right that men should abstain from it.

12 "No man shall uncover the shame of one with whom he has kinship; for I am the Lord your God. The shame of your father and mother you shall not uncover, for it is your mother. You shall not uncover the shame

13 Of your father's wife, for it is the shame of your mother. You shall not uncover the shame of your sister who was begotten by your father or your mother. Whether she was born to him from outside or whether she is a kinswoman of yours you shall not uncover her shame. You shall not uncover the shame either of your son's daughter, or the shame of the daughter of your daughter, for it is your own shame. You shall not uncover the shame of the daughter of your father's wife, for she is your sister, the daughter of your mother, and you shall not uncover her shame. You shall not uncover the shame of your father's sister, for she is of your father's house. You shall not uncover the shame of your mother's sister, for she is of your mother's house. You shall not uncover the shame of the wife of your father's brother, for she is your kin[swoman]. You shall not uncover the shame of your son's wife, for she is your son's wife. You shall not uncover the shame of your daughter and the wife of your brother's son, for it is your own shame. You shall not uncover the shame of your brother's wife, for it is your brother's shame as long as your brother lives. You shall not uncover the shame of a woman and that of her daughter, nor that of the daughter of her son, nor that of the daughter of her daughter. You shall not cause their shame to be uncovered; it is your house and it is sin.

14 "And you shall not take to wife a maiden and her sister so as to make them jealous each of the other, and you shall not uncover their shame, nor the shame of the one or the other as long as the first sister is alive. You shall not go to a menstruous woman, until she is purified, to uncover her shame while she is still unclean. And you shall not go to the wife of your neighbor to lie with her, and you shall not let your seed enter her.

15 "And you shall not vow your children to Moloch to defile the Name of the Holy One, the Name of the Lord.

16 "And you shall not lie with a man as with a woman, for it is pollution.

17 "And you shall not go to a beast and you shall not lie with it so as to make your seed go out upon it, that you may not be polluted by it. And a woman shall not go to a beast to lie with it, for it is pollution. And you shall not pollute yourselves with any of these things, for with them the nations whom I have driven out before you have polluted themselves, and with them you shall not pollute your bodies.

18 And sanctify you your souls and your bodies to God, for He is the Holy One, and He loves those who sanctify their souls and their bodies to Him. For He is holy, and to be feared, and He is high, and merciful, and compassionate. And to Him praise is fitting for ever and ever. Amen."
