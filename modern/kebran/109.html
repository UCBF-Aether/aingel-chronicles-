109. CONCERNING HIS CRUCIFIXION

1 And the Prophets also prophesied concerning the Crucifixion of Christ.

2 Thus Moses, the servant of God, prophesied and said, "You shall see your salvation crucified upon the wood, and shall not believe."

3 Thus David prophesied and said, "Many dogs have seized Me; and they drove nails through My hands and

4 My feet; and they counted all My bones; though they knew Me they despised Me; and they divided My garments among themselves; and they cast lots for My apparel."

5 Thus Isaiah prophesied concerning the Incarnation and Crucifixion of Christ, and said, "Who believes our word, and to whom is the arm of the Lord revealed? And we spoke like a child before Him: and He is like a root in parched ground, He has no beauty and no form; and His form is more rejected and abased than [that of] any man. He is a broken man and a man of suffering; for He has turned away His face, and they treat Him with contempt and esteem Him as nothing."

6 Thus Solomon prophesied and said, "Let us kill the righteous man, for he is a burden to us; he sets himself up against our works, he resists our intentions continually, and we are an abomination to him because of our sins." And he continued, saying, "My son, let not wicked men lead you astray; if they say to you, 'Come with us, be a partner with us, let us hide innocent blood and take plunder from him; and let there be one purse common to us all': withdraw yourself from their footsteps, for let it not be through you that the birds find the net."

7 Thus David prophesied and said, "They cast gall into My food, and they gave Me vinegar to drink to [quench] My thirst."

8 Thus prophesied Isaiah the Prophet and said, "He has taken our disease and carried our sickness, and by His wound we are healed; and we saw Him suffering, and wounded in his pain; and He opened not His mouth in His pain, and He came to be slaughtered; like a lamb before his shearer He opened not His mouth in

9 His suffering until they took away His life; and they knew not His birth; through the sin of My people have I come even to death." 1

10 Thus Jeremiah the Prophet prophesied and said, "And they took the price of the honourable one thirty [pieces of] silver, whom they had honoured among the children of Israel. And God said to me, Cast it into the melting pot, and test it [and see] if it is pure; and they gave it for the field of the potter; as God has commanded me I will speak." 2

11 Thus Isaiah the Prophet prophesied and said, "They counted Him with the sinners, and brought Him to death." 3

12 Thus David the Prophet prophesied and said, "Those who hate Me wrongfully are many, and they have rewarded Me with evil for good." 4

13 Thus Zechariah the Prophet prophesied and said, "And they shall look upon Him Whom they have crucified and pierced." 5

14 Now there are still very many passages which have been written and many prophecies which might be mentioned concerning His coming, and His Crucifixion, and His death, and His Resurrection, and His second coming in glory. But we have only mentioned a few of the prophecies of the prophets—we have mentioned one of each kind—so that you may hear, and believe, and understand, even as it is said in the Acts of the Apostles, "By the Gospel You have guided us, and by the Prophets You have comforted us; for the words of the Prophets make right the faith of those who doubt." 6
