71. CONCERNING MARY, THE DAUGHTER OF DAVID

1 And from this it is evident that Mary was the daughter of David, and that Joseph was the son of David. Therefore was Mary betrothed to Joseph her kinsman, as it is said in the Gospel, "O Joseph, son of David, fear you not to take to wife Mary your betrothed, for that which is to be born of her is of the Holy Spirit, the Word of God." And there was born of her God, the Word, Light of Light, God of God, Son of the Father, Who came and delivered His creation; from the hand of Sheôl, and from Satan, and from death He has delivered all of us who have believed in Him, He has drawn us to His Father and has raised us up into heaven His throne to become His heirs; for He is a lover of man, and to Him praise belongs for ever. Amen.
