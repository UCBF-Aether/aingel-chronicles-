Kebra Nagast

Chapter 88

88. HOW HE HIMSELF RELATED TO HIS MOTHER HOW THEY MADE HIM KING

1 "And when these had become silent there rose up the priest who gave us the Commandments, with the fear of God, and he shed tears while our bodies quaked and our tears flowed down. God is indeed, without falsehood, in our hearts, and He dwells in His commandment. And His commandment is uttered, and it removes itself not from those who love Him and who keep His commandment, and He is with them continually. And now, hearken to the judgment and laws which the elders and the sons of the mighty men of Israel brought, which they wrote before King Solomon and have given to us, so that we may not turn aside either to the right hand or to the left from what they have commanded us. And also they told us and made us to understand that we bear death and life, and that we are like to a man who has fire in his left hand and water in his right, and who can put his hand into

2 Whichever he pleases. For punishment and life are written in it; for those who have done evil, punishment, and for those who have done good, life."

3 And ’Êlmĕyâs and Azariah brought forth that writing which was written before God and before the King of Israel, and they read it before Mâkĕdâ and before the great men of Israel. And when they heard these words all those who were round about, both small and great, bowed down and bowed down, and they glorified God Who had made them hear these words and had given them this commandment, so that they might perform the justice and judgment of God. And moreover, He made them members of His house, for Zion was among them, and she is the habitation of the glory of God, and she delivered them from all evil, and blessed the fruit of their lands, and multiplied their sheep and cattle, and blessed their wells of water, and blessed their labours, and the fruit of their gardens, and made their children to grow up, and protected their aged men, and became the foreguard and rearguard wheresoever they dwelt, and vanquished their enemies wheresoever they went. And all the people of Ethiopia rejoiced.

4 And the Queen said to her son, "My son, God has given to you the right, walk you in it and withdraw not yourself from it, neither to the right hand nor the left. And love you the Lord your God, for He is merciful to the simple-minded. For His way is known from His commandment, and His goodness is comprehended through the guidance of His word."

5 Then she turned towards ’Êlmĕyâs and Azariah and all the mighty men of Israel [saying], "Do you protect him and teach him the path of the kingdom of God, and the honor of our Lady Zion. And whatsoever our Lady loves not let us not do. Tell us [this] truly and carefully for ever and from generation to generation, so that she may not be angry with us, if we do not

6 Perform her service well, so that God may dwell with us. And you, my son, hearken to the word of your fathers, and walk in their counsel. And let not drink make you foolish, nor women, nor pride of apparel, nor the bridles and trappings of horses, nor the sight of the weapons of war of those who are at the head or at the rear. But let your confidence be in God and in Zion, the Tabernacle of the Law of God, your Creator, so that you may vanquish your enemy, and so that your seed upon the earth may multiply, and so that your foes and adversaries, near and far, may be overthrown."

7 And those sons of mighty men answered with one voice, "O our Lady, we are with you always, and we will remember the lord, the King. Behold, what is written and the performance of it shall take place if the God of Israel shall be to him a helper, and if he hearkens to the word of his mother; and we will inform him about the path of doing good works. For there is no one to be found in these days as wise as yourself, except our lord the King. You have drawn us here as your servants with our Lady, the heavenly Zion, the Tabernacle of the Law of the Lord our God, just as a man draws a camel that is loaded with valuable possessions with a little piece of thin, tough cord fastened over his nose. And now, reject us not and treat us not as strange people, but make us like to your slaves who wash your feet, for whether we die or whether we live we are with you; we have no longer any hope in the country of our birth, but only in you and in our Lady, the heavenly Zion, the habitation of the glory of God."
