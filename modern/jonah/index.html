Jonah

Jonah is a short story with a long shadow, told in just 4 chapters. A prophet named Jonah is sent to preach to Nineveh, the capital of Israel's feared enemy, and he runs the other way — by sea. A storm, a great fish, a prayer from the deep, and then a reluctant sermon follow, and Nineveh repents. The surprise is at the end: Jonah is angry that God showed mercy, and God answers him with a plant, a worm, and a question about compassion for a whole city.

In this edition Jonah is printed from the King James Version of 1611, the public-domain text as received. It belongs here as one of the Twelve Prophets, a set the Ethiopian canon keeps whole alongside the larger prophetic books. Small as it is, it widens the story: God's concern reaches outsiders, and even the prophet still has something to learn.

Watch for how often Jonah would rather be right than be kind, and how patiently God keeps teaching him.
