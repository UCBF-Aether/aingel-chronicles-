Psalm 125

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 They that trust in the LORD shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed, but abids forever.

2 As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people from henceforth even forever.

3 For the rod of the wicked shall not rest upon the lot of the righteous; lest the righteous put forth their hands to iniquity.

4 Do good, O LORD, to those that be good, and to them that are upright in their hearts.

5 As for such as turn aside to their crooked ways, the LORD shall lead them forth with the workers of iniquity: but peace shall be upon Israel.
