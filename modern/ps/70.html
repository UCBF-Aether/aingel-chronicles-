Psalm 70

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Make haste, O God, to deliver me; hurry to help me, O Lord.

2 Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul: let them be turned backward, and put to confusion, that desire my hurt.

3 Let them be turned back for a reward of their shame that say, Aha, aha.

4 Let all those that seek you rejoice and be glad in you: and let such as love your salvation say continually, Let God be magnified.

5 But I am poor and needy: hurry to me, O God: you are my help and my deliverer; O LORD, do not delay.
