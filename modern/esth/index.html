THE BOOK OF ESTHER

Esther

Esther is the story of a Jewish queen in Persia who risks her life to save her people. Chosen as queen to King Ahasuerus, Esther learns of a plot by the proud official Haman to destroy all the Jews, and, coached by her cousin Mordecai, she finds the courage to speak. In most English Bibles the book has 10 chapters, but the Ethiopian canon keeps its fuller form. Here you will find 17 chapters: the King James Version for chapters 1 to 10, and the Greek additions, known as the Rest of Esther, printed as chapters 11 to 17, restoring prayers and royal letters the shorter form lacks. It belongs here in that complete shape, just as the Ethiopian Church has read it. Watch for Esther's brave words about approaching the king uncalled, and for how hidden things keep coming to light.
