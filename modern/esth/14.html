Esther

Chapter 14

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Then Mardocheus thought upon all the works of the Lord, and made his prayer to him,

2 Saying, O Lord, Lord, the King Almighty: for the whole world is in your power, and if you have appointed to save Israel, there is no man that can gainsay you.

3 For you have made heaven and earth, and all the wondrous things under the heaven.

4 You are Lord of all things, and there is no man that can resist you, who are the Lord.

5 You know all things, and you know, Lord, that it was neither in contempt nor pride, nor for any desire of glory, that I did not bow down to proud Aman.

6 For I could have been content with good will for the salvation of Israel to kiss the soles of his feet.

7 But I did this, that I might not prefer the glory of man above the glory of God: neither will I worship any but you, O God, neither will I do it in pride.

8 And now, O Lord God and King, spare your people: for their eyes are upon us to bring us to nought; yea, they desire to destroy the inheritance, that has been your from the beginning.

9 Despise not the portion, which you have delivered out of Egypt for your own self.

10 Hear my prayer, and be merciful to your inheritance: turn our sorrow into joy, that we may live, O Lord, and praise your name: and destroy not the mouths of them that praise you, O Lord.

11 All Israel in like manner cried most earnestly to the Lord, because their death was before their eyes.

12 Queen Esther also, being in fear of death, resorted to the Lord.

13 And laid away her glorious apparel, and put on the garments of anguish and mourning: and instead of precious ointments, she covered her head with ashes and dung, and she humbled her body greatly, and all the places of her joy she filled with her torn hair.

14 And she prayed to the Lord God of Israel, saying, "O my Lord, you only are our King: help me, desolate woman, which have no helper but you.

15 For my danger is in my hand.

16 From my youth up I have heard in the tribe of my family that you, O Lord, took Israel from among all people, and our fathers from all their predecessors, for a perpetual inheritance, and you have performed whatsoever you did promise them.

17 And now we have sinned before you: therefore have you given us into the hands of our enemies,

18 Because we worshipped their gods: O Lord, you are righteous.

19 Nevertheless it satisfies them not, that we are in bitter captivity: but they have stricken hands with their idols,

20 That they will abolish the thing that you with your mouth have ordained, and destroy your inheritance, and stop the mouth of them that praise you, and quench the glory of your house, and of your altar,

21 And open the mouths of the heathen to set forth the praises of the idols, and to magnify a fleshly king for ever.

22 O Lord, give not your sceptre to them that be nothing, and let them not laugh at our fall; but turn their device upon themselves, and make him an example, that has begun this against us.

23 Remember, O Lord, make yourself known in time of our affliction, and give me boldness, O King of the nations, and Lord of all power.

24 Give me eloquent speech in my mouth before the lion: turn his heart to hate him that fights against us, that there may be an end of him, and of all that are likeminded to him.

25 But deliver us with your hand, and help me that am desolate, and which have no other help but you.

26 You know all things, O Lord; you know that I hate the glory of the unrighteous, and abhor the bed of the uncircumcised, and of all the heathen.

27 You know my necessity: for I abhor the sign of my high estate, which is upon my head in the days in which I show myself, and that I abhor it as a menstruous rag, and that I wear it not when I am private by myself.

28 And that your handmaid has not eaten at Aman's table, and that I have not greatly esteemed the king's feast, nor drunk the wine of the drink offerings.

29 Neither had your handmaid any joy since the day that I was brought here to this present, but in you, O Lord God of Abraham.

30 O you mighty God above all, hear the voice of the forlorn and deliver us out of the hands of the mischievous, and deliver me out of my fear."
