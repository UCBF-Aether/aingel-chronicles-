Gessew

Chapter 18

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [Topic:] Concerning the priests who strike everyone.

2 A bishop or an elder who strikes a Christian or one who does not believe, when he has sinned, and seeks by this deed to make men fear — we command that he be deposed. For the Lord did not command us to do this.

3 And not in any place — for when they struck him, he did not strike.

4 And when they reviled him, he did not revile.

5 And when they afflicted him, he did not take vengeance or become angry.
