Gessew

Chapter 23

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [Topic:] Concerning the elder or the deacon whom the bishop has expelled.

2 If a bishop has expelled an elder or a deacon, another bishop shall not allow him to enter again, unless he who expelled him permits it, or if he has died.
