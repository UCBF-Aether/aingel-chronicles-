Chapter 36

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 If a bishop or an elder or a deacon will not eat a little food and drink a small measure of wine during the festival days, and in secret considers these things wrong, and becomes a cause of offense to the people — let him be deposed.
