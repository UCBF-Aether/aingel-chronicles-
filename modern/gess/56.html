Gessew

Chapter 56

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 The writings of Moses: Genesis; Exodus; Leviticus; Numbers; Deuteronomy. Joshua the son of Nun. Judges. Ruth. 8 books of Kings — the first and the second; 6 books: the third and the fourth. The book of the chronicles of the kings. Ezra, first and second. The book of Job. Esther. Tobit. Ecclesiastes. 150 psalms. Of the church, of the council (gloss?). The Song of Songs. The twelve minor prophets. Isaiah. Jeremiah. Daniel. Ezekiel.

2 These [writings] teach your children.

3 Besides these: the wisdom of Solomon. Judith. And the books of Kufale [Jubilees]. Jesus the son of Sirach, the very wise.

4 And these are the books of the new law: 4 gospels — Matthew, Mark, Luke, John; and the Acts of the Apostles; and 2 letters of Peter; 3 letters of John; the letter of James; the letter of Jude; 14 letters of Paul; and the Apocalypse which is the vision of John; and 2 letters of Clement.
