Gessew

Chapter 49

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 If a bishop or an elder or a deacon, or any of the clergy, fasts with the Jews, or celebrates the Passah with them, or accepts from them of their festival [gifts?] — leaven or whatever is like it — let him be deposed; and in the case of a layman, let him be expelled.

2 And if a layman brings oil or lamps into the temple of the gentiles or the synagogue of the Jews, let him be expelled.
