Gessew

Chapter 53

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Let not a bishop be ordained who has been newly baptized.

2 He who has come from among the gentiles, or has been living in sins and has been baptized, shall not be ordained as bishop at once; nor is it allowed that he who has not been tried shall become the teacher of other men — but he shall become this by the grace of God.

3 Nor shall it be allowed the bishop that he shall gather and accumulate wealth; but he shall be found [as] a servant of the church; but if not, he shall not perform the episcopal office.

4 There is no one who can serve two masters, according to the command of our Lord.

5 And we command that no servant shall be ordained [as] priest but with the consent of his master, lest his master be offended; for in this way the households are diminished.

6 And if ever there is found a servant in his time who is worthy of the station of ordination — as there has appeared to us Onesimus — and his master sets him free and lets him go forth from his household, let him be ordained.
