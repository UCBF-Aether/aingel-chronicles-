Gessew

Chapter 2

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [Topic:] Concerning the things that it is fitting to bring upon the altar.

2 If a bishop or an elder brings anything upon the altar of the Lord, apart from what we have commanded — anything he brings and presents as an offering — or he brings honey, or milk, or of birds, or of any other beast, besides what our Lord commanded: let him be deposed.

3 And they shall not be allowed to bring anything upon the altar except ears of grain and grapes in their season, and pure oil of the lamps, and frankincense at the time of the holy eucharist.

4 But the other remaining fruits, let them be sent into the house, and let them be for the bishop and for the elders; but let them not bring them upon the altar.

5 And let the bishop and the elders and the rest of the ordained divide them among themselves.
