Chapter 21

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 [Topic:] Concerning him who calls in aid that he may take the church.

2 If a bishop obtains a position through the princes of this world, and rules the church through them — let him be deposed and expelled: he, and all of them who follow him.
