Gessew

Chapter 42

1 [Topic:] Concerning the denial of Christ and the denial of the priesthood.

2 If any of the clergy denies on account of the fear of man — either of the Jews, or of the gentiles, or of the heretics — and if it is the name of Christ which he has denied, let him be removed; and if it is the name of the priesthood, let him be deposed.

3 And if he repents, they shall receive him, and he shall enter as a layman.
