Gessew

Chapter 41

1 [Topic:] Concerning the priest whom the people convict of sexual immorality or something else.

2 If there is a dispute among the believers with regard to sexual immorality, or luxurious life, or other fault, and they convict him — he shall no longer be among the clergy.
