Josippon

Chapter 22

1 Of God, that there may be joy, two [hosts]; as the ordinance pleases in it — the ordinance which is not the Devil's; and they were lost, Adam by, with; and there came forth from his seed those who pleased God; all of them the kingdom, and; and one of them was lost, and; and the and the house, and he sought a reckoning: [thus] it. The third, he says: that those nine hosts of angels who were not lost do not know men in their likeness; and — the one who was lost is Adam and his seed; and at the time — for in it they lift up his likeness to the. And as for Adam, he is the likeness of God; and as for the woman, she signifies the mercy of God. And as for the yoke of Abraham, it is Christ, who sanctified the world and it from; and He [restored] that which was fitting over the affliction of Adam in his labors of, which for their sakes, until He return them into the kingdom of heaven, for whose sake joy was made. And so also says the apostle Paul: — he who knew no sin made [himself] the head of sin for our sakes, that we might become the righteousness of God in Him. And this is that which he says: He made the head of sin — Christ, upon His head: He was not born, and He was not begotten in the desire of Adam; by the hand of death upon His head, nor for — those who died for our sakes — from the Devil, instead of their ransom; who was followed by him who is not fitting to die instead of him; and He took us from; we became; His servants; — it became us; and the unclean spirits, and for the demons, over — from us, from the time of His birth the time of Baptism. And this is that which comes forth from Him, the unclean spirit, which is from the host of the Devil; and there enters into it the Holy Spirit; and: for the of death is for all men: the sin of Adam their father, and their sins, of those who [sin] a second time, became the Devil's, who follows them, because of these two works. For because of the sin of Adam he kills him who sinned; and as for him who sins not, he kills [him] by the sin of Adam and his own sin; he destroys the soul from perdition — the Devil — when he sings praises to God, and he proclaims that for whose sake he was created; when he was rebuked once, he sought and repented concerning it, and was: the glory and the proclamation in its time: all of it — this is sin, when: concerning it, from before: for falsehood removes sin, for him who is from among [us], by the Son of God. And as for him who denies the Holy Spirit, is taken from him, and there enters into him an unclean soul, and with him seven other [spirits] more wicked than he; they enter and they dwell in it, so that their end becomes worse than their beginning. As he says in the [Gospel]; and when a liar is he, and is opened — a — and believes in the of God, He takes [him]; when they killed Him, or: for He, when He established His in wrath, returns: that He made for the Holy Spirit [a place] to stand; that He cannot cast them out for the unclean spirits; and [he] departed from the mercy of God; for the mercy of God departs not far from him who repents, and who repents — [he departs] not from Him who denies willingly, not by violence, or by fear of death, or by the sword: for he removes mercy far from himself. And as for him also who is weak in, for the sake of salvation from [men]; or for the sake of love of office, or of honor, and lies in his soul that he, after this, will repent and does repent — this is he: the Holy Spirit departs far, and by His power from him the unclean spirits, by legions of legions and legions. And that which he says: this; it cannot be forgiven by repentance: — he is for the Holy [Spirit]; and it shall not be forgiven forever; and mercy of God shall not forth from him at the time of his denial; and the unclean spirits prevail over him, until the denial be in all his heart. And many men, while they hope [in] lying, deny, and they; and they say in their soul: We shall repent after this, and for us. But as for them, the anger of God overtakes them, and death, before; and He joins them [to] him who is the of eternity; for they sinned willingly, and they trusted in a liar, and they forgot the word of God; for death — as a host who knows not — at the time when the master of the house comes to him unawares. Behold, I have revealed to you, my beloved, all that you asked from me concerning the of for the Son of God and the Crucifixion. And I have revealed the testimonies concerning from the holy Scriptures concerning this. Behold, I am [here]; I will reveal to you in another book of interpretation many testimonies, and thanksgiving, and honor, and worship forever and ever. Amen.
