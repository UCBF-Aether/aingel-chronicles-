Josippon

Chapter 50

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 The second discourse, concerning the ordinances of the fast of the one day, Wednesday and Friday, which is the love of God: may He enlighten the eyes of your heart by the light of the Holy Spirit, the Comforter, that you may discern the mystery of the Godhead.

2 I have agreed with you that I would reveal to you the ordinances of the fast of the one day, Wednesday and Friday; there is that which I have told you; and know its meaning, and hold fast, as the fathers, the holy apostles, said in their canons — the seventh and the tenth — whoever does not fast the one day —

3 Wednesday and Friday, and always he observes

4 — if he is of the priesthood [he is cut off]; and if he is a layman, he shall be driven from among the Christians.

5 He is severed and cursed: if he is a priest,, and a stranger, and his curse [is] from God; and if he does not fast also the one day always — there is that which I have told you — that you may know the reason of this: when God brought forth the children of Israel

6 the land of Egypt, as they journeyed in their going forth, and afterwards He led them into the land of Canaan, which is the land of Jerusalem; and you are in it; and He gave them days over the nations, while they dwelt in it, being sent, and kings in it. And there were in their borders nations, Canaanites, while they spread into all the coasts of the shore of the sea, from Gaza and Ashkelon to the harbor of the sea of the Philistines, and Dan and the Canaanites.

7 And Israel used to offer, and was in the land,, to the LORD; and they offered their offerings in another [place]; and it was without Him. And Solomon built a sanctuary in Jerusalem; and it was the first house of the sanctuary which was built in Jerusalem to the LORD in all the earth; and he built in it an altar for the oblation; for the house of the sanctuary is the house of the Christians, and the mysteries [are] in it: they offer upon it offerings, and [there is] its sanctifying; to it Israel used to offer, coming from all the land of Canaan, to this house of the sanctuary, two times.
