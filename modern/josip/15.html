Josippon

Chapter 15

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 and he was also like Adam; and he lived, when he was created — there being in them shame that is known from her —; and she was like the angels, for the light of their faces covered their flesh.

2 And when He created Eve, He wrought a wondrous work for that Adam: He took from Adam; He created the completion of her bones from his sides, without, that He might show that He is Almighty to take from her; He is the bone that is closely joined; He makes from her another completion in her sides, as He created for her the completion; and she was made like Adam; and He works from one another, for He is Almighty over every work.

3 And know this saying: that the woman has the bone of her side added to the man until now, as the wise men, the interpreters, have spoken; and it was confirmed together with the Book of the LORD in their.

4 For Satan, when he had finished questioning all the beasts that stood firm, and had come to the garden, and saw Adam and Eve standing firm, without, and they had glory and a voice, all of them were amazed. He knew in his heart that they were of the company of the hosts of the angels, being within the compass of the beasts; for his thought was set against this. And then he said in himself: I know, lest it come to pass as he destroyed the hosts, and they rise up and cast down her order, and remain to the ages.

5 And I know also that the LORD is righteous and.

6 Judgment; and there is no respect of persons with Him.

7 And these, when they are lifted up like me, He will cast them down, as He cast me down, when I was lifted up against Him and became like.

8 And when they do these things in envy, and when they do them like me, He will cast them down, as He cast them down like me; and if I make them do my work, He will overshadow me with a pretext of my own; for it befits me to take a pretext against them until they do it of their own desire, without compulsion, and in the doing, that a pretext of their own trespass may be fitting against them.

9 And then he hid his spirit from them within the serpent, and spoke to them from it, saying: Why did God forbid you, that you should say: Of all the trees that are in the garden shall you eat? He said this saying because he supposed in his heart that God had laid a commandment upon them, that they might decline by their commandments.

10 As He did with us concerning the glory and the holiness.

11 And I did not see in their hiding, nor does anything command them that they should be kept from them, except the trees: at the time He commanded them not to eat of any of them. And concerning this thought which he thought concerning the judgment of God, which we have mentioned before: at the time He commanded them not to eat of that which is not written in the Book of the Teaching, and from all the beasts.

12 And Eve said: God has not forbidden us to eat of all the trees; but He forbade us the eating of the fruit of the tree; and He said to us: You shall not eat of it, nor shall you touch it, for on the day that you eat of it, you will surely die.

13 And Satan said: It is not that you shall die when you eat of it; but God knows that you, when you eat of it, shall become angels, according to His likeness.

14 And then she longed to become like an angel, and she ate of it, and gave it to Adam that he might become like her.

15 And he ate of it.

16 And when God stripped them of the garment of light, as He stripped the Devil of it, and did not cast them down as He cast him down into the earth, that they might be in judgment, according to His judgment of the Devil, they knew that they had sinned in likeness, and were likened to him in themselves; and they heard from the serpent, and believed him, and desired from the serpent to become angels.

17 And they deceived them in their deeds, and they were not perfected, nor filled with all wisdom; for the Book of the Teaching says: God brought to Adam all the beasts and the wild beasts, to see what he would call them, and what they should be to all of them.

18 And the names which he gave them have firmness to the ages; God showed by this in truth that Adam was filled with all wisdom.

19 And therefore God bare witness that He created Eve like Adam in every work; and she was not, but wise.

20 And therefore this is named, that which follows against the Devil and the serpent: By what pretext did he come into the garden? This is it: that God brought to Adam every beast, and the serpent was subtle above every beast, and the serpent was subtle above all; He left them in their thought in the garden, that they might guard it. And by this Satan found a pretext by which to beguile them, for he saw them rejoicing in it greatly and.
