Josippon

Chapter 39

1 And again the people bow down, their head, and ask the priests of God — who is righteousness — in His hand, in the body of Satan, which He gave to His disciples, that they should bind and loose every bond of sin, that He may loose them from their sins, and root up all their iniquity, and save them from evil, and bring them into the heavenly kingdom.

2 And after this little while the body ascends; He said: His gift is lifted up above all the people, as it was pierced from them; He said: "Osa, mestal."

3 And all the people cry out, as the thief on the right said: "Remember us, O LORD, when You come in Your kingdom."

4 And it is fitting for them, the forgiveness of all their sins, and joy within the town, as He asks for the thief from Him this gift; everyone takes that which stands at the time of the Holy One, in faith and in fear.

5 From him who was his offering, or he who ate, from him who draws near, or he who does not draw near; and he who cannot draw near, he is he who knows, that takes the portion.

6 And when they cannot, he shall be as a prince who says to God: "I am not worthy that You should come under my roof;" those who are commanded by Your word, and one singleness is exalted.

7 And then shall he be who sought judgment by faith, and was ashamed from the reproach of faith; likewise shall he be who believes and stands firm at the time of the Holy One.

8 And when he who cannot draw near leaves the offering in humility and in lowliness and in faith, he takes that which the prince took, a hundred; and concerning this it is fitting for the faithful, when he draws near or does not draw near, that he be in fear and in trembling, until Christ ascends from upon the altar; and the ascension — when the body is fulfilled, all his body shall be fulfilled, and he shall ascend; today he proceeds from the body; and after this, when he stood before the ascension, they cannot set upon him the might of the Holy Spirit, which He sent upon His disciples after the ascension; and if he is he who in the time of giving — all — and went out before the ascension of Christ — and the ascension shall be to him from the portion of Judas Iscariot, who said concerning him, and the holy Gospel, that he is he who in the night of the day of Friday, the night in which Christ gave to His disciples from His body and His blood, forsook the apostles and went out alone, before Christ came forth; and then Satan entered to him, when he went out before the holy offering was fulfilled.

9 For the holy Gospel said that he was present, and went out then; and the disciples abode with Christ, until he went out, and went out with him.

10 And likewise he who goes out before the ascension of Christ from the house of the Christians became in the likeness of Judas.

11 And if it be a great house, a work it is not fitting for them to go out.

12 And he who is in earnest at the time of the Mass, and does not go out until the offering ascends from the altar, takes the crown that keeps, which we have mentioned to you before.

13 And by the faith of the priests who ordained a law to Christ — and they cannot know to declare the books — he shall be driven, he who willed, from the law, before the offering ascends from the altar.

14 And they perish, their souls, and the soul of all the people; for God commanded men that they should hear the commandment of the priests, if they command them in that which is commanded.

15 And when they transgress them, they take destruction forever.

16 As the hosts of the Devil: and when men heard the commandment of the Devil for their head, and obeyed him in that which God breaks, and were kept back from the praises and the holiness, and went out with him, forever.

17 Likewise shall fall and perish everyone who goes out from the house of the Christians, by wrath the priests shall drive him out, before the offering is fulfilled.

18 And if they hear concerning them, in that which God does not promise, they take judgment with them, as the hosts of the Devil were judged with him; likewise he who holds back at the time of the Holy One, and does not hear the declaration of the books and of the holiness, and is adorned, takes judgment upon me; for he is, instead of him who runs for Christ, they polish him; for he is he who takes by an unclean spirit and an unclean body.

19 And you are he: as the books and the holiness made them hosts before the offering, they sanctify the faithful, and His ascension they cleanse, as I have shown you; and after this it is fitting to take the offering, as men cannot be, who are without sin, for one hour that abides in the wilderness alone.

20 And he who shall be joined with men, causing to sin, burns from her by a word which is not fitting; or by an oath, or by reviling, or by a curse of his neighbor.

21 Or by withholding from this: he is he who in these works abode, and was defiled, and was cleansed; and he wishes for the hearing of the reading of the books, and the hearing of the prayer of light, that he may cast away his defilement, and be sanctified.

22 And after this he eats and drinks from the holy flesh and the blood of Christ; and he is holy, and is converted; and he who does not love the books and the holiness, and is confounded, shall be reckoned — his body shall be reckoned, and his blood is for Christ; and he also is unclean.

23 And Christ shall humble him again exceedingly; He said in the tongue of the prophet: "Be humbled"; for He said in the tongue of the prophet: "Be humbled; for him who humbles me I will exalt, and I will honor him who honored me"; and He swore this, as the likeness of him who sows seed in earth which is not watered, and does not plow it; he also destroys the seed, and it does not sprout for the earth.

24 Likewise men, if he was in fatherhood, sought you, as you sow for you: he shall be as earth, a thing of many defilements; and if he comes into the house of Kerkemtedan, and hears the voice of their books, and the Gospel, the voice of God — the water of life — as the LORD God said; and he is watered by that water from newness, that cleanses sin.

25 And when he is watered, and Tesfa comes, the Holy Spirit descends upon him, and cleanses him from sin, as earth is cleansed from defilement.

26 And then he receives the holy offering, and bears fruit in him, as earth that is opened, the chosen one, that bears the seed that is sown, and it bears fruit in it.

27 Behold, I have shown you the glory and the holiness which is equal upon all the faithful; and apart from Him they cannot do anything at all from the commandment of God; for by the glory the Holy Spirit abides in them, and the unclean spirits are driven out from them, and they are made to do the work for God.

28 And whoever departs from going to the house of the Christians in the morning and in the evening, and departs from their prayer at the time of the Holy One, as I have told you, and reaches death in that day, and shall be reckoned forever and ever — as the Devil and his hosts fell from her — and behold, I have shown you the glory and the holiness, that it is fitting upon all the faithful, all of them, at all the days of their life.

29 For God has appointed for them these works — they are the cause of their salvation and of what is their life; and this is it: that in every day and in every night, seven and two hours, God appointed for them three times to reach to the gate of the house of the Christians, in the morning, and in the evening, and at the time of the Holy One; and these three times at which the Christian reaches to the gate are not the measure of two hours; and there remains for them seven and two hours, in which they do the work of the flesh that passes away; and God gives them from the wealth of this world a doubled reward, in place of the work which they forsook, in place of those two hours, while it is charged to them upon that which cleanses them, for the life of their souls — which is His glory, of God, and His holiness, and the descent of the Holy Spirit upon them, and the forgiveness of their sins.

30 And likewise in every week, seven days, God has appointed upon men their work, that it rule not over him.

31 And He said to them: "Work the work of the flesh two days, and work for the spirit one day."

32 And in that one day, work in it the work that is spiritual, from morning until evening; it shall be for you in it a work without reading the word of God in the holy house of the Christians. And He said again: "When you read, a good reading read." And all who are gathered to you, and you do for them, God shall give you a reward on their account, in the kingdom.
