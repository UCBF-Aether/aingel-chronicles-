Josippon

Chapter 79

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 of the kings: and all the reckoning from Adam until Nebuchadnezzar.

2 The third and the fourth in the vision of Daniel.

3 four: and the beginning of the reckoning of the four, at the beginning of the kingdom of Nebuchadnezzar, for he said.

4 I make known that which they knew concerning Daniel.

5 the third and the fourth: and this is the reckoning from the Book of Judges.

6 And to all of them; and again Saul reigned over them.

7 After him, until the beginning of the kingdom of Nebuchadnezzar in Babylon.

8 from the Book of Kings to Alexander until the birth of Christ.

9 from Nebuchadnezzar until the birth of Christ, as he said.

10 Behold, the Book of Creation makes known.

11 Behold, the Book of Christ.

12 after the death of Abraham until the beginning of Christ.

13 after the birth of Christ the earth.

14 And Adam from the tree, and on Friday Christ was crucified upon the wood.

15 for Adam, and he fell from the tree and from the garden.

16 that the resurrection of our Lord from the dead.

17 our resurrection for as the resurrection death.

18 and likewise we shall rise for his resurrection.

19 Christ: on the day on which Adam ate of the tree, and they fell from the garden, likewise on that day and night was Christ crucified, and he restored Adam into the garden; for it was on Friday.

20 from the womb of the virgin and he came forth from the tomb an angel.
