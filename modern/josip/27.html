Josippon

Chapter 27

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Their going forth from Sheol is by Jesus, for Christ.

2 And the Church is in the likeness of the tent. And in it — that which was baptized in it: the manna which came down from heaven: for Christ — they live by it, them. As the children of Israel were in it, in the tents they ate the manna. And the prophet — the rock which was cleft, which no cut: men Gabriel — for Christ was born of a Virgin, without the seed of man, and He became a great mountain, and filled all the world, and the were. And as for the Church, it is likened to the tent. And the wilderness, in truth, is also filled with Paradise, as we before. For in Paradise, in it, one rivers flow and water the thirst of the whole earth. And so also the Church: in it one rivers — one rivers of life — flow in it, and they water the thirst of men in all the earth, who thirst for the word of God, who love it.

3 As David the prophet says in the Psalm of the hundred and fourth: "As the deer longs for the springs of water." And he says in the Psalm of the one and seven: "Sweet to my throat is Your speech; more than honey, and the honeycomb is its taste to my mouth." And in Paradise [there are] many trees; and in the Church [there are] many books, which are in Paradise. He said [concerning] the days: He said: There will be in that day a great famine — not of bread and water; God says: [It is] of the word. I show [you] that His word is bread and water; and — now the hunger is from Him; for they speak, and they do not understand its interpretation; for God closed their eyes and their hearts and their ears, as the prophet said concerning them.

4 And in Paradise [is] the tree of life, of which God said — He who says concerning it: from it: they are of God, who protect them for the souls, and they them.

5 As Moses said in the Law: — "For not by bread alone does man live, but by every word which proceeds from the of God." I show [you] that the word of God is the bread of life — He is; and so also Isaiah the prophet moreover named the word of God bread and water, and he said: concerning the Jews forever.

6 And in the Church [is] the tree of life, which in truth is the flesh and the blood of Christ.

7 Of which Christ said: "He who eats My flesh and drinks My blood lives forever."

8 And as for the Church, which in is the Paradise of God, He says the earth from. — Adam was in Paradise, and he until the world; and — Sheol; and when Christ raised [him] from he entered into Paradise — moreover men who will be — from, and he is born afresh, and he falls into and he will be in judgment; and when — Christ with judgment — enters into them — anew, as He brought in Adam Paradise — anew he is born the new birth — born from God: he will be with Him in forever; for he is an infant, a babe, who has no strength to feed himself the food. Except: they anoint him with his mother, until he becomes like her, and he eats food.

9 So also he who is born of God by Baptism — they anoint him [with] the flesh, and they him for Christ, when he abides in this world; for he is fleshly, and there is not to him strength, that he should eat food: Christ is spiritual altogether.

10 Except those who suck the milk of Christ — which is His flesh and His blood — until they become as Christ after death, and they live in this life; and they receive that which is given; and the receivers distant; and they are by them; far; and they suck them the milk of and the blood of Christ: — a pool and from milk — it is fleshly — for them milk which flows from Christ, in a fullness which does not pass, and a which does not pass away.

11 For thus He said to you: "And — I am with you — that you may eat and drink with Me at My table in My kingdom." Gabriel: that they may dwell so, as gives birth to a child, and he becomes like them. And by the Baptism of holy Christianity a man becomes [free] from the power of the Devil, and he becomes — who was born, who was born without desire — as the incarnation was for Christ; for the Spirit of Christ from the birth of desire, and the will have no authority over him, when the Spirit of Christ is in it.

12 For the Spirit of Christ — who is the Holy Spirit — is a likeness of Joshua the son of Nun; He is He who them —, the believers — He will reign in the kingdom of heaven; He will reign in the kingdom of heaven, from which the Devil and his host fell.

13 And He saves them from him, and He overcomes for them, as Joshua the son of Nun did with the children of Israel.

14 And the believers will bring the children of Israel from the fruit and the land. So also the priests, who show — [if] they know the kingdom of heaven — they will bring to the believers the fruit of the kingdom of heaven: the flesh and the blood of Christ. He is a sweet fruit, which men brought forth [before] the eye; He is from the whole earth, that the children of Israel may see it, and taste it, and know the taste, and desire that land, that they may rejoice in its many fruits.
