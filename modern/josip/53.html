Josippon

Chapter 53

1 And the Mena'erawit — and there went forth from them twelve disciples, their names fifteen, apostles, and twelve; and they were called by their names, disciples; and [some] men shall arise, saying that he is a prophet; and others say: "Look, he is Christ"; and they shall be received; they shall reveal this from the fruits of Rome. The twelve apostles [asked] exactly, that

2 In the fortress it was revealed to the priests of the Jews and to their portions, and by their Pharisees — those who hear them and say to them: Today shall [the word] be revealed concerning their work, and concerning their reckoning; in purity [are] their works, and in purity their doings, from the instruction of the people of God, which is fitting for the men.

3 — and He them, and they shall be lifted up to Him, and He shall gladden the people.

4 They and they shall cry out above at His wonders, and shall say: "There is none from God [like Him]."

5 These are from Satan; for he walks in defilement, and they raise him up for a portent; and in him he heals the sick by his [arts], and he charges them with their faults, their women, and he does not lift up.

6 Many women — for commanded — and there was in this word a troubling of the heart of the people, and of the people also, concerning the Great One who.

7 As was the custom. And when the prophet drew near to the tree, a prophet from Jerusalem, [to the place] whose name is Bethany; and today the Nazarene dwelt there by night, which is the first [day] of the month Nisan, from the beginning, at the feast of the Passover, on the fourteenth day, on the twelfth, the day; and He commanded them and came forth from the tree. And He commanded His disciples

8 that they should go to Baqwa; and there should be found [he] whose name is Beth-Qura; and his brothers should be found by the sea; and his brothers should be found, Dumu; and our Lord Jesus was taken up.

9 The princes of the kingdom shall not command them, neither shall they ask [anything] of them.

10 And he remained thus three years, and they were not received.

11 Now believes in Him [everyone], that He is Christ, from the fruits of Rome.

12 And when He was twelve years [old], in fullness, His disciples went up to Jerusalem, before the feast of Na't.

13 — sus Christ [was] upon her at that time. And when His disciples saw this, it seemed to them that He had begun to reign, and would destroy them by Rome, the adversaries of the Jews; and the twelve disciples cried out with a voice in the Hebrew tongue, [in] the city of the Jews, and their disciples, to the Son of David — its interpretation: Our Savior, the Son of David —.

14 The name — He who me at the first; gladness entered in, which reigned afterwards over Judah; and God made covenant that Christos, from our kings, should be born from the seed. He cried out [to] him: Our Savior, from Rome — my hatred also, and David — mentioned this prophecy, in the third psalm of the eleventh-and-second: The LORD said: Our Savior [is] a shadow [upon] the ways. Blessed is he who comes — the LORD. And likewise they used to say: "Our Savior, O Son of David! Blessed is he who comes in the name of the LORD." And David also

15 In [this] — concerning their salvation from Rome — these are from the Devil and [her] hosts. And her disciples and the Jews them from Rome. And when he cried out in this — the above — which they shall be received, he shall reveal to them.

16 And they gave them to their servant, to them — the Jews who dwell — and

17 the city and the who came to the feast of gladness from all the cities and from; all of them, all of them shall receive Him; and in their midst He shall be known firstly. And He shall be known, known; and they shall open, in His, Hosanna, O Son of David! And they believed in Him, all of them, that He is our Christ, their King, concerning that which was cast formerly from His kingdom; and it shall come to pass that He shall do [it], that He shall cast them out to Rome; they shall seek them by; and they Him, from.
