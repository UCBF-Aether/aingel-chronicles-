Josippon

Chapter 9

1 The second discourse: concerning the becoming-man of the Son of the LORD, and His crucifixion. beloved of the LORD: may He enlighten the eyes of your heart by the dawn of the Holy Spirit the Paraclete, that you may follow the mystery of the divinity.

2 First: that which you asked me — that I should write for you a book, and reveal to you in it the being of the LORD and His union, and the reason of the incarnation of the Son of the LORD, and His crucifixion, and. That which you asked, I have written for you a book, and I have revealed to you in it, and you, the being of the LORD and His union; and I have made known to you that the LORD is a being without division, and, and I do not ask of you [anything] from the word of the LORD, by Himself alone.

3 For our Lord Jesus Christ said in the holy kingdom: There is none that knows the Son except the Father, and there is none that knows the Father except the Son, and him to whom He wills to reveal [Him] to him.

4 Behold, the Son rejoiced, and revealed His knowledge, and the knowledge of His Father and of the Holy Spirit, to the holy prophets and apostles, and to our fathers the teachers of the holy Church; and they revealed that to the believers, because in them is the Holy Spirit through the holy baptism, and their eyes are shining; and they understand this Holy Spirit who dwells in them.

5 As Paul the apostle said: — there is none that can say that Jesus is Lord except by the Holy Spirit. And this also is as a word that proceeds from a father: a tongue from a heart, a fleshly speaking spirit — they do not understand its interpretation except by the flesh, which is, a human speaking spirit, as the spirit that proceeds from it is a word.

6 beasts, and all the creeping things: but there is not in them a rational speaking breath; they do not understand the interpretation of the word that proceeds from a speaking, rational spirit, for they have hearing.

7 And so also bodies: but in them are rational spirits.

8 And so also were they baptized: but there is not in them the Spirit of the LORD, the Holy One; they do not understand the word of this Spirit of the LORD, the Holy One; and when a man comes to be, there comes in him a rational and speaking spirit.

9 But when there is not in them the Spirit of the LORD, the Holy One — who is [in] all of them that were baptized — they do not understand the word of the LORD, as the beasts do not understand the word of man, for there is not in them the Spirit of the LORD.

10 And so also the apostle Paul said concerning them: For they who are of the breath, in whom there is not the Holy Spirit, do not receive that which is of the Spirit of the LORD; for it is in his speech foolishness, because the Spirit of the LORD is not in them — as [a word] is spoken in it, and as a man speaks to those in whom there is not the spirit of man.

11 And they that were baptized, all of them — the Spirit of the LORD is in them; and if not in their faith, they do not understand fully the interpretation of the word of the LORD, concerning the knowledge of the righteous in it. They are not naked from the Spirit of the LORD; but they are as a child that does not discern the interpretation of the word of man because of [his] youth; and he is not naked from the human speaking spirit, but because of [his] youth, for he is a child: he does not understand that.

12 And when he grows up in it in years, and knows, all the believers understand likewise, in whom is the holy Spirit of the LORD from His Christian baptism; but they are too small for the teaching of the word of the LORD, and in it sin then, they understand by the Spirit of God, who dwells in them,.

13 They are taught and instructed, and believe His exposition, as a child [learns] the interpretation of the speech of man, when he is taught and learns in it, and receives and believes by the spirit of man that is in them. And to our fathers, the apostles and the prophets, the Son revealed the mystery of the LORD, which was hidden in the Holy Spirit; and they revealed it to the believers, in whom is the Holy Spirit.

14 And behold, I have revealed to you in the former book, from the words of those who spoke in their books interpretations good and great — know — and to the knowing ones among all the believers, in whom is the Spirit of the LORD.

15 And behold, I reveal to you in this book, by the help of the Holy Spirit, the reason of the incarnation of the Son of the LORD and His crucifixion, and again the mention of His exposition, from the fourth section.

16 The first section: know that in it His exposition [is] the end of Adam — a word, that its interpretation may reach in fullness to the few in understanding; and know that the word shall not tarry over them, and they shall not forget its interpretation.

17 And the second section mentions: the end — its interpretation, in a few words, that he who asks may understand its interpretation in few words.

18 The first [section] says: that which the LORD made — heaven and earth — before He made [them]; and then He says: on the earth He created them, the angels, in the midst of heaven: spirits without bodies.
