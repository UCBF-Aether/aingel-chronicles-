Josippon

Chapter 37

1 And again, continually he glorifies God and sanctifies, and hallows His word; at its time He abides continually; the grace of the Holy Spirit is to him who is apart from them; for He is He who sharpens the praises and the sanctifying; and hearing His word — God — for He is He who quickened the soul with God; it was not the season that we should ask.

2 And He said: that it is for him who eats and for him who drinks; for by it his body lives in the world; and when God knew that man quickens a body — life — and quickens the life of his body, exceedingly more than the life of his soul.

3 And therefore the life of his soul is exalted; and therefore the spirit of the time, and the life of the body of the time, exceeds the time of the spirit, that this may come upon man, and he does continually.

4 Man does not desire the time of those who are not the beginning of salvation; and by the indwelling of the Holy Spirit in man, continually.

5 That you do not delay concerning the ordinance of God upon man, every day and every hour: that he go to the house of the Christians without excuse, and magnify in it in fear all the day, and ask the forgiveness of his sins, in the matter that is past; and ask that there be appointed for him a pure angel, that he may seal him at that time from the hosts of Satan, and his snares at that time; those are the ones who guard the holy soul from them, at that time of the night.

6 And likewise he goes to the house of the Christians at the time of the setting of the sun; he lifts up his head, the beginning of the night; he glorifies, and bewails his sins by day, continually.

7 And he seeks that there be sent to him a pure angel, who shall guard him in that night from the snares of the hosts of Satan.

8 And at that time those are the ones who are guarded from them by the might of the Holy Spirit, in that time of the night.

9 This one thing is fitting for all the faithful: that they all, at one time with one accord, pray into the house of the Christians at that hour.

10 And by doing.

11 And if there is in a city a house of the Christians, it is fitting that he pray in it, while his heart is troubled and sorrowful; for in it is found the house of the Christians.

12 For the house of the Christians is Paradise to the LORD, as I have shown you in the Book of the Interpretation of the Words, four, and Joshua the son of Nun.

13 And whoever forsakes and leaves one of these two prayers, and does not glorify God, takes and is taken from this, and does not return to do, that he not forsake nor leave, at the time of the life from the hours of those.

14 No, he who does the work of the flesh passes away in the time of that day, and attains to death in that time of the day; from the beginning he rejoices, and goes forth into eternal darkness, which is in it, the Devil and his hosts; for he is he who was kept back from the glory of God, like them.

15 This, if he was kept back from the glory of God by excuse: he shall be in the likeness of the Devil and his companions.

16 And if he was kept back from this in the work of life, that passes away: no, he seeks a work from the work of that spirit; and they made Judas from the fathers of God; for he shall be in the likeness of the Devil and his companions.

17 And if he was kept back in the time of those [days], while he seeks honors in the likeness of Judas, who was made for the land, that passes away, he loves that which presses more than your crushing: that which abides forever; and he shall be in the likeness of those who make an idol; not so — God forsook from him His glory; and he went, and took his body, gold — no.

18 Pure.

19 And gold, and pure vessels.

20 And therefore the LORD said a glory to him, which of wrath is not worthy to the two servants who are made subject to God and to the vessels; and it is not fitting for any of the faithful to forsake [going] from the going to the house of the Christians in the time of those two days, and to excuse himself by a work from all works; and this shall be a wretchedness for the sight.

21 And likewise it is fitting that he come and fulfill it at the time of the Mass; and there shall not be seen, before its season, the finishing of the Mass; whether he be able to draw near, or whether he does not draw near — for he is he that is fitting, that he may glorify God and sanctify Him; for His glory is among those things which we have mentioned, and His holiness — it, at the time of the offering, an offering, at the time of the Mass — there is heard the writing, which is the word of the LORD, which proceeds from His mouth, which our Lord Jesus Christ said: for the Spirit in him came to life; and it is not in vain, for bread is the life of the flesh, and the word of God is the life of the soul; and his soul came to life by the hearing of the holy books, and the hearing of the Gospel; and he sanctifies God with the angels, three: for the priest says, You are God, who stand; before You are the angels, and to You, the angels.
