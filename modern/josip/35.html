Josippon

Chapter 35

2 [The text of this verse stands in chapter 34, verse 7 (opening) and verse 8; it is not duplicated here.]

3 And He is He who made baptism, that it should set the children of Adam free from the works of the Devil, that they might die pure, and ascend into Paradise, and that He should bear them with that which ascended from Sheol, until they should be in their under the hosts of the Devil; and He ascends, and inherits the ordinance of the height of heaven.

4 You — from her fell the Devil and the hosts of the Devil; they took counsel against the of baptism, concerning the ordinance of this book, that you might inquire of this: God gives you understanding to every work. You desire to know that the angels are pure spirits, without flesh, and the flesh of the beasts is brutish, without a rational soul; and their life is their blood, for they have no rational soul.

5 And when God ordained for Adam that he should abide in the ordinance — you, from whom fell the Devil, the chief of the angels.

6 God ordained for Adam a pure spirit, rational, prophetic, like the angels; and He set it in living flesh, until the sinner should die, and should inherit with them the judgment which is forever, and should be far removed from the kingdom which is forever.

7 All this which is in this book: your being, which the faithful work, that they may prevail by it over him that is burnt, which I mention to you and them.

8 And when He made known to him the glory, that the rational soul, shut up in an animal body, became one with it, and had no power to do the will, as the angels do.

9 And then the soul — the Holy Spirit entered into Adam, and the Holy Spirit dwelt with the rational soul of Adam; and the soul became one and was strengthened over his flesh, which is brutish, by the power of the Holy Spirit; and the flesh obeyed the soul, and was made ready to do the will of their Creator.

10 And when Adam obeyed the Devil, and heard from him that in which he trusted him concerning him, and believed, and transgressed against his Creator, the grace of the Holy Spirit departed from him, and the Devil ruled over him; and there was with his soul an unclean spirit from the satans, in place of the Holy Spirit, which abides not without will, to do sin.

11 And likewise he did to all that have been born of his seed, generation after generation.

12 And when Christ came for our life, He set the children of Adam free from the unclean spirit which the Devil sets over them, that he might make them do sin.

13 And by baptism there returns to them the Holy Spirit, whom God breathed into the face of Adam; and they overcome the hosts of the Devil, and He gives them power to do the will of God; when He is there, He abides in them, and He dwells in them, if it be they who glorify Him and sanctify Him at the times which He has appointed for them.
