Josippon

Chapter 40

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And if you do not know, you shall not do; do not go to another city, until you shall see that which abides for you; as you go, you seek the work of this world in the district which has no sacrifice, to the other which has a sacrifice.

2 And the day of the life of the Spirit passes from you without work; because you do not know, that you — until the other day — shall not, that you may find the life of your spirit; truly God commanded that He not do the work of this world on the day of rest; therefore that men should forsake everything, and abide in the holy books, which are the spiritual life, and should pray over it without delay, and be occupied in labors, as he does the border of eternal life; for the reading of the books of God cleanses the soul and the body, and they are cleansed from sin, in the portion for His disciples, in the holy Gospel; truly it is a vineyard, and its fathers withered, and every ordinance that bears no fruit and dies is done; and that which bears fruit is it that is cleansed, as He created many, and it was fulfilled.

3 Behold, I have shown you in this word which I have told you in it.

4 It is shown: they shall spring up; men shall be cleansed from all of it, and shall bear fruit, the fruit of eternal life.

5 And he who does not hear the word, and does not speak His books continually, shall be without fruit; and his Father cuts him off from the burden of the Son, as they cut off the branch from the vineyard.

6 And he who speaks the books continually on the holy day, he abides in Christ, as the branch abides in the vineyard; for the cleansing of Christ cleanses him, and sanctifies him from his sin, and causes him to bear — he bears — the fruit of eternal life.

7 Beloved, this — and for the rest which we have mentioned, the hours of the faithful; and in them they shall overcome the satans — which is our going to the house of the Christians in the morning and in the evening, at every time; and we tarry at the time of the Holy One without ceasing, from the, until it is fulfilled.

8 And the hearing of the books of God on the holy day — all of it, and to the rest; if men work continually, He abides, in it the Holy Spirit; and he shall be continually who is born of God; and there shall not be over him the hosts of sin.

9 As John the evangelist said in the epistle: "For everyone who is born of God does not sin; for his seed abides firm, and he cannot sin; for he is born of God."

10 And again he said in it: "He who is born of God keeps himself, and the evil one does not draw near to touch him."

11 And all the faithful who are baptized — they are born of God — as the seed of the Holy Spirit abides firm in them, by which they are born of God; and He keeps them from the evil one and from the hosts; and they cannot sin; but they do good, without their desire; for the Holy Spirit works for them over him; for He is firm in them; and He loves to abide in them; if he keeps these commandments, He abides in them, and keeps them from the evil one; and they have no power to sin, for the Holy Spirit hinders them from sin, and keeps them from the evil one who draws them into the doing of sin.

12 And if they sin by weakness, then the Holy Spirit who dwells in them takes them, and draws them to shame by leave; and likewise if they forsake one of these three commandments, the Holy Spirit forsakes them, and an unclean, evil spirit overcomes them, and draws them to the doing of sin, by work and by violence; and God shall not be in them, if it be so; knowledge: he who is born of God does not sin.

13 As John the apostle said: Behold, what is the greatness from these commandments, by which it shall be that men delight with Christ, the Son of God, in the glory of the kingdom; and he shall be who is born of God.

14 And they are not fat, and are not drunk, and have no weariness; and sweat does not perish as and thirst; and sweat does not perish; for he is he who from of old goes to the work of this world; he goes forth to the house of the Christians; and after its fulfillment, work — he goes to her in the evening; and sweat does not perish.

15 And at the time of the Holy One, if all — all — at every time, its measure is as one hour.

16 From seven and seven hours — and there is not in this again a — in those festivals they shall find the seeking of eternal life in the kingdom of heaven; and God gives a doubled [reward] instead of that which they have left in those two other days, concerning the trading of the holy day; for fatness is grace; it is in His fatness.

17 As He said in the tongue of the prophet: "I who slew, and I make alive; I am He who burns; I who smote, and I heal; whom I heal."

18 But He gives to men a double [reward] instead of that which they have left from the work of this world, as He said in the holy Gospel — He said: "Do not be anxious for what you shall eat, and for what you shall drink; for the nations seek all this; and your Father who is in heaven knows that you are in need of all this, of which you are needy;" but this [seek you] first: the kingdom and the righteousness; and it shall be given to you a double [reward] instead of that which was left from us, for the sake of this seeking, that took the kingdom and the righteousness; and it shall be given to us a double [reward] instead of that which is without the reading of the word of God, first; for He is the Father of us all, and He is He who gives us that which is fitting, and that which is fitting, and that which is fitting; for He knows that we desire — we seek all this; He gives to us the kingdom; and He reckons to us all this; He is He who gives to us; for He is He who, as we are humbled, strengthens us for this temporal world. Behold, beloved: these three commandments, keep them; for they are the salvation of everyone who believes in Christ; and in them they overcome the evil enemy, who is he who prevails over them; and they have no power over him, without these three commandments.

19 For in them the Holy Spirit abides firm in them, and strengthens them against the evil host, and they overcome him, while they are upon the earth in the flesh.

20 And after their going forth from the flesh, He gives them victory over the hosts of the Devil in the air, and saves them — joy and life forever.

21 And the evil one, moreover — when he knew that these three commandments are they, a fence for the faithful, and in them they overcome him — and he proceeds against them in their, and hinders the enemy, and troubles them from them, until he separate them from her, and the Holy Spirit forsakes them, and he prevails over them, and destroys them.

22 And they, moreover, beloved, shall burn in faith; readiness shall be with them, as a wall, that they may receive in it the buckler, the darts of Satan and his spears shall trouble them from them — the commandments.

23 As the apostle Paul said in the epistle to the people of Ephesus: "Take the sword of the Holy Spirit, which is the word of God, in all those [times], and in all, while you pray at every time in the Holy Spirit; and be watchful over this, at every time."

24 I show that the word of God, which abides in it — or which abides upon him — and His glory and His holiness continually, is the sword of the Holy Spirit, which is in truth.
