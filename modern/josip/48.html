Josippon

Chapter 48

1 This which says, in joy: spiritual is he who is in the reading of the books of God, which is the rest of the soul, and life, and your joy: as God said — for man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word which proceeds out of the mouth of God; for the word of God, it is the life of your spirit, as the bread is the life of the flesh.

2 And if there be a man who loves a companion for him who hears the reading of God, God gives the portion of the righteous [in] His reading in the kingdom of heaven; and the portion shall be that which is greater than the portion which takes up the dead; and in the first [place] he is greater than in the first [place], he.

3 For he who takes up the dead shall take up the body which is present [and] shall die.

4 And as for him who reads the word of God on the day of the Sabbath, he shall take up souls from their sins, that they may live to everlasting; and the alms of this are greater than the alms of him who spends upon the orthodox; and He satisfies them, for He is [He who] filled and watered the perishing flesh and the perishing

5 And this, moreover, satisfies the soul and waters [it], which lives forever.

6 How much is he who takes up, who reads on the day of God the word of God, for him who causes [others] to hear the reading.

7 And as for him who does not know the reading, it is fitting for him to go to him who causes [others] to hear the reading, without toil.

8 And if he be not in the city in which he dwells, [in] that [place] where [one] causes [others] to hear the reading, he shall go to another city where [one] causes [others] to hear the reading, as he goes for the sake of a perishing work, from a city which has no market to another city in which there is a market; and he who from the life of the soul shall perish from God, as his flesh perishes, which in the work of the life of his flesh, from the life of this world.

9 This is the reason of the Sabbath.

10 And as for him who does not a spiritual work, he shall be lost from the life everlasting, and from the life of this world.

11 For he who abides in a perishing work, from a spiritual work, on the day of the Sabbath, has destroyed the soul, and shall be lost from the life everlasting; and God shall judge him on the day of the third and in sorrow, in earth and in heaven, as He said: By the hand of; and it was not.

12 If you keep the commandment on the day of the Sabbath, God gives life and good, and much is His power; and He to him always in earth and in heaven; and God gives on two days great things, the which they on that day; and He blesses, and in it.

13 God says: men shall rest on this day; and men shall rest with Him; and the son and the daughter and the manservant and the maidservant shall rest.

14 And concerning this, it is fitting for a man that he work not, for a woman, the work of the world, and that he not diminish the glory of God and the holiness of God, but that he go with the woman to God and to the house of the Christians; and he shall rest in the voice of God; and so shall he do for our sons and our daughters and our servants and our maidservants and the stranger who dwells in the house; and he shall not make them [do] the work of the world. And as for him who does not rest, he [is] in the hearing of the word of God alone; and God shall seek blood from his hand; for he is a good rest for the soul alone; but it is fitting for him that he be grieved concerning their former state, as a soul; and He shall make good to them in the being joined to the glory of God and to the holiness and to the hearing of His word; and they shall not be made in the work of: the work of the flesh, except that which they keep for food alone.

15 And as for that which is food and drink, which the flesh cannot [do] without it, they shall do this in the night, before the glory and the holiness.

16 And if they are able, they shall do this on the day before; for God them in this until the evening.

17 For He says: I rested, and men rested; and our sons and our servants and our maidservants and the stranger, who is his house, rested; and our asses rested; and the rest of the asses — which He says — is the rest of His flesh; that is: our asses are the soul.

18 And He commanded that [a man] rest not with food and drink on the day of the Sabbath, which is the day of rest, that the being joined to the reading every day, both of them; and the righteous and joy shall; for He is [He] who, when He was hungry, for the souls in hunger, and shall from life.
