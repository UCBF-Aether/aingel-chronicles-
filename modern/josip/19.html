Josippon

Chapter 19

1 The word of the incarnation: the weakness hid from the Devil the mystery of the divine word, as He said in; and He took hold, and was grieved, and He said: "My soul is sorrowful, even to death."

2 And He rebuked His disciples concerning their slumber, and asks them to watch with Him; and He said: "For the spirit indeed is willing, and the flesh is weak"; and He prayed in.

3 And our Lord said to them: "Do not grieve for Me"; the cup of death which was is not Peter, His disciple and beloved; and they said concerning Him: my Lord; and He was wroth upon him, and surnamed him Satan, and said to him: "Get behind Me, Satan"; and He was wroth and rebuked him thus, for he had said to Him: that You should be crucified and die.

4 And our Lord — if He had come, He is — it is not concerning, as He said: For for this hour I came; and if the sin fall not away, my order is, and from in truth it shall be hidden.

5 And if it die, much fruit shall grow; we have shown that He is: if it die not, it abides for His law.

7 And therefore He was wroth against Peter, when he said to Him: Lord, that You should die.

8 And in the knowledge of the third day, that He might hide His divinity from the Devil, He prayed in the garden that the cup of death might pass from Him; and He said: "Not my will, but Your will, be done" — [He did this] as a likeness to Satan, that he might think Him a weak man who loves not death; and He prayed much; and it became sweat like water and blood. And by the works of weakness which He revealed, the mystery — this great word — was hidden from the Devil, until, from the much that He revealed of weakness, there descended to Him angels from heaven, who strengthen Him; for in wrath with great fear; and they knew not the cause of the mystery, what it is.

9 And when the Devil knew and doubted concerning His divinity, and his heart, he brought priests, and seized them, as He stood in prayer:

10 Saying this word which we have mentioned; and they condemned Him to death.

11 And afterward they spat in His face, and they mocked Him and smote Him, and delivered Him to Pilate, the governor of Rome; and they said concerning Him that He had said: "I am the King of the Jews"; and the governor, for the hosts of Caesar — our Lord, King of Rome.

12 And they answered, they stripped Him, concerning Adam and his seed — the seeking and the affliction that are fitting for them; and they mocked Him; and afterward they stripped Him of His garments, concerning Adam — the that was fitting for him from God; He sought that He might become.

13 And He was naked, and, and a mocking was made upon Him.

14 As God said: "Behold, He was made as one of us"; and the mocking, because it is fitting for them that they should upon Him to the ages.

15 My Lord Christ — concerning Him, the mocking that is fitting for Him, when was made God — the nakedness that is fitting over Adam to the ages.

16 When they stripped Him of His garments, and clothed Him with a robe of scarlet, in place of the garments with which Adam and Eve had been clothed, when they transgressed.

17 And they set upon His head a crown of thorns, in place of the thorns and the which He commanded the earth to bring forth for them when they sinned; the thorn — His head shows that he is their; for the thorn is.

18 For concerning the chosen tree Adam [fell]: He said: "The tree of the Cross [bare] nakedness"; and they suspended His hands and His feet between heaven and earth.

19 And He was naked upon the tree, as Adam was naked on account of the tree; and they suspended His hands upon the tree on account of Eve, when she stretched forth her hands to the tree and took of its fruit.

20 And they suspended His feet upon the tree on account of Eve, when she went to the tree to take of its fruit.

21 And they again; and He was naked, crucified, in place of the affliction that is fitting over Adam, as we have shown at the first: that all in Him, that is fitting over Adam and his seed to the ages.

22 For in wrath they sinned like the Devil; and when the Devil saw, by this great weakness, that He is a man, and he came to frighten Him with terror, he His blood, and he and brought down His spirit into Sheol.

23 And when he drew near to Him, the sun was darkened over all the earth, and darkness came; and He cried, saying to the thief on His right hand: "Truly, you shall be with Me in Paradise."

24 And he saw that He was, and he feared, and that he should flee, and he drew not near; for he knew Him; and then God hid His divinity from him; and He cried, as one afraid of him: "My God, My God, why have You forsaken Me" — the words which he heard.

25 And again He said — after the custom, thus, to him who dies — that he should concerning the affliction; and when He said this word, the Devil upon Him, and saw in His face, as the afflictions were done by all the seed of Adam.

26 And when he drew near to Him, fearing — then the breath of the incarnation expired by His will; it was not the Devil who it; and when the Devil desired to seize His breath, He seized him by the power of His divinity, to slay him in place of his slaying; for His breath — the of Christ, which was separated from His body — became one [portion] of His divinity, as He revealed thus by the power of the divinity which was one with it. And when the Devil desired, and was not able: then the earth shook with joy, that concerning the perishing of the enemy; and the rocks were split, and the graves were opened, and many of the holy saints that were dead arose.

27 This which the holy Gospel says shows us that in that hour Satan was bound.

28 And from the of the fear which came upon him, the breaths — all the hosts of Satan — the many holy breaths [came forth] from their, and entered into their houses, into the graves — into their houses, into the graves — and arose at that time.

29 And when you hear that the breath of the incarnation of Christ was separated from His body, and it is one in its being — the Son was not [separated] so that His body should be from the divinity; for His body is from the Holy and from Mary the Virgin; and He is anointed by the being of the Holy Spirit, God; for His Holy Spirit was with Him, and was not separated from Him at all; and He showed, by the firmness of the Holy Spirit — he is the host — after death, from His body: for He is the mystery of death; and He is from His; and He is the Dead on our account; for He was wrapped in in the, as He was [wrapped] in swaddling clothes in the tomb.
