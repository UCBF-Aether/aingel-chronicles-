Josippon

Chapter 80

1 Behold, the Book of Creation: and Christ was, and he took.

2 The LORD, and as his seed you shall see him concerning Christ, as he said.

3 The apostle in the message to the men of Galatia, and he willed that they should know: God has revealed to me.

4 by which Eve transgressed, and she took sorrow and the curse of man upon the sinners.

5 Joy and blessing by the word of the angel Gabriel to her: 'Rejoice, you full one, for the LORD is with you, you among women, for '.

6 and she bore for the virgin, and likewise.

7 Adam the house of our Lord: Christ was crucified.

8 He said Eve took; Christ rejoiced Adam from the tree, and he fell from the garden; likewise Christ the garden.

9 she bore, for as he came forth from the womb of the virgin on the day of his birth, and the seal of her virginity was not broken, and he came forth.

10 As the angel announced to them the birth, so they announced to them.

11 Name.

12 And this that I have mentioned is a sign to you concerning their rebuke.

13 To Abraham, until the birth of our Lord Jesus Christ.

14 from Adam until the birth of Abraham.

15 and hundred.

16 The hundred of the life of Abraham: hundred.

17 There remained, until the birth of Christ, hundred; hundred.

18 God made known by a sign.

19 To him be glory and power and honor and praise, forever and ever. Amen.

20 heavenly holy.

21-35
