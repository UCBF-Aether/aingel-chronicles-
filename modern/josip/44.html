Josippon

Chapter 44

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 A discourse of haste, concerning the denial of the day of the Sabbath and its completion:, friend — God shall enlighten the eyes of your heart by the light of His holy, He comforted, that you may perceive the mysteries of the kingdom.

2 That which I commanded you, that I should declare to you the completion of the day of holiness and glory: know that it is all the work of the day of the Sabbath; it is the day of God; and its name among all the children of baptism; for all of them call it Kyriake, in the tongue of the Greeks; its interpretation: the day of God, in which He rose from the dead; and concerning this we were commanded, that we should rejoice in it with spiritual songs, in every week, and that we should not do in it the work of this world — the hosts — but that there be in it, in our gatherings of assembly, [works] for God; for it is His day; and in it [let us] work the works of God, in which the believers inherit the kingdom of God forever, and the life which shall not pass away from being saved.

3 And the works of this world — forever in them there is revealed the life forever; you are the life which passes away, and the world passes away.

4 God said: Work for the life of this world which passes away.

5 Two days, and on the day of the Sabbath, work for Me alone; for it is My day, and I will give you your joy instead of what you worked for Me — My kingdom forever, and My life forever, and My flower which does not fade; and I will bless you in your work in the six days which you work in it; and I will not put you into the in which you are.

6 that it may be for you on My day [a recompense] for what you worked for Me in it.

7 And when you have worked for Me your desires — the three lives, and the three portions, earthly and heavenly — and as you worked for Me [in] that which is, I separated you from my peoples and from my and from my joy forever; and I grieved you in the work of the flesh, which you made it; it is better than my work; and your food, which consumes you, the which shall be for you on My day, from the of which consumes you — or in the suffering of — I tried you in it, that you should give all your labor.

8 Or they steal your, or by they your.

9 Or destroy your seed, or slay that which was tried from your friend; and you shall be grieved, and you shall be sad on earth and in heaven; for I am He who enriches, and I make poor and I kill and I make alive.

10 Friend, this is the interpretation of the word of God concerning the day of the Sabbath: that it is the day in which it is not fitting that they should do in it a work which is not without work.

11 And the work is: His glory — of God — and His holiness, always; and the hearing of His holy books all the day long: this is the work of God which is fitting that they should do on the day of the Sabbath, which is the day of God; and by this work the believers live a good life, which is on earth and in heaven.

12 And concerning this it is fitting for the believers that they keep the commandment, and rejoice [in] the work of this world, and rest from him in this.

13 And many of the believers neglect in this from their works forever; they neglect God in this light; and their is greater than the of those who do the work of this world; for they do the work of this world, for they take their food in the life which passes away, which presses upon it, while they are in this world.

14 And as for those who work for God, they gain the kingdom forever and the life which does not pass away; God did not neglect on the day of the Sabbath [to turn] from earthly gain [and] loss, to the gain — the gain forever, heavenly.

15 And if we neglect the earthly works and do not the heavenly works, behold, there are lost from us the two lives together.

16 And they do the work of loss — gain by — from us, and our gain, neither in this world nor in the heavens.

17 And their — of him who neglects on the day of the Sabbath [to turn] from the work of this world, and does not the work of heaven on the day of — is greater than the of those who do in it the work of this light.

18 The world, and their perdition is greater than the perdition of those who — for they did not honor the day of God, and the days of this world, precious from them; for God, for the honor of His own day — His blessing — the heavenly [believers], that they should do in it the work of heaven, that their gain in it may be greater than the gain of the days [of this world]; for their gain is in two days — loss and.

19 And their in this day increases always, forever; and the honor of this day and its exaltation is greater than [that of] two days, on account of its heavenly work; and he who gained the gain forever on the days — the seventh — and gained not the gain of God on the day of God: behold, he sought not the day of God [to be] a help, from him who worked in it the work of this world — loss; for it is a very small gain — the gain of this world; and Paradise — he gained not in it forever, nor in heaven; behold, truly he sought and made the two days of this world precious [away] from him.

20 And his is great; for he sought not the day of God, which He commanded that they should honor it.

21 And God increases [him] who sought not; and him who honors Me, him will I honor; and he who dishonors God, and does not to Him work on the day of God, shall be dishonored on earth and in heaven; and he who honors God on the day, and does to Him work, God shall honor on earth and in heaven.

22 Know, friend, that all the of the books of the commandment, and all of them from this commandment; and all of them concerning it — strive, and be diligent in it, that you may be saved from and may find the good life, which is on earth and in heaven.
