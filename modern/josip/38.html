Josippon

Chapter 38

1 The cherubim, who have many eyes, and the seraphim, who have six wings, glorify continually, without silence; and when they say it, the priests give the holy greeting which is in the house of the Christians, to all.

2 Holy, holy, LORD; heaven and earth are full of Your holy glory, all of them. Those who pray in the house of the Christians to God, the Holy Trinity, by this manner of the Masses — and He also sanctifies them from their sins.

3 As Isaiah the prophet said: "For He who glorifies Me, while I glorify, and He who sanctifies Me, while I sanctify" — and by this Mass the faithful are sanctified from their sins.

4 For Mass in the Syriac tongue, and in the Hebrew — its interpretation is gladness; and it is a Mass of joy.

5 And the Mass which is called pure, it is, truly, for this interpretation again, that it may be made known: for by the Mass the faithful are sanctified from their sins.

6 And after this Mass they shall be saved, their heads.

7 And he says thus: "We ask You, our God — we, Your servants"

8 A company of those who are not changed, as the Holy Spirit descended upon us.

9 And upon the garden an offering, that it may sanctify them and cleanse their body and their blood, for Jesus Christ, Your Son; beloved is he who loves this great gift, which shall be for all men who stand in the house of the Christians; for the Spirit of God, the Holy One, descended upon them, before it descended upon the offering.

10 For the priests make an end upon us, and upon this offering.

11 He is He who descends upon the people, before the offering.

12 And as He sanctifies the earthly bread of straw, and makes it heavenly, that does not pass away.

13 Likewise He sanctifies all the people who stand before Him, in the sight of them upon them, and cleanses them from their sins that perish, and makes them pure to eternal life; for the fruit of the Holy Spirit is a spirit of cleansing, and He is it.

14 As we have interpreted for you: He is He who cleanses a man, when He descends upon him, from his sins, altogether.

15 And if it be as a boundless sea, which cannot be stayed, that it may cleanse from them the gift of the offering — and if it does not draw near — as He cleanses everyone upon whom He descends, in the holy depth, from all sin.

16 And after this the Holy Spirit descends upon the holy offerings.

17 The priests say: "Our God, righteousness is in Your being, as You have sanctified this offering by the sight of the Holy Spirit upon it; likewise sanctify us also, from iniquity that is hidden and that is manifest."

18 And He opened from us the reckoning and the thought which does not please Your goodness; and we are cleansed utterly — our souls and our body and our spirit and our thoughts and our hearts — that with a pure heart and a bright soul and pure lips he may say, in Mary, Our Father — from us he is blessed and sanctified.

19 As Your only Son Jesus Christ taught us. And He said to us: When you pray, say thus — let the people say: "Our Father who are in heaven, hallowed be Your name; Your kingdom come; and Your will be done; as in heaven, so also on earth; our bread of sustenance, which is needful for us, give us.

20 Forsake iniquity and envy; no, deliver us and loose him who has sinned, for us, and; lead us not into the temptation of sin; neither forget us; and deliver us from all evil, by Christ our Lord. Amen."

21 He does not will at all: great is this season of this crucifixion.

22 For Christ — of God, a little while was present with them; and the deadly ones stand before Him, while they say to His Father: Our Father who are in heaven, reveal that whoever dwells with Him in the exalted kingdom, and they ask Him that He may be delivered, that He may dwell within them, that the hearing of their mouths may be heard, and the striving; and those are the enemies, by their works.

23 And again they ask Him that His kingdom may come to them, which is the Holy Spirit, that He may drive forth from them their enemies, who make them work for sin.

24 And again they are able to ask that the desire be fulfilled in them on earth, as in heaven among the angels; and the desire in His glory and His holiness continually; for the angels glorify Him and sanctify Him without ceasing; for He remembered that they fell not, as men and hosts fell; and likewise He upon those who are from Him, in the part of Ipile and Imhayaw, that they may glorify Him and sanctify Him continually, without weariness, and may fulfill their desire which is in Him, in the will, continually.

25 And concerning that which they say: "Our needful bread, give us today."

26 That which is called, In the world which comes, in heaven: for the needy ones are in the world which comes; which He said: He is the need of the angels, the glory of God and His holiness and His remembrance; and there comes that which is the need of the angels — the glory and the holiness and the praise; for the angels have not this work, in that they live, and it is fulfilled for them.

27 And likewise the faithful, if they were in heaven: the savor of their living house, like them.

28 And He is their need in heaven; and therefore they ask God, while they say: "Your glory and Your holiness and Your offering — this is our bread of rest in heaven — give us today upon earth; for behold, if we are not fed today upon earth, we shall seek a savor in heaven."

29 And therefore they ask Him that He lead them not into temptation which hinders them from the good.

30 But He saves them from evil, by Jesus Christ, for there is no savior besides Him.

31 And by it the priests give, while he says: "No, I have sought You, O holy Father, Nirir, who loves goodness; and lead us not into temptation, and do not hide our sins; they enter upon us, hasten, save us from works that do not profit — from her thought and her shouting and her noise and her drunkenness and the confusion of him who is amazed; and cast away from us, and all his troubles that are sown.

32 Save us, and hide from us Your wonders that were added to sins; and save us by Your holy power, by Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen."
