Josippon

Chapter 12

1 When they die in repentance, they go in their souls into the garden of delight, until they become according to the number of the host who fell with the devil; the LORD shall bring their spirits into their bodies, and shall raise them from the dead, and shall lift them up into the high order, the heavenly kingdom, above all the angels.

2 From which the devil and his host fell; and He shall reign with Christ in the place where He is; and in it [are] the of men, which He took from them, into the kingdom that does not pass away, and delight that does not end, and life without death.

3 And because of that great kingdom our Lord Jesus Christ was crucified, that He might men by His hidden power; and because of it, men and the host of Satan envy Him, and fight against Him, night and day, and weary Him with the works of the flesh, from the time of glory and sanctification, until death reaches us in that time, before the reckoning and the departing from that kingdom, as they fell; and [is] upon them in the judgment of the ages.

4 And therefore the LORD says to us, Watch; and He commands us that we do not hearken to them when they weary us in the times and seasons with glorifyings and, and draw [us] away from repentance; for we do not know: death shall be in that time, before the repentance [ends].

5 And He said in the Gospel: the mornings — know and understand: when He knows, in the evening time the order shall come from; and He does not leave them to in it.

6 So also you, be watchful at all times.

7 For at a time when you do not know, and do not think, He shall come to you.

8 A sign makes known to us: for death [is] like a thief; a man does not know when it shall come to him.

9 And if he is wearied from the glory of the LORD, and His, one time, and death reaches in it,

10 And it is fitting that he be watchful always, and guard [his] soul, as the master of the house guards himself from the thief, who does not know when he shall come to him. This, thus, [I have spoken] to you a few words, and to you in it an exposition, that you may read with fewness of understanding, and know in fewness, without trouble and without weariness, and repeat His name to this place many times, until it enter into their knowledge, and they understand [it]; for they are as children who suck milk, because of the fewness of from food, which they eat in fullness.

11 And you — now I not, neither repeat to you, the exposition; and I to you according to the order, by the help of the Holy Spirit who is [in me], what I have mentioned to you from this [matter]: they are healed in it, the [believers] and the teachers and the knowers of the holy books. And this indeed is as this work which I have mentioned to you: it is the mystery of the LORD, His own, hidden from all creation, from angels and from men.

12 As the apostle Paul said, in the epistle to the men of Ephesus: He said, To me, who am the least of all the saints, was given His grace — to the nations concerning Christ, who has no — and to enlighten all [men].

13 What is the order of this mystery, which was hidden from the creation, in the being of the LORD, who all [things]?

14 That now it may be known to the principalities and to the powers that are in the heavens, through the Church, by the wisdom of the LORD, which is manifold in its joining.

15 And says: the order of this mystery — the orders which to you now — that the LORD ordered that He should be condemned for men in it, by a judgment without suffering; and that is the entering-in of Christ, and His crucifixion.

16 And he said that it was hidden in the bosom of the LORD, and [with] the chiefs of the angels.

17 The waters did not know it, until it appeared to them from the children of the Church, to whom was given the wisdom of the LORD in the holy kingdom.

18 And he said again in this epistle: For He revealed [it] to me, and showed me, as you wrote — as He left all that we should do; and my thought was amazed in this place concerning Christ, who did not know: He was born, the former One, from the living, that now it is revealed to the holy ones, to His apostles and to the prophets, in the of holiness.

19 He makes known that the foundation of the wisdom of this mystery was hidden from all the fathers, the prophets — to whom it was revealed, as Christ revealed to His apostles, the former disciples, and the Church, whom He called prophets in truth, because of their knowledge of this mystery, which was revealed to them in the holy kingdom; and in it they became more glorious than the prophets and the who were before them.

20 As our Lord said, and our, Jesus Christ:

21 For many prophets and kings desired to see what you see, and did not see; and to hear what you hear, and did not hear. And as for them: blessed are your eyes that [see], and your ears that hear.

22 And Paul also the apostle said, writing concerning this mystery, in the epistle to the men of.

23 He said: I rejoice in my sufferings for you, and I fulfill the of the suffering of Christ in my flesh, for the sake of His order — which is the Church of the LORD; but they are the dwellings of the believers, in which; I to the order of the LORD, which He gave me for you, that I should fulfill the word of the LORD — the mystery which was hidden from before the ages, before men were created.

24 And now it is made manifest to His holy ones: the LORD shall reveal His name upon His glory in this place.

25 For this [matter], behold, it was hidden from before the creation of the world, and [hidden] from the prophets and the holy ones and the angels.

26 And as to the cause — concerning what was it hidden, as we have mentioned? For it was hidden from Satan and his host, as there was hidden the soul of and Eve, in the serpent.

27 And Paul the apostle, writing, reveals to us this, in the epistle to the men of, saying: The depth of wisdom — we speak to the wise; and their, which passes away; and the wisdom of the rulers of this [world], which passes away.

28 The wisdom of the LORD we speak, which was hidden, and which He revealed before the world was created, which the LORD prepared for our glory; and the rulers of the world did not know it — for if they had known the Lord of glory, they would not have crucified [Him].

29 The apostle makes known here all the good which we have mentioned upon — that it is a heavenly — this which we told you from: Christ, and His crucifixion, which came in to the LORD.

30 And this order is of the LORD.

31 From before the world was created; as He said: that all of them — Adam fell — by the Son He should raise them up to His glory.

32 And therefore the apostle said: For it was, this [matter], from before the world was created, for our glory. And he makes known that the devil and his host, the guides of this world, did not know this mystery; for the LORD hid from them this mystery, that He might take them.

33 Justice.

34 And therefore he said: They would have known the wisdom which the LORD ordered, if they had not crucified the LORD; for the devil him.

35 For He became flesh; and He knew that He had become flesh and become man, that He might hide the divinity from Satan, until he should be troubled over Him, and they should follow with Him — that man — from Him, [for] the deliverance from the slaying, from; but he supposed that He was a weak angel, and a breath of, and said that [He is] the ruler of this world.

36 I am he, the devil and his host.

37 For shall his; know that he said to them once, in the way — who is called the devil, the ruler of this world.

38 And there is for us in its time; for the apostle Paul reveals in the epistle many times.

39 For the rulers of this world are Satans.

40 For he said in the epistle to the men of Ephesus — — writing concerning the LORD, and with firmness of power: Put on the whole armor of the LORD, the standing of the guides of Satan; for your wrestling is not with flesh and blood, but with the rulers of darkness, the guides of evil, which are under heaven.

41 This word He plainly revealed concerning them, that they are the rulers and the of this world; for they are his kings, when they reigned over Adam and his seed; and their hearing of this mystery, which was hidden from the high ones and from the low ones — the LORD revealed from all, in Christ, in the holy kingdom; for the holy kingdom seeks every work.

42 And the knowledge of the LORD shall be known, as the apostle said: For that which we have mentioned — from the creation of the angels, and the fall of the devil and his host —

43 The LORD did not mention to Moses, in the Book of Creation, concerning, that a little, O Israel, and the abundance of their love for the worship of created things; know that He — for — mentioned the creation of the angels and the order; and a word from: this — Let us make man in our image, and our likeness, and our face.

44 And He said again: Behold, Adam is become as one of us.

45 And He said again: Behold, — He confounded their tongues from — that He said these words to the angels, and made them creators with Him; and from they deny the kingdom and the holy kingdom, when their mystery appeared.

46 There are none to whom He said these words but the angels whom He mentioned, that He them from before Him; and therefore the LORD left the mention of them — the angels — and did not mention, as [He did] concerning Him, in the heavens, creature, that they might not share with Him in the worship, that they might not make a which pertains to the Son and to the holy kingdom.

47 And so also He said by the tongue of Hosea the prophet: I created the host of heaven, and I do not make you know [them] in it, O Israel, that you do not draw near to them in worship.
