Josippon

Chapter 46

1 And the dusk of the day of Friday: they loosed [Him] against Him in death, and delivered Him up to him who did not know, the governor of Rome; and they spoke concerning Him, that he wants to take the kingdom for himself; and they subjected [Him] to the hosts of the governor over Him in the assembly; their word [was] until they scourged Him, and they mocked Him, as they mock, until they cried out among kings.

2 And again they crucified Him upon the tree, and pierced His hands and His feet; and while they mocked Him concerning the everlasting kingdom.

3 And they were troubled in toil and in silence, and were able, and they made themselves ready in this with wrath, until He died for us by His will, in the thoughts of the day of Friday; and after the remembrance of the body, until the going down, they buried Him in the sepulcher, as the dead, on the day of the eve.

4 And on the day of the Sabbath the dead were sought, and He brought up all the souls which were in the toil of Sheol, and He led them into Paradise, and He gave them rest in everlasting joy: the soul of Adam, and the soul of all his seed which was born from the earth, forty and four and two hundred forty and four years; He rested from his toil, and He gave rest also to all his seed from toil forever. This is the day of rest in truth, for Him, and for all who keep watch, Adam, those who were formed, as He showed him, and their Creator; and not as they in the creation of creations; and not as the rest on the first day. Behold, make you able with the ears of your heart our knowledge in the midst of this toil and the rest, rest from the toil of rest.

5 And woe to him who is an offense to him to whom it is fitting: for him who does not rest on this day — utterly, from his offense — for him who does not rest on the first day; for he is [one] who, when he was made, [there was] offense and destruction upon the soul which does not rest with him on the day which is spent in it, and does not rest the rest which is in truth.

6 Blessed [is] the beloved; for the man who does not rest on this day is cut off from his people, to Christ.

7 And a father of witnesses shall fall; and blessed [is] he who stands: the rest is not this which you blame from His work, alone; those who have believed the Sabbaths and for them: and they read the Holy Scriptures on the day of the Sabbath, all of them — this is its work; he shall take the spirit of life, and the everlasting possession, which is better than the work of the world, in which he takes life, life.

8 For God says: the Rest — this is it — the flesh does not rest, but [it is] the rest of the soul.

9 For He, on this day, rested the souls which were shut up in Sheol, from their toil, forever in it.

10 And in it it is fitting for the believers that their souls rest in the glory of God and in His holiness, and that they hear His word every day; for I have written for you in the book as a likeness; Simeon — for the believers — that God made the soul of man as the angels, and his flesh as the beasts; and if the soul sins — glory and holiness and the hearing of the word of God — it dies from the life of God forever, as the flesh dies from life, destruction; if it sins, spoiled and spent, in the rest of the flesh, as the flesh of the beasts; and the word of God, it is the rest of the soul, as it is fitting.

11 And concerning this God says: Make you two days for the life of the flesh of the beast, as it is gathered together.

12 He sought for life: of asses [was] for Him; make you for the life of the soul which lives, as it is gathered together: an enclosure for the soul; and this shall be that which is better than this — it is gathered together; He sought for the ass; for He is [He] who, when He gathered together for the ass, He sought, and did not gather together for the soul — an enclosure — from it; and he did not take [away]; the ass shall live from his; and they shall not be able to live.

13 And if He gather together an enclosure for the soul first, [there is] life; and He is [He] who is able, according to the measure that there is life, as; it is gathered together; He sought for the ass.

14 And concerning this our God says: He sought first my kingdom and my righteousness; and take you no thought for all which is desired from the work of the world.

15 He is able — that which is joined to His glory, to His holiness and to the hearing of His word; the of his soul [is] life everlasting; God gives to him who wills from the work of the world; these and He in the being joined to His glory and His holiness; for the bread is in His hand, and He is upon all work; and He is [He] who in; and He gives a hand to him who is commanded, to him.
