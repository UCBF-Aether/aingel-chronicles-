Josippon

Chapter 1

1 In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit.

2 The writing of God, the discourse which he composed.

3 Abba Sawiros the holy

4 his love, which is with the exposition of the teaching.

5 The first discourse, concerning the exposition of the being of the LORD and His union: the beloved Jesus Christ, the Son of God, shall enlighten the eyes of your heart in light.

6 peace

7 To the only Son and blessing

8 while he inquires into the faith

9 what I asked the exposition

10 understanding the first

11 he remembered the good without the LORD; thus his remembrance, but

12 That the LORD: the word which he says — the Son of God before them

13 Mary the Virgin was that she should bear; and before Adam, that which is of Mary.

14 He was born of him from before: the Son who is forever with the LORD

15 Of peace: he is light forever; light

16 That he is light, and their union is from this likeness.

17 He was born: light from light — his birth

18 The Son, light from light: Jesus Christ, the Son of God, the Only-begotten, who was begotten of the Father before the ages.
