Josippon

Chapter 4

1 The soul and the angels, and cattle and wild beasts, and creeping things.

2 The Holy Spirit, as he says: the Holy Spirit a work — he created.

3 the exposition and a book the heart: the book of the word of the LORD the likeness.

4 God said: Let us make man in our image and in our likeness.

5 God said heaven and earth and birds and beasts: this he said by his word and by his Spirit.

6 The Book says: and God saw that it was good; and God made.

7 God said: Let there be light; and God saw the light.

8 And it a likeness a mystery heaven earth.

9 to the LORD and his union: the Son, and by his Spirit, for he is his likeness, as his Father.

10 A word — and his word is the Son, who is born from the heart — always, without cutting and without separation; so the Son who is forever is the word of the LORD.

11 men, a likeness a spirit for men.

12 for the Son, by the Spirit holy, as he says.

13 The LORD the Son: the word from the heart always.

14 that God and he life.

15 God the Son the LORD and his likeness: we have not seen the LORD, nor his likeness.

16 the LORD the only Son, and he was incarnate, and the Holy Spirit the word, the Son.
