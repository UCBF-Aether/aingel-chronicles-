Josippon

Chapter 64

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 When Jesus Christ — and when he saw — returned from him, he desired in baptism that upon him, that he might fulfil the wisdom which he ordained for the salvation of the world.

2 And he cried with a loud voice, saying: "My God, my God, behold me; and hear me: do not forsake me."

3 And when Satan heard, he said: This — who is sent again; and there is none that is sent from me; and with might the powers are sent.

4 And he came by the power the breath and, which he gives — one he fulfilled upon my shadow — he suffered

5 which he slew.

6 And when he, for his redemption, bound him in the depth of Sheol, being bound with fetters which to Adam and to his seed, who was to destroy them by — them to him; and he brought them up from his depths, and he sent them forth from him by the loosing of the power of his slaying; and he pierced death, which without; for they pierced his side after death, and there flowed forth from him blood and water.

7 And again he restored the breath into his nostrils, in the days of his life; and

8 And thus he taught them, that he might bring him who seeks their faith.

9 Into the holy Church; and they pray to the Father through the Son, that he may cast out from them the unclean spirit which dwelt in his house from the day of his birth; and he turns his face toward the east, and renounces Satan and all his hosts, and the unclean spirit which was with him from the bosom of the devil goes out from him; and again he turns his face toward the east, and believes in Christ; and they pray to the Father through the Son, that the Spirit may descend upon the water; and they baptize him in it, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Spirit; and his disciples appeared to him, and he made them beautiful — this is the baptism.

10 This work saves those who believe: my God — Satan, the unclean spirits, the angels of death, who guard all that is born — says: Holy. And the Spirit dwells in him; this sanctified breath of the LORD was into the face from when he created him, and it was parted from him when he transgressed.

11 The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ is for them that believe in him in baptism; and their distance — he dwells in them; he saves them and guards them from all.
