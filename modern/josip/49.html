Josippon

Chapter 49

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And concerning this, God commanded that we eat and drink and rejoice on the Sabbath day in full; and He commanded concerning the flesh — its name is the flesh of beasts, mingled — and He appointed a day for us, for the day; and it is confirmed by this word of God: He appointed for the flesh, food and drink; goes forth for the work of the Spirit on the Sabbath day; and He appointed upon it, in rest.

2 Behold, I have loved that which I have revealed to you; and take heed in your heart, and teach them the name of the Faith, and to others; for it is the foundation of the life which is eternal, and of the life of this world; and they rooted it in [the two] in earth and in heaven; and [there is] deliverance from judgment, and rest from the two works of toil, and riches from the hidden treasures in earth and in heaven.

3 For you know that which is written in [the book of] the Kings: that God appeared to Solomon, and said to him: "Ask of Me that which I shall give you."

4 And Solomon said to Him: "I have asked of You that You would give me a wise heart, that I may judge rightly in the midst of this great people."

5 The Scripture says: And God was pleased at this word; for he asked of Him a heavenly work, which abides, and he did not ask of Him an earthly work, which perishes.

6 And God said to him: "Because you have asked neither length of days, nor abundance of riches, nor the soul of your enemies, but you have asked wisdom — and it is the work which pleases Me — behold, I give you the wisdom which you have asked of Me, which I have given to none before you, nor after you."

7 "And I will add to you, above this, that which you have not asked of Me: length of days, and abundance of riches, and the soul of your enemies."

8 Behold, I have loved how Solomon, when he desired the work which abides alone, and did not seek the work which perishes, God gave him the abiding [thing] which he had asked, and added to him, above this, the perishing work which he had not asked; for He knew that which he desired.

9 And likewise He says in the Gospel: "Seek My kingdom and My righteousness, and I will add to you that which you desire of the work of this world, which is food and drink and clothing."

10 Likewise everyone who labors for the glory of God and His holiness, and hears His word on the Sabbath day in full, shall obtain in this kingdom of heaven: God shall give that [portion], and shall grant him good and rest from all that wearies, from the work of this world — food and drink and clothing — as He did to Solomon. And he who does not labor thus for the life of his soul, from the eternal kingdom, but inherits eternal judgment — God shall afflict him in this world, and shall weary him all the days of his life, and shall deprive him of the two rests together, in heaven and in earth; for God is mighty and able.

11 And he who labors for the day of the LORD, and shall be exalted with the LORD on the day of His resurrection in full — he also shall abide with the LORD on the day of the resurrection; he shall be exalted on the day of glory and honor, as it was done on earth on the Sabbath day. And he who does not labor for the glory of God and His holiness, and does not hear His word on the Sabbath day, shall go forth from the fathers of God and from His glory, as the Devil fell when he was withheld from glory and holiness on the Sabbath day: look, they are in it — they fell; and in it shall fall all who do not labor for the glory of God and His holiness, as they fell.

12 And behold, I have revealed to you in the book of Kasit, the spiritual writing, that for those who are weak in work, their portion is appointed upon them on the Sabbath day; and it is appointed for glory and for holiness at the times, twice on the Sabbath day: the morning, and two and a hundred and four, and the evening, within, upon the day which is read, all the day; for when they are steadfast [refraining] from the perishing work, it is fitting for them that they be joined to prayer at the times, within, upon the day which is read.

13 And on the two days also, the others — it is not fitting for them save for eating; it is not fitting for them save for eating, in the book — as I have shown you in the book of Helit of the host: they shall pray wherever they are, according to the measure of their strength and their knowledge, if they do not their work; and God listened to them.

14 I have loved: behold, I have shown you the glory of the Sabbath day; and in it is the life of the faithful, and their joy which is in earth and in heaven is upon them, and work is in it; and teach all whom you have found, [to them], the faithful; and behold, I have revealed to you the reason of the fast: one day, Wednesday and Friday, in the peace of God. Amen.
