Josippon

Chapter 18

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 His divinity from him; and He hid Himself again from all men, and His fame was not made manifest, that He is from above, and He was not — until He had thirty-four years, like Adam at the time when he was created.

2 For Adam was created a complete man, like the Son of thirty-four years; know this: Adam, in the Hebrew tongue, its interpretation is "man".

3 And when He was thirty-four years, He was baptized by John in the river Jordan; and the Holy Spirit descended upon Him in the likeness of a dove.

4 And a voice came from heaven — that is: This is My Son, who is Mine, whom I love; and in Him I am well pleased — which says: This is My beloved Son, in Him I am well pleased. And the Devil knew again; he knew completely.

5 And then He went forth into the wilderness; He went forth thirty days and thirty nights.

6 And when the Devil saw Him, he and doubted concerning Him.

7 He supposed that He is a man; and when He had completed thirty days and thirty nights, He hungered from much: these things He said; so did He, and He was not.

8 These — He believed; He hungered; their appearance was changed, and the garden was not.

9 And then God knew his thought, that he knew — he knew that He was not; then He said: He hungered — as it says in the holy Gospel: for He fasted; He hungered afterward. And the Devil drew near to Him when he heard this; he drove from, and he doubted and drew near to Him, the Devil, when he heard that He hungered; and he approached Him, that he might entice him — if He is the Son of God. And God did not reveal to him His divinity in one word; He was giving him from a place, that He might show him that He is a man; He longed for the Law, like all who are under the Law; and He gave to the place.

10 And the Devil returned and doubted, and said: This man — in truth!

11 And God said concerning him, as He said formerly concerning Israel:

12 That He is My Son — in a far likeness and — that He is He, the Perfect; and Israel is toward his head, the pretext of his thought; and you are the head of the pretext, and you are their; that he supposed, in the pretext of the enmity, the pretext of Adam; and he was thinking from Him; and the name of; for He was not; and He said: If it is not — until the Coming.

13 And behold, the perfection of the order was completed, and was wrought, that there should be children for Adam; and the perfection that shall be wrought for Adam you do not know.

14 For in his Satan does not know what was wrought concerning him, before he became a perfect man, with all that is for Adam; and He said:

15 God said to David: I will not leave you, that you be covered; and God said to Adam: I will not leave you, that you be covered — and Adam knew.

16 And God knew that he was the maker of; and by he was not healed; and hunger and thirst, and he was not from it, at the coming, from covered; he was not magnified; and his; and he is from that day; he in himself alone.

17 And when He was baptized, and His Father revealed His divinity, and the Devil came to Him to test Him — it was hidden from him, himself — in that which we have spoken, from the fast and the hunger.

18 And he thought in him that He is the Son of God, like Israel in glory alone; for God prepared this order and wisdom before the world was created.

19 As was prophesied from the holy books of old: for Israel, a son, until the time when they call Him Christ, a son; afterward the Devil did not know these, that He is like Israel.

20 And so He gave to the prophets authority, and they made books, until the time when Christ should make books.

21 The Devil thinks that He is like them.

22 And so they surnamed the prophets gods and anointed ones, until He should be called Christ by the name; he did not know; for the children of Seth were called sons of God; and they say to: He made us God.

23 And He says to them, the, in the Psalm of the hundred-and-third: you are gods; and He says to them: You are all called sons of the Most High.

24 And He made Elijah and Elisha slay death, and them that had the leprosy, and they wrought many wonders; and by much wisdom the mystery of Christ was hidden from the Devil, when he wrought wonders.

25 For the Devil was, was, seeing Christ work a sign; he thinks that He is God.

26 And then that he might know the inquiry of this.

27 And then our God hid His divinity from him by the weakness which He wrought in hunger and in thirst — yea, by weakness; yea, by thirst — and He in him; and he thinks that He is like those at His coming aforetime: in thirst — yea, in thirst — those whom we have mentioned.

28 And when they call Him, saying in: Yea, it is said concerning Him that He is God — he lied in Him thus; and when they say that He is the Messiah, he supposed Him to be like; yea, David; yea, others who were called by the [garden's?] name. And the inquiry of this: that God wrought no power to show by it His divinity, and He does not speak at all a word by power, except that which He does then, works of weakness, works of man — that all the works which had been: Satan opens the hearts of all who draw near to Christ, and they doubt in Him, and He makes them weak; He opens them in that which was in Him.

29 For He is He who, when he saw Christ working great wonders,.

30 And further, he saw works of weakness; they said in their soul: that God does not — yea, He hungers; yea, He thirsts; and He is wearied.

31 And when was not, by saying, weak, from the doing of this.

32 And according to this word He said: All who in the of Christ — yea, yea — who make Him weak on account of one of all His works; for they learned this from the Devil; for he does not know the of Christ; and seven of the believers — the Devil drew them in this knowledge and understanding; if the Devil taught them, they have learned, and they are not received.

33 And our God was hiding His divinity from Satan until the third day of the crucifixion, when He had given to His disciples the mysteries of death — the ordainer of the orders — and today, glorious and, He shall show them the mystery of the divinity in many words.

34 As it is written concerning John, who — which in its interpretation is.

35 And when He said: No can come to My Father but by Me; if you had known Me, you would have known My Father also.

36 To My Father, and from My Father: and the Father is in Me; and they made covenant that He should send to them the Holy Spirit, the; and that which He says to them: Lo, I will send [Him] from My Father; and My Father shall send in My name; and He is the: for He shall take of Mine, and shall declare to you all that is My Father's — it is Mine.

37 And He spoke with His Father in the speech of revelation, angelic, when He says to Him: Father, glorify Me with the glory which I had with You before the world was created.

38 And You also — My Father, who is unseen, is He.

39 And the word which He said: That they all may be one, as we are one; and You are one, and You are in Me, and I in them; and You have loved Me before the world was created.

40 And when Satan heard this word, he knew — the knowledge which in truth has no; and then God did a weakness which was not that which is greater than them.
