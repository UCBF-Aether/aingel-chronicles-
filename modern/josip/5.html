Josippon

Chapter 5

1 He said to his disciples in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit in the beginning.

2 He said this word to the Son and this word of God: to the Son and to the Holy Spirit, while he was angry at Adam.

3 the Holy Spirit, while he was angry at Adam.

4 David said in the psalm: He that sits in the heavens — the LORD shall laugh at them; he shall be wroth with them.

5 the LORD: one of Us.

6 And I spoke this word: it is not to be likened.

7 Adam and he ate from the tree the serpent.

8 All men had one tongue: behold, all of them are one lip and one tongue; and they began to do this.

9 The Book says God Sodom and Gomorrah: the LORD rained from heaven, from the LORD, fire and brimstone upon Sodom and Gomorrah.

10 That God said: Behold, let us go down — the Son and the Holy Spirit: Behold, let us go down, let us divide their tongues; he revealed in truth that the Son and the Holy Spirit were with him, to whom he said: Behold, let us go down.

11 And he, the Son of God, while he says the Son: The LORD said to me: You are my Son; this day I have begotten you.

12 He said to him: the Son and the Son of God: Sit at my right hand.

13 the LORD and he: A scepter of righteousness is the scepter of your kingdom: you have loved righteousness and hated wickedness; therefore God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of gladness above your fellows.

14 While he says Isaiah the prophet the Holy Trinity I heard the angels: and so Isaiah the prophet heard, while he asks concerning the Holy Trinity, the one God, while he says: I heard the angels who praise him.

15 the LORD the Son the word full of holiness: Holy is the LORD of hosts; the whole earth is full of your holiness; and the earth is full of his praise.

16 the Son the LORD Isaiah the prophet — and the prophecy of Isaiah was fulfilled concerning the manifestation of the Holy Trinity, and the earth is full of his praise.
