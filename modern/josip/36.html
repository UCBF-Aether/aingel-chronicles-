Josippon

Chapter 36

1 And if he is kept back from His glory and His holiness, the power of God forsakes them, and they are strengthened: no, our Lord Jesus Christ — and for the sake of Adam and his seed He went down by leave into sin; and our Lord descended into Sheol, and brought up their souls, and took them out from it, and brought them back into Paradise; for from faith it was that they did sin without their desire, by leave; and unclean spirits were set over them, and made them do sin without their desire, as it was before the baptism of our Christianity; and no one can bring them in, except by the power of the arm of the Holy Spirit; for our Lord Jesus Christ and the Holy Spirit: for a man as he is found pure, who with Him one hundred and four hundred took his likeness; and he whose likeness is one hundred and four hundred — and he whose likeness is one hundred and four hundred could not hasten to him whose likeness is one hundred and four hundred, except by the power of the Holy Spirit, when the power stood still.

2 And Paul the apostle says in the epistle to the land of Rome:

3 For by a man who has not the Holy Spirit, sin is worked without desire; and he cannot do that which is God's, and that which is fitting, that you may know: now, look, you live, and in it is the Holy Spirit; and we are loosed by the law, when He dwells in it, and we hasten to do the will of God without desire; as in it, if there be found from us, sin is worked without desire.

4 And I will show you, beloved: look, you live in it; the Spirit is holy; and I implore you by our Lord Jesus Christ, that you should seal the doing, and teach them all who say, from after the baptism; and He took a body which had no, and was reckoned with Peter and Paul, His holy apostles.

5 And if you ask, and have sealed, and have done in Him; and if you do not teach him who cannot — from after the baptism.

6 And the Holy Spirit departs from you, and delivers you to the hosts of the Devil, that he may destroy you; for you have known the good, and have taught your fellow, the faithful.

7 And if he who asked you concerning this — you desire to know that the Spirit neither eats sin, nor drinks water, nor tastes the savor of the world.

8 And do not be glad with the gladness of food and drink.

9 And the savor of it, and the joy of it: the glory of God and His holiness — hearing the voice of the eating one.

10 As the LORD said in the Law and in the holy Gospel: For man does not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds out of the mouth of the LORD; and the flesh lives by bread, as a beast.

11 And the spirit lives by the word of the LORD, as the angels; and if a man, when he sins — he who eats and drinks — his flesh dies; for the flame which was created in it speaks blood, and his flesh dies; and likewise, when he sins — the praise of man, the glory of God and His holiness — and he hears His voice, the spirit dies from the presence of God; for the satans seize it, and are burnt by deed, and the Holy Spirit departs from it.

12 And if you desire to know the inquiry of this: remember the work of the Devil and his hosts — those who were spirits of power from the former angels, three — and when they were kept back from the glory of God and His holiness, sin prevailed over them, and the grace of the Holy Spirit departed from them; to eternal darkness they fell.

13 Likewise, my beloved, he that is kept back from all, and forsakes all: the unclean spirits and the hosts of the Devil are driven out from him, and they cannot prevail at all, nor be able over him at any time; and the grace of the Holy Spirit is worked for the doing of the will of God, without desire; beloved, there is none who can do the will of God, without the being of the Holy Spirit within him.

14 And the Holy Spirit is not strongly dwelling in him apart from God and His holiness always; and hearing His voice in its season: for God is merciful; He knew that man sharpens the life of the spirit; and the life of the flesh cannot be His to God and His holiness; and hearing His voice in its season, the grace of the Holy Spirit departs, as it departed from the Devil and his hosts, and forsakes them, that they may do that in which they please
