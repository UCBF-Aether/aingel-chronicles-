Josippon

Chapter 43

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And behold, I have prepared for you, in another book, the cause of their grief for the apostles on one day — Wednesday and Friday; and I have prepared for you, in another book, the completion of His glory on the day of the Sabbath: how the fast of Wednesday and Friday is fitting upon all the believers — men, and women, and the young, and the small; and those who are perfected in them from their youth, this is fitting for them: for servants and for, in this week. These, friend, are not able to fast, and are not able to labor for Him who absolved them, and they are not able to do [it]; and whatever is good — if they have not kept all the three commands: for they, if they have not kept them, the power of the Holy Spirit is not with them, and they cannot do [it]; and whatever is good: for Satan strives against them — these three commands — and He taught all the believers; and know that God shall give you a joy which is greater than the joy of him who takes a gift; for he who takes a gift takes — and who does not die.

2 And by these commands, take them for the souls, from where they sinned, and free them from the hosts, and give them life forever; and keep, that you teach men this time, without weakness; for the enemy sought to blind their hearts from their heavenly [things] and from their inward [things]; for he knows that by them they shall overcome him, and they shall prevail over him by their inward [things] and be strengthened; and by the reading of the holy books, and His might and His holiness — of our Lord Jesus Christ: to Him belongs the glory, and the holiness, and the honor, and to His Father, and to the Holy Spirit, now and always, to the ages of ages. Amen.
