Josippon

Chapter 45

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And know not that the day of the Sabbath is the day of God in oldness and in life; for God worked in it all His works in oldness and in life; man, it was not revealed to you from this, in oldness, He said: the Book of Creation — the head of the earth — that in it God worked all His creations, as He revealed to you in the Book of the Interpretation of the Incarnation for the Son of God, and His seed.

2 And I, man, will not declare to you again a time, that you may make known to him who does not perceive the desire of the book.

3 And God said in the Book of Creation, which rested — He said: His hand is to Moses, the chief of His prophets — Work you: on the day of the Sabbath He made heaven for the day in which are the spiritual angels; and in it He created all their orders.

4 And in it He created heaven and all that is in it.

5 And in it He made the light; and in it the one nature of those whom He created on the day of the Sabbath.

6 For on the day of the Sabbath He created heaven, for Him who separated — that which He created — in the beginning of the Sabbath.

7 And on the third day He created all [things] in the of the earth, which He created on the day of the Sabbath.

8 And on the fourth day He created the sun and the moon and the stars, from the light which He created on the day of the Sabbath.

9 And on the fifth day He created the fish and the birds and the birds, from the water which He created on the day of the Sabbath.

10 And on the day of Friday He created the beasts and the wild beasts that move, from the earth which He created on the day of the Sabbath.

11 And in it was their work: He made Adam, and for Eve He created their body from the four natures which He created for them on the day of the Sabbath.

12 And He created their soul, as an entrance, from the light which He created for them on the day of the Sabbath.

13 And on the day of the Sabbath God did all the work; it is the day of God in oldness, which He worked in it.

14 God [did] all the work.

15 the heart of him who does not declare to anything on the day of the Sabbath, declares: our Lord Jesus Christ, who was born of, the holy Virgin, and there shone forth a light — He said — [upon] the earth, as He created the light on the day of the Sabbath.

16 And in it He appeared, that He is Christ, the King of the Jews, whom they await: when He entered Jerusalem, they took palm branches in their hands, He said, and all the Jews believed in Him, and Hosanna — while they say: Hosanna to the Son of David, whose interpretation is: for the name of Jesus in their tongue, its interpretation is Savior.

17 And on the day of the Sabbath He was called again, on this day, the Holy [One]; and on the day of the Sabbath He rose from the dead, and gave life to the souls of all the children of Adam, who were dead in Sheol, and brought them up into the rest forever, into the Paradise of joy.

18 And on the eighth day after His resurrection He declared again to His disciples that He is risen in His body.

19 When Thomas put his hand into His side, and in it they were convinced — all those who believed in Him — of that which they saw.

20 And on the day of the Sabbath was the feast of the fourth — the Holy Spirit — upon His disciples; and He made them of God and of His Father, and His brethren, and partakers with Him in the kingdom; and He made us to them, and to all those who are baptized with His baptism and believe in their; and swiftly on the day of the Sabbath, in which there descended upon them the Holy Spirit, [there was] preaching in Christ to the Jews who were in Jerusalem, until there believed from them souls; and after that from their holiness, and it is the day of of God, and the day of faith; and in it we were commanded that our bodies should be for us for the life forever, and that we should rejoice with Him in the joy forever; and it is the day of God, in which He did all the work in oldness and in life, and it is the day of rest, and which is forever in it, and the day of His resurrection and of the resurrection of all creation; and it is the day of the kingdom which is first, and which.

21 And it is the head of the days which are to Him in this world and in; and commanded us concerning it, that we should not do in it a work which is not without work, and should not neglect the work; and in it [men] hasten after this His work — His glory and His holiness, and the reading of His books — always, from morning until evening.

22 And He ordained — the soul which does not this work thus, always, on the day of the Sabbath, shall go forth from the people; and I have declared to you that which [is] of this incarnation.

23 And concerning it, God taught all who were found of all the believers — one, and the nations of servants, and the — that they should keep [it] and do [it], and should be saved from destruction forever, and should seek the life of the kingdom forever.

24 And he who did not keep it, as I said to you — behold — that the nations our Lord by His: that which is hidden from our Lord; and as for you — for [it is] the name of his Lord.

25 And as for you: for God says to them with one word of the words: I, in two days created — by one word — all creation; and on the seventh day I rested from all My work; and I blessed it — the seventh day — I hallowed it, for in it I rested.

26 And then shall a man rest on that day, and shall give rest to the man, and to the son, and to the, and to his servant, and to his maidservant, and and the stranger who dwells in the house; and every soul which does not rest on that day, that soul shall be cut off from her people: this word — its name by its — by one word.

27 And they did the work which He commanded them in it all their days, and did not transgress Your commandment: when they found a man gathering sticks on the day of the Sabbath, they asked the sticks how they should do in it; and He commanded that they should stone him with stones, that he should die.

28 What is it that God, in it, when He created creation in two days? He did not rest; He rested on the day of the first [day], and magnified thus; and He commanded them that they should rest with Him in it; everyone who has understanding knows that He did not faint in doing creation; for swiftly He completes, without weariness.

29 And if He did not faint, neither did He rest again; for He rested — [He] says — [He] declares to us concerning weariness; it was before rest. And if He did not faint, and did not rest again, He magnified this commandment thus to everyone who does not rest with Him.

30 And how much more [punishment] was decreed upon everyone who does not rest with Him on the day of the Sabbath, which is the day of rest in truth.

31 And he who knew, in his ignorance, weariness and rest — his name is He who, before the day of the first [day], did not faint in the creation of His creations, and did not rest again in it on account of weariness; and He rested — [He] says — He knew by this: weariness in truth, and rest in truth, which was on the day of the Sabbath; for Christ, the Word of God who was incarnate, is He who created His creations; for God in it created all the work, and did not faint in the creation of His creations; for He was not fleshly, and did not faint; and He was not fleshly, and weariness did not come [upon Him] by that which He does; but He does all the work without weariness, by His will; and He was incarnate, and I — to Him [are] flesh and blood; and they chose Him — and for the — and He was wearied for our sake; weariness in truth, from before the day of the Sabbath, until by His soul; and on the day of the Sabbath He rested, and gave us rest with Him; for He, in the night of Friday, gave up His head for our sake, by His will, to suffering; and He endured weariness for our sake; and they bound Him, and took Him to the court of the chief priest; and they sat that night all of it; they mocked Him, and struck Him, and cast spittle into His face.
