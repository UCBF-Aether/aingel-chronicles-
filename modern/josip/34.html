1 And water and wine — [for] our Lord [is] the glory: flesh within the belly; and [when] she bore Him — the breasts, the milk which is from that — again.

2 And when His flesh grew — as our flesh grows, by the eating of bread and the drinking of water which is mixed with wine — [He likened] it to us in every deed, without sin. And He said to the thief: Truly as you are today the drinking of blood; but beautifully He said: You shall be with Me today in My paradise.

3 And so all who must arrive, and at the time of the Mass, at every time; and whoever does this in this faith — if death comes upon him on that day — is with Christ in paradise; for our Lord said: In My hand is righteousness. And therefore every believer who stands at the time of the Mass must in fear and in trembling, and to ask, and to plead toward Him who [stands] before Him, that He make him fit for paradise, and forgive him, as [He forgave] in My paradise; which He said to all the, when they look upon Him in them; and we believe in, at every time of the; and from they have offered in Him.

4 And in Your kingdom, at Your time, that he should eat for the thief; and sins were multiplied in the faith; and they were sanctified, as he said: Remember me, O Lord, when You come into Your kingdom. And he did not mock, and he did not think the light of the world; and he was not troubled, as; and he spoke not one word without for who you, or for the Devil who was sent with him; and he did not go forth from the house of the Christians, until all His flesh from upon the Cross.

5 And after the Ascension of all, He [sought] His and heaven, that they should ask [earnestly] that there should be in it [room for] the Holy Spirit, as [He rested] upon the apostles after His Ascension; and [they] must read the five psalms — of the forty and the seven and the ten — as in them [is told] His Ascension; and to ask that the Holy Spirit does not depart from upon him. This all the believers must: [what] they do after His Ascension [at] the altar; for [then] the holy [sacrifice] was renewed in them at that time.

6 And [whoever] reads the five psalms: he reads thus: O my Lord Jesus Christ, who ascended into the heavens, fill me with the fullness of the Holy Spirit, which You [appointed] for Your apostles after Your ascension; and brings forth from on high [to] the world; and comes.

7 For to You is the glory, and the honor, and the thanksgiving, with Your Father, the merciful, and the Holy Spirit, who is equal with You in, and forever, in the Name, Amen. 8 And when they sinned, they believed; and the Devil reigned over them; and He killed them, and brought them down into Sheol, by their obedience to him, God in His soul, and concerning him; and He brought up his soul from Sheol, to paradise, which was the first, until should be loosed to them from the of Adam; as the hosts with the Devil, until their from beneath them.
