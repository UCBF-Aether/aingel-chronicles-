1 The third discourse, concerning the interpretation of many words from the Law, and Joshua the son of Nun, which shows the glory of the orthodox Christian faith, and the beloved holy ones. May God enlighten the eyes of your heart with the light of the Holy Spirit, that you may know the mystery, as I revealed the book of the Law, and Joshua the son of, which shows to you the interpretation of this which I for you in the Book of the Covenant, [which] follows the Son of God and. For I for you — Joshua the son of Nun — he is the interpretation: he shows and he the orthodox faith of the Christians, and he shows every word which I have not told you from the Law; and Joshua the son of Nun shows the word of our Lord Jesus Christ, who says: "For the Scriptures are concerning Me." And in our Lord Jesus Christ is fitting the glory, as Adam and Eve were created; and He left them in the Paradise — not; and in it at all — who, as the is to the heavenly height of ordinance: from her; the Devil fell, with, and his host; and the Devil also they them by error, until the earth over them, with — upon their seed, by; and after a little: God the Son which I mentioned for you — in the flesh [of] the Incarnation his head is from the Devil; the Devil said: that he is a weak runner — he ran to Him, as also Christ killed him for his sake, and He took all the seed of his; and He went on the way to where Adam was; and — that he may be with the men who die in their name the hosts. And it became a hiding; closes for him who reveals, and its hidden things; to him, my beloved, to whom I revealed for you: from this — for Abraham and for his seed; for it joins me: as I revealed in few words. For many men do not read the book of the Law, and the book of Joshua the son of Nun, and they do not understand their history; and I show you the interpretation of their history. And as for those who read, for him who does not know their history and does not understand their interpretation: and I reveal to you their history briefly, and I mention its interpretation after this: written with the Devil, and because he ascended into the way of heaven, for whom they were created. that which he says in the Law and — Nun, concerning Abraham: — he is an example of all of which I mentioned to you, and his seed, from the — in the book of the Creation, from the Law: that Abraham was a man of Sardawi dwelling in Haran, a city in the land of Iraq: God appeared to him, and removed him from it — he, and his wife, and they them into tents in the wilderness, in the land of Kap'an, which is the land of Jerusalem. And they covenanted that He would give [as] inheritance the whole land to him and to his seed after them; and there were those who dwelt in that land, a people, who worshiped idols; for all who were in that land in those days worshiped idols, in the land of Canaan and in other foreign [lands]. Abraham dwelt in tents until God gave Isaac; he remained in tents, as his father, while he waited for God, that He would give him the inheritance of the land of Canaan, as He had covenanted with them. And Isaac also fathered Jacob, whom God named; and he sold [his birthright] to foreign men; and as for them, he sold [it] to — and they dwelt in Egypt; in those days they worshiped idols; and he reigned — from Pharaoh, the of Egypt. And [there were] many occasions of. Those who made occasion for him an occasion, until he became a seer for Pharaoh. And he sent him the land of Canaan, and he brought Jacob Israel his father, and all his, the sons of Jacob, and he brought them the land of Egypt, and he them to the land of Goshen; and Israel multiplied and increased; and Isaac and Jacob his son were there, dwelling in tents, [observing] the covenant. And Jacob Israel fathered twelve men. And thirty of them saw a dream; and he was Joseph, while he knew that he would become a ruler; and his brethren envied him concerning this; they sought to kill him. And after Jacob died, and all the sons, and Joseph; and there was a Pharaoh who knew not; another Pharaoh was appointed who knew not [the kindness] of the children of Israel; the Hebrews multiplied exceedingly, and he feared, when he [saw] and they prevailed over the people of. And he took [and] made them serve with work and they make for them from his host them. And moreover he commanded that every male that was born among them into the river, as and God would make [them] grow. And they multiplied; and they dwelt thus — days had passed; God had mercy upon them.

2 And He appeared to one of them: Moses. And He sent him to Pharaoh, king of Egypt; and he said to him, that a sacrifice. And we shall be free in the wilderness for two days; until there is for him an offering; and for you. And as for Pharaoh, when he heard their, he knew that they had taken counsel against him. And he added punishment upon their punishment. And God said to Moses: "Strike the sea with your staff." And when he struck, all of the land of Egypt; and contrariwise: the Egyptians became thirsting for water from it — water; and the Hebrews drank from it water. And Pharaoh said to Moses: "Take away from me this plague; I will not leave them to go forth." And when removed the plague, God hardened [the heart], when [it was] for Pharaoh, and he would not let [them] go in. And when God was commanding that He should smite with a plague, He never said: "I Myself will harden the heart of Pharaoh. And I will not let them go, until they see My power in it, and they know My name in the earth."
