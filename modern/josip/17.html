Chapter 17

1 And all the waters that are in it; for from the time of the prophets and the righteous fathers the darkness of the demons was not lifted from them, as — until the Voice appeared, which is the Son; the light appeared; as He says: For the light is not made manifest — God said: Let there be light.

2 This saying God said: the Voice appeared; and so were the prophets and the righteous fathers, until they became as waters, that they might be gathered and be watered in that day; and they dwelt beneath the darkness, by the envy of Satan, in his service.

3 And they [sinned] concerning their transgression; and the Spirit of God went and came upon them — which is the Holy Spirit — and spoke to them by them prophecy concerning the coming of His Son; and He said to them all:

4 Their head — John, and and — between, He goes and comes upon them; for he could not endure them because of the transgression of Adam their father; for he was to them within them, from their mother, from the hosts of the Devil, by violence.

5 There is no of this; and when the Word of God, the Only-begotten Son, came and was incarnate, He did this — He drove out the darkness with judgment.

6 And He revealed the light; He said: The earth, toward the holy, the glory of life, and of the birth which has in it — the unclean spirits from men.

7 The darkness which had been made them — that is, the unclean spirits; and when He drove them out from men by the baptism of Christianity, it was possible for them [to attain] the glory of life and the kingdom of the heavens; the darkness which had been of the heavens.

8 For the Gospel, in the tongue of the Greeks — its interpretation — proclaimed and preached.

9 And He made known its tidings; it was revealed that God prepared this order before the creation of the world was created. And so it was, it was: Adam, when he ate of the tree, at the time became from the voice of the walking of God in the garden; and he hid himself among the trees; and God called, saying: Adam, Adam, where are you?

10 In the seeking of God, the Good, who was with him — for the sound of Your feet is not heard except in flesh, or God: Adam — these He said: He revealed to Adam from that time that there is nothing left for you of salvation from that which until I am incarnate and become man; and I will do that which man does, from and.

11 And the works which the Son of God wrought, that He might and snatch him from the Devil.

12 Behold, He revealed to Adam, in the time of his transgression, that the Son is He who spoke to Adam; and He is He who saved them, all his seed.

13 For He says in the Gospel, the true [word] for God: There is none who has seen at all; the Son of the Father, who is in the bosom of His Father — He has declared.

14 This saying: that He is He who spoke to all men, all of them; and so He who spoke with Adam, and [revealed] to him that which is, that He might do for his salvation — that He might hide His divinity from the Devil in the incarnation and in His becoming man. And he is this: when he was, and he became, and became man, he knew that he could: from the desire of Adam; for all who were born by the desire of Adam became servants of the Devil in their without their.

15 When he was incarnate apart from the desire of Adam, from death, and his doing in Sheol with Adam his father; and from that time Satan found a pretext, when he slays and they do not pass to him his ransom, if he did it; and he knew, if he was incarnate from without desire.

16 And Satan saw the woman, and knows that she is a woman without desire; from his knowledge of her heart he will annul the order.

17 For he is he whom, when they did not know him, they were not.

18 And if they were not and were not, it was not possible that He should snatch men from him by violence; and He wrought the wisdom of His divinity, until Satan did not know, when he saw the Virgin, a woman.

19 And He made the Virgin Mary the betrothed of Joseph.

20 And this was by Him in the house, from before He was incarnate of her.

21 And when He was incarnate of her, He was incarnate of her.

22 And when he drove them out from the place of His incarnation, He was incarnate, and He completed it; from her He was incarnate; and the Devil supposed that she is the knowledge of enmity.

23 And by this his heart was blinded, and his heart was bound, and his knowledge was taken from him.

24 And he was thinking as he thought: for they say, He is the son of Joseph.

25 And God was able to and to become a perfect man in one moment, and He did not do this, lest the Devil should know all; He endured in the womb ten months, and endured the birth and the breasts and little by little; and He did all the works of man in the mystery which He ordained in His wisdom for our salvation; for He is God, the Son; from He is not separated from any place, neither is any place separated from Him, as He revealed to him in the Book of the Interpretation: the two and their union — He was incarnate of the Holy Spirit and of Mary the Virgin in the flesh of man, and not in the flesh of beast; for the hosts; and He and eats and drinks and sleeps and — He alone, without a human spirit; and their dwelling they know the passing joy.

26 And the flesh of man is flesh, and blood, and sleep, and the kingdom of human nature, speaking: You are the union — as the light is united with the oil.

27 So also the Only-begotten Son took flesh from Mary the Virgin — a human, speaking spirit — and was one with this spirit which was united with Him: a union in the flesh.

28 And the Only-begotten Son became one in spirit and in flesh, without separation; and the middle did not know a human spirit; the spirit is an expanse: when it became one by the bringing forth of the expanse, with Him, above and below — that which is above, and that which is below, that which is below.

29 And the flesh became upon the earth, and the spirit which became human was one with Him, as the Word; the expanse above — that which is above and that which is below, that which is above, that which is above, and that which is below, that which is below.

30 love this mystery concerning the union with the Son of God.

31 For great is that which shall be revealed — the interpretation — that the Son of God came down from heaven and was incarnate; and as for this, that He came down: the earth was not void of Him, nor was He confined in one place alone, for He fills every place.

32 And His descent — this says, this saying — that He was humbled, and became one with the flesh, and became man; for this descent is great; and He is He whom they shall find, and seize, and and Him, in the flesh which became one in Him, as it says in the holy Gospel: The Word became flesh.

33 And when man was born, there were angels to the shepherds, saying: [We announce] to you that great joy shall be to all men — this: Behold, there is born to you today a Savior, who is Christ the Lord, in the city of David.
