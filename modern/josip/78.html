Josippon

Chapter 78

1 Together with the creation that came forth, the heavenly and the earthly, and they said that they should glorify and bless the LORD: let all creation glorify and bless: Bless the LORD.

2 the LORD

3 O all you waters that are above the heavens, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

4 O all you powers, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

5 O sun and moon, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

6 O all you stars of heaven, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

7 O showers and dew, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

8 O clouds and winds, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever. O all you spirits, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

9 O all you fire and flame, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

10 O hail and, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

11 O nights and days, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

12 O light and darkness, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

13 O Israel, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

14 O, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

15 O, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

16 O spirits and souls of the righteous, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever.

17 O Hananiah and Azariah and Mishael, bless the LORD; praise him and exalt him forever: for he has delivered us from Sheol.

18 Bless the LORD, the God of gods; praise him, for he is good; for his mercy is forever.
