1 Meqabyan

Chapter 16

1 The people of Tyre and Sidon, and those who dwell in the of the sea, and the crossing of the river Jordan, and Gilead, and Karan, and the Canaanites, and the Jebusites, and the Girgashites, and Edom, and the people of Amalek.

2 And all the nations in their cities, and in their, and in their ordinances, and in their, and in the of their land; and all of them, as God appointed them, they.

3 And among them are they whose is good, and who know God.

4 And among them are they whose works are evil, and who do not know God who made them; and them, as they did by the hand of Ameneser, king of Syria.

5 For he raises up the power of Damascus, and of Samaria before the king of Egypt.

6 The mountains of Gilead, and the of Gelabuhe, and all Samaria, and Pathos of the land of Judah, and those who dwell in the mountains of Arabia, and Media, and Parthus, and Siwsigya, and Cappadocia.

7 And there dwell in their thick nations of, and those who do not know God, and do not keep His commandment.

8 He it is that shall recompense them according to the evil of their works, and according to the work of their hands.

9 For beyond Caesarea, and the people of Gilead and Amalek, they gather together to against the city of God — you that are full of righteousness, in which praises the Holy One of Israel, the God of Israel of hosts, who is above the chariot of the Cherub, He dwells; and a thousand thousands of thousands stand before Him, and thousands; and they are sent to Him in fear and trembling.
