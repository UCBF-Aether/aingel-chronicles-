1 Meqabyan

Chapter 9

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 The curse of Gilboa, and the mountains of Lebanon — if the rain of mercy from God does not do come down upon them, they shall not yield grass for the cattle and for the wild beasts. (cf. 2 Sam 1:21)

2 And the mountains of Gilead also — and the of — shall not give to the, and to the that are seen, and to the beasts of the desert, and, and, and the mighty.

3 And so the proud and the hypocrites who formerly for pride and for error — if the dew of mercy from God does not do come down upon them, the dead shall not rise, and, and — and.

4 And those who dwell, and, and.

5 And those who sing without law, and the, and — who trust in their idols, and to them, and play to them with the drum, and with the, and with the pipe.

6 And they serve with their idols, and sing by the work of their hands — who do not save themselves; they shall be judged in the day of judgment at the resurrection.

7 O fool and of mind — it seems to you that the dead shall not rise.

8 Do not think thus; for — when the horn is blown at the resurrection of the dead, by the mouth of Michael the archangel. (cf. 1 Thess 4:16)

9 The mountain and shall be shaken; the and the deep shall; and they shall become.

10 And there shall be a resurrection for all that is flesh.
