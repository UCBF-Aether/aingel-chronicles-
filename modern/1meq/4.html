1 Meqabyan

Chapter 4

1 And he,, when he saw that they were dead, commanded that their bodies be burned with fire until they became as dust, and be scattered to the wind; but he was not able to burn, and he brought them forth from.

2 And again they kindled a fire upon them from noon until dawn, but it did not consume them. And again he said: " cast their bodies into the sea."

3 And they did as the king commanded them, and cast them into the sea; but the sea did not swallow them, and great stones, and they did not sink into the sea, but floated; for the Spirit of God was upon them, and bore them.

4 While eagles and birds shaded them with their wings from the heat of the sun, and them —

5 And they did so, but not; and the beasts and the birds their bodies — and their bodies remained.

6 And when they saw the shining of their bodies — like the of the sun, and like the tabernacle of light — the angels kept their bodies.

7 And; and then their bodies, for the five martyrs.

8 And they appeared to him by night — to that wicked king —

9 And when he awoke, he was afraid, and sought to flee; for; and his knees trembled, and he feared, for it seemed to him that they would kill him.

10 And concerning this he said: "What do you desire, and what shall I do for you?"

11 And they answered and said to him: "We are not those whom you slew, and burned, and commanded to be cast into the sea; but, for God guarded our bodies, for we trusted in Him; for he who trusts in Him shall not be moved, and we who trusted in Him."

12 And he said to them: "What gift shall I give you which I did to you, evil — for I did not know that such punishment would befall me."

13 "And now seek for me; I will give you a gift, that my soul not in death, and do not bring me down alive into Sheol."

14 "For; for the law of your fathers is mercy."

15 And the blessed martyrs answered and said to him: "But as for us, we will not ransom you; [but?] there is God, our God, who will redeem you — for."

16 "But we appear to you, that we are alive — because of the perversity of your heart; but as for us, though you think that you slew us, to us life."

17 "But you and your gods shall go down into the burning judgment of fire, from where there is no escape, forever."

18 "Woe to you — you and your gods, whom you worship and to whom you bow down. Forsake; bow down to God who created you."

19 And the king spoke again and said to them: "What do you command me, that I may do all that you desire?"

20 And they said to him: "Not for our sake, but for your sake — that you do not go down into the burning judgment of fire."

21 "For these your gods — gold and silver, wood and stone, the work of men's hands, which have neither soul, nor heart, nor mind."

22 "For they do but kill, and do not give life; they do not smell, and do not perceive; but they lead you astray by the spirit of demons, which do not desire that any man be saved."

23 "And especially those like you — O blind — who make it seem to you that they are the ones who created you; for it was you yourselves who made them!"

24 "For the spirits of demons and of satans gather, to you into the sea of fire."

25 "But forsake this error, and worship God, our God; and this shall be our death, a gift, that we may profit our souls."

26 But he and feared; for —

27 And he said: "Now I know that truly the dead shall rise — they who have become ashes; and I."

28 And then they vanished from the eyes of that king; and from that day he forsook; and he — Sirusaydan the mighty and.

29 And he had delighted in the error of his heart and in his gods — for they led him astray many times; and not he alone erred, but as he did, so he led astray many, until they forsook their Maker.

30 And they sacrificed their children and their offspring to demons, and did the desire of their heart, which their father Satan taught them, that he might work which God not.

31 And, and they defiled, and all; and they defiled their souls, for Satan hardened their hearts.

32 But Sirusaydan the mighty and, who did not know his Maker, prided himself in his gods.

33 "How should God give the kingdom to those who do not know Him?" And He says:, if they repent and come to Him.

34 "But if they repent and come to Him, He will love them; but if they refuse, He will punish them with the trembling of Gehenna forever."

35 Let a king — as his kingdom is great — fear God; and.

36 And the — as their honor is great — let them their Maker.

37 For He created all creation, and He is the Lord of heaven and earth, who; for there is no other God but Him, in heaven above and on earth beneath.
