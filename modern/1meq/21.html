1 Meqabyan

Chapter 21

1 David trusted in God, and He was his helper; for he trusted in Him, and He delivered him from the hand of Saul the king.

2 And from Absalom his son, and from the ʾElofəlawəyanə, and from the Amalekites and the Edomites, and from the Qärənewonə — yes, from all these that rose up against him — God delivered David, for he trusted in Him and kept His law.

3 But as for those who did not trust in Him — the evil kings — God did not deliver them, for they were given over into the hand of their enemies, for cut off from God is he.

4 Hezekiah trusted in God, and He delivered him from the hand of the great Syrian.

5 But Manasseh his son was given over into the hand of his enemy, for he did not set his trust in God. And as for those who gave Manasseh over to his enemy — it was not [done] thus, but they bound him and carried him into their land; for he did not set his trust upon God, and did not fear Him who made him and exalted him and made him great.

6 And therefore he was held back from the gift which had been given him, for he did not direct his way to God his God — that his days might be at rest, and that his enemy might rise against him, and that there might be strength and might and fəṣəmä and käwalä.

7 For better is trust in God than many hosts, and [better] than trust in horses and chariots and bows.

8 For the one who trusts in Him, in God, shall live and be honored and be exalted exceedingly.

9 But as for those who did not trust in God, and trusted in much wealth — they became estranged from His grace which He had given them; for God is no respecter of persons.

10 But as for those who trusted in Him, He will keep them; and as for those who serve Him, He will put them in [His] service — and their help shall not be slow in their tribulation, when their enemies rise up against them; for they did not set their hearts on His will to God, but kept His commandments to God.

11 And the one who is firm in the will of God, and in the will of His commandments, and keeps His law — to him He shall be a helper in his tribulation.

12 And what shall they say of the one who does not do trust in God? He is not better than the beasts that perish in the earth — the one who is puffed up in his heart and exalts himself against his God, yes, against his Maker.

13 And what is man's pride? His glory passes away, and his exaltation, and his honor, and his riches.

14 And his trust shall not save his pride from death; for The one who made him is God, and it is The one who is his Maker.

15 And let those who trust in God keep His law; and let those who keep His law keep His commandments; and let those who keep His commandments keep His judgments — for their enemies shall fear Him, and they shall fear His will; for The one who is with Him shall not be ashamed.

16 And let those who trust in God keep His commandments; and let those who keep His commandments, that they keep His judgments — and let the commandment fear Him and His will; and the law is theirs.

17 And the one who loves God keeps His commandments; and the one who loves God, let the servant love the Son; and the one who loves his father loves him whom he fathered.

18 And what man is as the man of God — the man that God, that He makes him to dwell in His good place; and in the midst of the garden He planted him, and He made him, and He set him, and He gave him to eat?

19 And what shall they say of those who do not trust in God? God shall make His face shine upon the righteous

20 But as for those who do not trust in God — why do they trust in the gods, and [why do] they not receive the grace of the kingdom?

21 For they trust in the counsel of men, that they may cut off the grace of God; but God will not give them the grace of His kingdom.

22 And therefore is it said, "Let those who trust in the Lord dwell in the good land," for He feeds them with much wealth.

23 And therefore the righteous are exalted over all the earth; and therefore do they bring forth the fruit of righteousness, and their seed is blessed, for God loves the righteous.

24 And as for those who trust in Him, He brings them low and lifts them up

25 And therefore let those who trust in God fear Him and keep His law; for their enemies shall be delivered into their hands.

26 And therefore the enemies

27 But as for those who do not trust in God — He shall not have mercy upon them, and they shall receive their reward from their enemies.

28 And the one who trusts in Him

29 And the one who trusts in Him, in God — blessed is the man that trusts in Him.

30 In heaven He shall give them the honor which He prepared for their fathers — for Abraham, and for Isaac, and for Jacob, and for David, and for Hezekiah, and for Samuel — these that were kept from His commandment and from His judgment to God.

31 As He gave them the inheritance which He sware to their fathers — to Abraham, and to Isaac, and to Jacob — these that from the beginning were chosen, that they might rejoice in His kingdom.
