1 Meqabyan

Chapter 25

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 I say to their souls: when I seize them in My anger, says the Lord, whom all obey; for perverse is their way, and all that walk in the way of Satan are destroyed.

2 And the way of God they do not know, and they have turned their backs, and turned away from the law.

3 And I will recompense them according to the evil of their works, afterward in the day of judgment; I will visit them, as it is written: I have kept the evil of their works, and they shall not escape from My hand; for I am the Lord, who kills and makes alive, who rebuks and pardons.

4 And there is none that escapes from My hand, neither under the earth, nor above the heavens, nor in the sea, nor in the depths of the waters; for I am the Lord, from the end to the ends of the world, and every soul is in My hand.

5 He commanded under the earth, and to the fishes in the midst of the sea, and to the birds in the midst of the air, and to the in the wilderness, from the end to the end, and to the ends of the world — this is He.

6 And there is none that escapes from My hand, in heaven above or in earth beneath; for I am The one who did it, and was silent, and it was shown before My eyes. And there is none that says to Me: Where have you turned? and, What do you?

7 And I have commanded above the angels and the elders, and every name that is named — this is He; and all the birds of heaven, and the beasts of the desert, and men — this is He.

8 And the breath of the hyena, and the of the evening, and the sea of is gathered, from the deep its, for the one who shall come to it in the latter days.

9 For He shall judge the living and the dead, in Noba and Saba and Ethiopia and India, and all that them, and their.

10 And every one sees in truth, with the staff that is in his hand; and he sees with the staff of his; for his staff is exalted above all staves.

11 And His horn is weighty above all horns, and His kingdom is exalted above all kingdoms, and His dominion has seized all the world, which encompasses all, and there is none that measures it.

12 He commands the clouds above, and makes the grass to spring for men beneath, and gives fruit upon the field.

13 And He feeds all according to His will, with His and with His fruit and with His, to all that He created, to cattle and to creeping things under the earth, and to the beasts and to men upon the earth — who gives to the one who prays, and does not do reject their supplication, for and for of death.

14 And again their prayer is increased to us — who pray to Him at all times; but their is to the wicked, as the soul of the wild ass, and their gathering to the unrighteous, as our blood.

15 And their joy also is as the bird, and the beauty of their pasture is transient — of gold and silver upon the earth — and their gold also profits them not; but their is for the righteous, and in the day of their it shall be swallowed in.

16 And the store of our and our is known; and all that passes, as the day that turns — it has passed; and like the word that goes forth from the mouth, it does not do return; its is for sinners; and like the shadow that passes, so the beauty of their dwelling; and like a garment, so their is for sinners before God.

17 But the righteous — but He says: God rebuks them not; but when they show mercy, and weep openly over the dead and the poor, and when they clothe the naked with their garments, and when they do not withhold the seed of their house which God gave them, that they may give to the stranger and to the poor —

18 And they do not wrong openly the faithful, and do not repay evil for evil, and do no iniquity in the reckoning of their months, nor in the measure of their balances; for the word of God is righteousness and glory, as this.
