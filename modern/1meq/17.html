1 Meqabyan

Chapter 17

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And he answered Edomyas and Amalek: "God, whose is the judgment of heaven and earth, shall not rule over them; into his hand he has trodden the house of his sanctuary, for they are a foreign people who do not walk in righteousness."

2 And there is no fear of God before their eyes; for [they stop at] no lewdness, nor the shedding of blood, nor the eating of sacrifices, and abominable things, and carrion, nor aught that is like to these — reprobates and gəmunanə.

3 They have no faith, and no salt; for they are haters of good, and they do not know God; and the way of peace which was laid — and affliction, and every unclean deed, and hukätä, and zäfänä, and täwənetä — as their father Säbəlyanos taught them.

4 For he envied them, with his demons, and taught them all evil deeds —, and greed, and affliction, and the seizure of others' goods by violence, and theft, and the eating of carrion, and abominable things, and lewdness.

5 And all that is like to this: fornication — woman with man — and the shedding of blood, and the eating of carrion, and sacrifice to idols, and the killing of a man's life by violence, and strife, and contention, and səsəʿətə, and every evil deed which God hats; and this teaching he brought to them — he, Ṣäromusäbəlanəyos — that he might turn them from the way of God: he seized all the world.

6 But as for him: humility to God, and yäwəhatə, and he loved his neighbor, and had mercy on his brother, and was beloved, and peace with all people.

7 And be you not partial before men, nor seizers, nor wrongdoers, nor afflicters, nor fornicators — woman with man — nor doers of evil, nor [doers of] violence against their neighbors, nor plunderers in violence, who wrong their neighbors.

8 And they scorn, and wag their heads, and give themselves to evil, and hasten to deceit, that they might bring men down into the punishment of judgment forever.
