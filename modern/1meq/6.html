9 And when flesh comes to Him, from the taste of the fragrance of its fruit, the soul goes forth in great joy that is upon it.

10 And they rejoice there — the good kings who did the will of God. and; He to the station of life forever in the kingdom of heaven.

11 And I saw that as [it is in] heaven, so [it is] on earth: as their kingdom was great on earth, and as they honored Him and bowed down to Him, so in heaven they are honored and exalted and rejoice — if they did good in their life upon earth.

12 But in their kingdom and in their which God gave them; and they did not judge in righteousness and justice, and did not save the oppressed and the, and the children of the dead; for they despised the voice of the poor and needy.

16 But in heaven is the Ruler of all, and all souls are in His hand, and all life is in His mouth; who gives honor to those who honor Him, and loves those who love Him; for He rules all, and establishes all.

17 And He tries the heart and the reins; for He is the King of heaven and earth, who grants the prayer of the one who prays to Him with a pure heart.

19 Not by your might were you lifted up to this kingdom, and not by your wisdom did you sit upon this throne; but of His own will He set you upon the throne of the kingdom. He shall try you, whether you establish the people — as Saul in those days, whom the people made, and he rejected the word of God and the word of Samuel the prophet, and did not slay the king of Amalek and his people.

20 For God said to Samuel the prophet: "Go, say to Saul: Go and strike the land of Amalek, and slay all their kings and their, from man to beast; for they have me, forsaking the law, and worshipping gods, and bowing down to idols, and in their and in all corrupt work which has no profit."

21 And therefore, when they God, He sent [him] to slay them.

29 And when he heard [it] he feared, and the king rose from his throne, and laid hold on Samuel's garment; and great was Samuel's, and his garment was rent.

30 And Samuel said to Saul: "God has rent your kingdom."

31 And again Saul said to Samuel: "Honor me before the people, and me before God, that He may me." And great was Samuel's at his word; for he feared the word of God his Maker, and did not fear the mortal king.
