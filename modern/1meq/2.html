1 Meqabyan

Chapter 2

1 And there was a man of the tribe of Benjamin, whose name was Meqabyos.

2 And he had —

3 and he found them, and said to them: "Do not bow down, and do not sacrifice, to the gods of Sirusaydan the king, as he has commanded."

4 "But if you refuse, we will seize you, and lead you away to the king, and destroy all your goods, as the king has commanded."

5 And they raised their voices and said to him: "These are good and upright; but as for us, there is [One] to whom we bow down — the God of our fathers, the Maker of heaven and earth, and of the sea and all that is in it, who made the sun and the moon and the stars, and the clouds; He is the God in whom we trust, whom we worship, and in whom we believe."

6 But as for these men — there went up to the king against him four commanders, and their carriers who bore the goods of their fields.

7 And when he desired to seize these holy ones, they escaped from their hands; and there was none who touched them. And they went, carrying the goods of their fields; for they were upright, and strong, and exceedingly mighty.

8 And there was among them one who slew a bear by throttling, and strangled it, like.

9 And there was among them one who slew a lion with a single cast of a stone, or with a stroke of wood, at once.

10 And there was among them in battle one who slew a hundred at once with a single sword; and thus their name became renowned throughout all the land of Moab, and among the Chaldeans.

11 And they were strong in might; and in them were beauty, and grace, and speech.

12 And a marvel was the beauty of; but above all, that which gave strength to the of their heart was that they toward God, and did not fear death.

13 And when they terrified them with the, there was none who was able to seize them; but they, the strong ones, escaped into a mountain.

14 But the entered into the city, and the of the city, and threatened them, saying: "If you do not bring to these the Meqabyan, we will burn your city with fire, and send [word?] to the king, and destroy your city."

15 And at that time there went out the people of the city — the poor, and the, and the Hebrew women, and; and they all cried out together, and lifted up their eyes to the mountain, and cried to them, saying: "do not Destroy us — neither us nor our city."

16 And when together, they sought mercy from God.

17 And they turned their faces toward the east, and prayed to God, together their hands, and said: "We cry to You, O Lord, against these transgressors of Your law and of Your commandment."

18 "But as for us, we will not consent to hear the word of that proud one who does not do believe in Your law — for [these are but?] stone and silver, and wood and stone, the work of men's hands."

19 "And he bows his head, as though these had made him — while You, You are their Maker, who kill and make alive; but he is a pourer-out of the blood of men, and a devourer of the flesh of men."

20 "But as for us, we will not consent to look upon the face of that proud one, nor to listen to his word."

21 "But if You command us, we will go to him, and our souls to death for the sake of; yet we will not listen to the word of that proud one, when he says: 'Eat to my gods!'"

22 "But as for us, O Lord, with all our heart we trust in You — the God of our fathers, who do Your will and walk in Your law — the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, who try the heart and the reins."

23 "And You to the righteous and to the sinner, and search that which is in the heart of man; and nothing is hidden from before You."

24 "But as for us, we have no God but You."

25 "But grant us might and strength, and in this work which we to You, that we may give our souls to death for Your holy name's sake."

26 "And as You to Israel in the land of Egypt — You heard the prayer of Jacob — so now we ask You, O holy God."

27 And when You appeared to them —

28 And; and they fled from the sun from the east.
