1 Meqabyan

Chapter 1

1 And there was a man whose name was Sirusaydan, a tempter to evil; and he prided himself in the multitude of his horses, and in mighty strength that was under his hand.

2 And there were many gods, idols, to whom he bowed down, and whom he worshipped; and he sacrificed to them by day and by night.

3 And it seemed to him, in the pride of his heart, that they were the ones who gave him might and strength.

4 who offered continual sacrifice to his gods.

5 And still it seemed to him that they gave him dominion over the adversaries and over all that he desired.

6 And he sacrificed for himself by day and by night.

7 And he set over them craftsmen of the gods.

8 And as for them, they pretended, and told him that his gods devoured by day and by night — yet they themselves devoured from this defiled sacrifice.

9 And still they carried on, that they might enjoy themselves; and they devoured others like themselves. And yet again they sacrificed for themselves, and they made others, that they might enjoy themselves — others like him.

10 But he trusted in his idol, which was of no use.

11 And it seemed to him that they were the ones who had created him — as they had nourished him and made him king — in the perversity of his heart and in the of his mind; for Satan had blinded his heart, so that he did not know God, who had created him and made him and formed him from nothingness — neither he nor his — that he might go with him into judgment, and be tormented with him forever, and with those whom they called gods, though they were not gods.

12 And in truth he called them the dead; for even now they were not living.

13 And as for those who trusted in them — dust upon them, O children of men, apart from the living — for the spirit of Satan entered, which led them astray into it, with their idols; and it spoke with them according to the desire of their heart, and appeared to them as they wished.

14 And when they saw that their desire was fulfilled to them, they were stirred and did their will, until they sacrificed their children and their offspring that came forth from their wombs, and poured out innocent blood — the blood of their children and their offspring.

15 And nothing restrained them, for Satan made it sweet to them according to their desire for evil, that he might bring them down with him into this judgment — that is, Gehenna — from which there is no escape, forever and ever.

16 And as for Sirusaydan — he was mighty — and he had gods: one in the likeness of a man, and two in the likeness of a woman.

17 And he prided himself in these his gods, which were of no use, and he honored them exceedingly, and sacrificed to them at dawn and at noon.

18 And some men he made to enjoy themselves; and for others he devoured from this defiled sacrifice. And for others he appointed, that they might devour; and exceedingly they gave themselves up to evil.

19 And he built for his gods a shrine, of gold, and of silver, and of cedar.

20 And he overlaid them with gold and with silver; and he made a throne in the of his house.

21 And he set a guard there, and sacrificed to them in the mornings.

22 And as for him, it seemed to him that his gods devoured; and and he gave a silver dish — made, with of sandals.

23 And he said to his ministers: "Arise! Give them — let my gods eat from my slaughter, and let them drink from all my vessels; and if it is not enough for them, I will add more for them."

24 And from this defiled sacrifice he prepared everything, that they might eat and drink.

25 And he, in his evil, sent his messengers — who — throughout all his kingdom, that they might search out and know whether there is any who does not do sacrifice, and who does not do bow down to them: "Bring him, and punish him before him, with the sword and with fire; and plunder his goods, and burn his house with fire, and take all that he has."

26 "But as for him — I, I will show him torment and judgment, if he forsake worship and sacrifice to my gods; for great and exalted are they, and by their exaltation they made us."

27 "And they created the heaven and the earth, and the great sea, and the sun, and the moon, and the stars, and the winds, and the rains, and all that moves — for our food and for our drink."

28 "But those who do not worship them shall be punished with distress and with affliction, and they shall find no mercy."
