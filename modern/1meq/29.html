1 Meqabyan

Chapter 29

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And Egypt oppressed the children of Israel with oppression and with the work of, and with all the work that they made them do, and with that had no.

2 And they set over them, and them; and they to God, that He should deliver them from all their of Egypt.

3 And at that time the prince sent to them their, Moses and Aaron, and delivered them from the work of; for God sent them, that they should bring forth His people from the house of bondage of Pharaoh, king of Egypt — for in his pride he withheld their from the people, that they should not and not sacrifice to God in the wilderness.

4 For to the God them; and for the pride of Pharaoh God drowned him with his host in the midst of the Red Sea.

5 And likewise they that the word of God — whereas [they are] kings and princes, and in all that He set them and made them kings, that they should do His will — and He gives them their to them that in goodness, and they honor His great name.

6 And if they establish My kingdom, I will establish their kingdom, says God who rules over all.

7 Listen to me, and I will listen to you: keep My commandment, and I will keep your soul; walk in My commandment, and I will walk according to your heart.

8 Love Me, and I will love your life; to Me, and I will you.

9 And trust in Me, and I will deliver you, says God who rules over all.

10 And: draw nigh to Me, and I will draw nigh to you — for the way of uprightness I love, says God who rules over all. your hands, sinners and, and the evil of your heart.

11 And I will remove My anger from you, and turn you in and in mercy.

12 And I will remove far from you the enemies that do, as I delivered David My servant from many that met him — from the hand of Goliath the, and from the hand of Saul that to slay him, and from the hand of Absalom his son that sought to take the kingdom.

13 And likewise I will deliver those who do My will, and keep My commandment, and in My law; and I will cause them to inherit honor, and set them above all, that they may rejoice in this world and in the world to come.

14 And they shall be with the blessed kings that to God in the of their life — as Samuel, when he was a child, whom God chose from his youth in the of his life.

15 And he told that he should tell to Eli the priest, the aged and good in his deeds — to Samuel the prophet, when he in the house of God the Lord.

16 And when he in the house of God, when he, he appoints the people, and kings by the commandment of God; and the anointing of the kingdom in his hand, when he did the will of God his Lord — for he before Him, that he should rule the people whom God chose from the children of Israel.

17 And whereas Saul was in the kingdom, God said to Samuel the prophet: "Go, anoint David the son of Jesse, that is of the house of Judah; for I have him."
