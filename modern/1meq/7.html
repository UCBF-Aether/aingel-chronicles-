1 Meqabyan

Chapter 7

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And if you are king, and if you are a ruler — what are you? you are not God, who created you.

2 And He brought you forth from where you were not, that you should do His will, and walk in His commandment, and fear His judgment; as you your servants, and the dominion that is over them — even so there is to you One who you, and dominion over you.

3 As you withhold the measure of mercy from those who do evil, even so there is to you God, who shall withhold you and bring you down into the judgment from where there is no escape, forever.

4 And as you punish the one who trespasses against you, and grant him no pardon — and you, why will you not seek pardon of God?

5 And as you desire that they should fear you — why will you not fear God your God? For He it is who created you, and made you king over all His work, that you should shepherd His people and feed them in righteousness.

6 And do not respect the person of the great and of the small; but judge in righteousness and in equity, as God appointed you. Of whom are you afraid — beside whom you keep neither His way nor His commandment?

7 And do not turn aside to the right hand nor to the left — as Moses commanded the children of Israel, and said to them: "Behold, I have set before you fire and water; stretch forth your hand to that which you will." (cf. Deut 30:15; Sir 15:16-17)

8 And hear the word which I have appointed to you, that you might hear His word, and do His commandment. And lest you say: "There is no crossing — who shall bring it, that I may behold and hear His word, and do the commandment of God?"

9 "And if you say again: 'Who shall go up for us into heaven, and bring down to us this word — O God — that we may hear it and do it?' — And He says: 'Behold, the word is nigh to you, in your mouth; and behold, in your hand.'" (Deut 30:12-14)

10 And if you have not listened to the book, you have not listened to God your God; and if you have not kept my word, you shall not prosper by it; and you have not done His commandment.

11 And you — you sought after the eternal estate, because you did not love His commandment, and did not do His will — His, who magnified you and honored you above all your people, that you might shepherd them in righteousness.

12 And He set you on high, and as head over all His people, while you remembered the name of your God who created you, and gave you [all] that you should shepherd the people in righteousness and in equity.

13 And from these [things] evil [comes] to you through your teaching and through your learning — while you remember the way of God, and while you mend your mind, and open in uprightness.

14 And when they are judged before you, do not respect the person, and take no bribe from a man, that you might acquit him; and from a pure man, that you might condemn him — for this earth, it is all His.

15 And if you have done His will, He shall prolong your days.

16 Remember that you shall rise from the dead before His face, and shall be examined in all that you have done — whether of evil, and whether of good.

17 And if you have done good, you shall dwell in the dwelling of the good kings — you shall remain in the houses of light; but if you have done evil, you shall dwell in the dwelling of the evil kings — and God shall not spare your glory.

18 But you, when you see the greatness of your glory, and the expanse of your hosts, and the produce of your field gathered, and you behold your horses, and the messenger that is under your hand, and those who blow the horn and make melody before you on the pipe —

19 But you, when you see all this, your heart is lifted up, and you stiffen your neck, and do not remember Him who gave you all this glory. Is it not when He says to you "Forsake [it]," that you forsake — even you?

20 And you give your glory to another; for you have turned away from the food wherewith He fed you.

21 And by this you are chastened, and you are reckoned, and you are ruled; for suddenly death shall come upon you, and in the resurrection there shall be judgment, and the work of man shall be examined.

22 And to the kingdoms of the earth there is none that gives honor; and to the rulers of this earth the crown shall fall from them. For master and judge shall stand together in the condition of judgment; for a righteous ruler is He.

23 In the condition of the soul shall tremble, and the books shall be opened, and the work of man shall appear; and there is none that hides himself.

24 When the resurrection of the dead [comes]; for the earth shall give forth her womb which was sealed upon her — as a woman does not do hold [back] her womb — when the time is ripe for bearing, and she hastens to bring forth that which is in the belly.

25 And as the clouds withhold the appointed rain — that they do not go where God commanded them, that they do not come down — even so it is not possible that the dead should not rise, [when] the time is ripe for the resurrection; for the word of God brought forth all from where there was not, and the word of God brought all into being.

26 And again the word of God shall bring forth all from beneath, as Moses says: "Not by bread alone does man live, but by every word that proceeds out of the mouth of God." (Deut 8:3)

27 Behold, you know that by the word of God the dead shall live.

28 And again He says in the second law, concerning those who shall be kings and rulers, and those who do the will: "In the affliction of [this] life you shall keep them; and in due time their foot shall slide — for the day of their calamity is nigh." (cf. Deut 32:35)

29 And again God says to them — to those who embitter His — "Know you, know you, that I, even I, am He — God: I kill, and I make alive." (Deut 32:39)

30 "I wound, and I heal; I bring down to Sheol, and again I bring up to heaven; and there is none that delivers out of My hand." (Deut 32:39)

31 Thus says God concerning the kings and rulers who do not keep His commandment: "Keep My law and My statutes, that you may enter into My kingdom which is forever; for the kingdom that is on earth is passing, and it fleets from noon until dawn."

32 For is God's — for honor and for dishonor: this one He abases, and this one He honours. (cf. 1 Sam 2:7)

33 The one who does not do do the will He shall cast down; and the one who does the will He shall set up.
