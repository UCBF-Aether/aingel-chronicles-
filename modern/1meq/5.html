1 Meqabyan

Chapter 5

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 The visionary exalted himself; and in the first fäṣän God planted him, that his belly should be filled from his foot to his head; and in the second fäṣän he died.

2 And again Nimrod exalted himself, who made a bed of out of great splendor; to him also God gave glory.

3 And again Nebuchadnezzar exalted himself, and said: "There is no other king but me; and I am he who made the sun to rise upon the earth." And from much pride he spoke thus.

4 And God drove him out from among men —

5 And when He said, "Again I will bring him into his kingdom" — who is the one who could shut him out? was proud against God his Maker, who did not make the earth.

6 And who is the one who transgressed against Him — His law and His ordinance? And to whom did He not apportion the earth?

7 But as for you, Sirusaydan — do you desire to be proud against your Maker? And again, it shall be to you as to them: that He humble you from your pride, and bring you down into the land of death.

8 And bring down into Sheol, of darkness, where there is weeping and gnashing of teeth, into a great pit and burning, where their flame is not quenched forever.

9 But you are man and earth; you who have become flesh shall die, like those proud kings like you, who forsook the goods of this world.

10 But as for us, we say: you are man, and not God; for God is the Maker of the heavens and the earth, and the Maker of your flesh.

11 And He humbles the proud, and exalts the lowly; and He gives strength to the weak, and gives weakness to the strong.

12 And He gives death to the living, and He raises the dead who sleep in the grave and have become dust.

13 And He sets them apart for service in life, from the works of sin.

14 O king Sirusaydan, why do you pride yourself in your defiled gods, which have no profit?

15 God made the heavens, and founded the earth; He created the great sea; He set the sun and the moon; the seasons and the years He ordained.

16 He sends man forth to his work by day, and he toils until the evening; and the stars of heaven are hidden at His word.

17 And He numbers them all by their names; and there is none whom God does not do know.

18 And the angels of heaven — He it is who that they should be sent to Him and praise His holy name; and He sends him to all the angels, because of those who shall inherit life.

19 The angel Raphael was sent to Tobit, and delivered Tobiah in the land of Raguel.

20 And an angel was sent to Gideon, that he might put to flight; and an angel was sent from before Moses when He divided for Israel the Red Sea.

21 For God says: "I alone am their guide, and there is no strange god with them."

22 And He made them go up upon of the earth.

23 And He fed them, and satisfied them with honey from the rock; for He loved them exceedingly.

24 But as for you, He made you king over His people, and gave you dominion over the four kingdoms, that you should keep the people, and shepherd [them] in righteousness, and do the will of the one who made you.

25 And He made you king a second time, that you should love God your God; for — and He made you king —

26 But as for you, O righteous one, love God your God a second time, as He loved you and you have trusted in all His; and do the will of God, that your days may be prolonged upon the earth, and with you.

27 And He shall set you above all your enemies, and make you dwell upon all your thrones, and spread His wings over your head.

28 If you obey, God shall choose you, as He chose Saul from the children of Israel, and made [him] king over Israel His people — when he sought the asses of his father — and he sat upon the throne with Samuel the prophet.

29 And He gave him a great portion above his brethren, and said to him: "Behold, from this day God has made you king over His people — "

30 And show to those who do good, good; and to those who do evil, evil; for God has set you over them, that you should kill and make alive.

31 And to those who do good, that you render to them good; and to those who do evil, that you render to them evil; for to you has God given all you desire, that you may do His will — whether you punish or whether you make alive.

32 But as for you, do the will of God at all times and in every hour, that He may do your will in all you have and in all you have asked, when you pray before Him; for it is you that have made to the one who rules all, to the one who is above.

33 And as for the one who has none to establish him, He Himself establishes all.

34 As for the one who has none to appoint him, He Himself appoints all.

35 As for the one who has none to him, He Himself all.

36 As for the one who has none to set apart him, He separates all.

37 As for the one who has none to bring him back, He Himself brings back all; and there is none that escapes from His hand; for the judgment of heaven and earth is with Him.

38 And there is nothing hidden before His eyes, but [all is] naked before Him.

39 And He sees all, but Him sees none; He hears the prayer of the one who prays to Him — and his prayer — for He formed man in His image and in His likeness.

40 And He feeds all from His storehouse that does not do fail; for He is King — He endures forever.
