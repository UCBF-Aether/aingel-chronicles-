1 Meqabyan

Chapter 33

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And yet I fed them manna in the wilderness, bread as David says, and the bread of his angels they ate — the children of ʾəmäḥəyawə; and all this I set before them, that they might serve me in righteousness and uprightness.

2 But they did not serve me; and I will visit them, says God of all who rules; and they despised me, and walked in a trackless.

3 And I, according to their works will I recompense them, and according to the work of their hands will I visit them; for they forsook my worship, and did not endure my ordinance and my counsel; and I will bring them down into the judgment of heaven which is in the heavens.

4 And upon the earth I will shorten their days, for they did not keep my commandment; and I will despise them.

5 But you, O king, are not; you are a man, you that die and perish; for tomorrow you shall become wood and trees and dust.

6 And now you exalt yourself, and are proud, as the one who does not do dy forever.

7 For you are a man who now show yourself alive, and tomorrow is death, says God of all who rules.

8 But if you keep my word and my commandment, I will cause you to inherit the holy city, with those who did my will — honored kings, whose dwellings are bright, and beautiful are their crowns, and their thrones of gold and of silver; who are, who dwell upon them.

9 And you shall rejoice in the midst of his city, which is nigh, a dwelling for the good servants.

10 But for the evil servants, it shall not be permitted to them to enter into this city, in which dwell the honored kings.

11 For they did not keep my word and my ordinance, says God of all who rules.
