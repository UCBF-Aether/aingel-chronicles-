1 Meqabyan

Chapter 22

1 Remember — behold — the names of those who do good, and do not forget their deeds.

2 And be zealous, that your name may be as the name of their fathers — that in heaven you may rejoice with them in the dwelling of light, which He prepared for the elect, [for] kings and princes that did the will of God.

3 Again, know and learn the names of the evil kings and princes — that they may be punished and put to shame among men after their death.

4 Who in heaven shall be punished with the wicked and the ʿalawəyanə — that did not the will of God; for though they see and hear, they do not make their way straight.

5 But you, do not go in the way of the wicked, whom God rejected because of the evil of their deeds; but be elect, and integrity and uprightness.

6 And open to righteousness, and justify the poor and the dying; and deliver [them] from the hand of those who oppress them — the sinners.

7 And stand for the dying as a father, that you may deliver him from the hand of the one who oppresses him; and dread [shall fall] when he is cut off from among the dying in your midst — that you not be afraid in the sea of fire, in which are punished the sinners that did not repent.

8 Make straight your steps in the way of peace, and seek peace, and follow after it — for the eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, and His ears to their supplications.

9 Be holy to the LORD against those who do evil, that He may root out their memorial from the earth; and there shall not remain of them any that is left — neither in the wilderness, nor in ʿarəfət.

10 And I will not turn away My face, until I destroy those who do not keep My word — for I am a jealous God, who repays those who hate Me, and those who do not keep My word.

11 And I will honor those who honor Me, and keep My word.
