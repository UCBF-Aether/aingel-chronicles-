1 Meqabyan

Chapter 18

1 Remember how you came to God, and all was in His hand; and stand firm in the work which you have done, that you may stand before Him.

2 For so are the giant children — evil and wicked — and there is none strong nor mighty among them; and their stature, and the greatness of their seed; and they did not set God before them, and do not know that He is their Maker, who brought them from where they were not.

3 But as for their fathers — while they were angels, they sang with the angels in the heavens; but when they sinned, their lust, and they went down into the punishment of judgment forever.

4 And they took wives from the daughters of men, for God made for them flesh like that of men, that He might bring them into bondage through the pride of their heart, and reprove them, if they kept the commandment and the law.

5 But as for them, they did not keep the commandment, and He brought them down into the judgment of burning fire, with Diyabolos their father; and because of them the days were shortened for man, for God changed them — the angels who had sinned — into men.

6 And he led astray the children of men with them, and brought them down into Sheol, in their judgment.

7 But in former time, the days of man — when they, to 120 years — for the days of man were shortened, because the angels sinned with the daughters of Qayəl.

8 And God said: "My spirit shall not remain in them, for they are flesh and blood."

9 And therefore our days are shortened; and moreover there are those who die while they are babes, for few are our days beside our fathers', because of our sins and our iniquities.

10 But as for our fathers, long were their days, for they kept the commandment, and did not turn from God.

11 And they instructed their children, that they might teach them; and they instructed their offspring, that they should not transgress the commandment of God.

12 And therefore long were their days in righteousness, because they did not transgress the commandment of God, with their children and their offspring.
