1 Meqabyan

Chapter 32

1 But you, O kings and princes, hear my word, and keep my commandment, says God of all who rules; as the children of Israel despised me, and served other gods after me, while I am God their God, who kept them, and saved them, and them from their fathers, and fed them with all that they desired.

2 And I made them to ride upon the high places of the earth, and fed them with the blessing of the land; and as they desired: the fruit of the vine which they did not plant, and oil of the olive, and the of water, the sweet which is not bitter.

3 And honey, and milk of cattle, with fat of wheat, and he clothed śärgʷə, and all that they desired.

4 And there is none that withheld from them that which they asked of me.
