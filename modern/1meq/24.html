1 Meqabyan

Chapter 24

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 But Gideon, when he believed God, with his small host — and the thousands of hosts which had no number, as locusts, and their host.

2 Trust you not in your gods; for there is no god beside Me. I am King and Governor.

3 For I am He, the Lord your God, who brought you forth from the land of your bondage, and raised you up, and you, and you; and why have you set up and made king another god beside Me?

4 And all this have I done for you; and what have I given you, and what have I desired of you, except the remedy of your salvation — that you walk in My law and in My ordinances and in My commandment? And I have given you the remedy of your souls.

5 Says the Lord, to whom all: I will bring your heads into humiliation and captivity and.

6 For the wrath of God shall come upon those who do this, and upon those who listen to them, and upon those who do their will, and upon those who go with them, and upon those who walk in their commandment.

7 For a people shall rise up against you, whom you do not know and who do not know you, and they shall devour the fruit of your labor — because you have not done the will of God, which He did for you, as His prophets spoke, from Samuel the prophet onward; Ezekiel and Enoch spoke, and Asaph sang.

8 And he said: The enemies shall come, their loins girt, tearing their garments, and there shall be no other law among them, except fornication and drunkenness; and they weave gold and silver, and every work of affliction which God does not do hat.

9 But they shall prevail against the, in fornication and drunkenness; when they wake from their sleep, from morning until evening they run in evil works; and for their feet there is no way of peace, but their path is shame, and in their way.

10 And they do not know the way of peace, and the fear of God is not before their eyes; they are a wanton people and, that have no salt, and no faith; drunkards and fornicators and winebibbers and drunkards; and without law they have corrupted in the pasture, and they pour blood, and and, and they seize the goods of their masters by iniquity.

11 And mockers without law, and dissolute, and there is no fear in their faces; for they do not fear God who made them.

12 And they are not ashamed before the man that sees them, nor before the one who rebuks them, nor before the elder; and when they hear of the goods of the world, they forget that which their eyes have not seen, and when they have seen with their eyes, it seems to them what they have uttered with their mouths; for the fear of God is not before their eyes.

13 And their rulers also take counsel with fornicators; and what they have spoken in the morning, they undo in the evening; for they are liars, and there is no uprightness in their mouths.

14 And their kings also, the crafty, run to evil; for they oppress the poor and needy, and they do not drive them to the stranger from the hand of those who oppress them.

15 And therefore there shall be no kings that are; but they shall to the stranger and to.

16 But they are the murderers and the proud, who drink blood, and have no mercy, like a mother, and a bird like its young; for narrow is all their way that they have seen, and that which is theirs they forget.

17 And they have no mercy on the poor and the stranger; but they love to gather to themselves, and seize the goods of their masters by violence; and all that they find they heap up, that they may store it in their treasuries, and rejoice in it.

18 Woe to their souls, when God shall take them in His anger; because they did not establish their ordinance in their life; for they shall be suddenly extinguished, as the one who goes forth to the way and finds nothing, and brings nothing into his house.

19 And likewise they shall be extinguished in the land in one, when God rebuks them; they shall perish in their time. For God does not do hasten to quench him, but waits until he enter into repentance.

20 But if he does not do enter into repentance, God shall quench them, as of old those who were before them, who did not keep the commandment of God in purity.

21 And the fornicators, the flesh of man, and the drinkers of blood — and the fear of God is not in them at any time; for quickly they pass to the, when they rise from their, and they do not rest from the work of iniquity.

22 Of fornication and drink and in evil, and ḫagwələ — that they may destroy many souls upon the earth.
