1 Meqabyan

Chapter 3

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And there came a voice from heaven, saying: "Abiya, and Sila, and Fentos — the work of God Most High — as you have seen these who died and rose again before you, so shall you rise after your death, and your faces shall shine in the kingdom of heaven."

2 And they went with them to, and were [numbered?] with the martyrs there.

3 And when they prayed, and, and worshipped God, neither death nor the king's punishment frightened them.

4 And they went to them; and they were not afraid of them, but were as sheep that have no evil in them. And when they came to them, they seized them, and beat them, and bound them, and scourged them, and brought them to the king, and set them before him.

5 And the king spoke again and said to them: "You transgressors — how is it that you do not sacrifice, nor bow down, to my gods?"

6 And these holy and blessed and elect and glad ones — Abiya and Sila and Fentos — the honored, answered him as with one mouth:

7 And they said to him boldly, O king: "We will neither sacrifice nor bow down to unclean gods, which have neither heart nor mind."

8 "And moreover — gold and silver, wood and stone, the work of the hands of the children of men, the work of men's hands, which have neither soul, nor heart, nor mind; and they do not smell what is set before them, and do not perceive what is."

9 And the king answered them: "Why do you thus, and against my honored gods? For."

10 And they raised their voices and said to him: "But as for us, we will despise them, and honor them not; for they are as nothing to us."

11 And the king spoke again and said to them: "According to the evil of your deeds will I punish you, and with great affliction, and with scourging, and with fire."

12 "And now tell me: will you sacrifice to my gods, or not? Else I will lift against you the scourge,, the rope, and the sword."

13 And they raised their voices and said to him: "We will neither bow down nor sacrifice to unclean gods." And the king commanded that they beat them with, and afterward with, and after that them, until.

14 And then they bound them, and cast them into the house of burning, which is within the house of, until he should devise their death and their punishment.

15 And they led them into the house of burning, and bound them with a grievous bond without mercy; but they remained in the house of burning three days and three nights.

16 And after three days the king commanded that a herald go forth, that there assemble the princes, and the counsellors, and the, and the elders of the city.

17 And the king's herald went about; Sirusaydan commanded that they bring these blessed ones — Abiya and Sila and Fintos — and set them before him, bound and afflicted.

18 And the king said to them: "Behold what you have chosen, while you stayed these three days; and."

19 And the blessed ones lifted up their to God, and said to him: "But as for us, we are, and we will not to what you speak — full of evil and uncleanness."

20 And; and he commanded that they stone them, and renew their wounds, and their blood upon the ground.

21 And again he commanded that they burn them with, and their flesh; and they did so. And the blessed ones said:.

22 And again he commanded that they bring wild beasts — leopards, and bears, and lions — that they might tear them.

23 And he commanded the keepers of the beasts to set them upon them; and they did as he commanded them. And they bound the holy martyrs, and again bound them with.

24 But the beasts; and when they came to the martyrs, they bowed down to them, and them.

25 And they came, and frightened the keepers; and they led them until they brought them before the king.

26 And there were slain — five persons.

27 And many were shattered as they cried out in terror, until the king left his throne and fled; and with they seized the beasts, and led them to their dens.

28 And they came —

29 And the martyrs lifted up [their voice?] and said to their brethren: "Is it not for us that after we stand as martyrs? And if you fear, go and flee."

30 And their young brethren said: "We will stand with you before the king; and if you die, we will die with you."

31 And then the king saw, that the blessed ones.

32 And the king commanded that they seize the five brethren, and cast them into the house of burning, until he should devise their punishment; and into the house of burning, and bound them with a grievous bond without mercy.

33 And king Sirusaydan said:.

34 "But I will them according to the evil of their deeds; and I will burn their flesh with fire until they become ashes, like upon a great mountain, that the wind may carry them away."

35 And after he said this, three; and he commanded that they bring the holy ones, and the holy ones drew near. He commanded that they kindle a fire in the furnace, and in a great, and they cast into it of fire — pisa, and papira, and sabḥa, and maʿarä gəra, and ʾäfəlaṭis, and kəbərit.

36 And when the furnace was kindled, they went to the king, saying: "We have done as you commanded us."

37 And he commanded that they take them and cast them into the furnace of fire; and they did as the king commanded them. And they cast the blessed ones into it, committing their souls to God.

38 And the angels received them, and led their souls into the bosom of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob — into the garden of delight.
