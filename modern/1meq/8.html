1 Meqabyan

Chapter 8

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 And concerning the resurrection of the dead also — hear my word: they plant trees, and make hedges, and vineyards, and wine; and they make [wine] from it — for God brings forth the fruit from where there was not.

2 The showers of water and of earth —

3 And God shall water its root from —

4 The winds shall gather the souls — of water, and of earth, and of the sun, and of the wind — for this he went, as the going-forth from the flesh.

5 And the Spirit of God shall give fruit in the midst of them; even so [shall be] the resurrection of the dead.

6 And He said: From —

7 The earth was where it was, and became ashes; and the water was where it was, and became dew.

8 And the wind also was where it was, and became wind; and the fire also was where it was, and became the burning of the sun.

9 And the Spirit of God shall return into the hand of its Maker, and shall remain in that which He willed; for when the time comes, He willed.

10 And He shall set them in the houses of light — the souls of the righteous; but the souls of the sinners He shall shut up in the houses of darkness, until the appointed time He brings them forth.

11 And He said to Ezekiel the prophet: "Call to the four winds, that they may gather, and become one spirit." (Ezek 37:9)

12 And when he prophesied thus, with one word they were gathered from the four winds.

13 And He brought the water of dew; and again He brought the burning of the sun.

14 And again He brought the ashes of the earth; and the wind also He brought as breath.

15 And God also brought the soul from its dwelling — from the bones — and with one word they were gathered, and there was a resurrection.

16 And again I will show you that which is a parable to you: there shall be evening, and you shall sleep; and there shall be morning, and you shall rise. But you — when you sleep, you are dead.

17 And when you awake, [it is] a parable of the resurrection; but the night also is a parable of this world, whose darkness is all of it — and the whole world sleeps in it, for the darkness of night covers them.

18 And the coming of the morning also is a parable of the resurrection of the dead: in it the darkness is scattered, and there shall be light in the whole world; and man shall rise, and be fruitful in his work.

19 And so this is the new world, and the resurrection of the dead; for this world is passing, like this night.

20 And when the sun comes, and shines — a parable of this world — as David says: "And in the sun He set His tabernacle." (Ps 19:4)

21 And when He shall come in the new world — as the sun shines now — when God shall come to him over it, He shall shine in the new world; for He says: "A sun that does not do set, and a lamp that is not quenched — God is light to him." (cf. Isa 60:19-20)

22 And again He shall raise the dead in the twinkling of an eye; and again I will set before you one parable from your sowing which you sow — and it lives in it — whether you have reaped wheat, or you have reaped barley, or you have reaped — and every work of man which is sown in all the earth: if it does not do dy, there is none that lives. (1 Cor 15:52, 36-37)

23 And when he dies, his flesh is devoured in the dust with his, like the flesh of man which you see.

24 And his flesh is devoured in the earth; it —

25 Behold, it is — if it does not do dy, it cannot live; and when you bring forth grain in abundance.

26 And He shall give from God fruit in this grain which was sown, and He shall clothe [it with] flesh anew. (cf. 1 Cor 15:38)

27 And, and, and, and — it shall not be hidden from you; but behold, how abundant is the of that which you sowed!

28 Do not be a fool, and behold your seed — how abundant! Even so [shall be] the resurrection of the dead; and as they shall be recompensed according to their works. (1 Cor 15:36)

29 And again hear my word: for seed is not changed to another seed — for if you sowed wheat, it.

30 Barley — and if you sowed thus, it; And from among the trees: if you plant a fig, its fruit shall yield for; and if you plant a walnut, there shall be to you.

31 And if you plant sweet fruit, it shall yield bitter; and if you plant a bitter tree, they shall eat the of sweetness.

32 Even so: if a sinner dies, they shall the of the righteous at the resurrection; and if a righteous man dies, they shall the of the sinner at the resurrection — but all, according to his work, shall be recompensed; and according to the fruit of his doing, and according to the work of his hands, he shall be recompensed; and there is none that shall be in the sin of another.

33 The cedar of Lebanon is planted, and makes long and great boughs — but if the dew does not do fall upon it, its shall not; but it shall wither and be plucked up.

34 And is filled from its root — if the rain of does not do come down upon it.

35 And so, if it does not do come down upon them — and to life from God — the dead shall not be raised.
