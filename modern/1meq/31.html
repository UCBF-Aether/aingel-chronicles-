1 Meqabyan

Chapter 31

📖 Study notes on this chapter — commentary from the public-domain greats, kept apart from the Scripture text.

1 Kings are not appointed without my will, and the poor are not without my command, and the mighty do not prevail without my will.

2 And I gave to David grace, and wisdom to Solomon; and I added years to Hezekiah.

3 And I shortened the days of Goliath; and I gave strength to Samson, and again I weakened his strength.

4 And I saved him — I, David my servant — from the hand of Goliath the räʾäyətawi.

5 And yet from the hand of Saul the king, and yet from the hand of Kal'u the räʾäyətawi who fought against him, and from the hand of all those who fought against him and warred against him; for he kept my commandment.

6 And I loved him, even him; and to all those who kept my commandment — kings and princes — I loved them, and gave them strength and victory over their enemies; for they gladdened me.

7 And again I gave them the land of inheritance which I sware to their fathers; a pure and broad land, that they might inherit the land of their fathers.
