1 Meqabyan

Chapter 26

1 But the poor — now they reckon in their; but if the rich are increased, they are as dry wood, which has no moisture; and the one who has no moisture puts forth no root, and the one who has no root puts forth no branch.

2 But if it puts forth no branch, it bears no fruit; for the branch is for the bearing of fruit; and the one who has no fruit has no good work; for the head of man is the fruit of righteousness.

3 But the one who does righteousness brings forth uprightness, and God rejoices over the man that does righteousness and uprightness.

4 And He gives his prayer to the one who prays, and his, and does not do forsak the righteous, because of the righteousness which he did.

5 But the sinner that is without repentance, because of the evil of his works which he did — for righteous is God, and He loves righteousness; and He does not do respect the person of the mighty, nor the poor in judgment; for He is the King of heaven and earth, and every soul is in His hand.
