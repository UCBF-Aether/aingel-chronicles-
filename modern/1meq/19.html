1 Meqabyan

Chapter 19

1 And when the children of Cain multiplied, they made harps and timbrels and psalteries and bəʿəza, and all täwənetä and zäfänä.

2 And again there were born children to Cain — good and polluted — from the wife of Abel the righteous, whom he killed for her sake, for polluted was she; and after he killed his brother, he took [a wife] there, and ʾəntakətəni was she.

3 And he married from his father's [house] — he took his [wife] there — into the desert of Qefaz, by the road of ʿArab; and her children, the polluted ones, who became polluted like ʾAmon.

4 And therefore the angels descended to the children of Cain, and they did not delay a single hour, and they took wives for themselves, whom they chose.

5 And therefore God was angry with us and with them, for together we were led astray into error with them because of their sins.

6 And Diyabolos also led astray our father Adam and our mother Eve into his error, while he deceived them and said to them: "You shall be gods, like God your Maker."

7 But as for them, in their they believed, and transgressed the commandment of their Maker, who brought them forth from nothingness, that they might bow down and praise His holy name.

8 But as for them who despised the gift of God for themselves — He, their Lord, forsook them; and to Him.

9 As David says: "In his pride the sinner scorns the poor; for through the pride of Diyabolos, Adam — [who had been] established in the judgment of righteousness before God his God — [fell].

10 And likewise we — [like] the angels who sinned with the daughters of Qayəl — were led astray into their error; and therefore our days are shortened from the days of our fathers, which God gave us.

11 But as for them, they did good, and there is nought whereby evil may come nigh them, for they made firm their hearts toward God, and they teach their children and their offspring, that they do not depart from the commandment of God which He taught them.

12 But as for them, if they do [good], yet do not tell their children, and do not teach them, there is none who shall them.

13 As David says: "And they did not hide from their children to the [next] generation, but told the praises of God — His might and His wonders which He did — that they might teach their children, that they might and do [good], and keep His judgment to God, and that they might keep the commandment, as their fathers who kept [it] to God in the śənä of their deeds."

14 And they did not forsake the commandment which their fathers kept for them, when the fear of God came upon them, nor His judgment — even as they were taught by their fathers.

15 And their children also were taught by their fathers, that they might their gəʿəzomu, and praise to their Maker, for they kept the commandment and loved Him.

16 And He hears their prayer, and does not do hide Himself from them; but as for Him, He is merciful.

17 Abundant is His, and He does not do execute with all His punishments.
