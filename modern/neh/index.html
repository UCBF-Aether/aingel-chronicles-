Nehemiah

Nehemiah continues the homecoming that Ezra began, but its hero is a builder. Nehemiah was cupbearer to the Persian king, a trusted servant far from home, when news came that Jerusalem's walls still lay in ruins. With the king's permission he travels there, rallies a discouraged people, and rebuilds the wall in the face of mockery and threats, all in 13 chapters of energetic, first-person storytelling. In this edition the text is the King James Version of 1611, printed as received. Nehemiah belongs in this canon as part of the shared story of restoration, showing that prayer and practical work belong together. It pairs naturally with Ezra, and together they show a small community recovering its life. Watch for how often Nehemiah pauses mid-task to pray, as if prayer were simply part of the work.
