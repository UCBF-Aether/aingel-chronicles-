Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Isaiah is again the witness: the Holy Trinity, which was from of old, was revealed through Isaiah the prophet, and his prophecy is fulfilled concerning its manifestation. The chapter also gathers Gospel scenes, the dove descending and the Father's voice saying 'This is my Son, whom I love', and on Mount Tabor, and concludes that Father, Son, and Spirit are each Living and Speaking, joined in will, power, and work.
