The Text — Josippon 55 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 Morning. And on the morning of Friday — days for the day of the Roman's rest — they spoke concerning him, that he showed you kingship; and you did not know that they would stray(?).

2 And they were offered up. And thy priests say unto the people: This which he said unto you — you did not know that he would have mercy on you, that he might keep your offences in what you wrought in your doings — mercy. And it shall be seen

3 mightily, upon the kingship. And they clothed you — Kasamido, who is born — from upon you kingship and dominion; and he, from upon the rod and the crown of thorns, made his head shine: this deed, in seven, at the seventh hour; this was accomplished(?).

4 And they took counsel together in the kingdom of the nations, and clothed you with garments of lights; ... thy day [?].

5 It shall be within the order of the day from which the Devil fell. And they clothed our Lord with garments of kingship, and mocked him because of the garments of Adam,

6 which was hidden from him in his gluttony. On Wednesday, the hour that is therein — they named for their brethren the beasts and the birds; and therein are the trees of the riches of our Lord in ...; and they brought him forth, that they should follow him.

7 And at the second hour, which is therein, Eve was formed from the side of Adam on Friday; and therein are the trees, the trees of our Lord; and they brought him forth, that he might [?].

8 Adam entered from the tree at the second hour on Friday, and he was naked of the garments of the angels; so also our Lord followed(?) the Devil — the tree — naked, from the second hour until they were driven out. And as he brought Adam out of Paradise at the tenth hour on Friday, so therein is the taste of the vine, which is wondrous(?), with gall and with the bitterness of death; for it is from the violence of the Devil ...(?) from fear, but death in its bitterness, which cannot be hidden: today is the taste of the bitter vine(?) at the tenth hour, in place of the bitter death which Adam tasted, and his seed. And at that time he conquered the Devil [?].

9 He lightened its riches. And the second hour, which is therein — Adam and Eve were in it, and they brought them unto the tree, and spread out his hands ... and they hung his hands(?); and they crucified our Lord(?) upon the tree, and hung him; they hung ... his hands(?).

10 They crucified him upon the tree from which Adam ate [?]; and as ... death and his hosts, and they slew — instead of Bisihab(?) — at the tenth hour on Friday; and he went down and entered into Sheol ... and saved Adam the man and all his seed from the tree; and he shall be ... (?), instead of Bisihab, without violence, as he revealed unto thee in the book of the interpretation of the assembly and of the crucifixion. The word of the prophets was fulfilled, who said that he is he who [?].

11 ... their light, that Adam might be raised, and that they might be comforted from them, and the dead live ... he destroyed the Devil and his hosts with all his ...; as he said in the second ...(?): death — as he revealed unto us in that book, and saved them

12 for Adam and for all his seed, from them; and he quickened them from Sheol, and raised them up unto life everlasting

13 He is he who in joy ... made them inherit the order of the Devil.

14 Forty-one(?) ... of them, which fell from it.

15 And therefore it behoveth all ...(?), from that which was crucified, men and women, priests; and they appointed again, that they should [?] — this(?) — he cursed from Paradise. Its apostles fast on Wednesday and on Friday, a warfare, until the tenth hour; for it behoveth men and women to fast in it until the tenth hour.

16 And they are likened unto the disciples in their grief, and are reckoned with God — I, in the sufferings of his flesh — that he may be a partaker with him in his joy and in the morning. And a partaker with his disciples in their joy and in their glory. And whoso eateth in it before the tenth hour, a little ..., for him who was slain for his sake; and ... suffering.

17 On Friday always they grieve with the disciples of our Lord, and they suffer

18 their flesh in blood, as they said ... unto our Lord, from grief — the suffering which was entrusted for their sake in his flesh on Friday; he shall arise for them: this, according as they suffered, ... their wages(?); for my flesh — John — from hunger and thirst, and ... his ...(?), the ...(?) which was entrusted for his sake. And he shall partake with the naked(?) one ... and the Jews(?) and the Devil and his hosts.

19 in their joy. And their portion was with them; and the apostles said: If a man be a priest, he shall be removed from the priesthood; and if he be a layman, he shall be driven out from the house of the Christians, from the tenth hour.

20 He pierced the Devil and his hosts; and Adam rejoiced, and his seed: this is the cause ...(?) on Wednesday and on Friday; and from ... he revealed unto us that he is from ...; but in his bitterness, when he brake their ..., and the gushing of water and blood from his side, from after death. And again he brought them down from the tree, and buried him in the tomb on Friday. And on the day which is ...(?), he sought from us, and gave us joy.

21 He was sought. And therefore it behoveth all men, that they should [?].

22 In the name of his word. Amen. A discourse of Simeon, concerning the priests: fasting ... It is ...(?); it behoveth that there be the fellowship of the love of God; may he make the eyes of thy heart to shine with the light of the Holy Spirit ... that thou mayest learn the mysteries of the Godhead.

23 First, I have offered unto thee the glory of praise and of sanctification and of the ordinances of today and of order; and likewise I offer(?) unto thee the glory of the fast of these days, Wednesday and Friday; for it behoveth all ...(?), men and women, ... [?]; and we also with them in two portions, and let us rejoice with them, and ... on the day which is always in the second hiding, that we may be with them in their praise and in their glory for ever. May this be unto all kingship ... in the morning, and in the mercy of ... [?] Jesus Christ for ever.

24 I ask, that thou mayest know: for is he not ...? As concerning food and drink alone he is not ...; and he hath no sin. For all that our Lord did, when he appeared on earth in the flesh, he hath no morning; for he did all the works of men, which have no morning; and he did eat and [?].

25 and drink. And therefore we make known unto you: food and drink are sin. And therefore he said in his holy Gospel: For everything that entereth into the mouth of men ...; and afterwards, I and he who is forbidden food and drink which have no sin — the fast is his — he is forbidden from desire, or a wife alone.

26 Which ... for us from sin. And he is his, when God created

27 for our father Adam and for our mother Eve, and their sin within ...; they were not ...; and desire was given unto them, or a wife [?].

28 When the natures were added — the riches of others, a hundred — and they devoured it and destroyed it. And when Adam was created, and ... were in them, the natures which were added — the riches of others — he created not, forty natures within them, in their balance, naked, with power. They burned in their balance, naked, with power; with dust, with power; and dryness, naked, with moisture, with power. And one was joined unto the other, for power is within them ...(?); and ... from them ... [?].

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter sets the Friday crucifixion against Adam and Eve: the tree from which Adam ate, the second hour and the tenth hour on Friday, descent and the raising of Adam and his seed from Sheol, and the destruction of the Devil and his hosts. It then turns, through a discourse of Simeon on the priests, to fasting, food and drink, and the Gospel saying about what enters a man's mouth. Much of the second half is fragmentary.
