How to read the labels

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter is set as column blocks, not verses, as the Trace panel notes. Its surviving thread is love of neighbour: the first commandment to love God and the second, like it, to love one's neighbour as oneself, followed by prohibitions, do not steal, bear false witness, lie, covet, or revile, with sayings of Paul and David on hatred and liars, and on judgment.
