Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter turns to the creation of the angels, which it says the Lord left mentioned, and to David's psalm calling the heavens to rejoice. The wording is heavily broken, so this summary follows only the readable thread: angels, a psalm of David, and the kingdom spoken of in that psalm.
