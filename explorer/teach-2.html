Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Verses 9 to 15 continue the teaching in fragmentary form, with many readings marked uncertain in the translation. The chapter speaks of light given within the hearts of those who fear him, of a likeness enduring for ever, of being chosen out of all the ways of Satan, called here the pilot of souls, and of one who was from before the world with the Father and saw the world in the enclosure of sin. It speaks of flesh put on, corruptible and incorruptible, and closes with one preached by the apostles, praised by the angels, and glorified by the prophet.
