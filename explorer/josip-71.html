Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Written as an exposition of the praise of Moses and Miriam, this chapter reads the Red Sea song (“in glory he hath triumphed gloriously: the horse and his rider he cast into the sea”) as Christ's praise on the wood of the cross, and Pharaoh's drowning as the Devil and his hosts cast into hell below, with Christ drawing righteousness up as Moses drew Israel through the sea.
