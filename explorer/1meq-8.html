Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter teaches the resurrection through parables: as trees, vines and crops are brought forth from nothing, as the four winds, dew, sun's burning and earth's ashes are gathered at Ezekiel's word into one spirit, and as sleep gives way to morning and sown seed dies to live again, so God will raise the dead in the twinkling of an eye. Seed keeps its kind, wheat yields wheat, fig yields fig, and so each person is recompensed according to his own work, none bearing another's sin; the righteous rise to houses of light, the sinners to houses of darkness until the appointed time. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
