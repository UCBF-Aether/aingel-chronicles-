The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — 2 Book of the Covenant 6 (J. Cooper & A. J. Maclean, The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1902), public domain (archive.org cu31924029296170))

1 But if they are severally chosen to receive‘ the laver, let them be proved and investigated first, how they have lived while catechumens,’ if they have honoured widows, if they have visited the sick, if they have walked in all meekness and love,” if they were earnest in good works. But let them be borne witness to by those who bring them.

2 And when they hear the Gospel, let a hand be laid on them daily.

3 Let them be exorcised from that day when they are chosen. And let them be baptized in the days of Pascha. And when the days approach, let the bishop exorcise each one of them separately by himself, so that he may be persuaded that he is pure. For if there be one that is not pure, or in whom is an unclean spirit, let him be reproved by that unclean spirit.

4 If then any one is found under any such imagination, let him be removed from the midst [of them], and let him be reproved and reproached that he hath not heard the word of the commandments and of instruction faithfully, because the evil and strange spirit abided in him.

5 Let those who are about to receive the laver be taught on the fifth day of the last week only, to wash and bathe’ their heads. But if any woman then be in the customary flux, let her also take in addition another day, washing and bathing beforehand.

6 Let them fast both [on] the Friday and [on] the Saturday.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Those severally chosen to receive the laver are first to be proved and investigated: how they have lived while catechumens — whether they honoured widows, visited the sick, walked in all meekness and love, and were earnest in good works — and they are to be borne witness to by those who bring them. When they hear the Gospel, a hand is to be laid on them daily. They are to be exorcised from the day they are chosen, and baptized in the days of Pascha; when the days approach, the bishop is to exorcise each one separately by himself, so that he may be persuaded that he is pure — for if there is one who is not pure, or in whom is an unclean spirit, he is to be reproved by that unclean spirit. If anyone is found under any such imagination, he is to be removed from their midst, reproved and reproached for not having heard the word of the commandments and of instruction faithfully, because the evil and strange spirit abided in him. Those about to receive the laver are to be taught, on the fifth day of the last week only, to wash and bathe their heads; but if any woman then is in the customary flux, she is also to take an additional day, washing and bathing beforehand. They are to fast both on Friday and on Saturday. Interpreted Chapter text above; the study-notes file for this chapter is silent, so no commentary is cited

Unfamiliar terms

  • laver. The baptismal washing: the chapter uses “receive the laver” and “be baptized” for the same step.
  • catechumen. A person under instruction before baptism, whose life is examined before being chosen for the laver.
