The Text — Kebra Nagast 18 (E. A. Wallis Budge, The Queen of Sheba and Her Only Son Menyelek (1922), public domain, for chs 1–118; Ge'ez text for ch. 119)

*18. HOW THE ORTHODOX FATHERS AND BISHOPS AGREED*

1 And again the Council of the Three Hundred and Eighteen answered and said, "Amen. This is the salvation of the children of Adam. For since the Tabernacle of the Law of God hath come down, they shall be called, 'Men of the house of God,' even as David saith, 'And His habitation is in Zion.' And again he saith by the mouth of the Holy Ghost, 'And My habitation is here, for I have chosen it. And I will bless her priests, and I will make her poor to be glad. And unto David

2 will I give seed in her, and upon the earth one who shall become king, and moreover, in the heavens one from his seed shall reign in the flesh upon the throne of the Godhead. And as for his enemies they shall be gathered together under his footstool, and they shall be sealed with his seal.'"

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

In plain language: this chapter records how the Orthodox Fathers and bishops agreed, as the book presents it: Amen, this is the salvation of the children of Adam. Since the Tabernacle of the Law of God has come down, they say, God's people shall be called men of the house of God, as David says, His habitation is in Zion. The chapter closes with a royal promise: seed in Zion, a king on earth, and in heaven one from his seed reigning in the flesh on the throne of the Godhead, with his enemies gathered under his footstool.
