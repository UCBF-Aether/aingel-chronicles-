Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter opens as an address to kings and rulers — walk not in the proud man’s ways, but fear the Lord God who created you — and then retells the Balak and Balaam story: Balak son of Zippor hires Balaam to curse Israel, but God turns the curse into blessing, and Balaam will not transgress the word of God. From that example the chapter draws its warning to the present: former peoples who despised God were destroyed by flood, by enemies, and by exile, their priests led away and Jerusalem made a ruin plowed as a field. Yet he will not destroy Israel utterly, but spare them for the sake of their fathers Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob — and kings are told to remember those who walked in purity, that they might inherit the kingdom of heaven.
