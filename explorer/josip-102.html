Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter does not read as ordinary verses: it keeps the manuscript's own register identifiers. Register A reckons years and remainders for the nativity and the baptism; register B gives canticles and prayers, including a Benedictus-like song, the prayer of Simeon, and Song of Songs passages, many marked with holes; register C reckons the Passover, resurrection and ascension by the epacts of sun and moon in the month of Mäggabit, and closes on faith and works.
