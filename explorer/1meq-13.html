Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter pronounces woe on Tyre, Sidon and the nations from Samaria and Galilee to Cyprus and beyond Jordan, covered in darkness and the shadow of death, and on a proud son raised up as God's instrument who sets his image everywhere and says he will set his throne above the heavens and be as the Most High, addressed with the words, How art thou fallen, thou morning star from heaven. Sun darkened, moon turned to blood and stars falling mark the latter days, the enemy is brought down to punishment and Gehenna, and the chapter closes by recalling Israel's own story: wonders in Egypt, the pillar of cloud and fire, and manna at Sinai, all forgotten in rebellion. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
