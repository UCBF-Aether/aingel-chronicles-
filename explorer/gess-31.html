The Text — Gәssәw 31 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 [Topic:] Concerning the setting [?] of the bishop over the property of the church.

2 We command therefore that the bishop be the one who shall have control of the property of the church; for he is the one to whom are entrusted the precious souls of men. What, therefore, is all the property that is given to him, that he should have command over it by his will and should feed the poor with it — through them [?], by the hand of the elders and the deacons — in the fear of God and in trembling?

3 And he shall take for himself [?] of it according to his desire, if he is in need [?] concerning what they ask — from him [?], the brethren from afar [?] who visit him [?] ask of him [?] — so that he may not lack what they ask of him [?]; for it is a law of God [commanding?] those who serve at the altar, that they shall also be sustained from the altar; for the soldiers of a king will not fight against his enemies at their own sustenance.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The bishop is to have control of church property, because the precious souls of men are entrusted to him; he feeds the poor with it through the elders and deacons, in the fear of God and in trembling. He may take from it for himself according to his need, including for visiting brethren from afar, on the ground the canon states as a law of God: those who serve at the altar are sustained from the altar, as a king's soldiers do not fight at their own sustenance.
