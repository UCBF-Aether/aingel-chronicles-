How to read these pages

On every chapter page, the text itself and the editorial explanation are kept visibly apart. The Scripture panel holds the passage in its own bordered parchment, in larger type. Everything else — the Understand, Trace, and Perspectives panels — is editorial explanation, set in a different background and headed as explanation. Never mistake one for the other.

Every substantive claim on these pages carries one of three labels:

  • Documented — directly supported by a cited source.
  • Interpreted — a reasoned explanation or proposed relationship.
  • Unresolved — evidence is insufficient to establish the claim.

A claim does not become Documented merely because a commentary states it. Missing evidence stays missing, never reconstructed.

What this is

89 reading units, 2082 chapters. No accounts, no payments. Sources are the site’s own corpus, public-domain commentaries, the Concordance, and Sources & Method.

The 81 canonical books appear here as 87 reading units — paired works read apart, plus the Prayer of Manasseh appendix — with 2 bonus works outside the canon, for 89 units in all. See Why 81? for the full breakdown.
