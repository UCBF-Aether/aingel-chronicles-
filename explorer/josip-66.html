Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Headed as a discourse on the priests and the true faith, this chapter names Alexandria and a council under Abba Cyril, and argues the Trinity as undivided: Father, Son and Holy Spirit, one being without separation, shown at the baptism by the voice from heaven, “This is my Son whom I love”, and by “Let us make man in our image”. The text is fragmentary, with several readings marked (?). Interpreted Reading of this chapter's text as printed above; the study-notes file for this chapter is silent
