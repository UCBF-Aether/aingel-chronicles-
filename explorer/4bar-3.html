The Text — 4 Baruch 3 (Ge'ez text (project Ge'ez corpus))

1 And when the sixth hour of the night was come, whereof the Lord had told Jeremiah, that he should go up with Baruch, they went up within the walls of the city, and abode watching.

2 And there was the sound of a trumpet, and angels came forth from heaven, holding torches of fire in their hands, and stood within the walls of the city.

3 And when Jeremiah and Baruch saw them, they wept, saying: Now we know that the word is true.

4 And Jeremiah besought the angels, saying: I beseech you all, destroy not the city, until I ask one word of the Lord;

5 and the Lord answered the angels, saying: Destroy not the city, until I speak with Jeremiah my chosen.

6 Then spake Jeremiah, saying: I beseech thee, my Lord, give me leave to speak with thee. And he said unto him: Speak, my chosen Jeremiah, whatsoever thou wilt.

7 And Jeremiah said unto him: Behold, now we know, my Lord, that thou wilt deliver the city into the hand of the enemy, and the people shall be carried away to Babylon. What wilt thou that I do with the holy vessels of our ministry, which thou hast committed unto us? and what wilt thou that I do concerning them?

8 And the Lord said unto him: Take them, and commit them to the earth, and to the house of the sanctuary, saying:

9 And thou, O earth, hear the voice of thy Creator, who formed thee in the abundance of waters, who sealed thee with seven seals: take again thy beauty [?], and guard the vessels of thy ministry until the coming of the Beloved.

10 And Jeremiah answered and said: I beseech thee, my Lord, shew me what I shall do for Abimelech the Ethiopian; for he hath done many kindnesses unto the people and unto thy servant Jeremiah: he was distinguished [?] above all the men of the city, and he drew me out of the miry pit; and I would not that he should see the destruction and desolation of the city, lest he be grieved.

11 And the Lord said unto Jeremiah: Send him into the vineyard of Agrippa, by the mountain way; and I will hide him, until I bring the people again into the city;

12 and thou, Jeremiah, go with the people until ye come to the land of Babylon, and abide prophesying unto them, until I bring them into their city;

13 but leave thou Baruch here in Jerusalem.

14 And when the Lord had spoken all this unto Jeremiah, he departed from Jeremiah into heaven.

15 And Jeremiah and Baruch entered into the house of the sanctuary, and committed all the vessels of their ministry unto the earth, as the Lord had commanded them; and straightway the earth swallowed them, and they twain abode and wept.

16 And in the morning Jeremiah sent Abimelech, saying: Take a basket, and go by the mountain way into the vineyard of Agrippa, and bring a few figs for the sick of the people; for the favour of the Lord is upon thy head, and his glory. And he went as he commanded him.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

At the sixth hour of the night, as the Lord had told Jeremiah, he and Baruch go up within the walls of the city and watch. The chapter is the night before the fall, spent in watching from the walls, and it ends in the morning with Jeremiah sending Abimelech with a basket to the vineyard of Agrippa for figs.
