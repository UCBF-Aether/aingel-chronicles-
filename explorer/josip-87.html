Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter gives a brief life of Christ: the holy Virgin in Bethlehem in Herod's days, the Magi and the star, baptism in the Jordan, teaching, resurrection on the third day in the month of Miyazya, and ascension, followed by the affliction of believers. Verses 12–30 are printed entirely as [?], so the chapter's second half does not survive in the printed text.
