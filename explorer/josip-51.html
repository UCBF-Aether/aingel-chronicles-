The Text — Josippon 51 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 It was told(?) concerning them: These nations forsake [them] in their borders(?), and they remove them(?), and they afflict them(?), until they return unto God and repent.

2 And they appointed them(?) over them. At the first, God commanded them that they should build a great sanctuary and one city, and should offer therein sacrifices and oblations, and

3 for the feasts, as God commanded them — and the beasts — at the feast of Na't, and at the feast of Baptism, and at the feast of Baptism, which is the feast of days, they should come unto the house of the sanctuary, and come two times, and offer their sacrifices and their hymns.

4 And He requited [?] — God — unto everyone who offereth [?] them, from them, their offering(?).

5 And [as for] him who burneth(?) incense, and [offereth] in all the borders(?) of the land which is round about the house of the sanctuary, which is in the city of Jerusalem; and there was among them(?).

6 They spake together, and God gave unto them, and it was done unto them, that God is [present], that He would send unto them His Christ(?), and would destroy for them their enemies, and would confirm them(?).

7 From among them(?) they shall inherit their land; and He shall appoint their habitation, and shall appoint them; from them(?) they shall not return; they shall perish for ever.

8 [Pointer: no verse text stands at this slot.]

9 Strength: that He shall give them to inherit the plantings — they shall call thee the heavenly [one] — which fell from her, she and her hosts; and whereof they say unto them, that He shall make alive their habitation; the word is this: that He shall raise them(?) from Imi'el unto life; joy entered in; and it is the life which is eternal, which is the house of life unto eternity; and they shall not return, they shall not die, as I have revealed unto thee.

10 In the book of the Interpretation: the incarnation of the Son of God, and His crucifixion — in the book of the Interpretation it was seen(?). And [this] (?) [?] name.

11 This word the prophets used to speak unto David of Israel, and they declare its interpretation; for the word of the prophets is that Christ shall deliver them from their distress, as it saith — from that which I have told thee — and [her] hosts. And that which they say unto them, that He shall give them to inherit the land of Israel — this [?] word — Israel used to think that He would deliver them from the hand of the Philistines and the Canaanites, and give them to inherit their land; and they believed not(?) that they should call thee, and [her] hosts; and they are the worshippers in truth. And when Israel used to serve idols under a king, they did not ... upon them [?].

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Nations that forsake God in their borders, remove his people, and afflict them until they return to him are the chapter's opening subject. God, it says, would send his Christ, and the prophets' word spoken to David of Israel is declared as its interpretation: Christ shall deliver.
