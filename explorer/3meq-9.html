Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter addresses proud man directly: today you are a man, tomorrow you shall be earth and ashes, wood and boards in the grave, and the depths of Sheol shall be given to you and to those who do your works. It traces the pride to a teacher, Säbəlyanos, who refused to bow to the work of his God — and sets against the sinners, who shall be claimed by Satan for transgressing law and ordinance, the righteous, who abstain from foul deeds and forbidden foods, are set apart from evil ways, and whom the Lord loves, has mercy on, and delivers from all their tribulations.
