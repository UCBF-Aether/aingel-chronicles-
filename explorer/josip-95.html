Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter carries the dispute beyond Chalcedon: persons and two orders, Athanasius, the Word made flesh without change, suffering in the flesh by his will while the divinity does not suffer, and the soul-and-body analogy for the union. Its argument is fragmentary, with the key terms, two, orders, union, repeatedly marked (?), so its precise positions should not be stated more firmly than the printed words allow.
