The Text — 1 Enoch 105 (R. H. Charles, The Book of Enoch (London: SPCK, 1917; reprint of his 2nd edition, 1912); Project Gutenberg no. 77935)

*God and the Messiah to dwell with Man.*

1 In those days the Lord bade (them) to summon and testify to the children of earth concerning their wisdom: Show (it) unto them; for ye are their guides, and a recompense over the whole earth.

2 For I and My Son will be united with them for ever in the paths of uprightness in their lives; and ye shall have peace: rejoice, ye children of uprightness. Amen.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

In only two verses, this chapter has the Lord bid His witnesses summon and testify to the children of earth concerning their wisdom, for they are their guides, with a recompense over the whole earth. He promises that He and His Son will be united with the children of uprightness for ever in the paths of uprightness, and they shall have peace.
