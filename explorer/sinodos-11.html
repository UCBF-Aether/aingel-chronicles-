Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 55, its numeral marked corrupt, concerns the prayer heard after teaching: when the teacher has finished his discourse, Christians pray apart, believers separated from unbelievers, and believing women pray apart and stand apart in the church. Believers then assemble among themselves, man with man and woman with woman, and the women cover their heads with veils or headcloths. The chapter's fourth verse is itself noted in the text as lost to severe script corruption, with foreign characters intruding in the source.
