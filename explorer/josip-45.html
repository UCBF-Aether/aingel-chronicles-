Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The Sabbath is the day of God in oldness and in life, the chapter argues, because God wrought all his works in it in oldness and in life. It then turns to Christ entering Jerusalem, welcomed with palm branches as King of the Jews whom they awaited, and reflects on weariness and rest in creation.
