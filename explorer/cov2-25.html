The Text — 2 Book of the Covenant 25 (J. Cooper & A. J. Maclean, The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1902), public domain (archive.org cu31924029296170))

*CONCLUSION*

1 When ye the faithful accomplish these things, teach and instruct one another, causing the catechumens to make progress, as loving all men‘; ye do not perish, but will be in Me and I will be among you.

2 But alway let the faithful take care that before he eat he partake of the Eucharist,’ that he may be incapable of receiving injury.

3 When ye teach these things and keep [them], ye shall be saved, and evil heresy shall not prevail against you.

4 Lo, then, I have taught you now all [things] that ye desire; and those things which I have spoken with you [of] from the beginning, and have taught and commanded you before I should suffer, ye know.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The book's conclusion: when the faithful accomplish these things, teaching and instructing one another and causing the catechumens to make progress, as loving all men, they do not perish but will be in Him and He will be among them. Always the faithful are to take care that before eating they partake of the Eucharist, that they may be incapable of receiving injury. When they teach these things and keep them, they shall be saved, and evil heresy shall not prevail against them. Lo, then, He has taught them now all things that they desire, and the things He spoke with them from the beginning, and taught and commanded them before He should suffer, they know.
