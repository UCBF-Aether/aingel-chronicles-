Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

In a long address to the daughter of Jerusalem, the chapter charges her with the works of Sodom and Gomorrah: a vine planted in pride whose wine brings corruption, child sacrifice to demons, and worship of strange gods she loved more than the Maker who fed her from her youth. God answers that he planted her for honour and holiness and she lifted her head to shame, that she shall go down into Sheol, the lower depths, with unquenched fire, yet those who keep his word and walk in his commandment shall come to blessing and honour before him. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text

Unfamiliar terms

  • Sheol, the lower depths. The chapter itself glosses the name: those who loved strange gods shall go down into Sheol, the lower depths, where its fire is not quenched; the reading of lower depths carries an uncertainty mark in the text. Documented Chapter text, verse 22
  • Gehenna. The place of burning judgment named throughout this book, whence, in this chapter's words, there is no escape for ever. Documented Chapter text, verses 15, 39
