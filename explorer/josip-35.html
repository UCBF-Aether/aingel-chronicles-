The Text — Josippon 35 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 [The text of this verse stands in chapter 34, verses 6 and 8; it is not duplicated here.]

2 [The text of this verse stands in chapter 34, verse 7 (opening) and verse 8; it is not duplicated here.]

3 And He is He who made baptism, that it should set the children of Adam free from the works of the Devil, that they might die pure, and ascend into Paradise, and that He should bear them with that which ascended from Sheol, until they should be in their [?] under the hosts of the Devil; and He ascendeth, and inheriteth the ordinance of the height of heaven.

4 Thou — from her fell the Devil and the hosts of the Devil; they took counsel against the [?] of baptism, concerning the ordinance [?] of this book, that thou mightest enquire of this: God giveth thee understanding unto every work. Thou desirest to know that the angels are pure spirits, without flesh, and the flesh of the beasts is brutish, without a rational soul; and their life is their blood, for they have no rational soul.

5 And when God ordained for Adam that he should abide in the ordinance — thou, from whom fell the Devil, the chief of the angels.

6 God ordained for Adam a pure spirit, rational, prophetic, like the angels; and He set it in living [?] flesh, until the sinner [?] should die, and should inherit with them the judgment which is for ever [?], and should be far removed from the kingdom [?] which is for ever.

7 All this which is [?] in this book: thy being, which the faithful [?] work, that they may prevail thereby over him that is burnt [?], which I mention unto thee and [?] them.

8 And when He made known unto him the glory, that the rational soul, shut up in an animal body, became one with it, and had no power to do the will, as the angels do.

9 And then the soul — the Holy Spirit entered into Adam, and the Holy Spirit dwelt with the rational soul of Adam; and the soul [?] became one and was strengthened over his flesh, which is brutish, by the power of the Holy Spirit; and the flesh obeyed the soul, and was readied to do the will of their Creator.

10 And when Adam obeyed the Devil, and heard from him that wherein he trusted him concerning him, and believed, and transgressed against his Creator, the grace of the Holy Spirit departed from him, and the Devil ruled over him; and there was with his soul an unclean spirit from the satans, in place of the Holy Spirit, which abideth not without will, to do sin.

11 And likewise he did unto all that have been born of his seed, generation after generation.

12 And when Christ came [?] for our life, He set the children of Adam free from the unclean spirit which the Devil setteth over them, that he might make them do sin.

13 And by baptism there returneth unto them the Holy Spirit, whom God breathed into the face of Adam; and they overcome the hosts of the Devil, and He giveth them power to do the will of God; when He is there, He abideth in them, and He dwelleth in them, if it be they who glorify Him and sanctify Him at the times which He hath appointed for them.

14 And if they forsake [?] from His glory and His holiness, the power of God forsaketh them, and they are strengthened unto [?]

Overview

The chapter's first verse is not a new text but a note: it states that its text stands in chapter 34, verses 6 and 8, and is not duplicated here. The remaining verses reflect on the rational soul shut up in an animal body, becoming one with it and without power to do the will, and on the power of God forsaking those who forsake his glory and holiness.
