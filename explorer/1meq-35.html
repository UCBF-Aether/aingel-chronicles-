Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter pronounces woe on the mighty men of Israel who did not justice to the children of the dead, who shut their ears to justice morning and evening and dwelt in pride and lifting up of the head. God will bring scourge and sickness upon them, their city shall become a desert that passers-by clap their hands over in scorn, thorns shall cover it and beasts dwell in it, for the anger of God has fallen on it because of pride of heart. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
