Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter names faithful deliverers, Hosea, Gideon, Samson, Barak, Deborah and Judith, through whom God raised judges to save Israel from oppressors, blessing their cattle, crops and pastures while they kept his way. Meqabis, hearing this word, strives in repentance and orders his whole life by it: tithes, firstfruits, sacrifices and the commandments the Jews keep, he keeps likewise, though his people are Moabite, praying that his repentance be accepted, that seed be given in Zion, and that he not be separated from the congregation of Israel.
