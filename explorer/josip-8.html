Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter concerns the resurrection and the world to come: all shall rise from the dead and live one life without death for ever. After confessing the Holy Spirit, it confesses one holy Church, the congregation of the apostles, as sharing one covenant of resurrection with Christ. The closing verse units survive only as fragments. Interpreted Chapter text as printed above; study-notes file for this chapter is silent
