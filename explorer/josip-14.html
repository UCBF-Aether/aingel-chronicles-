Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Disobedience is the chapter's subject: creatures who displeased the Lord their Creator were cast to the earth for their fall. It then sets out the making of the high heaven and its orders with the four natures beneath, and closes with Eve made from Adam's rib, flesh filling its place.
