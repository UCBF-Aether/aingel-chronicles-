The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — 1 Book of the Covenant 17 (J. Cooper & A. J. Maclean, The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1902), public domain (archive.org cu31924029296170))

1 But to us also Jesus said:—Because that ye also have asked Me concerning the rule ecclesiastical, I deliver and make known to you how ye ought to order and command him who standeth at the head of the Church, and to keep the perfect and just and most excellent rule, in which My Father who hath sent Me is well pleased.

2 Verily I say unto you, that he who knoweth the power of this commandment and of this testament, and doeth according to those things which are written therein, shall be made like! unto the angels who praise My Father and shall be holy unto God.

3 But My Father is mediator and all His host, that if their sins are as the sand of the sea[shore] which cannot be numbered, and any of them, understanding these words, shall do them, these sins shall be forgiven him, and he shall live in Me.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Jesus delivers the rule ecclesiastical: how to order and command him who stands at the head of the Church, and to keep the perfect, just and most excellent rule in which the Father who sent Him is well pleased. Whoever knows the power of this commandment and testament and does what is written in it shall be made like the angels who praise the Father and shall be holy unto God. And the Father is mediator, with all His host, that if their sins are as the sand of the seashore, any of them who understands these words and does them shall have his sins forgiven and shall live in Him.
