Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter pictures the bright dwelling prepared for good kings, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, David, Solomon and Hezekiah, a heavenly palace of gold and silver surpassing the mind of man, set against the fate of kings who judged not in righteousness, saved not the poor and needy, and go down into Sheol, the sides of darkness. It then retells Saul's fall at length: commanded through Samuel to smite Amalek utterly, he spared its king and cattle, his garment was rent as he seized Samuel's robe, Samuel announced God had rent the kingdom from him, and the kingdom passed to David, a warning that God sets kings on thrones and removes them. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
