Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter follows the struggle over the Chalcedon faith under kings and bishops: Abtimanos refusing the king's command to restore that faith, Theodosius of Alexandria, a rival bishop appointed, Jerusalem and Julius, a great judgment and feast, and priests and deacons taking the oblation while pagans are said to have defiled the church. Tears and gaps in the text leave much of the sequence uncertain. Interpreted Reading of this chapter's text as printed above; the study-notes file for this chapter is silent
