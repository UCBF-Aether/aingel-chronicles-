Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A third discourse opens, concerning the interpretation of many words from the Law, and Joshua the son of Nun, which show the glory of the orthodox Church. Its narrative thread begins with Moses: God appeared to him and sent him to Pharaoh, king of Egypt, to demand freedom for sacrifice.
