Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Those who fear His words and do them in truth and with a faithful mind shall watch and pray without ceasing, reckoning continual supplication as a work, not wandering or going about in this world, not anxious, but with an austere soul and a mind that doubteth not, daily taking up the cross to do the will of the Father in heaven with a meek heart. For the Lord, who is anxious about those who trust in the truth and cares for them, sends them what is right and fitting, by the hands of those whom He knows.
