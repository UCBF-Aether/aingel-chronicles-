The Text — Qalementos 33 (Ge'ez text (project Ge'ez corpus, chapter-level))

1 (1) O priest, offer not upon the altar fat and flesh, and set not upon the altar aught except pure wheaten bread, which said, "My flesh it is" — for God called it the bread of heaven. (2) And mix [?] not, except with the juice of the grape — for God called it the water of life. (3) And the standing corn, of the grain of wheat, when it cometh in; and afterward let it not be withheld from you: bring ye the oblation. (4) And let no priest offer wine upon the altar except what is mixed with water — for our Lord Christ said: "I am the water of life."

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The altar offering is strictly limited: no fat or flesh on the altar, only pure wheaten bread, which Christ called his flesh and God called the bread of heaven; nothing mixed except the juice of the grape, called the water of life; the standing corn of wheat brought as oblation; and wine on the altar only mixed with water, for Christ said he is the water of life.
