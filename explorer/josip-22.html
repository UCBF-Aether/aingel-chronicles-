Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A single long discourse unit survives here, on joy, two hosts, and an ordinance that is not the Devil's, with Adam named among those lost. The wording is so broken that no fuller summary can honestly be given; the readable thread is joy set against loss and against the Devil's ordinance.
