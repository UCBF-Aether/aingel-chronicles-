Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A prophet, called the seer, speaks an oracle against the sinner: rejoice now in the hour of light, but the Lord, the Holy One of Israel, will avenge himself upon you, and a sore disease shall come into your heart until your remembrance is destroyed from the earth. Yet the oracle also opens a door — repent from all the evil you have done, humble yourself with sighing and weeping, and the Lord will forgive. Meqabis hears it, weeps before the Lord, clothes himself with dust, tears his garment, and puts on sackcloth. Interpreted The chapter text itself; no study note exists for this chapter
