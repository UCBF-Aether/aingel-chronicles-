The Text — Gәssәw 48 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 [Topic:] Concerning him who does not fast in the forty [days], and on the fourth [day?], and on the sixth [day?].

2 If a bishop or an elder or a deacon, or a reader or a psalm-singer, does not fast in the holy forty days and on the fourth and the sixth, let him be deposed — except if illness of body prevents him [?].

3 And in the case of a layman, let him be expelled.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Clergy, readers, and psalm-singers who do not fast in the holy forty days and on the fourth and the sixth are to be deposed, unless illness of body prevents them; a layman who does not fast is expelled. The canon states the fast, the weekly days, and the single excuse of bodily illness. Interpreted Paraphrase of the chapter's own text; Standard corpus, Gessew 48
