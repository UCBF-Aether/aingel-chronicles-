The Text — Didascalia 37 (J. M. Harden, The Ethiopic Didascalia (London: SPCK, 1920), public domain (Cornell University Library scan, archive.org cu31924096083336))

*OF THE SERVICE OF THE HOSTS OF SPIRITUAL ONES AND THE HOLY CHURCH*

1 ALL the spiritual hosts of angels are a flame of fire, and the holy cherubim with six wings glorify Thee with a loud voice, and sing with a voice that is never silent, and say, ‘“ Holy, holy, holy, Lord of Hosts, the holiness of Thy glory [is perfect and]! filleth the heavens and the earth.””?

2 The (whole) number of these angels, thrones and authorities, dominions and principalities, and powers, the cherubim and seraphim cry and say, Blessed be the Lord the God of Israel, and blessed be this Church of the nations (which) is like unto the host of heaven.

3 Day and night with holy soul and perfect heart thus they sing,’ and say, “The Chariot, of God is ten thousand times ten thousand and thousands of thousands of them that glorify the Lord in the holy mount of Sinai.’ The heavens and all that is therein give humble thanks to Thee, for Thou hast hung the heavens as a vault, and hast laid the foundations of the earth, and established it on the waters; and Thou hast separated darkness from light, and numbered the stars in their fulness, and called them all by their names.

4 The whole creation declareth Thy greatness, and the trees of the field also Thou hast made to grow. Wherefore the whole creation of mankind must needs offer up to Thee glory and thanksgiving, sacrifice and offering and incense through our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, Thy only-begotten Son. Thou alone art Almighty, good, and loving mercy, and beneficent, for Thine is the glory and power for ever and ever. Amen.

5 Thou art in heaven, and Thou art on earth, and Thou

6 art in the sea; Who hast gathered together the whole creation from the confines of the earth, and there is no end of Thy greatness. For it is not we who have said this, but Thou didst say, ‘‘! am the Lord God who am above the heavens,” and am present in every place, and besides me there is no other god.

7 True is Thy word, and there is none that is like unto Thee, the holy God, the Lord of the Saints, and the souls of all are in Thy hand. Thou art worthy of glory and exaltation, Whose throne is unchangeable, and (Whose) strength decayeth not, and (Whose) greatness none can declare, and the excellence of (Whose) beauty is marvellous.

8 There is none that dareth to draw nigh to thy habitation, and the knowledge of Thy wisdom is without number. Thy righteousness hath no end, Thou needest none to counsel Thee, nor seekest any to help Thee; Thou hast no beginning nor any end; Thy kingdom passeth not away, and there is none that can overcome Thy power, and innumerable are the hosts of angels who minister unto Thee.

9 Thou art the Father of wisdom, and the maker of the whole creation, and the framer of the law. Thou destroyest the ungodly, and rewardest the righteous. (Thou art) God the Father of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, the Lord of those who give Him humble thanks, in Whom is no falsehood ;. Who takest no bribe in judgment, Whose praises are innumerable.

10 To Thee is due the honour, and glory, and worship which every rational soul sendeth up to Thee for ever and ever. Amen.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

In plain language: this chapter describes the service of the spiritual hosts and the holy Church. All the hosts of angels are a flame of fire, and the six-winged cherubim, with thrones, authorities, dominions, principalities, powers and seraphim, sing without ceasing, Holy, holy, holy, Lord of Hosts. The Church of the nations is likened to that heavenly host, and the chapter closes in praise of the Father of wisdom, Maker of all creation and framer of the law, who destroys the ungodly and rewards the righteous.
