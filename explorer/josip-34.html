Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Water and wine, flesh within the belly, and the breasts and milk from which she bare him are the chapter's bodily images of the incarnation. After the Ascension, room is sought for the Holy Spirit, and the chapter ends with sin's reign: the Devil reigned over sinners, slew them, and brought them down into Sheol.
