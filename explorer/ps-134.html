The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Psalms 134 (King James Version (1611) for Psalms 1–150; Charles Thomson, The Septuagint (1808) for Psalm 151)

1 Behold, bless ye the LORD, all ye servants of the LORD, which by night stand in the house of the LORD.

2 Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, and bless the LORD.

3 The LORD that made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The brief night psalm: behold, bless the LORD, all servants of the LORD who by night stand in the house of the LORD; lift up your hands in the sanctuary and bless the LORD. The answer comes as a blessing in return: the LORD who made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion. Interpreted Psalm text above; study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. Henry closes fifteen songs of degrees fitly: design stir up ministers go on with work in night when solemnities of day over; dialogue reading — first two verses priests Levites sat up all night keep watch of house called spend guard not idle talk but acts devotion, last verse those called pray for exhorter high priest or captain of guard, or mutually exhort and pray one another; two-fold blessing — our blessing God speaking well of him; call to Levites servants by office appointed minister holy things, attended sanctuary kept charge (Num 3:6-10), some by night stood guard holy things not profaned rich things not plundered, ark in curtains more need guards, attended fire altar lamps candlestick not go out, probably devout pious Israelites sat up with them, one departed not temple night or day (Luke 2:37); called bless Lord thus keep awake keeping employed, redeem time holy exercises, how spend time better than praising God, excellent husbandry fill vacancies pious meditations ejaculations, modest reasonable converse God when nothing else do; stood in house remember where, holiness holy work became house; bless in concert or each himself; call to us Christians made priests to God Levites (Isa 66:21), servants place name in house sanctuary stand before minister, even by night under eye access him, therefore bless again bless, think speak glory goodness. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 2. Henry lifts hands: let them lift up hands in doing it token lifting hearts, lift hands in holiness (Hammond) or sanctification as fit when lift in sanctuary, remember appointed wash before minister taught lift holy hands prayer praise; ushered Behold note commanding attention — look about Sirs when in God's presence conduct accordingly; lift hands prayer praise vows, do work diligence cheerfulness elevation of mind. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 3. Henry returns blessing: God's blessing us doing well for us taught desire; whether watchmen blessing captain, Levites high priest, whoever chief singular Lord bless thee, or one upon many Lord bless thee each particular thee and thee you blessing God Lord bless you — not material; learn need desire no more make happy than blessed of Lord, whom he blesses blessed indeed; blessings out of Zion spiritual blessings covenant communion with God best blessings most earnest for; great encouragement come God for blessing he made heaven earth therefore all blessings both at disposal upper nether springs; beg blessings not only selves but others, not only Lord bless me but Lord bless thee testifying fulness divine blessings enough for others as us and good-will, pray for those exhort us, though less blessed of greater (Heb 7:7) yet greater must be prayed for by less. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
