The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Numbers 26 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 And it came to pass after the plague, that the LORD spake unto Moses and unto Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, saying,

2 Take the sum of all the congregation of the children of Israel, from twenty years old and upward, throughout their fathers’ house, all that are able to go to war in Israel.

3 And Moses and Eleazar the priest spake with them in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho, saying,

4 Take the sum of the people, from twenty years old and upward; as the LORD commanded Moses and the children of Israel, which went forth out of the land of Egypt.

5 Reuben, the eldest son of Israel: the children of Reuben; Hanoch, of whom cometh the family of the Hanochites: of Pallu, the family of the Palluites:

6 Of Hezron, the family of the Hezronites: of Carmi, the family of the Carmites.

7 These are the families of the Reubenites: and they that were numbered of them were forty and three thousand and seven hundred and thirty.

8 And the sons of Pallu; Eliab.

9 And the sons of Eliab; Nemuel, and Dathan, and Abiram. This is that Dathan and Abiram, which were famous in the congregation, who strove against Moses and against Aaron in the company of Korah, when they strove against the LORD:

10 And the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed them up together with Korah, when that company died, what time the fire devoured two hundred and fifty men: and they became a sign.

11 Notwithstanding the children of Korah died not.

12 The sons of Simeon after their families: of Nemuel, the family of the Nemuelites: of Jamin, the family of the Jaminites: of Jachin, the family of the Jachinites:

13 Of Zerah, the family of the Zarhites: of Shaul, the family of the Shaulites.

14 These are the families of the Simeonites, twenty and two thousand and two hundred.

15 The children of Gad after their families: of Zephon, the family of the Zephonites: of Haggi, the family of the Haggites: of Shuni, the family of the Shunites:

16 Of Ozni, the family of the Oznites: of Eri, the family of the Erites:

17 Of Arod, the family of the Arodites: of Areli, the family of the Arelites.

18 These are the families of the children of Gad according to those that were numbered of them, forty thousand and five hundred.

19 The sons of Judah were Er and Onan: and Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan.

20 And the sons of Judah after their families were; of Shelah, the family of the Shelanites: of Pharez, the family of the Pharzites: of Zerah, the family of the Zarhites.

21 And the sons of Pharez were; of Hezron, the family of the Hezronites: of Hamul, the family of the Hamulites.

22 These are the families of Judah according to those that were numbered of them, threescore and sixteen thousand and five hundred.

23 Of the sons of Issachar after their families: of Tola, the family of the Tolaites: of Pua, the family of the Punites:

24 Of Jashub, the family of the Jashubites: of Shimron, the family of the Shimronites.

25 These are the families of Issachar according to those that were numbered of them, threescore and four thousand and three hundred.

26 Of the sons of Zebulun after their families: of Sered, the family of the Sardites: of Elon, the family of the Elonites: of Jahleel, the family of the Jahleelites.

27 These are the families of the Zebulunites according to those that were numbered of them, threescore thousand and five hundred.

28 The sons of Joseph after their families were Manasseh and Ephraim.

29 Of the sons of Manasseh: of Machir, the family of the Machirites: and Machir begat Gilead: of Gilead come the family of the Gileadites.

30 These are the sons of Gilead: of Jeezer, the family of the Jeezerites: of Helek, the family of the Helekites:

31 And of Asriel, the family of the Asrielites: and of Shechem, the family of the Shechemites:

32 And of Shemida, the family of the Shemidaites: and of Hepher, the family of the Hepherites.

33 And Zelophehad the son of Hepher had no sons, but daughters: and the names of the daughters of Zelophehad were Mahlah, and Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah.

34 These are the families of Manasseh, and those that were numbered of them, fifty and two thousand and seven hundred.

35 These are the sons of Ephraim after their families: of Shuthelah, the family of the Shuthalhites: of Becher, the family of the Bachrites: of Tahan, the family of the Tahanites.

36 And these are the sons of Shuthelah: of Eran, the family of the Eranites.

37 These are the families of the sons of Ephraim according to those that were numbered of them, thirty and two thousand and five hundred. These are the sons of Joseph after their families.

38 The sons of Benjamin after their families: of Bela, the family of the Belaites: of Ashbel, the family of the Ashbelites: of Ahiram, the family of the Ahiramites:

39 Of Shupham, the family of the Shuphamites: of Hupham, the family of the Huphamites.

40 And the sons of Bela were Ard and Naaman: of Ard, the family of the Ardites: and of Naaman, the family of the Naamites.

41 These are the sons of Benjamin after their families: and they that were numbered of them were forty and five thousand and six hundred.

42 These are the sons of Dan after their families: of Shuham, the family of the Shuhamites. These are the families of Dan after their families.

43 All the families of the Shuhamites, according to those that were numbered of them, were threescore and four thousand and four hundred.

44 Of the children of Asher after their families: of Jimna, the family of the Jimnites: of Jesui, the family of the Jesuites: of Beriah, the family of the Beriites.

45 Of the sons of Beriah: of Heber, the family of the Heberites: of Malchiel, the family of the Malchielites.

46 And the name of the daughter of Asher was Sarah.

47 These are the families of the sons of Asher according to those that were numbered of them; who were fifty and three thousand and four hundred.

48 Of the sons of Naphtali after their families: of Jahzeel, the family of the Jahzeelites: of Guni, the family of the Gunites:

49 Of Jezer, the family of the Jezerites: of Shillem, the family of the Shillemites.

50 These are the families of Naphtali according to their families: and they that were numbered of them were forty and five thousand and four hundred.

51 These were the numbered of the children of Israel, six hundred thousand and a thousand seven hundred and thirty.

52 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

53 Unto these the land shall be divided for an inheritance according to the number of names.

54 To many thou shalt give the more inheritance, and to few thou shalt give the less inheritance: to every one shall his inheritance be given according to those that were numbered of him.

55 Notwithstanding the land shall be divided by lot: according to the names of the tribes of their fathers they shall inherit.

56 According to the lot shall the possession thereof be divided between many and few.

57 And these are they that were numbered of the Levites after their families: of Gershon, the family of the Gershonites: of Kohath, the family of the Kohathites: of Merari, the family of the Merarites.

58 These are the families of the Levites: the family of the Libnites, the family of the Hebronites, the family of the Mahlites, the family of the Mushites, the family of the Korathites. And Kohath begat Amram.

59 And the name of Amram’s wife was Jochebed, the daughter of Levi, whom her mother bare to Levi in Egypt: and she bare unto Amram Aaron and Moses, and Miriam their sister.

60 And unto Aaron was born Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

61 And Nadab and Abihu died, when they offered strange fire before the LORD.

62 And those that were numbered of them were twenty and three thousand, all males from a month old and upward: for they were not numbered among the children of Israel, because there was no inheritance given them among the children of Israel.

63 These are they that were numbered by Moses and Eleazar the priest, who numbered the children of Israel in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho.

64 But among these there was not a man of them whom Moses and Aaron the priest numbered, when they numbered the children of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai.

65 For the LORD had said of them, They shall surely die in the wilderness. And there was not left a man of them, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.

How to read the labels

Every substantive historical claim in the editorial panels below carries one of three labels, plus a source reference:

  • Documented — directly supported by a cited source.
  • Interpreted — a reasoned explanation or proposed relationship.
  • Unresolved — evidence is insufficient to establish the claim.

A claim does NOT become Documented merely because a commentary states it. Commentary views are attributed to their authors and labeled Interpreted unless a primary textual source directly supports the claim. Where evidence runs out, the page says Unresolved and stops — it does not fill the gap.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter is the second census, taken in the plains of Moab after the plague, by Moses and Eleazar. The fighting men are numbered again tribe by tribe, and the total is set against the first census, with the note that the land will be divided by lot, to the more a larger inheritance and to the fewer a smaller. The Levites are numbered separately, from a month old upward, and receive no inheritance among Israel. The chapter closes with the point of the whole count: of the men numbered at Sinai not one remains except Caleb and Joshua, for the LORD had said they would surely die in the wilderness. Interpreted Editorial overview, grounded in the chapter text and its study notes

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. It came to pass after the plague, that the Lord spoke to Moses and to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest, saying, take the sum of the congregation. Henry notes Moses did not number the people except when God commanded him, and the new numbering came after the plague, for a new generation that would enter the land. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 2. Take the sum of all the congregation of Israel, from twenty years old and upward, throughout their fathers' houses, all that are able to go forth to war in Israel. The rule was unchanged from Sinai: fighting men, twenty and upward, counted by fathers' houses, but the men counted were almost entirely new. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 3. Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke with them in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho. Eleazar now stood where Aaron had stood at the first census, and the plains of Moab, within sight of the land, were the fitting place for counting its future inheritors. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 4. Take the sum of the people, from twenty years old and upward, as the Lord commanded Moses and the children of Israel, which went forth out of the land of Egypt. The command repeated the exodus generation's census for the generation that would actually possess Canaan. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 7. These are the families of the Reubenites, and they that were numbered of them were forty and three thousand and seven hundred and thirty. Reuben, the eldest son, now counted fewer than at Sinai, and Henry notes the total of the whole census was nearly the same as at Sinai, though the tribes within it had shifted greatly. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 9. And the sons of Eliab were Nemuel, and Dathan, and Abiram. This is that Dathan and Abiram, which were famous in the congregation, who strove against Moses and against Aaron in the company of Korah, when they strove against the Lord. The register pauses on the rebels' names, so that the census itself remembers the rebellion that had cut off the old generation. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 10. And the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed them up together with Korah, when that company died, what time the fire devoured two hundred and fifty men, and they became a sign. The census record turns the rebellion into a sign: the earth and the fire together marked the fate of those who strove against the Lord. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 11. Notwithstanding the children of Korah died not. Henry takes special notice of this: Korah's children died not as Dathan's and Abiram's did, and seem not to have joined even their own father in rebellion, so that if we partake not of sinners' sins, we shall not partake of their plagues. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 14. These are the families of the Simeonites, twenty and two thousand and two hundred. Simeon had fallen most sharply of all, from nearly sixty thousand at Sinai to twenty-two thousand two hundred, a loss the census records without explaining, in the shadow of Baal-peor and the plague. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 18. These are the families of the children of Gad according to those numbered of them, forty thousand and five hundred. Gad's families were registered as well as the tribe, Henry notes: the census preserved families, not only tribal totals, because the land would be divided by families. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 19. The sons of Judah were Er and Onan, and Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. The register remembers Judah's first two sons only by their death in Canaan, before the exodus, so that the tribe's present families descend through Shelah and Pharez alone. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 22. These are the families of Judah according to those numbered of them, threescore and sixteen thousand and five hundred. Judah remained the largest tribe, now grown beyond its Sinai number, keeping the pre-eminence Jacob had assigned it. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 25. These are the families of Issachar according to those numbered of them, threescore and four thousand and three hundred. Issachar, like Judah, had grown since Sinai, and its families were counted for the inheritance that would match its numbers. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 27. These are the families of the Zebulunites according to those numbered of them, threescore thousand and five hundred. Zebulun's count, like the others, was taken family by family, so that the division of the land could follow the register exactly. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 33. And Zelophehad son of Hepher had no sons, but daughters, and the names of the daughters of Zelophehad were Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah. The register pauses on five daughters with no brother, whose names would soon raise the question of inheritance answered in the next chapter. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 34. These are the families of Manasseh, and those numbered of them, fifty and two thousand and seven hundred. Manasseh, through Machir and Gilead, had grown into a tribe whose families would soon hold land on both sides of the Jordan. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 37. These are the families of the sons of Ephraim according to those numbered of them, thirty and two thousand and five hundred. Ephraim, once the larger half of Joseph, now counted fewer than Manasseh, a reversal the census records without comment. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 41. These are the sons of Benjamin after their families, and they that were numbered of them were forty and five thousand and six hundred. Benjamin's families were counted in full, a tribe that had grown by ten thousand since Sinai despite the wilderness years. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 43. All the families of the Shuhamites, according to those numbered of them, were threescore and four thousand and four hundred. Dan, through the single family of Shuham, counted the second largest tribe, a host from one house. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 47. These are the families of the sons of Asher according to those numbered of them, fifty and three thousand and four hundred. Asher's families, through Jimna, Jesui and Beriah, were registered for the northern inheritance they would receive. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 50. These are the families of Naphtali according to their families, and they that were numbered of them were forty and five thousand and four hundred. Naphtali closed the tribal register, its families counted like the rest for the coming division. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 51. These were the numbered of the children of Israel, six hundred thousand and a thousand seven hundred and thirty. Henry notes the total was nearly the same as at Sinai, six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty then, six hundred and one thousand seven hundred and thirty now, a new generation of almost exactly the old strength. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 53. Unto these the land shall be divided for an inheritance according to the number of names. The census was taken for division: the land belonged to the numbered names, so that counting the people was counting the inheritors. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 54. To many you shall give the more inheritance, and to few you shall give the less inheritance: to everyone shall his inheritance be given according to those that were numbered of him. Henry states the general rule of equity: many received more, fewer received less, in proportion to the census numbers. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 55. Notwithstanding, the land shall be divided by lot, according to the names of the tribes of their fathers they shall inherit. Though the portions were proportioned by numbers, the particular lands were settled by lot, so that at last the matter must be settled by God's providence, with which all must be satisfied. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 56. According to the lot shall the possession thereof be divided between many and few. The lot overruled human prudence in the final assignment: equity fixed the size, providence fixed the place, and both together were God's division. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 57. And these are they that were numbered of the Levites after their families: of Gershon, the family of the Gershonites, of Kohath, the family of the Kohathites, of Merari, the family of the Merarites. Henry notes Levi was God's tribe, therefore it was not numbered with the rest, but alone, and it came not under the sentence that none should enter Canaan except Caleb and Joshua. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 59. And the name of Amram's wife was Jochebed, daughter of Levi, whom her mother bore to Levi in Egypt, and she bore to Amram Aaron, Moses, and Miriam. The Levite register reaches back to Egypt for its most famous family: Levi's own daughter, born in Egypt, mother of the three leaders of the exodus. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 60. And to Aaron was born Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. Aaron's four sons are named in the register, two of whom would die and two of whom would carry the priesthood forward into the land. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 61. And Nadab and Abihu died, when they offered strange fire before the Lord. The register remembers the strange fire as it remembered Korah: the priesthood's history included its own judgments, recorded beside its numbers. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 62. And those numbered of them were twenty and three thousand, all males from a month old and upward, for they were not numbered among Israel, because there was no inheritance given them among Israel. The Levites numbered twenty-three thousand, counted from a month old as before, and still without land inheritance, since their inheritance was the Lord's service and its tithes. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 63. These are they that were numbered by Moses and Eleazar the priest, who numbered Israel in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho. The second census, like the first, was taken by named men in a named place, and its authority rested on Moses and Eleazar together. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 64. But among these there was not a man of them whom Moses and Aaron numbered, when they numbered Israel in the wilderness of Sinai. Henry notes the execution of the sentence on the murmurers is observable here: not one man now numbered had been numbered then, except Caleb and Joshua. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 65. For the Lord had said of them, they shall surely die in the wilderness, and there was not left a man of them, save Caleb son of Jephunneh, and Joshua son of Nun. Henry closes with God's righteousness in his threatenings and his truth in his promises: death made awful havoc, yet all was appointed in perfect wisdom, justice and truth, and Caleb and Joshua stood as living proof that God's promise outlived his sentence. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry

And those numbered of them were twenty and three thousand, all males from a month old and upward, for they were not numbered among Israel, because there was no inheritance given them among Israel. The Levites numbered twenty-three thousand, counted from a month old as before, and still without land inheritance, since their inheritance was the Lord's service and its tithes. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 63. These are they that were numbered by Moses and Eleazar the priest, who numbered Israel in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho. The second census, like the first, was taken by named men in a named place, and its authority rested on Moses and Eleazar together. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 64. But among these there was not a man of them whom Moses and Aaron numbered, when they numbered Israel in the wilderness of Sinai. Henry notes the execution of the sentence on the murmurers is observable here: not one man now numbered had been numbered then, except Caleb and Joshua. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 65. For the Lord had said of them, they shall surely die in the wilderness, and there was not left a man of them, save Caleb son of Jephunneh, and Joshua son of Nun. Henry closes with God's righteousness in his threatenings and his truth in his promises: death made awful havoc, yet all was appointed in perfect wisdom, justice and truth, and Caleb and Joshua stood as living proof that God's promise outlived his sentence. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
