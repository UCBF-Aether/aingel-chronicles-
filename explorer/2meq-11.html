Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter holds up Moses interceding: when God desired to destroy his people and his brother was wroth, Moses prayed, Have mercy, O God, on thy people, and God loved him exceedingly above his Levite brethren. Against him stand Korah and Asaph, who claimed the priesthood for Levi's children, offered unaccepted incense and were swallowed by the earth into Sheol alive with tabernacle and cattle, and the chapter turns the example into warning and promise: transgress not, and God shall do thy desire and keep thy kingdom; transgress, and thou shalt go down like Korah into everlasting burning fire. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
