Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter states the union plainly as the chapter presents it: God in the flesh, with a human soul, not two natures, nor two wills, nor two operations, grounded in “The Word became flesh, and dwelt upon us” and in “Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee”, with the flesh ascending only as he who descended ascended. Cyril is named at its close. Interpreted Reading of this chapter's text as printed above; the study-notes file for this chapter is silent
