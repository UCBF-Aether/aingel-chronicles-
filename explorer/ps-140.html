The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Psalms 140 (King James Version (1611) for Psalms 1–150; Charles Thomson, The Septuagint (1808) for Psalm 151)

1 Deliver me, O LORD, from the evil man: preserve me from the violent man;

2 Which imagine mischiefs in their heart; continually are they gathered together for war.

3 They have sharpened their tongues like a serpent; adders’ poison is under their lips. Selah.

4 Keep me, O LORD, from the hands of the wicked; preserve me from the violent man; who have purposed to overthrow my goings.

5 The proud have hid a snare for me, and cords; they have spread a net by the wayside; they have set gins for me. Selah.

6 I said unto the LORD, Thou art my God: hear the voice of my supplications, O LORD.

7 O GOD the Lord, the strength of my salvation, thou hast covered my head in the day of battle.

8 Grant not, O LORD, the desires of the wicked: further not his wicked device; lest they exalt themselves. Selah.

9 As for the head of those that compass me about, let the mischief of their own lips cover them.

10 Let burning coals fall upon them: let them be cast into the fire; into deep pits, that they rise not up again.

11 Let not an evil speaker be established in the earth: evil shall hunt the violent man to overthrow him.

12 I know that the LORD will maintain the cause of the afflicted, and the right of the poor.

13 Surely the righteous shall give thanks unto thy name: the upright shall dwell in thy presence.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A plea for deliverance from the evil and violent man, who imagines mischiefs, gathers continually for war, and has sharpened his tongue like a serpent with adders' poison under his lips. The proud have hidden snares, cords, nets by the wayside and gins for the speaker's feet. Against them he says to the LORD, "Thou art my God: hear the voice of my supplications," and appeals to God the Lord, strength of his salvation, who covered his head in the day of battle. The prayer asks that the wicked's desires not be granted nor his device furthered, that the mischief of their own lips cover those who compass him, that burning coals fall on them and they be cast into deep pits to rise no more, and that the evil speaker not be established, since evil shall hunt the violent man. The close is confidence: the LORD will maintain the cause of the afflicted and the right of the poor, the righteous shall give thanks to his name, and the upright shall dwell in his presence. Interpreted Psalm text above; study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)

Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 3. Henry sharpens tongues serpent adders poison lips: spiteful full malice Satan himself — sharpened tongues serpent infuses venom tongue, malignity all say think nothing under lips but adders poison; calumnies counsels aimed destroy David secretly as stung serpent snake grass, endeavoured infuse malice others make seven times children hell; malignant tongue makes like old serpent, poison lips certain sign poison heart. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 4. Henry preserves from hands wicked violent overthrow goings: same prayer as v1 — deliver preserve keep, violent men evil men, plotted overthrow goings draw sin trouble ruin reputation interest life, hunters hidden snare spread net set gins designs undiscovered likely take effect fall hands ere aware; great persecutors great politicians formidable, Lord preserves simple without arts. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)

Henry proud hid snare cords net gins way: proud conceited confident success resemble Satan reigning ruining sin pride; pride persecutors present terror yet encouragement persecuted, more haughty faster ripening ruin, pride goes before destruction; hunters snare net gins as v4 — hidden that undiscovered take effect. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)

Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 7. Henry covers head day battle: assurance help happiness — O God Lord Jehovah Adonai, Jehovah self-existent self-sufficient infinitely perfect, Adonai stay support ruler governor therefore strength salvation strong Saviour nay salvation itself from whom in whom salvation, not only strong Saviour very strength salvation stress hope laid all in all make happy preserve happiness; experience formerly care — covered head day battle, pleaded Saul service country many time jeoparded life high places field, pleads God wonderfully protected provided better helmet securing head than Goliath's, kept day battle Philistines suffer not fall treacherous intrigues false-hearted Israelites; God able preserve secret fraud as open force, experience power care dangers one kind encourage trust depend dangers another, nothing shorten Lord's right hand. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 8. Henry grants not desires wicked further not device lest exalt: believing foresight shame confusion persecutors — disappointment prayed lusts not gratified lust ambition envy revenge, Grant not desires wicked frustrate let not see ruin interest earnestly wish, hear voice supplications; projects not take effect blasted — further not wicked device let not Providence favour designs cross them, suffer not proceed chain wheels stop career pursuits; pray enemies God's people not succeed enterprises, David prayer Ahithophel turn counsels foolishness; plea lest exalt value success evidence God favoured, proud prosper made prouder impudent God insolent people therefore Lord do not prosper them. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 9. Henry mischief lips cover heads compassers: destruction prayed or prophecy original bears, prayer spirit prophecy all one; foretels ruin own enemies — mischief own lips cover heads evil wished come themselves curses blown back faces designs laid turn own ruin (Ps 7:15-16); makers mischief slandering tale-bearing misrepresenting neighbours spreading ill-natured characters stories dread consequence think sad condition when mischief accessory made return themselves. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 10. Henry burning coals fall cast fire deep pits rise not: judgments God fall burning coals allusion destruction Sodom, deluge waters above beneath met drowning world windows heaven opened fountains great deep broken, complete ruin enemies Christ kingdom not only burning coals cast above (Job 20:23; 27:22) but themselves cast fire beneath heaven hell wrath God Judge rage Satan tormentor concur miserable; fire not furnace perhaps escape but deep pit cannot rise, Tophet deep large (Isa 30:33). Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 11. Henry evil speaker not established earth evil hunt violent overthrow: all others like them — evil speakers shaken never established earth, got fraud falsehood calumny unjust accusation will not prosper last, wealth vanity diminished, let not Doeg think reign long doom theirs (Ps 2:5), lying tongue moment lip truth established for ever; evil doers destroyed — evil hunt violent man blood-hound hunts murderer discover, lion hunts prey tear pieces, mischievous brought light brought ruin destruction appointed run down overthrow, evil pursues sinners. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 12. Henry knows Lord maintain cause afflicted judgment poor: foresight deliverance comfort persecuted — God do justice delivering wronged commit themselves, know Lord maintain just injured cause afflicted people not suffer might always prevail right though right poor little pretend right to; God patron oppressed innocence much more persecuted piety, those know him cannot but know this. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 13. Henry righteous give thanks name upright dwell presence: they do him justice ascribing glory deliverance — surely righteous make conscience rendering God due as men theirs shall give thanks name when find cause pleaded jealousy prosecuted effect; closing upright dwell presence denotes favour — admit dwell presence grace here glory hereafter safety happiness, and duty — attend as servants keep presence masters both honour receive commands; true thanksgiving even thanksliving, use all deliverance serve God more closely cheerfully. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
