Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Answering Edomyas and Amalek, the chapter denounces a people without fear of God, lewdness, bloodshed, eating sacrifices and carrion, and abominable things, taught, it says, by Säbəlyanos and his demons, who envied them and taught theft, violence, fornication and idol sacrifice to turn them from God's way. Against that stands the humble way: humility toward God, love of neighbour, mercy to brother and peace with all people, with a warning not to be partial, violent, plundering or deceitful, lest they hasten others down into everlasting punishment. Several Ge'ez terms in this chapter are left untranslated in the text, and no gloss for them is offered here.
