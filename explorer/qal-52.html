The Text — Qalementos 52 (Ge'ez text (project Ge'ez corpus, chapter-level))

1 Concerning the ordinance of the altar. (1) And let them sanctify the oblation upon the altar which is sealed with meron, at the first season when the chief priests and the priests are gathered together, and all the deacons, that there may be glory and joy. (2) And likewise on Sabbaths and on feasts let them read at the altar the Gospel of John; for he is the speaker of the Godhead.
