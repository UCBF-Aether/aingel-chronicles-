Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter strings together short commandments spoken in the names of James, Nathanael, Judas, Thomas and John: no divining, augury or star-gazing, since all this leads toward idolatry; no lying, since falsehood leads to theft, and no love of gold; no murmuring, arrogance or haste to evil, since by these comes denial — but meekness, mercy, peacemaking, a pure heart and humility, trusting that nothing comes to pass except from God. It then turns, through Thomas and John, to honouring the teacher of God's word and to priests on the right and on the left, and ends with James on appointing a reader — material that recurs in chapter 4.
