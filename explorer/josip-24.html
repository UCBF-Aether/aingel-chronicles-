Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Pharaoh's heart hardened as God took Israel away, the chapter says, and Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob are likened to Adam and Eve as fathers of the children of Israel. The tabernacles in the wilderness are then read as a likeness of Paradise, outside as Paradise is outside. Interpreted Chapter text as printed above; study-notes file for this chapter is silent
