Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Christ's weakness was the hiding of his divinity from Satan, the chapter argues, until Satan should know he is a weak man, astonished at the abundance of his wonders. Spirits are taken into the garden where Adam and Eve were at the first and left there, and the chapter reflects on patience after a fall from glory and sanctification.
