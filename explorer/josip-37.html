The Text — Josippon 37 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 And again, continually he glorifieth God [?] and sanctifieth, and halloweth [?] His word; at its time He abideth continually; the grace of the Holy Spirit is unto him who is apart from them; for He is He who sharpeneth the praises and the sanctifying; and hearing [?] His word — God — for He is He who quickened the soul with God [?]; it was not the season that we should ask.

2 And He said [?]: that it is for him who eateth and for him who drinketh [?]; for by it liveth his body [?] in the world; and when God [?] knew that man quickeneth a body — life — and quickeneth the life of his body, exceedingly more than the life of his soul.

3 And therefore exalted is the life of his soul; and therefore the spirit of the time, and the life of the body of the time, exceedeth the time of the spirit, that this may come upon man, and he doeth continually.

4 Man desireth not the time of those who are not the beginning of salvation; and by the indwelling of the Holy Spirit in man, continually.

5 That thou delay not concerning the ordinance of God upon man, every day and every hour: that he go unto the house of the Christians without excuse, and magnify therein in fear all the day, and ask the forgiveness [?] of his sins, in the matter which is past; and ask that there be appointed for him a pure angel, that he may seal him at that time from the hosts of Satan, and his snares at that time; those are they who guard the holy soul from them, at that time of the night.

6 And likewise he goeth [?] to the house of the Christians at the time of the setting of the sun; he lifteth up his head, the beginning of the night; he glorifieth, and bewaileth his sins by day, continually.

7 And he seeketh that there be sent unto him a pure angel, who shall guard him in that night from the snares of the hosts of Satan.

8 And at that time those are they who are guarded from them by the might of the Holy Spirit, in that time of the night.

9 This one thing is fitting for all the faithful: that they all, at one time with one accord, pray into the house of the Christians at that hour.

10 And by doing.

11 And if there be in a city a house of the Christians, it is fitting that he pray in it, while his heart is troubled and sorrowful; for therein is found the house of the Christians.

12 For the house of the Christians is Paradise unto the LORD, as I have shown thee in the Book of the Interpretation of the Words, four, and Joshua the son of Nun.

13 And whoso forsaketh and leaveth one of these two prayers, and glorifieth not God, taketh [?] and is taken from this, and returneth not [?] to do (?), that he forsake not nor leave, at the time of the life (?) from the hours (?) of those.

14 Nay, he that doeth the work of the flesh passeth away in the time of that day, and attains unto death in that time of the day; from the beginning he rejoiceth, and goeth forth into eternal darkness, which is in it, the Devil and his hosts; for he is he who was kept back from the glory of God, like them.

15 This, if he was kept back from the glory of God by excuse: he shall be in the likeness of the Devil and his companions.

16 And if he was kept back from this in the work of life, that passeth away: nay (?), he seeketh a work from the work of that (?) spirit (?); and they made Judas from the fathers of God; for he shall be in the likeness of the Devil and his companions.

17 And if he was kept back in the time of those [days], while he seeketh honours in the likeness of Judas, who was made for the land, that passeth away, he loveth that which presseth more than thy crushing: that which abideth for ever; and he shall be in the likeness of those who make an idol; not so — God forsook from him His glory; and he went, and took his body, gold — nay.

18 Pure.

19 And gold, and pure vessels.

20 And therefore the LORD said a glory unto him, which of wrath is not worthy unto the two servants who are made subject unto God and unto the vessels; and it is not fitting for any of the faithful to forsake [going] from the going to the house of the Christians in the time of those two days, and to excuse himself by a work from all works; and this shall be a wretchedness for the sight.

21 And likewise it is fitting that he come and fulfil it at the time of the Mass; and there shall not be seen, before its season, the finishing of the Mass; whether he be able to draw near, or whether he draw not near — for he is he that is fitting, that he may glorify God and sanctify Him; for His glory is among those [?] things which we have mentioned, and His holiness — it, at the time of the offering, an offering, at the time of the Mass [?] — there is heard the writing [?], which is the word of the LORD [?], which proceedeth from His mouth, which our Lord Jesus Christ said: for the Spirit in him came to life; and it is not in vain, for bread is the life of the flesh, and the word of God is the life of the soul; and his soul came to life by the hearing of the holy books, and the hearing of the Gospel; and he sanctifieth God with the angels, three: for the priest saith, Thou art God, who standest; before Thee are the angels, and unto Thee, the angels.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Continual glorifying, sanctifying, and hallowing of God's word, with the grace of the Holy Spirit abiding, is the chapter's opening note. It then gives practical counsel: a troubled heart should pray in a house of the Christians where prayer is found, and should come to fulfil the Mass at its proper time, not before its season.
