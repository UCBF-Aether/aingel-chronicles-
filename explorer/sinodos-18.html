Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 43 in this chapter concerns deacons appointed to the bishop, who are to admonish them concerning the sick, that the priests may visit them, and the time of private morning prayer: the faithful rise from sleep, wash their hands and pray before doing any work. The chapter then gives communion distribution — This is the body of Christ, This is the blood of Christ, each answered Amen — and a blessing prayer over the servants and handmaids for the washing of the second birth, the remission of sins and the grace of the Holy Spirit. It closes with its own colophon: Finished is the order of baptism. Interpreted Summary of this chapter's text, as printed above
