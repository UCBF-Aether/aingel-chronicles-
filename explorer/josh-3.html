The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Joshua 3 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 And Joshua rose early in the morning; and they removed from Shittim, and came to Jordan, he and all the children of Israel, and lodged there before they passed over.

2 And it came to pass after three days, that the officers went through the host;

3 And they commanded the people, saying, When ye see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God, and the priests the Levites bearing it, then ye shall remove from your place, and go after it.

4 Yet there shall be a space between you and it, about two thousand cubits by measure: come not near unto it, that ye may know the way by which ye must go: for ye have not passed this way heretofore.

5 And Joshua said unto the people, Sanctify yourselves: for to morrow the LORD will do wonders among you.

6 And Joshua spake unto the priests, saying, Take up the ark of the covenant, and pass over before the people. And they took up the ark of the covenant, and went before the people.

7 And the LORD said unto Joshua, This day will I begin to magnify thee in the sight of all Israel, that they may know that, as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee.

8 And thou shalt command the priests that bear the ark of the covenant, saying, When ye are come to the brink of the water of Jordan, ye shall stand still in Jordan.

9 And Joshua said unto the children of Israel, Come hither, and hear the words of the LORD your God.

10 And Joshua said, Hereby ye shall know that the living God is among you, and that he will without fail drive out from before you the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Hivites, and the Perizzites, and the Girgashites, and the Amorites, and the Jebusites.

11 Behold, the ark of the covenant of the LORD of all the earth passeth over before you into Jordan.

12 Now therefore take you twelve men out of the tribes of Israel, out of every tribe a man.

13 And it shall come to pass, as soon as the soles of the feet of the priests that bear the ark of the LORD, the LORD of all the earth, shall rest in the waters of Jordan, that the waters of Jordan shall be cut off from the waters that come down from above; and they shall stand upon an heap.

14 And it came to pass, when the people removed from their tents, to pass over Jordan, and the priests bearing the ark of the covenant before the people;

15 And as they that bare the ark were come unto Jordan, and the feet of the priests that bare the ark were dipped in the brim of the water, (for Jordan overfloweth all his banks all the time of harvest,)

16 That the waters which came down from above stood and rose up upon an heap very far from the city Adam, that is beside Zaretan: and those that came down toward the sea of the plain, even the salt sea, failed, and were cut off: and the people passed over right against Jericho.

17 And the priests that bare the ark of the covenant of the LORD stood firm on dry ground in the midst of Jordan, and all the Israelites passed over on dry ground, until all the people were passed clean over Jordan.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Israel moves from Shittim to the Jordan in flood, and the crossing is staged around the ark of the covenant: the priests carry it into the river ahead of the people, and when their feet touch the water, the stream stops and stands in a heap upstream, leaving dry ground. The whole nation crosses over opposite Jericho, and the chapter stresses that this is how Israel will know the living God is among them and Joshua's leadership is confirmed.   • Verse 5. Joshua told the people to sanctify themselves, for tomorrow the Lord would do wonders among them, since to experience God's love and power we must put away sin and not grieve the Holy Spirit. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 6. Joshua spoke to the priests to take up the ark of the covenant and pass over before the people, and they did so. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 7. The Lord told Joshua that this day he would begin to magnify him in the sight of all Israel, that they might know that as he was with Moses so he would be with Joshua. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 8. Joshua was to command the priests bearing the ark that when they came to the brink of Jordan's waters they should stand still in Jordan. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 9. Joshua called the children of Israel to come and hear the words of the Lord their God. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 10. Hereby they would know the living God was among them and would without fail drive out the Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites, and Jebusites, for God's appearances for his people ought to encourage faith and hope. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 11. The ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth would pass over before them into Jordan, and the word of the Lord was as truly with Joshua as with Moses. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 12. They were to take twelve men out of the tribes of Israel, out of every tribe a man, in preparation for the memorial to come. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 13. As soon as the soles of the priests' feet rested in Jordan's waters, the waters coming down from above would be cut off and stand upon a heap, a miracle never done before except in the dividing of the Red Sea. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 14. When the people removed from their tents to pass over Jordan, the priests bearing the ark went before the people. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 15. As those bearing the ark came to Jordan and their feet were dipped in the brim of the water, for Jordan overflowed all its banks in harvest time, the power of God was magnified. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 16. The waters from above stood and rose up upon a heap far from the city Adam beside Zaretan, and those coming down toward the salt sea failed and were cut off, so the people passed over right against Jericho. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 17. The priests bearing the ark stood firm on dry ground in the midst of Jordan while all Israel passed over clean, and Henry notes this passage shadowed the believer's passage through death to heaven, with Jesus, typified by the ark, gone before.
