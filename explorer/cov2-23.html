The Text — 2 Book of the Covenant 23 (J. Cooper & A. J. Maclean, The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1902), public domain (archive.org cu31924029296170))

*BURIALS*

1 If a poor man die, let those who provide for each one, provide for his clothing. If any one who is a stranger die and he have no place to be buried, let those who havea place give [it]. But if the Church hath [a place] let it give [it], And if he have no covering, let the Church similarly give it. But if he have not grave clothes, let him be shrouded.

2 But if a man be found to have possessions, and do not leave them to the Church, let them be kept for.a time; and after a year let not the Church appropriate them, but let them be given to the poor for his soul.

3 But if he desire to be embalmed, let the deacons provide for this, a presbyter standing by.

4 If the Church have a graveyard, and there be one who abideth there and keepeth it, let the bishop provide for him from the Church, so that he be no burden to those who come there.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

If a poor man dies, those who provide for each one are to provide for his clothing. If a stranger dies and has no place to be buried, those who have a place are to give it; if the Church has a place, it is to give it, and if he has no covering, the Church is similarly to give it; if he has no grave clothes, he is to be shrouded. But if a man is found to have possessions and does not leave them to the Church, they are to be kept for a time, and after a year the Church is not to appropriate them but they are to be given to the poor for his soul. But if he desires to be embalmed, the deacons are to provide for this, a presbyter standing by. If the Church has a graveyard, and there is one who abides there and keeps it, the bishop is to provide for him from the Church, so that he is no burden to those who come there.
