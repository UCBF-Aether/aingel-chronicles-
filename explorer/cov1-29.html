The Text — 1 Book of the Covenant 29 (J. Cooper & A. J. Maclean, The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1902), public domain (archive.org cu31924029296170))

*OF WHAT SORT A PRESBYTER OUGHT TO BE*

1 Let a presbyter be ordained being testified to by all the people, according to what has been said before ; skilled in reading, meek, poor not money-loving, having laboured much in ministrations among the weak, proved to be pure, without blame; if he have been as a father to the orphans, if he have ministered to the poor; if he have not grown cold [in his love] for the Church’; if in all things he be pious, quiet, so that being [thus] he may in all respects be worthy to have those things that are fitting and suitable revealed to him by God, and also may be counted worthy of the gift of healing.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A presbyter is to be ordained being testified to by all the people, according to what has been said before: skilled in reading, meek, poor, not money-loving, having laboured much in ministrations among the weak, proved pure and without blame; if he has been as a father to the orphans, if he has ministered to the poor, if he has not grown cold in his love for the Church, if in all things he is pious and quiet — so that being thus he may be worthy to have what is fitting and suitable revealed to him by God, and to be counted worthy of the gift of healing.
