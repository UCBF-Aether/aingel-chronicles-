The Text — 1 Book of the Covenant 33 (J. Cooper & A. J. Maclean, The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1902), public domain (archive.org cu31924029296170))

*OF DEACONS*

1 The deacon is appointed, chosen like the things which have before been spoken of. If he be of good conduct,’ if he be pure, if he have been chosen for purity and for abstinence from distractions; if not, yet [if he] be the husband of one wife,’ borne witness to by all the faithful, not entangled in the businesses of the world, not knowing a handicraft, without riches, without children.

2 But if he be married or! have children, let his children be taught to work piety and to be pure, so that they may be approved by the Church,’ according to the rule of the ministry. But let the Church take care for them, so that they may persevere in the law and in the work of the ministry.

Overview

The deacon is appointed, chosen like the things spoken of before: if he be of good conduct, pure, chosen for purity and for abstinence from distractions; if not, yet if he be the husband of one wife, borne witness to by all the faithful, not entangled in the businesses of the world, not knowing a handicraft, without riches and without children. But if he be married or have children, his children are to be taught to work piety and to be pure, so that they may be approved by the Church according to the rule of the ministry, and the Church is to take care for them so that they may persevere in the law and in the work of the ministry.
