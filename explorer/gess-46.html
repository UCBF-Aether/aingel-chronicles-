The Text — Gәssәw 46 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 [Topic:] Concerning him who strikes a man until he dies, and him who overpowers [?] a virgin.

2 If a priest has a quarrel [?] with another and strikes him, and he dies — let him be deposed on account of the hardness of his heart; and in the case of a layman, let him be expelled.

3 And if a layman has violated [?] a virgin before she has been promised [?], and has cohabited with her — let him be expelled.

4 And he shall not marry another, but he shall abide with her whom he has forced [?], even if she is poor and deformed [?].

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A priest who quarrels with another, strikes him, and he dies, is deposed for the hardness of his heart; a layman in the same case is expelled. And a layman who violates a virgin before she has been promised and cohabits with her is expelled, and must not marry another but abide with the woman he forced, even if she is poor and deformed.
