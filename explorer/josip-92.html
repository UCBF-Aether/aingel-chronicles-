How to read the labels

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A fragmentary council narrative: a bishop deposed and banished, a written repentance and restoration, the death of Theodosius and the reign of Marcian, Leo of Rome, Dioscorus questioned, the Book of Leo read in the assembly, the dispute over two natures after the union, and exiled bishops restored. Many names, places and motions are marked (?) or [...], so the sequence is only broadly recoverable.
