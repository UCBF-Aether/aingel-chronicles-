The Text — Sinodos 35 (Ge'ez text of G. Horner, The Statutes of the Apostles; or, Canones Ecclesiastici (London: Williams & Norgate, 1904), public domain)

1 Canon 26[?]: Concerning rest on the two sabbaths[?]: let them rest on the sabbath and on Sunday. Let the believers, men and women, be kindly disposed[?] … let the servants devote themselves[?] to the church[?], as we taught.

2 And … from work … let them be devoted[?] to the church, and to the teaching of the worship of God, and the teaching of the Holy Spirit, which inheriteth the kingdom of heaven in the world to come.

3 For on the sabbath day God rested from all His work which He did; and He finished the heaven and …[?], and the earth, and all that is therein.

4 … for the night and for the day, for the sun and for the moon and for the stars, and for the seasons and for the reckonings[?].

5 And … the waters were gathered into their places[?] …[?].

6 And all these He did in all the days[?], and He finished His work; and God rested on the sabbath day.

7 And He named it[?] the sabbath, and sanctified it, and set it apart from all the days, and blessed it.

8 And He showed …[?] that it be a memorial of His rest and a memorial of His work …[?].

9 And God rested from all His work, showing us[?] that we should honour the sabbath[?] … from all creation, which He created and finished; He rested on this seventh [day].

10 And …[?] the holy sabbath, blessed and honoured …[?].

11 And therefore it said in the Law of Genesis[?]: And God blessed the sabbath day — this is the sabbath — and sanctified it[?], for therein He rested from all His work.

12 … God did … and showed that … holy … shall be called the sabbath of rest[?].

13 Hear and know the honour of the sabbath: for it was not from man that the sabbath was first honoured, but from God its Maker and its Creator it was honoured.

14 And He honoured it and blessed it, and ordained rest for man and …[?].

15 And moreover concerning the memorial of His rest and His work, that it may be known that He is the Maker and Creator of all. And He said: Keep ye My sabbaths, for it is a sign between Me and you, and your generations.

16 For the day of Sunday[?] is the day of the resurrection of our Lord Jesus Christ; this is called the first-day sabbath[?]; and both are the appointed[?] sabbaths.

17 …[feast-list fragment]… the Pascha[?] … the world[?].

18 And when they pierced His side, there came forth from Him blood and water.

19 And when they crucified the Lord, all creation feared and trembled before Him, because of what the godless Jews did; and they could not bear to look upon the Lord in His mocking[?].

20 And at the time …[?] when He bowed[?] His head for us, and gave us rest …[?].

21 And at the cock-crow[?] … for at this hour … the tidings of the coming of the day, for the work of the day[?].

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 26 concerns rest on the two sabbaths: believers rest on the Sabbath and on Sunday, servants devoting themselves to the church, to the teaching of God's worship and of the Holy Spirit. The Sabbath is grounded in creation — God finished heaven, earth and all therein, rested, named the day Sabbath, sanctified and blessed it as a memorial of his rest and work and a sign between him and his generations — while Sunday is the day of the resurrection of the Lord Jesus Christ, called the first-day sabbath, so both are appointed sabbaths. A feast-list fragment follows, then the crucifixion itself: his side pierced with blood and water, all creation fearing and trembling, his head bowed giving rest, and the cock-crow bringing tidings of the coming day.
