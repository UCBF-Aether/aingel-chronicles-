Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This opening discourse is Trinitarian from its first line, written in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Its readable thread concerns the being of the Lord and his union, with the Son named as light from light, Jesus Christ, the Only-begotten, begotten of the Father before the ages. Much of the chapter survives only in fragments, so this summary follows the readable thread rather than the broken wording.
