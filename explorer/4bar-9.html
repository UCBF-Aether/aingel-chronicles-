Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The book closes in worship and in a strange, gap-marked scene: the people rejoice and offer sacrifice for seven days, Jeremiah offers alone on the tenth day and prays at length for his people, and the chapter ends with people stoning a man they suppose to be Jeremiah — while, the text says, Jeremiah himself stands in their midst — and then burying him and marking the tomb as the helper of Jeremiah. The text, with several passages marked uncertain, leaves the scene standing without explanation. Interpreted Chapter text and study notes for this chapter
