The Text — Teach 1 (Printed Ge'ez of Lifchitz & Grébaut (1940), pp. 44–52, for chs 2–4; project Ge'ez corpus for chs 1 and 5)

1 Concerning the hidden teaching, which was spoken of old by Peter Fora[?], unto the hidden believers. The hidden teaching is as followeth: He who was before, and is; who came; who suffered; and died; and was buried; and rose again. He brake[?] [the gates] of death; he opened [them]; and the dead arose. Was it not he alone who became man — whose flesh is of the Holy Spirit — for Adam, with his soul; who left not his soul in death; who in the Spirit called unto Adam, and made him live.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The hidden teaching, as the chapter presents it, was spoken of old by Peter Fora — the name is printed with a mark of uncertainty — unto the hidden believers. In brief compass it runs: He who was before, and is; who came, suffered, died, was buried and rose again; who broke the gates of death and opened them, and the dead arose; who alone became man, whose flesh is of the Holy Spirit, for Adam, with his soul; who left not his soul in death, and who in the Spirit called unto Adam and made him live.
