Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter is heavily fragmentary, and no continuous story can be recovered from it alone. Its recoverable thread concerns the twelve disciples and apostles, divided responses to Jesus (some calling him a prophet, others Christ), priests and Pharisees, a Bethany and Passover setting in Nisan, and cries of “Hosanna, O Son of David”, with Rome named as the adversary of the Jews. Many words and whole clauses are marked (?) in the text itself, so any fuller narrative would be invention, not reading.
