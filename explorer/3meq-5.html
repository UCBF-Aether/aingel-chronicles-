Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Meqabis’s children keep their father’s ordinance: they fear the Lord, give their goods to the poor, and comfort the meek, holding to the food and conduct laws the chapter sets out. Then Ṣiruṣaydan, king of the Chaldeans, destroys their cities and carries them off into idol districts, where the chapter contrasts two ways of life — those who know not the Lord, eating carrion and blood and worshipping Beelphegor, an idol the chapter calls deaf, dumb, blind, and powerless to kill, make alive, plant, or forgive. The children of Meqabis, by contrast, keep apart from that defilement. Interpreted The chapter text itself; no study note exists for this chapter
