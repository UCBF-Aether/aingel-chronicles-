Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter contrasts two ways in seven verses: the way of Cain, who slew his brother through jealousy, followed by all who do iniquity, anger and hatred against their neighbour, and the way of Abel, who loved God and offered with a pure heart. The wicked, it says, shall be rebuked, their judgment read from books of shame before angels, men and all creation in the day of judgment, when fire is kindled whence there is no escape unto eternity, while to all who walk in truth God shows love, as to righteous Abel. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
