The Text — Jubilees 9 (R. H. Charles, The Book of Jubilees — his translation from the Ethiopic (1902; the text as reprinted in the Apocrypha and Pseudepigrapha of the Old Testament, 1913))

1 And Ham divided amongst his sons, and the first portion came forth for Cush towards the east, and to the west of him for Mizraim and to the west

2 of him for Put and to the west of him [and to the west thereof] on the sea for Canaan.

3 And Shem also divided amongst his sons, and the first portion came forth for Elam and his sons, to the east of the

4 the river Tigris till it approacheth the east, the whole land of India, and on the Red Sea on its coast, and the waters of Dedan, and all the mountains of Mebri and Ela, and all the land of Susan and all that is on the side of Pharnak to the Red Sea and the river Tina.

5 And for Asshur came forth the second portion, all the land of Asshur and Nineveh and

6 Shinar and to the border of India, and it ascendeth and

7 skirteth the river.

8 And for Arpachshad came forth the third portion, all the land of the region of the Chaldees to the east of the Euphrates, bordering on the Red Sea, and all the waters of the desert close to

9 the tongue of the sea which looketh towards

10 Egypt, all the land of Lebanon and Sanir and 'Amana to the border of the Euphrates.

11 And for Aram there came forth the fourth portion, all the land of Mesopotamia between the Tigris and the Euphrates to the north of the Chaldees to the border of the mountains of Asshur and the land of 'Arara.

12 And there came forth for Lud the fifth portion, the mountains of Asshur and all appertaining to them till it reacheth the Great Sea, and till it reacheth the east of Asshur

13 his brother.

14 And Japheth also divided the land of his inheritance amongst his sons.

15 And the first portion came forth for Gomer to the east from the north side to the river Tina; and in the north there came forth for Magog all the inner portions of the north until it reacheth to the sea of Me'at.

16 And

17 Libya (west of Egypt).

18 for Madai came forth as his portion that he should possess from the west of his two brothers to the islands and to the coasts of the islands.

19 And for Javan came forth the fourth portion every island and the islands which are towards the border of Lud.

20 And for Tubal there came forth the fifth portion in the midst of the tongue which approacheth towards the border of the portion of Lud to the second tongue, to the region beyond the second tongue unto the third tongue.

21 And for Meshech came forth the sixth portion, all the region beyond the third tongue till it approacheth the east of Gadir.

22 And for Tiras there came forth the seventh portion, four great islands in the midst of the sea, which reach to the portion of Ham [and the islands of Kamaturi came out by lot for the sons of Arpachshad as his inheritance].

23 And thus the sons of Noah divided unto their sons in the presence of Noah their father, and he bound them all by an oath, imprecating a curse on every one that sought to seize the portion which had not fallen (to him) by his lot.

24 And they all said, "So be it ; so be it," for themselves and their sons for ever throughout their generations till the day of judgment, on which the Lord God shall judge them with a sword and with fire, for all the unclean wickedness of their errors, wherewith they have filled the earth with transgression and uncleanness and fornication and sin.

25 Noah's Sons led astray by Evil Spirits ; Noah's Prayer ; Mastema ; Death of Noah.

How to read the labels

Every substantive historical claim in the editorial panels below carries one of three labels, plus a source reference:

  • Documented — directly supported by a cited source.
  • Interpreted — a reasoned explanation or proposed relationship.
  • Unresolved — evidence is insufficient to establish the claim.

A claim does NOT become Documented merely because a commentary states it. Commentary views are attributed to their authors and labeled Interpreted unless a primary textual source directly supports the claim. Where evidence runs out, the page says Unresolved and stops — it does not fill the gap.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

In plain language: Ham divided amongst his sons, and the first portion came forth for Cush towards the east, and to the west of him for Mizraim. Interpreted Study notes for this chapter

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators. Where a note is credited to Matthew Henry or Jamieson-Fausset-Brown, it is on the parallel Genesis passage; see the note on voices in Perspectives.

  • Verse 1. Ham divided amongst his sons, and the first portion came forth for Cush towards the east, and to the west of him for Mizraim. JFB on Genesis 10:6 notes Ham's sons emigrated southward, Cush in Arabia and Mizraim in Egypt, Upper and Lower, and the division runs westward from Cush. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 2. To the west of Mizraim came Put, and to the west thereof, on the sea, came Canaan. The westward order completes Ham's four sons: Cush in the east, then Mizraim, Put, and Canaan on the sea. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 3. Shem also divided amongst his sons, and the first portion came forth for Elam and his sons, to the east of the river Tigris. The Shemite division begins with Elam in the east, as Ham's began with Cush. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 4. Elam's portion runs till it approaches the east, the whole land of India, and on the Red Sea on its coast, and the waters of Dedan, and all the mountains of Mebri and Ela, and all the land of Susan and all on the side of Pharnak to the Red Sea and the river Tina. Elam holds the far east, from India and the Red Sea coast to Susan and the Tina. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 5. For Asshur came forth the second portion, all the land of Asshur and Nineveh and Shinar and to the border of India. Asshur's portion centres on Assyria, Nineveh and Shinar, reaching toward India. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 6. This verse continues Asshur's portion: it ascends and skirts the river. The boundary follows the river's course, the Tigris line of verse 3. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 7. This verse completes Asshur's boundary: it ascends and skirts the river. The river-skirted border closes the second Shemite portion. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 8. For Arpachshad came forth the third portion, all the land of the region of the Chaldees to the east of the Euphrates, bordering on the Red Sea, and all the waters of the desert close to the tongue of the sea. JFB on Genesis 10:24 places Arphaxad's posterity in the extensive valley of Shinar on the Tigris, toward southern Mesopotamia, including the region on the east side of the river. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 9. This verse continues Arpachshad's portion: the tongue of the sea which looks towards Egypt. The desert waters reach the sea-tongue facing Egypt. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 10. The portion closes with all the land of Lebanon and Sanir and 'Amana to the border of the Euphrates. Lebanon, Sanir and Amanah bring Arpachshad's portion west to the Euphrates border. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 11. For Aram came forth the fourth portion, all the land of Mesopotamia between the Tigris and the Euphrates, to the north of the Chaldees, to the border of the mountains of Asshur and the land of 'Arara. JFB on Genesis 10:23 notes that in the general division of the earth, Armenia, Mesopotamia and Syria fell to Aram's descendants. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 12. For Lud came forth the fifth portion, the mountains of Asshur and all appertaining to them, till it reaches the Great Sea. Lud holds Asshur's mountains westward to the Great Sea. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 13. This verse completes Lud's portion: till it reaches the east of Asshur his brother. Lud's land borders his brother Asshur's land on the east, closing the Shemite division. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 14. Japheth also divided the land of his inheritance amongst his sons. The third division begins, matching Shem's and Ham's divisions before it. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 15. The first portion came forth for Gomer to the east from the north side to the river Tina, and in the north there came forth for Magog all the inner portions of the north until it reaches the sea of Me'at. Gomer holds the east-north to the Tina, Magog the inner north to the Me'at sea. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 16. This verse is the hinge to the next portion: and for Madai came forth his portion. The sentence is completed by verse 18's islands and coasts. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 18. For Madai came forth as his portion that he should possess from the west of his two brothers to the islands and to the coasts of the islands. Madai holds west of his brothers, out to the islands and their coasts. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 19. For Javan came forth the fourth portion, every island and the islands towards the border of Lud. Javan holds the islands, reaching toward Lud's border, the island portion of Japheth's inheritance. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 20. For Tubal came forth the fifth portion, in the midst of the tongue which approaches towards the border of the portion of Lud, to the second tongue, to the region beyond the second tongue unto the third tongue. Tubal's portion is counted by tongues of land, from Lud's border through the second tongue to the third. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 21. For Meshech came forth the sixth portion, all the region beyond the third tongue till it approaches the east of Gadir. Meshech holds beyond the third tongue eastward to Gadir, which Charles's note identifies with Cadiz. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 22. For Tiras came forth the seventh portion, four great islands in the midst of the sea, which reach to the portion of Ham, and the islands of Kamaturi came out by lot for the sons of Arpachshad as his inheritance. Tiras holds four great islands toward Ham's portion, with the bracketed Kamaturi islands falling by lot to Arpachshad's sons. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 23. Thus the sons of Noah divided unto their sons in the presence of Noah their father, and he bound them all by an oath, imprecating a curse on everyone that sought to seize the portion which had not fallen to him by his lot. The threefold division is sealed by oath and curse: seizing another's lot is accursed. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 24. They all said, 'So be it; so be it,' for themselves and their sons forever throughout their generations till the day of judgment, on which the Lord God shall judge them with a sword and with fire, for all the unclean wickedness of their errors, wherewith they have filled the earth with transgression, uncleanness, fornication and sin. The double amen binds every generation till the judgment by sword and fire, the sanction behind the oath of verse 23. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
  • Verse 25. This unit is the heading for the narrative that follows: Noah's sons led astray by evil spirits, Noah's prayer, Mastema, and the death of Noah. It divides the division of the earth from the demonology and death that open chapter 10. Interpreted Study note, summarizing R. H. Charles
