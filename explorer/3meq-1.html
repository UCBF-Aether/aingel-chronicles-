Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The book opens with destruction: Meqabis of Moab, whose city is Remat, falls on the Jews from Mesopotamia of Syria to the border of Jerusalem, joined by Edomites, Tyrians, and Amalekites, and makes Jerusalem like a trough of filth, its corpses food for birds and beasts. The chapter frames the sack as punishment — when the children of Israel sinned, the Lord sent Meqabis against them, because they had despised his word. Much of the wording is marked uncertain in the translation, so the details of names and places in this chapter should be read as the text gives them, not as settled history.
