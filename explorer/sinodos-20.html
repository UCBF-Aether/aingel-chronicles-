Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter gathers canons on daily discipline and the mystery. Deacons are to be subject to the bishop and report the sick for the priests to visit; the faithful awake, wash their hands and pray, hasten to the church where the Spirit is, and choose the word of teaching above work. Those who would partake of the mystery are to taste nothing beforehand and to be admonished with discipline, while those who desire to abstain in penitence are not cut off from the cup yet may not partake except as the faithful — and one who falls from Christ's body or cup is likened to one who denied Christ and finds condemnation. The chapter ends with deacons and priests gathering in prayer to the bishop and teaching in the churches. Interpreted Summary of this chapter's text, as printed above
