Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This is the fullest ordination liturgy in the book's first part: a bishop chosen from all the people with priests and deacons, hands laid on him on the Sabbath morning, and prayers asking for the power of the Holy Spirit that he may feed the flock and minister without fault. The rite continues through the people's responses — Holy things to the holy, The Lord be with you all, And with thy spirit — to the bread and cup words, Take, eat; this is my flesh, for the remission of sins, and This is my blood, with remembrance of death and resurrection and a prayer for the Holy Spirit on the receivers, and closes with a prayer for the ordination of deacons and the dismissal, Depart in peace.
