Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter honours the Meqabyan, the children of Meqabis, who believed, refused the sacrifice of idols and the eating of impure sacrifice, and committed their souls unto death, knowing the dead shall rise and there shall be judgment after the resurrection. Better, it says, is one day of rejoicing in the Garden than a thousand years of life on earth; the martyrs knew their life from this passing world, trusted in God, and shall rejoice in the latter day in soul and flesh, reigning in the world to come where there is no sickness, sorrow or death, their faces shining sevenfold more than the sun.
