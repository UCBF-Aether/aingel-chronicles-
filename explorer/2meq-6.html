Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Sirusaydan sets up a great image with a priesthood of falsehood, offers wheat, firstfruits and rams, and imagines the gods eat and drink; when the children of Meqabis refuse to bow and cry to his gods, his ministers seek their destruction and the king, wroth, has them brought before him. Threatened with torment, they refuse, are cast into fire and commit their souls to God, and the chapter gives them a long speech after the flames: God alone made heaven, earth, sea, sun, moon and stars, kills and gives life, chastises and shows mercy, and Sirusaydan, taught by Satan, shall go down with his gods into Gehenna, whence there is no escape.
