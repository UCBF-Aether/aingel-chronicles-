Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter reckons days from evening (the day of Friday is used as the example), and treats offerings, night watch and prayer, standing at Mass, and washing: a man who lies with a woman on one night takes an offering on the second day without washing in water, since water cleanses only the defilement of the flesh. It closes with fasting from food in order to fast from desire, and Paul's saying that those without the law shall perish.
