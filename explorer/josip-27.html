Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Going forth from Sheol is by Jesus Christ, the chapter says, and the Church is presented as the Paradise of God, with Adam in Paradise set beside it. Its practical thread is that priests bring the people the flesh and blood of Christ, that believers may see, taste, and desire it.
