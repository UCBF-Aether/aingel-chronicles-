The Text — Gәssәw 18 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 [Topic:] Concerning the priests [?] who strike everyone.

2 A bishop or an elder who strikes a Christian or one who does not believe, when he has sinned, and seeks by this deed to make men fear — we command that he be deposed [?]. For the Lord did not command us to do this.

3 And not in any place [?] — for when they struck him, he did not strike.

4 And when they reviled him, he did not revile.

5 And when they afflicted him, he did not take vengeance or become angry [?].

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A bishop or elder who strikes a Christian or an unbeliever who has sinned, in order to make men fear, is to be deposed: the Lord did not command this. The canon then holds up the counter-example in three clauses — when struck, he did not strike; when reviled, he did not revile; when afflicted, he did not take vengeance or become angry.
