Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter is among the book's most fragmentary: a proud figure goes about in evil and affliction, the sun is made to stand still at Gibeon as in Joshua's day for the destruction of Canaanite armies, and a conqueror spreads from Zebulon to Macedonia, Samaria, Assyria, Kedar and Sidon, bowing to idols and eating dead flesh and blood. Its through-line survives the gaps: God avenges the proud who walk not in his commandment and wipes their memorial from the earth, while to doers of good he renders good, and so shall it befall all who spurn God by evil deeds.
