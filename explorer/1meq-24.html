Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Opening with Gideon, who believed God with a small host against numberless thousands, the chapter turns to God's complaint: he brought Israel from bondage and asked only that they walk in his law, yet they set up another god beside him. Prophets from Samuel onward, Ezekiel, Enoch and Asaph are named, warned of a lawless, drunken, fornicating enemy people who oppress the poor, seize goods by violence and have no mercy; God waits for repentance, the chapter says, but if they enter not into it, he will quench them as he did those before them who kept not his commandment. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
