Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter addresses earth and death themselves: earth has gathered kings, rulers, great ones, the strong, the knowledgeable and the beautiful, and death has taken their souls by the will of the good God, fearing neither might nor greatness, sparing neither poor nor strong, good nor evil. Earth drank their blood and gathered their flesh until the blowing of the trumpet, when the dead shall rise as the twinkling of an eye and be judged as their works, and then shall rejoice those who did good.
