The Text — Gәssәw 13 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 [Topic:] Concerning him who marries two [women], or a concubine [?].

2 He who marries two women, or a widow, after his baptism, cannot be ordained as a bishop or an elder or a deacon, nor shall he at all be numbered with the ordained priests.

3 He who marries a concubine [?], or a woman who has become suspected [?], or a woman who has been violated [?], or a harlot [?], or a maidservant, or a woman who appears in the theatre [?] — he shall not be ordained as a bishop or an elder or a deacon, and he shall not at all be counted among the ordained priests. And he who dwells with two sisters, or with the daughter of his brother — he shall not be able to become a priest.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This canon bars certain marriages from ordination altogether. A man who after baptism marries two women, or a widow, cannot be ordained bishop, elder, or deacon, nor counted among the ordained priests at all. The same bar covers marrying a concubine, a suspected woman, a violated woman, a harlot, a maidservant, or a woman who appears in the theatre — and a man living with two sisters or his brother's daughter cannot become a priest.
