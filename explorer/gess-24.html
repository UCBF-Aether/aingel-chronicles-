The Text — Gәssәw 24 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 Let a foreign bishop not receive [them] — his elders and his deacons [?] — but let him cut them off [?]; as the inferior clergy — except if they have his permission: let them be ordained and joined.

2 And if they are preachers of righteousness, let them enter; but if not, let them give them what they need, and let them not join with them; for many are such — impure.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A foreign bishop must not receive another bishop's elders and deacons, but cut them off as inferior clergy — unless he has their own bishop's permission, in which case they may be ordained and joined. Travelling preachers of righteousness may enter; others are to be given what they need but not joined, for many such are impure. Interpreted Paraphrase of the chapter's own text; Standard corpus, Gessew 24

Unfamiliar terms

  • Foreign bishop: A bishop other than the cleric's own. This canon forbids him to receive another bishop's elders and deacons without their bishop's permission. Interpreted Reading of the chapter's own usage; Standard corpus, Gessew
  • Preachers of righteousness: Travelling preachers whose teaching is sound. They alone, among travelling clergy, may enter; others receive material help but are not joined. Interpreted Reading of the chapter's own usage; Standard corpus, Gessew
