Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter's theme is hiddenness: Christ hid his divinity from the Devil and from all men, and his fame was not made manifest that he is from above. The Devil thinks him like other men, and the chapter reflects on a divine word spoken and not done in the way the Devil expected.
