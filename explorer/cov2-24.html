The Text — 2 Book of the Covenant 24 (J. Cooper & A. J. Maclean, The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1902), public domain (archive.org cu31924029296170))

*HOURS OF PRAYER*

1 Let the people alway take care about the early dawn, that arising and washing their hands they immediately pray. And so let each one go to the work which he willeth.

2 Let all take care to pray at the third hour with mourning and labour, either in the church, or in the house because they cannot go (to the church). For this is the hour of the fixing + of the Only-begotten on the cross.

3 But at the sixth hour similarly let there be prayer with sorrow. For then the daylight was divided by the darkness. Let there be then that voice which is like to the prophets, and to creation mourning.

4 At the ninth hour also let prayer be protracted, as with a hymn of praise that is like to the souls of those who give praise to God that lieth not, as one who hath remembered His saints, and hath sent His Word and Wisdom to enlighten them. For in that hour life was opened to the faithful, and blood and water were shed from the side of our Lord.

5 But at evening, when it is the beginning of another day, shewing an image of the resurrection, He hath caused us to give praise.

6 But at midnight let them arise praising and lauding because of the resurrection.

7 But at dawn [let them arise] praising with psalms, because after He rose He glorified the Father while they ‘ were singing psalms. But if any have a consort or wife [not] faithful, let the husband who is faithful go and pray at these times without fail.!

8 Let those who are chaste not lessen [them]. For the adornments of heaven give praise, the lights, the sun, the moon, the stars, the lightnings, the thunders, the clouds, the angels, the archangels, the glories, the dominions, the whole [heavenly] army, the depths, the sea, the rivers, the wells, fire, dew, and all nature that produceth rain.

9 All the saints also give praise and all the souls of the righteous. These, then, who pray are numbered together in the remembrance of God.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The people are always to take care about the early dawn, arising and washing their hands and immediately praying, and so each one going to the work he wills. All are to take care to pray at the third hour with mourning and labour, either in the church or in the house because they cannot go to the church — for this is the hour of the fixing of the Only-begotten on the cross. At the sixth hour similarly there is to be prayer with sorrow, for then the daylight was divided by the darkness, with a voice like the prophets and creation mourning. At the ninth hour also prayer is to be protracted, as with a hymn of praise like the souls of those who give praise to God that lies not, as one who has remembered His saints and sent His Word and Wisdom to enlighten them — for in that hour life was opened to the faithful, and blood and water were shed from the side of the Lord. At evening, when it is the beginning of another day, showing an image of the resurrection, He has caused us to give praise. At midnight they are to arise praising and lauding because of the resurrection. At dawn they are to arise praising with psalms, because after He rose He glorified the Father while they were singing psalms. But if anyone has a consort or wife who is not faithful, the faithful husband is to go and pray at these times without fail. Those who are chaste are not to lessen them. For the adornments of heaven give praise — the lights, sun, moon, stars, lightnings, thunders, clouds, angels, archangels, glories, dominions, the whole heavenly army, the depths, sea, rivers, wells, fire, dew, and all nature that produces rain. All the saints also give praise, and all the souls of the righteous. These, then, who pray are numbered together in the remembrance of God.
