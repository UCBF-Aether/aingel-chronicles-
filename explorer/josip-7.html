Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter states its creed-like claims plainly: the Son is as his Father and God in the Father, the Holy Spirit is God as the Spirit of the Father and of the Son, and each is Lord. It confesses one Lord Jesus Christ, the only Son of God, and one baptism, oblation, and priesthood in one Church, the congregation of the apostles. The chapter then breaks off: its last three verse units stand as bare marks of loss (see the Trace panel). Interpreted Chapter text as printed above; study-notes file for this chapter is silent
