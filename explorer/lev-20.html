The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Leviticus 20 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

2 Again, thou shalt say to the children of Israel, Whosoever he be of the children of Israel, or of the strangers that sojourn in Israel, that giveth any of his seed unto Molech; he shall surely be put to death: the people of the land shall stone him with stones.

3 And I will set my face against that man, and will cut him off from among his people; because he hath given of his seed unto Molech, to defile my sanctuary, and to profane my holy name.

4 And if the people of the land do any ways hide their eyes from the man, when he giveth of his seed unto Molech, and kill him not:

5 Then I will set my face against that man, and against his family, and will cut him off, and all that go a whoring after him, to commit whoredom with Molech, from among their people.

6 And the soul that turneth after such as have familiar spirits, and after wizards, to go a whoring after them, I will even set my face against that soul, and will cut him off from among his people.

7 Sanctify yourselves therefore, and be ye holy: for I am the LORD your God.

8 And ye shall keep my statutes, and do them: I am the LORD which sanctify you.

9 For every one that curseth his father or his mother shall be surely put to death: he hath cursed his father or his mother; his blood shall be upon him.

10 And the man that committeth adultery with another man’s wife, even he that committeth adultery with his neighbour’s wife, the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.

11 And the man that lieth with his father’s wife hath uncovered his father’s nakedness: both of them shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.

12 And if a man lie with his daughter in law, both of them shall surely be put to death: they have wrought confusion; their blood shall be upon them.

13 If a man also lie with mankind, as he lieth with a woman, both of them have committed an abomination: they shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.

14 And if a man take a wife and her mother, it is wickedness: they shall be burnt with fire, both he and they; that there be no wickedness among you.

15 And if a man lie with a beast, he shall surely be put to death: and ye shall slay the beast.

16 And if a woman approach unto any beast, and lie down thereto, thou shalt kill the woman, and the beast: they shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.

17 And if a man shall take his sister, his father’s daughter, or his mother’s daughter, and see her nakedness, and she see his nakedness; it is a wicked thing; and they shall be cut off in the sight of their people: he hath uncovered his sister’s nakedness; he shall bear his iniquity.

18 And if a man shall lie with a woman having her sickness, and shall uncover her nakedness; he hath discovered her fountain, and she hath uncovered the fountain of her blood: and both of them shall be cut off from among their people.

19 And thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy mother’s sister, nor of thy father’s sister: for he uncovereth his near kin: they shall bear their iniquity.

20 And if a man shall lie with his uncle’s wife, he hath uncovered his uncle’s nakedness: they shall bear their sin; they shall die childless.

21 And if a man shall take his brother’s wife, it is an unclean thing: he hath uncovered his brother’s nakedness; they shall be childless.

22 Ye shall therefore keep all my statutes, and all my judgments, and do them: that the land, whither I bring you to dwell therein, spue you not out.

23 And ye shall not walk in the manners of the nation, which I cast out before you: for they committed all these things, and therefore I abhorred them.

24 But I have said unto you, Ye shall inherit their land, and I will give it unto you to possess it, a land that floweth with milk and honey: I am the LORD your God, which have separated you from other people.

25 Ye shall therefore put difference between clean beasts and unclean, and between unclean fowls and clean: and ye shall not make your souls abominable by beast, or by fowl, or by any manner of living thing that creepeth on the ground, which I have separated from you as unclean.

26 And ye shall be holy unto me: for I the LORD am holy, and have severed you from other people, that ye should be mine.

27 A man also or woman that hath a familiar spirit, or that is a wizard, shall surely be put to death: they shall stone them with stones: their blood shall be upon them.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter attaches penalties to the crimes forbidden in the previous section. Giving seed to Molech, turning to mediums and familiar spirits, cursing father or mother, adultery, and the incest and unnatural unions listed here are punished by death, usually by stoning, or by being cut off, or by dying childless, according to the case. The chapter repeats the food distinction and closes with the ground of the whole law: Israel is to be holy to the LORD, who has severed them from other peoples to be his own, lest the land spew them out as it spewed out the nations before them. What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. The LORD speaks to Moses, giving penal sanctions for the crimes of chapter 18, beginning with giving seed to Molech. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 2. Whoever of Israel or sojourning strangers gives seed to Molech shall surely be put to death, the people of the land stoning him, the stoning being carried out by witnesses pushing him from an eminence onto a stone, then completing the death with stones. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 3. God will set His face against that man and cut him off, because he has given seed to Molech, defiling the sanctuary and profaning God's holy name. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 4. If the people of the land hide their eyes from the man and do not kill him, that is, connive at countrymen practising Molech's horrid rites, God denounces severest penalties against the connivers and their families. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 5. God will set His face against that man and his family and cut off him and all who go a-whoring after him to commit harlotry with Molech, the family sharing the penalty for connivance. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 6. The person who turns to mediums and familiar spirits to go a-whoring after them, God will set His face against him and cut him off, as with Molech harlotry. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 7. Therefore they shall sanctify themselves and be holy, for the LORD is their God, the minute specification of crimes here showing their sad prevalence among idolatrous neighbours and Israel's proneness to follow them. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 8. They shall keep His statutes and do them, for He is the LORD who sanctifies them, holiness being both command and gift. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 9. Everyone who curses father or mother shall surely be put to death, his blood being upon him, and where no mode is described, stoning is meant. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 10. The man who commits adultery with his neighbour's wife, adulterer and adulteress, shall surely be put to death, stoning being the implied mode. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 11. A man lying with his father's wife has uncovered his father's nakedness, and both shall surely be put to death, their blood upon them. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 12. A man lying with his daughter-in-law, both shall surely be put to death, for they have wrought confusion, their blood upon them. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 13. A man lying with mankind as with womankind has committed abomination with the other, and both shall surely be put to death, their blood upon them. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 14. A man taking a wife and her mother commits wickedness, and they shall be burnt with fire, the only instance of another capital form, probably stoning first and the body afterwards consumed by fire. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 15. A man lying with a beast shall surely be put to death, and the beast shall be slain, the unnatural crime being purged with both parties. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 16. A woman approaching any beast to lie down thereto shall be put to death, woman and beast, for they have wrought confusion. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 17. A man taking his sister, father's daughter or mother's daughter, and seeing her nakedness, it is a wicked thing, and they shall be cut off in the sight of their people, the public excision affixing indelible ignominy. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 18. A man lying with a woman in her sickness and uncovering her fountain shall both be cut off from among their people, the same public cutting off as for sister incest. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 19. He shall not uncover the nakedness of his mother's sister or father's sister, for he uncovers his near kin, and they shall bear their iniquity, the minute enumeration showing the prevalence of such crimes among neighbours. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 20. A man lying with his uncle's wife has uncovered his uncle's nakedness, and they shall bear their sin and die childless, either having no children by God's judgment or their offspring being denied ordinary privileges in Israel. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 21. A man taking his brother's wife, it is an unclean thing, he has uncovered his brother's nakedness, and they shall be childless, the lesser grade of penalty for this alliance. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 22. They shall keep all His statutes and judgments and do them, that the land where He brings them to dwell does not spew them out, as it spewed out the nations for these crimes. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 23. They shall not walk in the manners of the nation God casts out before them, for they committed all these things, and therefore God abhorred them. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 24. God has said He will give them the land flowing with milk and honey for an inheritance, and He has separated them from other people, their selection being to preserve knowledge and worship of the true God amid universal apostasy, with clean-unclean distinction as a great means of separation. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 25. They shall put difference between clean beasts and unclean, and between unclean fowls and clean, and not make their souls abominable by beast, fowl, or creeping thing separated as unclean, the meat distinction being repeated here with emphatic solemnity. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 26. They shall be holy unto the LORD, for He is holy, and He has severed them from other people, that they should be His. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 27. A man or woman with a familiar spirit, or a wizard, shall surely be put to death, the people stoning them with stones, their blood upon them, stoning being the mode where none is described. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
