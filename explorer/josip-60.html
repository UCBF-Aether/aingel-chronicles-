Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The first half continues the reckoning of the First Day and a forty-day fast from desire and food. The second half then presents, without yet judging it, an account of the Formed One: an angel, a body without breath for five days, breath created within it, and all the angels commanded to worship Adam, save one who transgressed and worshipped not. The judgment on that account comes in the next chapter, not this one. Interpreted Reading of this chapter's text as printed above; the study-notes file for this chapter is silent
