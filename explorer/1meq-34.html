Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter prophesies kingdoms rising against kingdoms, Medes, Rome against Macedonia and Nineveh and Persia, Ethiopia against Alexandria, Moab against Amalek, brother against brother, with slaughter, famine and the scourge of God, until the day of God comes like lightning from east to west and every one is recompensed according to his deeds. Judah, Cyprus, Samaria, Damascus, Babylon, Arabia, India, Noba, Saba, Egypt and Ethiopia are all named in the judgment, and the chapter closes with God as he who kills and makes alive, tries sinners as gold and silver in fire, and will try the deeds of all Israel and all nations. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
