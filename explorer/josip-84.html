Overview

This chapter opens with an account concerning the assembly of Gabriel and Abba Sawiros, a book meant to help believers know their faith, a queen and sister, and the Jacobites, and then gives two headings, “The account of holy Mary” (verse 15) and “The account of the teaching of the fathers” (verse 20).
