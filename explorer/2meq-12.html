Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Woe is pronounced on the mighty men of Israel who kept not what God commanded in the tabernacle but followed pride, fornication and falsehood; God's wrath burns against them like fire before wind, yet he is merciful, remembers they are flesh and blood, and promises deliverance to those who cry to him, trust him and keep his commandment. The chapter then turns to Sirusaydan, boasting that his time is as the time of heaven and that he makes the sun and dies not for ever, and while the word is unfinished, the angel of death, Ṭələməyakos, smites his heart, he dies, and the Chaldean army plunders his city and burns it with fire.
