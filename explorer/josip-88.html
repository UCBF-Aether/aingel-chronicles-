Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Headed as the second discourse and exposition of the apostolic fathers' faith, this chapter argues from the prophets and the apostles: ascension and the sending of the Spirit, healings and wonders, the virgin birth with its seal unbroken, Isaiah's suffering servant, the brazen serpent hung on a tall tree after the likeness of the cross, David's psalms of crucifixion, resurrection and ascension, and the testimony of the apostles and of John at the baptism.
