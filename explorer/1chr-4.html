The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — 1 Chronicles 4 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 The sons of Judah; Pharez, Hezron, and Carmi, and Hur, and Shobal.

2 And Reaiah the son of Shobal begat Jahath; and Jahath begat Ahumai, and Lahad. These are the families of the Zorathites.

3 And these were of the father of Etam; Jezreel, and Ishma, and Idbash: and the name of their sister was Hazelelponi:

4 And Penuel the father of Gedor, and Ezer the father of Hushah. These are the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephratah, the father of Bethlehem.

5 And Ashur the father of Tekoa had two wives, Helah and Naarah.

6 And Naarah bare him Ahuzam, and Hepher, and Temeni, and Haahashtari. These were the sons of Naarah.

7 And the sons of Helah were, Zereth, and Jezoar, and Ethnan.

8 And Coz begat Anub, and Zobebah, and the families of Aharhel the son of Harum.

9 And Jabez was more honourable than his brethren: and his mother called his name Jabez, saying, Because I bare him with sorrow.

10 And Jabez called on the God of Israel, saying, Oh that thou wouldest bless me indeed, and enlarge my coast, and that thine hand might be with me, and that thou wouldest keep me from evil, that it may not grieve me! And God granted him that which he requested.

11 And Chelub the brother of Shuah begat Mehir, which was the father of Eshton.

12 And Eshton begat Bethrapha, and Paseah, and Tehinnah the father of Irnahash. These are the men of Rechah.

13 And the sons of Kenaz; Othniel, and Seraiah: and the sons of Othniel; Hathath.

14 And Meonothai begat Ophrah: and Seraiah begat Joab, the father of the valley of Charashim; for they were craftsmen.

15 And the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh; Iru, Elah, and Naam: and the sons of Elah, even Kenaz.

16 And the sons of Jehaleleel; Ziph, and Ziphah, Tiria, and Asareel.

17 And the sons of Ezra were, Jether, and Mered, and Epher, and Jalon: and she bare Miriam, and Shammai, and Ishbah the father of Eshtemoa.

18 And his wife Jehudijah bare Jered the father of Gedor, and Heber the father of Socho, and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. And these are the sons of Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh, which Mered took.

19 And the sons of his wife Hodiah the sister of Naham, the father of Keilah the Garmite, and Eshtemoa the Maachathite.

20 And the sons of Shimon were, Amnon, and Rinnah, Benhanan, and Tilon. And the sons of Ishi were, Zoheth, and Benzoheth.

21 The sons of Shelah the son of Judah were, Er the father of Lecah, and Laadah the father of Mareshah, and the families of the house of them that wrought fine linen, of the house of Ashbea,

22 And Jokim, and the men of Chozeba, and Joash, and Saraph, who had the dominion in Moab, and Jashubilehem. And these are ancient things.

23 These were the potters, and those that dwelt among plants and hedges: there they dwelt with the king for his work.

24 The sons of Simeon were, Nemuel, and Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul:

25 Shallum his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son.

26 And the sons of Mishma; Hamuel his son, Zacchur his son, Shimei his son.

27 And Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters: but his brethren had not many children, neither did all their family multiply, like to the children of Judah.

28 And they dwelt at Beersheba, and Moladah, and Hazarshual,

29 And at Bilhah, and at Ezem, and at Tolad,

30 And at Bethuel, and at Hormah, and at Ziklag,

31 And at Bethmarcaboth, and Hazarsusim, and at Bethbirei, and at Shaaraim. These were their cities unto the reign of David.

32 And their villages were, Etam, and Ain, Rimmon, and Tochen, and Ashan, five cities:

33 And all their villages that were round about the same cities, unto Baal. These were their habitations, and their genealogy.

34 And Meshobab, and Jamlech, and Joshah, the son of Amaziah,

35 And Joel, and Jehu the son of Josibiah, the son of Seraiah, the son of Asiel,

36 And Elioenai, and Jaakobah, and Jeshohaiah, and Asaiah, and Adiel, and Jesimiel, and Benaiah,

37 And Ziza the son of Shiphi, the son of Allon, the son of Jedaiah, the son of Shimri, the son of Shemaiah;

38 These mentioned by their names were princes in their families: and the house of their fathers increased greatly.

39 And they went to the entrance of Gedor, even unto the east side of the valley, to seek pasture for their flocks.

40 And they found fat pasture and good, and the land was wide, and quiet, and peaceable; for they of Ham had dwelt there of old.

41 And these written by name came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and smote their tents, and the habitations that were found there, and destroyed them utterly unto this day, and dwelt in their rooms: because there was pasture there for their flocks.

42 And some of them, even of the sons of Simeon, five hundred men, went to mount Seir, having for their captains Pelatiah, and Neariah, and Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi.

43 And they smote the rest of the Amalekites that were escaped, and dwelt there unto this day.

How to read the labels

Every substantive historical claim in the editorial panels below carries one of three labels, plus a source reference:

  • Documented — directly supported by a cited source.
  • Interpreted — a reasoned explanation or proposed relationship.
  • Unresolved — evidence is insufficient to establish the claim.

A claim does NOT become Documented merely because a commentary states it. Commentary views are attributed to their authors and labeled Interpreted unless a primary textual source directly supports the claim. Where evidence runs out, the page says Unresolved and stops — it does not fill the gap.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter records the families of Judah and Simeon, including Jabez and his prayer, and ends with Simeonites who drove out the remaining Amalekites and settled in their place. Interpreted Editorial summary of this chapter’s text and study notes

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. The sons of Judah here are descendants, not immediate sons, for with the exception of Pharez none were Judah's immediate sons. JFB notes the others are mentioned solely to introduce Shobal, whose genealogy the historian intended to trace. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 9. Jabez was more honourable than his brethren, and his mother named him for sorrow. JFB eulogises him for sincere and fervent piety and perhaps public and patriotic works, and notes Jewish writers made him an eminent doctor of the law whose scribes gave a town his name. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 10. Jabez called on the God of Israel to bless him, enlarge his coast, keep his hand with him, and keep him from evil so it might not grieve him, and God granted his request. JFB reads the prayer as a vow like Jacob's, made on entering a critical service, probably expelling Canaanites, and notes the plea plays on his name, grief: let me have no more sorrow. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 13. The sons of Kenaz were Othniel and Seraiah. JFB notes Kenaz was grandfather of Caleb, who from that relationship is called a Kenezite. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 14. Seraiah begat Joab, father of the valley of Charashim, for they were craftsmen. JFB renders it the valley of craftsmen, where persons of one trade dwelt together from father to son, probably carpenters near Jerusalem. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 17. She bare Miriam among the sons of Ezra, a verse JFB finds obscure as it stands and clears by rearrangement: Bithiah, Pharaoh's daughter, whom Mered took, bore Miriam, while his wife Jehudijah bore the others. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 18. His wife Jehudijah bore Jered, Heber, and Jekuthiel, and these are the sons of Bithiah, Pharaoh's daughter, whom Mered took. JFB calls Jehudijah the Jewess, to distinguish her from the Egyptian wife, and notes the Egyptian princess must have renounced idolatry and converted to Israel's God before such a marriage. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 21. The sons of Shelah include Laadah, father of Mareshah, and the families of fine-linen workers of Ashbea's house. JFB sees here hereditary trades among the Hebrews, with linen knowledge most probably acquired in Egypt. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 22. Jokim, the men of Chozeba, Joash, and Saraph had dominion in Moab, and Jashubi-lehem, and these are ancient things. JFB improves the rendering to sojourned in Moab but returned to Beth-lehem, identifying the potters of the next verse as king's workmen at Netaim and Gedera on Judah's border. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 23. These were the potters, and those that dwelt among plants and hedges, dwelling with the king for his work. JFB identifies them as potters employed by the king in his own work at Netaim and Gedera. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 24. The sons of Simeon are classed along with Judah, because their possession was partly taken out of Judah's extensive territory. JFB notes differences from other genealogies arise because some persons bore more than one name. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 27. Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brethren had not many children, nor did their family multiply like Judah's. JFB cross-refers the census figures showing Simeon's smaller increase. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 31. These were their cities unto the reign of David. JFB explains that through Simeonite sloth or cowardice some allotted cities were only nominally theirs, never taken from the Philistines until David, when he assigned them to Judah. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 38. These mentioned by name were princes in their families, and their fathers' house increased greatly. JFB links their increase to the migration that follows, as Simeon, holding only part of Judah's land, was forced to seek accommodation elsewhere. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 39. They went to the entrance of Gedor, to the east side of the valley, to seek pasture for their flocks. JFB describes their settlement in the fertile pastures of Gederah, soon broken up by nomad plunderers driving them from place to place toward Mount Seir. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 41. These written by name came in Hezekiah's days, smote the tents and habitations found there, destroyed them utterly, and dwelt in their rooms for pasture. JFB places this Simeonite expansion in Hezekiah's reign, after their earlier displacement. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 42. Some Simeonites, five hundred men under Pelatiah, Neariah, Rephaiah, and Uzziel, sons of Ishi, went to Mount Seir. JFB notes this as the final push of the displaced Simeonites seeking a settlement by force. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 43. They smote the rest of the Amalekites that escaped, and dwelt there unto this day. JFB closes the Simeonite migration with this conquest of Mount Seir, completing their search for pasture begun at Gedor. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
