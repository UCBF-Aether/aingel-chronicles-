Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter meditates on standing before God in fear and trembling, when no work done on earth shall be left or hidden, and no provision, garment or guide is known for the way, whether to light or darkness, sea or Sheol, led by angels whose faces are not seen and speech not heard. Angels of light are sent for the righteous, angels of darkness for sinners, to take them into everlasting judgment as their works deserve; and the chapter closes by naming the sinners' path as the way of Cain and the counsel of Balaam's violence. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
