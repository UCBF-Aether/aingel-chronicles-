Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter is a reckoning: from Adam to Nebuchadnezzar, through Daniel's visions, the Book of Judges, Saul, and the Book of Kings down to Alexander and the birth of Christ, citing the Book of Creation and the Book of Christ. It then sets Adam's eating from the tree and fall from the garden on Friday against Christ crucified on the tree on the same day, and his resurrection as the pattern of ours.
