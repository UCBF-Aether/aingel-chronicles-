Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter answers the Formed-One account of chapter 60 and calls it a lying discourse, measured against the book of Moses: Adam was created with bone and breath and blood and heart and speech, Eve was created from him on the same day, and the angels' worship and the fall are then argued from Isaiah's words about setting a throne on high and being like the Most High. Abba Theophilus of Alexandria is named as an authority in the argument. Interpreted Reading of this chapter's text as printed above; the study-notes file for this chapter is silent
