Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

God's glory and holiness, the houses in which the deed was done, and the children of Adam until the fall are the chapter's opening thread. It then gives the rule on leaven for every soul, and speaks of the Holy Spirit descending on the Virgin, four with them as he was four before.
