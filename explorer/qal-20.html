Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter turns from blessing the children of believers and the priest who does according to God's law to a warning against Satan's snare, told through Simon the sorcerer: after helping Philip and showing wonders, Simon sought the name of priesthood and the gift of the Holy Spirit in haste from Peter, and Peter drove him away. The Lord's verdict on Simon and Simon's end are lost in gaps. Words marked [?] or … in the text are uncertain or missing in the corpus, and this overview does not fill them. Parallels

Both Qalementos 20 and Acts 8 name a Simon associated with sorcery, with Philip, and with seeking the gift of the Holy Spirit from the apostles. Qalementos 20 says Simon had been a helper to Philip, had shown wonders, and sought from Peter the name of priesthood and the gift of the Holy Spirit in haste. Acts 8 says Simon used sorcery in Samaria, believed and continued with Philip, saw the Holy Spirit given through the laying on of the apostles' hands, and offered money, saying, Give me also this power, that on whomsoever I lay hands, he may receive the Holy Ghost. Documented Qalementos 20 (corpus qal.json); Acts 8:9-19 (corpus acts.json)

Differences

The accounts differ in what Simon seeks and how Peter answers. In Acts 8 Simon offers money and Peter rebukes him, saying thy money perish with thee, urges repentance, and Simon asks Peter to pray for him. In Qalementos 20 no money is mentioned, the fault is haste in seeking priesthood, Peter says the Lord told him something about Simon that is lost in a gap, and Peter drives Simon from him. Qalementos 4 and 6 invoke Simon again as a judged figure, but neither gives the fuller story found here. Documented Acts 8:18-24 (corpus acts.json); Qalementos 20, 4, 6 (corpus qal.json)
