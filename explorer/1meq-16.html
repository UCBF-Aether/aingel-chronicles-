Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter is largely a roll of peoples and places, Tyre and Sidon, the Jordan crossing, Gilead, Canaanites, Jebusites, Girgashites, Edom, Amalek, Arabia, Media, Cappadocia and beyond, among whom some know God and some know him not, all to be recompensed according to their works. Its verses are fragmentary and several readings uncertain; the through-line is that nations gather against the city of God, where the Holy One of Israel dwells above the cherubim, and God will repay each according to the work of their hands.
