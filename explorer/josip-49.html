The Text — Josippon 49 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 And concerning this, God commanded(?) that we eat(?) and drink and rejoice(?) on the Sabbath day in full; and He commanded(?) concerning the flesh — its name is the flesh of beasts, mingled — and He appointed(?) a day for us, for the day (?) [?]; and it is confirmed by this word of God: He appointed for the flesh(?), food and drink; (?) goeth forth(?) for the work of the Spirit on the Sabbath day; and He appointed upon it, in rest(?).

2 Behold, I have loved that which I have revealed unto thee; and take heed in thy heart, and teach them the name of the Faith, and unto others; for it is the foundation of the life which is eternal, and of the life of this world; and they rooted it in [the two] in earth and in heaven; and [there is] deliverance from judgment, and rest from the two works of toil, and riches from the hidden treasures in earth and in heaven.

3 For thou knowest that which is written in [the book of] the Kings: that God appeared unto Solomon, and said unto him: Ask of Me that which I shall give thee.

4 And Solomon said unto Him: I have asked of Thee that Thou wouldest give me a wise heart, that I may judge aright in the midst of this great people.

5 The Scripture saith: And God was pleased at this word; for he asked of Him a heavenly work, which abideth, and he asked not of Him an earthly work, which perisheth.

6 And God said unto him: Because thou hast asked neither length of days, nor abundance of riches, nor the soul of thine enemies, but thou hast asked wisdom — and it is the work which pleaseth Me — behold, I give thee the wisdom which thou hast asked of Me, which I have given unto none before thee, neither after thee.

7 And I will add unto thee, above this, that which thou hast not asked of Me: length of days, and abundance of riches, and the soul of thine enemies.

8 Behold, I have loved how Solomon, when he desired the work which abideth alone, and sought not the work which perisheth, God gave him the abiding [thing] which he had asked, and added unto him, above this, the perishing work which he had not asked; for He knew that which he desired.

9 And likewise He saith in the Gospel: Seek ye My kingdom and My righteousness, and I will add unto you that which ye desire of the work of this world, which is food and drink and clothing.

10 Likewise everyone who laboureth for the glory of God and His holiness, and heareth His word on the Sabbath day in full, shall obtain in this kingdom of heaven: God shall give that [portion], and shall grant him good and rest from all that wearieth, from the work of this world — food and drink and clothing — as He did unto Solomon. And he who laboureth not thus for the life of his soul, from the eternal kingdom, but inheriteth eternal judgment — God shall afflict him in this world, and shall weary him all the days of his life, and shall deprive him of the two rests together, in heaven and in earth; for God is mighty and able.

11 And he who laboureth for the day of the LORD, and shall be exalted with the LORD on the day of His resurrection in full — he also shall abide with the LORD on the day of the resurrection; he shall be exalted on the day of glory and honour, as it was done on earth on the Sabbath day. And he who laboureth not for the glory of God and His holiness, and heareth not His word on the Sabbath day, shall go forth from the fathers of God and from His glory, as the Devil fell when he was withheld from glory and holiness on the Sabbath day: lo, they are in it — they fell; and in it shall fall all who labour not for the glory of God and His holiness, as they fell.

12 And behold, I have revealed unto thee in the book of Kasit, the spiritual writing, that for those who are weak in work, their portion is appointed upon them on the Sabbath day; and it is appointed for glory and for holiness at the times, twice on the Sabbath day: the morning, [?] and two and a hundred and four, and the evening, within, upon the day which is read, all the day; for when they are steadfast [refraining] from the perishing work, it is fitting for them that they be joined unto prayer at the times, within, upon the day which is read.

13 And on the two days also, the others — it is not fitting for them save for eating; it is not fitting for them save for eating, in the book — as I have shown thee in the book of Helit of the host: they shall pray wheresoever they are, according to the measure of their strength and their knowledge, if they do not their work; and God hearkened unto them.

14 I have loved: behold, I have shown thee the glory of the Sabbath day; and in it is the life of the faithful, and their joy which is in earth and in heaven is upon them, and work is therein; and teach all whom thou hast found, [unto them](?), the faithful; and behold, I have revealed unto thee the reason of the fast: one day, Wednesday and Friday, in the peace of God. Amen.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter commands that God ordered eating, drinking, and rejoicing on the Sabbath day in full, and speaks of the flesh by its name. Solomon is the example: desiring the abiding work and not the perishing work, God gave him the abiding thing, and the chapter closes by showing the glory of the Sabbath as the life and joy of the faithful.
