How to read the labels

  • Documented — directly supported by a cited source.
  • Interpreted — a reasoned explanation or proposed relationship.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A short fragment continuing the psalm exposition: the refrain “for ever is mercy”, praise and order, hell, and a mention of Elijah. The chapter is too broken for a fuller summary; its surviving thread is praise of the LORD set against the fall of Satan and his hosts.
