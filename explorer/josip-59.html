Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter is a calendar of fasting and feasting: the offering at the tenth hour or in the evening, abstinence before taking the body of Christ, the First Day set against the day of assembly, the fast of the sufferings of the Saviour, fasting before the day of baptism but not on it, and the months of Yakatit and Nisan. It also notes differing customs among Romans, Franks, Ninevites and Armenians. The reckoning is fragmentary and several figures are marked (?).
