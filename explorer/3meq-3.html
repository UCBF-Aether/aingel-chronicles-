Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The Lord sends the prophet to Meqabis, prince of Moab, with a message that cuts both ways: it was not by his own hand that he conquered, and now he must repent with all his heart and be steadfast. His repentance is accepted; he forsakes idols, removes them from his house, sets wise teachers over his household, and teaches them the commandment of God. The chapter then widens into a cycle that will recur through the book: after God saves them, the people forget him and return to idols; oppressors are sent against them; they cry out; and he teaches and forgives them again.
