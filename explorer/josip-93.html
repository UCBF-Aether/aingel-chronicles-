Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter continues the council proceedings around Dioscorus: Leo's teaching that the two natures do their own deeds, thrones and a veil before the emperor, a fragment about a woman at a tomb, the emperor's order that a council gather at Chalcedon, and a book from the 207 fathers confessing God and man, in two orders and two works. The proceedings are heavily broken. Interpreted Reading of this chapter's text as printed above; the study-notes file for this chapter is silent
