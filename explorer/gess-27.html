The Text — Gәssәw 27 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 And let there be a meeting of the bishops twice in a year; and let them examine concerning the ordinance of the divine office, and let them explain [?] concerning the heresies [?] and the offences [?] which are in the church.

2 And the first meeting shall be in the middle of Pentecost; and the second meeting on the 12th of Tekemt.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The bishops are to meet twice a year to examine the ordinance of the divine office and to explain the heresies and offences in the church. The canon names the two meetings precisely: the first in the middle of Pentecost, the second on the 12th of Tekemt. Interpreted Paraphrase of the chapter's own text; Standard corpus, Gessew 27
