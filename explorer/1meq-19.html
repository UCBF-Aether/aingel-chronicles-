Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter traces corruption from Cain, whose children made harps, timbrels and psalteries, and from Cain's murder of Abel and taking of a wife, through the angels descending to the children of Cain, to Diyabolos deceiving Adam and Eve with, Ye shall be gods. Against this stands the duty the chapter presses throughout: fathers must tell their children God's praises, might and wonders, that the next generation may keep his commandment, for God hears the prayer of those who keep it and is merciful, and does not execute all his punishments. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
