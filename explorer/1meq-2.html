Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A Benjamite named Meqabyos is introduced, and the chapter turns to holy ones who refuse Sirusaydan's gods, answering that they bow only to the God of their fathers, Maker of heaven, earth, sea, sun, moon and stars, the God of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. Strong in battle, one throttling a bear, one slaying a lion with a single cast, one slaying a hundred with a single sword, they escape their captors to a mountain, while the city below pleads with them and they pray eastward, trusting God even unto death rather than heed the proud king's word.
