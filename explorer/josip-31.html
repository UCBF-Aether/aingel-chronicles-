The Text — Josippon 31 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 They were formed in the side of a virgin, without blemish. And when He said of the four of them — and let him know that He is pure — there followeth Him the seeking of God, a title. And He shall take [flesh] by the side of a virgin, without blemish, and He shall enter, even He; and by the side, before the first, they hold a title because of impurity; and by the side which holdeth not a tit[?]le, [?] from the holiness of the virgin.

2 And I am from the desire of Adam; and there followeth Him not death, nor shall He see corruption; and judgment was not upon Him — judgment unto the Devil — for He was not born of the desire of A[?]dam. They made [Him a servant], and He did no sin, as[?] they subjected Him; the servitude of them He [?] alone [bore], a virgin, withou[?]t seed, and without blemish, whom life eternal followeth, and whom judgment followeth not. And [?] the children of Adam — impurity is in them, a blemish — and there followeth them death, unto eternity, [?] them, when Christ died and they hanged Him upon the wood, the wood, and they [?] Him by His soul; for He is death, whom death followeth not ... [?] them, by whom He became the children of Israel, when He said, and He shall cause them to slaughter [the lamb] for their deliverance, which[?] beareth their death; and a virgi[?]n, perfect, without blemish. And by the shedding of His blood He saved us from the Devil, and from his hosts — which is the power of Pharaoh — He saved us, in the day of the first and the second of the month, which is the day of the joy of the Jews. From then, on the morrow after it, He was slaughtered by the side, wherein there is deliverance, these from Pharaoh and his hosts. Behold all that I have revealed unto thee from above, from God; and concerning the lamb, that he was with Him, concerning Christ, by the side, unto God.

3 For He said concerning the new month: It shall be the head of the months unto you; for in it the children of Adam were renewed, from the destruction wherein they were, unto God — He who without blemish, a virgin, perfect, died because of the sins of men. When they laid upon Him that which is not fitting, He took Adam and all his seed, the desire of desire, without years; He is, by His side, He who aforetime was in the Garden of delight of Eden; and He entered unto him a second time.

4 And this new month is the head of the months of their salvation, and the beginning of their newness of life for ever.

5 And as for that which He said: Keep ye the lambs from the first day of it, and keep them, until the first and the fourth of it, and slaughter it at the setting of the sun.

6 He said in the Gospel: The ... said: For the year in which Christ was crucified became the head of the month Nisan, of the year of the Jews, wherein was joy, from the first day, Friday. And on that day — it is the fourth of it — lo, our Lord Jesus Christ [was] in the city of Jerusalem, when He was lifted up, as the type of servitude[?]; and all the children of Israel received Him, and from among them [they knew] that He is Christ, their king, who holdeth them for their salvation; and they all cried before Him in their tongues, when they said: Hosanna to the Son of David — which, in its interpretation, is: Save us, O Son of David; blessed is the king who cometh in the name of God; Hosanna in the highest. On that day, at the hour of the slaughter of the lamb, at the setting of the sun, when He resteth in the ... of the night of Friday, at the time of the first and the second of the month — the time wherein God commanded them that they should slaughter the lamb therein.

7 And again He took bread into His hands, when He slaughtered the lamb; and He said unto them: This is My body, which is broken for you, and for many, for the remission of their sins; this do in remembrance of Me.

8 And when they had eaten, He took the cup, and said: This is My blood of the new covenant, which is shed for many, for the remission of their sins.

9 And this do in remembrance of Me. I have shown you by this completed word of God, which He commanded you, that ye should keep it on this day. For ... they received Him, and from among them, and from among [them they knew] that He is Christ, who ... from the seed of David; and they asked Him that He would save them, and make them to rejoice in ... and in .... And He abode with them [?] one day, which is the first and the fourth of it, with His disciples, in the evening of that day, this word: that by the will of Go[?]d He was ... unto them, that they should slaughter Him in the night of that day, that they might be exalted in Him. For He is the lamb which was appointed, and became a covenant appointed for the salvation of the children of Israel from Pharaoh and his hos[?]ts; and by this lamb, a new covenant, and the salvation of all the children of Adam, [?] ... and ...; and all those who are [?] born in this world, unto eternity, [saved] from the Devil and his hosts. Behold, beloved, what God [did], when He said: Take(?) it, ... (?) — He said: The lintels of your doorposts, which are above and which are below — therein, if I fill your houses for the believers — and its name is ... [?] as the house; and the doorposts ... and the lintels, which are above and which are below, are for men. For when this second [coming] is unto Christ: I, when I see the blood upon the lintels of your houses, know a sign, and I will hide you, and the destroying angel shall not draw nigh unto you.

10 For the blood of Christ, which the believers drink, is the sign of Christ, which by day keepeth and by night hideth, from the demons, the destroying angels; and if it reacheth unto the house of a Christian ... at the time of the liturgy and the offering, the blood of Christ shall be sprinkled, and shall be sealed for his soul. And when [the soul] goeth forth from the flesh, that it may ascend unto the Garden, they shall see His body — the lamb's body — with water; those who eat His body — the lamb's — with water; those who eat that which is roasted with fire: so also, in truth, Christ hath fed us, with the lamb of God, His body, bread roasted with fire; for He is our Word, with Him. Concerning this He said: Eat ye; I am ...; your loins [be] girded. So the believers eat His body, and ... Christ; I am ..., my loins girded, His body ...; and I am ..., my loins girded, His heart ..., believing in it; for this is His body, which was ... therein. He said: Eat ye it, and I will place it in your hands.

11 Likewise the [end] for the believers is His cross.

12 By it Christ smote [them].

13 He said: Eat ye, and put ye [them] also into your shoes. For the believers, when they walk in the way of Christ — and the persecutors in His way — wherein are set for them snares, and thorns, and stumbling-blocks, that they may ensnare them.

14 God said: Loose ye your shoes from off your feet.

15 Mark ye all your flesh [against] all their snares, for they are serpents, biters.

16 And God said unto the serpent: I will put enmity between thee, and between the children of Eve, and between thy children, and the children of [?]

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter speaks of those formed in the side of a virgin without blemish, and gives the command 'This do in remembrance of Me' as a completed word of God to be kept on its day. It closes by quoting God's word to the serpent: enmity between Eve's children and the serpent's children. Interpreted Chapter text as printed above; study-notes file for this chapter is silent
