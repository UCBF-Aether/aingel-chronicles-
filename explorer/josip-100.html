Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter expounds “my Father and their Father, and my God and their God”, with the foot-washing as Christ's example of humility to his disciples, and again names believers as Jacobites. It then cites Gregory on the kingdom and all things beneath Christ's feet, and rejects dividing the Gospel word or separating the work of God from the work of man, and two natures after the union.
