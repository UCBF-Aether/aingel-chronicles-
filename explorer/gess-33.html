The Text — Gәssәw 33 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 A bishop or an elder or a deacon who attends the baptism of the heretics or their eucharist [?] — we command that he be deposed.

2 For what communion hath Christ with Satan? Or what part have believers with those who do not believe?

Overview

A bishop, elder, or deacon who attends the baptism of the heretics or their eucharist is to be deposed. The canon states its ground as a rhetorical question: what communion has Christ with Satan, or what part have believers with those who do not believe?
