Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Only two verses survive in this chapter division, both heavily damaged. Canon 26 concerns the death of a ma'səb: where a clause is lost to encoding damage, the text provides that something be done in the name, that a dead ma'səb be remembered many days, and that her successor be appointed by word alone and joined with the remaining ma'səbs. What the office itself entailed is not explained anywhere in the surviving text of this chapter. Interpreted Summary of this chapter's text, as printed above
