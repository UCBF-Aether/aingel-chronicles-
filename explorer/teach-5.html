The Text — Teach 5 (Printed Ge'ez of Lifchitz & Grébaut (1940), pp. 44–52, for chs 2–4; project Ge'ez corpus for chs 1 and 5)

1 The dead arose; Sheol he trampled; by his death, death he destroyed. After he rose on the third day, he glorified the Father, saying: O God, the Word, undefiled[?], like as thou who in the Spirit didst call Adam, and madest him live. As saith Moses, and likewise the prophets — we also, in him have we trusted; unto him we give glory; unto whom be glory and power, for ever and ever. Amen.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A single-verse closing doxology: the dead arose, Sheol was trampled, and by his death death was destroyed. After rising on the third day, he glorified the Father, as the God and Word, undefiled — the word is marked uncertain — who in the Spirit called Adam and made him live. As Moses and likewise the prophets spoke, so, the chapter says, we also have trusted in him, to whom be glory and power for ever and ever. Amen.
