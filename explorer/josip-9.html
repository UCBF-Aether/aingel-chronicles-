Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A second discourse opens here, concerning the becoming-man of the Son of the Lord and his crucifixion. Those baptized, it says, have the Spirit of the Lord in them, and without faith they do not fully understand the interpretation offered, before the chapter turns to the chief of the host and the orders of angels as spirits.
