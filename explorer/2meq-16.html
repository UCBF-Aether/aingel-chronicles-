Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter argues resurrection from the reader's own body and seed: the labours of hands, feet and head are God's making, and as planted seed keeps its kind, vine stays vine, fig stays fig, so the dead shall rise, soul and flesh together, at the trumpet's blowing in the dew of God's mercy. The doers of good rise to the resurrection of life, to a garden of rejoicing with no sickness or toil, while evildoers rise to the resurrection of judgment with the devil who deceived them, cast into darkness with weeping and no escape for ever.
