The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Exodus 40 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

2 On the first day of the first month shalt thou set up the tabernacle of the tent of the congregation.

3 And thou shalt put therein the ark of the testimony, and cover the ark with the vail.

4 And thou shalt bring in the table, and set in order the things that are to be set in order upon it; and thou shalt bring in the candlestick, and light the lamps thereof.

5 And thou shalt set the altar of gold for the incense before the ark of the testimony, and put the hanging of the door to the tabernacle.

6 And thou shalt set the altar of the burnt offering before the door of the tabernacle of the tent of the congregation.

7 And thou shalt set the laver between the tent of the congregation and the altar, and shalt put water therein.

8 And thou shalt set up the court round about, and hang up the hanging at the court gate.

9 And thou shalt take the anointing oil, and anoint the tabernacle, and all that is therein, and shalt hallow it, and all the vessels thereof: and it shall be holy.

10 And thou shalt anoint the altar of the burnt offering, and all his vessels, and sanctify the altar: and it shall be an altar most holy.

11 And thou shalt anoint the laver and his foot, and sanctify it.

12 And thou shalt bring Aaron and his sons unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and wash them with water.

13 And thou shalt put upon Aaron the holy garments, and anoint him, and sanctify him; that he may minister unto me in the priest’s office.

14 And thou shalt bring his sons, and clothe them with coats:

15 And thou shalt anoint them, as thou didst anoint their father, that they may minister unto me in the priest’s office: for their anointing shall surely be an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations.

16 Thus did Moses: according to all that the LORD commanded him, so did he.

17 And it came to pass in the first month in the second year, on the first day of the month, that the tabernacle was reared up.

18 And Moses reared up the tabernacle, and fastened his sockets, and set up the boards thereof, and put in the bars thereof, and reared up his pillars.

19 And he spread abroad the tent over the tabernacle, and put the covering of the tent above upon it; as the LORD commanded Moses.

20 And he took and put the testimony into the ark, and set the staves on the ark, and put the mercy seat above upon the ark:

21 And he brought the ark into the tabernacle, and set up the vail of the covering, and covered the ark of the testimony; as the LORD commanded Moses.

22 And he put the table in the tent of the congregation, upon the side of the tabernacle northward, without the vail.

23 And he set the bread in order upon it before the LORD; as the LORD had commanded Moses.

24 And he put the candlestick in the tent of the congregation, over against the table, on the side of the tabernacle southward.

25 And he lighted the lamps before the LORD; as the LORD commanded Moses.

26 And he put the golden altar in the tent of the congregation before the vail:

27 And he burnt sweet incense thereon; as the LORD commanded Moses.

28 And he set up the hanging at the door of the tabernacle.

29 And he put the altar of burnt offering by the door of the tabernacle of the tent of the congregation, and offered upon it the burnt offering and the meat offering; as the LORD commanded Moses.

30 And he set the laver between the tent of the congregation and the altar, and put water there, to wash withal.

31 And Moses and Aaron and his sons washed their hands and their feet thereat:

32 When they went into the tent of the congregation, and when they came near unto the altar, they washed; as the LORD commanded Moses.

33 And he reared up the court round about the tabernacle and the altar, and set up the hanging of the court gate. So Moses finished the work.

34 Then a cloud covered the tent of the congregation, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

35 And Moses was not able to enter into the tent of the congregation, because the cloud abode thereon, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.

36 And when the cloud was taken up from over the tabernacle, the children of Israel went onward in all their journeys:

37 But if the cloud were not taken up, then they journeyed not till the day that it was taken up.

38 For the cloud of the LORD was upon the tabernacle by day, and fire was on it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, throughout all their journeys.

How to read the labels

Every substantive historical claim in the editorial panels below carries one of three labels, plus a source reference:

  • Documented — directly supported by a cited source.
  • Interpreted — a reasoned explanation or proposed relationship.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

On the first day of the first month Moses sets up the tabernacle, arranges its furniture, anoints it, and puts the testimony in the ark behind the veil. Then the cloud covers the tent and the glory of the LORD fills the tabernacle, so that Moses cannot enter, and the cloud thereafter guides Israel: when it lifts they journey, when it rests they camp. Interpreted Plain-language summary of the chapter text, checked against the study notes for this chapter

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. And the LORD spake unto Moses, giving the order for setting up the tabernacle. Henry notes when a new year begins, we should seek to serve God better than the year before, and in half a year the tabernacle was completed, for when hearts are earnest in a good cause, much may be done in a short time. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 2. On the first day of the first month shalt thou set up the tabernacle of the tent of the congregation. Henry observes the day was the new year's day, the first day of the first month, that the house might begin with the year. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 3. And thou shalt put therein the ark of the testimony, and cover the ark with the vail. Henry notes the ark went in first, covered with the vail, for the throne must be placed before the furniture. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 4. And thou shalt bring in the table, and set in order the things that are to be set in order upon it, and thou shalt bring in the candlestick, and light the lamps thereof. Henry observes the table and candlestick followed: the bread set in order and the lamps lighted, that the holy place might be furnished and shining. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 5. And thou shalt set the altar of gold for the incense before the ark of the testimony, and put the hanging of the door to the tabernacle. Henry notes the golden altar stood before the ark, and the door hanging closed the holy place, for prayer stood nearest the throne. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 6. And thou shalt set the altar of the burnt offering before the door of the tabernacle of the tent of the congregation. Henry observes the brazen altar stood before the door, for sacrifice came before entrance: none entered the house except by the altar. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 7. And thou shalt set the laver between the tent of the congregation and the altar, and shalt put water therein. Henry notes the laver stood between altar and tent, for washing stood between sacrifice and service. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 8. And thou shalt set up the court round about, and hang up the hanging at the court gate. Henry observes the court was set up round about, with its gate hanging, enclosing the whole house. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 9. And thou shalt take the anointing oil, and anoint the tabernacle, and all that is therein, and shalt hallow it, and all the vessels thereof, and it shall be holy. Henry notes the anointing hallowed house and vessels together, for the oil sanctified all it touched. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 10. And thou shalt anoint the altar of the burnt offering, and all his vessels, and sanctify the altar, and it shall be an altar most holy. Henry observes the altar of sacrifice was anointed to be most holy, for the place of atonement was itself made holy. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 11. And thou shalt anoint the laver and his foot, and sanctify it. Henry notes the laver and its foot were anointed, for even the washing vessel was sanctified. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 12. And thou shalt bring Aaron and his sons unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and wash them with water. Henry observes the priests were washed at the door, for cleansing preceded clothing and anointing. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 13. And thou shalt put upon Aaron the holy garments, and anoint him, and sanctify him, that he may minister unto me in the priest's office. Henry notes the high-priesthood was in Aaron's family till Christ came, and in him, the substance of all these shadows, it continues forever. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 14. And thou shalt bring his sons, and clothe them with coats. Henry observes the sons were clothed in coats, simpler than Aaron's, yet still holy garments. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 15. And thou shalt anoint them, as thou didst anoint their father, that they may minister unto me in the priest's office, for their anointing shall surely be an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations. Henry notes the sons' anointing matched their father's, and the priesthood was everlasting throughout their generations, until the true High Priest came. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 16. Thus did Moses, according to all that the LORD commanded him, so did he. Henry observes the refrain again: when the commandments of God are continually attended to as the rule of working, all will be done well. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 17. And it came to pass in the first month in the second year, on the first day of the month, that the tabernacle was reared up. Henry notes the rearing fell on the new year's day of the second year, a year after the Exodus began, and the house was raised in the wilderness, not deferred till Canaan. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 18. And Moses reared up the tabernacle, and fastened his sockets, and set up the boards thereof, and put in the bars thereof, and reared up his pillars. Henry observes those who are unsettled in the world must not think this excuses want of religion, as if it were enough to begin to serve God when they begin to be settled, for a tabernacle for God is very needful even in a wilderness. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 19. And he spread abroad the tent over the tabernacle, and put the covering of the tent above upon it, as the LORD commanded Moses. Henry notes the tent and its coverings were spread over the boards, as commanded, that the house might be roofed before it was furnished. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 20. And he took and put the testimony into the ark, and set the staves on the ark, and put the mercy seat above upon the ark. Henry observes the testimony went into the ark, the staves were set, and the mercy seat placed above, for law within and mercy above was the order of the throne. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 21. And he brought the ark into the tabernacle, and set up the vail of the covering, and covered the ark of the testimony, as the LORD commanded Moses. Henry notes the ark was brought in and covered with the vail, for the most holy place was veiled from the holy place. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 22. And he put the table in the tent of the congregation, upon the side of the tabernacle northward, without the vail. Henry observes the table stood on the north side, without the vail, facing the candlestick across the holy place. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 23. And he set the bread in order upon it before the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses. Henry notes the bread was set in order before the LORD, always before him, the perpetual provision of the house. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 24. And he put the candlestick in the tent of the congregation, over against the table, on the side of the tabernacle southward. Henry observes the candlestick stood over against the table on the south, light facing bread across the holy place. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 25. And he lighted the lamps before the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses. Henry notes the lamps were lighted before the LORD, for the house must shine as well as be furnished. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 26. And he put the golden altar in the tent of the congregation before the vail. Henry observes the golden altar stood before the vail, nearest the throne, the place of prayer. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 27. And he burnt sweet incense thereon, as the LORD commanded Moses. Henry notes the sweet incense was burned at once, for the house was no sooner furnished than it was used. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 28. And he set up the hanging at the door of the tabernacle. Henry observes the door hanging closed the holy place, the last veil before the house was complete. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 29. And he put the altar of burnt offering by the door of the tabernacle of the tent of the congregation, and offered upon it the burnt offering and the meat offering, as the LORD commanded Moses. Henry notes the altar stood by the door, and the first offerings were made there, for the house opened with sacrifice. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 30. And he set the laver between the tent of the congregation and the altar, and put water there, to wash withal. Henry notes the laver was set between tent and altar with water in it, ready for the priests' washing. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 31. And Moses and Aaron and his sons washed their hands and their feet thereat. Henry observes Moses himself washed with Aaron and his sons, for the mediator also needed cleansing before he ministered. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 32. When they went into the tent of the congregation, and when they came near unto the altar, they washed, as the LORD commanded Moses. Henry notes the washing was repeated at every entrance and every approach to the altar, for purity must be renewed at every service. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 33. And he reared up the court round about the tabernacle and the altar, and set up the hanging of the court gate. So Moses finished the work. Henry notes the court closed the whole, and so Moses finished the work, for the house, its furniture, its priests, and its court were all complete. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 34. Then a cloud covered the tent of the congregation, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle. Henry notes the cloud covered the tabernacle even in the clearest day, for it was not a cloud which the sun scatters, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle, for in light and fire the Shechinah made itself visible. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 35. And Moses was not able to enter into the tent of the congregation, because the cloud abode thereon, and the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle. Henry observes so dazzling was the light, and so dreadful the fire, that Moses was not able to enter, but what Moses could not do, our Lord Jesus has done, whom God caused to draw near, and who has invited us to come boldly even to the mercy-seat. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 36. And when the cloud was taken up from over the tabernacle, the children of Israel went onward in all their journeys. Henry notes the cloud guided the camp through the wilderness: while it rested, they rested, and when it removed, they followed it. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 37. But if the cloud were not taken up, then they journeyed not till the day that it was taken up. Henry observes the rule was absolute: no cloud, no march, for the people moved only as God moved. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 38. For the cloud of the LORD was upon the tabernacle by day, and fire was on it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, throughout all their journeys. Henry closes the book on the token seen day and night by all Israel, that they might never again question, Is the LORD among us, or is he not? And being taught by the Holy Spirit to follow Christ's example and depend upon him, we shall be kept from losing our way, and be led in the midst of the paths of judgment, till we come to heaven, the habitation of his holiness. Blessed be God for Jesus Christ! Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
