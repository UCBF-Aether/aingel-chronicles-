Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter mixes deacon discipline, the appointment of bishops and the appointment of readers. Matthew's opening rule grounds deacons' appointment in the witness of two or three, with warnings against murmuring, double speech and oath-swearing, and a reminder that the day of the Lord is near; Peter then says the books shall teach the remaining commandments. The bishop rule covers a small town's request to neighbouring churches, the candidate's character — good, without sin or oath, a lover of the poor, able to interpret the books, humble — and the priests standing twelve on the right and twelve on the left, before James's reader rule, repeated from chapter 3, closes the chapter.
