Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

After a generation that feared God and gave to the poor, Sirusaydan, king of the Chaldeans, destroys the cities of the children of Meqabis, leads them captive, seizes their property and brings them into a land of idols, dead flesh, blood and lawlessness, where they take fathers' wives and sisters and do all evil. Yet the children of Meqabis are kept apart as devoted: they eat not dead flesh, and their impurity and sins are laid upon the Chaldeans, while the chapter closes by denouncing Baal Peor, a graven image of brass, gold and silver that eats not, drinks not, kills not, gives not life, thinks not and shows no mercy.
