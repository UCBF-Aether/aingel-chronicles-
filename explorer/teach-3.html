Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Verses 16 to 22 continue the teaching: no desire of man can remove the soul far from God, and love does not turn away from God; those baptised are perfect believers who have saved their souls from what appears but does not exist in reality. The inward man is told to hear how Christ, having suffered, went down into Sheol and took the dead captive unto life — though the middle of that verse is textually corrupt and the edition declines to translate it — and the chapter closes with the will of Jesus Christ and all the mysteries of salvation for the saints in glorification.
