The Text — Josippon 29 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 These giants are without number, and the believers are as locusts.

2 And the place of those who sought them: the believer is he who is exalted above all the heavens. Those who were asked of us concerning the believers — as Christ, who was for their sakes upon the wood, and was lifted up, and saved them, all of them, and bound [him] in Sheol, from their hands: He is the Victor, He is the Arab. And the demons are giants, abundant, and all men are before them as locusts; but those who believed in Christ glorify and hallow continually. He is who giveth us victo[?]ry over them; and if we love the wo[?]rd, we cannot fight them, and we canno[?]t prevail against their kingdom; it perishe[?]d. And they declare His scrip[?]tures, that He is who giveth them rest.

3 And the Holy Spirit; and they dwell with Him in the kingdom of the heavens. And the priest who teacheth not the people, his brethren, concerning Christ and His liturgy — know thou that he shall perish from before the[?]m, as one spy perished from before the people, and he shall inherit his eternal judgment with the Devil and his hosts, and shall be far removed from that eternal kingdom.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Giants without number are set against believers as locusts, the chapter's stark image of the Church's smallness against its foes. It also warns the priest who does not teach the people concerning Christ, and closes by commending the book itself as a great and glorious word by which believers taste the faith.
