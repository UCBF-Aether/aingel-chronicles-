The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — 1 Samuel 10 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 Then Samuel took a vial of oil, and poured it upon his head, and kissed him, and said, Is it not because the LORD hath anointed thee to be captain over his inheritance?

2 When thou art departed from me to day, then thou shalt find two men by Rachel’s sepulchre in the border of Benjamin at Zelzah; and they will say unto thee, The asses which thou wentest to seek are found: and, lo, thy father hath left the care of the asses, and sorroweth for you, saying, What shall I do for my son?

3 Then shalt thou go on forward from thence, and thou shalt come to the plain of Tabor, and there shall meet thee three men going up to God to Bethel, one carrying three kids, and another carrying three loaves of bread, and another carrying a bottle of wine:

4 And they will salute thee, and give thee two loaves of bread; which thou shalt receive of their hands.

5 After that thou shalt come to the hill of God, where is the garrison of the Philistines: and it shall come to pass, when thou art come thither to the city, that thou shalt meet a company of prophets coming down from the high place with a psaltery, and a tabret, and a pipe, and a harp, before them; and they shall prophesy:

6 And the Spirit of the LORD will come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, and shalt be turned into another man.

7 And let it be, when these signs are come unto thee, that thou do as occasion serve thee; for God is with thee.

8 And thou shalt go down before me to Gilgal; and, behold, I will come down unto thee, to offer burnt offerings, and to sacrifice sacrifices of peace offerings: seven days shalt thou tarry, till I come to thee, and shew thee what thou shalt do.

9 And it was so, that when he had turned his back to go from Samuel, God gave him another heart: and all those signs came to pass that day.

10 And when they came thither to the hill, behold, a company of prophets met him; and the Spirit of God came upon him, and he prophesied among them.

11 And it came to pass, when all that knew him beforetime saw that, behold, he prophesied among the prophets, then the people said one to another, What is this that is come unto the son of Kish? Is Saul also among the prophets?

12 And one of the same place answered and said, But who is their father? Therefore it became a proverb, Is Saul also among the prophets?

13 And when he had made an end of prophesying, he came to the high place.

14 And Saul’s uncle said unto him and to his servant, Whither went ye? And he said, To seek the asses: and when we saw that they were no where, we came to Samuel.

15 And Saul’s uncle said, Tell me, I pray thee, what Samuel said unto you.

16 And Saul said unto his uncle, He told us plainly that the asses were found. But of the matter of the kingdom, whereof Samuel spake, he told him not.

17 And Samuel called the people together unto the LORD to Mizpeh;

18 And said unto the children of Israel, Thus saith the LORD God of Israel, I brought up Israel out of Egypt, and delivered you out of the hand of the Egyptians, and out of the hand of all kingdoms, and of them that oppressed you:

19 And ye have this day rejected your God, who himself saved you out of all your adversities and your tribulations; and ye have said unto him, Nay, but set a king over us. Now therefore present yourselves before the LORD by your tribes, and by your thousands.

20 And when Samuel had caused all the tribes of Israel to come near, the tribe of Benjamin was taken.

21 When he had caused the tribe of Benjamin to come near by their families, the family of Matri was taken, and Saul the son of Kish was taken: and when they sought him, he could not be found.

22 Therefore they enquired of the LORD further, if the man should yet come thither. And the LORD answered, Behold he hath hid himself among the stuff.

23 And they ran and fetched him thence: and when he stood among the people, he was higher than any of the people from his shoulders and upward.

24 And Samuel said to all the people, See ye him whom the LORD hath chosen, that there is none like him among all the people? And all the people shouted, and said, God save the king.

25 Then Samuel told the people the manner of the kingdom, and wrote it in a book, and laid it up before the LORD. And Samuel sent all the people away, every man to his house.

26 And Saul also went home to Gibeah; and there went with him a band of men, whose hearts God had touched.

27 But the children of Belial said, How shall this man save us? And they despised him, and brought no presents. But he held his peace.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Samuel pours a vial of oil on Saul's head, kisses him, and says the LORD has anointed him captain over his inheritance, then gives three signs that will meet him that day: two men by Rachel's sepulchre saying the asses are found, three men at Tabor giving him two loaves, and a company of prophets at the hill of God, where the Spirit will come on him and he will be turned into another man. All the signs come to pass, Saul prophesies among the prophets until men ask, Is Saul also among the prophets, and he tells his uncle only that the asses are found, saying nothing of the kingdom. At Mizpeh the public lot narrows tribe by tribe to Benjamin, to Matri, to Saul son of Kish, who is found hid among the stuff; Samuel presents him, the people shout, God save the king, the manner of the kingdom is written in a book and laid up before the LORD, and Saul goes home to Gibeah with a band of men whose hearts God has touched, holding his peace before the despisers. Interpreted Overview, grounded in the chapter text and its study notes

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. Samuel took a vial of oil, poured it on Saul's head, kissed him, and said, Is it not because the LORD hath anointed thee to be captain over his inheritance? The sacred anointing, Henry notes, pointed at the great Messiah, the Anointed One, King of the church, anointed with the oil of the Spirit without measure. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)
  • Verse 2. When thou art departed today, Samuel said, thou shalt find two men by Rachel's sepulchre at Zelzah, who will say the asses are found and thy father sorrows for thee. The first sign directed Saul to an ancestor's grave, Henry observes, to remind him of his mortality — with a crown before him, he must think of the grave where all his honour would be laid in the dust. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)
  • Verse 3. Then at the plain of Tabor three men going up to God to Bethel would meet him, one carrying three kids, another three loaves, another a bottle of wine. The signs came punctually, confirming Samuel's word step by step. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)
  • Verse 4. They will salute thee and give thee two loaves of bread, which thou shalt receive. The worshippers' gift would mark Saul as one blessed on his way. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)
  • Verse 5. After that thou shalt come to the hill of God, where the Philistine garrison is, and meet a company of prophets coming down with psaltery, tabret, pipe, and harp, prophesying. From Samuel's time, Henry notes, there appear to have been schools where pious young men were brought up in Divine things. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)
  • Verse 6. The Spirit of the LORD will come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, and shalt be turned into another man. The Spirit of God changes men, Henry says, wonderfully transforming them; Saul, praising God in the communion of saints, became another man — though it may be questioned if he became a new man. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)
  • Verse 7. When these signs are come unto thee, do as occasion serves thee, for God is with thee. The signs were both confirmation and commission. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)
  • Verse 8. Thou shalt go down before me to Gilgal, and I will come down to offer burnt offerings and peace offerings; seven days shalt thou tarry until I come and show thee what to do. The Gilgal appointment would later test Saul's obedience. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)
  • Verse 9. When Saul turned his back to go from Samuel, God gave him another heart, and all those signs came to pass that day. He found God had given him another disposition of mind, Henry notes, yet an outward show of devotion and a sudden change should not be too much relied on. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)

  • Verse 12. One of the same place answered, But who is their father? Therefore it became a proverb, Is Saul also among the prophets? Prophecy was God's gift, not a family inheritance. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)

  • Verse 23. They ran and fetched him, and when he stood among the people he was higher than any from his shoulders upward. The hidden man, once produced, looked every inch the king. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)

Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)

  • Verse 26. Saul also went home to Gibeah, and there went with him a band of men whose hearts God had touched. If the heart bends the right way at any time, Henry says, it is because God has touched it; one touch is enough when it is Divine. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)
  • Verse 27. But the children of Belial said, How shall this man save us? And they despised him and brought no presents, but he held his peace. Thus differently are men affected toward our exalted Redeemer, Henry reflects: a remnant submit to him, while others despise him and ask how this man shall save us, and they will be punished. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Concise)
