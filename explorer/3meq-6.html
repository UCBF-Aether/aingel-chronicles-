Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The proud Ṣiruṣaydan appoints lying priests and ministers of idols, and when he finds the children of Meqabis righteous, worshipping the Lord their Creator and keeping their father’s commandment, he has them brought before him and commands them to eat and sacrifice to his gods. They refuse — we will not obey you in this matter, and will not sacrifice to your defiled gods — and though he threatens them with tortures and kindles a fire and casts them in, they deliver up their souls to the Lord. By night their life after death is shown to the sleeping king, and the dead speak to him: the Lord your Creator is One, there is no other god besides him, and it was Satan, not the king alone, who taught the evil deed.
