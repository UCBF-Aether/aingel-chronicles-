Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter returns to Egypt: Pharaoh oppresses Israel with hard labour, God sends Moses and Aaron to bring his people from bondage, and drowns Pharaoh with his host in the Red Sea, the pattern for the chapter's promise to kings and princes who do God's will. Heed me, and I will heed you; keep my commandment, and I will keep your soul, God says; he will deliver those who do his will as he delivered David from Goliath, Saul and Absalom, and set them with the blessed kings, as Samuel, chosen from his youth, anointed kings by God's commandment, closing with God sending Samuel to anoint David son of Jesse. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
