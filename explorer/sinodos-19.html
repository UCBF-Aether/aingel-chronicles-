Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 49 concerns the firstfruits to be brought: the people give the first of the produce with rejoicing for the bishop, who blesses and proclaims, and the prayer praises God for making every creature fruitful through his Son. The priest then blesses the fruits themselves — clusters, wine, figs, pomegranates — and the chapter adds rules for eating at the Pascha season, for fasting on vigils and Sabbaths with bread and water, and for the release of the fast after Pentecost, though not for the Pascha that follows. Several verses are too corrupt to summarise beyond their headings, including one likely concerning husband and wife. Interpreted Summary of this chapter's text, as printed above
