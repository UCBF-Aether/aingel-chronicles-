Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 50 opens with kings not appointing rulers from under them over deacons, priests and people, since bishops and priests come from the people and their standing is from God, not from men. The chapter then gives the testing of travelling apostles and prophets — receive those who come in the Lord's name, test them, let them not remain beyond a day or two, give firstfruits to the prophets or, lacking a prophet, to the poor, and fast not as the hypocrites but on the fourth day and the preparation day — naming Silas and Agabus among the prophets and Miriam, Deborah, Esther, Judith, Elizabeth, Hannah and Philip's daughters among women who prophesied of old, none exalted over others. It closes with the seating order of the assembly — elders seated, virgins honoured, children kept, visitors examined by the deacon and seated in their places, places yielded in love — and a charge to the bishops to keep the canon as the Lord commanded.
