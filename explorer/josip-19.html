Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The word of the incarnation, the chapter says, is that weakness hid the mystery of the divine word from the Devil. Its passion thread follows the stripping of garments and a scarlet robe put on in place of the garments with which Adam and Eve had been clothed, and the pouring into the chalice, with the Holy Spirit present with his body.
