Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The king tries to burn the martyrs' bodies to dust and scatter them to the wind, then to sink them in the sea, but fire does not consume them and the sea will not swallow them; angels guard their shining bodies, and by night the martyrs appear to the terrified king. They tell him they are alive because God guarded them, refuse his offered ransom, and warn him to forsake gods of gold, silver, wood and stone and worship God; the king, confessing that the dead shall rise, forsakes his error, while the chapter closes by urging every king to fear God, since there is no other God in heaven above or on earth beneath. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
