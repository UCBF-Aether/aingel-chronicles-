Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This short chapter is a single woe against the idol-worshipper: you have not known the Lord who created you, nor his commandment and his word, and you shall be cut off with no escape, you and your gods. The idols, it says, are the work of man’s hand, in whom Satan speaks to destroy the heart of fools — and better than their worshippers are the beasts God created for food, which have no judgment beyond one death.
