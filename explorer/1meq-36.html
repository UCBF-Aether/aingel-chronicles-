Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The book's final chapter warns Macedonians, Amalekites and Ishmaelites against pride, they exalt themselves to heaven and go down into Sheol, then holds up the faithful as the pattern: Job kept from all evil, Abraham delivered from kings and from idolatry, and Abraham, Isaac and Jacob teaching their children to keep God's commandment. Israel, fed with manna whose verse calls it the bread of a word the reading leaves uncertain, turned instead to Baal, Dagon, Apollo and Artemis and the gods of the nations; yet the chapter closes with resurrection hope, the righteous shall shine as stars in the kingdom of heaven, as God promised Abraham, and the evildoers shall be judged for ever and ever. Amen. Interpreted Chapter text
