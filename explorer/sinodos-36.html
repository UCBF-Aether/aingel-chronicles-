The Text — Sinodos 36 (Ge'ez text of G. Horner, The Statutes of the Apostles; or, Canones Ecclesiastici (London: Williams & Norgate, 1904), public domain)

1 Canon 27[?]: And if ye be not able to go unto the church because of the unbelievers — thou, O bishop, celebrate the liturgy[?] in thy house, that the servants of God be not gathered[?] with the profane.

2 For it is not the place that sanctifieth the man, but the man sanctifieth the place.

3 And if the profane seize the place, depart[?] from it, for they have defiled it.

4 As the holy man sanctifieth the church …[?].

5 …[?] and if ye be not able to assemble among yourselves, in the house or in the church, let each one sing praise where he is by himself, and let him read the book[?], and pray, if there be two …[?].

6 And let not the believers pray with the …[?] Christians in the house[?], for it is not right …[?] the mysteries which the saints partake.

7 And let not the … of God pray with the godless[?].

8 … what fellowship hath light with darkness? And for the believers with the …[?] — if they consort[?] with …, let them be separated and go out from the church.

9 Statute 70: Concerning the commemoration of them that slept, which shall be kept … with psalm and prayer, because of Him who rose … our Lord, on the third day.

10 And let them keep …[?] the memorial of the living and the dead.

11 And let them keep also at the fulness of the month, after the likeness of the ancient practice[?].

12 And thus …[?] the people for Moses …[?].

13 And let them keep also at the fulness of …[?].

14 And let them keep at the half[?] of the year.

15 And let them keep also at the fulness of the year, after the likeness of their memorial.

16 And let them give of their goods[?] to the poor, of them that died, as a ransom[?] for their souls, for their memorial. And this we say concerning the servants of God alone[?].

17 But as for the profane: if all the world were given to the poor, it would not profit them[?].

18 For while he was in his life, he was an enemy to God; and … as he was His enemy, when he goeth out from the world there is no mercy for him, for there is no …[?] with Him …[?].

19 And behold the man and his work.

20 And behold the man whose words and deeds are good[?].

21 And if ye are invited, eat orderly[?] and in the fear of God; … ye shall pray for them that are removed[?] from this world. And ye, O presbyters and deacons of … Christ …[?].

22 And let them not …[?] … for they are … under God … and His only Son, our Lord Jesus Christ — the presbyters and deacons and the authorities[?] of the church …[?].

23 …[?] wine … but … let them not drink wine to be drunken.

24 … the drunkard … thorns spring up in the hand of the drunkard[?].

25 And this which we said is not for the priests alone, but for all the Christian people, who are called by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, who said …: "Woe …"[?].

26 …[?] to whom shall it be? Is it not to them that are assiduous in wine-drinking[?], and to them that … and go after …[?].

27 Canon 29[?]: Concerning them that are cast out[?] for the faith, and them that flee from city to city for the faith, and concerning the remembrance of the word of the Lord.

28 … for the spirit is willing and the flesh is weak[?]; and he fleeth from place to place[?].

29 And let them accept the spoiling of their goods, that they may keep the name of Christ and deny Him not; assist them and give them all that they need, and fulfil the statute of God our Lord.

30 And all this we command you … and let every one stand in the order[?] which was given him.

31 And go not beyond the statute which was ordained[?] for him.

32 It is not ours, this statute, but God's the Father's; for He said: He that receiveth you receiveth Me, and he that receiveth Me receiveth Him that sent Me.

33 And he that despiseth you despiseth Me, and he that despiseth Me despiseth [My Father] that sent Me.

34 Are there not these without soul, yet they keep the statute which was given them — the day and the night, the sun and the moon and the stars, and the winds[?], and the changes of the seasons[?]?

35 And the weeks and the hours and the days are subjected[?] to that which was ordained for them, as it was said before.

36 And He made a statute and gave them, and they pass it not.

37 And concerning the sea He said: I have set for it a statute[?]; and I have set for it a barrier[?] and doors, and I said to it: Reach as far as this, and pass not beyond[?].

38 How much more is it not proper for you to move anything which was ordained for you according to the will of God? Yet there are some who reckon this as nothing[?], that they confuse the orders[?].

39 And … let them not confuse the ordinations[?] … nor take[?] for themselves … a name[?] which was not given to them; and go not beyond …[?].

40 And therefore they provoke God[?].

41 Like the children of Korah, and Uzziah the king, who took by force the priesthood without the commandment of God.

42 And the former were consumed with fire, and the latter had his forehead marked[?] with leprosy[?], for they provoked God, Jesus Christ, who commanded that it be so; and they pained the heart of the Holy Spirit, and despised His testimony[?].

43 … for them that do this deed …[?].

44 And … they that offer the oblation and the bread of blessing[?], until they …[?] … honour … in the work of the priesthood … this is the likeness of the great [priesthood] …[?].

45 And we declare[?] that we may teach them this; and henceforth[?] we speak of Moses, the servant of God, to whom God spoke face to face.

46 As He speaketh … with His companion[?], who said: I know thee more than all … whom God spoke [with] face to face, in signs[?] and wonders and dreams and angels and interpretation[?] …[?].

47 … for the priests … and not … the work of the law[?] …[?].

48 And that which He commanded the priests, let not the Levites[?] approach … but …[?].

49 And if he transgress and go out from his service which he received, … death[?].

50 And moreover this maketh clear to us what happened to Saul, when he thought to offer sacrifice by his own will[?], which the prophet had not given him[?].

51 And Samuel the high priest[?] … brought upon him … sin, and cursed him for ever[?].

52 And the prophet feared not because of the anointing wherewith he had anointed him to be king.

53 And God hath made us to know the great deed[?], revealing[?] what happened to Uzziah the king; and vengeance waited not[?] because of his pride[?], and He made him a stranger[?] … his kingdom.

54 Concerning the … priesthood which …[?].

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 27 provides for worship under pressure: if the faithful cannot go to the church because of unbelievers, the bishop celebrates the liturgy in his house, for it is not the place that sanctifies the man but the man who sanctifies the place, and if the profane seize the place the faithful depart, since they have defiled it. Statute 70 then gives the commemoration of the departed, kept with psalm and prayer because of him who rose on the third day, at the month's fullness, the half-year and the year's fullness, with alms given to the poor as a ransom for the souls of God's servants alone. Canon 29 follows on exiles for the faith, who accept the spoiling of their goods and are to be assisted, and on keeping one's ordained order unconfused — illustrated by Korah's children consumed with fire, King Uzziah marked with leprosy for seizing the priesthood, Levites barred from the priests' work, and Saul rebuked by Samuel for offering sacrifice by his own will.
