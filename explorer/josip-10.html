Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter treats the angels, who live in God without food and glorified him when he created them, and then the growth of their children, whom the devil feared lest they return to the Lord and be taught. Its thread is that God's works are done so that his divinity may be known, swiftly and openly. Interpreted Chapter text as printed above; study-notes file for this chapter is silent
