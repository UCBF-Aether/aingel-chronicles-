The Text — Sinodos 30 (Ge'ez text of G. Horner, The Statutes of the Apostles; or, Canones Ecclesiastici (London: Williams & Norgate, 1904), public domain)

1 Concerning him who desireth to partake of the holy mystery, and him who desireth to partake of the holy mysteries which are of the service of God: let him be brought by the deacons unto the bishop.

2 or by the presbyters; and let them examine them concerning the cause[?] for which they were called, that they may stand and first hear the word of God.

3 And let there be testimony concerning them from them that brought them; and let them examine them diligently[?] with firmness concerning their works.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Only three verses survive in this chapter. They concern one who desires to partake of the holy mystery: he is to be brought by the deacons, or by the presbyters, unto the bishop, examined concerning the cause for which he was called so that he may stand and first hear the word of God, with testimony concerning him from those who brought him, and diligent, firm examination concerning his works.
