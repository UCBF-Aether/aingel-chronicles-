The Text — 1 Meqabyan 32 (Ge'ez text (project Ge'ez corpus, verse-level))

1 But ye, O kings and princes, hear my word, and keep my commandment, saith God of all who ruleth; as the children of Israel despised me, and served[?] other gods after me[?] , while I am God their God, who kept them, and saved them, and …[?] them from[?] their fathers, and fed them with all that they desired.

2 And I made them to ride upon the high places of the earth, and fed them with the blessing of the land; and as they desired: the fruit[?] of the vine which they planted not, and oil of the olive, and the …[?] of water, the sweet which is not bitter[?].

3 And …[?] honey, and milk of cattle, with fat of wheat, and he clothed[?] … śärgʷə[?] , and all that they desired.

4 And there is none that withheld[?] from them that which they asked of me.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A four-verse address to kings and princes: hear God's word and keep his commandment, unlike Israel, who despised him and served other gods after him, though he kept, saved and fed them, making them ride on the high places of the earth with the fruit of vines they planted not, oil of olive, honey, milk and fat of wheat. Nothing, the chapter says, did he withhold from what they asked of him. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states.
