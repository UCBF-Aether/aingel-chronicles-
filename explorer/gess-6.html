The Text — Gәssәw 6 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 [Topic:] If a bishop, or one of the priests, who has not received [?] the eucharist at the time of the solemnization, declares the reason for this — and if there is what made him unworthy — let him be forgiven [?].

2 But if he does not declare his reason, let him be expelled; for he became a leader, that sin should come upon the people, and he made them take offence at him who offered the eucharist [?], as though he had not offered it in purity.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A bishop or priest who has not received the eucharist at the solemnization must declare his reason: if something made him unworthy, he is forgiven. But if he gives no reason, he is expelled, because as a leader he has cast suspicion on the one who offered the eucharist, as though it had not been offered in purity, and has led the people into offence. Interpreted Paraphrase of the chapter's own text; Standard corpus, Gessew 6
