Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter remembers the fallen angels: while they were angels they sang in heaven, but lust led them to take wives from the daughters of men, and God brought them down into burning judgment with Diyabolos their father, shortening human days, from the former span to 120 years, because of their sin. It closes by contrasting the fathers, whose long days came from keeping the commandment and teaching it to their children, with the present shortness of days, blamed on sin and on failing to teach the next generation. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
