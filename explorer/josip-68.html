Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Using the mixture of water and fire (which join without either being changed) as an analogy, this chapter argues that Christ's eating and drinking were not compelled by hunger or thirst or nature, but done by his will: “My food is that I do the will of him who sent me and finish his work.” The analogy and the argument survive only in fragments, with many readings marked (?).
