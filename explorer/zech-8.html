The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Zechariah 8 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 Again the word of the LORD of hosts came to me, saying,

2 Thus saith the LORD of hosts; I was jealous for Zion with great jealousy, and I was jealous for her with great fury.

3 Thus saith the LORD; I am returned unto Zion, and will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and Jerusalem shall be called a city of truth; and the mountain of the LORD of hosts the holy mountain.

4 Thus saith the LORD of hosts; There shall yet old men and old women dwell in the streets of Jerusalem, and every man with his staff in his hand for very age.

5 And the streets of the city shall be full of boys and girls playing in the streets thereof.

6 Thus saith the LORD of hosts; If it be marvellous in the eyes of the remnant of this people in these days, should it also be marvellous in mine eyes? saith the LORD of hosts.

7 Thus saith the LORD of hosts; Behold, I will save my people from the east country, and from the west country;

8 And I will bring them, and they shall dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and they shall be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness.

9 Thus saith the LORD of hosts; Let your hands be strong, ye that hear in these days these words by the mouth of the prophets, which were in the day that the foundation of the house of the LORD of hosts was laid, that the temple might be built.

10 For before these days there was no hire for man, nor any hire for beast; neither was there any peace to him that went out or came in because of the affliction: for I set all men every one against his neighbour.

11 But now I will not be unto the residue of this people as in the former days, saith the LORD of hosts.

12 For the seed shall be prosperous; the vine shall give her fruit, and the ground shall give her increase, and the heavens shall give their dew; and I will cause the remnant of this people to possess all these things.

13 And it shall come to pass, that as ye were a curse among the heathen, O house of Judah, and house of Israel; so will I save you, and ye shall be a blessing: fear not, but let your hands be strong.

14 For thus saith the LORD of hosts; As I thought to punish you, when your fathers provoked me to wrath, saith the LORD of hosts, and I repented not:

15 So again have I thought in these days to do well unto Jerusalem and to the house of Judah: fear ye not.

16 These are the things that ye shall do; Speak ye every man the truth to his neighbour; execute the judgment of truth and peace in your gates:

17 And let none of you imagine evil in your hearts against his neighbour; and love no false oath: for all these are things that I hate, saith the LORD.

18 And the word of the LORD of hosts came unto me, saying,

19 Thus saith the LORD of hosts; The fast of the fourth month, and the fast of the fifth, and the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth, shall be to the house of Judah joy and gladness, and cheerful feasts; therefore love the truth and peace.

20 Thus saith the LORD of hosts; It shall yet come to pass, that there shall come people, and the inhabitants of many cities:

21 And the inhabitants of one city shall go to another, saying, Let us go speedily to pray before the LORD, and to seek the LORD of hosts: I will go also.

22 Yea, many people and strong nations shall come to seek the LORD of hosts in Jerusalem, and to pray before the LORD.

23 Thus saith the LORD of hosts; In those days it shall come to pass, that ten men shall take hold out of all languages of the nations, even shall take hold of the skirt of him that is a Jew, saying, We will go with you: for we have heard that God is with you.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Again the word of the LORD of hosts comes, jealous for Zion with great jealousy, and the chapter piles promise on promise: the LORD is returned to Zion and will dwell in Jerusalem, called a city of truth; old men and women with staffs for age and boys and girls playing will fill its streets; the people will be saved from east country and west country and dwell in Jerusalem in truth and righteousness. Let your hands be strong, the seed will be prosperous, the curse among the nations will become a blessing, truth and peace will be judged in the gates, the fasts of the fourth, fifth, seventh, and tenth months will become joy and gladness, and many peoples and strong nations will come to seek the LORD in Jerusalem, ten men taking hold of the skirt of a Jew and saying, we will go with you, for we have heard that God is with you. Interpreted Study notes for this chapter, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. Again the word of the Lord of hosts came to me, saying, because after the rebuke about fasting, a new word of promise follows, still from the Lord of hosts. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 2. Thus says the Lord of hosts, I was jealous for Zion with great jealousy, and I was jealous for her with great fury, because the jealousy of chapter one returns, now with fury against Zion's enemies, as the ground of the promises that follow. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 3. Thus says the Lord, I am returned unto Zion, and will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem, and Jerusalem shall be called a city of truth, and the mountain of the Lord of hosts the holy mountain, because return means dwelling in the midst, and the city takes new names, city of truth and holy mountain. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 4. Thus says the Lord of hosts, there shall yet old men and old women dwell in the streets of Jerusalem, and every man with his staff in his hand for very age, because the sign of restored peace is age itself, old men and women sitting in the streets with staffs, living to very age. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 5. And the streets of the city shall be full of boys and girls playing in the streets thereof, because the other sign of peace is childhood at play in the streets, where war once emptied them. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 6. Thus says the Lord of hosts, if it be marvellous in the eyes of the remnant of this people in these days, should it also be marvellous in mine eyes, says the Lord of hosts, because what seems marvellous to the remnant is not marvellous to God, whose eyes measure differently. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 7. Thus says the Lord of hosts, behold, I will save my people from the east country, and from the west country, because salvation gathers from east and west alike, from every quarter of the scattering. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 8. And I will bring them, and they shall dwell in the midst of Jerusalem, and they shall be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness, because the gathering ends in dwelling, and the covenant is renewed in truth and righteousness. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 9. Thus says the Lord of hosts, let your hands be strong, ye that hear in these days these words by the mouth of the prophets, which were in the day that the foundation of the house of the Lord of hosts was laid, that the temple might be built, because the hearers of the prophets, in the very days of the foundation, are told to let their hands be strong for the building. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 10. For before these days there was no hire for man, nor any hire for beast, neither was there any peace to him that went out or came in because of the affliction, for I set all men every one against his neighbour, because before the rebuilding, labour went unhired and travel unsafe, since God had set every man against his neighbour. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 11. But now I will not be unto the residue of this people as in the former days, says the Lord of hosts, because the former treatment is expressly reversed for the remnant, and now is set against before. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 12. For the seed shall be prosperous, the vine shall give her fruit, and the ground shall give her increase, and the heavens shall give their dew, and I will cause the remnant of this people to possess all these things, because prosperity is spelled out in seed, vine, ground and dew, all given to the remnant to possess. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 13. And it shall come to pass, that as ye were a curse among the heathen, O house of Judah, and house of Israel, so will I save you, and ye shall be a blessing, fear not, but let your hands be strong, because the people who were a curse among the nations will become a blessing, and the command is repeated, fear not, let your hands be strong. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 14. For thus says the Lord of hosts, as I thought to punish you, when your fathers provoked me to wrath, says the Lord of hosts, and I repented not, because as surely as punishment was purposed and not repented of, so the following promise of good is equally sure. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 15. So again have I thought in these days to do well unto Jerusalem and to the house of Judah, fear ye not, because the same resolve that once purposed punishment now purposes good toward Jerusalem and Judah, and the command is fear not. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 16. These are the things that ye shall do, speak ye every man the truth to his neighbour, execute the judgment of truth and peace in your gates, because the promised good requires present duties, truthful speech and judgment of truth and peace in the gates. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 17. And let none of you imagine evil in your hearts against his neighbour, and love no false oath, for all these are things that I hate, says the Lord, because imagined evil and loved false oaths are named among the things God hates, not merely the acts that follow them. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 18. And the word of the Lord of hosts came unto me, saying, because the fast question of chapter seven is now answered directly, with a word from the Lord of hosts. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 19. Thus says the Lord of hosts, the fast of the fourth month, and the fast of the fifth, and the fast of the seventh, and the fast of the tenth, shall be to the house of Judah joy and gladness, and cheerful feasts, therefore love the truth and peace, because the four fasts of captivity will become joy, gladness and cheerful feasts, and the condition is love of truth and peace. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 20. Thus says the Lord of hosts, it shall yet come to pass, that there shall come people, and the inhabitants of many cities, because the promise widens again, with people from many cities yet to come. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 21. And the inhabitants of one city shall go to another, saying, let us go speedily to pray before the Lord, and to seek the Lord of hosts, I will go also, because city will stir city, each urging the other to go speedily to pray and seek the Lord, and each answering, I will go also. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 22. Yea, many people and strong nations shall come to seek the Lord of hosts in Jerusalem, and to pray before the Lord, because the pilgrimage grows to many peoples and strong nations, all seeking the Lord in Jerusalem. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 23. Thus says the Lord of hosts, in those days it shall come to pass, that ten men shall take hold out of all languages of the nations, even shall take hold of the skirt of him that is a Jew, saying, we will go with you, for we have heard that God is with you, because ten men of every language will seize a Jew's skirt, asking to go with him, since they have heard that God is with the Jews. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

Unfamiliar terms
