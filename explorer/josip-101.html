Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The first half is a fragmentary disputation on God and man: only he who descended ascended, “I in the Father, and the Father in me”, and “I and my Father are one”, with the chapter distinguishing what is said of God alone without incarnation from what is said in the incarnation. The second half turns to a reckoning of the nativity by the sun and its remainder, concluding, in the teacher's reckoning, that the nativity was on the third day, though the figures themselves are mostly [?].
