Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This is the book's fullest baptismal chapter in the early canons. Canon 36 repeats the separated prayer of hearers and believers, with women veiled; canon 37 covers the laying of the hand on the catechumen; and canon 38 gives baptism itself: examination of candidates from about twenty years old, five weeks of watchful, pure teaching, Friday baptism by the bishop, adjuration of the unclean spirit, breathing upon them, anointing with holy oil in the name of Father, Son and Holy Spirit, and the bread and cup as body and blood, with milk and honey mixed for the fulfilment of the promise of a land flowing with milk and honey. The baptismal confession that closes the chapter runs from the virgin birth through Pontius Pilate, willing death, resurrection on the third day, ascension and coming judgment, to the resurrection of the flesh and the kingdom of heaven. Unfamiliar terms
