Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter largely recapitulates chapter 85, Egypt, law, Moses and Joshua's division of the land, transgression, repentance and idolatry, and then turns to the One born unseparated from the Father, sent as Light and pearl, appearing among Israel, with a fragment about the third day.
