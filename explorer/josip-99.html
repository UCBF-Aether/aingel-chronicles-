Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter treats suffering for the faith, scourges and martyrs' crowns, Dioscorus, Satan dividing the faith, and persecutions under Theodosius, Marcian and Anastasius, with believers brought by a priest named Jacob. It explains, in its own broken words, why believers were called Jacobites, and closes with the Son's saying, “I go to my God and your God.” Interpreted Reading of this chapter's text as printed above; the study-notes file for this chapter is silent
