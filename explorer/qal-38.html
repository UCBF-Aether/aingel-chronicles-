The Text — Qalementos 38 (Ge'ez text (project Ge'ez corpus, chapter-level))

1 (1) And keep ye the feast, from his resurrection, in the eighth season, which is the New Sunday. (2) For therein he came unto us — unto the company of the twelve — after his resurrection from the dead, while we were gathered in the upper room in Zion, the doors being shut. (3) For on this day Thomas found and touched with his hand the place of the nails in the side of our Lord Christ, and the place of the wounds [?] — even unto the inward part [?], the tips of his fingers; and our God made them new and gave them life. (4) And he said unto him: Doubt not, Thomas, but be believing.

Parallels

Both passages show the risen Christ coming to the disciples with the doors shut, and Thomas invited to touch the marks of the wounds — John records the invitation, while Qalementos narrates the touching. Qalementos 38 says Christ came to the company of the twelve in the upper room in Zion, the doors being shut, and Thomas found and touched the place of the nails and the wounds, and Christ said, Doubt not, Thomas, but be believing. John 20 says Jesus came when the doors were shut, and said to Thomas, Reach hither thy finger, and behold my hands, and thrust thy hand into my side, and be not faithless, but believing. Documented Qalementos 38 (corpus qal.json); John 20:26-27 (corpus john.json)

Differences

John 20 separates two appearances: Thomas is absent from the first, and the second comes after eight days, ending in Thomas's confession, My LORD and my God. Qalementos 38 presents a single feast scene in the eighth season, the New Sunday, adds that God made the wounded places new and gave them life, and gives no confession by Thomas. Documented John 20:19-29 (corpus john.json); Qalementos 38 (corpus qal.json)
