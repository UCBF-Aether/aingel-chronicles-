Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter opens in dread at judgment: no deed hidden, all standing before Him in fear and trembling, filled with shame, with no garment for nakedness. The speaker knows not the way where the soul shall be led — whether to darkness, or to the qälay [?] of the sea or the qälay [?] of Sheol, led by guides whose faces are darkness and whose speech is unheard. Words the chapter attributes to David the prophet — a troubled soul shown its way, hiding testimony, turning aside, sinning against him who warned — are quoted as a picture of that straying, and demons, it says, lead the straying soul to one who knows not the way. Against that escort stand the angels of light, sent to convey righteous souls to the light of life, while angels of darkness and demons receive sinners’ souls into eternal judgment according to their deeds. Woe follows: sinners led into ḥagwəl with no rest and no escape for ever, having gone in the way of Cain and, by the counsel of his error, unto Balaam, taking others’ goods by violence — and they shall be judged in burning fire. The chapter attributes its David quotation to David but does not name a source passage, and several of its key terms are left untranslated and marked [?].
