Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter sets out, in its own words, four positions on the resurrection: Jews who say, let us eat and drink, dust we shall die; Samaritans who say the flesh shall not rise, though the soul, unseen as wind and thunder, lives; Pharisees who say the dead rise but God clothes souls in heaven with another flesh, not earthly flesh; and Sadducees who say there is no resurrection at all, neither for soul nor flesh. The chapter answers them all with its own doctrine, the dead shall rise, flesh gathered to its place and souls put back into the flesh whence they went, at the trumpet's blowing in the twinkling of an eye, to stand before God and be judged as their deeds.
