Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 48 concerns the times of prayer: the faithful rise in the morning, wash their hands and pray before any work, and keep hours through the day tied in the text to the Passion — the hour of the crucifixion and binding to the wood, the sixth hour of darkness, the hour his side was pierced and blood and water came forth, midnight when the Bridegroom comes, and cock-crowing when Israel denied the Lord — with all creation, stars, plants, waters and angels, said to pray at the same hour. The chapter then turns against heretics who corrupted the apostles' teaching, insisting that signs and wonders do not exalt their workers: Moses in Egypt, Joshua, Samuel, David, Elijah and Elisha, and Daniel saved from lions are all named as workers of wonders who were not exalted over their brothers, for grace is from God, not from man's power.
