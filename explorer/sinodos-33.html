Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 24 is the book's fullest examination of candidates for entry: obedience and manner of life are required, slaves are taught to be at their masters' pleasure so God's word is not blasphemed, married couples are taught against adultery, the unmarried toward lawful marriage, and a man with a devil must be healed and purified before entering to partake. Then comes a long list of former deeds to be forsaken or the candidate sent away — idolatry, theatre people, charioteers, lyre and pipe players, dancing teachers, fighters unto death, circus performers, women practitioners whose Ge'ez titles are left unglossed in the text, spirit-consulters and star-gazers — followed by canon 25 on the concubine of an unbeliever, divorce writing before lawful remarriage, and the layman who teaches with good words, conduct and tongue.
