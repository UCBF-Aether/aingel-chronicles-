Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter continues the martyrs' speech to Sirusaydan: he and his gods, who have no soul or breath and profit none, have exchanged repentance for what profits nothing, and shall have no escape, dying and being gathered again into Gehenna. The speakers depart and are hidden from him; Sirusaydan remains in great fear until morning, then goes to the place of their corpses and finds them not, for God kept them that they should not be taken from their flesh. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
