Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter reaches the council of Chalcedon: Dioscorus enters to find exiled bishops seated, books of cursing are cried out, the emperor commands, Dioscorus is removed, and the council's confession is stated, that Christ is God and man in two natures which are his own, the Word made flesh and not divided from the flesh he took, in the likeness of a servant.
