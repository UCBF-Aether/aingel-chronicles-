Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The discourse continues on the Son as light from light and as the Father's likeness, insisting there is one Father and one Son, and that the Son was not created but begotten. It closes with one Spirit shared by Father and Son, and with baptism in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Much of the wording survives only in fragments.
