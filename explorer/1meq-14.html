Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter asks why God punished the former people, retells the flood that washed away the children of Cain, Noah's covenant, and Israel's repeated failure to walk as Abraham, Isaac and Jacob did, Levites, priests and scribes transgressing the commandment Moses gave, Love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and love thy neighbour as thyself. At Horeb the people make a molten calf, rejoice, eat, drink and rise up to play, Moses grinds the calf and the Levites slay among themselves, and the chapter closes by contrasting the fathers, who kept the commandment from youth, with children who remembered not the God who brought them out of Egypt. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
