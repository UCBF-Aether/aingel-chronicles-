Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter turns from narrative to warning, holding up proud kings, a visionary who exalted himself and died, Nimrod, and Nebuchadnezzar, who claimed there was no king but himself and was driven from among men, and tells Sirusaydan the same fate awaits him if he persists: humiliation into Sheol, where there is weeping and gnashing of teeth and unquenched flame. Against that stands God, who humbles the proud and exalts the lowly, raises the dead from dust, sent Raphael to Tobit and angels to Gideon and Moses, and made Sirusaydan king a second time that he might love God, keep his people in righteousness, and do God's will. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
