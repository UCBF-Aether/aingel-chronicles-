Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Two feasts are joined: the ascension into heaven and the descent of the Holy Spirit on the apostles. The Spirit descended like tongues of fire and taught the apostles a word they had not heard or understood before, forty days after the resurrection, when Christ ascended from the Mount of Olives in a chariot of light and returned not to them. Investigation (editorial — investigating a parallel, not concluding)

Disclaimer. This section investigates a question raised by shared events and shared imagery; it does not conclude that one text depends on the other or that the two accounts describe the same event in the same way. Interpreted Editorial framing for this section

Parallels

Both passages present an ascension from the Mount of Olives and a descent of the Holy Spirit like tongues of fire, though Acts presents them as separate events (Acts 1; Acts 2). Qalementos 39 keeps the feast of the ascension and the feast of the descent of the Holy Spirit on the apostles together, saying the Spirit descended like tongues of fire and taught them a word they had not heard before, forty days after the resurrection, from the Mount of Olives. Acts says Jesus was seen of them forty days, and the disciples returned from the mount called Olivet, and at Pentecost there appeared cloven tongues like as of fire and they were filled with the Holy Ghost. Documented Qalementos 39 (corpus qal.json); Acts 1:3, 12; Acts 2:1-4 (corpus acts.json)

Differences

Acts separates the events: the ascension in chapter 1, where a cloud receives Jesus, and Pentecost in chapter 2, where the emphasis is speaking with other tongues. Qalementos 39 joins them in one feast passage, says the Spirit descended upon their mouths, says Christ ascended in a chariot of light and returned not to them, and stresses a newly taught word rather than languages. Documented Acts 1:9; Acts 2:1-4 (corpus acts.json); Qalementos 39 (corpus qal.json)
