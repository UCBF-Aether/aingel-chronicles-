Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter gathers sayings on persecution and endurance: seizure, councils, synagogues, kings and governors, utterance given by the Holy Spirit, reward in heaven, losing life to find it, gaining the world and losing the soul, and the name Beelzebub. It then treats chastening, citing the apostle's “despise not the chastening of the LORD”, and the rich man and Lazarus, with Abraham refusing a drop of water on the tip of the finger, and closes with a doxology.
