Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter names those who believed — Hosea, Gideon, Samson, Barak, Deborah, and Judith — and says God raised up judges to save his people from their oppressors, blessing their land, cattle, and plantings while they walked straight. When they became evil in their deeds, he cast them into the hands of oppressors; when they returned with all their heart, he forgave them and remembered that they are flesh and blood. Meqabis, hearing this word, makes his own way straight, keeps the commandment of the Lord God of Israel, and orders his house, tithes, and offerings by the pattern the Jews keep.
