Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Waters and the darkness of demons, not lifted since the prophets and righteous fathers, open the chapter, which then turns to Mary made the betrothed of Joseph. The narrative thread follows a flight into Egypt and the hiding of divinity, though much of the wording survives only in fragments.
