Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter recounts councils and creed: Arius anathematized, the confession of one Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, Light from Light, begotten of the Father before the ages, who shall come to judge the living and the dead, the institution of the oblation, the condemnation of teaching that the Holy Spirit is a creature, the council at Constantinople under Theodosius, Gregory, Sabellius, and the rise of the two-sons teaching under Nestorius, with Dioscorus of Alexandria and the other patriarchs named.
