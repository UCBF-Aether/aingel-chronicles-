Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Those who die in repentance go in their souls into the garden of delight, the chapter says, until the number of the host who fell is complete. The matter was hidden from before the creation of the world, and from the prophets, the holy ones, and the angels, and the chapter's long units circle that hidden counsel.
