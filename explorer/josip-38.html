The Text — Josippon 38 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 The cherubim, who have many eyes, and the seraphim, who have six wings, glorify continually, without silence; and when they say it, the priests give the holy [?] greeting (?) which is in the house of the Christians, unto all.

2 Holy, ho[?]ly, LORD; heaven and earth are full of Thy holy glory, all of them. Those who pray in the house of the Christians unto God, the Holy Trinity, by this manner of the Masses — and He also sanctifieth them from their sins.

3 As Isaiah the prophet said: For He who glorifieth Me, while I glorify, and He who sanctifieth Me, while I sanctify — and by this Mass the faithful are sanctified from their sins.

4 For Mass in the Syriac tongue, and in the Hebrew — its interpretation is gladness; and a Mass of joy it is.

5 And the Mass which is called pure, it is, verily, for this interpretation again, that it may be made known: for by the Mass the faithful are sanctified from their sins.

6 And after this Mass they shall be saved, their heads.

7 And he saith thus: We ask Thee, our God — we, Thy servants [?]

8 A company of those who are not changed, as the Holy Spirit descended upon us.

9 And upon the garden an offering, that it may sanctify them and cleanse their body and their blood, for Jesus Christ, Thy Son; beloved is he who loveth this great gift, which shall be for all men (?) who stand in the house of the Christians; for the Spirit of God, the Holy One, descended upon them, before it descended upon the offering.

10 For the priests make an end upon us, and upon this offering.

11 He is He who descendeth upon the people, before the offering.

12 And as He sanctifieth the earthly bread of straw, and maketh it heavenly, that passeth not away.

13 Likewise He sanctifieth all the people who stand before Him, in the sight of them upon them, and cleanseth them from their sins that perish, and maketh them pure unto eternal life; for the fruit of the Holy Spirit is a spirit of cleansing, and He is it.

14 As we have interpreted for thee: He is He who cleanseth a man, when He descendeth upon him, from his sins, altogether.

15 And if it be as a boundless sea, which cannot be stayed, that it may cleanse from them the gift of the offering — and if it draw not near — as He cleanseth everyone upon whom He descendeth, in the holy depth, from all sin.

16 And after this the Holy Spirit descendeth upon the holy offerings.

17 The priests say: Our God, righteousness is in Thy being, as Thou hast sanctified this offering by the sight of the Holy Spirit upon it; likewise sanctify us also, from iniquity that is hidden and that is manifest.

18 And He opened from us the reckoning and the thought which pleaseth not Thy goodness; and we are cleansed utterly — our souls and our body and our spirit and our thoughts and our hearts — that with a pure heart and a bright soul and pure lips he may say, in Mary, Our Father — from us he is blessed and sanctified.

19 As Thy only Son Jesus Christ taught us. And He said unto us: When ye pray, say ye thus — let the people say: Our Father who art in heaven, hallowed be Thy name; Thy kingdom come; and Thy will be done; as in heaven, so also on earth; our bread of sustenance, which is needful for us, give us.

20 Forsake iniquity and envy; nay, deliver us and loose him that hath sinned, for us, and [?]; lead us not into the temptation of sin; neither forget us; and deliver us from all evil, by Christ our Lord. Amen.

21 He willeth not at all: great is this season of this crucifixion.

22 For Christ [?] — of God, a little while was present with them; and the deadly ones stand before Him, while they say unto His Father: Our Father who art in heaven, reveal that whosoever dwelleth with Him in the exalted kingdom, and they ask Him that He may be delivered, that He may dwell within them, that the hearing of their mouths may be heard, and the striving; and those are the enemies, by their works.

23 And again they ask Him that His kingdom may come unto them, which is the Holy Spirit, that He may drive forth from them their enemies, who make them work for sin.

24 And again they are able to ask that the desire be fulfilled in them on earth, as in heaven among the angels; and the desire in His glory and His holiness continually; for the angels glorify Him and sanctify Him without ceasing; for He remembered [?] that they fell not, as [?] men (?) and hosts fell; and likewise He [?] upon those who are from Him, in the part of Ipile and Imhayaw, that they may glorify Him and sanctify Him continually, without weariness, and may fulfil their desire which is in Him, in the will, continually.

25 And concerning that which they say: Our needful bread, give us today.

26 That which is called, In the world which cometh, in heaven: for the needy ones are in the world which cometh; which He said: He is the need of the angels, the glory of God and His holiness and His remembrance; and there cometh that which is the need of the angels — the glory and the holiness and the praise; for the angels have not this work, in that they live, and it is fulfilled for them.

27 And likewise the faithful, if they were in heaven: the savour of their living (?) house, like them.

28 And He is their need in heaven; and therefore they ask God, while they say: Thy glory and Thy holiness and Thy offering — this is our bread of rest in heaven — give us today upon earth; for lo, if we are not fed today upon earth, we shall seek a savour in heaven.

29 And therefore they ask Him that He lead them not into temptation which hindereth them from the good.

30 But He saveth them from evil, by Jesus Christ, for there is no saviour besides Him.

31 And by it the priests give, while he saith: Nay, I have sought Thee, O holy Father, Nirir, who loveth goodness; and lead us not into temptation, and hide not our sins; they enter upon us, hasten, save us from works which profit not — from her thought and her shouting and her noise and her drunkenness and the confusion of him who is amazed; and cast away from us, and all his troubles that are sown.

32 Save us, and hide from us Thy wonders that were added unto sins; and save us by Thy holy power, by Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The cherubim with many eyes and the seraphim with six wings glorify continually without silence, and the priests give their response. The chapter is a liturgy in prose: the offering is sanctified by the sight of the Holy Spirit upon it, and it closes with the prayer 'Save us, and hide from us Thy wonders that were added unto sins; save us by Thy holy power, by Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.' Interpreted Chapter text as printed above; study-notes file for this chapter is silent
