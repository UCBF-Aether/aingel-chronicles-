Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Akrandes, king of Midian, gathers a great host of Syria, Cilicia and Damascus at the crossing of Jordan and demands tribute from Israel, threatening to carry off their goods and children; Israel weeps to God, and God gives power to three brethren, Mebqeyus, Meqabyos and Judah, who resolve to give their lives for their people. Mebqeyus strikes down the king and his brethren smite his guards right and left, the host flees, Israel is delivered, and then, the chapter says, Israel returns to evil and God raises against them a people they do not know. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
