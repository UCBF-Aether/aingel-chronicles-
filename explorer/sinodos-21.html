Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Only two verses survive under this chapter division, numbered 2 and 4. Statute 47 concerns burial: no man is to provide at his own request for the burial of a man in the burial performed for all, but let him give to the bereaved, to him that provides a place, and to him that abides for a brother's sake — and from this the bishop is to provide for the churches. The missing verse numbers are as printed; nothing further survives in this chapter. Interpreted Summary of this chapter's text, as printed above
