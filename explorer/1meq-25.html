Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter speaks in God's voice of a judgment none can escape, neither under earth, above heaven, in sea nor in the deep waters, for every soul is in his hand, and he will recompense each according to the evil of their works in the day of judgment. Against the transient beauty of the wicked, gold and silver that profit not, a passing shadow, a word that returns not, stand the righteous, who show mercy, weep over the dead and poor, clothe the naked, give to stranger and poor, wrong not the faithful, repay not evil for evil, and deal justly in measure and balance. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
