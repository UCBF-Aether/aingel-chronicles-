The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Zechariah 5 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 Then I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and behold a flying roll.

2 And he said unto me, What seest thou? And I answered, I see a flying roll; the length thereof is twenty cubits, and the breadth thereof ten cubits.

3 Then said he unto me, This is the curse that goeth forth over the face of the whole earth: for every one that stealeth shall be cut off as on this side according to it; and every one that sweareth shall be cut off as on that side according to it.

4 I will bring it forth, saith the LORD of hosts, and it shall enter into the house of the thief, and into the house of him that sweareth falsely by my name: and it shall remain in the midst of his house, and shall consume it with the timber thereof and the stones thereof.

5 Then the angel that talked with me went forth, and said unto me, Lift up now thine eyes, and see what is this that goeth forth.

6 And I said, What is it? And he said, This is an ephah that goeth forth. He said moreover, This is their resemblance through all the earth.

7 And, behold, there was lifted up a talent of lead: and this is a woman that sitteth in the midst of the ephah.

8 And he said, This is wickedness. And he cast it into the midst of the ephah; and he cast the weight of lead upon the mouth thereof.

9 Then lifted I up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came out two women, and the wind was in their wings; for they had wings like the wings of a stork: and they lifted up the ephah between the earth and the heaven.

10 Then said I to the angel that talked with me, Whither do these bear the ephah?

11 And he said unto me, To build it an house in the land of Shinar: and it shall be established, and set there upon her own base.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Two visions of judgment follow: a flying roll, twenty cubits by ten, which is the curse going forth over the whole earth against every thief and every false swearer, entering his house and consuming it with timber and stones. Then an ephah goes forth with a woman sitting inside it, named Wickedness, sealed under a talent of lead, and two winged women lift the ephah between earth and heaven to build it a house in the land of Shinar, where it will be set on its own base. Interpreted Study notes for this chapter, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. Then I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and behold a flying roll, because the sixth vision shows a scroll flying, a curse in motion, not yet resting, over the whole land. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 2. And he said unto me, what seest thou, and I answered, I see a flying roll, the length thereof is twenty cubits, and the breadth thereof ten cubits, because the roll's size, twenty by ten cubits, matches the porch of the temple, as if the curse were measured by the sanctuary it avenges. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 3. Then said he unto me, this is the curse that goes forth over the face of the whole earth, for every one that steals shall be cut off as on this side according to it, and every one that swears shall be cut off as on that side according to it, because the roll is written on both sides, with theft on one side and false swearing on the other, the two sins that break both tables of the law. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 4. I will bring it forth, says the Lord of hosts, and it shall enter into the house of the thief, and into the house of him that swears falsely by my name, and it shall remain in the midst of his house, and shall consume it with the timber thereof and the stones thereof, because the curse enters the sinner's house and stays there, consuming timber and stones together, until nothing remains. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 5. Then the angel that talked with me went forth, and said unto me, lift up now thine eyes, and see what is this that goes forth, because a second sight in the same chapter follows, and the prophet is told to look again at what goes forth. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 6. And I said, what is it, and he said, this is an ephah that goes forth, he said moreover, this is their resemblance through all the earth, because the ephah, the measure of corn, is the measure of the people's sins, seen alike through all the earth. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 7. And, behold, there was lifted up a talent of lead, and this is a woman that sits in the midst of the ephah, because under the leaden lid sits a woman, the figure of wickedness itself, shut inside the measure. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 8. And he said, this is wickedness, and he cast it into the midst of the ephah, and he cast the weight of lead upon the mouth thereof, because the angel names the woman wickedness, casts her back into the ephah, and seals the mouth with lead, so that wickedness is shut in. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 9. Then lifted I up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came out two women, and the wind was in their wings, for they had wings like the wings of a stork, and they lifted up the ephah between the earth and the heaven, because two winged women, swift as storks, lift the sealed ephah between earth and heaven, carrying wickedness away. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 10. Then said I to the angel that talked with me, whither do these bear the ephah, because the prophet asks where wickedness is being carried, which is the question the vision exists to answer. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 11. And he said unto me, to build it a house in the land of Shinar, and it shall be established, and set there upon her own base, because wickedness is carried to Shinar, to Babylon, its own home, where it will be settled upon its own base, removed from the holy land. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

Unfamiliar terms

  • Ephah. a grain measure; in Zechariah 5 it is the vessel carrying Wickedness, sealed with a talent of lead and carried to the land of Shinar. Interpreted Study notes for this chapter, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Flying roll. a great scroll, twenty cubits long and ten broad, which in Zechariah 5 is the curse going forth over the whole earth against thief and false swearer. Interpreted Study notes for this chapter, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
