The Text — 1 Meqabyan 31 (Ge'ez text (project Ge'ez corpus, verse-level))

1 Kings are not appointed without my will, and the poor are not …[?] without my command, and the mighty prevail not[?] without my will.

2 And I gave unto David grace, and wisdom unto Solomon; and I added years unto Hezekiah.

3 And I shortened the days of Goliath; and I gave strength unto Samson, and again I weakened his strength.

4 And I saved him — I, David my servant — from the hand of Goliath the räʾäyətawi[?].

5 And yet from the hand of Saul the king, and yet from the hand of Kal'u[?] the räʾäyətawi[?] who fought against him, and from the hand of all them that fought against him and warred against him[?] ; for he kept my commandment.

6 And I loved him, even him; and unto all them that kept my commandment — kings and princes — I loved them, and gave them strength and victory[?] over their enemies; for they gladdened me[?].

7 And again I gave them the land of inheritance[?] which I sware unto their fathers; a pure[?] and broad[?] land, that they might inherit the land of their fathers.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter states God's sovereignty over kings in brief: kings are not appointed without his will, the poor are not raised without his command, and the mighty prevail not without his will. David received grace, Solomon wisdom, Hezekiah added years, Goliath's days were shortened, and Samson was given strength and then weakened, David himself saved from Goliath, Saul and Kal'u because he kept God's commandment, and to all who keep it God gives strength, victory and the land of inheritance sworn to their fathers. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
