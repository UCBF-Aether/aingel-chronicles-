Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The serpent and Satan are distinguished in the chapter's argument: God remembered the serpent in one way and did not remember Satan in the same way concerning the desire that was joined. Ezekiel, Daniel, the three youths, and Ezra are cited, and the chapter speaks of the earth not remaining beneath darkness.
