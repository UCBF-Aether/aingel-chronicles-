The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — 1 Enoch 55 (R. H. Charles, The Book of Enoch (London: SPCK, 1917; reprint of his 2nd edition, 1912); Project Gutenberg no. 77935)

1 And after that the Head of Days repented and said: ‘In vain have I destroyed all who dwell on the earth.’

2 And He sware by His great name: ‘Henceforth I will not do so to all who dwell on the earth, and I will set a sign in the heaven: and this shall be a pledge of good faith between Me and them for ever, so long as heaven is above the earth. And this is in accordance with My command.’

*Final Judgement of Azâzêl, the Watchers and their children.*

3 ‘When I have desired to take hold of them by the hand of the angels on the day of tribulation and pain because of this, I will cause My chastisement and My wrath to abide upon them, saith God, the Lord of Spirits.

4 Ye †mighty kings† who dwell on the earth, ye shall have to behold Mine Elect One, how he sits on the throne of glory and judges Azâzêl, and all his associates, and all his hosts in the name of the Lord of Spirits.’

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

In plain language, this short chapter turns from destruction to promise and then to judgement. The Head of Days says He will not again destroy all who dwell on the earth, and sets a sign in heaven as a pledge of good faith for ever. The chapter then turns to the mighty kings, who will have to watch the Elect One sit on the throne of glory and judge Azâzêl, his associates, and his hosts. Interpreted Plain-language summary of the chapter text in the Text panel above

Unfamiliar terms

  • The Head of Days. Interpreted The name used in this chapter for God enthroned in glory (verses 1–2). 1 Enoch 55:1–2, Text panel
  • The Elect One. Interpreted The figure seated on the throne of glory who judges Azâzêl and his hosts (verse 4). 1 Enoch 55:4, Text panel
  • Azâzêl. Unresolved Named here as one to be judged; this chapter alone does not give his fuller history. 1 Enoch 55:4, Text panel
