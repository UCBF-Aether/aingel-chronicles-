The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Zechariah 6 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 And I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came four chariots out from between two mountains; and the mountains were mountains of brass.

2 In the first chariot were red horses; and in the second chariot black horses;

3 And in the third chariot white horses; and in the fourth chariot grisled and bay horses.

4 Then I answered and said unto the angel that talked with me, What are these, my lord?

5 And the angel answered and said unto me, These are the four spirits of the heavens, which go forth from standing before the LORD of all the earth.

6 The black horses which are therein go forth into the north country; and the white go forth after them; and the grisled go forth toward the south country.

7 And the bay went forth, and sought to go that they might walk to and fro through the earth: and he said, Get you hence, walk to and fro through the earth. So they walked to and fro through the earth.

8 Then cried he upon me, and spake unto me, saying, Behold, these that go toward the north country have quieted my spirit in the north country.

9 And the word of the LORD came unto me, saying,

10 Take of them of the captivity, even of Heldai, of Tobijah, and of Jedaiah, which are come from Babylon, and come thou the same day, and go into the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah;

11 Then take silver and gold, and make crowns, and set them upon the head of Joshua the son of Josedech, the high priest;

12 And speak unto him, saying, Thus speaketh the LORD of hosts, saying, Behold the man whose name is The BRANCH; and he shall grow up out of his place, and he shall build the temple of the LORD:

13 Even he shall build the temple of the LORD; and he shall bear the glory, and shall sit and rule upon his throne; and he shall be a priest upon his throne: and the counsel of peace shall be between them both.

14 And the crowns shall be to Helem, and to Tobijah, and to Jedaiah, and to Hen the son of Zephaniah, for a memorial in the temple of the LORD.

15 And they that are far off shall come and build in the temple of the LORD, and ye shall know that the LORD of hosts hath sent me unto you. And this shall come to pass, if ye will diligently obey the voice of the LORD your God.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Four chariots come out from between two mountains of brass, red, black, white, and grisled and bay horses, which are the four spirits of the heavens going forth from standing before the LORD of all the earth, the black to the north country and the grisled to the south, quieting God's spirit in the north. Then silver and gold from the captivity are made into crowns and set on Joshua the high priest, as a sign of the man whose name is the BRANCH, who will build the temple of the LORD, bear the glory, and sit and rule upon his throne as a priest upon his throne, with the counsel of peace between them both, and those far off will come and build in the temple. Interpreted Study notes for this chapter, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. And I turned, and lifted up mine eyes, and looked, and, behold, there came four chariots out from between two mountains, and the mountains were mountains of brass, because the eighth vision shows four chariots issuing between brass mountains, the fixed decrees of God, from which his judgments ride out. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 2. In the first chariot were red horses, and in the second chariot black horses, because the chariots are distinguished by colour, red for war and black for famine and mourning, as the judgments differ. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 3. And in the third chariot white horses, and in the fourth chariot grisled and bay horses, because white signifies victory and peace, while the grisled and bay, mottled and strong, signify mingled judgments. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 4. Then I answered and said unto the angel that talked with me, what are these, my lord, because again the prophet asks, and the pattern of question and answer carries the interpretation. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 5. And the angel answered and said unto me, these are the four spirits of the heavens, which go forth from standing before the Lord of all the earth, because the chariots are the four winds, God's ministers, going forth from his presence to execute his will over the earth. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 6. The black horses which are therein go forth into the north country, and the white go forth after them, and the grisled go forth toward the south country, because the judgments are assigned their quarters, black and white to the north, toward Babylon, and grisled to the south, toward Egypt. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 7. And the bay went forth, and sought to go that they might walk to and fro through the earth, and he said, get you hence, walk to and fro through the earth, so they walked to and fro through the earth, because the strong bay horses are sent to patrol the whole earth, not one quarter only. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 8. Then cried he upon me, and spoke unto me, saying, behold, these that go toward the north country have quieted my spirit in the north country, because the judgments on Babylon have quieted God's spirit, satisfying the anger that Babylon provoked. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 9. And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying, because the visions give way to a word of action, a symbolic crowning to follow the chariots. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 10. Take of them of the captivity, even of Heldai, of Tobijah, and of Jedaiah, which are come from Babylon, and come thou the same day, and go into the house of Josiah the son of Zephaniah, because the prophet is to take silver and gold from the newly arrived exiles and go the same day to Josiah's house, where the crowning will take place. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 11. Then take silver and gold, and make crowns, and set them upon the head of Joshua the son of Josedech, the high priest, because crowns of silver and gold are set on the high priest's head, joining priestly and kingly honour in one person, as a sign of the Branch. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 12. And speak unto him, saying, thus speaks the Lord of hosts, saying, behold the man whose name is The BRANCH, and he shall grow up out of his place, and he shall build the temple of the Lord, because the crowning points beyond Joshua to the Branch, who grows up from his place and builds the true temple. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 13. Even he shall build the temple of the Lord, and he shall bear the glory, and shall sit and rule upon his throne, and he shall be a priest upon his throne, and the counsel of peace shall be between them both, because the Branch both builds and bears glory, sitting as king and priest on one throne, with a counsel of peace between the two offices. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 14. And the crowns shall be to Helem, and to Tobijah, and to Jedaiah, and to Hen the son of Zephaniah, for a memorial in the temple of the Lord, because the crowns are laid up in the temple as a memorial to the donors, a witness that the Branch was expected and honoured. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 15. And they that are far off shall come and build in the temple of the Lord, and ye shall know that the Lord of hosts has sent me unto you, and this shall come to pass, if ye will diligently obey the voice of the Lord your God, because those far off will come to build, and the whole promise stands upon diligent obedience to God's voice. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

Unfamiliar terms

  • The BRANCH. the figure promised in Zechariah 3 and 6; the study notes say the crowning of Joshua points beyond Joshua to the Branch, who grows up from his place, builds the temple of the LORD, and sits as priest upon his throne.
