The Text — Josippon 36 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 And if he be kept back from His glory and His holiness, the power of God forsaketh them, and they are strengthened [?]: nay, our Lord Jesus Christ — and for the sake of Adam and his seed He went down by leave into sin; and our Lord descended into Sheol, and brought up their souls, and took them out from it, and brought them back into Paradise; for from faith it was that they did sin without their desire, by leave; and unclean spirits were set over them, and made them do sin without [?] their desire, as it was before the baptism of our Christianity; and no one [?] can bring them in, save by the power of the arm [?] of the Holy Spirit; for our Lord Jesus Christ and the Holy Spirit: for a man [?] as he is found pure (?), who with Him one hundred and four hundred [?] took (?) his likeness; and he whose likeness is one hundred and four hundred — and he whose likeness is one hundred and four hundred could not hasten unto him whose likeness is one hundred and four hundred, save by the power of the Holy Spirit, when the power stood still (?).

2 And Paul the apostle saith in the epistle to the land of Rome:

3 For by a man who hath not the Holy Spirit, sin is wrought without desire; and he cannot do that which is God's, and that which is fitting, that thou mayest know: now, lo, thou livest, and therein is the Holy Spirit; and we are loosed by the law, when He dwelleth therein, and we hasten to do the will of God without desire; as therein, if there be found from us, sin is wrought without desire.

4 And I will show thee, beloved: lo, thou livest therein; the Spirit [?] is holy; and I beseech thee by our Lord [?] Jesus Christ, that thou shouldest seal [?] the doing, and teach them all [?] who say, from after the baptism; and He took a body which had no [?], and was reckoned with Peter and Paul, His holy apostles.

5 And if thou dost ask, and hast sealed, and hast done in Him; and if thou teach not him who cannot — from after the baptism.

6 And the Holy Spirit departeth from thee, and delivereth thee unto the hosts of the Devil, that he may destroy thee; for thou hast known the good, and hast taught thy fellow, the faithful.

7 And if he who asked thee concerning this — thou desirest to know that the Spirit neither eateth sin, nor drinketh water, nor tasteth the savour of the world.

8 And be not glad with the gladness of food and drink.

9 And the savour thereof, and the joy thereof: the glory of God and His holiness — hearing the voice of the eating one.

10 As the LORD said in the Law and in the holy Gospel: For man liveth not by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD; and the flesh liveth by bread, as a beast.

11 And the spirit liveth by the word of the LORD, as the angels; and if a man, when he sinneth — he who eateth and drinketh — his flesh dieth; for the flame which was created therein speaketh blood, and his flesh dieth; and likewise, when he sinneth — the praise of man, the glory of God and His holiness — and he heareth His voice, the spirit dieth from the presence of God; for the satans seize it, and are burnt by deed, and the Holy Spirit departeth from it.

12 And if thou desirest to know the enquiry of this: remember the work of the Devil and his hosts — those who were spirits of power from the former angels, three — and when they were kept back from the glory of God and His holiness, sin prevailed over them, and the grace of the Holy Spirit departed from them; unto eternal darkness they fell.

13 Likewise, my beloved, he that is kept back from all, and forsaketh all: the unclean spirits and the hosts of the Devil are driven out from him, and they cannot prevail (?) at all, nor be able (?) over him at any time; and the grace of the Holy Spirit is wrought for the doing of the will of God, without desire (?); beloved, there is none who can do the will of God, without the being of the Holy Spirit within him.

14 And the Holy Spirit is not strongly (?) dwelling in him apart from God and His holiness always; and hearing His voice in its season: for God is merciful; He knew that man sharpeneth the life of the spirit; and the life of the flesh cannot be His (?) unto God and His holiness; and hearing His voice in its season, the grace of the Holy Spirit departeth, as it departed from the Devil and his hosts, and forsaketh them, that they may do that wherein they please (?)

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter continues the reflection on glory forsaken: if kept back from God's glory and holiness, his power forsakes them. It counsels against gladness in food and drink alone, and speaks of the Holy Spirit dwelling with the hearer, God's voice heard in its season, and God's mercy.
