Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter argues the union of Godhead and flesh by the analogy of soul and body: as soul and body are one man, so the incarnation is one, and the exposition of those who believe in two natures, two wills and two operations is rejected. It closes by naming the assemblies that hold this faith, Syrians and Armenians and Jacobites, and a fragment about Israel and the Syrians.
