Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

At 131 verses this is the longest chapter in the book, and it is almost entirely a baptismal order with many repeated liturgical blocks. It opens with offerings and the giving of names, examination of candidates' lives, books and psalms, and prayers for those who give their names, calling them from darkness into light. The water prayers recall creation, man's making in God's image, Paradise and the fall through the envy of Satan, and the Son sent not to judge but to save the world, asking that the water be sanctified and filled with the Holy Spirit for the second birth. The rite itself — facing east, adjuring Satan, confessing the Trinity thrice, baptism in the Jordan in the name of Father, Son and Holy Spirit, anointing with the seal of the Holy Spirit, laying on of the hand — is given several times over with variants, and the chapter closes with the blessing of fruits and with fast rules for pregnant women, the sick, the Passover season and after Pentecost.
