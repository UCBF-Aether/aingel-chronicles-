The Text — 4 Baruch 2 (Ge'ez text (project Ge'ez corpus))

1 And Jeremiah rent his garments, and cast ashes upon his head, and entered into the house of the sanctuary.

2 And Baruch saw Jeremiah [with] ashes strewn upon his head, and his garments rent; and he cried with a loud voice, saying: My father Jeremiah, what aileth thee? and what sin have the people committed? For when the people sinned, Jeremiah mourned, and cast ashes upon his head, and prayed for the people, that their iniquity might be forgiven them.

3 And Baruch asked him, saying: My father Jeremiah, what aileth thee, and what is become of the people?

4 And Jeremiah said unto him: Take heed that we rend not our garments, but let us rend our hearts; and let us put no water into the basins, that we may weep bitterly [?] until we fill them with our tears: for from henceforth he will have no mercy on this people.

5 And Baruch said: My father Jeremiah, what aileth thee?

6 And Jeremiah said unto him: God will deliver the city into the hand of the king of the Chaldeans; for he shall lead the people captive in affliction [?].

7 And when Baruch heard all this, he rent his garments, and said: My father Jeremiah, who hath shewed thee this? [?]

8 And Jeremiah said unto him: Abide with me until the sixth hour of the night, that thou mayest know that the word is true.

9 And they abode in the house of the sanctuary, weeping.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Jeremiah rends his garments, casts ashes on his head, and enters the house of the sanctuary; Baruch sees him so, and the two abide in the sanctuary weeping. The chapter is grief without action: the city is about to fall, and the prophet and his scribe can only mourn together in the holy place before what follows.
