Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A fragmentary chapter of council grief and dispute: John in Alexandria, a disputed royal message and book, the reign of Anastasius, a rival bishop set in Alexandria, rejection of the Chalcedon faith followed by killings with the sword, believers remaining in their provinces, and a closing fragment about Satan increasing hatred among men.
