Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter pictures the day of judgment in a long litany, day of darkness, distress, fear and trembling, lightning, when the evil shall be judged as they did evil and the pure as they did purity, when master is not honoured above servant nor great above small, and the doers of good rejoice while evildoers grieve and weep with sorrow that has no end. The righteous, who transgressed not and knew they shall rise, inherit the land of life promised to their fathers; the evildoers who believed not the resurrection face a worm that dies not, unquenched fire, smoke, tempest and hail.
