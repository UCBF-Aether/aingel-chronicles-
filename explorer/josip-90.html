Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter continues the testimonies, belief that Jesus is the Son of God overcoming the world, laying down life to take it again, the Pilate trial, crucifixion on Friday with the cry “Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani” and the psalm of pierced hands and feet and parted garments, resurrection and Thomas after eight days, and the purpose statement that these things are written that readers may believe and obtain life in his name. It then opens a third discourse on persecution, broken idols, and a council in the days of Diocletian and Constantine.
