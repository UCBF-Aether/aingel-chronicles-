Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Continuing the exposition of Moses' song, this chapter dwells on the LORD's right hand, the only-begotten Son, atonement, and the sea covering Pharaoh's hosts, and applies each image to Christ casting the Devil and his hosts down and the soul descending into hell. The application is often broken by [?], but the parallel, sea for hell and Pharaoh for the Devil, runs through the whole chapter.
