Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter treats baptism and its guarding: the baptised believer who confesses Christ as Son of God is not parted from the Holy Spirit, and heavenly angels guard him, while unclean spirits and the fear of death belong to the Devil. Paul to the Hebrews is cited for Christ as the destroyer of the power of death, Christ abolishes death and is the firstfruits of resurrection, and the chapter ends in a fragment about the angel of death and a province.
