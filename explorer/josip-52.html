The Text — Josippon 52 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 For the Satimaeans(?) and the Canaanites(?): Jonan. These — Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, had dominion over them, and blinded them, and laid waste their strongholds, and despoiled their sanctuary.

2 And Babylon remained, its exaltation [cast down]; and God cast them into the land of Jerusalem by the kingdom of Persia, after(?) the kingdom of Babylon; and he built the stronghold(?), and it served for their worship, and for their tables(?), and their feasts.

3 And they were made subject(?) unto the command of the Jonanites; few of them were Romans [?], as God had made covenant with me; and many of them became a portion unto the adversaries of Rome; and they sent unto them, and declared unto them.

4 The glory of their beauty(?) — and they say unto them: Know ye, God hath made covenant with me, that He said unto us: Our Christ, our King(?), shall confirm us from you, and shall reign over you. And Rome used to go forth against them, when they heard from them this word.

5 Yet they used to serve idols, and all the nations.

6 And when our Lord Christ was born — the Canaanites — and she was made subject unto the command of Rome, [which] abideth in Rome. And he appointed, from his prophet, an appointed one in the city of Jerusalem and in its district; and when [the word](?) of Rome [came], that Israel should serve — thinking that these [men], if the Christians should come, He would deliver them from them — in Bethlehem, in the kingdom of Rome, a star was seen(?); Magi came from the country of the east.

7 They knew not that verily the King of the Jews was born; and they followed him, on the way to go after him, seeking [him] for two years. And when he came unto the city of Jerusalem, in [?], and therein on the way he preached and said: Where is He that is born King of the Jews?

8 For behold, we have seen his star in the east, and we are come to worship him. And Herod was troubled exceedingly, when he heard that the King of the Jews was born.

9 And he remembered that which remained of the portions of the Jews who spake with him concerning him. And Herod brought the Jews and asked them where they said that the prophets [had spoken] — as, as ye have asked and inquired — as the prophet Mnias said unto them:

10 That He should be born in Bethlehem. And he brought the men; and there was not left [word] unto the king of the Jews, who would destroy them, for Rome.

11 And when they went forth from Jerusalem, until the star appeared unto them(?), and went before them, until it brought them(?) unto Bethlehem, the place(?) where Christ was; and they worshipped Him; and He commanded them in peace that they should not return unto Herod.

12 And they went by another way unto their own country; and the angel of the LORD appeared unto Joseph in her vision, and commanded that he should go with her and with the child unto the land of Egypt, and should declare [it].

13 The Magi — and he asked them: How long(?) is the time since(?) ye saw the star? And they said: Two years. And he said unto them: Go unto Bethlehem, and seek for the child; and if ye find him, come and tell me.

14 [?] came, that he might come and worship him(?). And Joseph told them this in writing; he feared(?) he would slay them; he abode until Herod died. And Herod, when he knew that the Magi men had gone(?) and returned not unto him, was wroth(?), and slew all the children of the Jews that were two years [old] and under(?) it, from ... (?), until it appeared(?) that it was so(?); and he slew thousands(?) of babes in Bethlehem, and all that were born.

15 And after the death of Herod, Joseph returned(?) by the command of the angel of the LORD unto the land of Jerusalem, and dwelt(?) in the city whose name is Nazareth, of the cities of Canaan, which entered in from Galilee; and Christ was brought up(?) therein likewise in every work, without sin; for He came that He might take away sin from on high, and work ... (?) from it; and He dwelt(?) in Nazareth, until His days became thirty years, as it is known(?), when He was created(?); and He sought(?) power for the Jews(?), Israel, His people(?), by wonders(?)

16 [by wonders], Christ. And the Jews used to think that He would destroy the Romans and reign over them in their land; and it was not(?) in their wisdom(?) any knowledge concerning [Him] — they call thee, and [her] hosts. And Christ used to go about, He and all the land of Judah, and with Him apostles and disciples; and none received Him, [saying] that He is the Christ; from the fruits of Rome it came to pass that He went; and He [sent] His disciples unto the city of Jerusalem for the feasts, in those days, one by one, which we mentioned before. Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter names peoples, the Satimaeans and Canaanites with Jonan, and recalls Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon, who had dominion over them, blinded them, and laid waste their land. Herod then brings the Jews and asks where Christ is to be found, while the Jews thought he would destroy the Romans and reign over them in their land, which the chapter says was not in their wisdom.
