The Text — Gәssәw 5 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 [Topic:] A bishop, an elder, and a deacon shall not mix themselves in with the doings of this world.

2 And if they mix themselves in, let them be separated [from the church].

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Short and absolute: a bishop, elder, or deacon must not mix himself in with the doings of this world, and if he does, he is to be separated from the church. The canon draws a line between clerical office and worldly entanglement without spelling out which doings it means. Interpreted Paraphrase of the chapter's own text; Standard corpus, Gessew 5
