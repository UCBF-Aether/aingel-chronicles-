Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 335, its number marked uncertain, concerns the laying of the hand upon Christians: after prayer the teacher lays his hand, prays and dismisses them, and those who will not hear instruction on conduct are brought forward and, if unwilling, sent away. The chapter then works through occupations: idol-makers, circus-goers, those who go to idol sacrifice, teachers of killing, charioteers, and craftsmen and keepers of idols must forsake their trade or be sent away — and even one committed to the rulers is not to do killing if so commanded.
