Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter opens by warning kings and rulers not to walk in the proud way, then retells Balat son of Sophor hiring Balaam to curse Israel as they came from Amalek toward Canaan: Balaam sacrifices and seeks a curse, but God turns curse into blessing, they that bless thee shall be blessed, and they that curse thee shall be cursed. Balat, wroth at blessing instead of cursing, is answered that Balaam cannot transgress God's word and speaks only what God puts in his mouth; the chapter closes with Israel's captivity, Jerusalem plundered and priests scattered, yet God sparing his people not for their sake but for Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
