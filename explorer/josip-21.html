Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This long discourse unit teaches that joy comes from remembrance, and reads the prophet's words on Christ bruising the Devil with his heel alongside his descent into Sheol and his ascension. Baptism is set in place of the Red Sea, freeing believers from servitude as Israel was freed from Pharaoh, and the chapter joins manna, the Bread of life, and water and blood from Christ's side to the rock that became the cornerstone.
