Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter follows a proud figure who goes about in evil, and recalls a day when the sun stood in the midst of heaven until the armies of the Canaanites, Hivites, Hittites, Perizzites, and Jebusites were destroyed — a past deliverance set against present pride. The man’s ways, the chapter says, are darkness, pride, and blood; he worships idols, eats what is forbidden, spares no one from the Sea of Tarshish to the Sea of Iyariko, and has no fear of God before his eyes. The close is a warning: the Lord will avenge himself in his time, the wicked shall be requited as they did evil, and the doers of good shall receive goodness from the Lord.
