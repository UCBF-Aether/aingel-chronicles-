The Text — 1 Book of the Covenant 43 (J. Cooper & A. J. Maclean, The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1902), public domain (archive.org cu31924029296170))

1 [Let her hymns of praise be said thus quietly :]

*NIGHT HYMN OF PRAISE OF WIDOWS*

2 Holy, holy, without spot, who hast Thy dwelling in the light, God of Abraham, and of Isaac, and of Jacob, God of Enoch and David, of Elijah, of Elisha, of Moses, of Joshua, and of the prophets and of the others who in truth preached Thy Name, God of the Apostles, the God who hast guided all things by Thy reason and hast blest them who lovingly trust in Thee; my soul praiseth Thee with the power of the spirit of my power, my heart praiseth Thee, O Lord, and Thy might, alway.

3 Let all my power praise Thee, O Lord, for if Thou wilt, J am Thine, O God, the God of the poor; for Thou art the Helper of them that lack, and Thou art He that looketh on the meek, and the Assister of the weak; assist me, O Lord, because by Thy grace Thou wast well pleased in me that I should be Thine handmaid, for Thou hast bestowed upon me a great name, that I should be called a Christian.

4 Thou who hast freed me from servitude that I may serve a servitude to God, the Mighty One who [art] for ever, who seest all, that I may praise Thee uncondemned. Yea, O Lord God, confirm my heart in Thee until it is perfected in the Holy Ghost.

5 Rejuvenate us for the edification of Thy holy Church, Son and Word and Thought of the Father, the Christ who camest for the salvation of the race of man, who didst suffer and wast buried, and didst rise, [and] also wast glorified by Him who sent Thee, turn, help, O Lord, set upright our thoughts by the strong faith of the Spirit. Glorify Thy Name in us.

6 For in Thy Father and in Thee and in the Holy Ghost is our hope for ever and ever.

7 With those who are with her let her say: Amen. But let her say the hymn of praise at dawn thus:

*HYMNS OF PRAISE AT DAWN OF WIDOWS WHO SIT IN FRONT*

8 O Eternal God, Guide of our souls, Maker of light, Treasure of life, who restest! in the praises and prayers of the holy ones, Lover of compassion merciful, kind, King of all, and God, our Lord, my spirit praiseth [Thee], sending [up] to Thee the unceasing voices of Thine handmaid, O Lord, who beseecheth Thee that Thou mayest perfect in Thine handmaid the spirit of reason and of piety and of right knowledge.

9 I praise Thee, O Lord, who didst take away from our poverty all disturbance and confusion, wrath and all contention and evil habit, who didst prepare [and] change the feelings of my understanding that I might serve Thee only, O God; who hast adorned Thy holy Church with various ministries, who drivest away from Thine handmaid all doubtfulness, fear, weakness ; and holdest the thoughts of those who rightly serve Thee ; I praise Thee, O God, who hast enlightened me with the light of Thy knowledge, through Thine Only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom [be] praise and might to Thee for ever and ever.

10 Amen?

11 And with those who are with her let her say: Amen.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter gives the widows' hymns of praise. The night hymn is to be said quietly: Holy, holy, without spot, who has His dwelling in the light, God of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, God of Enoch and David, of Elijah, of Elisha, of Moses, of Joshua, and of the prophets and of the others who in truth preached His Name, God of the Apostles, the God who guided all things by His reason and blessed those who lovingly trust in Him — my soul praises Thee with the power of the spirit of my power, my heart praises Thee, O Lord, and Thy might, always. Let all my power praise Thee, O Lord, for if Thou wilt, I am Thine, O God, the God of the poor; Thou art the Helper of them that lack, He that looks on the meek, the Assister of the weak — assist me, O Lord, because by Thy grace Thou wast well pleased in me that I should be Thy handmaid, for Thou hast bestowed on me a great name, that I should be called a Christian. Thou who hast freed me from servitude that I may serve a servitude to God, the Mighty One who is for ever, who sees all, that I may praise Thee uncondemned; yea, O Lord God, confirm my heart in Thee until it is perfected in the Holy Ghost. Rejuvenate us for the edification of Thy holy Church, Son and Word and Thought of the Father, the Christ who came for the salvation of the race of man, who suffered, was buried, rose and was glorified by Him who sent Thee — turn, help, O Lord, set upright our thoughts by the strong faith of the Spirit, and glorify Thy Name in us, for in Thy Father and in Thee and in the Holy Ghost is our hope for ever and ever. With those who are with her she says Amen. The hymn at dawn is then given: O Eternal God, Guide of our souls, Maker of light, Treasure of life, who restest in the praises and prayers of the holy ones, Lover of compassion, merciful, kind, King of all and God, our Lord — my spirit praises Thee, sending up to Thee the unceasing voices of Thy handmaid, beseeching Thee to perfect in Thy handmaid the spirit of reason, piety and right knowledge. I praise Thee, O Lord, who took away from our poverty all disturbance, confusion, wrath, contention and evil habit, who prepared and changed the feelings of my understanding that I might serve Thee only, O God, who adorned Thy holy Church with various ministries, drives away from Thy handmaid all doubtfulness, fear and weakness, and holds the thoughts of those who rightly serve Thee; I praise Thee, O God, who enlightened me with the light of Thy knowledge through Thy Only-begotten Son, the Lord Jesus Christ, by whom praise and might to Thee for ever and ever.
