Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A fragmentary teaching on the natures (earth, water, wind and fire), desire in Adam and Eve, washing and uncleanness until evening, and kingship loosened from the bond of the law when Christ came. Several verses are pointers rather than text (for example, verses 10, 12 and 14 direct the reader elsewhere), and the argument is often interrupted by [?], so only its general subject, purity and desire under the law, is secure.
