Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 53 concerns the death of bishops and the mourning canon, but most of the chapter is an ordination order: the people are asked three times whether they desire the chosen man as their chief, and answering Yea together, all lay hands on him while priests pray over the altar in silence and deacons read the Gospel. Bishops sit on thrones, greet all in the Lord and read the holy books, and after the Gospel one bishop speaks the grace of 2 Corinthians 13:14, answered And with thy spirit. The assembly is then ordered — only the faithful to stand within, greetings exchanged man with man and woman with woman, little children watched by a deacon, deacons standing at the doors of men and women, hands washed — and the chapter closes warning that one who keeps not the canon denies Christ and becomes an enemy of the faithful.
