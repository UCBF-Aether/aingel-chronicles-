The Text — Josippon 50 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 The second discourse, concerning the ordinances of the fast of the one day, Wednesday and Friday, which is the love of God: may He enlighten the eyes of thy heart by the light of the Holy Spirit, the Comforter, that thou mayest discern the mystery of the Godhead.

2 I have agreed with thee that I would reveal unto thee the ordinances of the fast of the one day, Wednesday and Friday; there is that which I have told thee; and know its meaning, and hold fast, as the fathers, the holy apostles, said in their canons — the seventh and the tenth — whoso fasteth not the one day —

3 Wednesday and Friday, and always he observeth(?)

4 — if he be of the priesthood [he is cut off]; and if he be a layman, he shall be driven from among the Christians.

5 He is severed and cursed: if he be a priest, [?], and a stranger, and his curse [is] from God; and if he fast not also the one day always — there is that which I have told thee — that thou mayest know the reason of this: when God brought forth the children of Israel (?)

6 the land of Egypt, as they journeyed in their going forth, [?] and afterwards He led them into the land of Canaan, which is the land of Jerusalem; and ye are in it; and He gave them days over the nations, while they dwelt in it, being sent, and kings in it. And there were in their borders nations, Canaanites, while they spread into all the coasts of the shore of the sea, from Gaza and Ashkelon unto the harbour of the sea of the Philistines, and Dan and the Canaanites.

7 And Israel used to offer, and was in the land(?), [?], unto the LORD; and (?) they offered(?) their offerings in another [place]; and it was without Him. And Solomon built a sanctuary in Jerusalem; and it was the first house of the sanctuary which was built in Jerusalem unto the LORD in all the earth; and he built therein an altar for the oblation; for the house of the sanctuary is the house of the Christians, and the mysteries [are] therein: they offer upon it offerings, and [there is] its sanctifying; unto it Israel used to offer, coming from all the land of Canaan, unto this house of the sanctuary, two times.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A second discourse on the fast of one day, Wednesday and Friday, opens as the love of God. Breach carries discipline: a priest is cut off and a layman driven from among the Christians. The chapter then turns to Israel's offerings in the land and to Solomon, though its closing lines survive only in fragments.
