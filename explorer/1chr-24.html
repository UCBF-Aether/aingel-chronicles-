The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — 1 Chronicles 24 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 Now these are the divisions of the sons of Aaron. The sons of Aaron; Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

2 But Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and had no children: therefore Eleazar and Ithamar executed the priest’s office.

3 And David distributed them, both Zadok of the sons of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their offices in their service.

4 And there were more chief men found of the sons of Eleazar than of the sons of Ithamar, and thus were they divided. Among the sons of Eleazar there were sixteen chief men of the house of their fathers, and eight among the sons of Ithamar according to the house of their fathers.

5 Thus were they divided by lot, one sort with another; for the governors of the sanctuary, and governors of the house of God, were of the sons of Eleazar, and of the sons of Ithamar.

6 And Shemaiah the son of Nethaneel the scribe, one of the Levites, wrote them before the king, and the princes, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and before the chief of the fathers of the priests and Levites: one principal household being taken for Eleazar, and one taken for Ithamar.

7 Now the first lot came forth to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah,

8 The third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim,

9 The fifth to Malchijah, the sixth to Mijamin,

10 The seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to Abijah,

11 The ninth to Jeshuah, the tenth to Shecaniah,

12 The eleventh to Eliashib, the twelfth to Jakim,

13 The thirteenth to Huppah, the fourteenth to Jeshebeab,

14 The fifteenth to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer,

15 The seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Aphses,

16 The nineteenth to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Jehezekel,

17 The one and twentieth to Jachin, the two and twentieth to Gamul,

18 The three and twentieth to Delaiah, the four and twentieth to Maaziah.

19 These were the orderings of them in their service to come into the house of the LORD, according to their manner, under Aaron their father, as the LORD God of Israel had commanded him.

20 And the rest of the sons of Levi were these: Of the sons of Amram; Shubael: of the sons of Shubael; Jehdeiah.

21 Concerning Rehabiah: of the sons of Rehabiah, the first was Isshiah.

22 Of the Izharites; Shelomoth: of the sons of Shelomoth; Jahath.

23 And the sons of Hebron; Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.

24 Of the sons of Uzziel; Michah: of the sons of Michah; Shamir.

25 The brother of Michah was Isshiah: of the sons of Isshiah; Zechariah.

26 The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi: the sons of Jaaziah; Beno.

27 The sons of Merari by Jaaziah; Beno, and Shoham, and Zaccur, and Ibri.

28 Of Mahli came Eleazar, who had no sons.

29 Concerning Kish: the son of Kish was Jerahmeel.

30 The sons also of Mushi; Mahli, and Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the sons of the Levites after the house of their fathers.

31 These likewise cast lots over against their brethren the sons of Aaron in the presence of David the king, and Zadok, and Ahimelech, and the chief of the fathers of the priests and Levites, even the principal fathers over against their younger brethren.

How to read the labels

Every substantive historical claim in the editorial panels below carries one of three labels, plus a source reference:

  • Documented — directly supported by a cited source.
  • Interpreted — a reasoned explanation or proposed relationship.
  • Unresolved — evidence is insufficient to establish the claim.

A claim does NOT become Documented merely because a commentary states it. Commentary views are attributed to their authors and labeled Interpreted unless a primary textual source directly supports the claim. Where evidence runs out, the page says Unresolved and stops — it does not fill the gap.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter lists the divisions of Aaron's sons, the priests, chosen by lot, and the corresponding divisions of the other Levites who served alongside them. Interpreted Editorial summary of this chapter’s text and study notes

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. These are the divisions of the sons of Aaron. Henry notes this is not division into variance, but distribution for dividing work: God, the God of order not confusion, gives every member place and work, especially in worship. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 2. But Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and had no children: therefore Eleazar and Ithamar executed the priest's office. Henry notes the priestly distribution descends through Eleazar and Ithamar after Nadab and Abihu's childless death. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 3. David distributed them, both Zadok of Eleazar's sons, and Ahimelech of Ithamar's sons, according to their offices in their service. Henry notes David, with Zadok and Ahimelech, divided the priests by their houses for regular discharge of office. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 4. There were more chief men found of Eleazar's sons than of Ithamar's: of Eleazar sixteen chief men of fathers' houses, of Ithamar eight. Henry explains Ithamar's house had dwindled since the sentence on Eli's family, which was of that house. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 5. Thus were they divided by lot, one sort with another, for the governors of the sanctuary, and governors of the house of God, were of Eleazar's sons and of Ithamar's. Henry notes the lot made disposal the Lord's, preventing quarrels and charges of partiality, as the lot causes contention to cease. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 6. Shemaiah son of Nethaneel the scribe, one of the Levites, wrote them before the king and princes, Zadok and Ahimelech, and the chief fathers, one principal household being taken for Eleazar, and one for Ithamar. Henry notes the lot was cast publicly and solemnly before king, princes, and priests, with Shemaiah as public notary, so no fraud or suspicion could arise. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 10. The seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to Abijah. Henry singles out the eighth course, Abijah, as Zechariah's course in Luke, showing David's courses continued in succession till the second temple's destruction. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 19. These were the orderings of them in their service to come into the house of the Lord, according to their manner, under Aaron their father, as the Lord God of Israel had commanded him. Henry notes each course was called by its founder's name, and the high priest is called Aaron, to be reverenced as father by inferior priests, as Christ is high priest over God's house. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 20. And the rest of the sons of Levi were these: of Amram's sons, Shubael, and of Shubael's sons, Jehdeiah. Henry notes these Levites, mostly named before, are here heads of twenty-four Levite courses attending the twenty-four priestly courses. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 31. These likewise cast lots over against their brethren the sons of Aaron in the presence of David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the chief fathers, the principal fathers over against their younger brethren. Henry notes principal fathers and younger brethren took place not by seniority but by lot, and priests are called brethren, not lords, over God's clergy. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry

Unfamiliar terms

  • courses. Divisions of priests and Levites, each named for its founder, serving in their appointed order in the house of the Lord. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry

Connections to other passages

  • The study note for verse 10 connects this passage to Luke's account of Zechariah, of the course of Abijah. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
