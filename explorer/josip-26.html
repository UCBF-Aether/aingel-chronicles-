Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A discourse of Paradise opens, introduced as a word written as a wonder among the Jews. Its thread is that God did not settle Adam and Eve in Paradise so that they might inherit the kingdom of heaven in that way, though the chapter's wording survives only in fragments.
