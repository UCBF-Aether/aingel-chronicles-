Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter teaches resurrection through grain and vine: a grain that dies not is not eaten and bears no fruit, but dying it takes root, rises as stalk and bears fruit, and so the vine, watered, rooted and ascending, gladdens the heart like wine, which the Psalm says gladdens the heart of man. The grain is likened to Adam, upon whom God's Spirit breathed; and God made the vine and its fruit that his name be blessed for those who do his will and believe the resurrection of the dead, whom he shall gladden in the abode of life.
