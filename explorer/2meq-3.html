Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The Lord sends the prophet to Meqabis with a hard word: it was not by his own strength that he destroyed the holy city, but by God's own will, and now, because of the children he has begotten, God will be gracious toward his sins if he repents with all his heart and forsakes idols. Meqabis bows to the prophet, clears his house of idols, has the children brought from Jerusalem taught God's commandment morning and midday, and sets wise teachers over them, and the chapter widens into Israel's own cycle: spurning God, chastised by oppressors, crying out, delivered by judges, then forgetting again.
