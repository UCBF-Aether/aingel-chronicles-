Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Addressed to proud man, thou art not God, the chapter warns that man is dust and ashes who shall become wood in his grave and go down into Gehenna, taught by Seblianos, who refused to bow to God's work and destroyed Adam in his pride. Yet God tries every person whether he does his will: if he makes his way straight, God blesses his labour, storehouse and pasture and subdues his enemies, while the sinners' way, darkness, violence, fornication, deceit, dead flesh and blood, is broad and leads to darkness for ever, and the righteous way, humility, love, peace, fasting, prayer and keeping from impure foods, leads into life. The final verse has no text in the source. Interpreted Chapter text
