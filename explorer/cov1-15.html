The Text — 1 Book of the Covenant 15 (J. Cooper & A. J. Maclean, The Testament of Our Lord, translated into English from the Syriac (Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1902), public domain (archive.org cu31924029296170))

1 Now after Jesus had spoken these words, Peter and John

2 Churches, and ordain for (impress upon) them the offices which suit each.

3 God who is your God.

4 and Thomas and Matthew and Andrew and Matthias and the rest said: O our Lord, truly Thou hast spoken to us now also words of warning and of truth, and though we are not worthy Thou hast bestowed upon us many things, and hast granted also to those of future generations who are worthy, to know Thy words and to flee from the snares of the Evil One.

5 But, O our Lord, we beseech Thee, make Thy perfect light to shine upon us, and upon those who are foreordained and separated to be Thine. Because! that we have many times asked Thee, we pray Thee teach us of what sort he ought to be who standeth at the head of the Church, or with what rule he should raise up and order the Church.

6 For it is urgent that when we are sent to the nations to preach the salvation which is from Thee, it should not escape us as to how it is fitting to arrange the mysteries of the Church. Therefore from Thy mouth, O our Saviour and Perfecter, we desire to learn without omission how the Chief of the holy things ought to please Thee, and [likewise] all those who minister in Thy Church.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

After Jesus has spoken these words of warning and truth, Peter, John, Thomas, Matthew, Andrew, Matthias and the rest thank Him for what He has bestowed on them and on worthy future generations, that they may know His words and flee the snares of the Evil One. They then ask Him to make His perfect light shine on them and on those foreordained and separated to be His, and to teach them of what sort he ought to be who stands at the head of the Church, and with what rule he should raise up and order the Church — urgent, they say, because they are to be sent to the nations to preach salvation and must know how to arrange the mysteries of the Church, learning from His mouth without omission how the Chief of the holy things ought to please Him, and likewise all who minister in His Church.
