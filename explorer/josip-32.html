Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Israel's deliverance by the hosts of God opens the chapter, which then joins the Holy Spirit as fire descending to the flesh of Christ until the morning, with the command not to break a bone. The chapter's wording is fragmentary, but its Passover imagery is the readable thread. Interpreted Chapter text as printed above; study-notes file for this chapter is silent
