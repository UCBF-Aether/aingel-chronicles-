The Text — Kebra Nagast 71 (E. A. Wallis Budge, The Queen of Sheba and Her Only Son Menyelek (1922), public domain, for chs 1–118; Ge'ez text for ch. 119)

*71. CONCERNING MARY, THE DAUGHTER OF DAVID*

1 And from this it is evident that Mary was the daughter of David, and that Joseph was the son of David. Therefore was Mary betrothed to Joseph her kinsman, as it is said in the Gospel, "O Joseph, son of David, fear thou not to take to wife Mary thy betrothed, for that which is to be born of her is of the Holy Spirit, the Word of God." And there was born of her God, the Word, Light of Light, God of God, Son of the Father, Who came and delivered His creation; from the hand of Sheôl, and from Satan, and from death He hath delivered all of us who have believed in Him, He hath drawn us to His Father and hath raised us up into heaven His throne to become His heirs; for He is a lover of man, and unto Him praise belongeth for ever. Amen.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This short chapter argues, as the book presents it, that Mary was the daughter of David and Joseph the son of David, so that Mary was betrothed to Joseph her kinsman. It quotes the Gospel words to Joseph, son of David, not to fear taking Mary, since what is born of her is of the Holy Spirit, the Word of God, and closes by calling him Light of Light, God of God, Son of the Father.
