Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This short chapter concerns the bread of blessing when the bishop speaks to all: if there is no bishop, the faithful take the bread of blessing from the priest, or from the deacon if there is no priest, and the bishop's asking and teaching are described in broken clauses. If the faithful alone are at the liturgy, they take the eulogia from the priest's or deacon's hand, but laymen by themselves are to keep silence and not make the eulogia — the chapter's point is that the blessed bread comes through an ordained hand, not from laymen alone.
