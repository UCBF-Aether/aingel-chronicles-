Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A bishop who does not instruct or consider the people given to him is to be separated until he learns to instruct, and the same applies to an elder and a deacon. But if the failure lies with the people rather than with him, he remains bishop while the priests of the city are separated for not admonishing the people as was fitting. The canon asks whose fault the failure is before it assigns the penalty. Interpreted Paraphrase of the chapter's own text; Standard corpus, Gessew 26
