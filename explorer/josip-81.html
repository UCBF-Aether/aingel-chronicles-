Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter opens with the love of God shining by the Holy Spirit and the mystery of the cross, and a note on swaddling and the eighth day. Verse 4 is a pointer, not text: it states that the text of that verse stands in chapter 80, verses 11–20, and is not duplicated here. The chapter then treats judgment, in which believers' souls ascend to paradise rather than descending to Sheol, and closes with the Beatitude of the poor in spirit and the strait gate.
