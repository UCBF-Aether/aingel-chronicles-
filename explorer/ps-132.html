The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Psalms 132 (King James Version (1611) for Psalms 1–150; Charles Thomson, The Septuagint (1808) for Psalm 151)

1 Lord, remember David, and all his afflictions:

2 How he sware unto the LORD, and vowed unto the mighty God of Jacob;

3 Surely I will not come into the tabernacle of my house, nor go up into my bed;

4 I will not give sleep to mine eyes, or slumber to mine eyelids,

5 Until I find out a place for the LORD, an habitation for the mighty God of Jacob.

6 Lo, we heard of it at Ephratah: we found it in the fields of the wood.

7 We will go into his tabernacles: we will worship at his footstool.

8 Arise, O LORD, into thy rest; thou, and the ark of thy strength.

9 Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy.

10 For thy servant David’s sake turn not away the face of thine anointed.

11 The LORD hath sworn in truth unto David; he will not turn from it; Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne.

12 If thy children will keep my covenant and my testimony that I shall teach them, their children shall also sit upon thy throne for evermore.

13 For the LORD hath chosen Zion; he hath desired it for his habitation.

14 This is my rest for ever: here will I dwell; for I have desired it.

15 I will abundantly bless her provision: I will satisfy her poor with bread.

16 I will also clothe her priests with salvation: and her saints shall shout aloud for joy.

17 There will I make the horn of David to bud: I have ordained a lamp for mine anointed.

18 His enemies will I clothe with shame: but upon himself shall his crown flourish.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A prayer that begins "Lord, remember David, and all his afflictions" — above all David's oath to the mighty God of Jacob that he would give no sleep to his eyes until he found a place for the LORD, a habitation for the God of Jacob. The ark, heard of at Ephratah and found in the fields of the wood, is called to arise into God's rest with the ark of his strength, priests clothed with righteousness and saints shouting for joy. For David's servant's sake the psalm asks God not to turn away the face of his anointed, recalling the LORD's sworn promise that fruit of David's body would sit on his throne, if his children keep God's covenant and testimony. The second half answers in God's voice: the LORD has chosen Zion, desired it for his habitation, calls it his rest for ever, promises to bless her provision and satisfy her poor with bread, clothe her priests with salvation, make the horn of David bud with a lamp ordained for his anointed, clothe his enemies with shame, and let his crown flourish. Interpreted Psalm text above; study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. Henry pleads Solomon's dedication plea, not his own merit — I am not worthy, but Lord remember David with whom thou madest covenant, as Moses prayed Remember Abraham first trustee (Exod 32:13); remember all afflictions, troubles of life occasioned by anointing, or care and concern about ark, uneasiness ark in curtains (2 Sam 7:2), humility meekness reading, pious devout affection of vow; not amiss to put God in mind of predecessors in profession, afflictions services sufferings, covenant, experiences of goodness, care and prayers for those after; apply to Christ Son of David and all his afflictions — remember covenant made with him and satisfaction made by him, remember all his offerings (Ps 20:3), all his sufferings. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 2. Henry binds vow to Lord, Mighty God of Jacob: vows made to God party as well as witness; Lord Mighty One of Jacob, Jacob's God mighty, power engaged for Jacob's defence deliverance, Jacob weak but God of Jacob mighty. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 3. Henry refuses settlement in bed till matter brought to head: thing long talked nothing done, till David one morning about public business vowed before night come to resolution and determine place either tent pitched for ark at beginning of reign or rather where Solomon should build temple, not fixed till latter end after pestilence for numbering people (1 Chr 22:1, This is house of Lord), perhaps occasion of judgment apprehensive controversy for dilatoriness; when needful work for God good task ourselves tie to time apt to put off, morning cut out work for day binding will do before sleep only with submission to Providence knowing not what day brings, especially great work of conversion must be solicitous zealous, resolve not enjoy comforts of life till laid foundation for hopes of better. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 4. Henry gives no sleep to eyes nor slumber to eyelids until place found — same intentness as v3, refusing rest while God's house unsettled, urgency carried into night watches. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 5. Henry finds place for Lord, habitation for Mighty One of Jacob: observed law frequent mention of place God would choose to put name there, all tribes resort; at crown no such place, Shiloh deserted none pitched, feasts not kept with due solemnity; David will find general rendezvous of all tribes, place of habitation, place for ark with room both priests and people to attend. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 6. Henry inquires after ark: people lamented its obscurity (1 Sam 7:2), heard of it at Ephratah — Shiloh in tribe of Ephraim, told it had been but gone; found at last in fields of wood — Kirjath-jearim city of woods; thence all Israel fetched with great solemnity beginning of David's reign (1 Chr 13:6), so building house for ark Solomon gratified all Israel, needed not go about seek ark anymore, now knew where to find it. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 7. Henry resolves attendance: let us but have convenient place and we will go into his tabernacle pay homage there, worship at footstool as subjects and suppliants, neglected for want of such place in days of Saul (1 Chr 13:3). Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 8. Henry prays God vouchsafe not only take possession but take up residence in temple built: Arise O Lord into thy rest, let this be it, thou even ark of thy strength, pledge of presence, mighty presence; with these words Solomon concluded prayer (2 Chr 6:41). Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 9. Henry clothes priests: give grace ministers to do duty, appear righteous both administrations and conversations according to rule; righteousness best ornament of minister, holiness towards God goodness towards all men habits of no dispute, thy priests discredit relation if not clothed righteousness; saints shout for joy did so when ark brought into city of David (2 Sam 6:15), will do so when priests clothed righteousness, faithful ministry joy of saints matter friend furtherance, helpers of your joy (2 Cor 1:24). Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 10. Henry pleads anointed not turned away: deny me not things asked, send me not away ashamed; anointed of Lord pleaded as type of Christ great anointed who in intercession urges designation, God's anointed therefore Father hears always; son of David — for his sake do not deny me, Christian's plea for sake of Christ our David in whom well pleased accept me, David signifies beloved made accepted in beloved, God's servant whom he upholds (Isa 42:1); no merit our own, for his sake in whom fulness of merit find favour, pray prosperity of church boldly for Christ's sake who purchased church with own blood, let ministers and people do duty. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 11. Henry swears in truth unto David will not turn: precious promises confirmed by oath heirs strong consolation (Heb 6:17-18), pleas or answers both ways, speak to God and hear what God will speak; God chose David's family royal confirmed by oath, David type of Christ made king with oath Lord sworn will not repent; did David swear to Lord find him house? Lord swore to David build him house, God behind with none in affections assurances; promise long succession kings from loins fruit of body set upon throne fulfilled Solomon, David lived to see with satisfaction (1 Kings 1:48); everlasting successor increase of government peace no end, Peter applies to Christ, David himself so understood (Acts 2:30) fruit of loins according flesh raise up Christ sit on throne, fulness of time gave throne of father David (Luke 1:32), he fulfilled condition kept covenant testimony did Father's will pleased him, therefore to him and spiritual seed promise made good, he and children God given all believers sit throne for evermore (Rev 3:21). Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 12. Henry conditions entail: if children in following ages keep covenant and testimony God shall teach them — God engaged teach, did part, Moses prophets, all expects keep what taught and keep to it, then children sit throne for evermore; kings before God upon good behaviour, commission quamdiu se bene gesserint during good behaviour; issue they did not keep covenant, entail at length cut off, sceptre departed from Judah by degrees; Christ fulfilled condition, so promise stands in him. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 13. Henry chooses Zion: God chose Mount Zion which he loved (Ps 78:68) for habitation of ark, said This is my rest for ever not merely residence for time as Shiloh; Zion city of David chose royal because God chose holy; God said Here will I dwell therefore David Here will I dwell, principle good for me to be near God; Zion type of gospel-church Mount Zion (Heb 12:22), full accomplishment there, Zion long since ploughed as field but church of Christ house of living God (1 Tim 3:15), his rest for ever blessed presence always even to end of world, delight in church and continuance of presence comfort joy of members. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 14. Henry rests for ever: Here will I dwell for I have desired it — same choice as v13 in God's own voice, rest not temporary, dwelling desired, presence abiding. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 15. Henry blesses provision abundantly: earth yield increase, where religion set up provision, blessing God will bless it (Ps 67:6) surely abundantly; little provision with abundant blessing more serviceable comfortable than great deal without; God's people special blessing upon common enjoyments peculiar sweetness; further satisfy her poor with bread — Zion has own poor to keep, God take care even them, providence kept from wanting provision enough, scarcity poor first feel so sure sign plenty if sufficient, obliged all sons of Zion charitable according ability church take care not neglected (Acts 6:1); grace kept from complaining though dry bread yet satisfied, Zion's poor reason content little world because better things prepared; spiritually provision for soul in word ordinances abundantly bless nourishment of new man, satisfy poor in spirit with bread of life, what God sanctifies may be satisfied with. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 16. Henry answers prayer v9 with more: desired priests clothed righteousness, promised God will clothe them with salvation — not only save them but make them and administrations instrumental for salvation of people, both save themselves and those hear them, add to church saved; whom God clothes righteousness will clothe salvation, pray righteousness then with it God gives salvation; desired saints shout for joy, promised shall shout aloud for joy, God gives more than ask, when gives salvation gives abundant joy. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 17. Henry buds horn of David in Zion: royal dignity increase more and more, constant additions to lustre; Christ horn of salvation denoting plentiful powerful salvation raised up made to bud in house of servant David; David promised use power for God's glory cut off horns wicked exalt horns righteous (Ps 75:10), in recompence God promises make horn bud, to those that have power use well more shall be given; lasting honour lamp ordained for anointed — Thou wilt light my candle (Ps 18:28), lamp likely burn brightly which God ordains, lamp successor when almost out another lighted by it, succession David shall not want man stand before God, Christ lamp and light of world. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
  • Verse 18. Henry shames enemies and flourishes crown: enemies formed designs clothed with shame when see designs baffled, enemies of all good governors expect clothed shame, especially enemies of Lord Jesus and government shall rise great day to everlasting shame contempt; upon himself crown flourish — government more and more his honour, full accomplishment Jesus Christ crown honour power never fade nor flowers wither, crowns earthly princes endure not to all generations (Prov 27:24) but Christ's crown endure to all eternity and crowns reserved faithful subjects fade not away. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry (Complete)
