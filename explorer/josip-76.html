Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter makes Satan the adversary of Adam and all his seed after the transgression and expulsion from Paradise, and treats music, drum, horn, lyre and psaltery, as the means by which Satan deceived men. Daniel's counsel about Nebuchadnezzar and the righteous of Israel is cited for the same pattern, and the names of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob appear in a fragment about faith and hell.
