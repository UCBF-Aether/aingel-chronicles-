The Text — 4 Baruch 4 (Ge'ez text (project Ge'ez corpus))

1 And in the morning the host of the Chaldeans compassed the city; and a great angel blew the trumpet, saying: Enter ye, O host of the Chaldeans; behold, the gates are opened unto you.

2 Then the king entered with his army, and they took all the people captive.

3 And then Jeremiah took the keys of the house of the sanctuary, and went forth out of the city, and cast these keys before the sun, saying: Unto thee I speak, O sun: take the keys of the house of God, and keep them until the day wherein the Lord shall require them of thee; for we were not found worthy to keep them, for we have been found unfaithful in our sins [?].

4 And while Jeremiah was yet weeping for the people, they brought him forth and dragged him, and led him away with the people unto Babylon.

5 But Baruch took ashes and cast them upon his head, and abode and wailed this wailing, saying:

6 Wherefore is Jerusalem made desolate [?]? but for the sin of the beloved people was she delivered into the hand of her enemies, for our sins and for the sins of the people;

7 but let not the sinners boast, neither let them say: We were able to take the city of God by our might; it was not by your strength that ye prevailed against her, but for our sins was she delivered unto you;

8 and our God will have mercy upon us, and bring us again into our city; but as for you, there shall be no life. Blessed are our fathers, Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob; for they departed out of this world, and saw not the destruction of this city.

9 And after he had spoken this, he went forth weeping, and said: I mourn for thee, O Jerusalem. And he went out of the city, and abode in a tomb; and the angels came unto him, and shewed him concerning all things.
