The Text — Numbers 29 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 And in the seventh month, on the first day of the month, ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work: it is a day of blowing the trumpets unto you.

2 And ye shall offer a burnt offering for a sweet savour unto the LORD; one young bullock, one ram, and seven lambs of the first year without blemish:

3 And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals for a bullock, and two tenth deals for a ram,

4 And one tenth deal for one lamb, throughout the seven lambs:

5 And one kid of the goats for a sin offering, to make an atonement for you:

6 Beside the burnt offering of the month, and his meat offering, and the daily burnt offering, and his meat offering, and their drink offerings, according unto their manner, for a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire unto the LORD.

7 And ye shall have on the tenth day of this seventh month an holy convocation; and ye shall afflict your souls: ye shall not do any work therein:

8 But ye shall offer a burnt offering unto the LORD for a sweet savour; one young bullock, one ram, and seven lambs of the first year; they shall be unto you without blemish:

9 And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals to a bullock, and two tenth deals to one ram,

10 A several tenth deal for one lamb, throughout the seven lambs:

11 One kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the sin offering of atonement, and the continual burnt offering, and the meat offering of it, and their drink offerings.

12 And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work, and ye shall keep a feast unto the LORD seven days:

13 And ye shall offer a burnt offering, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the LORD; thirteen young bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year; they shall be without blemish:

14 And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals unto every bullock of the thirteen bullocks, two tenth deals to each ram of the two rams,

15 And a several tenth deal to each lamb of the fourteen lambs:

16 And one kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, his meat offering, and his drink offering.

17 And on the second day ye shall offer twelve young bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs of the first year without spot:

18 And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner:

19 And one kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, and the meat offering thereof, and their drink offerings.

20 And on the third day eleven bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish;

21 And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner:

22 And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, and his meat offering, and his drink offering.

23 And on the fourth day ten bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish:

24 Their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner:

25 And one kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, his meat offering, and his drink offering.

26 And on the fifth day nine bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without spot:

27 And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner:

28 And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, and his meat offering, and his drink offering.

29 And on the sixth day eight bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish:

30 And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner:

31 And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, his meat offering, and his drink offering.

32 And on the seventh day seven bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish:

33 And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner:

34 And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, his meat offering, and his drink offering.

35 On the eighth day ye shall have a solemn assembly: ye shall do no servile work therein:

36 But ye shall offer a burnt offering, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the LORD: one bullock, one ram, seven lambs of the first year without blemish:

37 Their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullock, for the ram, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner:

38 And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, and his meat offering, and his drink offering.

39 These things ye shall do unto the LORD in your set feasts, beside your vows, and your freewill offerings, for your burnt offerings, and for your meat offerings, and for your drink offerings, and for your peace offerings.

40 And Moses told the children of Israel according to all that the LORD commanded Moses.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter completes the offering calendar with the seventh month. On the first day, the day of blowing trumpets, there is a holy convocation with its burnt and sin offerings. On the tenth day, the day of atonement, the soul is afflicted and a further set of offerings is made. From the fifteenth day the feast of tabernacles runs eight days, with a decreasing number of bullocks each day, from thirteen on the first day down to seven on the seventh, and one on the eighth day of solemn assembly, together with rams, lambs, and a daily goat for a sin offering. Interpreted Editorial overview, grounded in the chapter text and its study notes

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. And in the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you shall have a holy convocation; you shall do no servile work: it is a day of blowing the trumpets to you. JFB notes the seventh month of the ecclesiastical year was the first month of the civil year, corresponding to September, a New Year's Day celebrated with great festivity and trumpets, and the ordinance gave it a religious character, making it a solemn preparation for the sacred feasts, of which more were held in this month than at any other season, while Henry adds it was the space between harvest and seed-time, when leisure from pressing occupations should be spent in God's immediate service. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 2. And you shall offer a burnt offering for a sweet savour to the Lord: one young bullock, one ram, and seven lambs of the first year without blemish. The trumpets' day carried a modest burnt offering, one bullock, one ram and seven lambs, with the greater particularity of this chapter supplying what Leviticus had left general. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 5. And one kid of the goats for a sin offering, to make an atonement for you. In addition to the burnt offering, a sin offering was prescribed for the trumpets' day, so that the year's opening stood under atonement as well as praise. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 6. Beside the burnt offering of the month, and his meat offering, and the daily burnt offering, and his meat offering, and their drink offerings, according to their manner, for a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire to the Lord. JFB stresses the special offerings were not to interfere with the ordinary ones: the daily and monthly offerings still took place in their ordinary course, and Henry adds no extraordinary services should put aside stated devotions. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 7. And you shall have on the tenth day of this seventh month a holy convocation, and you shall afflict your souls: you shall not do any work therein. JFB identifies this as the great day of atonement, whose institution and observance were described in Leviticus, while Henry notes soon after the day in which men afflicted their souls followed the feast of tabernacles, in which they rejoiced before the Lord. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 8. But you shall offer a burnt offering to the Lord for a sweet savour: one young bullock, one ram, and seven lambs of the first year. The atonement day's burnt offering matched the trumpets' day, one bullock, one ram and seven lambs, and JFB notes these additional offerings, a large animal sacrifice for general expiation and a sin offering for sins mingled with the day's services, appear supplementary to Leviticus. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 11. One kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the sin offering of atonement, and the continual burnt offering, and the meat offering of it, and their drink offerings. The day's own kid stood beside the great sin offering of atonement and the continual offering, layer upon layer of atonement on the day of affliction. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 12. And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month you shall have a holy convocation; you shall do no servile work, and you shall keep a feast to the Lord seven days. JFB identifies this as the feast of booths or tabernacles, lasting seven days with the first and last kept as Sabbaths, distinguished by a greater amount and variety of sacrifices than any other feast, partly to supply past deficiencies at the year's end, partly as a liberal acknowledgment after the ingathering of fruits, and partly because God made the daily sacrifices decrease, consulting human weakness that grows weary of long-continued charge and labour. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 13. And you shall offer a burnt offering, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour to the Lord: thirteen young bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year; they shall be without blemish. The tabernacles feast opened at its fullest, thirteen bullocks, two rams and fourteen lambs, the largest single-day offering of the year. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 16. And one kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, his meat offering, and his drink offering. Every day of dwelling in booths carried its sin offering, and Henry notes our burnt offerings of praise cannot be accepted unless we have an interest in the great sacrifice Christ offered, making himself a sin offering for us. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 17. And on the second day you shall offer twelve young bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs of the first year without spot. The bullocks decreased by one each day, twelve on the second, as JFB notes the sacrifices varied in a progressive ratio of decrease every day. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 20. And on the third day eleven bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish. Eleven bullocks on the third day, the daily decrease continuing while rams and lambs held steady. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 23. And on the fourth day ten bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish. Ten bullocks on the fourth day, the feast's liberality tapering as the week wore on. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 26. And on the fifth day nine bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without spot. Nine bullocks on the fifth day, the decrease running its course toward the feast's close. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 29. And on the sixth day eight bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish. Eight bullocks on the sixth day, with the rams and lambs unchanged through the whole week. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 32. And on the seventh day seven bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish. Seven bullocks on the seventh day, the week's last day of booths, closing the decrease from thirteen to seven. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 35. On the eighth day you shall have a solemn assembly: you shall do no servile work therein. JFB notes the feast of tabernacles was brought to a close on the eighth day, the great day, with special offerings fewer than on any preceding day, and with many spontaneous gifts and services besides, so that there was full scope for a devout spirit, both in statutory obedience and in freewill offerings. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 36. But you shall offer a burnt offering, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour to the Lord: one bullock, one ram, seven lambs of the first year without blemish. The eighth day's offering fell back to the modest scale of the trumpets' day, one bullock, one ram and seven lambs, closing the year's greatest feast simply. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 38. And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, and his meat offering, and his drink offering. Even the closing assembly kept its sin offering beside the continual offering, so that the feast ended as it had run, with atonement beside praise. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
  • Verse 39. These things you shall do to the Lord in your set feasts, beside your vows, and your freewill offerings, for your burnt offerings, and for your meat offerings, and for your drink offerings, and for your peace offerings. JFB calculates the yearly offerings at public expense from these two chapters, exclusive of passover lambs, at goats fifteen, kids twenty-one, rams seventy-two, bullocks one hundred and thirty-two, lambs one thousand one hundred and one, a sum total of one thousand two hundred and forty-one animals. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 40. And Moses told the children of Israel according to all that the Lord commanded Moses. The whole calendar of offerings, daily, sabbath, monthly and yearly, was delivered by Moses exactly as commanded, closing the two chapters of festival law. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Matthew Henry
