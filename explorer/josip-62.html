Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Continuing the rebuttal, this chapter argues that death came from sin and from the Devil, not from God: there are two deaths, of the flesh and of Sheol, sin entered the world through one man (Paul is cited), and Christ's saying in John's Gospel about Satan as a murderer is quoted against the claim that God is the author of death. Saint Basil's prayers are also cited, and the chapter closes with Christ giving himself for our sake.
