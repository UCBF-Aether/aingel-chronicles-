The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Sirach 15 (King James Version, Apocrypha; public-domain text as received)

1 He that feareth the Lord will do good, and he that hath the knowledge of the law shall obtain her.

2 And as a mother shall she meet him, and receive him as a wife married of a virgin.

3 With the bread of understanding shall she feed him, and give him the water of wisdom to drink.

4 He shall be stayed upon her, and shall not be moved; and shall rely upon her, and shall not be confounded.

5 She shall exalt him above his neighbours, and in the midst of the congregation shall she open his mouth.

6 He shall find joy and a crown of gladness, and she shall cause him to inherit an everlasting name.

7 But foolish men shall not attain unto her, and sinners shall not see her.

8 For she is far from pride, and men that are liars cannot remember her.

9 Praise is not seemly in the mouth of a sinner, for it was not sent him of the Lord.

10 For praise shall be uttered in wisdom, and the Lord will prosper it.

11 Say not thou, It is through the Lord that I fell away: for thou oughtest not to do the things that he hateth.

12 Say not thou, He hath caused me to err: for he hath no need of the sinful man.

13 The Lord hateth all abomination; and they that fear God love it not.

14 He himself made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his counsel;

15 If thou wilt, to keep the commandments, and to perform acceptable faithfulness.

16 He hath set fire and water before thee: stretch forth thy hand unto whether thou wilt.

17 Before man is life and death; and whether him liketh shall be given him.

18 For the wisdom of the Lord is great, and he is mighty in power, and beholdeth all things:

19 And his eyes are upon them that fear him, and he knoweth every work of man.

20 He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, neither hath he given any man licence to sin.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Whoever fears the Lord will do good, and knowledge of the law obtains wisdom. The chapter refuses to blame God for a man's falling away and insists he has commanded no one to do wickedly and given no one a licence to sin; the choice, and its consequences, are the man's own. Interpreted Study notes for this chapter, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley

What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. He who fears the Lord will do good, and he who has the knowledge of the law will obtain her. Fear of God produces good deeds, and knowledge of the law obtains wisdom. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 2. As a mother she will meet him, and receive him as a wife married of a virgin. Wisdom meets her seeker with a mother's welcome and a bride's faithfulness. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 3. With the bread of understanding she will feed him, and give him the water of wisdom to drink. Wisdom nourishes like bread of understanding and water of wisdom, food and drink for the mind. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 4. He will be stayed upon her and will not be moved; he will rely upon her and will not be confounded. Leaning on wisdom gives stability and reliance without confusion. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 5. She will exalt him above his neighbours, and in the midst of the congregation she will open his mouth. Wisdom exalts above neighbours and opens the mouth in public assembly, giving a voice among the people. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 6. He will find joy and a crown of gladness, and she will cause him to inherit an everlasting name. Wisdom's portion is joy, a crown of gladness, and a name that lasts for ever. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 7. But foolish men will not attain to her, and sinners will not see her. Fools cannot reach wisdom, and sinners cannot even see her. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 8. She is far from pride, and men who are liars cannot remember her. Wisdom stands far from pride, and liars cannot hold her in memory. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 9. Praise is not seemly in the mouth of a sinner, for it was not sent him of the Lord. A sinner's mouth is no fit place for praise, since praise was not given to him by the Lord. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 10. Praise will be uttered in wisdom, and the Lord will prosper it. True praise is spoken in wisdom, and the Lord makes such praise prosper. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 11. Do not say, it is through the Lord that I fell away, for you ought not to do the things he hates. Falling away must not be blamed on God, since the fallen things are what he hates. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 12. Do not say, he has caused me to err, for he has no need of the sinful man. God does not cause error, and he has no need of a sinful man to serve his purposes. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 13. The Lord hates all abomination, and those who fear God do not love it. God hates every abomination, and the God-fearing share that hatred rather than loving it. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 14. He himself made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his counsel. Man was made by God and left to his own counsel, free to choose. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 15. If you will, keep the commandments, and perform acceptable faithfulness. Keeping the commandments is a matter of will, and faithfulness so performed is acceptable. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 16. He has set fire and water before you: stretch forth your hand to whichever you will. Fire and water are set before man as the great alternatives, and the hand chooses. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 17. Before man is life and death, and whichever he likes will be given him. Life and death lie before man, and his own liking decides which is given. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 18. The wisdom of the Lord is great, and he is mighty in power, and beholds all things. God's wisdom is great, his power mighty, and nothing escapes his sight. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 19. His eyes are upon those who fear him, and he knows every work of man. God's eyes watch the God-fearing, and he knows every human work. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
  • Verse 20. He has commanded no man to do wickedly, neither has he given any man licence to sin. God never commands wickedness nor licenses sin; sin is man's own choice, not God's order. Interpreted Study note, summarizing W. O. E. Oesterley
