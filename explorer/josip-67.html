Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter continues the argument about the threefold being, citing the LORD's dealings with Abraham and the angels' declaration, and then turns to a council in which Cyril, archbishop of Alexandria, excommunicates those who say Christ is two beings and two natures. Its position, as printed, is that the Son is not half of God and that flesh joined to a human soul is not two beings to be worshipped apart.
