Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A voice from heaven promises Abiya, Sila and Fentos that as they have seen others die and rise, so they shall rise after death and their faces shall shine in the kingdom of heaven; seized, beaten, bound and scourged before the king, they refuse to sacrifice or bow to gods of gold, silver, wood and stone that have neither soul, heart nor mind. After three days unharmed in the house of burning, they are stoned, burned and set upon by leopards, bears and lions, which bow to them instead, and at last cast into a furnace, where angels receive their souls into the bosom of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, into the garden of delight.
