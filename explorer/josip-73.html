Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter continues the praise exposition in very broken form: the Devil cast into hell, baptism, Adam's fall, and fragments naming the Philistines, Edom and Egypt, with Satan falling from the heavenly order. Beyond the repeated praise and sea-into-hell images, little continuous argument can be recovered from the printed text, and several lines are marked as partly hidden.
