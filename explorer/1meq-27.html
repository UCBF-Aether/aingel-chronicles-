Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter retells creation in order: God creates the world out of nothing, says, Let there be light, separates waters, makes herbs and trees on the third day, sun, moon and stars on the fourth, living creatures on the fifth, and on the sixth day beasts and cattle and Adam in his image and likeness, set to eat and praise God in the garden. One tree is forbidden, the tree that knows good and evil, the tree of death, and through the serpent Eve eats and gives to Adam, death comes upon him and his children, and God drives him out to eat the herb of the field in the sweat of his face. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
