The Text

Scripture — not editorial explanation

The Text — Leviticus 12 (King James Version (1611); public-domain text as received (Project Gutenberg no. 10))

1 And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

2 Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If a woman have conceived seed, and born a man child: then she shall be unclean seven days; according to the days of the separation for her infirmity shall she be unclean.

3 And in the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised.

4 And she shall then continue in the blood of her purifying three and thirty days; she shall touch no hallowed thing, nor come into the sanctuary, until the days of her purifying be fulfilled.

5 But if she bear a maid child, then she shall be unclean two weeks, as in her separation: and she shall continue in the blood of her purifying threescore and six days.

6 And when the days of her purifying are fulfilled, for a son, or for a daughter, she shall bring a lamb of the first year for a burnt offering, and a young pigeon, or a turtledove, for a sin offering, unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, unto the priest:

7 Who shall offer it before the LORD, and make an atonement for her; and she shall be cleansed from the issue of her blood. This is the law for her that hath born a male or a female.

8 And if she be not able to bring a lamb, then she shall bring two turtles, or two young pigeons; the one for the burnt offering, and the other for a sin offering: and the priest shall make an atonement for her, and she shall be clean.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter gives the purification law after childbirth. A mother who bears a son is unclean seven days, then continues thirty-three days in the blood of her purifying; for a daughter the periods are two weeks and sixty-six days. On the eighth day a son is circumcised. When her days are fulfilled she brings a lamb for a burnt offering and a bird for a sin offering, or two birds if she cannot afford a lamb, and the priest makes atonement for her and she is clean. What the chapter says, verse by verse

The notes below are the site’s study-note summaries of the public-domain commentators named. They are paraphrase summaries, not quotations from those commentators.

  • Verse 1. After laws about defilement from without, through unclean animals and carcasses, a series of statutes follows about impurity connected with persons themselves, traced through life's stages to death. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 2. A woman who has conceived and borne a male child is unclean seven days, as in the days of separation for her infirmity, the languor of menstruation, and everyone coming near her contracts a similar defilement. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 3. On the eighth day the child's foreskin is circumcised, at the end of the mother's week of seclusion, as ordered in Genesis 17. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 4. She then continues in the blood of her purification thirty-three days, touching no hallowed thing nor coming into the sanctuary, visitors being allowed to approach her though she is still excluded from public ordinances. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 5. If she bears a female child she is unclean two weeks and continues sixty-six days, a longer period taken as a symbolical remembrancer that woman was the introducer of sin and bore its heaviest punishment, a stigma removed by Christ. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 6. When her purifying days are fulfilled, for son or daughter, she brings a lamb of the first year for a burnt offering and a young pigeon or turtledove for a sin offering to the tabernacle door, on the forty-first day for a boy and the eighty-first for a girl. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 7. The priest offers them before the LORD and makes atonement for her, and she is clean from the flow of her blood, the burnt offering implying renewed surrender to God and the sin offering recalling the origin of sin and the child's fallen nature. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
  • Verse 8. If she cannot bring a lamb, she brings two turtledoves or two young pigeons, one for a burnt offering and one for a sin offering, and the priest makes atonement and she is clean, the offering Mary made, proving the humble condition of Jesus' family. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown

Connections to other passages

  • The study note for verse 3 connects this passage to Genesis 17: Circumcision on the eighth day is as ordered in Genesis 17. Interpreted Study note, summarizing Jamieson-Fausset-Brown
