Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter addresses those who believe not the resurrection, in flesh and soul together: they shall be judged as they did, for all are the fruit of Adam, and through Adam death came upon all, and all shall rise with Adam their father and be judged by what they did. Its closing verses turn elegiac, asking where the might of kings, the honour of rulers, the sweetness of food and wine have gone, death has gathered them all, and the chapter ends in that meditation. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
