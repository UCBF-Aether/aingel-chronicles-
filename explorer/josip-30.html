The Text — Josippon 30 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 See the works of our Lord Jesus Christ. And I revealed not unto thee the interpretation of the joy and of the lamb; and He promised[?] thee that thou shouldest interpret. Behold, I have shown thee from the beginning, and [?] thee: for the children of Israel, when[?] they were in the land of Egypt, under the servitud[?]e of Pharaoh and his hosts, and God desired to deliver them from[?] their servitude by the kingdom, and to keep[?] them until the day of the tenth and the fourth; and they shall keep it at the going down of the sun, in the evening of the tenth and the fourth day; when the lamb of the tenth and the fif[?]th entereth, its blood shall be sprinkled — He said — upon the lintels[?] of their doorposts, that which is abov[?]e and that which is below.

2 For the Spirit[?] was in the night of the lamb, [?] Moses; they shall sla[?]y it. Pharaoh and his hos[?]ts [sought] the blood upon the doorpos[?]ts of the children of Israel; [?] and He shall not enter unto the[?]m to destroy in the night those [?] of the houses of the Egyptians, [?] Pharaoh. For He i[?]s his: a sig[?]n ... I am: when I see the [?] blood upon their houses, [?] I will pass over those who dwell in the[?]m.

3 And when they slaughte[?]r the lamb, they shall not eat the fles[?]h as they eat it; they shall roast it with fire. For in the night of the lamb He delivered them from the land of Egypt, from the servitude of Pharaoh; they do this work in the night of that day, every year, that they may remember how He brought them forth with a strong hand, and with an outstretched and exalted and mighty arm.

4 For this day is the day of the joy of God; keep ye it, until generation is born unto your generations.

5 And this lamb is the lamb of the month of God.

6 And when ye come forth from the land of Egypt, ye shall not eat leavened [bread]; but ye shall eat unleavened, seven days. And every soul that eateth leavened shall ... ; they shall eat it in haste, their loins girt with a girdle upon[?] their loins, and their san[?]dals on their feet, and [?] their staves in[?] their hands; and they shall eat it in haste; and [?] of it, and from its beginning [?], from the house even unto the hea[?]d and the belly; and that which remainet[?]h of it, which [?] is leavened in those days, or leavened [bread] is found with her, shall be cut off from her people. This they said unto them, and they did that which He commanded them therein; and He is with all of them, to save them, as we have interpreted unto thee in the book which is from the beginning concerning it.

7 And He is I AM, as were the children of Adam, when they were under the servitude of the Devil and his hosts, in the world and in Sheol.

8 God revealed His Son, that He might make them free men from servitude, and give them the Garden and the kingdom of the heavens.

9 And He smote the Devil and his hosts with the plagues which I declared unto thee in the book of the interpretation of the incarnation; and there was hidden from him the head, in the work of man, which I revealed unto thee, until He pondered, as a weak man, and saved Adam from him by the shedding of His blood; for He is the Lamb of God, perfect, without blemish, alone.

10 Then God commanded the childre[?]n of Israel that He is [?] from him, and ... in the side of his ... , from the slaughter which is fitting for him. Likewise did our Lord Jesus Christ unto all the children of Adam: for Adam sinned, and became a servant unto the Devil, and there followed him death and judgment with the Devil, for ever the world [endureth]; and all his seed, who were born of the desire of those who sinned and of those who sinned not — they are servants unto the Devil, for they are of the desire of Adam; they did [it], for he who made a servant made the children servants. And our Lord Jesus Christ came, by the will of God, and was incarnate in flesh from the servan[?]

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

See the works of our Lord Jesus Christ, the chapter opens, though the interpretation of the joy and of the lamb is said not yet to be revealed. Its Passover thread is explicit: coming out of Egypt, no leavened bread is to be eaten, but unleavened bread for seven days, with commandments on the fitting slaughter.
