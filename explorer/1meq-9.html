Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

In a short chapter the argument narrows to a curse-image: if the rain and dew of God's mercy do not fall, Gilboa and Lebanon yield no grass, and so the proud and idol-serving, who sing to idols with drum and pipe, will not rise. Against the scoff, the dead shall not rise, the chapter answers with the horn blown at the resurrection by Michael the archangel, the shaking of mountain and deep, and the closing claim: there shall be a resurrection for all that is flesh. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
