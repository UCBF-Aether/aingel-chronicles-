The Text — Sinodos 10 (Ge'ez text of G. Horner, The Statutes of the Apostles; or, Canones Ecclesiastici (London: Williams & Norgate, 1904), public domain)

1 The thirty-fifth canon[? — the Ge'ez numeral is corrupt]: concerning the putting away[?] [of a wife]. As for him that hath put [her] away[?] — judge him as he willeth[?]; a man may put away his kept one[?].

2 And if a man have a wife, and she have not borne children[?] …[much lost]… let her be kept[?]; but if he take[?] another wife[?], let her be sent away.

3 And the man that hath a kept [wife][?] — let him put her away, or let him keep her[?] according to the law[?]; and if he be not willing, let him put her away, let him send her away.

4 And if there be any that hath put [her] away, judge him as he willeth[?]; for [?] … the Spirit … God … [fragment].

5 …[canon number lost]… concerning the time[?] wherein they hear the teaching …[much lost]… while they hear the word of instruction; and if there be a catechumen, and he knoweth good works, let days[?] not be granted unto him, but his work alone[?], which is manifest[?] concerning him.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Canon 35, its numeral marked corrupt in the text, concerns putting away a wife: a man may put away his kept one, a childless wife is to be kept unless he takes another, and a man with a kept wife must put her away or keep her according to the law, with judgment left as he willeth. The chapter's last verse turns to different matter — the time in which hearers receive teaching, and a catechumen known by good works whose days are not to be granted on that account alone — but most of that verse is lost.
