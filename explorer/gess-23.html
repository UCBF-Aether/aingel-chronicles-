1 [Topic:] Concerning the elder or the deacon whom the bishop has expelled.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Discipline is to hold across dioceses: if a bishop has expelled an elder or deacon, another bishop must not receive him back unless the bishop who expelled him permits it, or that bishop has died. One bishop's sentence is not another bishop's to lift. Interpreted Paraphrase of the chapter's own text; Standard corpus, Gessew 23
