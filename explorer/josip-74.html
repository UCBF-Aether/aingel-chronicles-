Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter expounds Miriam's song, “Sing unto the LORD, for in glory he hath triumphed gloriously”, alongside the psalm exposition: the sea is read as hell, Pharaoh's hosts as Satan's hosts, and the praise is sung in the name of the three Persons, Father, Son and Holy Spirit. The exposition itself is fragmentary, with whole clauses marked [?]. Interpreted Reading of this chapter's text as printed above; the study-notes file for this chapter is silent
