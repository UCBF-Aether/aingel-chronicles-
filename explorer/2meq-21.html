Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The book closes by contrasting perishing glory with abiding blessing: the sinners' houses, gathered in theft and violence, pass like cloud and smoke and like the cedar of Lebanon that David saw exalted and then found no more, while those who keep God's law receive blessing on land, cattle and the fruit of their hands and are set above surrounding peoples. The chapter then sets out the law of refuge as Moses commanded Joshua, the unwitting slayer saved in the city of refuge, the knowing killer judged, and ends with the generation of the righteous, from Adam and Seth, who believed God's word, walked in his commandment, and shall rejoice with him in his kingdom for ever.
