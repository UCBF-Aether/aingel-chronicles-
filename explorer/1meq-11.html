Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter turns to judgment on idolatrous peoples and cities, naming Armon, Arefatiha, Daqa, Tyre, Sidon, Edomyas and others in fragmentary verses, and above all to Jerusalem, shamed after the resurrection for walking not in God's law but in the slaying of the righteous. Its vine is likened to the vine of Sodom and Gomorrah, whose wine is bitter gall and the poison of asps; the chapter's readings are heavily uncertain and several verses break off, so the overview follows only what the surviving text states.
