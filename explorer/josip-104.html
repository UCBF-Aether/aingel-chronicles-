How to read the labels

  • Interpreted — a reasoned explanation or proposed relationship.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The book closes with a short prayer to our Lady: “To our Lady, the holy one, intercede; O Virgin, thou who didst bear God, the Saviour”, naming the fathers, apostles, manna, martyrs and saints, and asking her intercession at the time of prayer for the sake of our Lord Jesus Christ, with trust and glory ascribed for ever.
