Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter moves from death's sting and Satan's fall to Christ's birth in a cave and manger, Herod's seeking, the flight into Egypt, baptism with the dove and the Father's voice, and the wilderness, where Satan repeatedly tests whether Jesus is merely a weak man. Its answer, in Christ's own words as printed here, is that he gives his breath willingly, for the love of his Father, for the world.
