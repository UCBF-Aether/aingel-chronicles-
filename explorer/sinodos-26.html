The Text — Sinodos 26 (Ge'ez text of G. Horner, The Statutes of the Apostles; or, Canones Ecclesiastici (London: Williams & Norgate, 1904), public domain)

1 Statute 54. Concerning the ordination of presbyters and deacons; concerning subdeacons[?]; and concerning deaconesses, women; and subdeaconesses[?]; and female readers.

2 And thou, O bishop, ordain the presbyter, and lay hand upon his head, while the presbyters and all the deacons stand by.

4 And likewise the deacons: lay hand upon them.

5 … concerning the deaconesses and …[?] and female readers, women —

6 and aforetime we have spoken concerning them.

7 Statute 55. Concerning those who confess [the faith].

8 He who confesseth shall not be ordained; this is of his own will[?], for great honour is fitting for him, for he confessed God, and …[?] before peoples and kings.

9 And if he were called unto this ministry[?], [as] bishop or presbyter, let him be ordained.

10 And likewise, this is the ordinance concerning him who confesseth: and if he finished not, but turned back[?] and denied, let him be deposed; for not only did he deny Christ, but he is become an enemy, as one of the unbelievers.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Statute 54 concerns the ordination of presbyters and deacons, and also subdeacons, deaconesses, female readers and subdeaconesses: the bishop ordains the presbyter and lays hand on his head while presbyters and all deacons stand by, and likewise lays hand on the deacons. Statute 55 concerns those who confess the faith: a confessor is not ordained merely for confessing, since that is of his own will, yet great honour is fitting for him, for he confessed God before peoples and kings — and if he is called to the ministry as bishop or presbyter, let him be ordained, but if he turned back and denied, let him be deposed, for he has become an enemy like the unbelievers.
