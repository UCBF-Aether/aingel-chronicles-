The Text — Josippon 39 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 And again the people bow down, their head, and ask the priests of God — who is righteousness — in His hand, in the body of Satan (?), which He gave unto His disciples, that they should bind (?) and loose every bond of sin, that He may loose them from their sins, and root up (?) all their iniquity, and save them from evil, and bring them into the heavenly kingdom.

2 And after this little while the body ascendeth; He said: His gift is lifted up above all the people, as it was pierced from them; He said: Osa, mestal.

3 And all the people cry out, as the thief on the right said: Remember us, O LORD, when Thou comest in Thy kingdom.

4 And it is fitting for them, the forgiveness of all their sins, and joy within the town, as He asketh for the thief from Him this gift; everyone taketh that which standeth at the time of the Holy One, in faith and in fear.

5 From him who was his offering, or he who ate, from him who draweth near, or he who draweth not near; and he who cannot draw near, he is he who knoweth, that taketh the portion.

6 And when they cannot, he shall be as a prince [?] who saith unto God: I am not worthy that Thou shouldest come under my roof; those who are commanded by Thy word, and one singleness is exalted.

7 And then shall he be who sought judgement by faith, and was ashamed from the reproach of faith; likewise shall he be who believeth and standeth firm at the time of the Holy One.

8 And when he who cannot draw near leaveth the offering in humility and in lowliness and in faith, he taketh that which the prince took, an hundred (?); and concerning this it is fitting for the faithful, when he draweth near or draweth not near, that he be in fear and in trembling, until Christ ascendeth from upon the altar; and the ascension — when the body is fulfilled, all his body shall be fulfilled, and he shall ascend; today he proceedeth from the body; and after this, when he stood before the ascension, they cannot set upon him the might of the Holy Spirit, which He sent upon His disciples [?] after the ascension; and if he is he who in the time of giving — all — and went out before the ascension of Christ — and the ascension shall be to him from the portion of [?] Judas Iscariot, who said concerning him, and the holy Gospel, that he is he who in the night of the day of Friday, the night in which Christ gave unto His disciples from His body and His blood, forsook the apostles and went out alone, before Christ came forth; and then Satan entered unto him, when he went out before the holy offering was fulfilled.

9 For the holy Gospel said that he was present, and went out then; and the disciples abode with Christ, until he went out, and went out with him.

10 And likewise he who goeth out before the ascension of Christ from the house of the Christians became in the likeness of Judas.

11 And if it be a great house, a work it is not fitting for them to go out.

12 And he who is in earnest at the time of the Mass, and goeth not out until the offering ascendeth from the altar, taketh the crown that keepeth, which we have mentioned unto thee before.

13 And by the faith of the priests who ordained a law unto Christ — and they cannot know to declare the books — he shall be driven, he who willed, from the law, before the offering ascendeth from the altar.

14 And they perish, their souls, and the soul of all the people; for God commanded men that they should hear the commandment of the priests, if they command them in that which is commanded.

15 And when they transgress them, they take destruction for ever.

16 As the hosts of the Devil: and when men heard the commandment of the Devil for their head, and obeyed him in that which God breaketh, and were kept back from the praises and the holiness, and went out with him, unto for ever.

17 Likewise shall fall and perish everyone who goeth out from the house of the Christians, by wrath the priests shall drive him out, before the offering is fulfilled.

18 And if they hear concerning them, in that which God promiseth not, they take judgement with them, as the hosts of the Devil were judged with him; likewise he who holdeth back at the time of the Holy One, and heareth not the declaration of the books and of the holiness, and is adorned, taketh judgement upon me; for he is, instead of him who runneth for Christ, they polish him; for he is he who taketh by an unclean spirit and an unclean body.

19 And thou art he: as the books and the holiness made them hosts before the offering, they sanctify the faithful, and His ascension they cleanse, as I have shown thee; and after this it is fitting to take the offering, as men cannot be, who are without sin, for one hour that abideth in the wilderness alone.

20 And he who shall be joined with men, causing to sin, burneth from her by a word which is not fitting; or by an oath, or by reviling, or by a curse of his neighbour.

21 Or by withholding from this: he is he who in these works abode, and was defiled, and was cleansed; and he wisheth for the hearing of the reading of the books, and the hearing of the prayer of light, that he may cast away his defilement, and be sanctified.

22 And after this [?] he eateth and drinketh from the holy flesh and the blood of Christ; and he is holy, and is converted; and he who loveth not the books and the holiness, and is confounded, shall be reckoned — his body shall be reckoned, and his blood is for Christ; and he also is unclean.

23 And Christ shall humble him again exceedingly; He said in the tongue of the prophet: Be humbled; for He said in the tongue of the prophet: Be humbled; for him who humbleth me I will exalt, and I will honour him who honoured me; and He swore this, as the likeness of him who soweth seed in earth which is not watered, and plougheth it not; he also destroyeth the seed, and it sprouteth not for the earth.

24 Likewise men, if he was (?) in fatherhood (?), sought [?] thee (?), as thou sowest (?) for thee (?): he shall be as earth, a thing of many defilements; and if he come into the house of *Kerkemtedan* (?), and hear the voice of their books, and the Gospel, the voice of God — the water of life — as the LORD God said; and he is watered by that water from newness, that cleanseth sin.

25 And when he is watered, and *Tesfa* cometh, the Holy Spirit descendeth upon him, and cleanseth him from sin, as earth is cleansed from defilement.

26 And then he receiveth the holy offering, and beareth fruit in him, as earth that is opened, the chosen one, that beareth the seed that is sown, and it beareth fruit in it.

27 Behold, I have shown thee the glory and the holiness which is equal upon all the faithful; and apart from Him they cannot do anything at all from the commandment of God; for by the glory the Holy Spirit abideth in them, and the unclean spirits are driven out from them, and they are made to do the work for God.

28 And whoso departeth from going unto the house of the Christians in the morning and in the evening, and departeth from their prayer at the time of the Holy One, as I have told thee, and reacheth death in that day, and shall be reckoned for ever and ever — as the Devil and his hosts fell from her — and behold, I have shown thee the glory and the holiness, that it is fitting upon all the faithful, all of them, at all the days of their life.

29 For God hath appointed for them these works — they are the cause of their salvation and of what is their life; and this is it: that in every day and in every night, seven and two hours, God appointed for them three times to reach unto the gate of the house of the Christians, in the morning, and in the evening, and at the time of the Holy One; and these three times at which the Christian reacheth unto the gate are not the measure of two hours; and there remaineth for them seven and two hours, in which they do the work of the flesh that passeth away; and God giveth them from the wealth of this world a doubled reward, in place of the work which they forsook, in place of those two hours, while it is charged unto them upon that which cleanseth them, for the life of their souls — which is His glory, of God, and His holiness, and the descent of the Holy Spirit upon them, and the forgiveness of their sins.

30 And likewise in every week, seven days, God hath appointed upon men their work, that it rule not over him.

31 And He said unto them: Work ye the work of the flesh two days, and work ye for the spirit one day.

32 And in that one day, work ye in it the work that is spiritual, from morning until evening; it shall be for you in it a work without reading the word of God in the holy house of the Christians. And He said again: When thou readest (?), a good reading read. And all who (?) are gathered (?) unto thee, and thou doest for them, God shall give thee a reward on their account, in the kingdom.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The people bow and ask the priests of God, and the chapter warns that everyone who goes out from the house of the Christians in wrath is driven out by the priests before the offering. Its closing rule concerns one day given to spiritual work from morning until evening, as the chapter prints it, without reading the word of God in it.
