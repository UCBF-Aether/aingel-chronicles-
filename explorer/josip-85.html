Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter retells sacred history in brief: Adam placed in the garden, the angel's fall in saying “I will set my throne and be like unto the Most High”, nakedness covered with fig leaves, Noah's ark and covenant, Abraham, Sodom and Gomorrah, Jacob's sojourning, Egypt's enslavement with mortar and clay and the slaying of male infants, Israel's cry, the law and sacrifices, and Moses' signs and wonders bringing Israel out of Egypt.
