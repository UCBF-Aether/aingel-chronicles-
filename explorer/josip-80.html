Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This chapter sets Eve's transgression and its sorrow against Gabriel's greeting to Mary, “Rejoice, thou full one, for the LORD is with thee”, and the virgin birth in which the seal of virginity was not broken, and Adam's fall from the garden against Christ in the garden. It also gives a reckoning from Adam to Abraham and to the birth of Christ, most of whose figures are marked [?], and closes with a doxology. Its final unit, covering verses 21–35, is printed as a single [?]. Interpreted Reading of this chapter's text as printed above; the study-notes file for this chapter is silent
