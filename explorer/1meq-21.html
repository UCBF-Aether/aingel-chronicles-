Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter argues trust in God from examples: David delivered from Saul, Absalom, the Elofəlawəyanə, Amalekites and Edomites; Hezekiah delivered from the great Syrian; and by contrast Manasseh given over to his enemy because he set not his trust in God. Better is trust in God than many hosts, horses, chariots and bows, the chapter insists, the trusting shall live, be honoured and exalted, and inherit honour prepared for Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, David, Hezekiah and Samuel, while the proud who trust in wealth are estranged from grace. Several readings in this chapter carry the translation's uncertainty marks, and the overview follows only what the text states. Interpreted Chapter text
