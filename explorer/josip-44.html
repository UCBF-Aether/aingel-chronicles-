1 A discourse(?) of haste, concerning the denial(?) of the day of the Sabbath and its completion: [?](?), friend — God shall enlighten the eyes of thy heart by the light of His holy [?], He comforted, that thou mayest perceive the mysteries of the kingdom.

2 That which I commanded thee, that I should declare unto thee the completion of the day of [?] holiness and glory: know that it is [?] all the work of the day of the Sabbath [?]; it is the day of God; and [?] its name among all the children of baptism; for all of them call it Kyriake, in the tongue of the Greeks(?); its interpretation: the day of God, in which He rose from the dead; and concerning this we were commanded, that we should rejoice in it with spiritual songs, in every week, and that we should not do in it the work of this world — [?] the hosts — but that there be in it, in our gatherings of assembly, [works] for God; for it is His day; and in it [let us] work the works of God, in which the believers inherit the kingdom of God for ever, and the life which shall not pass away from being saved.

3 And the works of this world — for ever in them there is revealed the life for ever; thou art the life which passeth away, and the world passeth away.

4 God said: Work for the life of this world which passeth away.

5 Two days, and on the day of the Sabbath, work for Me alone; for it is My day, and I will give you your joy instead of what ye worked for Me — My kingdom for ever, and My life(?) for ever, and My flower which fadeth not; and I will bless you in your work in the six days which ye work in it; and I will not put you into the [?] in which ye are.

6 [?] that it may be for you on My day [a recompense] for what ye worked for Me in it.

7 And when ye have worked for Me your desires — the three lives, and the three portions, earthly and heavenly — and as ye worked for Me [in] that which is [?], I separated you from my peoples and from my [?] and from my joy for ever; and I grieved you in the work of the flesh, which ye made it(?); it is better than my work(?); and [?] your food, which consumeth you, the [?] which shall be for you on My day, from the [?] of [?] which consumeth you — or in the suffering of [?] — I tried you in it, that ye should give all your labour.

8 Or they steal your [?], or by [?] they [?] your [?].

9 Or destroy your seed, or slay that which was tried from your friend; and ye shall be grieved, and ye shall be sad on earth and in heaven; for I am He who enrich eth, and I make poor and I kill and I make alive.

10 Friend, this is the interpretation of the word of God concerning the day of the Sabbath: that it is the day in which it is not fitting that they should do in it a work which is not without work.

11 And the work is: His glory — of God — and His holiness, always; and the hearing of His holy books all the day long: this is the work of God which is fitting that they should do on the day of the Sabbath, which is the day of God; and by this work the believers live a good life, which is on earth and in heaven.

12 And concerning this it is fitting for the believers that they keep the commandment [?], and rejoice [in] the work of this world, and rest [?] from him(?) in this [?].

13 And many [?] of the believers neglect in this [?] from their works for ever [?]; they neglect God in this light; and their [?] is greater than the [?] of those who do the work of this world; for they do the work of this world, for they take their food in the life which passeth away, which presseth upon it, while they are in this world.

14 And as for those who work for God, they gain the kingdom for ever and the life which passeth not away; God neglected not on the day of the Sabbath [to turn] from earthly gain [and] loss, unto the gain — the gain for ever, heavenly.

15 And if we neglect the earthly works and do not the heavenly works, behold, there are lost from us(?) the two lives together.

16 And [?] they do the work of loss — gain by [?] — [?] from us, and [?] our gain, neither in this world nor in the heavens.

17 And their [?] — of him who neglecteth on the day of the Sabbath [to turn] from the work of this world, and doeth not the work of heaven on the day of [?] — is greater than the [?] of those who do in it the work of this light.

18 The world, and their perdition is greater than the perdition of those who — for they did not honour the day of God, and [?] the days of this world, precious from them; for God, for the honour of His own day — His blessing — [?] the heavenly [believers], that they should do in it the work of heaven, that their gain in it may be greater than the gain of the days [of this world]; for their gain is in two days — loss and [?].

19 And their [?] in this day increaseth always, for ever; and the honour of this day and its exaltation is greater than [that of] two days, on account of its heavenly work; and he who gained the gain for ever on the days — the seventh — and gained not the gain of God on the day of God: behold, he sought not the day of God [to be] a help, from him who worked in it the work of this world — loss; for it is a very small gain — the gain of this world; and Paradise — he gained not in it for ever, nor in heaven; behold, verily he sought and made the two days of this world precious [away] from him.

20 And his [?] is great; for he sought not the day of God, which He commanded that they should honour it.

21 And God increaseth [him] who sought not; and him who honoureth Me, him will I honour; and he who dishonoureth God, and doeth not unto Him work on the day of God, shall be dishonoured on earth and in heaven; and he who honoureth God on the day, and doeth unto Him work, God shall honour on earth and in heaven.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A discourse of haste opens, concerning the denial of the day of the Sabbath and its completion. Believers, it says, should keep the commandment, rejoice in the work of this world, and rest from him in this world, while all the books of the commandment are said to hang on this commandment and to strive diligently in it.
