The Text — Josippon 42 (BL EAP704/2/3 — Ge'ez codex of the Zena Ayhud, Christian redaction, copied 1595–1605)

1 And if his pretext became the purity of the faith — it is this which driveth Satan, weakened, away from him; and he saith unto his soul: Thou hadst power, O Satan, to cast me down — and not by deed.

2 He wisheth now that he may cast me down in weakness and in destruction — the destruction wherein he himself fell from heaven; for Satan and his hosts, when they were shut out from the glory of God and from His holiness, and were troubled and troubled exceedingly — or else they desired sin — [were cast down] from the kingdom of heaven; and He created me, that I might inherit their kingdom, from which they fell; and if thou troublest me [to depart] from His glory and His holiness, and from the reading of the books of God, and troublest me even as they [were troubled], I fall from Him, even as they fell.

3 And by this thought of good — which is the purity of the faith — he overcometh them, and doeth that which is fitting for him [to do] before the glory of God and His holiness and the reading of His books; and he is joined in fellowship with His martyrs who were slain; and he overcame, and took the crown of victory, and the kingdom of heaven abideth with Christ in the kingdom which endureth for ever.

4 And know, friend, that by these three cords Satan entereth into the believer at all times and at every hour, that he may trouble them and bring them unto the three commands; and he striveth not to cease from the crown of victory, nor shall the kingdom of heaven be fitting for them; and know: he who is fought against departeth by one of all the cords, and overcometh, and goeth unto the house of the Christians, with more joy than [the joy] which is without fighting; and the believer is fitting, that he be guarded, and that he keep [?](?) for the time; and he causeth men to stumble by stumbling from these three cords; and from him, at one time, there cometh a hearing from him, and glory at all times.

5 Upon him, in the assembly: from him he feareth not; and if he heareth not — from him he feareth not; and if he heareth not, that he should turn back unto him: for he is not able to dwell in the fence, and he rebuketh not in it a second time; for by a sniffing he snorteth; and if he snort once, and there entered a fence into it, he leaveth not always from dwelling.

6 And concerning this the apostles commanded the believers, and ordained for them: for unto you [belong] the prayers of the morning and the prayers of the evening; these are the prayers — at the second [hour], and at the third, and at the fourth hour, and at the hour of the evening meal, and in the spirit of the evening meal.

7 These three prayers are added upon those prayers; they are fitting for them, [to pray] wheresoever they will.

8 Until there is for them no work: they are fitting for them in unity and in deed.

9 And again, the priests shall not neglect one of them, that the enemy may not prevail over them and overcome them; for by this they drive them away at all times.

10 And as for those who are weak in the work of the flesh, these three prayers are not fitting for them — without the prayer of supplication; they are fitting for them, in their houses, with prayer and with prostration, that they may dwell in the house of the Christian in the morning and the evening; for it is a guard for them from Satan and his hosts, when they sleep; for there came in [a time] of making them to forget their entering in, and they know not until they awaken them; one cometh and reproveth him; and behold, Satan showeth them his form [in sleep]; swiftly he casteth them down [like] a demon, and by making them forget, and they awaken not — those who are indeed sleeping; and if a man hath prayed the prayer of supplication, he is not able — Satan — to appear unto him, nor to cast him down.

11 And if at all the supplication is mingled with those [prayers] — arise; these: reckon not upon them sin; for the prayer is reckoned unto them [as] falsehood, which is not [reckoned] at the completion of his days; this is the prayer which is fitting upon all the believers; but those who are weak in the work of the flesh — no — and women, freewomen, and servants, great and small.

12 And these one prayers: the third, and the second, and the fifth, and the half of the night are fitting for them, while they do their work.

13 Then they stand — or they bow down.

14 Or prostrate, or in every work; for God reckoneth unto them their prayers, as He reckoneth unto those who have no work; these stand and spend the night, while they watch; He said: their spending the night — God reckoneth unto them their prayer on account of their weariness in the work of the flesh, which presseth upon them; and the prayer of the morning and of the evening and of supplication are fitting for them; He addeth them with fear and trembling and prostration from them in the house of the Christian; the prayer of the morning and the evening, and the prayer of supplication in their houses; for they are not in the house of the Christian to pray — [it is] rather in their houses.

15 And as David the prophet said in the Psalm of the hundred and second: My soul, my soul, I will sing unto Thee.

16 He took away my sins from me for Thy sake; and likewise He said thus: In Thy sanctuary I appeared unto Thee, that I might behold(?) Thy newness.

17 And in the Psalm [?]: In the multitude of Thy mercy I will enter Thy house, and I will worship in the court of Thy sanctuary.

18 And in the Psalm of the ninth: Before Thy angels will I sing, and I will worship in the court of Thy sanctuary.

19 And concerning this it is fitting that the prayer of the morning and the evening be in the house of the Christian.

20 For these two prayers, they are the firstfruits of the day and of the night; and the prayer of supplication, let it be in the house; and these other one prayers, they pray them [?] where a man is, in [?], according to the measure of their strength and [?] by it.

21 For at the hour of the third hour there descended upon it the Holy Spirit upon the apostles, and they were comforted and strengthened, and He taught them all the mysteries of heaven and of earth; and it is the prayer of the Holy Spirit, and in it the believers are renewed; and in it they scourged Jesus Christ with scourgings; and at the seventh hour, in it Christ was crucified; and He saith [in] that [hour]: the tree of the Cross.

22 And concerning this it is fitting for us, that we pray in it unto Him who was crucified for our sake; and at the ninth hour He brought forth Adam from punishment and from the judgment of death; and in it Christ died, and broke the power of the Devil and his hosts — these are the rulers of death — and He turned Adam and his seed into Paradise; and concerning this it is fitting that we pray in it with thanksgiving and [?].

23 And in the middle of the night Jesus Christ was born in Bethlehem; and in it the dead were quickened; and in it He cometh to judge the living and the dead, as He said in the holy Gospel in the parable of the ten virgins.

24 And concerning this it is fitting that we pray in it with work and prayer and weeping; and as for those who have in them work: they shall become [worthy of] these one [prayers] which are fitting for them, according to the measure of their strength and of their power; and if they add unto this, their joy shall be [greater]; for they are fitting, according to the measure of their strength and of their knowledge.

25 And if it be that in their work [they are occupied], save in the night of the Sabbath — [the night] in which those who have in them work in it — they are fitting for them likewise: the third, and the seventh, and the fifth, and the evening, upon the reading of the books; for on the day of the Sabbath Christ rose from the dead.

26 And concerning this He taught them, that they should glorify Him and sanctify Him in it always; and in it He cometh to judge the living and the dead; and it is fitting for them that they read His books on the day of the Sabbath wholly, that they may be ready on that great day, and may rise with Him in His glory, as they were with Him in this world; and not as the believers [are] — as the pretext of God was completed, and He shall give wealth unto him on the day of their resurrection.

27 So shall He rejoice with them, and give them wealth for ever on the day of their resurrection; and as He is grieved with them, and they fast two days — Wednesday and Friday — these are sad in them; His helpers, on account of the suffering and the death and the crucifixion: so shall they be delivered from grief for ever, and from hunger, and from one [?] for ever; and He shall give them joy for ever with His helpers.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Purity of faith is presented as what drives Satan, weakened, away, and God reckons prayers alike for those with work and those without, who stand and spend the night in prayer. The chapter closes with rejoicing and wealth for ever on the day of resurrection, set against fasting for two days.
