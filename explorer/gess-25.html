The Text — Gәssәw 25 (Ge'ez text of W. Fell, Canones apostolorum Aethiopice (Leipzig, 1871), public domain)

1 [Topic:] It is fitting that the chief of the metropolitans [?] know the bishops of the whole province.

2 And it is their duty to know who is the first among them, and they shall call him the metropolitan [?]. And they shall not undertake an important work without the consent of the great one [?]; and they shall do … which is their duty [?] according to their dioceses [?] and according to the people who are in their dioceses [?].

3 But he who has been ordained as metropolitan over them, let him not do anything at all [?] without the consent of all the bishops; and thus let them all be one association, and praise God through Christ Jesus and the Holy Spirit.

4 And a bishop shall not dare [?] to ordain anyone [?] outside [?] of his diocese, in localities that do not belong to him [?].

5 And if he is found to have done this without the consent of the head — of this place and these villages and cities [?] — let him be deposed [?], he and those whom he ordained [?].

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

This canon organizes the province: the bishops are to know who is first among them and call him the metropolitan, and no important work is to be undertaken without the consent of the great one — while the metropolitan himself may do nothing at all without the consent of all the bishops, so that they are one association praising God. A bishop who ordains outside his own diocese without the head's consent is deposed, with those he ordained. Interpreted Paraphrase of the chapter's own text; Standard corpus, Gessew 25

Unfamiliar terms

  • Metropolitan: The first among the bishops of a province, whom the bishops are to know and call by that name. He acts only with all the bishops' consent, as they act only with his. Interpreted Reading of the chapter's own usage; Standard corpus, Gessew
  • Diocese: Each bishop's own territory; ordaining outside it without the head's consent brings deposition for bishop and ordained alike. Interpreted Reading of the chapter's own usage; Standard corpus, Gessew
