Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

In fragments, this chapter follows the Passion sequence: garments spread beneath his feet, a return to Bethany (Bitania), teaching and rebuking, a Wednesday fast kept by the apostles, Thursday night at Bitania and the betrayal table, Judas going forth, arrest in the night of Zoberb, mocking, and delivery to the lord of Rome, closing on Friday. The text is broken throughout, with readings marked (?), so the sequence above is only what the surviving words support.
