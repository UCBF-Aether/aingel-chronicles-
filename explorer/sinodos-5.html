Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The chapter's first five verses do not repeat chapter 4 but point to it, each reading (See ch4 v1.) and so on, before continuing with deacons as doers of good works by night and day, not exalting themselves above the poor, and with Cephas on three ma'asabat appointed for prayer. It then moves to the ordination of a bishop — salutation, the deacon bringing the oblation, the bishop's and people's exchanged responses, and a long thanksgiving prayer recalling that God sent his Son into a virgin's womb, born that his will might be fulfilled — and much of the prayer survives only in fragments.
