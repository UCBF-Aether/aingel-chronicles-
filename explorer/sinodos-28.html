The Text — Sinodos 28 (Ge'ez text of G. Horner, The Statutes of the Apostles; or, Canones Ecclesiastici (London: Williams & Norgate, 1904), public domain)

1 Statute 58. The bishop shall bless, and they shall not bless him; and he shall bless the firstfruits himself; and he shall ordain men; and he shall offer the oblation; and he shall administer the eulogia[?]; and from a bishop[?] he shall receive …[?] — never from the presbyters at all. The bishop shall depose all the ordained whom it is right to depose, except a bishop, for he alone is not able.

2 The presbyter shall bless and shall be blessed; and he shall take the blessing from the bishop and from a presbyter like himself; and he ministereth unto a presbyter like himself. He shall ordain[?], but shall not depose. Moreover, let him reprove[?] those who have sinned …[?].

3 If they be …[?] in such a case[?].

4 The deacon shall not bless, nor give the blessing; he shall take the blessing from the bishop or from the presbyter.

5 He shall not baptize nor offer; but when the bishop[?] hath offered, or if the presbyter be not there, he himself shall minister unto the people — not as one of the priests, but as a minister of the priests.

6 Statute 59. And afterward: the ordained presbyters shall not do the work of the deacons, nor anything which is not theirs.

7 The deaconesses, women, shall not bless, and shall do nothing of what the presbyters and deacons do.

8 But they shall keep the gates of the church; apart from this there is nothing at all else which they do.

9 except the ministry unto the presbyters, and the baptizing of women — for this is fitting for them. And the deacon shall reprove[?] the subdeacon[?], and the reader, and the singers, and the deaconess.

10 Thus indeed it is fitting …[?], if the presbyter be not present. But for the subdeacon[?] it is not fitting to reprove[?], neither the readers, nor the ministers of the Scripture, nor the deaconess, women.

11 neither those in orders nor the people — for they are the servants of the deacons.

12 Statute 60. Concerning firstfruits and tithes.

13 And all the firstfruits and the tithes they shall bring unto the bishops, and unto the presbyters, and unto the deacons, for their sustenance; but all the tithes they shall bring for the sustenance of the rest of the ordained that remain, and for the virgins, and for the widows, and for them that are afflicted by poverty[?], for the firstfruits are for the priests and for their ministers.

Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

Statute 58 sets out the ranks: the bishop blesses and is not blessed, blesses the firstfruits himself, ordains, offers the oblation and administers the eulogia; the presbyter blesses and is blessed, takes blessing from bishop and fellow presbyter, and shall ordain but shall not depose, as the text's uncertain wording has it; the deacon neither blesses nor gives blessing, neither baptizes nor offers, but ministers to the people as a minister of the priests. Statute 59 adds that presbyters shall not do deacons' work, and deaconesses shall not bless or do what presbyters and deacons do, but shall keep the gates of the church, minister to the presbyters, and baptize women. Statute 60 closes the chapter with firstfruits and tithes brought to bishops, presbyters and deacons for their sustenance. Interpreted Summary of this chapter's text, as printed above
