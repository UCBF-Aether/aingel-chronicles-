Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

The dead are to be remembered on the third, seventh, tenth, thirtieth, and fortieth days, and on two further days lost in gaps. On the fortieth day, the soul of one who died baptized is said to stand before Christ and be judged according to his work.
