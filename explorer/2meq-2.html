Understand (editorial explanation — not Scripture)

Overview

A prophet speaks judgment on the destroyer: God's wrath is kindled, his memorial shall be destroyed from the earth, and he is like grass before wind and ashes the winds carry away, yet repentance remains open, and if he repents not and humbles not himself with weeping, wailing and a pure heart, God will not forgive his sins. Meqabis weeps before God, puts on ashes, rends his garments and puts on sackcloth, declaring that God's word by his prophet stands: his eyes do not slumber, his ears are not deaf, and he will perform what he spoke in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye.
