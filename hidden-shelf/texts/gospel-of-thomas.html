The Gospel of Thomas

A sayings collection attributed to Jesus, kept for history.

NOT CANON. NOT SCRIPTURE. Kept here for extra thoughts and for historical value.
This book is not part of the Bible. Read it for extra thoughts and for historical value — and weigh everything against the Book itself.

Translated anew for Aingel from the Coptic text.

Translator's Note

Source manuscript. The complete text survives in Sahidic Coptic as the second tractate of Nag Hammadi Codex II (NHC II,2, pages 32–51), a codex copied around the mid-fourth century and found near Nag Hammadi, Egypt, in 1945; the manuscript is now held by the Coptic Museum in Cairo. Its closing title reads, in Coptic, ⲡⲉⲩⲁⲅⲅⲉⲗⲓⲟⲛ ⲡⲕⲁⲧⲁ ⲑⲱⲙⲁⲥ — "the gospel according to Thomas."

Original language. The Coptic is itself a translation: the work was almost certainly first composed in Greek. Three Greek fragments from Oxyrhynchus survive and overlap the Coptic — P.Oxy. 1 (sayings 26–33, and part of 77), P.Oxy. 654 (prologue and sayings 1–7), and P.Oxy. 655 (sayings 36–39). Where the Greek and Coptic differ in small details, the Coptic order and numbering used here are followed, and the difference is noted only where it changes the sense.

Method. This is a literal, fresh rendering made in my own words from the Coptic text as established in the standard critical edition (Bentley Layton's edition of Nag Hammadi Codex II,2–7, which prints the Coptic alongside the Greek fragments). Existing English renderings — above all Thomas O. Lambdin's Nag Hammadi Library translation, and the Greek-fragment renderings of Grenfell and Hunt — were consulted only as a sense check, to confirm that a saying had been understood; no wording has been copied from them. Where my phrasing happens to resemble a well-known rendering, that is because the Coptic leaves little room for anything else, not because the phrase was lifted.

Uncertainties — read honestly. The Coptic manuscript is in good condition overall, but it is not perfect. Damaged, lacunose, or genuinely doubtful passages are flagged inline as [gap in manuscript] or [uncertain] and are never smoothed into completeness. The clearest cases are Saying 61 (two passages where the speakers and the text break off), Saying 71 (its ending is lost), Saying 93 (its ending is lost), and Saying 101 (a word is missing mid-saying). Saying 30 and the second half of Saying 77 appear together in the Greek fragment P.Oxy. 1, but stand apart in the Coptic; both are given in their Coptic places here. Scholars also note that Saying 114 may have been added to the collection later than the rest; it is included because it stands in the Coptic manuscript, and the note is recorded here rather than hidden. Saying numbers are the standard modern numbering, not numbers written in the ancient manuscript.

What this book is. It is a collection of 114 sayings, with no story of Jesus' birth, death, or resurrection. Many sayings resemble sayings in Matthew, Mark, and Luke; many do not. Its date and authorship are debated — most scholars place its composition in the second century and do not regard the apostle Thomas as its author, whatever the prologue claims. Nothing on this page presents it as canonical Scripture.

Prologue

These are the hidden sayings spoken by the living Jesus, which Didymos Judas Thomas wrote down.

Saying 1

And he said: "Whoever discovers the meaning of these sayings will not taste death."

Saying 2

Jesus said: "Let the one who seeks go on seeking until he finds. And when he finds, he will be troubled. And when he is troubled, he will be amazed, and he will rule over the All."

Saying 3

Jesus said: "If those who guide you say to you, 'Look, the kingdom is in the sky,' then the birds of the sky will get there before you. If they say to you, 'It is in the sea,' then the fish will get there before you. No — the kingdom is within you, and it is outside you. When you know yourselves, then you will be known, and you will understand that you are children of the living Father. But if you do not know yourselves, then you live in poverty, and you yourselves are that poverty."

Saying 4

Jesus said: "A person old in days will not hold back from asking a little child seven days old about the place of life, and he will live. For many who are first will become last, and they will become a single one."

Saying 5

Jesus said: "Know what is right before your face, and what is hidden from you will be uncovered for you. For there is nothing hidden that will not be made plain."

Saying 6

His disciples questioned him and said to him: "Do you want us to fast? How shall we pray? Shall we give to the poor? What rule of eating shall we keep?" Jesus said: "Do not speak falsehood, and do not do what you hate, for all things are open in the sight of the truth. For there is nothing hidden that will not be revealed, and nothing covered that will remain without being uncovered."

Saying 7

Jesus said: "Blessed is the lion that a man eats, so that the lion becomes a man; and cursed is the man whom the lion eats, so that the man becomes a lion."

Saying 8

And he said: "A person is like a wise fisherman who threw his net into the sea and pulled it up from the sea full of little fish. Among them the wise fisherman found one fine, large fish. He threw all the little fish back into the sea and picked the large fish without trouble. Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear."

Saying 9

Jesus said: "Look: the sower went out, took a handful of seed, and scattered it. Some fell on the road, and the birds came and gathered it up. Others fell on rock, and did not take root in the earth, and did not put forth ears. And others fell on thorns, and they choked the seed, and worms ate it. And others fell on good earth, and it bore good fruit: it yielded sixty per measure and a hundred and twenty per measure."

Saying 10

Jesus said: "I have thrown fire upon the world, and see — I am watching over it until it burns bright."

Saying 11

Jesus said: "This heaven will pass away, and the one above it will pass away. The dead are not living, and the living will not die. In the days when you ate what is dead, you made it what is living. When you come to dwell in the light, what will you do? On the day when you were one, you became two. But when you become two, what will you do?"

Saying 12

The disciples said to Jesus: "We know you will leave us. Who will be our leader then?" Jesus said to them: "Wherever you find yourselves, you are to go to James the righteous, for whose sake heaven and earth came into being."

Saying 13

Jesus said to his disciples: "Set me beside someone and tell me whom I am like." Simon Peter said to him: "You are like a righteous angel." Matthew said to him: "You are like a wise philosopher." Thomas said to him: "Teacher, my mouth is altogether unable to say whom you are like." Jesus said: "I am not your teacher. Because you have drunk, you have become drunk from the bubbling spring that I have measured out." And he took him aside and told him three things. When Thomas came back to his companions, they asked him: "What did Jesus say to you?" Thomas said to them: "If I tell you one of the things he told me, you will take up stones and throw them at me, and fire will come out of the stones and burn you up."

Saying 14

Jesus said to them: "If you fast, you will bring sin upon yourselves; and if you pray, you will be condemned; and if you give alms, you will harm your spirits. When you go into any country and walk through the regions, if they welcome you, eat what they set before you, and heal the sick among them. For what goes into your mouth will not defile you, but what comes out of your mouth — that is what will defile you."

Saying 15

Jesus said: "When you see one who was not born of woman, fall on your faces and worship him. That one is your father."

Saying 16

Jesus said: "People think, perhaps, that it is peace I have come to cast upon the world. They do not know that it is division I have come to cast upon the earth: fire, sword, and war. For there will be five in a house: three against two, and two against three, father against son, and son against father. And they will stand alone."

Saying 17

Jesus said: "I will give you what no eye has seen, and what no ear has heard, and what no hand has touched, and what has never risen in the human heart."

Saying 18

The disciples said to Jesus: "Tell us how our end will be." Jesus said: "Have you found the beginning, then, that you look for the end? For where the beginning is, there the end will be. Blessed is the one who will take his place in the beginning: he will know the end, and he will not taste death."

Saying 19

Jesus said: "Blessed is the one who came into being before coming into being. If you become my disciples and listen to my words, these stones will serve you. For there are five trees for you in Paradise, which stay undisturbed summer and winter, and whose leaves do not fall. Whoever knows them will not taste death."

Saying 20

The disciples said to Jesus: "Tell us what the kingdom of heaven is like." He said to them: "It is like a mustard seed. It is the smallest of all seeds. But when it falls on tilled ground, it grows into a great plant and becomes a shelter for the birds of the sky."

Saying 21

Mary said to Jesus: "Whom are your disciples like?" He said: "They are like children who have settled in a field that is not theirs. When the owners of the field come, they will say, 'Give us back our field.' They will strip off their clothes in their presence, in order to hand the field back to them and give it back. Therefore I say: if the owner of a house knows the thief is coming, he will keep watch before he comes, and will not let him break into his house, his domain, to carry off his goods. You, then, guard yourselves against the world. Arm yourselves with great strength, lest the robbers find a way in to you — for the trouble you expect will surely come. Let there be among you a person of understanding. When the grain ripened, he came at once with his sickle in his hand and reaped it. Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear."

Saying 22

Jesus saw infants being nursed. He said to his disciples: "These infants being nursed are like those who enter the kingdom." They said to him: "Shall we, then, as children, enter the kingdom?" Jesus said to them: "When you make the two one, and when you make the inside like the outside, and the outside like the inside, and the above like the below, and when you make the male and the female one and the same, so that the male is not male and the female is not female, and when you make eyes in place of an eye, and a hand in place of a hand, and a foot in place of a foot, and an image in place of an image — then you will enter the kingdom."

Saying 23

Jesus said: "I will choose you, one out of a thousand, and two out of ten thousand, and they will stand as a single one."

Saying 24

His disciples said to him: "Show us the place where you are, for we must seek it." He said to them: "Whoever has ears, let him hear. There is light inside a person of light, and it lights the whole world. If he does not shine, he is darkness."

Saying 25

Jesus said: "Love your brother as your own soul; guard him as the pupil of your eye."

Saying 26

Jesus said: "You see the speck in your brother's eye, but you do not see the beam in your own eye. When you throw the beam out of your own eye, then you will see clearly to throw the speck out of your brother's eye."

Saying 27

Jesus said: "If you do not fast from the world, you will not find the kingdom. If you do not keep the Sabbath as a Sabbath, you will not see the Father."

Saying 28

Jesus said: "I stood in the middle of the world, and I appeared to them in flesh. I found all of them drunk, and I found none of them thirsty. And my soul grieved for the children of humankind, because they are blind in their hearts and do not see. For empty they came into the world, and empty they try to leave the world. But for now they are drunk. When they shake off their wine, then they will turn back."

Saying 29

Jesus said: "If the flesh came into being because of spirit, it is a marvel. But if spirit came into being because of the body, it is a marvel of marvels. Indeed, I am astonished at how this great wealth has taken up its dwelling in this poverty."

Saying 30

Jesus said: "Where there are three gods, they are gods. Where there are two or one, I am with him." [Note: in the Greek fragment P.Oxy. 1 this saying stands joined to the second half of Saying 77.]

Saying 31

Jesus said: "No prophet is welcomed in his own village, and no physician heals those who know him."

Saying 32

Jesus said: "A city built on a high mountain and made strong cannot fall, nor can it be hidden."

Saying 33

Jesus said: "Proclaim from your rooftops what you hear in your ear. For no one lights a lamp and puts it under a basket, nor does he put it in a hidden place; rather, he sets it on a lampstand, so that everyone who comes in and goes out will see its light."

Saying 34

Jesus said: "If a blind person leads a blind person, both of them will fall into a pit."

Saying 35

Jesus said: "No one can enter the house of a strong man and take it by force unless he binds his hands; then he will be able to plunder his house."

Saying 36

Jesus said: "Do not worry, from morning until evening and from evening until morning, about what you will wear." [Note: the Greek fragment P.Oxy. 655 gives a longer form here, about food and clothing and the lilies; the Coptic is short.]

Saying 37

His disciples said: "When will you be revealed to us, and when shall we see you?" Jesus said: "When you strip off your clothes without being ashamed, and take your garments and lay them under your feet like little children and trample them — then you will see the Son of the Living One, and you will not be afraid."

Saying 38

Jesus said: "Many times you have wanted to hear these words I am speaking to you, and you have no one else to hear them from. There will be days when you will look for me and will not find me."

Saying 39

Jesus said: "The Pharisees and the scribes took the keys of knowledge and hid them. They themselves did not go in, and they did not let in those who wanted to go in. You, however, be as wise as serpents and as innocent as doves."

Saying 40

Jesus said: "A grapevine has been planted outside the Father, and since it is not sound, it will be torn up by its roots and destroyed."

Saying 41

Jesus said: "Whoever has something in his hand will be given more, and whoever has nothing will be stripped of even the little he has."

Saying 42

Jesus said: "Become passers-by."

Saying 43

His disciples said to him: "Who are you, that you say these things to us?" Jesus said to them: "You do not know who I am from what I say to you; instead you have become like the Jews, for they either love the tree and hate its fruit, or love the fruit and hate the tree."

Saying 44

Jesus said: "Whoever speaks against the Father will be forgiven, and whoever speaks against the Son will be forgiven, but whoever speaks against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven, either on earth or in heaven."

Saying 45

Jesus said: "Grapes are not picked from thorns, nor are figs gathered from thistles, for they bear no fruit. A good person brings good things out of his storehouse; an evil person brings evil things out of his evil storehouse, which is in his heart, and speaks evil. For out of the overflow of the heart he brings forth evil things."

Saying 46

Jesus said: "Among those born of women, from Adam down to John the Baptist, there is no one greater than John the Baptist, that his eyes should not be lowered before him. Yet I have said: whichever one of you becomes a child will know the kingdom and will become greater than John."

Saying 47

Jesus said: "A person cannot ride two horses or draw two bows. And a servant cannot serve two masters, or else he will honour the one and despise the other. No one drinks old wine and at once wants to drink new wine. And new wine is not poured into old wineskins, lest they burst; nor is old wine poured into a new wineskin, lest it spoil it. An old patch is not sewn onto a new garment, for a tear would result."

Saying 48

Jesus said: "If two make peace with each other in this one house, they will say to the mountain, 'Move away,' and it will move away."

Saying 49

Jesus said: "Blessed are the solitary and the chosen, for you will find the kingdom. For you came from it, and to it you will return."

Saying 50

Jesus said: "If they say to you, 'Where did you come from?', say to them: 'We came from the light, the place where the light came into being by itself, and stood, and appeared in their image.' If they say to you, 'Is it you?', say: 'We are its children, we are the chosen of the living Father.' If they ask you, 'What is the sign of your Father in you?', say to them: 'It is movement and rest.'"

Saying 51

His disciples said to him: "When will the rest of the dead come, and when will the new world come?" He said to them: "What you are looking for has already come, but you do not recognise it."

Saying 52

His disciples said to him: "Twenty-four prophets spoke in Israel, and all of them spoke in you." He said to them: "You have left out the living one in your midst, and have spoken only of the dead."

Saying 53

His disciples said to him: "Is circumcision useful or not?" He said to them: "If it were useful, their father would beget them circumcised already from their mother. No — the true circumcision, in spirit, has become wholly profitable."

Saying 54

Jesus said: "Blessed are the poor, for yours is the kingdom of heaven."

Saying 55

Jesus said: "Whoever does not hate his father and his mother cannot become my disciple. And whoever does not hate his brothers and sisters, and take up his cross in my way, will not be worthy of me."

Saying 56

Jesus said: "Whoever has understood the world has found only a corpse, and whoever has found a corpse is greater than the world."

Saying 57

Jesus said: "The kingdom of the Father is like a man who had good seed. His enemy came at night and sowed weeds among the good seed. The man did not let them pull up the weeds; he said to them: 'I am afraid that when you go to pull up the weeds, you will pull up the wheat along with them.' For on the day of harvest the weeds will be plain to see, and they will be pulled up and burned."

Saying 58

Jesus said: "Blessed is the person who has suffered and found life."

Saying 59

Jesus said: "Look to the living one while you are alive, lest you die and seek to see him and be unable to."

Saying 60

They saw a Samaritan carrying a lamb on his way to Judea. He said to his disciples: "That man is going around with the lamb." They said to him: "So that he may kill it and eat it." He said to them: "While it is alive he will not eat it, but only when he has killed it and it has become a corpse." They said to him: "He cannot do otherwise." He said to them: "You too, seek a place for yourselves within rest, lest you become a corpse and be eaten."

Saying 61

Jesus said: "Two will rest on one bed: one will die, and the other will live." Salome said: "Who are you, man, that you [gap in manuscript] have come up onto my couch and eaten from my table?" Jesus said to her: "I am he who comes from the undivided. I was given some of my Father's things." [gap in manuscript — the speakers of the next lines are lost] "I am your disciple." [gap in manuscript] "Therefore I say: if he is destroyed, he will be filled with light, but if he is divided, he will be filled with darkness."

Saying 62

Jesus said: "It is to those worthy of my mysteries that I speak my mysteries. Do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing."

Saying 63

Jesus said: "There was a rich man who had a great deal of money. He said: 'I will put my money to work, so that I may sow and reap and plant, and fill my storehouses with produce, so that I lack nothing.' Those were his plans, but that very night he died. Let whoever has ears hear."

Saying 64

Jesus said: "A man had guests, and when he had made the dinner ready, he sent his servant to call the guests. He went to the first and said to him: 'My master invites you.' He said: 'I have business with some merchants; they are coming to me this evening, and I must go and give them my orders. I ask to be excused from the dinner.' He went to another and said to him: 'My master has invited you.' He said to him: 'I have just bought a house, and I am needed for the day; I will have no spare time.' He went to another and said to him: 'My master invites you.' He said to him: 'My friend is getting married, and I am to prepare the feast; I will not be able to come. I ask to be excused from the dinner.' He went to another and said to him: 'My master invites you.' He said to him: 'I have just bought a farm, and I am on my way to collect the rent; I will not be able to come. I ask to be excused.' The servant went back and said to his master: 'Those you invited to the dinner have asked to be excused.' The master said to his servant: 'Go out to the streets and bring back whoever you happen to meet, so that they may dine.' Traders and merchants will not enter the places of my Father."

Saying 65

He said: "There was a good man who owned a vineyard. He rented it to tenant farmers, so that they might work it and he might take the produce from them. He sent his servant so the tenants might give him the produce of the vineyard. They seized his servant and beat him, nearly killing him. The servant went back and told his master. The master said: 'Perhaps he did not know them.' He sent another servant; the tenants beat this one too. Then the owner sent his son and said: 'Perhaps they will respect my son.' But because the tenants knew he was the heir of the vineyard, they seized him and killed him. Let whoever has ears hear."

Saying 66

Jesus said: "Show me the stone the builders rejected. That one is the cornerstone."

Saying 67

Jesus said: "If one who knows the All still feels a lack in himself, he is wholly lacking."

Saying 68

Jesus said: "Blessed are you when you are hated and persecuted. Wherever you have been persecuted, they will find no place."

Saying 69

Jesus said: "Blessed are those persecuted within themselves: it is they who have truly known the Father. Blessed are the hungry, for the belly of the one who hungers will be filled."

Saying 70

Jesus said: "If you bring forth what is within you, what you have will save you. If you do not have it within you, what you do not have will kill you."

Saying 71

Jesus said: "I will tear down this house, and no one will be able to build it [gap in manuscript — the ending is lost]."

Saying 72

A man said to him: "Tell my brothers to divide my father's inheritance with me." He said to him: "Man, who made me a divider?" He turned to his disciples and said to them: "I am not a divider, am I?"

Saying 73

Jesus said: "The harvest is great, but the workers are few. Beg the Lord, therefore, to send workers out to the harvest."

Saying 74

He said: "Lord, there are many around the drinking trough, but there is nothing in the well."

Saying 75

Jesus said: "Many are standing at the door, but it is the solitary who will enter the bridal chamber."

Saying 76

Jesus said: "The kingdom of the Father is like a merchant who had a load of goods and found a pearl. That merchant was shrewd: he sold the goods and bought the pearl alone for himself. You too, seek his treasure that does not fail and does not pass away, where no moth comes near to eat it and no worm destroys it."

Saying 77

Jesus said: "It is I who am the light above them all. It is I who am the All. From me the All came forth, and to me the All returns. Split a piece of wood, and I am there. Lift the stone, and you will find me there."

Saying 78

Jesus said: "Why did you go out into the desert? To see a reed swaying in the wind? And to see a man dressed in fine clothes, like your kings and your great men? Fine clothes are upon them, and they cannot discern the truth."

Saying 79

A woman from the crowd said to him: "Blessed is the womb that bore you, and the breasts that nursed you." He said to her: "Blessed are those who have heard the word of the Father and have truly kept it. For there will be days when you will say: 'Blessed is the womb that has not conceived, and the breasts that have not given milk.'"

Saying 80

Jesus said: "Whoever has recognised the world has found the body, but whoever has found the body is greater than the world."

Saying 81

Jesus said: "Let the one who has grown rich become king, and let the one who holds power give it up."

Saying 82

Jesus said: "Whoever is near me is near the fire, and whoever is far from me is far from the kingdom."

Saying 83

Jesus said: "The images are plain to see for people, but the light within them stays hidden in the image of the light of the Father. He will be revealed, but his image will stay hidden by his light."

Saying 84

Jesus said: "When you see your likeness, you rejoice. But when you see your images, which came into being before you, and which neither die nor are revealed — how much you will have to endure!"

Saying 85

Jesus said: "Adam came into being from a great power and a great wealth, but he did not become worthy of you. For had he been worthy, he would not have tasted death."

Saying 86

Jesus said: "Foxes have their holes, and birds have their nests, but the Son of Man has nowhere to lay his head and rest."

Saying 87

Jesus said: "Wretched is the body that depends on a body, and wretched is the soul that depends on these two."

Saying 88

Jesus said: "The angels and the prophets will come to you and give you the things you already have. And you too, give them the things you have, and say to yourselves: 'When will they come and take what is theirs?'"

Saying 89

Jesus said: "Why do you wash the outside of the cup? Do you not see that the one who made the inside is the same one who made the outside?"

Saying 90

Jesus said: "Come to me, for my yoke is easy and my rule is gentle, and you will find rest for yourselves."

Saying 91

They said to him: "Tell us who you are, so that we may believe in you." He said to them: "You read the face of the sky and the earth, but you have not recognised the one before you, and you do not know how to read this moment."

Saying 92

Jesus said: "Seek, and you will find. Yet what you asked me about in former times, which I did not tell you then — now I want to tell it, but you do not ask after it."

Saying 93

Jesus said: "Do not give what is holy to dogs, lest they throw it on the dung heap. Do not throw pearls to pigs, lest they [gap in manuscript — the ending is lost]."

Saying 94

Jesus said: "Whoever seeks will find, and whoever knocks will be let in."

Saying 95

Jesus said: "If you have money, do not lend it at interest; give it to one from whom you will not get it back."

Saying 96

Jesus said: "The kingdom of the Father is like a certain woman. She took a little yeast, hid it in some dough, and made it into large loaves. Let whoever has ears hear."

Saying 97

Jesus said: "The kingdom of the Father is like a certain woman carrying a jar full of meal. While she was walking on the road, still some way from home, the handle of the jar broke, and the meal spilled out behind her on the road. She did not notice; she had seen no accident. When she reached her house, she set the jar down and found it empty."

Saying 98

Jesus said: "The kingdom of the Father is like a certain man who wanted to kill a powerful man. In his own house he drew his sword and thrust it into the wall, to find out whether his hand would carry it through. Then he killed the powerful man."

Saying 99

The disciples said to him: "Your brothers and your mother are standing outside." He said to them: "Those here who do the will of my Father are my brothers and my mother. It is they who will enter the kingdom of my Father."

Saying 100

They showed Jesus a gold coin and said to him: "Caesar's men demand taxes from us." He said to them: "Give Caesar what belongs to Caesar, give God what belongs to God, and give me what is mine."

Saying 101

Jesus said: "Whoever does not hate his father and his mother as I do cannot become my disciple. And whoever does not love his father and his mother as I do cannot become my disciple. For my mother [gap in manuscript], but my true mother gave me life."

Saying 102

Jesus said: "Woe to the Pharisees, for they are like a dog sleeping in the manger of the oxen: he neither eats himself nor lets the oxen eat."

Saying 103

Jesus said: "Fortunate is the person who knows where the robbers will break in, so that he may rise, gather his household, and arm himself before they invade."

Saying 104

They said to Jesus: "Come, let us pray today and let us fast." Jesus said: "What wrong have I done, or in what have I been overcome? But when the bridegroom leaves the bridal chamber, then let them fast and pray."

Saying 105

Jesus said: "Whoever knows the father and the mother will be called the son of a harlot."

Saying 106

Jesus said: "When you make the two one, you will become children of humankind, and when you say, 'Mountain, move away,' it will move away."

Saying 107

Jesus said: "The kingdom is like a shepherd who had a hundred sheep. One of them, the largest, strayed. He left the ninety-nine and looked for that one until he found it. When he had taken such trouble, he said to the sheep: 'I care for you more than for the ninety-nine.'"

Saying 108

Jesus said: "Whoever drinks from my mouth will become like me. I myself will become he, and the hidden things will be revealed to him."

Saying 109

Jesus said: "The kingdom is like a man who had a hidden treasure in his field without knowing it. And after he died, he left it to his son. The son did not know about the treasure. He inherited the field and sold it. And the one who bought it went ploughing and found the treasure, and began lending money at interest to whomever he wished."

Saying 110

Jesus said: "Whoever finds the world and becomes rich, let him give up the world."

Saying 111

Jesus said: "The heavens and the earth will be rolled up before you, and whoever lives from the Living One will not see death." Does not Jesus say: "Whoever finds himself is greater than the world?"

Saying 112

Jesus said: "Woe to the flesh that depends on the soul, and woe to the soul that depends on the flesh."

Saying 113

His disciples said to him: "When will the kingdom come?" Jesus said: "It will not come by watching for it. It will not be a matter of saying, 'Here it is' or 'There it is.' Rather, the kingdom of the Father is spread out over the earth, and people do not see it."

Saying 114

Simon Peter said to him: "Let Mary leave us, for women are not worthy of life." Jesus said: "Look, I will guide her so as to make her male, so that she too may become a living spirit like you males. For every woman who makes herself male will enter the kingdom of heaven." [Note: scholars observe this saying may have been added to the collection later than the rest; it stands in the Coptic manuscript and is given here as it stands.]
