TRADITION Unknown  ·  ERA 15th century  ·  STATUS LOCKED (undeciphered)

The Voynich manuscript is a whole illustrated book, written in the 15th century in an unknown language and an unknown script that nobody has cracked. Its pages fall into botanical, astronomical, and biological sections, with drawings of plants no botanist recognizes, star charts, and strange bathing figures.

It matters precisely because it resists reading: centuries of codebreakers, linguists, and scholars have proposed solutions, and none has held. Whatever it says — herbal, cipher, hoax, or something else entirely — remains unproven.

There is no readable text to host here, and any "translation" offered would be invented. So this shelf keeps the fact of the book, honestly, and nothing more.
