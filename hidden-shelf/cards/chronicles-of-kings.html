CITED IN Throughout 1–2 Kings  ·  STATUS LOST

The Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel and the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah were the royal annals of the two kingdoms, cited again and again throughout 1 and 2 Kings with the refrain, “are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings?”

They were court records of each king’s reign, not Scripture itself. They are lost as separate books, though their substance partly survives inside Kings, which drew on them.
