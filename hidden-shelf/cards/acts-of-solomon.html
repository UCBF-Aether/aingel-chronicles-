The Book of the Acts of Solomon

NOT CANON. NOT SCRIPTURE. Kept here for extra thoughts and for historical value.

CITED IN 1 Kings 11:41  ·  STATUS LOST

The Book of the Acts of Solomon is cited at the close of Solomon’s reign: “And the rest of the acts of Solomon, and all that he did, and his wisdom, are they not written in the book of the acts of Solomon?” (1 Kings 11:41).

It was a royal source — annals of Solomon’s reign, deeds, and wisdom — not Scripture itself. The book is lost as a separate work, and its contents beyond what Kings preserves are not known.
