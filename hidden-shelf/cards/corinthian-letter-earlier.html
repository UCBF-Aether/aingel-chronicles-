CITED IN 1 Corinthians 5:9  ·  STATUS LOST

Paul refers in 1 Corinthians 5:9 to an earlier letter he had already written to Corinth: “I wrote unto you in an epistle not to company with fornicators.” That earlier letter came before 1 Corinthians and is distinct from it.

It is lost. No copy survives, and its contents beyond that one instruction are not known.

HONEST NOTE: The later 3 Corinthians letters are a different, ancient text, not this earlier letter to Corinth.
