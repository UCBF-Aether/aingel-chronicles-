The Book of the Wars of the LORD

NOT CANON. NOT SCRIPTURE. Kept here for extra thoughts and for historical value.

CITED IN Numbers 21:14  ·  STATUS LOST

The Book of the Wars of the LORD is cited in Numbers 21:14 as a record of the LORD’s deeds in Israel’s wilderness journey. It appears to have been an early collection of songs or accounts of those wars and marches, from before Israel entered the land.

The original is lost. The only surviving piece is the short passage quoted in Numbers itself:

“What he did in the Red sea, and in the brooks of Arnon, And at the stream of the brooks that goeth down to the dwelling of Ar, and lieth upon the border of Moab.”

HONEST NOTE: That quoted scrap is the only surviving piece. Nothing else of this book is known, and nothing more of its contents is claimed here.
