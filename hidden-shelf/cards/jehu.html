The Book of Jehu son of Hanani

NOT CANON. NOT SCRIPTURE. Kept here for extra thoughts and for historical value.

CITED IN 2 Chronicles 20:34  ·  STATUS LOST

The Book of Jehu son of Hanani is cited as a record of the reign of Jehoshaphat, king of Judah: it was “written in the book of Jehu the son of Hanani, who is mentioned in the book of the kings of Israel” (2 Chronicles 20:34). Jehu son of Hanani was the prophet who rebuked Jehoshaphat for allying with Ahab.

The book itself is lost. No copy survives, and its contents beyond its place as a record of Jehoshaphat’s reign are not known.
