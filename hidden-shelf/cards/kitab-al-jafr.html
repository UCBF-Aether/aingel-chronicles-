TRADITION Shia, esoteric  ·  ERA Attributed to the early Imams  ·  STATUS DESCRIBED, NOT HOSTED

Kitab al-Jafr is a Shia esoteric book attributed to the Imams — above all Ali, and Ja'far al-Sadiq, whose name the tradition of jafr (letter divination) carries. In Shia telling it is a book of secret knowledge passed down through the Imams, holding hidden meanings of letters, knowledge of events past and to come, and the inner sense of revelation.

It matters historically because it sits at the heart of a long esoteric current: a claim that true knowledge is guarded, inherited, and veiled, and that the Imam holds the key. Writings on jafr have circulated for centuries in that current, and the book itself remains more a revered, elusive presence than a single text anyone can open and check.

This is a description card only. No text is hosted here, and no divination procedures or operative instructions are reproduced — its place in history is the point, not its practice.
