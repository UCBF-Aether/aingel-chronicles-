TRADITION Mandaean  ·  ERA Ancient, in manuscripts from the 16th century onward  ·  STATUS TOO LARGE TO TRANSLATE WHOLE

Ginza Rabba, the Great Treasure, is the holy book of the Mandaeans — their scripture, not ours. Written in Mandaic, an Eastern Aramaic dialect, it gathers theology, creation stories, ethics, history, and mythic narrative in the Right Ginza, while the Left Ginza turns to the soul's journey after death.

The Mandaeans are an ancient baptizing community still living today, and this book is the heart of their faith. It is kept on this shelf for historical value and honest curiosity, with that line kept clear: it is theirs, and it is not canon for us.

A full fresh translation from Mandaic is far too large a translation job for this shelf. This card therefore links outward rather than pretending the whole book could be translated here.

READ ELSEWHERE: Mark Lidzbarski's 1925 German translation, Der Ginza oder das grosse Buch der Mandäer, is freely readable at the Internet Archive: archive.org — Ginza, der Schatz oder das Grosse Buch der Mandäer. No fresh translation is included on this card.
