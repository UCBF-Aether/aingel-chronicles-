The Book of Jasher

The Book of the Upright

NOT CANON. NOT SCRIPTURE. Kept here for extra thoughts and for historical value.

CITED IN Joshua 10:13, 2 Samuel 1:18  ·  STATUS LOST

The Book of Jasher is a lost work cited by Scripture itself: “Is not this written in the book of Jasher?” (Joshua 10:13), and again as the source for David’s lament over Saul and Jonathan (2 Samuel 1:18).

Most likely it was a collection of ancient poems and songs — heroic and memorial verse from Israel’s early history — rather than a book of law or prophecy. The original is lost; no copy of it survives, and its contents beyond the passages that cite it are not known.

HONEST NOTE: Later works wearing the name — the medieval Sefer ha-Yashar and Ilive’s 1751 forgery — are NOT the original. They have their own cards on this shelf, and nothing on this page should be read as their text.
