TRADITION Christian, attributed to Mark  ·  ERA Surfaced 1958  ·  STATUS DISPUTED

In 1958 Morton Smith, working in a monastery library, reported finding a letter attributed to Clement of Alexandria that quoted a longer, secret version of the Gospel of Mark — a text meant, the letter said, for advanced initiates rather than ordinary readers.

Scholars still fight over it. Some argue the letter and its quotations are genuine ancient material; others argue the whole thing is a modern forgery, possibly Smith's own. The original manuscript has since gone missing, which keeps the argument from settling.

This shelf takes no side. The dispute itself is the honest record, and no text is hosted here as if the question were answered.
