The Teaching of the Mysteries

From the Teaching of the Mysteries, chapters 1–5

Section: Ethiopia's Own Books

This is the deepest book in the whole Bible, and the shortest way to tell it is the way a grandfather tells a small child about the deep water of the sea. You stand together on the safe, sunny shore. You hold his hand. And he tells you true things about the deep — slowly, and kindly.

The Teaching of the Mysteries is Peter's hidden teaching, spoken quietly to the believers: the wonderful secret of Jesus Himself. Here it is, the way Peter tells it.

Before there was anything — before the sea, before the stars, before the first morning — He was. He who was before, and is.

And He came. The great God came down to His own children, into our world, into a real body, born into a real family. He lived among us.

He suffered. He died — truly died, the way people die. And He was buried in a tomb of stone.

But listen, little one, because here the deep water starts to sparkle: death could not keep Him.

He broke the gates of death and threw them open. He called into the darkness — He called Adam, the very first man, and made him live again! The dead arose. By dying, He destroyed death itself. Sheol — the place of the dead — He trampled under His feet, the way you might stamp out a spark.

On the third day, He rose. He glorified His Father. And He opened the gates for everyone who trusts Him — for Moses believed in Him, and the prophets believed in Him, long before He came. Glory and power be to Him forever and ever.

That is the mystery, little reader. And now you have come to the very end of this whole big book of stories — from the garden where the world began, all the way to the deep, shining sea of God's secrets.

Some things God has shown us now — and oh, how wonderful they are! Some things He is keeping as a surprise, the way a father keeps a gift behind his back, smiling. And all of it — all of it — is kind.

So here is the last secret of the Mysteries, and it is not hidden at all: the God who made everything, and broke death, and keeps surprises behind His back — that God loves you.

You are loved, little one — that is the deepest mystery of all, and it was never really hidden.
