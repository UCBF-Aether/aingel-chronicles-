The Stories of Jesus

The Wedding at Cana

From the Book of John, chapter 2

Section: The Stories of Jesus

In a village called Cana, in Galilee, there was going to be a wedding — music and feasting and dancing, the happiest kind of day. Mary, the mother of Jesus, was there. And Jesus was invited too, with His new friends, His disciples.

Everything was joyful... until the worst thing that can happen at a wedding happened. The wine ran out.

In those days, running out of food or drink at your own wedding feast was a deep embarrassment — the kind of thing people whispered about for years. Somewhere in the kitchen, the servants were staring at empty jars. Mary found out. And Mary did exactly what a wise mother does: she went straight to Jesus.

"They have no wine," she told Him.

Jesus answered, "My hour has not yet come" — His time for showing who He was had not quite arrived.

But Mary simply turned to the servants and said the wisest sentence anyone says in the whole story: "Whatever He says to you — do it."

Now, standing nearby were six enormous stone water-jars, the kind used for washing, each big enough to hold a small bathtub of water. Jesus said to the servants, "Fill the jars with water."

So they filled them — bucket after bucket, sloshing and splashing — filled all six right up to the brim. Nothing but water.

"Now draw some out," said Jesus, "and take it to the master of the feast."

The servants must have exchanged a look. Take the man... a cup of water? But Mary had said: whatever He says, do it. So they dipped a cup and carried it to the master of the banquet, and he tasted it.

His eyes went wide.

He hurried to the bridegroom and said, "Everybody serves the good wine first, and saves the ordinary wine for later! But you — you have kept the best until now!"

It was wine — the finest of the whole feast — and it had been water when it went into the jars. Only the servants who had drawn the water knew where it had come from.

This was the very first of Jesus' signs — the first time He showed His glory out in the open, like a flag going up a pole. And His disciples saw it, and they believed in Him.

Jesus' first miracle didn't happen in a temple or a palace — it happened at a party, saving a family's happiest day.
