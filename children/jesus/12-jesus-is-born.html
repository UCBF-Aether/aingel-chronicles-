The Stories of Jesus

The Night Jesus Was Born

From the Book of Luke, chapter 2, and Matthew, chapter 2

Section: The Stories of Jesus

In Nazareth lived a young woman named Mary, and a kind carpenter named Joseph. An angel had told Mary wonderful news: she would have a baby, a special Baby — God's own Son — and His name would be Jesus, because He would save His people.

Then the emperor, far away in Rome, gave an order: everyone must travel to their home town to be counted. So Mary and Joseph set out on the road to Bethlehem, King David's town.

The little town was crowded full when they arrived. Every room was taken. No bed was left anywhere — except where the animals were kept. And there, among the hay and the soft breathing of the animals, on the most wonderful night in history, Jesus was born.

Mary wrapped her Baby in cloths and laid Him in a manger — a feeding box lined with hay — because there was no cradle for the King of heaven.

That same night, shepherds were out in the fields nearby, watching their sheep. Suddenly — light! The whole sky blazed with God's glory, and an angel stood before them. The shepherds were frightened.

"Don't be afraid," said the angel. "I bring you good news of great joy, for everybody! Today, in Bethlehem, a Saviour has been born — Christ the Lord. You will find the Baby wrapped in cloths, lying in a manger."

And suddenly the sky was full of angels, beyond counting, singing: "Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace!"

The shepherds ran into Bethlehem and found Mary and Joseph — and the Baby in the manger, just as the angel had said. They told everyone what they had seen, and everyone wondered. Then they went back to their fields, singing and thanking God all the way. And Mary kept all these things like treasures in her heart.

But the good news travelled further. In the East, wise men who studied the stars saw a new star rising, and knew: the King of the Jews has been born! They loaded their camels with gifts — gold, frankincense, and myrrh — and followed the star for many days.

The star stopped over the very house where the young Child was. The wise men went in, and when they saw Jesus with His mother, they bowed down and opened their treasures for Him. And God warned them in a dream not to go back the way they came — so they went home by another road, with wonderful news in their hearts.

All heaven sang that night. And the song has never stopped.

Glory to God in the highest — Jesus is born, and the good news is for everybody!
