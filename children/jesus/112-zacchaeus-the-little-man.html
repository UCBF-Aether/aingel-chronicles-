The Stories of Jesus

Zacchaeus the Little Man

From the Book of Luke, chapter 19

Section: The Stories of Jesus

Jesus was passing through the city of Jericho, and the whole town had turned out to see Him — when a small problem walked into the story. Literally small.

His name was Zacchaeus. He was the chief tax collector of Jericho — which made him rich, and also made him the most unpopular man in town, because tax collectors took money from their own neighbours for the Roman rulers, and often took extra for themselves. And Zacchaeus was short. Not a little short — properly, can't-see-over-anyone's-shoulder short.

Zacchaeus wanted to see Jesus. He really, really wanted to see Jesus. But the crowd was a solid wall of tall people, and no one was about to make room for the tax man.

So Zacchaeus did something a rich, important, dignified grown-up would normally never do. He ran — ahead of the crowd — and he climbed a tree. A sycamore tree, with handy low branches, standing right beside the road Jesus would take. Up he scrambled, robes and all, and perched among the leaves like a boy, waiting.

Jesus came along the road. He reached the tree. And He stopped.

He looked up, right into the branches, right at Zacchaeus — and He called him by name!

"Zacchaeus! Hurry up and come down. Today I must stay at your house."

Zacchaeus nearly fell out of the tree with joy. He scrambled down as fast as he could and welcomed Jesus to his house, beaming.

But the crowd muttered and grumbled: "He's gone to be the guest of a sinner!"

They were still grumbling when Zacchaeus stood up in front of everyone and said the most unexpected thing. "Look, Lord! Half of everything I own, I give to the poor. And if I have cheated anybody out of anything — I will pay them back four times as much!"

Four times! Somewhere in Jericho that day, people who had been cheated for years were about to get a knock on the door and a bag of money and an apology.

And Jesus said, "Today, salvation has come to this house. For the Son of Man came to seek — and to save — the lost."

Zacchaeus had climbed a tree to see Jesus. What he didn't know was that Jesus had come to Jericho looking for him.

Zacchaeus went up a tree to see Jesus — and found out Jesus had come to town to find Zacchaeus.
