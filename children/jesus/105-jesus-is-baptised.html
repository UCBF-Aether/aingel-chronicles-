The Stories of Jesus

Jesus Is Baptised

From the Book of Matthew, chapter 3, the Book of Mark, chapter 1, and the Book of Luke, chapter 3

Section: The Stories of Jesus

Malachi had promised that a messenger would come first, to prepare the way. And now, at last, the messenger arrived — and what a messenger he was!

His name was John. He lived out in the wilderness by the river Jordan, wearing a rough coat of camel's hair with a leather belt, and eating the food of the desert — locusts and wild honey. And he preached with a voice like a trumpet:

"Repent! Turn back to God — for the kingdom of heaven is at hand! Prepare the way of the Lord!"

Crowds poured out of Jerusalem and all the country round about to hear him. And John baptised them in the river Jordan — down under the water and up again — as they confessed their sins and promised God a new start.

"I'm only the messenger," John told them. "I baptise you with water. But One is coming after me who is mightier than I — I'm not even worthy to stoop down and untie His sandals. He will baptise you with the Holy Spirit."

Then one day, Jesus came.

He walked down from Galilee to the Jordan, to where John was baptising — and He asked John to baptise Him too.

John was stunned. "I need to be baptised by You," he said. "And You come to me?"

But Jesus answered gently: "Let it be so now. This is the right way for us to do all that God requires." So John agreed.

Jesus went down into the river, and John baptised Him in the water. And as Jesus came up out of the water, praying — suddenly the heavens opened!

John saw the Spirit of God coming down like a dove, and landing on Jesus. And a Voice spoke from heaven — God's own voice, out loud, for everyone to hear:

"This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased."

The Father speaking. The Son standing in the water. The Holy Spirit like a dove between them. The messenger's work was done, the way was prepared — and now the King's work could begin.

At the river, God said it out loud so nobody would ever have to wonder: This is my beloved Son.
