The Stories of Jesus

The Boy Jesus in the Temple

From the Book of Luke, chapter 2

Section: The Stories of Jesus

Every year, Mary and Joseph walked up to Jerusalem for the Feast of the Passover — the great spring festival — and when Jesus was twelve years old, He went with them, as families went together, whole crowds of cousins and neighbours travelling in one big happy caravan.

The feast ended, and the caravan started for home.

But Jesus had stayed behind in Jerusalem — and Mary and Joseph didn't know.

They travelled a whole day's journey, thinking He was somewhere in the crowd — walking with the cousins, perhaps, or running ahead with the other boys. When evening came and they looked for Him among all their relatives and friends, He wasn't there.

Imagine their hearts. Their boy was lost — in a big city, three days from home.

They turned straight around and hurried back to Jerusalem, searching for Him the whole way. One day passed. Another day. On the third day, they went into the Temple —

— and there He was.

The boy Jesus was sitting right in the middle of the great teachers of Israel, the learned old men in their long robes — listening to them, and asking them questions. And the teachers were astonished at Him: at His understanding, and at the answers He gave. This twelve-year-old was no ordinary schoolboy.

Mary went to Him with a mother's whole heart in her voice. "Son, why have You treated us like this? Your father and I have been searching for You, sorrowing!"

And Jesus answered — and these are the first words of His that anyone ever wrote down:

"Why were you searching for me? Didn't you know that I must be about my Father's business?"

Mary and Joseph didn't fully understand what He meant. Who does understand everything about Jesus all at once? But Jesus went home with them, back to little Nazareth, and He obeyed them and grew up in their house like any good son — while His mother kept all these things tucked away in her heart, turning them over and over, like treasures.

And Luke tells us how the growing went: "Jesus increased in wisdom and in stature, and in favour with God and man." He got taller. He got wiser. And God and everybody who knew Him loved Him more and more.

Even as a boy of twelve, Jesus knew whose house the Temple was — and still He went home, obeyed His mother and Joseph, and grew taller, wiser, and more loved every year.
