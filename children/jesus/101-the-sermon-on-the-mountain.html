The Stories of Jesus

The Sermon on the Mountain

From the Book of Matthew, chapters 5–7

Section: The Stories of Jesus

Jesus climbed a mountain to teach God's kingdom living. First, the Beatitudes:

"Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed are those who mourn, for they shall be comforted. Blessed are the gentle, for they shall inherit the earth. Blessed are those who hunger and thirst to do right, for they shall be filled. Blessed are the merciful, for they shall receive mercy. Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God. Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called children of God."

"You are the salt of the earth, the light of the world," He said. Nobody lights a lamp and hides it under a basket — they put it high to light the house. So let your light shine: people will see your good works, and praise your Father in heaven."

Then prayer:

"Our Father in heaven, Your name is holy. Your kingdom come, Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us our daily bread. Forgive us our sins, as we forgive those who sin against us. Lead us from temptation, and rescue us from evil. For Yours is the kingdom, the power, and the glory, forever. Amen."

"Don't store treasures on earth," He said, "where moths, rust and thieves spoil and steal them. Store treasures in heaven — where your treasure is, there your heart will be."

Don't worry! "Look at the birds:" "They don't sow, reap or fill barns — and your Father feeds them. Aren't you worth much more? Look at the lilies: they never spin or sew — not even Solomon was dressed like one. If God clothes grass like that, won't He look after you? Put God's kingdom first — and all you need will come."

"Ask, and it will be given. Seek, and you will find. Knock, and the door will open. If a child asks his father for bread, would he give a stone? Of course not! So your Father in heaven gives good things to children who ask."

Golden rule: "Do for others what you would want them to do for you."

Two builders. The wise man built on rock. Rain poured, floods rose, wind howled and beat it — and it stood firm, built on rock. The foolish man built on sand. Rain poured, floods rose, wind howled — and it crashed.

"Whoever hears my words and does them is like the wise man."

The crowds were astonished — He taught like one who knew.

A whole mountain of teaching — and at the top of it, one simple shape: trust your Father, shine like a lamp, build on the rock.
