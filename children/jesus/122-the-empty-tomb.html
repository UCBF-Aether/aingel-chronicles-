The Stories of Jesus

The Empty Tomb

From the Gospel of Matthew, chapter 28, the Gospel of Luke, chapter 24, and the Gospel of John, chapter 20

Section: The Stories of Jesus

Very early on the first morning of the week, while it was still dark, Mary Magdalene came to the tomb where Jesus had been laid. Another Mary came with her. Their hearts were heavy.

Suddenly the ground shook! An angel of the Lord came down from heaven, rolled the great stone away from the door of the tomb, and sat on it. The angel shone like lightning, and his clothes were as white as snow.

"Do not be afraid," the angel said to the women. "I know you are looking for Jesus. He is not here — He has risen, just as He said! Come and see the place where He lay. Then go quickly and tell His friends: He has risen from the dead!"

The women ran from the tomb as fast as they could — frightened, and full of joy.

Mary Magdalene found Peter and John and told them. Then Peter and John ran too — both running to the tomb! John ran faster and got there first. He stooped down and looked in, and saw the grave clothes lying there, empty.

Then Peter arrived and went straight inside. He saw the cloths lying neatly — and the cloth that had covered Jesus' face was folded up, all by itself, in a place of its own. Then John came in too — and he saw, and he believed.

The two of them went home, wondering. But Mary stayed in the garden, crying. She stooped down and looked into the tomb — and now there were two angels in white, sitting where Jesus had lain. "Woman," they said, "why are you crying?"

"Because they have taken away my Lord," she said, "and I do not know where they have put Him."

Then she turned around — and there was a man standing behind her. She thought he was the gardener.

"Woman, why are you crying? Who are you looking for?"

"Sir," she said, "if you have carried Him away, tell me where — and I will come and get Him."

And the man said one word — her name:

"Mary."

Her eyes flew open. It was not the gardener. It was *Jesus* — alive!

"Teacher!" she cried.

"Go to my friends," Jesus told her, "and tell them: I am going up to My Father and your Father, to My God and your God."

And Mary Magdalene ran with the best news ever carried anywhere: "I have seen the Lord! He is alive!"

He is risen! Thank You, God — the tomb is empty, the stone is rolled away, and Jesus is alive forever!
