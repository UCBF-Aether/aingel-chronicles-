The Stories of Jesus

Jesus Goes Home to Heaven

From the Gospel of Luke, chapter 24, and the Acts of the Apostles, chapter 1

Section: The Stories of Jesus

For forty days after He rose from the dead, Jesus showed Himself to His friends — alive! He ate with them, and walked with them, and taught them many things about the kingdom of God.

And He gave them a job to wait for. "Stay in Jerusalem," He said, "until the Father sends you the gift He promised. You will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you. And you will be My witnesses — telling everyone about Me — in Jerusalem, and in all the land, and right out to the far ends of the earth."

Then Jesus led His friends out of the city, as far as the little village of Bethany, on the hill called the Mount of Olives.

There He lifted up His hands — and He blessed them.

And while He was still blessing them, something wonderful happened. Jesus began to rise up into the sky! Up and up He went, higher and higher, while His friends watched with their mouths open — until a cloud received Him, and He was out of their sight. He had gone home to heaven, to His Father.

The friends stood there, still staring up into the empty sky.

Then — two men in shining white clothes were standing beside them! "Men of Galilee," the two angels said, "why do you stand here looking up into the sky? This same Jesus, who has been taken up from you into heaven, will come again — in just the same way as you have seen Him go."

So the friends were not sad. They bowed down and worshipped Jesus right there on the hill. Then they went back into Jerusalem with great joy, and they spent their days in the temple, praising God — waiting gladly for the gift He had promised them.

Jesus had gone home to heaven. But He had not gone away from them — not really — and one day, just as the angels promised, He will come again.

Thank You, Jesus, for blessing Your friends, and for going home to heaven to be with Your Father — we are waiting for You to come again, just as the angels promised.
