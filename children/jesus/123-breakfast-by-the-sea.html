The Stories of Jesus

Breakfast by the Sea

From the Gospel of John, chapter 21

Section: The Stories of Jesus

After Jesus rose from the dead, seven of His friends went back to the Sea of Galilee, and Peter said, "I am going fishing."

"We will come with you," the others said. So they climbed into the boat and fished all night long — and caught *nothing*. Not one fish. All night.

In the morning, just as the sky was turning light, someone stood on the shore. The friends did not know it was Jesus.

"Children," He called out, "have you caught any fish?"

"No!" they called back.

"Throw your net on the right side of the boat," He said, "and you will find some."

They threw the net — and splash! It filled up so full of big fish that they could hardly pull it in! And John whispered to Peter: "It is the Lord!"

Well — Peter could not wait for the boat. He wrapped his coat around himself and jumped straight into the sea and swam to shore! The others came in the boat, dragging the net behind them, stuffed with fish.

And what did they find on the shore? Jesus had made breakfast. There was a little fire of charcoal, with fish already cooking on it, and bread. "Bring some of the fish you have just caught," Jesus said. Peter helped haul the net up onto the beach. One hundred and fifty-three big fish — and even with so many, the net did not tear.

"Come and have breakfast," said Jesus. And He took the bread and gave it to them, and the fish as well. None of them needed to ask who He was. They knew. It was the Lord.

After breakfast, Jesus talked with Peter. Three times, not long before, Peter had said that he did not even know Jesus — and Peter had never stopped being sorry. Now Jesus asked him three questions, one for every sorry time:

"Simon, son of John, do you love Me?"

"Yes, Lord," said Peter. "You know that I love You."

"Feed My lambs," said Jesus.

"Simon, son of John, do you love Me?"

"Yes, Lord. You know that I love You."

"Feed My sheep," said Jesus.

"Simon, son of John, do you love Me?"

Peter's heart ached, because Jesus asked him the third time. "Lord, You know everything," he said. "You know that I love You."

"Feed My sheep," said Jesus. "Follow Me."

And Peter's heart was whole again. Jesus had not given up on him — not after the worst night of his life, not ever. That is the kind of friend Jesus is.

Thank You, Jesus, for breakfast on the shore — and for never giving up on Your friends, not even once.
