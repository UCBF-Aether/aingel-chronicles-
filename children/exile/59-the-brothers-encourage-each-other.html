Exile, Return, and Brave Scrolls

The Brothers Encourage Each Other

From the Second Book of Meqabyan, chapters 1–12

Section: Exile, Return, and Brave Scrolls

Long ago, a man named Meqabis did a very wrong thing — and then he did a very brave thing. He said sorry to God with all his heart. He tore his fine clothes, put on rough sackcloth, and wept. He took every idol out of his house. And from that day he taught God's ways to all the children in his house, morning and midday, every single day.

Many years passed. Meqabis's children grew up, and their children grew up, and they all remembered. Even when they lived in a faraway land, under a king who loved idols, the family of Meqabis kept God's commandments together. They prayed together. They taught the little ones together. And whenever one of them felt afraid or tired of being different, the others would gather round and say: "Remember! God is our Maker. Hold on. We are standing together."

Then the king sent for them. "Bow down to my gods," he commanded, "and eat of my sacrifice!"

And the brothers and sisters answered together: "We will not obey you in this. We will not bow to idols that cannot see, and cannot hear, and cannot help anyone. We bow to God alone — the Maker of heaven and earth, the sea, the sun, the moon, and the stars."

The king threatened them terribly, but it did no good — for they had made each other's hearts strong. When trouble came on them, they trusted God with their whole souls. And God showed them a wonderful secret: **life does not end.** He showed them their life after death — bright and safe with Him.

And here is the promise their book says out loud, for everyone who stands firm: the God who made us from the dust can raise us up again. He who brought us into being will bring us back to life at the time He chooses, to live with Him forever.

So if you ever feel small and alone for doing right, remember the brothers and sisters of Meqabis. They stood together, they held each other up — and their God never let them go.

Standing together makes brave hearts — and God will raise up everyone who holds on to Him.
