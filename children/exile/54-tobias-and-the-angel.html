Tobias and the Angel Travelling Friend

From the Book of Tobit, chapters 5–12

Section: Exile, Return, and Brave Scrolls

In the city of Nineveh, far from home, there lived a kind old man named Tobit. Tobit loved God, and he loved helping everyone — feeding the hungry, clothing the cold, and burying the dead when no one else would. But now Tobit's eyes had gone white and blind, and he could not see at all.

One day Tobit remembered something wonderful. Long ago, on a journey, he had left ten bags of silver with his friend Gabael, in a city far away called Rages. "Tobias, my son," he said, "go and bring the silver home. But the road is long, and you have never travelled it. Find a good man to go with you."

Tobias searched the streets, and he found a traveller who said, "I know the way to Rages well. I will go with you, and I will keep you safe." His name, he said, was Azarias.

But Azarias was not only a man. He was **Raphael, one of God's own angels** — sent down in disguise to walk beside Tobias. Nobody knew. Not yet.

Tobias's mother cried when he left, for she loved him so. But Tobit said, "Take no care. A good angel will keep him company, and he shall come home safe." And so Tobias and his travelling friend set out — and the young man's little dog trotted along with them.

That evening, by the great river Tigris, Tobias went down to wash in the water. WHOOSH! A huge fish leaped out of the river at him! Tobias caught it and hauled it onto the bank. "Keep its heart, and its liver, and its gall safe in your bag," said his friend. "You will need them. Trust me."

On they walked, until they came to the house of Raguel, Tobias's cousin, who had one daughter, named Sara. Sara had had a great trouble — a troubling spirit that only an angel could end — and she had prayed and prayed. That night Tobias married Sara, just as Raphael had planned. And when Tobias burned the fish's heart on the coals, the smoke drove the troubling spirit far, far away, and Raphael bound it fast. Sara's trouble was over, forever.

While the wedding feast went on for days and days, Raphael himself hurried to Rages, showed Gabael the paper, and carried all ten bags of silver back safely. What a friend!

At home, old Tobit counted the days and counted the days. His wife Anna watched the road every morning. "My son is gone," she wept. "Hold your peace," said Tobit. "He is safe."

Then one morning Anna cried out: "Look! Your son is coming — and the man who went with him!" And the little dog came running home ahead of them.

Tobias ran to his blind father and, just as Raphael had told him, he smoothed the fish's gall over his father's eyes. The white scales peeled away like tiny curtains — and Tobit could SEE! He hugged his son and cried for joy. "Blessed are all Your holy angels!" he sang.

Then the family tried to pay their travelling friend half of everything they owned. But Azarias smiled and told the truth at last: "I am Raphael, one of the seven holy angels who carry the prayers of God's people up to heaven. God sent me to heal you, and to help Sara, and to walk with Tobias all the way. Give your thanks to God — not to me."

And when they stood up from bowing… he was gone.

God sends His angels to walk beside us — sometimes so quietly that we only find out at the end of the road.
