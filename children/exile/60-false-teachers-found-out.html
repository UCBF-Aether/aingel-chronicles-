Exile, Return, and Brave Scrolls

False Teachers Found Out

From the Third Book of Meqabyan (selection)

Section: Exile, Return, and Brave Scrolls

The king of that faraway land had priests — but they were lying priests. Every day they set food and drink before a big idol and told the king, "Our god is eating! Our god is drinking!"

But the idol never ate a single bite. How could it? Listen to what the book says about that idol: "He eateth not, and he drinketh not. He killeth not, and he maketh not alive. He planteth not, and he plucketh not up. He rebuketh not, and he forgiveth not." The idol was deaf and dumb. It could not see, and it could not hear. It was only gold and silver and brass that men's hands had hammered together — and the priests knew it. They ate the god's dinner themselves, and kept the secret, and let the king go on believing a lie.

Then the children of Meqabis came before the king, and they were not fooled at all. "Your god cannot help you," they said, "because your god cannot do anything. He has no breath and no life. But our God made heaven and earth and the sea and everything in them. He hears us when we pray. He forgives us when we are sorry. He loves us." And the lying priests had nothing to say — because everyone could see it was true.

Long before, there was another test just like it. A king named Balak offered a man named Balaam bags and bags of gold and silver: "Curse God's people for me!" But Balaam answered: "I cannot change the word of God. What God puts in my mouth — that is what I will speak." And when he opened his mouth, out came not a curse but a blessing! A true teacher cannot be bought.

So how can you tell a true teacher from a false one? By their fruit. A false teacher serves himself and tells you what you want to hear. A true teacher is humble, and loves the truth, and serves the God who truly hears, and truly helps, and truly forgives.

God is alive and He hears you — and His true teachers love the truth, just like Him.
