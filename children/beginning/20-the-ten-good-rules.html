The Beginning and the Fathers

The Ten Good Rules

From the Book of Exodus, chapters 19–20

Section: The Beginning and the Fathers

God's people came to a great mountain called Sinai, and set up their camp in the desert below it. God called Moses up the mountain and gave him a message full of love to carry down:

“You saw what I did in Egypt — how I carried you out on eagles' wings and brought you to Myself. Now, if you will listen to Me and keep close to Me, you will be My own special treasure, out of all the peoples of the earth.”

The people answered together: “All that the LORD has spoken, we will do!”

Then God said He would come down on the mountain in three days. The people washed their clothes and got ready, and no one was to climb the holy mountain.

On the morning of the third day — thunder rolled! Lightning flashed! A thick cloud came down and covered the mountain, and a trumpet sounded, louder and louder and louder, until the whole mountain shook and trembled. Smoke poured up from the top like the smoke of a giant furnace, because the LORD had come down on it in fire. Moses spoke — and God answered him out of the thunder.

All the people stood far off, holding their breath, wide-eyed at the wonderful, mighty sight. Then God Himself spoke, and He gave His people ten good rules to live by — the Ten Commandments:

One: You shall have no gods but Me. Worship Me alone. Two: Do not make idols, or bow down to pictures and statues. Three: Always use My name with love — never carelessly. Four: Remember the seventh day. Keep it holy, and rest. Five: Honour your father and your mother. Six: Do not take anyone's life. Seven: Husbands and wives must keep their promises and be true to each other. Eight: Do not steal. Nine: Do not tell lies about other people. Ten: Do not spend your heart wishing for what belongs to someone else.

When the thunder and the trumpet grew very loud, the people trembled and said to Moses, “You speak with us — but let not God speak with us, it is too much for us!”

And Moses answered kindly: “Do not be afraid. God has come close so that you will always remember He is near — and so you will keep far away from doing wrong.”

Ten good rules, spoken out of the cloud — God's own directions for an honest, kind, thankful life.
