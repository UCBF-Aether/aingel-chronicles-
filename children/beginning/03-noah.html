The Beginning and the Fathers

Noah and the Great Boat

From the Book of Genesis, chapters 6–9

Section: The Beginning and the Fathers

Long after Adam and Eve, the world filled up with people — and the people forgot God. They were unkind to each other, and their hearts thought of hurtful things all day long. It made God sad.

But one man still listened to God. His name was Noah.

God said to Noah, "Build a great boat — bigger than any boat ever built. Build it of strong wood, with rooms inside, and a window, and a door in its side. A flood of water is coming to wash the earth clean. But I will keep you safe, and your family, and the animals with you."

So Noah built. Hammer, hammer, saw, saw — while everyone around him laughed. A boat, on dry land, with no sea in sight! But Noah trusted God's word more than people's laughter.

God sent the animals to Noah, two by two: elephants and mice, eagles and ants, lions and lambs, all coming across the fields to the great boat. Noah's family went in too — and God Himself shut the door.

Then the rain began. Rain like nobody had ever seen, pattering, pouring, roaring down for forty days and forty nights, until the water covered the fields and the hills and even the tops of the mountains. And the great boat floated, safe on the deep water, with everybody God loved tucked inside.

At last the rain stopped. A wind blew, and the water began to go down. Noah sent out a dove. The first time, she came back — no dry place to rest. The second time, she came back carrying a fresh green olive leaf in her beak. New leaves were growing! The world was coming back to life.

Then God opened the way out, and oh, what a parade — every animal stepping out onto the fresh green earth, and Noah's family with them, blinking in the sunshine.

God blessed Noah and said, "Never again will a flood cover the whole earth." And God put His promise in the sky where everyone could see it: a rainbow, with all its colours shining in the clouds.

Every rainbow in the sky is God keeping His promise where the whole world can see it.
