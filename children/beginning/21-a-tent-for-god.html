The Beginning and the Fathers

A Tent for God

From the Book of Exodus, chapters 25–27 and 35–40

Section: The Beginning and the Fathers

High on the smoky mountain, God gave Moses a beautiful idea.

God did not want to stay far away up in the cloud. He wanted to come close — to live right in the middle of His people's camp! So God said: “Let them make Me a sanctuary — a holy tent — that I may dwell among them.”

And God showed Moses exactly how to make it, piece by piece, like a pattern.

First, the people were invited to bring gifts — and oh, what gifts came pouring in! Gold and silver and shining stones. Yarn and cloth of blue and purple and scarlet red. Fine white linen, soft skins, sweet spices and oil. Everyone whose heart made them willing brought something, from the richest family to the smallest child. Morning after morning they came — until Moses had to announce: “Stop bringing! We have more than enough!”

Then the skilled hands went to work. Weavers wove curtains of blue and purple and scarlet, with angel-cherubim stitched into the cloth. Carpenters built tall frames of wood covered in gold. In the very heart of the tent stood the holiest treasure of all: a golden chest called the ark, with a golden lid, and on the lid two golden cherubim face to face, spreading their wings over it. There was a golden lampstand shaped like a flowering tree, and a golden table, and curtains and hooks and shining bowls — everything made exactly as God had shown Moses.

At last the day came to set up the tent. Frame by frame, curtain by curtain, it rose in the middle of the camp, with a great courtyard of white linen walls around it.

And then — wonder of wonders — the cloud of God came down and covered the tent, and the glory of the LORD filled it, bright and shining, so bright that even Moses could not go inside.

God had come to live with His people.

From that day on, the cloud stayed over the tent by day, and in the night it glowed with fire, so that everyone in the camp could see it. When the cloud lifted, the people packed up and traveled. Where the cloud rested, the people rested too. All through their journey, God's tent glowed in the middle of them — and God was never far away.

Right in the middle of the camp stood a tent shining with God's glory — God had come to live among His people.
