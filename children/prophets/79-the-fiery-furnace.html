The Fiery Furnace

From the Book of Daniel, chapter 3

Section: The Prophets

King Nebuchadnezzar built an enormous statue of gold — taller than fifteen men standing on each other's shoulders — and set it up on a plain. Then came the command: "When the music plays — the horns, the flutes, the harps, all together — everyone must fall down and worship the golden statue. Whoever does not fall down will be thrown into a blazing furnace."

The day came. The crowds gathered. The music played — and thousands of people fell down flat.

But three men stayed standing: Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, Daniel's friends. They worshipped the living God, and they would not bow to gold.

The king was furious. "Is it true?" he demanded. "One more chance! When the music plays, fall down — or into the furnace you go. And what god could rescue you from my hand then?"

The three friends answered calmly, and their words still ring like bells: "Our God, whom we serve, is able to rescue us from the furnace. But even if He does not — we will still not bow. We will not worship the gold."

The king's face changed with rage. "Make the furnace seven times hotter!" he roared. Mighty soldiers bound the three friends — coats, hats, and all — and threw them into the flames. The fire was so hot that its blast struck down the soldiers who threw them in.

Then the king jumped up in amazement. "Didn't we throw in three men, bound? Look! I see FOUR men walking around in the fire — loose, unbound, unhurt! And the fourth looks like a son of the gods!"

Inside the furnace, God had come to His friends. And there — in the middle of the fire — the three did something astonishing: they sang. They blessed God with one voice while the flames couldn't touch them, for the angel of God had made the inside of the furnace like a cool, whistling wind on a summer day.

"Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego, servants of the Most High God — come out!" called the king.

They walked out. The fire had not singed their hair. Their coats were not scorched. They didn't even smell of smoke.

And King Nebuchadnezzar declared to all the world: "Blessed be the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, who sent His angel and rescued His servants who trusted in Him!"

Trust God like the three friends: He is able to save — and He never leaves His own alone in the fire.
