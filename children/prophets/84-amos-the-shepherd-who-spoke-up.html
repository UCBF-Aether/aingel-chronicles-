The Prophets

Amos the Shepherd Who Spoke Up

From the Book of Amos, chapters 5, 7, and 9

Section: The Prophets

Amos was not a famous man. He was a shepherd from a little town called Tekoa, and he looked after sheep and sycamore-fig trees. "I was no prophet," he said. "I was just a herdsman following the flock — and the LORD took me and said, Go!"

God sent Amos to speak up for people who were being treated unfairly. In those days, the rich were trampling on the poor. Judges took bribes. The needy were sold for the price of a pair of sandals. People sang lovely songs to God on holy days — and cheated their neighbours the rest of the week.

God said: "I don't want your songs if your hearts are unfair. Away with the noise! Instead — let justice run down like water, and righteousness like a river that never dries up!"

Amos also prayed for the people — twice! God showed him a swarm of locusts coming to eat up the land, and Amos cried, "O Lord, forgive, I beg You! How can little Jacob stand?" And God relented: "It shall not happen." Then God showed him fire, and Amos prayed again — and again God relented. The shepherd who spoke up *to* the people also spoke up *for* them, to God.

Then God showed Amos a plumbline — a builder's string with a weight, held against a wall to show whether it stands straight or leans. God's people were leaning, said the plumbline. Crooked, like a wall about to fall.

A priest named Amaziah told Amos, "Go away! Stop speaking!" But Amos the shepherd stood his ground. He had not chosen this job. God had taken him from the sheep, and a man sent by God does not stop because someone tells him to hush.

And Amos's book does not end with warning. It ends with God's promise: "I will raise up the fallen tent of David. I will bring My people home. They will plant vineyards and eat the fruit; they will build gardens and enjoy them. And I will plant them in their own land — and they shall never be pulled up again."

Speak up for what is fair, pray for others — and remember God's story always ends with planting and home.
