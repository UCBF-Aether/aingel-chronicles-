The Prophets

Joel and the Locust Storm

From the Book of Joel, chapters 1–2

Section: The Prophets

Something terrible — and a little bit astonishing — happened in the days of the prophet Joel.

First came the palmerworms, munching. Then came the locusts, munching what the palmerworms left. Then came the cankerworms, and then the caterpillars — wave after wave after wave of hungry bugs, like an army with a million tiny jaws. They stripped the grapevines bare. They gnawed the fig trees white. They ate the wheat, the barley, the gardens — everything green in the whole land, gone. The fields were empty. Even the animals stood hungry and confused.

"Tell your children about it," said Joel, "and let your children tell their children, and their children another generation." Nothing like it had ever happened.

And Joel called everyone — the old men, the farmers, even the children and the tiniest babies — to come back to God with all their hearts.

"Don't tear your clothes because you're sorry," said God. "Rend your hearts instead. Open them right up. For I am gracious and merciful. I am slow to anger and great in kindness. Come back to Me, and watch what I will do."

And the people came back. And God — God pitied them, the way He always does when hearts turn toward Him.

He drove the bug-army away. He sent the former rain and the latter rain, pattering on the dry ground right on time. The fields turned green again. The threshing floors filled up with wheat until they overflowed. The vats brimmed over with new wine and oil.

"I will restore to you the years that the locust ate," God promised — every single lost year, given back.

And then God promised something even bigger — the biggest promise in Joel's book: "Afterwards, I will pour out My Spirit on ALL people. Your sons and your daughters shall prophesy. Your old men shall dream dreams, and your young men shall see visions. Everyone who calls on the name of the LORD shall be saved."

Even the locust years were not wasted. God gave them all back — and then poured out His Spirit on top.

Come back to God with your whole heart — He can restore even the years the locusts ate.
