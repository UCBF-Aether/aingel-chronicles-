The Prophets

The Prince of Peace Is Coming

From the Book of Isaiah, chapters 9 and 11

Section: The Prophets

Isaiah had seen the King on His throne — and God let him see something else: a day that was coming, the most wonderful day in the whole story.

"The people who walked in darkness," Isaiah sang, "have seen a great light! On those who lived in the land of deep shadow, a light has shone." And why? **"For unto us a Child is born; unto us a Son is given — and the kingdom shall rest upon His shoulders. And His name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, the Mighty God, the Everlasting Father, the Prince of Peace."** Of the greatness of His kingdom and peace there will be no end. It will last forever and ever.

And who is He? Isaiah saw Him growing like a green shoot from an old cut-down tree stump — from the family of Jesse, David's father. The Spirit of the LORD would rest on Him: wisdom and understanding, counsel and might. He would judge fairly — not by what eyes see or ears hear, but truly — and He would stand up for the poor and the meek of the earth.

And then Isaiah saw what His kingdom will look like, and it is like a picture out of a dream:

**The wolf will live with the lamb. The leopard will lie down with the young goat. The calf and the lion will eat together — and a little child will lead them.** The cow and the bear will graze side by side, and their babies will lie down together. The lion will eat straw like the ox. A baby will play safely by a snake's hole, and no one will be hurt at all. "They shall not hurt nor destroy in all My holy mountain — for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the LORD, as the waters cover the sea."

That is the day the whole world is waiting for. And it all begins with a Child.

A Child is coming whose name is Prince of Peace — and in His kingdom, even the wolf and the lamb are friends.
