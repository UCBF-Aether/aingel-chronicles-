Haggai Says: Finish the House

From the Book of Haggai, chapters 1–2

Section: The Prophets

God's people had come home from faraway Babylon, and the very first thing they needed to rebuild was God's house, the temple. They laid the foundation — and then... they stopped. For years and years, the temple sat unfinished, its stones growing weeds, while everyone went home and built beautiful houses for themselves, with fine wooden panels on the walls.

Then along came Haggai, God's messenger, with God's own question: "Is it a time for you to live in your finished houses — while My house lies in ruins?"

And God pointed out something the people had all been wondering about: "You plant much, but harvest little. You eat, but never have enough. You earn wages — and put them in a purse full of holes! Think about it. Consider your ways!" Everything felt leaky because the most important thing had been left undone.

"Go up into the hills," God said. "Bring down wood, and build My house. I will take pleasure in it, and I will be honoured." And then God added the words that change everything: "I am with you."

The people listened! Governor Zerubbabel, and Joshua the high priest, and every single person — God stirred up their spirits, and within a month they were all at work on the house of the LORD.

But some of the old people grew sad. They remembered Solomon's glorious temple from long ago, and this new one looked so small. "It looks like nothing beside the old one," they whispered.

God heard them too. "Be strong, Zerubbabel. Be strong, Joshua. Be strong, all you people of the land — and work! For I am with you. My Spirit remains among you. Do not fear! The silver is Mine and the gold is Mine, and the glory of this new house will be greater than the glory of the old one. And in this place, I will give peace."

From the very day they laid stone upon stone again, God promised: "From this day on, I will bless you."

And to Zerubbabel, the governor who led the work, God gave a special honour: "I will make you like My signet ring" — the king's own seal, kept close on his hand — "for I have chosen you."

First finish God's house. Then the blessings flow. Haggai's message is only two chapters long — and it got a whole nation building again.

Put God's things first, and hear Him say it over your work: "I am with you."
