The End and the Beginning Again

The New Jerusalem

From the Revelation of John, chapters 21–22

Section: The End and the Beginning Again

At the very end of John's vision, he saw the best thing of all.

He saw a new heaven and a new earth — and the holy city, the New Jerusalem, coming down from God, dressed as beautifully as a bride on her wedding day.

And he heard a loud voice from the throne: "Look! God has come to live with His people. They will be His, and He will be theirs. And God Himself will wipe away every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death, no more sadness, no more crying, and no more pain — for the old things have passed away."

And the King on the throne said: "Look! I am making everything new! I am the Beginning and the End. To everyone who is thirsty, I will give water from the spring of life — for free."

Then an angel carried John to a high mountain, and showed him the city shining with God's glory, bright as a precious jewel, clear as crystal. Its wall was high, with twelve gates — each gate one pearl! The wall was built of jasper. The city itself was pure gold, clear as glass. And its street — its one golden street — was gold you could see through, like the clearest glass.

The city had no temple, because God and the Lamb are its temple. And it did not need sun or moon — for God's glory lights it, and the Lamb is its lamp. Its gates are never shut — because there is no night there.

And John saw a river — a pure river of the water of life, clear as crystal, flowing out of the throne of God and of the Lamb, down the middle of the street. On both sides of the river grew the Tree of Life, bearing twelve kinds of fruit — new fruit every single month — and its leaves are for the healing of the nations.

There will be no more curse there. God's servants will see His face, and His name will be on their foreheads. And He will reign forever and ever.

And the Spirit and the bride say, "Come!" And let everyone who hears say, "Come!" And whoever is thirsty — come, and take the water of life freely.

John's last words are the words everyone whispers back: "Yes — come, Lord Jesus. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen."

The gates are open, the river is running, and the Tree is full of fruit — one day, little one, you will walk that golden street.
