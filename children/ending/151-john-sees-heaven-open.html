The End and the Beginning Again

John Sees Heaven Open

From the Revelation of John, chapters 1, 4 and 5

Section: The End and the Beginning Again

John was old, and far from home on a rocky little island called Patmos, because he would not stop telling people about Jesus. One Lord's Day, he heard a voice behind him — loud as a trumpet!

He turned around. There stood Jesus — shining! His face was like the sun blazing at noon. Around Him burned seven golden lampstands, which are His seven churches. In His hand were seven stars.

John fell down at His feet like a man who had fainted. But Jesus laid His right hand on him, gently, and said: "Do not be afraid. I am the First and the Last. I was dead — and look! I am alive for ever and ever."

Then John looked — and a door was opened in heaven! "Come up here," said the voice. And John saw the throne room of the whole world.

On the throne sat the King of everything, shining like jewels. Around the throne ran a rainbow, green as an emerald. In front of it stretched a sea of glass, clear as crystal, and seven lamps of fire were burning. Round the throne sat twenty-four elders in white clothes, with crowns of gold.

And there were four wonderful living creatures, covered in eyes and wings, and they never stop singing, day or night: "Holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty, who was, and is, and is to come!" And every time they sing, the elders bow down and lay their golden crowns before the throne, saying: "You are worthy, our Lord and God — for You made all things."

Then John saw a book in the hand of the King, sealed shut with seven seals. A mighty angel called out: "Who is worthy to open the book?" And no one — not in heaven, not on earth — could open it. John began to cry.

But one of the elders said: "Do not weep! Look — the Lion of the tribe of Judah has won! He can open the book."

John looked for a Lion. And what did he see? A Lamb!

There in the middle of the throne room stood the Lamb, and He came and took the book from the King's hand. Then everyone fell down! The elders held harps, and golden bowls full of sweet-smelling incense — which are the prayers of God's people. And they sang a brand-new song: "You are worthy — for You bought people for God from every family and language and people and nation!"

Then John heard the angels — ten thousand times ten thousand, thousands of thousands, too many to count! And every creature in heaven and on earth and under the earth and in the sea was singing too: "Blessing and honour and glory and power be to Him who sits on the throne, and to the Lamb, forever!"

One day heaven's door will open for you too — and the first voice you hear will say, "Do not be afraid."
