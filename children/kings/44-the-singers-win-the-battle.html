The Land and the Kings

The Singers Win the Battle

From the Book of 2 Chronicles, chapter 20

Section: The Land and the Kings

Terrible news came to King Jehoshaphat: "A great army is coming against you — Moab and Ammon and more besides, more soldiers than anyone can count!"

Jehoshaphat was afraid. So do you know what the king did first? He prayed. He called the whole land of Judah to fast and to come together and ask the LORD for help. Families streamed into Jerusalem from every city — fathers and mothers, and even the little ones stood before God in the temple court.

And the king prayed out loud: "O LORD God of our fathers — are You not God in heaven? You rule over all the kingdoms of the world, and nobody can stand against You. We have no strength against this great army, and we don't know what to do — but our eyes are on You."

Then, in the middle of the crowd, one of the singers — Jahaziel, a Levite whose family sang in the temple — stood up, full of the Spirit of the LORD. "Listen, all of you!" he cried. "This is what the LORD says: Do not be afraid, and do not be dismayed at this great army — for the battle is not yours. It is God's! Tomorrow, go down and meet them. You will not need to fight in this battle. Stand still — and see the salvation of the LORD."

And Jehoshaphat bowed his face to the ground, and all Judah fell down and worshipped.

Early the next morning they marched out into the wilderness. And the king said: "Believe in the LORD your God, and you will stand firm." Then he did the strangest thing a king has ever done before a battle.

He put the singers at the FRONT.

Ahead of the soldiers, ahead of the spears, out walked the choir — praising the beauty of God's holiness, and singing at the top of their voices: "Praise the LORD! For His mercy endureth for ever!"

And at the very moment they began to sing — God set a trap for the enemy. In the enemy camp, confusion broke out: the armies of Moab and Ammon and Mount Seir turned on each other in the dark of their own quarrelling, and by the time Judah's singers came over the hill, the battle was already over. God's people had not needed to lift a single sword. There were the treasures the fleeing armies had dropped, lying everywhere — so many riches and precious things that it took three whole days to carry them all home!

On the fourth day, everyone gathered in a valley and blessed the LORD. They named that place the Valley of Berachah — which means the Valley of Blessing.

Then they marched back to Jerusalem — Jehoshaphat at the front, and harps and trumpets playing all the way to the house of the LORD. And God gave the kingdom rest on every side.

When trouble is too big for you, remember the king's secret: put the singers at the front, and let God fight.
