The Land and the Kings

Samuel Hears God's Voice

From the Book of 1 Samuel, chapters 1–3

Section: The Land and the Kings

Hannah had no children; her heart ached for a baby.

At God's house in Shiloh, Hannah prayed — pouring out her heart to God, weeping, her lips moving in silence: “O LORD, if You will look at my sadness and remember me, and give me a son — then I will give him back to You, to serve You all his life.”

Eli the priest watched her lips move without words and misunderstood. “No, my lord,” said Hannah. “I am a woman with a sorrowful heart, pouring out my soul before the LORD.”

Then Eli answered, “Go in peace — may the God of Israel give you what you have asked.”

Hannah went away and ate, her face no longer sad.

God remembered Hannah, and she had a baby boy. She named him Samuel — “asked of God” — “because I have asked him of the LORD.”

When Samuel was weaned, Hannah kept her promise. She brought him to Eli. “O my lord,” she said, “I am the woman who prayed here. For this child I prayed — and the LORD has given me what I asked. So now I give him to the LORD; all his life he belongs to the LORD.”

So Samuel stayed at God's house to serve, helping Eli, in a linen robe. And yearly Hannah brought him a new coat she had sewn herself.

One night, before the lamp of God went out, Eli lay in his place, and Samuel lay nearby.

And a Voice called: “Samuel!”

Samuel ran to Eli: “Here I am! You called me!”

“I did not call,” said Eli. “Lie down again.”

Again the Voice called — “Samuel!” — and again Samuel ran to Eli. And a third time! Then Eli understood: the LORD was calling the child.

“Go, lie down,” said Eli. “And if He calls you again, say: ‘Speak, LORD — for Your servant is listening.’”

Samuel lay down. And the LORD came and stood, and called twice: “Samuel! Samuel!”

And Samuel answered: “Speak — for Your servant is listening.”

That night God spoke to the boy — serious words about what He would do, because Eli's sons had done wrong and Eli had not stopped them. Samuel lay until morning, afraid to tell Eli. But Eli called him: “Samuel — what did the LORD say to you? Hide nothing from me.” Samuel told him everything. Eli answered, “It is the LORD. Let Him do what seems good to Him.”

Samuel grew up, the LORD was with him, and all Israel knew God had made Samuel His prophet.

A little boy in a dark house heard God calling his name — and answered, “Speak, LORD; Your servant is listening.”
