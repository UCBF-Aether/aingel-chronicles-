The Land and the Kings

Enoch and the Angels

From the Book of 1 Enoch, and Genesis chapter 5

Section: The Land and the Kings

Most people in the Bible's first family lived a long, long time — but one of them was different from all the rest. His name was Enoch, and the Bible says something wonderful about him, in the simplest words:

Enoch walked with God.

Day after day, year after year, Enoch walked with God the way you walk with a dear friend — talking with Him, listening to Him, staying close. And God loved Enoch's company so much that one day, Enoch simply went home with Him. The Bible says, "Enoch walked with God, and he was not, for God took him." He didn't grow old and die like everyone else. He walked with God — all the way to heaven.

And on those walks, God opened Enoch's eyes to see amazing things.

Enoch saw the heavens shining with light upon light. He saw angels beyond counting — thousands and thousands of them, bright as morning. He saw the Holy and Great One Himself, and heard the secrets of how the stars are guided and the seasons are kept. Everything Enoch saw, he wrote down for people who would live long afterward — for us.

But Enoch also saw something sad. A company of angels called the Watchers, who were supposed to watch over the earth and guard it, had broken God's rules. They had come down to earth and taught people hurtful things, and the earth was filling up with trouble because of them.

Enoch was brave, and Enoch was kind. Even though the Watchers had done wrong, Enoch carried their message for them. He went and asked God, humbly, to forgive them.

God's answer was firm: the rules matter, and the Watchers could not undo what they had done. But listen to what else God said — because God's story never ends at "no." God promised that He would heal the whole earth. He would wash away the hurt the Watchers had caused, plant goodness like a garden that would grow forever, and fill the world with peace, so that all God's children could live glad and safe under the trees He planted.

So Enoch's book is two things at once: a window into heaven's wonders — and a promise that God will mend everything that is broken.

Enoch walked with God every day. And one day, he just kept walking.

God loves to walk with His friends — and every day, He is happy to walk with you too.
