The Land and the Kings

David's Song of Rescue

From the Book of 2 Samuel, chapter 22

Section: The Land and the Kings

This is the song David sang on the day the LORD rescued him from all his enemies. When you are in deep trouble and God pulls you out, this is what it sounds like.

"The LORD is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer. He is my shield, and my high tower, and my refuge. When the waves of trouble closed over me and I was afraid, I cried out to my God — and He heard my voice from His temple. My cry reached His ears!

"He reached down from above and took hold of me. He drew me out of the deep waters. He brought me out into a wide open place — and He rescued me because He delighted in me.

"He is my lamp: when everything was dark, the LORD lit up my darkness. He makes my feet like the feet of a deer, and sets me steady on the high places. His gentleness has made me great.

"Who is God, except the LORD? And who is a rock, except our God?

"The LORD lives! Blessed be my Rock! This is the God who is a tower of salvation for His king, and who shows mercy to His anointed — to David, and to his children, for ever."

Kings do not usually sing. But David could never stop singing about the God who rescued him — and this song is still being sung today, thousands of years later, by everyone God has ever pulled out of deep water. God loved it so much that He kept it for us twice: once here in Samuel's book, and once more in the Psalms, where it sings as Psalm 18.

When God pulls you out of deep water, the only proper thing to do is sing.
