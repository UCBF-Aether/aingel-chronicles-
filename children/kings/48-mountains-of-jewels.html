The Land and the Kings

Enoch and the Seven Mountains of Jewels

From the Book of 1 Enoch, chapters 24–25 and 52

Section: The Land and the Kings

On another journey, Enoch was carried away in a whirlwind toward the west — and there he saw a sight that made him ask questions.

Before him rose seven mountains, each different from the others — three toward the east and three toward the south, with deep valleys between them — and their stones were precious, coloured stones. The seventh mountain stood tall in the middle, rising like a throne's seat. Around the throne grew fragrant trees. And among them stood one tree Enoch had never seen — nor smelled — in all his travels.

"It had a fragrance beyond all fragrance," Enoch said, "and its leaves were fair, its blooms delightful. Its leaves and blooms and wood never wither — never, not for ever. Its fruit is beautiful, like a palm tree's fruit."

"How beautiful this tree is!" cried Enoch. "How lovely its smell!"

Then Michael, a holy and honoured angel, answered him. "Enoch, this high mountain — whose top is like the throne of God — IS His throne. It is where the Holy Great One, the Lord of Glory, the Eternal King, will sit when He visits the earth with goodness.

"And this fragrant tree? No one born on earth may touch it yet. It is kept for the great day at the end. Then it will be given to the righteous and holy. Its fruit will feed God's chosen ones, and the tree will stand in the holy place, beside the temple of the Eternal King. Its fragrance will live in their bones, and they will live long on earth — as their fathers lived long ago — and in all their days no sorrow, sickness, or trouble will touch them."

And Enoch blessed the God of Glory, the Eternal King, who had made such things for His people.

In that country, Enoch saw another wonder: mountains of metal — iron, copper, silver, gold, soft metal and lead. "What are these secret things?" he asked the angel.

And the angel said: "All these mountains will stand before God's chosen King — and before His face they will melt like wax before a fire, and run down like water from a hill. On that day, no one will be rescued by gold or silver. Iron will make no more armour. All earth's riches will be nothing — and the King will be everything."

So somewhere beyond the ends of the earth, there is a throne-mountain of jewels, and a tree whose fruit is kept fresh for God's friends — unwithered, all this time.

God is keeping a tree for His friends — and its fruit will be worth the whole world's gold.
