The Land and the Kings

The Angel Tells the Story Again

From the Book of Jubilees, chapters 2–5

Section: The Land and the Kings

You have heard the story of how God made the world. But did you know that heaven has its own copy of that story — told by an angel who was there?

When Moses stood on Mount Sinai, God sent His Angel of the Presence — one of the greatest of all the angels — and said: "Tell Moses the complete history of the creation. Let him write it all down." And the angel began:

"On the first day, God created the heavens and the earth, the waters and the deep — and He created us, the angels. Think of it! Before there were birds, there were angels of the winds and angels of the clouds, angels of the thunder and of the snow, angels of the spring and of the summer. God made everything, and we saw His works, and we praised Him.

"On the second day He made the great arch of the sky, and divided the waters above from the waters below.

"On the third day the waters gathered into seas and rivers, the dry land appeared — and up came the sprouting seed, the fruit trees, and the Garden of Eden.

"On the fourth day He set the sun and the moon and the stars in the sky, to give light, and to rule the day and the night — and to be His great calendar, marking the days, and the months, and the feasts, and the years.

"On the fifth day He created the great sea monsters in the deep, and the fish, and everything that flies.

"On the sixth day He created all the animals of the earth — and last of all, a man and a woman, and He gave them rule over everything He had made.

"And on the seventh day, God finished. And He made the seventh day holy — a Sabbath, a day of rest — as a great sign for ever.

"And here is the wonder of it: we angels kept that first Sabbath with Him in heaven, before anyone on earth had ever kept one. And God said: 'I will choose a people for Myself, and they will keep the Sabbath with the angels, on earth as it is in heaven — and I will bless them, and they will be My people, and I shall be their God.'"

So the angel told the whole beginning again — the Garden, and Adam naming the animals, and the flood that came in Noah's day, when God saved the one family whose person He accepted, and made His covenant with them. And Moses wrote it all down, every word.

The next time you hear the story of the world being made, remember: it is so important that it is kept in two places. We tell it down here — and the angels tell it up there.

The angels kept the very first Sabbath in heaven — and God invited His people to keep it with them.
