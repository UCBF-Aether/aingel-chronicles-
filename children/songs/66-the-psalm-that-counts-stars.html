The Psalm That Counts the Stars

From the Psalms of David, Psalm 147

Section: Songs and Wisdom

"Praise the LORD!" this psalm sings. "It is good to sing to our God — it is pleasant, and praise is beautiful!"

Listen to all the wonderful things this song says God does. He builds up His city and gathers home everyone who was scattered far away. **He heals the broken-hearted, and bandages up their wounds** — the way a kind nurse wraps a hurt knee, God wraps a hurt heart.

And now here is the verse to take outside on a clear night. Look up at the sky. Can you count the stars? Nobody can — there are too many, and some are too far away to see. But the psalm says: **God counts them.** He tells the number of the stars — every single one — **and He calls them all by their names.** The God who knows every star's name knows *your* name too.

The song goes on. God covers the sky with clouds and sends rain so the grass grows on the mountains. He feeds the animals, and even the baby ravens when they cry. He does not care most about the strong horse or the fast runner — what He delights in is everyone who trusts His mercy.

And He plays with the weather like a king scattering treasure: He gives snow like soft white wool, and sprinkles frost like ashes, and throws down His ice in little pieces — brr, who can stand before His cold? Then He sends His word, and everything melts, and the waters flow again.

Count the stars tonight if you can. God already has.

The God who calls every star by name knows your name too — and He heals broken hearts.
