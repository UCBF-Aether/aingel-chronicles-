Songs and Wisdom

Lady Wisdom Calls in the Street

From the Book of Proverbs, chapters 1, 8, and 9

Section: Songs and Wisdom

In King Solomon's book of wise sayings, Wisdom herself is pictured as a lady — and she is not hiding. She stands on the highest hill in the city, where everyone can see her. She stands where the paths cross. She stands at the city gates where people come in and out, and she **calls out**, loud and clear:

"To you, O people, I call! My voice is for everyone! Listen — I will speak true and excellent things. Everything I say is right; there is nothing crooked in my words. Take my teaching — it is better than silver! Choose what I know — it is better than the finest gold! **For wisdom is better than rubies, and nothing you could ever wish for compares with her!**"

Lady Wisdom has been around a very long time. "The LORD made me at the very beginning," she says, "before the mountains were settled, before the hills. When He prepared the heavens, I was there. When He set the sea in its place, I was beside Him, like a happy child at His side — rejoicing every day, and delighting in the people He made."

And she has built a house! Solomon says Wisdom has carved out her seven pillars and spread a feast inside, and she sends out the invitation to everyone passing by: come in, eat, learn, live. "I love those who love me," she promises, "and those who look for me early will find me."

So how do you find Lady Wisdom? You listen for her voice. She is calling still — in God's book, at the crossroads of every day, before you choose which way to go. Blessed are the children who stop, and listen, and keep her ways.

Before you choose today, stop and listen — Wisdom is calling, and she is better than rubies.
