The Shepherd Psalm

From the Psalms of David, Psalm 23

Section: Songs and Wisdom

King David wrote a song about God, and it is the most loved song in the whole world. It goes like this.

The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. That means: God is like a shepherd, and I am like His little sheep — and because He takes care of me, I have everything I need.

He makes me lie down in green pastures. He leads me beside the still waters, where it is quiet and safe to drink. He restores my soul — when I am tired inside, He makes me feel new again. He leads me on the right paths, because that is who He is.

Even if I walk through a dark valley — the valley of the shadow of death — I will not be afraid. Do you know why? **For You are with me.** The shepherd's rod and his staff are right there beside me, and they comfort me.

You spread a table for me, right where my enemies can see, like a feast at a king's house. You pour sweet oil on my head, the way you welcome an honoured guest. My cup runs over — it is so full of good things that it spills!

And this is how the song ends, and it is true for you too: Surely goodness and mercy will follow me all the days of my life — God's kindness will come after me everywhere I go, every single day — and I will live in the house of the LORD **for ever**.

You are God's own little sheep — and your Shepherd is with you in every valley, all the way home.
