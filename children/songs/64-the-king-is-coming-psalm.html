Songs and Wisdom

The King Is Coming Psalm

From the Psalms of David, Psalm 24

Section: Songs and Wisdom

This psalm begins with the biggest fact in the world: **The earth is the LORD's, and everything in it** — the seas and the rivers, the hills and the people, all of it. God made it all, and it all belongs to Him.

So who may climb God's holy hill and stand in His holy place? The psalm answers: the one with clean hands and a pure heart — hands that do what is right, and a heart that is true inside; the one who does not chase after empty, foolish things. That person receives God's blessing.

And then the psalm turns into a trumpet blast. Picture the great gates of the city, old and heavy and shut for a long, long time. The singers cry out to them:

"Lift up your heads, O you gates! Open up, you ancient doors — **the King of glory is coming in!**"

And the gates ask, "Who is this King of glory?"

And the singers answer, louder: "The LORD, strong and mighty! The LORD, mighty in battle! The LORD of hosts — **He** is the King of glory!"

Can you hear it? The doors swing wide. The King is coming. That is why God's people have always loved this song: one day the great King will come through the gates in glory — and everyone with clean hands and a pure heart will be there to welcome Him home.

Lift up your head — the King of glory is coming, and He is strong, and mighty, and good.
