The Church Begins and Grows

Wind and Fire: Pentecost

From the Acts of the Apostles, chapter 2

Section: The Church Begins and Grows

Ten days later came the feast of Pentecost, and all of Jesus' friends were together in one house in Jerusalem, waiting — just as Jesus had told them to wait.

Suddenly, there came a sound from heaven like a rushing, mighty wind — WHOOSH! — and it filled the house where they were sitting.

Then something like flames of fire appeared — tongues of fire that split and spread, and one flame settled on each person in the room, resting on every head like a crown of light. And they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and they began to speak in other languages — languages they had never learned! — as the Spirit gave them the words.

Now, Jerusalem was full of visitors from every nation under heaven, who had come for the feast. When they heard the WHOOSH, a crowd came running. And the crowd stood there amazed — because every person heard Jesus' friends speaking in their *own* language!

"We are from far-off lands," the visitors said, astonished, "north and south and east and west — and we hear these Galileans telling us, in our own languages, the wonderful works of God! What does this mean?"

Some people laughed and made unkind guesses. So Peter stood up with the eleven, and lifted up his voice, and told the whole crowd the truth:

"This is what the prophet Joel promised long ago! God said: 'In the last days, I will pour out My Spirit on all people.' Friends — God raised Jesus up from the dead, and we are all witnesses of it! And now Jesus, who has gone up to God's right hand, has poured out this Holy Spirit — and this is what you are seeing and hearing!"

"What shall we do?" the people cried — for their hearts were stirred through and through.

"Turn back to God," said Peter, "and be baptised, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ, and your sins will be forgiven — and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit too. The promise is for you, and for your children, and for everybody far away!"

And that very day, about three thousand people believed and were baptised!

And what a family they became. They learned together, and ate together, and prayed together, and shared everything they had with glad hearts, praising God every day. And every day, more people joined them — for the Holy Spirit had come, and the good news was running into all the world.

Thank You, God, for Your Holy Spirit — the wind, the fire, and the promise for us, and for our children, and for everybody far away.
