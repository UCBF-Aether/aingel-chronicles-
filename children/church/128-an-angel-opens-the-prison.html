The Church Begins and Grows

An Angel Opens the Prison

From the Acts of the Apostles, chapters 5 and 12

Section: The Church Begins and Grows

The leaders in Jerusalem were angry, because the apostles kept telling everyone about Jesus. So they arrested the apostles and put them in the public prison, and locked the doors tight.

But at night, an angel of the Lord came and opened the prison doors and brought them out. "Go," said the angel, "stand in the temple, and tell the people all the words of this life."

In the morning, the leaders sent officers to fetch the prisoners. The officers came back scratching their heads. "We found the prison shut up tight, with the guards standing outside the doors," they reported. "But when we opened it up — there was nobody inside!"

Then someone else ran in, all out of breath: "Look! The men you put in prison are standing in the temple, teaching the people!"

Years later, there was a king named Herod who hurt the church too. One of Jesus' twelve friends, James, was killed in those hard days, and many hearts were broken. Then Herod arrested Peter as well, and put him in prison, chained to two soldiers — one on each side — with more soldiers guarding the doors. Herod planned to deal with Peter after the feast.

But the church prayed. They prayed earnestly, without stopping, all night long.

And that very night — the night before Peter was to be brought out — Peter was *sleeping* between his two soldiers, bound with two chains. And suddenly an angel of the Lord stood there, and a light shone in the prison!

The angel touched Peter on the side and woke him. "Get up — quickly!" And the chains fell right off Peter's hands. "Put on your sandals," said the angel. "Wrap your cloak around you, and follow me."

Peter followed him out — past the first guard, past the second guard — and he thought he must be dreaming! They came to the great iron gate that leads out into the city, and the gate opened for them — *by itself*. They walked out into the street, and the angel was gone.

Then Peter knew. "Now I am sure it is true!" he said. "The Lord has sent His angel and rescued me!"

He hurried to a house where Jesus' friends were gathered, praying for him — and he knocked at the gate. A servant girl named Rhoda came to answer. She knew Peter's voice at once — and she was so happy that she ran back inside to tell everyone **without opening the gate!**

"It's Peter!" she cried. "Peter is standing at the gate!"

"You are imagining it!" they told her. "It cannot be Peter!" — even though they had been praying for him all night! But Peter went on knocking, knock, knock, knock — and at last they opened the gate, and there he was, and they were *astonished*.

Peter told them the whole story, of the angel and the light and the chains and the gate. Then he slipped away quietly to a safe place — while the prayer meeting was still buzzing with the wonder of it.

Thank You, God, that You hear Your people praying — and no prison, no chains, and no iron gate is stronger than You.
