The Church Begins and Grows

Jesus, Above All

From the Book of Colossians, chapter 1

Section: The Church Begins and Grows

Long ago in Colosse, the Christians sang a song about Jesus — and Paul loved it so much he wrote it into the front of his letter, so nobody would ever lose it. It is one of the biggest songs in the whole Bible, and it goes like this.

Jesus is the image of the invisible God. No one can see God — but when you look at Jesus, you are looking at exactly what God is like.

By Jesus, God made everything. Everything in heaven and everything on earth. Everything you can see — mountains and oceans, sparrows and stars — and everything you cannot see: the angels, and powers and kingdoms too big for our eyes. All of it was made by Him, and all of it was made for Him.

Before anything existed, He was already there. Before the first mountain, before the first star was lit — Jesus was. And even now, this very minute, He holds the whole world together. If He let go, everything would fall apart — but He never lets go.

He is the Head of the church, the way a head leads a body — because the church is His body, and we are all part of it together.

And He is the first to rise from the dead, never to die again — the beginning of the great resurrection morning that is coming for all God's children.

Why did God pack all this hugeness into one Person? Paul says it pleased the Father that in Jesus all the fullness of God should live — every bit of God, in a Man you could hug. And through Jesus, by His cross, God made peace — peace between heaven and earth, peace for everything and everyone, everywhere.

Then Paul prayed for his friends, the prayer he prayed for everyone: that they would be filled with knowing what God wants; that they would be fruitful in every good work; and that they would be strengthened with God's own strength, so they could be patient and joyful at the same time.

And he gave thanks for the greatest swap of all: God has rescued us out of the darkness and carried us into the kingdom of His dear Son — the kingdom of light, where sins are forgiven and nobody is lost.

"To whom God would make known the riches of this mystery," Paul wrote — and here is the mystery, the wonderful secret: Christ in you. Jesus, above all things, above the stars, above the angels — and as close as your own heart.

The Jesus who holds the whole universe together is the same Jesus who lives in your heart.
