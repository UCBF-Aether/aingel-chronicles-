The Church Begins and Grows

The Love Chapter

From Paul's First Letter to the Corinthians, chapters 13 and 15

Section: The Church Begins and Grows

In Paul's letter to the church in Corinth, there is a song — the beautiful song about love ever written. Paul wanted the Corinthians to know love is greatest, and nothing else works without it.

Suppose I could speak every language on earth, Paul said — even the languages of angels! If I do not have love, I am only a noise, like a clanging cymbal. *Clang, clang, clang* — just a racket.

Suppose I knew every secret, and had mountain-moving faith. If I do not have love, I am nothing.

Suppose I gave everything I own to feed the poor. If I do not have love, it does me no good.

So what *is* love? Paul tells us:

Love is patient — it can wait without grumbling. Love is kind. Love does not get jealous, does not brag, and is not stuck-up and proud. Love is not rude. Love does not think only about itself, and does not get angry quickly. Love does not keep a list of wrongs done. Love never cheers when something bad happens — love cheers for the truth.

Love bears all things, believes the best, hopes for the best, endures — it keeps going.

And love *never ends*.

One day, Paul said, we will not need prophecies, languages, or little bits of knowing anymore, because we will see clearly. Right now, it is like a face in a foggy glass. Then — face to face! Right now, I know things only in part. Then — I will know fully, the way God already knows me.

And Paul remembered growing up: "When I was a child, I talked like a child, I thought like a child. But when I grew up, I put away childish things." Some things are for now, some forever — and three things last forever: faith, hope, and love. And the greatest of these is love.

In the same letter, Paul gave a picture of the forever love is waiting for. A farmer puts a seed into the ground — and it lies there in the dark earth, finished. But God gives it a new body! Up comes a golden flower, or tall tree — far more beautiful than the seed was.

That is Paul's picture of what God will do for His friends. The body laid in the ground like a seed is only the beginning. God will raise it up — new, strong, and glorious — and death will be swallowed up in victory, gone forever.

Faith, hope, and love last forever. And greater than them all is love.

Thank You, God, for love — patient, kind, and never-ending — the greatest thing of all, now and forever.
