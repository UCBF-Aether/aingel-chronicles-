The Church Begins and Grows

The Armour of God

From the Book of Ephesians, chapter 6

Section: The Church Begins and Grows

Paul wrote this letter to a whole church — and he did not forget the children in it. He started with them: "Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right. Honour your father and your mother" — and that commandment comes with a promise tucked inside it: that things may go well with you, and you may live long and happily in the land.

Then, at the very end of his letter, Paul gave everyone a dressing-up picture to remember forever.

"Be strong in the Lord," he said, "and put on the whole armour of God."

Now, whose fight is this? Paul was careful to say: our fight is never against people — not against anyone you can see. People are never the enemy. The fight is against the darkness itself: against lies and fears and every trick that tries to pull us away from God. And for that fight, God Himself lends us His own armour, piece by piece, the way a king dresses a young soldier in the morning.

First, the belt of truth. A soldier's belt holds everything together — and God's truth holds us together. We say what is true, and we believe what is true.

Next, the breastplate of righteousness — God's goodness covering our hearts, the way a breastplate covers a soldier's chest.

Then the shoes of the gospel of peace, so our feet are ready to carry God's good news and God's peace wherever we walk.

Above all, Paul said, take the shield of faith. Faith is a great big shield we hold up in front of everything — and every burning arrow of fear or doubt that the darkness throws simply fizzles out against it and falls.

Then the helmet of salvation, guarding our heads and our thoughts: we belong to Jesus, and nothing can change that.

And last, the one piece you carry in your hand: the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God — God's own words, the strongest words in the world.

And when you are dressed, head to toe, in God's armour? Paul says there is one more thing to do the whole time: pray. Pray at all times. Pray for everyone. Pray for Paul too, locked in prison, that he would still speak boldly.

Then — having done all — just stand. Stand firm. Stand bright. The armour is God's, the strength is God's, and no one who stands in it stands alone.

Every morning you can dress in God's own armour — truth, goodness, peace, faith, and His word in your hand.
