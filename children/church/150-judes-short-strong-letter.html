The Church Begins and Grows

Jude's Short, Strong Letter

From the Letter of Jude

Section: The Church Begins and Grows

Jude was a servant of Jesus, and the brother of James. He sat down to write a happy letter about the rescue God gave us — but first, he said, I must write you a short, strong one. Hold on tight to the faith.

"Mercy to you," he begins, "and peace, and love — multiplied! May you have more and more of them, like loaves that never run out."

Then comes the strong part. "Dear friends, work hard to hold on to the faith that was given once, for all God's holy people." The faith is a treasure handed down from Jesus Himself. Some people were sneaking in and twisting God's kindness into an excuse to do wrong — as if grace were a permission slip for selfishness. It is not. Jude says: remember the old stories. Remember how God rescued His people out of Egypt, and how the story warns us to stay on His road. The stories guard us.

But Jude does not only say *hold on*. He says *build up!*

"But you, dear friends, build yourselves up in your most holy faith, like builders raising a strong house. Pray in the Holy Spirit. Keep yourselves in the love of God — stay right there, in the sunshine of His love — while you wait for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ, who brings you life forever."

And while you stand safe, look around for others. "Have mercy on the ones who are wobbling. Hold out your hand and help them, the way you would pull a friend back from the edge of a fire — gently, carefully, holding tight."

And then Jude ends with a blessing so beautiful that God's people have said it at bedtime ever since. Say it slowly:

"Now to Him who is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of His glory with great joy — to the only wise God our Saviour, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and forever. Amen."

Short letter. Strong letter. And it ends not with a warning, but with joy — the joy of a God who can keep even you from falling.

He is able to keep you, little one — so sleep in peace, and may mercy, peace and love be multiplied to you.
