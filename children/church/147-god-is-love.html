The Church Begins and Grows

God Is Love

From the Book of 1 John, chapter 4

Section: The Church Begins and Grows

Of all people who ever lived, John knew Jesus longest. He had been the young apostle leaning on Jesus' shoulder at supper. Now he was an old man — and of everything he had seen and heard, this is what he most wanted children to know.

He starts his letter as a grandfather gathers everyone: "Beloved — little children — listen. Not every voice that talks about God tells the truth, so test what you hear. Here is how you can know the true voice: it will say, gladly, that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh — that the Son of God really came, really lived, really is ours."

Then, gently: "You are of God, little children. And the One who lives in you is greater than the one in the world." Greater. Hold that word when anything frightens you: greater.

Then John says the thing — the biggest, simplest sentence about God:

"Beloved, let us love one another. For love is of God, and everyone who loves is born of God and knows God. He who does not love does not know God — for God is love."

God is love. Not God has love, like treasure in a vault. God is love, the way the sun is light. It is what He is, through and through.

And how do we know? How can we be sure? John answers: because God proved it. "In this the love of God was shown toward us: that God sent His only begotten Son into the world, that we might live through Him." Not because we loved God first — "He loved us, and sent His Son to make everything right between us and God." God made the first move. God always does.

So what is our move? Only one: "Beloved, if God so loved us, we also ought to love one another."

No one has ever seen God. But watch when we love each other: "God dwells in us, and His love is made perfect in us." People who have never seen God can see His love, living in us.

And love does one more thing: it throws fear out of the house. "There is no fear in love. Perfect love casts out fear." A child who knows she is loved — loved first, loved by God Himself — need not be afraid of anything. "We love Him," John finishes, "because He first loved us."

There it is, from Jesus' oldest friend to the youngest reader: He first loved us. Begin there, and everything else grows.

God is love, He loved you first — and perfect love throws every fear out of the house.
