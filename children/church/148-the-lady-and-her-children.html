The Church Begins and Grows

The Lady and Her Children

From the Second Letter of John

Section: The Church Begins and Grows

John was an old, old man now — the last of Jesus' twelve friends still living. He had walked with Jesus. He had leaned on Jesus' shoulder at supper. And he still loved to write letters to God's family.

This letter is a tiny one — so small it fits on one page. John wrote it to a dear lady and her children.

"Dear Lady," he wrote, "I love you in the truth — and not just me. Everyone who knows the truth loves you too, because the truth lives in us and will be with us forever. Grace and mercy and peace be with you, from God the Father and from Jesus."

Then John told her the thing that made his old heart sing: "I am so happy! I found your children walking in the truth, just as our Father told us to walk."

Walking in the truth — that is John's way of saying: they live the way God says, and they believe the true things about Jesus.

And what is the rule for the walking? John says it is not a new rule at all. It is the oldest rule there is:

"Love one another. This is love: that we walk God's way. You have heard this rule from the very beginning — so walk in it."

Truth and love. Love and truth. In John's letter they hold hands, like a mother and her children on a sunny road. The truth tells us who Jesus really is. And love is how we walk, step by step, day by day.

John adds one careful word: "Watch out for tricksters who do not tell the truth about Jesus. Hold on to what you were taught, so you will have the whole gift — the Father and the Son together."

And at the end, John smiles through the page. He says he has so much more to tell her — but paper and ink are too small for it. "I hope to come and see you," he writes, "and talk face to face, so that our joy will be full."

"The children of your dear sister send you their love," he finishes. And that is the whole letter — one page long, and warm enough to fill a house.

Walk in the truth, little one, and love as you go — that road leads all the way home.
