The Church Begins and Grows

The Hall of Faith

From the Book of Hebrews, chapters 11–12

Section: The Church Begins and Grows

Imagine a hall, its walls hung with God's friends' pictures. That is Hebrews chapter eleven — a portrait gallery — and it begins with a definition to carry: "Faith is being sure of what we hope for, and certain of what we cannot see."

Ready? Walk slowly. You have met most of them already.

Here is Abel, who brought God his best gift. Here is Enoch, who walked with God until God took him home, never seeing death.

Here is Noah, who trusted God's warning of an unseen flood, and built the boat on dry land while everybody laughed. Here is Abraham, who packed up for an unseen country, because God said "Go" — and he lived in tents in the promised land, looking to a better city, whose builder and maker is God.

Here is Sarah, laughing for joy — at ninety she held her promised baby. Here is Jacob, leaning on his staff, blessing his grandsons with dying breath. Here is Joseph, certain: "God will surely visit you" — asking that his bones be carried home.

Here is baby Moses in his basket, and grown, who said no to a palace in Egypt — no to treasure, no to a prince's name — choosing God's suffering people, seeing the invisible King. Here is the Red Sea, standing in walls; Jericho's walls, falling flat. Here is Rahab, who hid the spies and hung the scarlet cord.

And then the writer hurries — "What more shall I say? Time would fail me!" — Gideon, Samson, David, Samuel, all the prophets: people who through faith stopped lions' mouths, walked out of fire unburned, were made strong from weakness. And some — this part matters — never received the promise while they lived. They saw it from far away, waved at it, and died trusting. God was not ashamed of them. He was proud to be their God, and has built them their city.

Now — here is the wonder at the hall's end. The last picture frame is empty. It is yours.

"Therefore," says the writer, "since we are surrounded by so great a crowd of witnesses" — the whole hall, cheering like a stadium — "let us throw off everything that slows us, and run with patience the race set before us, looking to Jesus — the Author and Finisher of our faith — who for the joy set before Him endured the cross, and sat down at the right hand of God's throne."

The witnesses are watching. The champion waits at the finish. Run.

All of God's friends are cheering from the hall — and your picture belongs there too, so run your race looking at Jesus.
